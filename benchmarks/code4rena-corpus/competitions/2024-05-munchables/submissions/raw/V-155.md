# Vulnerability details

## Impact

The `getLockedWeightedValue` function will always return 0 due to incorrect calculations of `deltaDecimal`. This will cause the functionality for calculating the weighted value to fail.

## Proof of Concept

For example if we have token with 18 decimals `deltaDecimal` will be equal to 10 ** 0 which is 1.

```javascript
uint256 deltaDecimal = 10 **
                    (18 -
                        configuredTokens[configuredTokenContracts[i]].decimals);
```

Consider an example where the quantity is 100 and the USD price is 100.

```javascript
lockedWeighted +=
                    (deltaDecimal *
                        lockedTokens[_player][configuredTokenContracts[i]]
                            .quantity *
                        configuredTokens[configuredTokenContracts[i]]
                            .usdPrice) /
                    1e18;
```

For our example, `lockedWeighted` will be `1 * 100 * 100 / 1e18` which is equal to `0.00000000000001`. This value means our `lockedWeighted` will be equal to 0. Even with a bigger price and quantity, our `lockedWeighted` will be 0. The calculations are wrong because the current implementation works with non-decimal numbers but at the end divides by `1e18`.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

```diff
uint256 deltaDecimal = 10 **
-                    (18 - configuredTokens[configuredTokenContracts[i]].decimals);
+                    (configuredTokens[configuredTokenContracts[i]].decimals);
```

```diff
lockedWeighted +=
                    (deltaDecimal *
                        lockedTokens[_player][configuredTokenContracts[i]]
                            .quantity *
                        configuredTokens[configuredTokenContracts[i]]
                            .usdPrice) /
-                    1e18;
+                    (configuredTokens[configuredTokenContracts[i]].decimals);
```
