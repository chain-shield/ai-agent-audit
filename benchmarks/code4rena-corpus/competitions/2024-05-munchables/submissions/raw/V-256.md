# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The USDB and WETH tokens on the Blast network earn yield through rebasing.
User's can loose the their tokens due to yield rebasing

## Proof of Concept
Both USDB and WETH yield mode are AUTOMATIC by default. LockManager.sol uses internal accounting to track all lock/unlock amounts for users. For instance, when users lock amounts(_quantity), locked amount will increase by amount(quantity)
`lockedToken.quantity += _quantity;`
 and user's balance will increase by amount accordingly. 

The accrued yield due to automatic rebasing are lost as they cannot be claimed.

## Tools Used
Manual Analysis

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Implement a way to claim the yields to the locked tokens.
