# Vulnerability details

## Impact

In the current `_lock` function implementation, 
tokens locked outside the lockdrop period are not added to the `lockedToken.remainder`, 
which means they are not considered for minting NFTs when the next lockdrop period opens. 

This creates a discrepancy for users who lock tokens outside the lockdrop period 
and expect them to count towards NFT minting in future lockdrop periods.

Impact : Medium
Likelihood : High

## Proof of Concept

_Tokens Locked Outside Lockdrop Period: _

NFT Cost : 2000 USDB


_1. First Lock (Outside Lockdrop Period)_

-- Bob locks 1900 tokens on  March 29th 
-- lockupDuration : 60 days
-- These tokens are not added to the remainder for NFT minting


_2. Second Lock (Within Lockdrop Period) _
 (lockdrop period is announced to be open from April 1 to April 30)

-- Bob locks 100 tokens on April 2nd
-- Only these 100 tokens are added to remainder and considered for NFT minting, 
   and Bob misses out on potentially minting 1 NFT for the previously locked 1900 tokens.

## Tools Used

Manual Review, Foundry

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Modify the `_lock` function to account for 
tokens locked outside the lockdrop period and add them to the remainder.
This ensures they are considered for minting NFTs when the next lockdrop period opens.
