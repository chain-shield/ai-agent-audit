# Vulnerability details

##Impact

The end variable from the LockDrop struct is never used to determine the locking time or unlocking conditions. As a result, tokens can be unlocked before the lockdrop.end value.

##Proof of Concept

For example, the configureLockdrop method can be initialized with a minLockDuration value that is less than the value of (lockdropdata.end - lockdropdata.start). Consequently, the lockdropdata.end value will have no effect on the behavior of the unlock or lock functions.

##Tools Used

Manual review

##Recommended Mitigation Steps

Add a require statement in the unlock method that includes a condition based on lockdrop.end.
