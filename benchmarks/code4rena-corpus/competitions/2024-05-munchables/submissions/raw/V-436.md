# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Users can lock ETH or ERC20 tokens on behalf of a contract that is not prepared to handle ETH or not designed to allow the unlock and withdrawal of ERC20 tokens. 

This results in the permanent loss of ETH / such tokens.


## Tools Used
Manual review, Foundry.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Consider allowing users to set trusted/whitelisted addresses for their profiles, and only allow them to use `lockOnBehalf` for addresses that are in their whitelists.

Alternatively, consider disallowing the `lockOnBehalf` function to be used for contract addresses.
