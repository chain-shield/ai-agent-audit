# Vulnerability details

## Issue: Direct ERC20 operations without safety checks.


Lines:

`token.transferFrom(_tokenOwner, address(this), _quantity);` #376
`payable(msg.sender).transfer(_quantity);`  #420
`token.transfer(msg.sender, _quantity);`    #423


## Impact: 

The absence of safety checks in `ERC20` token transfers presents a critical vulnerability, opening the door to potential exploits by malicious actors. Without proper validation mechanisms, unauthorized transfers or unexpected behaviors may occur, leading to the loss or manipulation of user funds. This vulnerability undermines the integrity and security of the smart contract, posing a significant financial risk to users.

## Mitigation: 

To mitigate these risks, it is recommended to use the `SafeERC20` library or manually implement safety checks by using require statements. `SafeERC20` provides safer alternatives for performing `ERC20` operations by handling potential errors and ensuring correct execution. Additionally, require statements can be used to validate inputs and enforce security measures, reducing the risk of exploits or unexpected behavior.
