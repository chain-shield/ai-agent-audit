# Vulnerability details

## Impact

When a user unlocks tokens, they only receive the quantity they requested, even if it means leaving a remainder in the contract. If the user wants to withdraw the remaining tokens, they must lock tokens again and wait at least 30 days before they can unlock and withdraw the remainder. This process is required solely for withdrawing the leftover tokens.

## Proof of Concept

## Tools Used

Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Add a method for the user to be able to withdraw the remainder
