# Vulnerability details

## Vulnerability Detail
In the `LockManager` contract, the calculation of the locked token value and the issuance of rewards (like NFTs) depend on the precision (decimals) of the token being locked. The contract does not correctly handle tokens with different decimal places, which will lead to incorrect calculations, allowing users to exploit the system to gain an unfair advantage or causing under/overestimation of locked values.
Also, the number of NFTs issued is calculated based on the `nftCost` parameter, which assumes a standard decimal format. If a token with fewer decimals is used, the calculation may result in fewer NFTs being issued than intended, or if more decimals are used, more NFTs than intended.
NOTE: Even though the ERC20 tokens used by the protocol are currently just USDB and WETH, it states that "assume we can add more to the future for use in LockManager" which brings this vulnerability into scope.
## Impact
- Users may receive an incorrect number of NFTs due to miscalculations based on token decimals. This can lead to an unfair distribution of rewards, either diluting the reward pool or concentrating rewards in fewer hands.
- The locked token value could be overestimated or underestimated. Overestimation may allow users to gain undue influence or benefits, while underestimation could disincentivize legitimate users from participating.
