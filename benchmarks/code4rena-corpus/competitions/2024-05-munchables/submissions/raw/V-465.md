# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Due to the wrong implementation of the [zero address check](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-munchables/blob/57dff486c3cd905f21b330c2157fe23da2a4807d/src/managers/LockManager.sol#L157), any [proposer](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-munchables/blob/57dff486c3cd905f21b330c2157fe23da2a4807d/src/interfaces/ILockManager.sol#L57) with an address that is not zero(`address(0)`) will not be able to propose a new USD pricing proposal.
Hence making the protocol vulnerable to incorrect accounting and incorrect pricing of tokens.
