# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Token prices may be maliciously delayed, resulting in erroneous prices.

## Proof of Concept
In the contract, the price of the token is determined based on proposals and votes initiated by 5 PriceFeeds. And [only one price proposal can exist at the same time](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-munchables/blob/57dff486c3cd905f21b330c2157fe23da2a4807d/src/managers/LockManager.sol#L157-L158). Therefore, if there is a PriceFeed that continuously initiates proposals with wrong prices, proposals with correct prices cannot be initiated, and price updates will be delayed.

## Tools Used

manual

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
It is recommended to obtain the price of chainlink. If the price is not updated after a certain period of time, the effective price of chainlink shall prevail.
