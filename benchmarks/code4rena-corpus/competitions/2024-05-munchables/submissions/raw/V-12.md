# Vulnerability details



## Description

The `LockManager` contract is designed to lock ERC20 tokens. One of these tokens, USDB, is implemented using the `ERC20Rebasing` contract, which supports different yield modes and automatic rebasing. The lock mechanism in `LockManager` may not correctly account for the accrued yields resulting from the rebasing of USDB. Consequently, the yield accrued due to automatic rebasing can be lost, as it cannot be properly claimed or reflected in the locked amounts.



## Impact

- **Loss of Accrued Yield:** The primary adverse outcome is that any yield accrued due to automatic rebasing may be lost, as `LockManager` does not account for these changes. Users will not be able to claim the additional tokens generated through rebasing, potentially resulting in significant financial loss over time.



## Proof of Concept



### Locking USDB:

```solidity

// User locks 1000 USDB

lockManager.lock(tokenAddress, 1000 * 10**18);

```



### Rebasing Event:

Assume USDB undergoes a rebasing event, increasing the balance by 10%. User's balance in the `ERC20Rebasing` contract reflects 1100 USDB due to the automatic rebalancing. However, this increase is not accounted for in the locked balance.



### Attempting to Unlock:

```solidity

// User attempts to unlock 1000 USDB

lockManager.unlock(tokenAddress, 1000 * 10**18);

```



### Potential Errors:

- The `lockedTokens[msg.sender][tokenAddress].quantity` in `LockManager` remains 1000 USDB, ignoring the rebased yield.

- The additional 100 USDB yield is not accessible or claimable, effectively resulting in a loss for the user.



## Tools Used

- Manual Review





## Recommended Mitigation Steps



### Regular Updates to Locked Amounts:

Implement a mechanism to update locked amounts regularly by querying the rebased balance from the `ERC20Rebasing` contract. This ensures yields from rebasing are accurately reflected and claimable.
