## Impact

## Proof of Concept
[configuredTokenContracts](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-munchables/blob/57dff486c3cd905f21b330c2157fe23da2a4807d/src/managers/LockManager.sol#L22) array it has no where to delete or remove unused tokens, the admin can only push more tokens [configuredTokenContracts.push(_tokenContract)](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-munchables/blob/57dff486c3cd905f21b330c2157fe23da2a4807d/src/managers/LockManager.sol#L122).

This could be problematic if more number of tokens pushed in future, that will affect `getLockedWeightedValue` method loop and might cause out of gas error, because of [configuredTokensLength](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-munchables/blob/57dff486c3cd905f21b330c2157fe23da2a4807d/src/managers/LockManager.sol#L466) loop.
