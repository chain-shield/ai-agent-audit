# Vulnerability details

## Impact
A user can have their tokens locked without their permission. This would lead to potential financial asset loss or losing control over their assets.

## Proof of Concept
Attacker calls `lockOnBehalf` with `_tokenContract`, `_quantity`, and another user's address `(_onBehalfOf)`.
Tokens are locked on behalf of the other user without their permission.

## Tools Used
Manual Code Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Add permission check to make sure that only the owner of the tokens or an authorized party can lock tokens on behalf of another user. This can be done by implementing Codex or use 3rd party library. (OpenZeppelin’s Access Control)
