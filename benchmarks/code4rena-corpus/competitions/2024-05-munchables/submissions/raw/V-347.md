# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Lock functionality may not be working because of overflow in special case, if `uint32(block.timestamp) + uint32(lockDuration) > type(uint32).max`

## Proof of Concept
There can be case of `uint32(block.timestamp) + uint32(lockDuration) > type(uint32).max` after about 82 years.

For example: 
lockDuration = 30 days (as minimum) = 2592000 seconds
while block.timestamp < type(uint32).max && block.timestamp > type(uint32).max - 30 days, uint32(block.timestamp) + uint32(lockDuration) will revert.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
wrap the line with unchecked block, or use uint256 instead of uint32
