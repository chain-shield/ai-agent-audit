# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Both USDB and WETH yield mode are AUTOMATIC by default, The protocol currently does not account for the rebasing nature of tokens like USDB and WETH within its locking mechanism. This results in inaccurate token calculations and potentially unclaimed yields for users who lock these tokens.

## Proof of Concept
When a user locks a rebasing token like USDB or WETH, the locked amount is determined at the time of locking.
Example: A user locks 100 USDB tokens. Over time, due to rebasing, the user's balance of USDB tokens increases to 110. The protocol, however, continues to consider only the initial 100 USDB tokens in the locking mechanism.

After rebasing, the protocol should query the current balance and adjust the internal records to ensure the user's increased balance is considered
