# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Due to the contract's failure to adjust locked balances dynamically based on changes in token values caused by rebasing events, users may experience impermanent loss when unlocking their tokens. This loss occurs because the contract only tracks balances based on the initial quantity locked without considering changes in token values over time.

## Proof of Concept

The protocal teams intends to use USDB/WETH tokens and the contract is to be deployed on Blast L2. Both USDB and WETH yield mode are AUTOMATIC by default.
The `lockManager` contract makes use of internal accounting to track Amount locked by users. When tokens are locked, `they are held within the contract` and the user becomes eligible to receive rewards based on the locking duration and the amount of tokens locked. 

During the lock period, the total supply of USDB and WETH undergoes rebasing events causing changes in the value of individual token balances.

Upon unlocking, the specified original quantity of tokens is transferred back to the user's wallet along with any accrued rewards based on the locking duration and the amount of tokens locked. The tokens may be worth less due to the uncaptured `rebasing effect on individual users` while on lock.

The  contract  fails to account for the impact of rebasing events to individual users who had locked their USDB or WETH tokens on the contract.

## Tools Used
Manual Analysis
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
 Modify the `LockManager` contract to dynamically adjust locked user balances based on changes in token values caused by rebase events.
