# Vulnerability details

## Impact
**Incorrect Calculation of numberNFTs**
When `quantity` is less than `configuredToken.nftCost`, the calculation `(quantity - remainder) / configuredToken.nftCost` will result in `numberNFTs` being set to 0.
This means that even if the user has enough tokens to mint at least one NFT, the function will not mint any NFTs.

**Incorrect Calculation of remainder**
Since `remainder` is calculated as `quantity % configuredToken.nftCost`, when `numberNFTs` is 0, the entire quantity will be stored as the remainder.
This means that the user's entire locked token amount will be stored as the remainder, and no NFTs will be minted.

To illustrate the concept:
Suppose `quantity` is 5, and `configuredToken.nftCost` is 10.
```solidity
remainder = 5 % 10 = 5
numberNFTs = (5 - 5) / 10 = 0
```
In this case, even though the user has enough tokens to mint one NFT, the function will set `numberNFTs` to 0 and store the entire `quantity` of 5 as the `remainder`.

This issue can lead to the following problems:

**Users may not receive any NFTs:** Even when users have enough tokens to mint at least one NFT, the function will not mint any NFTs if their `quantity` is less than `configuredToken.nftCost`.

**Incorrect state management:** The `lockedToken.remainder` and `lockedToken.quantity` will not be updated correctly, leading to inconsistencies in the contract's state.

**User frustration:** Users may become frustrated if they are unable to mint NFTs even when they have enough tokens, potentially leading to a negative user experience.

## Proof of Concept
The issue lies in the following lines: [LockManager.sol#L363-L364](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-munchables/blob/57dff486c3cd905f21b330c2157fe23da2a4807d/src/managers/LockManager.sol#L363-L364)
```solidity
                remainder = quantity % configuredToken.nftCost;
                numberNFTs = (quantity - remainder) / configuredToken.nftCost;
```
The problem is that the calculation of `numberNFTs` does not account for the case where `quantity` is less than `configuredToken.nftCost`. In such a scenario, `numberNFTs` will be calculated as 0, and the entire `quantity` will be stored as the `remainder`.

For example, if quantity is 5 and `configuredToken.nftCost` is 10, the calculation will result in `remainder` being 5 and `numberNFTs` being 0, even though the user should be able to mint at least one NFT.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
The calculation of `numberNFTs` and remainder should be modified to handle the case where quantity is less than `configuredToken.nftCost`. Possible solution is to swap the order of the calculations.
```solidity
numberNFTs = quantity / configuredToken.nftCost;
remainder = quantity % configuredToken.nftCost;
```
This way, `numberNFTs` will be calculated correctly, and the `remainder` will be the leftover amount after minting the maximum possible number of NFTs, even when `quantity` is less than `configuredToken.nftCost`.
