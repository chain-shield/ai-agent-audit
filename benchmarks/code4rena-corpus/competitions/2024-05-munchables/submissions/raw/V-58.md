# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Attackers can pre-empt transactions to gain more rewards.

## Proof of Concept
The price of user-locked tokens in the contract is determined based on voting. [The price will be updated when the number of agreed votes reaches the threshold](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-munchables/blob/57dff486c3cd905f21b330c2157fe23da2a4807d/src/managers/LockManager.sol#L202-L204).
Imagine the following situation. If the price of token A drops in the market now, the contract votes to change the price. At this time, the attacker monitors the transaction that changes the price. He initiates the transaction first and calls [the harvest function](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-munchables/blob/57dff486c3cd905f21b330c2157fe23da2a4807d/src/managers/AccountManager.sol#L116). As a result, [the getLockedWeightedValue function](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-munchables/blob/57dff486c3cd905f21b330c2157fe23da2a4807d/src/managers/LockManager.sol#L461) will return more locked value than after the token price drops, and thus he [can get more rewards](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-munchables/blob/57dff486c3cd905f21b330c2157fe23da2a4807d/src/managers/AccountManager.sol#L363-L369). cause losses to the agreement.
