# Vulnerability details

## Impact
It is impossible to read locked weighted value for players, breaking the logic of the protocol

## Proof of Concept
According to the readme, high decimal tokens (> 18) tokens are in scope. However, they are not handled in the `getLockedWeightedValue` function.

```solidity
uint256 deltaDecimal = 10 **
(18 - configuredTokens[configuredTokenContracts[i]].decimals);
```
After adding a high decimal token, then the `getLockedWeightedValue` will revert due to underflow.

## Tools Used

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Either modify the `deltaDecimal` calculation to allow high decimal tokens or don't allow them at all.
