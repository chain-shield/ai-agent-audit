# Vulnerability details

## Impact
A price feed can approve and disapprove a USD proposal. The threshold becomes irrelevant as the price feed becomes neutral for this proposal.

## Proof of Concept
We can assume that the price feed will be automated. If the token price varies during the proposal, and if the heartbeat of the price feed is not dependent on the proposal cycle, the price feed will first approve and then disapprove.

## Tools Used

Manual review 

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Add a condition for disapproval in the approve function and vice versa for the disapprove function.
