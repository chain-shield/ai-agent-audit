# Vulnerability details

## Impact
As clearly mentioned in the [contest details](https://code4rena.com/audits/2024-05-munchables#top) munchables supports tokens less than 6 decimal places.
But in [getLockedWeightedValue](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-munchables/blob/57dff486c3cd905f21b330c2157fe23da2a4807d/src/managers/LockManager.sol#L461) 
In case of tokens less than 6 decimal places, it will lose precision and show less amount hence making the user lose his funds.


## Proof of Concept
A brief walkthrough of [getLockedWeightedValue](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-munchables/blob/57dff486c3cd905f21b330c2157fe23da2a4807d/src/managers/LockManager.sol#L461) in the given case.
Case: Token A (2 decimal places)
>Quantity: 24352453453
>End result(assuming pricing of Token A is $1): 243524534
*It only shows precision up to  **9 decimal places** in this case, leading to the User losing his funds.*
