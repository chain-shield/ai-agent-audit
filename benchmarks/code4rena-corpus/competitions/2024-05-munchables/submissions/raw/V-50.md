# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Prices may expire and incorrect prices may appear.

## Proof of Concept
According to the current price logic, 5 nodes vote, and 3 PriceFeeds need to agree before the price will be updated correctly. Imagine a situation where the price needs to be updated when the price drops, if there are 2 votes in favor and 2 votes against. There is also a PriceFeed that has been delayed in voting due to network problems and other reasons. At this time, there will be a delay in price updates, and users may make profits through [harvest](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-munchables/blob/57dff486c3cd905f21b330c2157fe23da2a4807d/src/managers/AccountManager.sol#L116). The protocol cannot prevent this loss in time.


## Tools Used
manual

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
It is recommended to prepare backup measures for price updates. Or add more PriceFeeds.
