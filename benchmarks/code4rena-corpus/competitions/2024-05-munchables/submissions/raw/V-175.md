# Vulnerability details

The `issue` here is that the calculation of the NFT amount and minting to the user is done in an `if statement` meaning that if the `lockdrop` period has not started yet or has already ended no minting of NFT will occur but the function `doesn't` revert in this situation leading to users depositing assets and it is locked for a period of time without earning any NFT from the lockup.


## Impact
This will lead to user asset and `time` being wasted as it is locked by the protocol without any NFT rewarded for lockup

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
The `_lock()` should be corrected to revert when not in `lockdrop` period saving users from wasting their time and locking up for no reason.
