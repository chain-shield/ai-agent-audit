# Vulnerability details


## Impact

A potential overflow can occur in the calculation of `lockedWeighted`, which can cause the _lockedWeightedValue of some tokens to always revert. Looking at the code implemented

LockManager line 472  // We are assuming all tokens have a maximum of 18 decimals and that USD Price is denoted in 1e18.

AS stated in the link tokens above decimal 18 are in scope, hence this will lead to a revertion if such tokens are ever added.

## Proof of Concept

The following code snippet demonstrates the potential overflow:

```solidity
uint256 deltaDecimal = 10 ** (18 - configuredTokens[configuredTokenContracts[i]].decimals);
lockedWeighted += (deltaDecimal * lockedTokens[_player][configuredTokenContracts[i]].quantity * configuredTokens[configuredTokenContracts[i]].usdPrice) / 1e18;
```

The issue lies in the calculation of `deltaDecimal`. If `configuredTokens[configuredTokenContracts[i]].decimals` is greater than 18, the subtraction results in a negative exponent for `10**`. This negative exponent can lead to unexpected behavior or overflows during the multiplication. Therefore the transaction automatically reverts.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

1. **Support Decimals Above 18:**

   ```solidity
   uint256 deltaDecimal = configuredTokens[configuredTokenContracts[i]].decimals > 18 ?
       1e18 ** (configuredTokens[configuredTokenContracts[i]].decimals - 18) :
       10 ** (18 - configuredTokens[configuredTokenContracts[i]].decimals);
   ```

   This approach uses exponentiation with `1e18` for decimals above 18, avoiding negative exponents and potential overflows.

2. **Restrict Token Addition:**

   - Implement a mechanism to restrict adding new tokens to those with decimals less than or equal to 18 during token registration.
   - Clearly document and communicate this restriction to users.
