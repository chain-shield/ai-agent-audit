# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The price feeds have no way to know the current price of the proposedPrice from LockManager, because of this the price feeds wouldn't have sufficient information to approve or disapprove the USD price.

## Proof of Concept
`USDUpdateProposal usdUpdateProposal; //@audit Can't be accessed by other contracts`
