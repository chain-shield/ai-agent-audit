# Vulnerability details

If a player locks a value less than the `nftCost`, they will not receive any NFTs because the amount they have locked is insufficient to meet the minimum requirement for one NFT. Instead, the entire locked amount will be treated as a remainder, which will be stored in the player's `lockedToken.remainder`. even when` lockPeriod ` have not ended. This is displeasing as every lock should result in at least one NFT.
The `nftCost` can't be known to players as there no the `getNftCost` function users will have to guess the amount.

## Impact
Without knowing the correct `nftCost` users are forced to lock any amount.
Players who deposit an amount less than the NFT cost will not receive any NFTs because their locked funds are insufficient to cover the cost of one NFT. This will lead  to displeasure among users, as their locked funds are stored as a remainder, which might not be immediately clear or useful unless additional funds are deposited later to meet the NFT cost. Assuming they can afford it.

## Proof of Concept
When a player locks funds that are less than the `nftCost`, the calculation logic results in the entire amount being treated as a remainder, and no NFTs are awarded.
https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-munchables/blob/57dff486c3cd905f21b330c2157fe23da2a4807d/src/managers/LockManager.sol#L363-L364 
```solidity
                // calculate number of nfts
                remainder = quantity % configuredToken.nftCost;
                numberNFTs = (quantity - remainder) / configuredToken.nftCost;
```


Example Scenario:
- Suppose the `nftCost` is 1.5 ETH.
- A player locks 1 ETH, which is less than the `nftCost`.

The calculation will be:

```solidity
uint256 remainder = 1 % 1.5; // remainder = 1
uint256 numberNFTs = (1 - 1) / 1.5; // numberNFTs = 0
```
In this case, the `remainder` is 5 ETH, and `numberNFTs` is 0.

The remainder of 1 ETH is stored in the player's `lockedToken.remainder`:
https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-munchables/blob/57dff486c3cd905f21b330c2157fe23da2a4807d/src/managers/LockManager.sol#L379
```solidity
lockedToken.remainder = remainder;
lockedToken.quantity += _quantity;
lockedToken.lastLockTime = uint32(block.timestamp);
lockedToken.unlockTime = uint32(block.timestamp) + uint32(_lockDuration);
```
Since the amount locked is less than the `nftCost`, no NFTs are issued, and the entire amount is simply stored as a remainder.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
 Consider setting a minimum lock amount that is equal to or greater than the `nftCost` to prevent users from locking insufficient amounts. This ensures that every lock results in at least one NFT being awarded.
Include a `getNftCost` function.
