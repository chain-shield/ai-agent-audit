# Vulnerability details

## Impact
This 
- breaks core contract invariant and 
- leads to leak of funds

because users can  earn points without locking tokens for the full length of time that was originally set when the tokens where locked.

## Proof of Concept
Users can set their lockup duration of a particular `tokenCotract` by calling `setLockDuration(...)` however due to it’s wrong logic implementation, users can manipulate (reduced) their originally set lockup periods after locking a token.

For simplicity, assume there are 3 `tokenContract` A, B and C, `MaxLockDuration` = 90days, `MinLockDuration`  = 20 days and time `t` started at day 0 

- Alice had previous locked A on day 30 in a previous  `lockdrop` event for a duration of 50 days
    - `lastLockTime` = day 30
    - `lockDuration` = 50 days
    - `unlockTime` = day 80
- Alice unlocks her position on day 81.
- On **day 85** Alice performs another `lockdrop` by calling `lock(...)` with token A for a duration of the same 50 days
    - `lastLockTime` = day 85
    - `lockDuration` = 50 days
    - `block.timestamp` = day 85
    - `unlockTime` on `L382` = `block.timestamp` + `lockDuration` = day 85 + 50 days = day 135
    - Alice can unlock in the next 50 days
- On **day 90** Alice calls `setLockDuration(...)` with `_duration` = 45 days,
    - time remaining before unlock is 45 days
    - `L249` checks correctly and passes
    - `L261` to `L262` checks correctly as → days 90 + 45 days == days 135
    - `lastLockTime` = day 85  (cached on `L267`)
    - `block.timestamp` = day 90
    - `unlockTime` is updated on `L268`  = `lastLockTime` + `_duration` = day 85 + 45 days = day 125
    - Alice has successfully reduced her lock time by 10 days
- On **day 95** Alice calls `setLockDuration(...)` with `_duration` = 30 days,
    - time now remaining before unlock is 30 days
    - `L249` checks correctly and passes
    - `L261` to `L262` checks correctly as → day 95 + 30 days == day 125
    - `lastLockTime` = day 85  (cached on `L267`)
    - `block.timestamp` = day 95
    - `unlockTime` is updated on `L268`  = `lastLockTime` + `_duration` = day 85 + 30 days = day 115
    - Alice has successfully reducing her lock time by an additional 10 days
- Alice can now `unlock(...)` 15 days before the end of her originally set lockup time because she reduced her unlock time from day 135 back to day 115 a 20 day difference and yet she is entitled to all points owed all the honest users (`L410` will not revert).
