# Vulnerability details

## Impact
This can lead to funds getting stuck and some players will not be able to withdraw

## Proof of Concept
From the [audit documentation](https://code4rena.com/audits/2024-05-munchables#toc-3-erc20-token-behaviors-in-scope) Fee on transfer tokens are in the scope of this audit.


When players lock their tokens with `lock(...)` function the player’s `lockedToken.quantity` is updated to include the amount tokens that the player entered.

However, if the the `tokenContract` is a Fee on Transfer token the players `lockedToken.quantity` will be over inflated

- Alice locks 20_000e18 token A which has a 1% fee on transfers
- Alice `lockedToken.quantity` = 20_000e18
- 20_000e18 is transfered from Alice wallet to `LockManager` but 1% fee applies and balance of `LockManager` = 19_800e18
- Alice tries to `unlock(...)` but her tokens are stuck because there is not enough funds for her to withdraw and she can only withdraw exactly `lockedToken.quantity`

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Modify the `_lock(...)` such that the balance `LockManager` is checked  before and after the transfer is made from the user and use the difference to update the `lockedToken.quantity`  and even the `numberNFTs` variables respectively for the player.
