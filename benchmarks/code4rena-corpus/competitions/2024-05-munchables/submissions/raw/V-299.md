# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The condition `configuredTokens[tokenContract].nftCost != 0` is used to determine whether the USD price of a token contract should be updated or not In the `_execUSDPriceUpdate` function. This condition may not be correct because the `nftCost` variable represents the cost of minting an NFT in terms of the token contract's native token, not the USD price.

If the intention is to update the USD price only for token contracts that have a non-zero USD price, then the condition should be `configuredTokens[tokenContract].usdPrice != 0` instead of `configuredTokens[tokenContract].nftCost != 0`.

If the intention is to update the USD price for all token contracts included in the proposal, regardless of their current USD price, then the condition `configuredTokens[tokenContract].nftCost != 0` should be removed entirely.

## Proof of Concept
The USD price of a token contract is a crucial parameter that affects various aspects of the system's functionality, such as the calculation of locked token values, the cost of minting NFTs, and potentially other operations that rely on accurate USD prices. Setting incorrect USD prices could lead to inconsistencies and errors throughout the system, potentially causing financial losses or other unintended consequences.

Consider the following scenario:

There are two token contracts: `tokenA` and `tokenB`.
`tokenA` has a non-zero `nftCost` (e.g., 1 ETH) and a zero `usdPrice`.
`tokenB` has a zero `nftCost` and a non-zero `usdPrice` (e.g., $1000).
A proposal is made to update the USD price of both token contracts to $2000.

Using the current condition `configuredTokens[tokenContract].nftCost != 0`, the USD price of `tokenA` would be updated to $2000, even though its initial `usdPrice` was zero. However, the USD price of `tokenB` would not be updated because its `nftCost` is zero, despite having a non-zero initial `usdPrice`.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
The condition `configuredTokens[tokenContract].nftCost != 0` should be replaced with `configuredTokens[tokenContract].usdPrice != 0` if the intention is to update the USD price only for token contracts that have a non-zero USD price. Alternatively, if the intention is to update the USD price for all token contracts included in the proposal, the condition should be removed entirely.
