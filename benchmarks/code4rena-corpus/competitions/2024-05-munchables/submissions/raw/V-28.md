# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Price feed can block update of the price by sending new proposals right after rejection of the previous one. If an attacker will gain control over 1 of the price feeds he can constantly propose new usd price (that wouldn't be correct according to 4 others price feeds) and because only admin can ban price feed and change config - it could bring some issues, because of the delay of admin actions, in highly volatile market this could bring an issue when usd price can't be updated because of the malicuous price feed that is blocking 4 other price feeds.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Add some protection so that if a price feed proposes an incorrect price several times in a row, it cannot propose a new price for a certain period or until a certain number of proposals have been made by others.
