# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The use of `transfer()` for transferring ETH in the `unlock()` function can result in transaction failures due to the fixed gas stipend of 2300 gas units forwarded by `transfer()`. This can be problematic in the following cases:
1. If the recipient contract has a fallback function that requires more than 2300 gas, the transfer will fail.
2. Changes in gas costs, such as those introduced by [EIP-1884](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-1884), can increase the gas required for operations within the fallback function, making previously functioning contracts fail. [Gas Cost Increase for SLOAD, BALANCE and EXTCODEHASH](https://chainsecurity.com/istanbul-hardfork-eips-increasing-gas-costs-and-more/#:~:text=Therefore%2C%20EIP%2D1884%20raises%20the,from%20400%20to%20700%20gas.)
3. If the recipient contract is called through a proxy, the additional gas usage can exceed 2300 gas.
4. Some multisig wallets require more than 2300 gas for their operations.

As a result, users may find themselves unable to unlock their ETH from the contract, leading to locked funds.
