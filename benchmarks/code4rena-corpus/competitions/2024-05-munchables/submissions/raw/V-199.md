# Vulnerability details


## Impact
Users can increase the unlockTime by using the setLockDuration function.
Although there is validation to prevent reducing the lock duration, it can be bypassed due to the incorrect setting of unlockTime.

## Proof of Concept
After one block, lastLockTime becomes less than block.timestamp. 
Users can call setLockDuration with playerSettings' duration minus 1, thereby bypassing the LockDurationReducedError.
