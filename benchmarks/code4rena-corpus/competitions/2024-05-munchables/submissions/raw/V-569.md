# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The structure for approving and disapproving USD price updates currently allows for an address to disapprove and then approve a proposal, but not the other way round ([LockManager.sol#L225](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-munchables/blob/57dff486c3cd905f21b330c2157fe23da2a4807d/src/managers/LockManager.sol#L225)). In this scenario, counters for both approvals and disapprovals are incremented. In a situation where there is a split vote, it could be that some of the disapproving addresses might be forced to approve to clear the proposal pipeline. The safer option would involve disapproving instead, since voting thresholds cannot change during an ongoing proposal.

## Proof of Concept
1. There are 5 assigned voters and threshold is set to 3 for both approval and disapproval.
2. Voter 1 submits the proposal and thus cannot vote.
3. Voters 2 and 3 approve.
4. Voters 4 and 5 disapprove.
5. Either voter 4 or 5 is forced to approve to clear the proposal queue.
6. If the proposal is considered disagreeable, the admin would have to decrease

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Allow voters to either switch their voting position or disapprove of the proposal, regardless of whether they have already approved or not.
