# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Users will lose their remainder assets.

## Proof of Concept
[Remainder represents the remainder of the user's deposited assets that cannot be divisible by nftcost](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-munchables/blob/57dff486c3cd905f21b330c2157fe23da2a4807d/src/managers/LockManager.sol#L363). This part is also the user's asset. However, [in the unlock function](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-munchables/blob/57dff486c3cd905f21b330c2157fe23da2a4807d/src/managers/LockManager.sol#L401), users cannot withdraw this part of the assets. They will lose these assets.

## Tools Used
manual

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
It is recommended to add the user's remainder when checking the number of user unlocks.
