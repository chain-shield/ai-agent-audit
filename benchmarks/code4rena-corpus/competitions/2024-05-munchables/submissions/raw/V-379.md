# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In cases of emergencies, if the protocol wants to stop any activity going on, they can't stop the prices from getting updated as [proposeUSDPrice](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-munchables/blob/57dff486c3cd905f21b330c2157fe23da2a4807d/src/managers/LockManager.sol#L142), [approveUSDPrice](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-munchables/blob/57dff486c3cd905f21b330c2157fe23da2a4807d/src/managers/LockManager.sol#L177), [disapproveUSDPrice](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-munchables/blob/57dff486c3cd905f21b330c2157fe23da2a4807d/src/managers/LockManager.sol#L210) do not have `notPaused` modifier making the `lockmanager.sol` prone to wrong token's pricing issues.
