# Vulnerability details

## Impact
User's locked funds may not be able to be unlocked due to unsafe casting to uint32 of block.timestamp.

## Proof of Concept
2 ^ 32 - 1 = 4294967295 (about 136 years)
There is a case of block.timestamp > type(uint32).max after about 82 years from now on. The current codebase does not account for this case. 
So the condition `if (lockedToken.unlockTime > uint32(block.timestamp))` is true, then unlock() will revert for many years.

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
we need to take account into the case of block.timestamp > type(uint32).max.
