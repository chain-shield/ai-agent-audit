# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Players funds can get stuck in the lock manager without a way to withdraw as long as attacker decides.

## Proof of Concept
Anyone can call `lockOnBehalf(...)` to lock tokens on behalf of a registered player, however this is dangerous for player who already has a `_tokenContract` locked and moreso the attack path is cheap as the attacker only pays for gas

Assume time starts from t = day 0

- on day 30 Alice, a registered player  has  locked 2_000_000 token A for 50 days to be unlocked on day 80
- Today is day 40 (10 days after Alice locked tokens), and Bob a malicious user  notices that if he adds to Alice locked position, he will extend her `unlockTime` by 10 days as seen on `L382` to `L384` (this is because `block.timestamp` will always be fresh so now `unlockTime` = day 40 + 50 days = day 90


- Bob call `lockOnBehalf(...)` for Alice with 1 wei of token A,
- Alice who locked at t = day 30 could originally unlock on t = day 80 will not be able to unlock until t = day 90
- Bob can continue locking on behalf of Alice with dust amount every 10 days to extend Alice unlock time indefinitely
- He can increase the interval to make the attack cheaper

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Increase the amount of _tokenContract that can be locked on behalf of a player. To make the attack path expensive or better still avoid locking on behalf of anyone.
