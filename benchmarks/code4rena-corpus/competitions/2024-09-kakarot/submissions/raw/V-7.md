## Impact
parse_access_list and parse_storage_keys functions use a recursive calls to traverse through the access list and storage keys. The recursion depth depends on the length of the access array list and the number of storage keys inputted in the functions being called.

A hacker can manipulate this by creating a transaction that is moderately big but affordable using the access list or pasting addresses with a big amount of storage keys. This pushes the traversing though the array list to hit big depths. This will eat up gas and reach 8M gas. Leading to a denial of service.
