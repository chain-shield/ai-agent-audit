# Vulnerability details

## Summary
The `DualVmToken.sol` contract has issues with how Ethereum and StarkNet addresses are handled in functions like `approve` and `transfer`. Specifically, when an Ethereum address is used as `msg.sender`, it may be converted into a StarkNet address that is greater than or equal to `STARKNET_FIELD_PRIME`. Similarly, StarkNet addresses might also exceed this limit. The `_approve` and `_transfer` functions do not currently handle these conversions or validate if the converted or original StarkNet address is within the allowed range. This causes problems with querying `balanceOf` and `allowance`, and prevents authorized spenders from using the `transferFrom` function to transfer tokens on behalf of the token holder. The recommended fix is to ensure proper address conversion and validation in the relevant functions.

## Proof of Concept
In the `DualVmToken.sol` contract, the `balance`, `allowance`, and `transferFrom` functions convert Ethereum addresses into their corresponding StarkNet addresses. Then, the `_balanceOf`, `_allowance`, and `_transferFrom` functions check all parameters to ensure that the StarkNet addresses are not >= `STARKNET_FIELD_PRIME`.

The `approve` function in the `DualVmToken.sol` contract allows a token holder (`msg.sender`) to authorize another address (either an Ethereum or a StarkNet address) to use a certain amount of tokens. However, the issue is that `msg.sender` could be an Ethereum address, and when this address is converted into a StarkNet address, it might be >= `STARKNET_FIELD_PRIME`. Additionally, `msg.sender` could already be a StarkNet address, which may also be >= `STARKNET_FIELD_PRIME`.

This leads to the following issues:
- The `balanceOf` and `allowance` functions cannot be used to query `msg.sender`'s balance or approved amounts.
- More critically, after `msg.sender` (an Ethereum or StarkNet address) uses the `approve` function to authorize a `spender`  to use a certain amount of tokens, the authorized `spender` cannot use the `transferFrom` function to transfer tokens on behalf of the token holder `msg.sender`.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
In both the `approve` and `transfer` functions, Ethereum addresses of `msg.sender` should be converted into the corresponding StarkNet addresses. Additionally, the `_approve` and `transfer` functions should check if `msg.sender`, after conversion or already being a StarkNet address, is greater than or equal to `STARKNET_FIELD_PRIME`.
