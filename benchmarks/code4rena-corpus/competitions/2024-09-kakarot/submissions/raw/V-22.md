Type: Underconstrained Computation
Location: Account.compute_starknet_address, Lines 519-521, 122, 574.

The compute_starknet_address function computes a Starknet address from an evm_address without validating or constraining the input. Since evm_address is a felt, it can be any integer modulo the prime field size, including zero(0) and excessively large values. An attacker could supply malicious zero(0) or large evm_address values to manipulate the computed Starknet address, potentially causing collisions or unauthorized access to accounts.
