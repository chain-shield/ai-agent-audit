## Impact
The return value of the IERC20.transferFrom call is not checked. If the call fails, the function proceeds without handling the failure, which can lead to inconsistent state and potential security issues. Failing to check the return value of critical external calls can result in unintended behavior. If transferFrom fails and the failure is not detected, the contract may assume that the transfer was successful, leading to discrepancies in balances and potential double-spending attacks.
