## Proof of Concept
The vulnerability related to Reentrancy Attacks typically arises when a function can be recursively called before the initial execution completes, often leading to inconsistent state or unexpected behaviors.The recursive function parse_access_list() could potentially be exploited to perform a denial-of-service (DoS) attack. If an attacker creates a large access list or carefully crafted transaction, it might exhaust computational resources.
