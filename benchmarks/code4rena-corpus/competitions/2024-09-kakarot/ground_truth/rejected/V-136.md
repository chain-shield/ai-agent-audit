## Brief Summary

The `execute_from_outside` function in `account_contract.cairo` processes user transactions within the EVM. However, it lacks validation for `data_offset` in the `call_array` and `tx_data_len` in the `packed_tx_data`, leading to potential out-of-bounds access in calldata. This vulnerability allows a malicious prover to manipulate `packed_tx_data`, which could result in unintended transaction execution. The impact is critical, as it enables arbitrary transaction manipulation in the EVM.
