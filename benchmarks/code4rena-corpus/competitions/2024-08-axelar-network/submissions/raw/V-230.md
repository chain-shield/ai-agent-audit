# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Some tokens (e.g., cUSDCv3) contain a special case for amount == type(uint256).max in their transfer functions that results in only the user's balance being transferred.
While handling those tokens, the protocol will lost tokens.

## Proof of Concept
I will take [InterchainTokenService.interchainTransfer](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-axelar-network/blob/4572617124bed39add9025317d2c326acfef29f1/interchain-token-service/contracts/InterchainTokenService.sol#L454-L478) as an example. 
In `InterchainTokenService.interchainTransfer`, `InterchainTokenService._takeToken` is called.
And in [InterchainTokenService._takeToken](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-axelar-network/blob/4572617124bed39add9025317d2c326acfef29f1/interchain-token-service/contracts/InterchainTokenService.sol#L1189-L1197), `TokenHandler.takeToken`'s implementation is used.

[TokenHandler.takeToken](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-axelar-network/blob/4572617124bed39add9025317d2c326acfef29f1/interchain-token-service/contracts/TokenHandler.sol#L91-L123) is defined as:

While [cUSDCv3](https://etherscan.io/address/0x9e4dde024f7ea5cf13d5f09c51a1555305c99f0c#code) is used, `amount == type(uint256).max`, [only the user's balance will be transferred](https://etherscan.io/address/0x9e4dde024f7ea5cf13d5f09c51a1555305c99f0c#code#F1#L930)

So if Alice calls `InterchainTokenService.interchainTransfer` with `type(uint256).max` as `amount`, on the source chain, the `InterchainTokenService.interchainTransfer` will transfer Alice's cUSDCv3 to the system by `_takeToken`, suppose Alice's cUSDCv3 balance is 1 wei, so 1 wei cUSDCv3 will be transferred to the system, but **type(uint256).max** will be encoded into the message.
And on the dest chain, `InterchainTokenService._giveToken` in [InterchainTokenService.sol#L750](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-axelar-network/blob/4572617124bed39add9025317d2c326acfef29f1/interchain-token-service/contracts/InterchainTokenService.sol#L750) will be called to transfer cUSDCv3 back to the user.
Because `amount` here is **type(uint256).max**, while calling [TokenHandler.giveToken](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-axelar-network/blob/4572617124bed39add9025317d2c326acfef29f1/interchain-token-service/contracts/TokenHandler.sol#L45-L79), all the system's balance of cUSDCv3 will be transferred to the user.
