# Vulnerability details

## Vulnerability details
It is stated on the competition's page that rebasing/deflationary/inflationary tokens should be supported and are [In scope](https://code4rena.com/audits/2024-08-axelar-network#top:~:text=Balance%20changes%20outside,In%20scope). However, the transfer of such tokens through ITS will be dangerous and will cause errors in the protocols accounting balance, causing users to get more funds than intended or some users being unable to claim their transferred tokens of the destination chain.

## Impact
Loss of funds for users of the protocol and accounting errors in the ITS.

## Proof of Concept
If rebasing tokens are transferred through the ITS the following issue is likely to arise:
1/ User transfers 1000 of a rebasing token from chain A to chain B, so 1000 tokens are locked in the `TokenManager` for the token on chain A
2/ Before the transfer is executed on chain B the token rebases, decreasing balances by 10%
3/ The user finishes the bridging on chain B and receives 1000 tokens, even though they should have only received 900
4/ The 100 tokens that are added to the user are taken out of other users' locked tokens, causing further accounting issues

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
One long-term solution would be to implement a special `TokenManager` contract designed for handling rebasing tokens.
