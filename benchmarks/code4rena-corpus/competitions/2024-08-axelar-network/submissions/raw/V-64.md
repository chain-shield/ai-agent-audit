# Vulnerability details

## Vulnerability details
Currently, in order for tokens to be transferred from one chain to another they first need to be locked/burnt on the source chain and then unlocked/minted on the destination chain. The issue is that there are several cases where the tokens will not be able to be recovered on the destination chain, causing a loss of funds for users.

## Impact
Due to the lack of a recovery function for stuck funds, users may suffer a loss of their bridged funds.

## Proof of Concept
Here are the possible scenarios where recovering tokens on the destination chain will be impossible:

1/ If the `flowLimit` becomes less than the transferred funds, before the token bridge has been executed on the destination chain, the initiator of the token transfer will not be able to recover their funds on the destination chain. The only way for this to happen would be if the `flowLimit` is increased to a value higher than the amount of tokens. This may not occur, as it will be fully dependent on the token manager operator, which may be malicious.

2/ If a `TokenManager`, corresponding to the source `tokenId`, has not been deployed on the destination chain it will be impossible for the tokens to be transferred to the destination address, as `TokenHandler.giveToken` will fail.

3/ If a destination chain changes or entirely removes another chain's trusted address, all pending transfers coming from that chain will not be executable. This will prevent users who have transferred tokens from the updated/removed chain and are yet to claim them, from ever retrieving their funds. 
This becomes much more dangerous as with the addition of the ITS Hub, many chains will change their `InterchainAddressTracker` configurations.

4/ There may not be enough locked tokens on the destination chain to transfer to the destination address. This will prevent the user from receiving their bridged tokens until more tokens are locked on the destination chain, which may never happen.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Add a recovery mechanism for stuck tokens on the source chain.
