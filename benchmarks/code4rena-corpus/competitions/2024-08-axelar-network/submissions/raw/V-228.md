# Vulnerability details

## Impact 
The `abi_encode` method in the `ItsMessage` enum of the `interchain-token-service` package's `abi.rs` contract converts its three structs into specific formats for ABI encoding. However, the `amount` field in the `InterchainTransfer` struct, used for cross-chain transfers, isn't converted to a solidity `uint256` type before encoding, causing type mismatches and transfer issues.

## Proof of Concept
In the `interchain-token-service` package, the `ItsMessage` enum within the `abi.rs` contract includes an `abi_encode` method that handles the conversion of different struct variants, such as `InterchainTransfer`, into specific formats for ABI encoding. The `InterchainTransfer` struct is primarily used for handling cross-chain token transfers, where the `amount` field represents the transfer amount. Currently, the `amount` is not converted to a solidity `uint256` type before encoding, which can lead to type mismatches and ultimately cause cross-chain transfer failures.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
To ensure compatibility and successful cross-chain transfers, it is recommended to convert the `amount` field in the `InterchainTransfer` struct to a `uint256` type before performing ABI encoding, e.g., `amount: U256::from_le_bytes(amount.to_le_bytes()).into()`. This will prevent type mismatches and ensure that the transfer amount is properly handled during cross-chain operations.
