# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Providing the v,r and s params in `ERC20Permit::permit()` makes it possible to re-use the signature as they are visible in the mempool. Within a small range of time someone can re-use the signature to permit more ERC20 tokens.

## Proof of Concept
Here is an example of the exploit although it is well known issue with ERC20s:
Joe permits some X value to Susan.
Susan obtains the signatures and uses them to permit more.
Joe gains more tokens and Susan re-permits again with the same parameters they provided in the original permit in step 1.

## Tools Used
Manual review.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Most contracts utilize nonces when generating signatures to ensure that the contract hasn't been used before. When a nonce is injected into a signature, it makes it impossible for re-use, assuming of course the nonce feature is done correctly.
