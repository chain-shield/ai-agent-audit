# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The `abi_decode` method in the `interchain-token-service` package incorrectly converts the `token_manager_type` field in the `DeployTokenManager` struct to a `u8` type instead of a `uint256`, causing decoding issues and preventing smart contracts from properly accessing the `token_manager_type`.

## Proof of Concept
In the `interchain-token-service` package, the `abi_decode` method is responsible for decoding the struct types of `ItsMessage` enum, including the `DeployTokenManager` struct. However, during the decoding process, the `token_manager_type` field is being converted to a `u8` type instead of the expected `uint256` format. This mismatch in data type conversion leads to incorrect decoding results, making it impossible for the smart contract to correctly interpret the `token_manager_type`.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
To fix this issue, the `abi_decode` method should be modified to convert the `token_manager_type` field to the `uint256` format instead of `u8`, e.g., adding `token_manager_type: Uint256::from_le_bytes(decoded.tokenManagerType.to_le_bytes())` after the `u8` of `token_manager_type`. This adjustment will ensure accurate decoding and allow smart contracts to properly retrieve the `token_manager_type`, preventing potential errors and ensuring seamless operation.
