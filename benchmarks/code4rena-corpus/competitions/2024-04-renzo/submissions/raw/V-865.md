# Vulnerability details

## Impact
When the protocol issues allocation basis point to different operator delegators, there is a lack of check that the total allocated basis point for all operator delegators involved is not > 10000/100%. This lack of verification will allow the same piece of the Total TVL to be allocated to the different Operator Delegators among other impacts like 

- Process of Distribution of the total TVL will be impended, as some Operator Delegators, will have their share of the total TVL allocated to another Operator Delegator.
- Incorrect TVL Threshold Calculation
- An Operator Delegator that should not be Considered as below the threshold will be incorrectly assumed to be below the threshold.

## Proof of Concept
The issue here is fairly simple to decipher, in a normal calculations, where tokens or deposits are sent to contract and the amount of tokens that each contract will receive is based upon basis points/% the total allocated should not be greater than 10000bps/100%, in any case should this happen then the amount of tokens that will be shared will not be enough for all receiving contract, But in the context of the `RestakeManager` of the Renzo protocol, the impact is more than that as it affects the manner in which Operator Delegators are chosen for deposits and withdrawals.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
A Check should be implemented in the `addOperatorDelegator` and the `setOperatorDelegatorAllocation` to make sure that the total allocations is not more than the maximum which is 10000/100%, this kind of safety check will ensure that the TVL can service all Operator Delegators at any given time based on the allocation bps, and Operator Delegators below threshold are correctly identified and Provided tokens for staking
