# Vulnerability details

## Impact
dilution of rewards from genuine stakers that pay time and risk with their ETH to front runners stakers, giving much lower yield to genuine stakers and risk free rewards to front running stakers

## Proof of Concept
when the user deposit to the protocol the [`calculateTVL()` function](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/1c7cc4e632564349b204b4b5e5f494c9b0bc631d/contracts/RestakeManager.sol#L274) doesn't count for rewards that are outside the protocol, those rewards is coming to the protocol manually or with script assuming if it reaches a specific amount it get sent to the protocol.
now the edge case is this:
1. users stake for 2 weeks
2. a reward is getting added to the protocol,
3. a memepool watcher front run the reward txn
4. submits a withdrawal request just after the reward txn if the withdraw buffer is suffecient and `maxTVL` is not reached there is nothing preventing his request
5. the attacker now basically got a risk free reward affecting other users
now, yes he will have to wait for the withdrawal `WithdrawQueue::coolDownPeriod` 
but if not profitable this can be used to just harm genuine stakers or decrease their yield(i.e competitor) and nothing prevents him from doing it again and again

## Tools Used
manual review
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
implement an off-chain abi calls like how chainlink price feeds works to keep track of every wei of reward before the rewards enter the system
then implement this oracle in [`calculateTVL()` function](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/1c7cc4e632564349b204b4b5e5f494c9b0bc631d/contracts/RestakeManager.sol#L274)
