# Vulnerability details



## Impact
Changing a token's strategy via [OperatorDelegator::setTokenStrategy](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Delegation/OperatorDelegator.sol#L106-L114) function fails to consider the already deposited LRTs into the previous strategy. This will deplete the total value locked and thus increase how much new stakers get minted for their ezETH deposits, allowing them to instantly make a profit for their deposit, therefore stealing from previous stakers on withdrawal.

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Oracle/RenzoOracle.sol#L123-L149
