# Vulnerability details

Due to the rebasing mechanism in stETH, stETH transfers will frequently result in [1-2 wei less](https://docs.lido.fi/guides/lido-tokens-integration-guide/#1-2-wei-corner-case) than the expected transfer amount. 

In Renzo, depositing into the Restake Manager leads to a string of token transfers.

```
caller ---> RestakeManager ---> OperatorDelegator ---> Strategy
```

Every transfer will result in 1-2 wei less stETH for the recipient. However, each transfer expects to transfer the same amount. If one of the contracts (RestakeManager or OperatorDelegator) do not have extra stETH, the deposit will revert. This is the most likely outcome since RestakeManager and OperatorDelegator are not meant to hold token balances beyond the length of a function executing. Also, the Operator Delegator would [deposit its token balances](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/main/contracts/Delegation/OperatorDelegator.sol#L307-L309) back to Eigenlayer every time it completes a queued withdrawal, emptying its balance of stETH.


## Impact
This issue prevents users from depositing stETH into Renzo since it will frequently fail.

## Proof of Concept
Below are the relevant steps in a transaction that reproduces the issue:
1. Alice deposits 1000 stETH into Restake Manager. The 1000 stETH are [transferred](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/main/contracts/RestakeManager.sol#L540) from Alice to Restake Manager. The Restake Manager only receives 998 stETH.
2. The Restake Manager then [deposits](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/main/contracts/RestakeManager.sol#L562) 1000 stETH into the Operator Delegator. The Delegator [transfers](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/main/contracts/Delegation/OperatorDelegator.sol#L151) the 1000 stETH from the Restake Manager to itself. The transaction could revert here if the Restake Manager did not have 1-2 extra wei on top of the 998 stETH received from Alice. The Operator Delegator receives only 998 stETH.
3. The Operator Delegator [deposits](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/main/contracts/Delegation/OperatorDelegator.sol#L168) the 1000 stETH into the Eigenlayer Strategy Manager. The Strategy Manager then [transfers](https://github.com/Layr-Labs/eigenlayer-contracts/blob/dev/src/contracts/core/StrategyManager.sol#L330) the 1000 stETH from the Delegator to itself. The transaction reverts here since the Operator Delegator only has 998 stETH because its balance of stETH was recently emptied with a [`completeQueuedWithdrawal()`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/main/contracts/Delegation/OperatorDelegator.sol#L306-L309). 

## Tools Used
Manual Review, Foundry

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Consider calculating for the deposit amount by subtracting the token's post-balance with the token's pre-balance on every transfer. This should be the deposit amount used in RestakeManager's deposit() and OperatorDelegator's deposit().
