# Vulnerability details

## Impact

Initial user deposits are stolen by attackers who inflate share price.

## Proof of Concept

Depositing to `RestakeManager`, in any function, does not have any slippage checks. Thus, an attacker may deposit 1 asset, getting 1 `ezETH`, donate assets to one of the contracts that tracks TVL (such has the `WithdrawQueue`), and wait for some user to deposit a quantity of tokens smaller than the donation, rounding down and receiving less `ezETH` than supposed. The rounding is claimed by the attacker, who can then withdraw.

## Tools Used

Vscode

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Do an initial deposit of `ezETH` to cover this edge case.
