# Vulnerability details

## Impact
temporary freeze of deposits and withdrawals and complete Denial Of Service.

medium due to low likelihood 

## Proof of Concept
Call to `latestRoundData` could potentially revert and make it impossible to query any prices which the whole protocol's `minting`, `burning`, `depositing` and `withdrawing` depends on. the assumption of trusted 3rd party may lead to un wanted harm.

Chainlink's multisigs can immediately block access to price feeds at will. Therefore, to prevent denial of service scenarios, it is recommended to query Chainlink price feeds using a defensive approach with Solidity’s try/catch structure. In this way, if the call to the price feed fails, the caller contract is still in control and can handle any errors safely and explicitly with examples like 2ry emergency oracle or returning last returned price that lies with in a limited area of price of deviation.

## Tools Used
manual review
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Surround the call to `latestRoundData()` with `try/catch` instead of calling it directly. In a scenario where the call reverts, the catch block can be used to call a fallback oracle or handle the error in any other suitable way like 2ry emergency oracle or returning last returned price that lies with in a limited area of price of deviation.
