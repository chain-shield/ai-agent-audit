# Vulnerability details

## Impact

Malicious users profit from frontrunning and the shares/tvl ratio will be incorrect.

## Proof of Concept

`WithdrawQueue::withdraw()` pulls the ezETH from the withdrawer and calculates the corresponding tvl amount. Then, in `WithdrawQueue::claim()`, the shares are burned and the tokens are sent to the user. This means that a user may see a slashing event, frontrun it and withdraw, not taking the loss. Additionally, the ratio will be modified when the user claims the tokens, as it will burn the ezETH tokens and send the assets as calculated previously, not the current ratio, for the other users loss.

## Tools Used

Vscode

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Calculate the ratio at the time of `claim()` and send the lowest amount of assets to the user. This is how LIDO handles it in the withdrawal queue, for example.
