# Vulnerability details

## Impact

Protocol becomes insolvent as it becomes impossible to finalize withdrawals. It may be possible to finalize withdrawals if the withdrawal buffer is increased.

## Proof of Concept

`OperatorDelegator::completeQueuedWithdrawal()` finalizes withdrawals for strategy tokens and `ETH`. If the token balance of a given strategy exceeds the withdrawal buffer, it deposits to a strategy; however, the protocol may have reached the maximum strategies allowed in EigenLayer in the meantime. [Here](https://github.com/Layr-Labs/eigenlayer-contracts/blob/dev/src/contracts/core/StrategyManager.sol#L300-L306) is the check in EigenLayer for the maximum strategies. The flow that would cause this is:
1. Strategies are added until reaching 32, the limit.
2. All the funds are withdrawn from a strategy, [deleting](https://github.com/Layr-Labs/eigenlayer-contracts/blob/dev/src/contracts/core/StrategyManager.sol#L373-L378) it from the strategies on `OperatorDelegator::queueWithdrawals()`.
3. At the moment there are only 31 strategies from the `OperatorManager`, but some user deposits on another strategy, increasing the value to 32.
4. `OperatorDelegator::completeQueuedWithdrawal()` is called, the token balance is greater than the buffer and it deposits to the strategy. it will revert as it will try to add strategy 33 and revert. Withdrawals of all the tokens and ETH in the batch will be on hold due to this.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Check if the maximum strategy limit is reached before depositing.
