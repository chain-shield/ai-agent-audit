# Vulnerability details

## Vulnerability Detail
Users on an L2 can interact with the renzo protocol on mainnet by calling `xRenzoDeposit::depositETH()`

This will end up minting the user xezETH on the L2 and converting the deposited ETH to nextWETH.

Then later an admin (allowedBridgeSweepers) will call `xRenzoDeposit::sweep()`, this function will take the balance of nextWETH in the contract and bridge it down to the L1, The L1 contract will unwrap, deposit in Renzo, and lock up the ezETH in the lockbox on L1.

The problem is that inbetween the user's call to `depositETH()` and the admin's call to `sweep()`, the price of ezETH can change, this price change will result in a different amount of ezETH being minted to back the xezETH on the L2. 

Having different amounts of xezETH compared to the ezETH backing it is dangerous as the lockbox always converts xezETH to ezETH in a 1:1 ratio.

The following are the worst case scenarios, they ignore fees for simplicity sake. 

**Scenario 1:** Price of ezETH goes up in between the calls.
Initially 1 ezETH = 1ETH

1. Alice calls `xRenzoDeposit::depositETH()` depositing 5 ETH and is minted 5 xezETH
2. Bob calls `xRenzoDeposit::depositETH()` depositing 5 ETH and is minted 5 xezETH
3. The price of ezETH grows to 1 ezETH = 2 ETH
4. Admin calls `sweep()`, this will result in `xRenzoBridge` depositing the 10 ETH into the protocol and minting 5 ezETH, the 5 ezETH will be deposited into the xezETHLockbox
5. Now Alice will be able to burn her 5 xezETH for 5 ezETH because the lockbox always converts xezETH to ezETH in a 1:1 ratio
6. Now Bob is stuck with a useless xezETH and will not be able to redeem it for anything

**Scenario 2:** Price of ezETH goes down in between the calls.
Initially 1 ezETH = 2ETH

1. Alice calls `xRenzoDeposit::depositETH()` depositing 10 ETH and is minted 5 xezETH
2. The price of ezETH drops to 1 ezETH = 1 ETH
3. Admin calls `sweep()`, this will result in `xRenzoBridge` depositing the 10 ETH into the protocol and minting 10 ezETH, the 10 ezETH will be deposited into the xezETHLockbox
4. Now Alice will be able to burn her 5 xezETH for 5 ezETH 
5. Now 5 ezETH is stuck in the lockbox and cannot be redeemed 

**Scenario 3:** Price of ezETH goes up in between the calls.
Initially 1 ezETH = 1ETH

1. Alice calls `xRenzoDeposit::depositETH()` depositing 5 ETH and is minted 5 xezETH
2. The price of ezETH grows to 1 ezETH = 2 ETH
3. Admin calls `sweep()`, this will result in `xRenzoBridge` depositing the 5 ETH into the protocol and minting 2.5 ezETH, the 2.5 ezETH will be deposited into the xezETHLockbox
4. Now Alice will only be able to burn 2.5 of her 5 xezETH for 2.5 ezETH
5. The only way she can redeem the remaining 2.5 xezETH is if she waits for another user to deposit from an L2 then steal their ezETH in the lockbox, effectively passing her problem to them.

These scenarios do involve significant price changes BUT even smaller changes in price will result in having different amounts of xezETH compared to the ezETH backing it and will cause significant loss of funds.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
will require a redesign of the current L2 deposit and sweep system. 

Is it necessary for an admin to call `sweep()`, one solution would be to implement the `sweep()` function directly after the logic for `deposit`.
