# Vulnerability details

## Impact
`RestakeManager#calculateTVLs` is used to retrieve total value of assets in the system, denominated in ETH. However, its logic does not include RewardHandler's balance, making the ezETH price smaller than it should be (until rewards are forwarded), and enabling sandwich attacks.

## Proof of Concept

1. TVL is 100 ETH;
2. RewardHandler's balance just got updated from 0 to 9 ETH due to execution layer rewards;
3. Bob deposits 1e18 ETH into Renzo and receives 1e18 ezETH;
4. Bob sends 1 wei to RewardHandler, which sends RH balance (9 ETH) to DepositQueue; TVL becomes 110 ETH; ezETH price becomes 1.1 ETH.
5. Bob requests redemption of 1e18 ezETH;
6. After the cooldown period, Bob claims 1.1 ETH, making 0.1 ETH in profit.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Add RewardHandler's ETH balance to `totalTVL` in `RestakeManager#calculateTVLs`.
