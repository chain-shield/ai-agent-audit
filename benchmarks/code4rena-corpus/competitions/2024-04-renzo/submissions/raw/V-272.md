# Vulnerability details

## Impact
https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Bridge/L2/xRenzoDeposit.sol#L168C4-L191C6

When using the `deposit` function, the user will lose their funds without receiving xzETH in return.

## Proof of concept
After a deposit is made and wrapped to ETH, there is no minting of xzETH to the user. Minting was supposed to occur, but it didn't. If a user deposits their last ETH and WETH hopping they will mint something out, they will lose everything.

[DepositETH](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Bridge/L2/xRenzoDeposit.sol#L168) was meant to act like [Deposit](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Bridge/L2/xRenzoDeposit.sol#L204)function since it include all necessary logic.

## Tools Used
Manual review, NextWindhusler.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Include a minting mechanism after the ETH of a user has been wrapped, and let the user get xzETH back as collateral.
