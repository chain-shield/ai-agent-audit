# Vulnerability details

## Impact
`RestakeManager#chooseOperatorDelegatorForDeposit` does the following:

1. iterates through OperatorDelegator's balances until there's an OD with value smaller than `TVL / 10000` 

2. If there isnt such OD, choose the first one

This approach heavily favors the first OD in the list, bringing them as much as `100% - 0.01% * (number of ODs - 1)` of deposits

## Proof of Concept

1. Let's assume there's 10 OD's in the system, and each deposit is 32 ETH.
2. First deposit of 32 ETH is sent to the first OD, second to the second OD, and so on
3. 11th deposit is sent to the first OD again
4. 12th deposit is sent to the first OD again
and so on, until there's 92 x 32 ETH deposits in the first OD

After that, OD's 2-10 will receive 1x32 ETH deposit each, after which OD#1 will be receiving next 91 deposits again.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Deposits should be allocated to the OD with the lowest TVL.
