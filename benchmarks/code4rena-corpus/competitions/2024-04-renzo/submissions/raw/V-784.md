# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Users `withdrawing` after a slashing event may receive less than their entitled amount, bearing the cost of the slashing penalty, which should be distributed across all token holders.

## Proof of Concept

User initiates a []withdrawal via [withdraw()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/1c7cc4e632564349b204b4b5e5f494c9b0bc631d/contracts/Withdraw/WithdrawQueue.sol#L206) function.

Slashing event occurs, reducing TVL.
[restakeManager.calculateTVLs()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/1c7cc4e632564349b204b4b5e5f494c9b0bc631d/contracts/Withdraw/WithdrawQueue.sol#L217) returns a reduced TVL.

User's redeemable amount is calculated based on the reduced TVL.
User receives less upon claiming, effectively paying the slashing penalty.

Example:

    Deposit such that we have 2 validators.

    Bob requests to withdraw 8 ezETH, expecting to receive an equivalent amount in ETH.

    Before Bob's withdrawal is processed, a slashing event occurs, reducing the total value locked (TVL) and creating an 8 ETH deficit.

    Bob's withdrawal is processed, but due to the deficit, the amount of ETH he receives is less than expected. If the deficit covers his entire withdrawal, he could receive 0 ETH.

    Carol requests to withdraw 31.99 ezETH after the slashing event.

    When Carol's withdrawal is processed, the deficit has already been covered by Bob's withdrawal, so she receives the full 31.99 ETH equivalent.

    As a result, Bob has absorbed the entire slashing penalty, while Carol's withdrawal is unaffected, leading to an inequitable outcome.

    Effectively, Carol's withdrawal has been subsidized by the slashing penalty that was fully absorbed by Bob, leading to an unfair distribution of losses.

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Create a penalty distribution system to spread slashing costs proportionally among all token holders.
