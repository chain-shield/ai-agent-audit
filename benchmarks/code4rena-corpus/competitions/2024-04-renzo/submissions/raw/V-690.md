# Vulnerability details

The function [RenzoOracle::calculateMintAmount()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/main/contracts/Oracle/RenzoOracle.sol#L123) is used by the protocol to calculate the amount of `ezETH` to mint based on the TVL and the amount of assets deposited, but doesn't implement a virtual shares system to prevent inflation attacks.

If the protocol TVL or the amount of `ezETH` in circulation is currently `0` an attacker can monitor the mempool for a deposit and frontrun it for profit.

## Impact

An attacker can frontrun a deposit for profit when the protocol TVL or the amount of `ezETH` is `0`.

## Proof of Concept

Let's assume Alice is performing the first the deposit of `1 ETH`, an attacker monitoring the mempool notices this and he:

1. Deposits `1 wei` via [RestakeManager::depositETH()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/main/contracts/RestakeManager.sol#L592), which will mint `1 wei` of `ezETH`.
2. Send `0.5 ETH` directly to the protocol, as a donation. This will increase the TVL and `1 wei` of `ezETH` will now be worth `0.5 ETH + 1 wei`.
3. The deposit that got frontrun goes through, Alice is supposed to receive `1.99 wei` of `ezETH` but due to precision loss she will only receive `1 wei` of `ezETH`.

The current state of the protocol is:

- TVL: `1 ETH` + `0.5ETH` + `1 wei` = `1.5ETH` + `1 wei`
- `ezETH` in circulation: `2 wei`

This implies `1 wei` of `ezETH` is valued at ~`1.5ETH / 2` = `0.75ETH`. The attacker can now withdraw his `1 wei` of `ezETH` for a profit of ~`0.25ETH` (`0.75ETH` - `0.5ETH`).

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Implement a virtual shares system, like [openzeppelin does for ERC4626 vaults](https://docs.openzeppelin.com/contracts/4.x/erc4626#defending_with_a_virtual_offset).
