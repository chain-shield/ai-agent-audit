# Vulnerability details

## Impact

Protocol becomes insolvent as it becomes impossible to finalize withdrawals. It may be possible to finalize withdrawals if the withdrawal buffer is increased.

## Proof of Concept

`OperatorDelegator::completeQueuedWithdrawal()` finalizes withdrawals for strategy tokens and `ETH`. If the token balance of a given strategy exceeds the withdrawal buffer, it deposits to a strategy; however, the strategy may have been dewhitelisted to deposit in EigenLayer, in which case the transaction will revert and funds will not be finalized. [Here](https://github.com/Layr-Labs/eigenlayer-contracts/blob/dev/src/contracts/core/StrategyManager.sol#L328) is the whitelist modifier in EigenLayer.

## Tools Used

Vscode

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Before depositing, check if the strategy is whitelisted for depositing. Withdrawals still work as the whitelist is only for depositing.
