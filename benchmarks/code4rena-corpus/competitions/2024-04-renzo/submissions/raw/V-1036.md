# Vulnerability details

## Impact
choosing an `OpereatorDelegator` that has exceeded its maxTVL can lead to loss of funds

## Proof of Concept
When `RestakeManager::deposit(...)` is called, an `OperatorDelegator` (OD) is chosen based on whether or not they have exceeded their max TVL allocation by calling `RestakeManager::chooseOperatorDelegatorForDeposit(...)` however due to a logical flaw in the implementation, an OD that has exceeded its max TVL can be chosen. for deposit.

The downside to this is that the chosen `OperatorDelegator` may have exceeded it `maxTVL` and shares may not be assigned by EigenLayer Strategy as most strategy implementations assign shares based on whether or not the OD has reached its max TVL and as such user will make deposit that becomes dormant in the protocol diluting the rewards of the protocol.
