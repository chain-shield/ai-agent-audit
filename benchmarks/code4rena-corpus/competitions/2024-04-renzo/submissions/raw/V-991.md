# Vulnerability details

## Impact

Withdrawals will permanently fail as the `nonReentrant` modifier is executed twice while still entered, leading to insolvency.

## Proof of Concept

`OperatorDelegator::completeQueuedWithdrawal()` [has](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/main/contracts/Delegation/OperatorDelegator.sol#L269) the `nonReentrant` modifier. When calling `delegationManager.completeQueuedWithdrawal(withdrawal, tokens, middlewareTimesIndex, true);`, the ETH withdrawn will be sent to the `receive` function of the `OperatorDelegator, as seen [here](https://github.com/Layr-Labs/eigenlayer-contracts/blob/dev/src/contracts/pods/EigenPod.sol#L431). This means all queued withdrawals will never be completed and the protocol becomes insolvent.

## Tools Used

Vscode

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

The `nonReentrant` modifier should allow receiving funds in `receive` if the caller is the `EigenPod`.
