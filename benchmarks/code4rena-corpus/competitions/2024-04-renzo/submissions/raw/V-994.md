# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Withdrawals are never finalized and the protocol becomes insolvent.

## Proof of Concept

`OperatorDelegator::completeQueuedWithdrawal()` sends the funds to be withdrawn to the `DepositQueue`, which forwards it to the `WithdrawQueue`. However, `DepositQueue::fillERC20withdrawBuffer()` has the `onlyRestakeManager` modifier, only allowing it to be called by the `RestakeManager`. Due to this, withdrawals will always fail and the protocol becomes insolvent.

## Tools Used

Vscode

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Modify the modifier to allow being called by the `OperatorDelegator`.
