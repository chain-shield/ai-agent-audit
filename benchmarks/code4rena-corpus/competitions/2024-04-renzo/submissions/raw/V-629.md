# Vulnerability details

## Impact

Attackers can interrupt any withdrawing from `WithdrawQueue` contract, by front-running.

## Proof of Concept

Let's consider following scenario:
1. Bob, the malicious user, fully fills `stETH` withdraw buffer of `WithdrawQueue` contract.
2. Everytime anybody tries to withdraw their `stETH` by calling `WithdrawQueue.withdraw()` , Bob withdraws all buffer by front-running. Then, the available amount to withdraw becomes 0, leading to the reversal of former's transaction.
3. And after that, if someone tries to deposit `stETH`, Bob fully fills `stETH` withdraw buffer again by front-running. Then, Bob is able to withdraw all that buffer anytime, facilitates the interrupting any following withdrawings.

## Tools Used

Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

There should be a cooldown mechanism between depositting and withdrawing.
