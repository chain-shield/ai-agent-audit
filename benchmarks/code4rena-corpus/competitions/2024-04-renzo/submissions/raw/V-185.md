# Vulnerability details

## Impact
If the protocol receives any ETH, from Eigen Layer maybe as reward it is sent for fullWithdrawl via the DepositQueue contract after filling the withdraw buffer.

The withdrawQueue contract get filled by 3 ways ->
New Deposits
Daily Rewards Coming in the Protocol.
Manual withdrawal from EigenLayer. Permissioned call from OperatorDelegator.

So for rewards coming in to the protcol users can claim those by 
calling the withdraw -> claim

An attacker can take advantage of this to steal a part of the rewards:

Mint a sensible amount of LRTTokens by depositing an accepted asset
Call `WithdawlQueue.withdraw`, after which the value of the LRTTokens just minted will immediately increase.
then claim those by `claim`
