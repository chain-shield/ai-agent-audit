# Vulnerability details

## Vulnerability Details

The `bool success = payable(tx.origin).send(gasRefund);` function intends to transfer the refund amount to `tx.origin` (the sender of the transaction) but it is not equipped to handle changes in the underlying `.send()` due to its limitation of a fixed gas price of 2300. In situations where the Recipient's Fallback Function Consumes More Than 2300 Gas the function may revert and lead to denial of service.

## Impact
The use of `.send()` instead of `.call` may cause the function to revert, if the gas price is above 2300, because of the limitation with `.send()` which may lead to denial service.


## Proof of Concept

`bool success = payable(tx.origin).send(gasRefund);`

The `.send()` function intends to transfer an ETH amount with a fixed amount of 2300 gas. However, these function is not be equipped to handle changes in the underlying functions which may use higher amounts of gas in the future. If the recipient Fallback Function Consumes More Than 2300 Gas, `.send()` may revert. Hence, the vault and the contract owner will not be able to call certain sensitive functions.
