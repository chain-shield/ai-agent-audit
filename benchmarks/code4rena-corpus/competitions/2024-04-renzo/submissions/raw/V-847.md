# Vulnerability details

## Description
Chainlink recommends that all Optimistic L2 oracles consult the Sequencer Uptime Feed to ensure that the sequencer is live before trusting the data returned by the oracle. This check is not implemented in `RenzoOracleL2.getMinRate()`

When utilizing Chainlink in L2 chains like Arbitrum, it's important to ensure that the prices provided are not falsely perceived as fresh, even when the sequencer is down.


## Impact
Use of stale price in case of sequencer down in L2 chains like arbitrum

## POC
[Reference](https://medium.com/@lopotras/l2-sequencer-and-stale-oracle-prices-bug-54a749417277)

## Recommended mitigation
Query sequencer uptime feed before returning the price in `RenzoOracleL2.getMinRate()`

Oracle
