# Vulnerability details

## Impact
in RenzoOracleL2 , wrong check will suscept the contract to wrong calculations
## Proof of Concept
in the [`intialize` function](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/1c7cc4e632564349b204b4b5e5f494c9b0bc631d/contracts/Bridge/L2/Oracle/RenzoOracleL2.sol#L23-L33) it checks for decimals if its larger than 18
but if the decimals is lower than 18 it won't revert but will still miss the calculation

## Tools Used
manual review
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
correct the line to
```solidity
if (_oracle.decimals() != 18) revert InvalidTokenDecimals(18, _oracle.decimals());
```
