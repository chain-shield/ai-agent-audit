# Vulnerability details


## Impact
Renzo does support a blacklist token, e.g., wBETH. If a user is added to the blacklist during the cooldown period, they will not be able to claim wBETH, as it is transferred back to the same blacklisted address.

https://etherscan.io/token/0xa2E3356610840701BDf5611a53974510Ae27E2e1#writeProxyContract#F2

## Proof of Concept
Alice submit a withdraw request, _amount = 5 ETH, _assetOut = _wbethToken. Cooldown period ends, Alice call `withdrawQueue.claim()`, the token transfer to `msg.sender` failed due to address being blacklisted. Alice funds stuck into the contract.
