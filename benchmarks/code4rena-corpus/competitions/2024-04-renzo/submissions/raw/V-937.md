# Vulnerability details

## Impact
`totalWithdrawalQueueValue` calculation will be wrong due to wrong `Oracle` fetched for `collateralToken` in `renzoOracle.lookupTokenValue` cause there is a small bug present in `calculateTVLs()`

Impact will be high beacuse `totalWithdrawalQueueValue` direct contribute to `TVL` of protocol, as `totalWithdrawalQueueValue` is inaccurate so will be `TVL`

And TVL is a crucial part of protocol.

For more detailed bug explanation refer `POC` part
