# Vulnerability details

## Impact

The griefing attack delays the staking process, potentially causing the node to miss out on rewards or incur additional transaction costs. It also disrupts the smooth operation of the staking system.

## Proof of Concept

**1. lst Transfer to Node:**

- A certain amount of `lst` tokens is transferred to a specific node. This transfer could be part of a larger staking operation or a separate transaction.

```solidity
            strategyManager.depositIntoStrategy(tokenStrategyMapping[_token], _token, _tokenAmount);

```

**2. Expected EigenLayer Stake:**

- After receiving the `lst` tokens, the node is expected to stake them on EigenLayer. This staking process is crucial for participating in the consensus mechanism and earning rewards.

**3. Griefing Opportunity in `depositIntoStrategy`:**

- An adversary identifies an opportunity to grief the staking process by exploiting the `depositIntoStrategy` function. This function is likely responsible for handling the actual staking on EigenLayer.

**4. Dust Dropping and Front-Running:**

- The adversary drops a small amount of tokens (dust) into the pool associated with the `depositIntoStrategy` function. This dust deposit could be strategically timed to front-run a transaction submitted by the RestakeManager.

**5. Forced Asset Transfer:**

- The adversary's front-running action disrupts the staking process, forcing the node to transfer some assets back to the pool. This transfer is necessary to successfully stake on EigenLayer.



## Tools Used

Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

- Implement mechanisms to prevent dust deposits from affecting the staking process. This could involve setting minimum deposit thresholds or filtering out dust transactions.
