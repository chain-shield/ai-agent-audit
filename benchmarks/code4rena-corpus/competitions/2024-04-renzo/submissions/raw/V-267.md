# Vulnerability details

Using `.transfer` forwards 2300 gas which may not be enough if the recipient is a contract because the 2300 gas is only enough to emit an event. ` .transfer` forwards a fixed gas amount of 2300 with the assumption that it will prevent callbacks into the contract via reentrance to mitigate the reentrancy vulnerabilities. This is false as gas price aren't fixed and are subject to change.

## Impact
`recoverNative` () will fail if the receiver is a contract and the gas price changes, if the gas price gets reduced it opens room for reentrance and if the gas price gets increased the transfer will fail as the forwarded gas won't be enough to complete the transaction.

if the `to` address isn't an EOA  but a contract the contract can call back(re-enter)into the contract if the gas price changes in a favourable way and drain the contract of the remaining Eth , or fail if the gas price changes in a non favourable way locking Eth in the contract.
## Proof of Concept
```solidity
function recoverNative(uint256 _amount, address _to) external onlyOwner {
//@audit - This forwards 2300 gas, which may not be enough if the recipient is a contract and gas costs change.
        payable(_to).transfer(_amount);
    }
```
The purpose of The `recoverNative` is to send back any ETH mistakenly sent to the contract or any ETH that fails to be brigded back to the respective sender the function can only be called by the` owner`, if the `to` address isn't an EOA  but a contract the contract can call back(re-enter)into the contract if the gas price changes in a favourable way and drain the contract of the remaining Eth , or fail if the gas price changes in a non favourable way locking Eth in the contract.
