# Vulnerability details

## Impact

Incorrect calculation of `TVL` and the loss of fund from it.

## Proof of Concept

 The loop variable `i` is the index of the array of `operatorDelegators`. In [L318](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/main/contracts/RestakeManager.sol#L316-L321), however, `i` is used to index the array of `collateralTokens`.
