# Vulnerability details

## Impact
This can lead to counting the value of ETH in the protocol twice toward the TVL leading to an inflated TVL.

## Proof of Concept
This happens because when the RestakeManagerAdmin calls [`RestakeManager::addCollateralToken(...)`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/RestakeManager.sol#L220-L241) there are no checks to ensure the collateral being added is not NATIVE ETH and as such the value of ETH can be counted twice when calculating. TVL as the [`RestakeManager::calculateTVL()`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/RestakeManager.sol#L274) includes the balance of ETH in the deposit pool.

If ETH was added to the list of collateral tokens, then ETH would have been counted in the [loop](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/RestakeManager.sol#L289) and also added separately in L365 to the totalTVL as shown below
