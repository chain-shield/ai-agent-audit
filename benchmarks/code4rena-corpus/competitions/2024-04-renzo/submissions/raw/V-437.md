# Vulnerability details

## Impact

completeQueuedWithdrawal cannot run properly and, in severe cases, may result in an inability to withdraw funds correctly.

## Proof of Concept

When OperatorDelegator withdraws funds, it first calls `queueWithdrawals` and then `completeQueuedWithdrawal`. However, when `completeQueuedWithdrawal` is called, a portion of the withdrawn amount is sent to the WithdrawQueue contract, and the remaining amount is deposited back into the strategy manager.

During this process, when depositing into the strategy manager, the calculation of shares is also performed based on the deposited amount. However, if the `tokenAmount` is too small, the calculated shares will be 0, causing the entire transaction to revert.

The deposit process into `strategyManager.depositIntoStrategy` is as follows:

`strategyManager.depositIntoStrategy -> _depositIntoStrategy -> StrategyBase.deposit` 

https://github.com/Layr-Labs/eigenlayer-contracts/blob/15b679b08e7a3589ff83a5e84076ca4e0a00ec0b/src/contracts/core/StrategyManager.sol#L323-L342

`depositIntoStrategy` calls the strategy, but all strategies inherit from `StrategyBase`, and the deposit implementation in `StrategyBase` is as follows:

https://github.com/Layr-Labs/eigenlayer-contracts/blob/15b679b08e7a3589ff83a5e84076ca4e0a00ec0b/src/contracts/strategies/StrategyBase.sol#L96-L123

The calculation for `newShare` is: `amount * virtualShareAmount / virtualPriorTokenBalance`. If `newShare` is 0, it will revert.

The reason for a too-small `tokenAmount` is that some amount is transferred to the WithdrawQueue contract, and the remaining amount may not be enough to generate 1 share in the strategy manager, causing `completeQueuedWithdrawal` to fail to run properly.

In severe cases, when only `completeQueuedWithdrawal` can provide funds for the WithdrawQueue to withdraw, users' funds will be stuck in the contract. The only way to withdraw is to send funds to the contract, but this will also result in some loss of funds.

Therefore this will result in core functionality not functioning properly
