# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The bug causes the Total Value Locked (TVL) to be calculated incorrectly. This means that important financial calculations across the protocol are wrong. Incorrect TVL affects reward distribution, minting ezETH, wrong calculations of rate of ezETH etc.
