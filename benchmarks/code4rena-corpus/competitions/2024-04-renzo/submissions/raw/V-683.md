# Vulnerability details

It's impossible to withdraw ETH from Eigenlayer because [OperatorDelegator::completeQueuedWithdrawal()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/main/contracts/Delegation/OperatorDelegator.sol#L265) will always revert due to `nonReentrant` modifiers.

When completing a withdrawal for ETH the function [OperatorDelegator::completeQueuedWithdrawal()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/main/contracts/Delegation/OperatorDelegator.sol#L265) needs to be called, which will internally perform the following calls:

1. [DelegationManager::completeQueuedWithdrawal()](https://github.com/Layr-Labs/eigenlayer-contracts/blob/mainnet/src/contracts/core/DelegationManager.sol#L323)
2. [EigenPodManager::withdrawSharesAsTokens()](https://github.com/Layr-Labs/eigenlayer-contracts/blob/mainnet/src/contracts/pods/EigenPodManager.sol#L221)
3. [EigenPod::withdrawRestakedBeaconChainETH()](https://github.com/Layr-Labs/eigenlayer-contracts/blob/mainnet/src/contracts/pods/EigenPod.sol#L431)
4. [OperatorDelegator::receive()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/main/contracts/Delegation/OperatorDelegator.sol#L501)

The call flow starts and ends in the same contract: `OperatorDelegator`. Both the functions [OperatorDelegator::completeQueuedWithdrawal()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/main/contracts/Delegation/OperatorDelegator.sol#L265) and [OperatorDelegator::receive()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/main/contracts/Delegation/OperatorDelegator.sol#L501) have a `nonReentrant` modifier, which will make the call revert.

## Impact

ETH can't be withdrawn from Eigenlayer to Renzo, making it impossible for stakers to exchange their `ezETH` for `ETH`.

## Proof of Concept

It's possible to verify this by following the flow outlined above. Note that the function `EigenPod::withdrawRestakedBeaconChainETH()`(`4`) transfers `ETH` to the `EigenPod` owner, which is the `OperatorDelegator` of which the `receive()` function will be triggered.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Remove the `nonReentrant` modifer from the [OperatorDelegator::receive()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/main/contracts/Delegation/OperatorDelegator.sol#L501) function.
