# Vulnerability details

## Impact

Price oracles are very crucial for Renzo protocol as they provide updated exchange rates for ezETH, which is essential for maintaining accurate value representation and facilitating transactions within the protocol. However, in the current implementation of fetching the `latestRoundData()` from the oracle, there is a flaw which could lead to a DoS on functionalities relying on oracles.

## Proof of Concept

The vulnerability stems from the use of Chainlink oracle's `latestRoundData()` in several places in the protocol without employing a recommended safer `try/catch` approach for querying asset prices. This oversight could lead to potential Denial-of-Service, as https://blog.openzeppelin.com/secure-smart-contract-guidelines-the-dangers-of-price-oracles/ mentions, it is possible that Chainlink's multisig can instantly block access to price feeds, causing functions relying on it to revert.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Implement a safer try/catch approach for querying Chainlink prices. This approach ensures that if access to price feeds is blocked, the protocol can gracefully handle the situation without causing disruptions to critical functionalities.
