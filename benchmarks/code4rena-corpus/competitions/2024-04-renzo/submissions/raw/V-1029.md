# Vulnerability details

## Impact

Withdrawals are never paused.

## Proof of Concept

The `WithdrawQueue` contract intends to have pausing functionality, as it inherits `PausableUpgradeable` and defines `pause()` and `unpaused()` functions. However, it will not ever be able to be paused as the functions are never called, never enforcing pausability.

## Tools Used

Vscode

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Implement the pausing functionality correctlly by applying the `whenNotPaused` modifier.
