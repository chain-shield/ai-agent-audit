# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Funds can get stuck for some time during mass withdrawal request since `WithdrawalQueue::withdraw(...)` will always revert.

## Proof of Concept
With due respect to the developers, this is a result of design flaw. 


Currently, when the protocols `WithdrawalQueue` is available balance less than the amount a user intends to withdraw  and there are no funds in the `DepositQueue` to fill the buffer, while there are yet unclaimed queued withdrawals, any attempt by a user to call [`WithdrawalQueue::withdraw(...)`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Withdraw/WithdrawQueue.sol#L236) will revert because the protocol does not implement  the `WithdrawalQueue::withdraw(...)` function to queue withdrawals from Eigen layer. Although the sponsor confirmed in the discord that :

*admin accounts will manually unstake from EigenLayer **periodically** through 2 step process*

I assume the admin will unstake to fill the `withdrawalBufferTarget[asset]`

This means that users funds will be stuck if there are no incoming deposits (and rewards are not yet sufficient to handle the withdrawal requests) because: 

- this resolution  begs the question of **how does the admin know how much asset to are being withdrawn in the event of a mass withdrawal when the** `withdrawalBufferTarget` **will not be able to handle such volume** at once
- the `withdraw(...)` function cannot queue withdrawals because `claimReserve[_asset]` is already taken care of by the current balance of the `WithdrawalQueue` pending the `cooldown` period after which user can claim their asset
- withdrawal form EigenLayer is subject to a 7 day delay.

While *manually unstaking periodically_* seemsto make sense in the short term, in the event of a mass withdrawal and there are no incoming deposits, the `WithdrawalQueue::withdraw(...)` call will fail because as seen on `L240` the `amountToRedeem` will be greater than the amount available to withdraw and the call will revert.

## POC

- `withdrawalBufferTarget[_asset]` is set to 100 ether
- current asset balance in the withdrawal queue = 10 ether
- `claimReserve[_asset]` is currently 10 ether
- The are still actively stake asset worth 1000 ether
- there is a mass withdrawal to the tune of 500 ether in failed withdrawals
- admin can only unstakes to fill 100 ether **periodically** because they are not aware of the 500 ether requested in failed withdrawals.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Consider queueing withdrawal from Eigen layer in `epochs` instead of reverting when the `amountToRedeem` is greater than the amount available to withdraw from the `WithdrawalQueue` instead of waiting for the admin to periodically withdraw.

Similar protocols like RioStaking protocol allows their withdraw function under the same condition, to queue withdrawal form EigenLayer
