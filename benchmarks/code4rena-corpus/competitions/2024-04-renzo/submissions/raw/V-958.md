# Vulnerability details

## Impact
`adminGasSpentInWei` is recorded in the `OperatorDelegator` contract to track the amount of gas fees spent by the admin. It is deducted from staking rewards when they are transferred from the `DelayedWithdrawalRouter` to the `OperatorDelegator` contract and paid to the admin. Thus, `adminGasSpentInWei` can be considered a liability of the protocol.

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Delegation/OperatorDelegator.sol#L470-L474

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Delegation/OperatorDelegator.sol#L501-L526

It is noted that `adminGasSpentInWei` increases with each admin operation, yet staking rewards might only be withdrawn occasionally. Therefore, when calculating the protocol's TVL in `RestakeManager` through `calculateTVLs`, it is essential to deduct `adminGasSpentInWei`. Otherwise, uninformed users might mint ezETH without awareness of this fee, and when staking rewards are withdrawn, the TVL could suddenly drop, leading to losses.

## Proof of Concept

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
It is recommended that the calculation of TVL should deduct `adminGasSpentInWei` in the `OperatorDelegator` to accurately reflect the protocol's true TVL.
