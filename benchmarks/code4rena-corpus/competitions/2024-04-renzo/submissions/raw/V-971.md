# Vulnerability details

## Impact

Protocol becomes insolvent as it becomes impossible to finalize withdrawals. It may be possible to finalize withdrawals if the withdrawal buffer is increased.

## Proof of Concept
`OperatorDelegator::completeQueuedWithdrawal()` finalizes withdrawals for strategy tokens and `ETH`. If the token balance of a given strategy exceeds the withdrawal buffer, it deposits to a strategy; however, the protocol may never finalize the withdrawal because when it tries to deposit, the max tvl of a strategy may be [reached](https://github.com/Layr-Labs/eigenlayer-contracts/blob/dev/src/contracts/strategies/StrategyBaseTVLLimits.sol#L81) on this deposit attempt, reverting the transaction and not allowing any token or ETH to be withdrawn.

## Tools Used

Vscode

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Check if the max tvl of the strategy is reached before depositing.
