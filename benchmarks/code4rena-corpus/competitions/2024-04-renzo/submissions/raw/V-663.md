# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Removing `operatorDelegator` without checking if they have shares in the `strategyManager` can lead to:

- loss of funds for any user who had depeosited before the removal but is withdrawing after the OD is removed.
- `_collateralToken` being stuck in the Eigen Layer without a way to withdraw
- Also this can break accouncting as the TVL will be understated because the removed operator has assigned assigned to it from EigenLayer when `RestakeManager::deposit(...)` was called but when `calculateTVLs(...)` is called to evaluate the `totalTVL`, the value will be understated because the `calculateTVLs(...)` function will [loop](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/RestakeManager.sol#L289-L300) through all the ODs in the `operatorDelegators` array leaving out the removed `operatorDelegators` even when it still has actively staked shares.

## Proof of Concept
When a _collateralToken is deposited using the [`RestakeManager::deposit(...)`]() function, funds are approved for a chosen `operatorDelegator` (OD) to deposit in Eigen Layer `strategy` by calling `operatorDelegator.deposit(...)`. When the OD deposits the funds into Eigen layer (EL) by calling `strategyManager.depositIntoStrategy(...)` to deposit the funds into the Strategy of the _collateralToken, the `strategy` assigns shares to the chosen OD who made the EL deposit and [saves it in a mapping for the  [`operatorDelegator`](https://github.com/Layr-Labs/eigenlayer-contracts/blob/6de01c6c16d6df44af15f0b06809dc160eac0ebf/src/contracts/core/StrategyManager.sol#L336) in the `StartegyManager`’s storage as seen [here](https://github.com/Layr-Labs/eigenlayer-contracts/blob/6de01c6c16d6df44af15f0b06809dc160eac0ebf/src/contracts/core/StrategyManager.sol#L309)

```solidity
EigenLayer/StrategyManager.sol

  function _addShares(address staker, IERC20 token, IStrategy strategy, uint256 
  shares) internal {
    
    ...

      // add the returned shares to their existing shares for this strategy
  @>  stakerStrategyShares[staker][strategy] += shares;

  }
```

However, when [`RestakeManager::removeOperatorDelegator(..)`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/RestakeManager.sol#L160) is called to remove an OD, no checks are performed to ensure the OD being removed has no pending shares assigned in the `strategyManager` and as such this can lead to:

- funds getting stuck in Eigen Layer. The effect of this becomes more pronounced during a mass withdrawal event.
- understated TVL value because an OD with actively staked shares is not taken into account

Even if the OD is later added to the list of active ODs, all calls to the [`calculateTVLs(...)`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/RestakeManager.sol#L302) function during the period when the OD was removed would have returned am understated  TVL which would have in turn lead to miscalculation of shares assigned to other ODs by the `strategyManager`

In fact, if  a user who deposited with all OD shares intact decides to withdraw when a removed OD has shares in it, he will get less than the value he deposited when he withdraws due to the reduced `totalTVL` value.


## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Consider checking if the operatorDelegator to be removed has shares assigned in the strategyManager and revert or implement a way to transfer the shares assigned before removing the operatorDelegator.
