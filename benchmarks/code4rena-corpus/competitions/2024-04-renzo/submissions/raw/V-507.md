# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The absence of a sanity check for the price before casting in the `getMintRate` function could lead to potential errors in the price calculation, especially if the oracle returns a negative price. This oversight poses a risk of miscalculating the scaled price, potentially resulting in incorrect token minting rates.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Implement a sanity check for the price before casting in the `getMintRate` function for Renzo Oracle L2, similar to the one implemented in the Renzo Oracle. This check should ensure that the price retrieved from the oracle is non-negative before proceeding with further calculations, thus preventing potential underflow errors and ensuring consistent and safe token minting rates.
