# Vulnerability details

## Impact
To ensure the price feed is not stale, both `RenzoOracle` and `RenzoOracleL2` implement the following check:
```solidity
        if (timestamp < block.timestamp - MAX_TIME_WINDOW) revert OraclePriceExpired();
```
where `MAX_TIME_WINDOW = 86400 + 60`. However, Chainlink price feeds with heartbeat of 1 day sometimes take slightly more than `86460` seconds to update. Therefore, each `deposit` or `withdraw` that would be minted in that small window (after the `86460` seconds and before the feed is actually updated) will revert, and the affected user will have to submit another transaction.

## Proof of Concept

Alice deposits 32 stETH into Renzo at t = 86472 seconds after the last CL price feed update. Her transaction reverts with `OraclePriceExpired()` and she needs to submit a new one.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Consider slightly increasing the staleness tolerance, e.g. to `1 day + 5 minutes`.
