# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Renzo protocol design is susceptible to typical vulnerability of Vault-type protocol where first user can donate funds to protocol to artificially increase share price, leading to loss of funds to other users. 

## Proof of Concept
First user donates funds directly to `DepositQueue`, depositing ETH.
https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Deposits/DepositQueue.sol#L163

All funds in excess of `bufferToFill` remain on `DepositQueue` balance. 
https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Deposits/DepositQueue.sol#L176
https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Deposits/DepositQueue.sol#L294

They are subsequently included in TVL calculations: 
https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/RestakeManager.sol#L352

This TVL amount is then used in 'deposit' function of first user, leading to all deposit below certain amount valuate to 0 ezETH and reverting, rendering the share of deposits depending on donation size impossible. 

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Oracle/RenzoOracle.sol#L146

Also see https://github.com/code-423n4/2023-11-kelp-findings/issues/42

for similar issue and mechanism.
