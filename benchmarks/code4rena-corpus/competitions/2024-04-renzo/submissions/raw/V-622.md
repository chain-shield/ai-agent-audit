# Vulnerability details

## Impact

The amount of `xezETH` minted to the user is smaller than actual amount of `ezETH` generated on L1, leading to loss of funds to the user.

## Proof of Concept

When depositting through `xRenzoDeposit` contract, there is a swapping from deposit token to `nextWETH` at `L239` of `_deposit()` function. At `L253` of `_deposit()` function, the returned value `amountOut` of `_trade()` function is used to calculate the `xezETHAmount` that is the amount of `xezETH` to be minted to the depositor. And then, the swapped `nextWETH`s are sent to L1 and are exchanged to `ezETH`. However, there is an imbalance between the total supply of `xezETH` and `ezETH`, because the `amountOut` is differ from the amount of `nextWETH` that are sent to L1.

The difference occurs at the swap function `_trade()`.

At the `_trade()` function, `amountNextWETH` at `L375` is the amount of `nextWETH` swapped and arrived to the `xRenzoDeposit` contract itself by the connext contract. And during swap, some fees were deducted from original input amount. So, it is not needed to decrement the amount of `nextWETH` like at `L387`. Furthermore, as mentioned at `L440` of `sweep()` function, the whole amount of swapped `nextWETH` are all sent to L1 without any bridging slippage because they are already `nextWETH`. As a result, the amount of `xezETH` minted to the user is smaller than actual amount of `ezETH` of L1, leading to loss of funds to the user.

## Tools Used

Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Recommend not to decrement the `amountNextWETH` by the bridge router fee at `L387` of `_trade()` function, or to reduce the amount of `nextWETH` to send to L1 at `sweep()` function.
