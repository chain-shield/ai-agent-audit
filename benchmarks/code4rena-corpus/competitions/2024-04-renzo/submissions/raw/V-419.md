# Vulnerability details

## Impact
`RestakeManager#deposit` treats stETH as any other token, which is prone to the known stETH rounding issue. 

https://docs.lido.fi/token-guides/steth-on-aave-caveats/

As `RestakeManager#deposit` relies on its balance, and assumes it has received exactly `_amount` from the depositor, it then splits `_amount` into `bufferToFill` and `_amount - bufferToFill`. If `_amount` is transferred from the user to the RestakeManager with a precision loss, but both `bufferToFill` and `_amount - bufferToFill` are transferred without precision loss, the contract will not be able to execute the latter transaction, as it would own only `_amount - 1` of stETH, but would attempt to transfer `_amount - bufferToFill + bufferToFill = _amount` of stETH.

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/RestakeManager.sol#L546-L562

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Use `balanceOf(address(this))` instead of `_amount`.
