# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The total TVL is miscalculated.
The issue will cause many unexpected errors.

## Proof of Concept
`RestakeManager.sol#calculateTVLs` function is the following.
```solidity

```
`totalWithdrawalQueueValue` means the total sum of values of collaterals in the `withdrawQueue`.
But `L318` multiplies the value of `collateralTokens[i]` with `collateralTokens[j].balanceOf(withdrawQueue)`.
Since the value of `collateralTokens[i]` differs from the value of `collateralTokens[j]`, the `totalWithdrawalQueueValue` is miscalculated.
