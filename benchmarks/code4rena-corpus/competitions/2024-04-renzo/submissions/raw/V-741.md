# Vulnerability details


## Cause

`RestakeManager`'s `chooseOperatorDelegatorForDeposit` function can cause the deposit to exceed the operator delegator's allocation limit by an arbitrarily large amount. This is because the [TVLs without adding the deposit](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/main/contracts/RestakeManager.sol#L378-L381) are used for the calculation instead of TVLs as they would be after the deposit. As long as the allocation is not 0 and does not previously exceed the limit, any amount will be deposited into the first operator delegator that was under its allocation before the new deposit.

## Impact

When more than one operator delegator is used, the allocation limit is a safety mechanism that limits the exposure to a particular operator (and the AVSes it validates). Due to this issue, the allocation limit safety mechanism fails in limiting the exposure of the protocol since it can be arbitrarily surpassed. If the underlying AVS is not profitable enough, or justifies only a certain allocation due to risk management, this issue may cause loss of funds or loss of yield to ezETH holders.

## Proof of Concept

1. An operator delegator is added with a small allocation to limit the exposure and funds deposited into it.
2. If the `chooseOperatorDelegatorForDeposit` loop reaches it, it will allow a deposit of any size, immediately breaking the allocation limitation and safety mechanism.
3. The AVS that is now overallocated to, is insufficiently profitable, or puts staked funds in more risk than intended, causing potential loss of funds and yield.
