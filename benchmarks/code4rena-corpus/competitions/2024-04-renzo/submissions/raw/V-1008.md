# Vulnerability details

The [`OperatorDelegator`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/main/contracts/Delegation/OperatorDelegator.sol) contract allows an admin to queue withdrawals of multiple tokens from different strategies in the EigenLayer protocol via the [`queueWithdrawals()`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/main/contracts/Delegation/OperatorDelegator.sol#L193) function. These queued withdrawals are later completed by the admin calling the [`completeQueuedWithdrawal()`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/main/contracts/Delegation/OperatorDelegator.sol#L265) function.

However, the `completeQueuedWithdrawal()` function calls [`DelegationManager.completeQueuedWithdrawal()`](https://github.com/Layr-Labs/eigenlayer-contracts/blob/84ca899c715dbc577af1c8db5965188b061fe464/src/contracts/core/DelegationManager.sol#L305) with the `receiveAsTokens` parameter hardcoded to `true`. If any of the strategies included in the queued withdrawal fails or reverts, this will cause the entire withdrawal to revert. 

As mentioned in this [comment](https://github.com/code-423n4/2023-04-eigenlayer-findings/issues/132#issuecomment-1575599410) and further confirmed [here](https://github.com/code-423n4/2023-04-eigenlayer-findings/issues/132#issuecomment-1582291971), users can work around this by setting `receiveAsTokens` to `false`, allowing them to queue a new withdrawal excluding the failing strategy. However, the `OperatorDelegator` has no way to do this, as it can only call `completeQueuedWithdrawal()` with `receiveAsTokens == true`. 

This means a single failing strategy can block queued `OperatorDelegator` withdrawals indefinitely.

## Impact
Admin withdrawals from the `OperatorDelegator` can be blocked if any of the strategies included in their queued withdrawal fails or reverts when completing the withdrawal. The only way to recover is to upgrade the `OperatorDelegator` contract.

## Proof of Concept
1. Admin calls `OperatorDelegator.queueWithdrawals()`, including a withdrawal from `StrategyA` and `StrategyB`
2. Later, when trying to complete the withdrawal via `OperatorDelegator.completeQueuedWithdrawal()`, `StrategyB` is paused or fails
3. The call to `DelegationManager.completeQueuedWithdrawal()` with `receiveAsTokens == true` reverts due to the failure in `StrategyB`
4. The admin's entire withdrawal, including from `StrategyA`, is blocked
5. There is no way for `OperatorDelegator` to recover the withdrawal from `StrategyA`, short of upgrading the contract

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Consider adding a recovery function in `OperatorDelegator` that the admin can call in case a queued withdrawal fails. This function should call `DelegationManager.completeQueuedWithdrawal()` with `receiveAsTokens == false`, allowing the shares to be transferred to the withdrawing address, who can then queue a new withdrawal excluding the failing strategy.

It is important to ensure the shares are accredited to an address that is included in the TVL calculations, or to perform the emergency withdrawal and redeposit atomically in order to ensure no inaccuracies in TVL.
