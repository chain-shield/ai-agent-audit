# Vulnerability details

## Impact

The protocol could be unable to receive the desired amount of deposits due to the requested withdrawal amounts being factored into the deposit limitation checks, even though the actual utilized assets are insufficient

## Proof of Concept

As you can see at `L510` of the `RestakeManager.deposit()` function, there is a check to ensure the deposit amount does not cause an overflow of the deposit limitation. However, this check does not consider the requested withdrawal amounts.

In fact, all assets in the `WithdrawQueue` contract, including the requested withdrawal amounts (`claimReserve`s), are included in the `totalTVL` calculation. But these assets were already determined to be claimed at a later time, so it is improper to include them in the deposit limitation check.

As a result, protocol could be unable to receive the desired amount of deposits due to the requested withdrawal amounts being factored into the deposit limitation checks, even though the actual utilized assets are insufficient

```solidity

        IERC20 _collateralToken,

            revert MaxTVLReached();

        [...]

```

This problem also occurs in `RestakeManager.depositETH()` function.

## Tools Used

Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

The requested withdrawal amounts held in the `WithdrawQueue` contract should not be included in the `maxDepositTVL` check.
