# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Interactions with non-standard ERC20 tokens that do not return a `boolean` from approve may cause transactions to revert.

## Proof of Concept
Non-standard ERC20 tokens deviate from the expected approve return value, leading to potential reverts when interacting with contracts assuming a boolean return.
[Similar Finding](https://solodit.xyz/issues/m-1-certain-erc20-token-does-not-return-bool-from-approve-and-transfer-and-transaction-revert-sherlock-none-footium-git).

## Tools Used
Manual Review.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Utilize OpenZeppelin's SafeERC20 safeApprove method to handle ERC20 tokens that may not return a boolean.
