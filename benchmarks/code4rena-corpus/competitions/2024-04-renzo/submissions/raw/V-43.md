# Vulnerability details

## Impact
full consumption of [2300 hard coded gas](https://solidity-by-example.org/sending-ether/#:~:text=transfer%20(2300%20gas%2C%20throws%20error)) leading to permanent freeze of user funds
this is rated as medium due to the low number of affected persons but still much hard for them

## Proof of Concept
in [WithdrawQueue](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/main/contracts/Withdraw/WithdrawQueue.sol) contract, the function [claim()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/main/contracts/Withdraw/WithdrawQueue.sol#L279-L312) uses high level [transfer](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/main/contracts/Withdraw/WithdrawQueue.sol#L303-L305) method, this is a problem with [native ETH](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Withdraw/WithdrawQueue.sol#L303) why?, as it has [2300 hard coded gas](https://solidity-by-example.org/sending-ether/#:~:text=transfer%20(2300%20gas%2C%20throws%20error))

this a problem due to the following

1. a protocol building on top of ezETH that handles eth transfers and doing strategies for his users, this protocol interacting with ezETH through a contract with fallback function triggered when receiving ETH, the contract implements a logic around withdrawals consuming more than the [2300 hard coded gas](https://solidity-by-example.org/sending-ether/#:~:text=transfer%20(2300%20gas%2C%20throws%20error)) leading to out of gas revert
2. a normal user interacting through with the protocol through a contract implementing logic around fallbacks too 

in the above scenarios if its a protocol building on top of ezETH the results will be catastrophic due to permanent funds freeze

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
using [low level call](https://solidity-by-example.org/call/#:~:text=Solidity%20by%20Example-,Call,-call%20is%20a) with no gas limit as this wouldn't be dangerous on protocol cause the user can't grief himself as he is the one calling [claim()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/main/contracts/Withdraw/WithdrawQueue.sol#L279-L312)
and sure check for returned data for success or implement OZ library for low level calls
