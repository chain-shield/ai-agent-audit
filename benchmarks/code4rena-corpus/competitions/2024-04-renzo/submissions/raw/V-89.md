# Vulnerability details

`RestakeManager::deposit(...)` currently lacks a check to ensure that the amount sent to `OperatorDelegator::deposit(...)` is greater than zero. Consequently, the function will consistently revert if the amount to deposit is less than or equal to the withdraw queue deficit.


## Impact

`RestakeManager::deposit(...)` first deposits the tokens in withdraw queue to fill the deficit (if there is any). Only then the tokens are sent to Operator Delegator. But it doesn't check if the amount sent to Operator Delegator after deducting the deficit is greater than zero or not.
