# Vulnerability details

## Impact
This can lead to loss of funds accross different `_depositTokens` and for the renzo protocol

## Proof of Concept
Users can call `xRenzoDeposit.deposit()` to deposit a (`_depositToken`) for instance `WETH` on L2. However the `xRenzoDeposit` contract approves the `connext` bridge to spend the full deposit amount. But due to slippage and `bridgeFee` deductions, the users are expected to specify `_minOut`  *which is the minimum number of `xezETH` to accept to ensure slippage minimums.* 

The transaction flow is as shown below

|→ `xRenzoDeposit::deposit(…)`
|→→ `xRenzoDeposit::_deposit(...)`
|→→→ `xRenzoDeposit::getBridgeFeeShare(_amountIn);`
|→→→ [`xRenzoDeposit::_trade(...)`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Bridge/L2/xRenzoDeposit.sol#L367)
|→→→→ [`depositToken.safeApprove(...)`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Bridge/L2/xRenzoDeposit.sol#L369)
|→→→→ `connext.swapExact(...)`
|→→→ `...`

Some funds are still left in the  [approval](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Bridge/L2/xRenzoDeposit.sol#L369) given to `connext` after the [`connext.swapExact(...)`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Bridge/L2/xRenzoDeposit.sol#L375) is called this is owing to slippage and as such `connext` can still spend the unused funds. IN the event of an exploit the `xRenzoDeposit` contract will loose all remaining amount from the last deposit transaction from different `_depositToken`s.

### Exploit Scenario

- `bridgeFeeShare` = 5%
- Expected slippage from swap exact = 10%
- `_amountIn` = 1_000_000e18
- After `bridgeFeeShare` is charged, `_amountIn` = 950_000e18
- `connext` is approved to spend `_amountIn` = 950_000e18
- Due to the current pool reserves or even slippage when `connext.swapExact(...)` is called, it spends 855_000e18 still having approval for the unspent amount of up to 95_000e18 `_depositToken`s
- If there is an exploit in  the `connext` bridge before `sweep()` is called, the unspent amount could be stolen through `connext`.

The will cascade across all allowed _depositTokens in the xRenzoDeposit contract leading to material loss of funds

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Consider revoking the remaining funds from the approval given to the connext bridge after a trade(...) is called during a deposit transaction.
