# Vulnerability details

## Impact
`xRenzoDeposit::deposit` allows users to deposit tokens in exchange for `xezETH`. Due to an error regarding conversion from `nextWETH` to `xezETH` during deposit, users will be minted an incorrect amount of `xezETH`, likely much less than actually owed.

## Proof of Concept
`xRenzoDeposit::deposit`

`amountOut` is the amount of `nextWETH` that is received from swapping the deposited tokens for `nextWETH` via the call to `_trade`.

`_lastPrice` is the price of `xezETH/nextWETH` retrieved from the `L2` oracle:

`RenzoOracleL2::getMintRate`
https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/main/contracts/Bridge/L2/Oracle/RenzoOracleL2.sol#L50
```javascript
    /**
     * @notice Pulls the price of ezETH
     * @dev reverts if price is less than 1 Ether
     */
    function getMintRate() public view returns (uint256, uint256) {
        (, int256 price, , uint256 timestamp, ) = oracle.latestRoundData();
        if (timestamp < block.timestamp - MAX_TIME_WINDOW) revert OraclePriceExpired();
        // scale the price to have 18 decimals
        uint256 _scaledPrice = (uint256(price)) * 10 ** (18 - oracle.decimals());
        if (_scaledPrice < 1 ether) revert InvalidOraclePrice();
        return (_scaledPrice, timestamp);
    }
```

Now that we have established `amountOut` is in `nextWETH` and `_lastPrice` is in `xezETH/nextWETH`, let's look at the calculation for the amount of `xezETH` to mint:

```javascript
uint256 xezETHAmount = (1e18 * amountOut) / _lastPrice;
```

This calculation is incorrect, let's look at the units:

$$
\frac{1e^{18} \times \text{amountOut (nextWETH)}}
{\text{\_lastPrice (xezETH/nextWETH)}}
$$



This is equivalent to:

$$
\frac{1e^{18} \times \text{amountOut (nextWETH)} \times \text{nextWETH}}{\text{\_lastPrice (xezETH)}}
$$



It's clear to see that the unit conversion is incorrect. This does not give us the price in terms of `xezETH`. The correct calculation should be:

$$
\frac{\text{amountOut (nextWETH)} \times \text{\_lastPrice (xezETH/nextWETH)}}{1e^{18}}
$$

The `nextWETH` units here "cancel" and we receive the price in terms of `xezETH`.

Therefore the current calculation is incorrect and users will not be minted the correct amount of `xezETH`. It's likely that they will be minted much less than what is actually owed to them.
