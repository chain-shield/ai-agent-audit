# Vulnerability details

## Impact
If the Pod Owner's beacon chain ETH balance decreases, which could be due to slashing or penality, someone can provide proof of this to the `EigenPod.verifyBalanceUpdates()` function to record the beacon chain balance updates.

As a result, `podOwnerShares[podOwner]` will be deduct [here](https://github.com/Layr-Labs/eigenlayer-contracts/blob/15b679b08e7a3589ff83a5e84076ca4e0a00ec0b/src/contracts/pods/EigenPod.sol#L223), which directly impacts Renzo since its total TVL drops,

Stakers could front-run these updates by requesting a withdrawal, avoiding any penalties. Let's see how.

## Proof of Concept

- Before `verifyBalancUpdates()` being called, stakers send a `withdrawQueue.withdraw()` txn which allow them to redeem their ezETH shares token before tvl changes. 

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Withdraw/WithdrawQueue.sol#L229
```solidity
        // calculate totalTVL
        (, , uint256 totalTVL) = restakeManager.calculateTVLs();

        // Calculate amount to Redeem in ETH
        uint256 amountToRedeem = renzoOracle.calculateRedeemAmount(
            _amount,
            ezETH.totalSupply(),
            totalTVL
        );
```

This `amountToRedeem` is cached to `withdrawRequests[staker]` and is seperated from the totalTVL for future claim.

- `EigenPod.verifyBalancUpdates()` is called, `podOwnerShares[podOwner]` will be updated, the totalTVL penalized. As a result, other innocent stakers have to bear those losses.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

During withdraw request, if it possible to cache sharesAmount instead of its value in corresponding collateral token, this could be prevented. And when user finally claim after cooldown(i.e. 7 days) period, the claimable value then can be calculated with up to date tvl.
