# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The `OperatorDelegator` can receive rewards through a partial withdrawal upon invoking the [OperatorDelegator::verifyAndProcessWithdrawals](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Delegation/OperatorDelegator.sol#L405-L424) function, or when a validator exists and the excess of 32 ether is claimed by the OperatorDelegator as a reward on call to the [OperatorDelegator::verifyAndProcessWithdrawals](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Delegation/OperatorDelegator.sol#L405-L424) function, for a full withdrawal.

**DETAILS**:
       When [OperatorDelegator::verifyAndProcessWithdrawals](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Delegation/OperatorDelegator.sol#L405-L424) function is called, the function will go on to make an external call to [EigenPod::verifyAndProcessWithdrawals](https://github.com/Layr-Labs/eigenlayer-contracts/blob/90a0f6aee79b4a38e1b63b32f9627f21b1162fbb/src/contracts/pods/EigenPod.sol#L235-L280) function, which after computation, on either instance mentioned above(partial or full withdrawal, where on full withdrawal the amount set to be withdrawn is above the max stake), the rewards are scheduled to be sent as a delayed withdrawal to the OperatorDelegator contract via [DelayedWithdrawalRouter::createDelayedWithdrawal](https://github.com/Layr-Labs/eigenlayer-contracts/blob/90a0f6aee79b4a38e1b63b32f9627f21b1162fbb/src/contracts/pods/DelayedWithdrawalRouter.sol#L68-L91) function, whose current delay is 7 days.

The issue here is that, an attacker can take advantage of the sudden change in the contract total value locked to extract value.
After 7 days, the attacker can:

+ mint ezETH via a call to [RestakeManager::depositETH](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/RestakeManager.sol#L582-L584) function
+ Call  Eigen Layer [DelayedWithdrawalRouter::claimDelayedWithdrawals()](https://github.com/Layr-Labs/eigenlayer-contracts/blob/90a0f6aee79b4a38e1b63b32f9627f21b1162fbb/src/contracts/pods/DelayedWithdrawalRouter.sol#L100-L105) function to send the scheduled withdrawal to the OperatorDelegator contract.
+ Call [WithdrawQueue::withdraw](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Withdraw/WithdrawQueue.sol#L206-L263) function, to burn all of his ezETH for ether.


 **Let's look at some values:**
Assuming the request is a full withdrawal, with an accumulated reward of 1 ether.

Initial State:
current ezETH supply = 32e18
current protocol TVL = 32 ether

Note that I am omitting other factors that could have influenced these values.

Assuming the attacker is Bob, If Bob deposits 5 ether via a call to [RestakeManager::depositETH](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/RestakeManager.sol#L582-L584) function, the amount Bob receives is calculated at [RenzoOracle::calculateMintAmount](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Oracle/RenzoOracle.sol#L123-L149) function.

Where:
inflationPercentaage = 1e18 * 5e18 / 32e18 + 5e18 ==> 135135135135135136
newEzETHSupply = 32e18 * 1e18 / 1e18 - 135135135135135136 ==> 37e18

Bob Receives = newEzETHSupply - currentEzETHSupply
 ==> 37e18 - 32e18 --> 5e18 ezETH

STATE:
current ezETH Supply = 37e18
current TVL = 37 ether

After Bob calls [DelayedWithdrawalRouter::claimDelayedWithdrawals()](https://github.com/Layr-Labs/eigenlayer-contracts/blob/90a0f6aee79b4a38e1b63b32f9627f21b1162fbb/src/contracts/pods/DelayedWithdrawalRouter.sol#L100-L105) function and the reward sent to the OperatorDelegator contract, the new state will now be:
current ezETH Supply = 37e18
current TVL = 38 ether

When Bob then makes a withdrawal request via a call to [WithdrawQueue::withdraw](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Withdraw/WithdrawQueue.sol#L206-L263) function, to burn all of his ezETH for ether.
The amount of ether Bob receives is calculated [here](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Oracle/RenzoOracle.sol#L152-L164):
redeem amount = current TVL * ezTH to burn / ezETH supply 
 = 38e18 * 5e18 / 37e18 ==> 5135135135135134700

Bob instantly gets an extra ~0.14 ether, to be claimed via [WithdrawQueue::claim](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Withdraw/WithdrawQueue.sol#L279-L312) function, after the cool-down period. Thus stealing unsuspecting users' rewards.

Note that the amount of value extracted from the reward is dependent on Bob's deposit, and thus increases as the deposit amount increases
