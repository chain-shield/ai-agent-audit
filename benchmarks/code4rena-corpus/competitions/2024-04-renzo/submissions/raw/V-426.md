# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The primary concern during a flash crash is that the oracle might return incorrect prices that are still within the acceptable range (i.e., between the minimum and maximum prices it reports). This can happen because the oracle's mechanisms to filter out extreme price movements might not be sufficient during extreme market conditions.
During a flash crash, the price of an asset can plummet significantly, but Chainlink's price feed might continue to report the incorrect minimum price due to its built-in minimum and maximum price filters. This incorrect price is still within the acceptable range for the oracle, making it appear valid

The protocol may suffer substantial financial losses due to toxic debt accumulation, insolvency risks, and unfair liquidations. Attackers can exploit these vulnerabilities by buying assets at artificially inflated prices, depositing them into lending/borrowing platforms, and borrowing against these assets at inflated values, leading to a drain of value from these platforms.
## Proof of Concept
The `RenzoOracleL2.sol` contract demonstrates the use of Chainlink's AggregatorV3Interface for fetching price data. The `getMintRate()` function checks for price staleness but does not account for incorrect prices during flash crashes.
```
    function getMintRate() public view returns (uint256, uint256) {
        (, int256 price, , uint256 timestamp, ) = oracle.latestRoundData();
        if (timestamp < block.timestamp - MAX_TIME_WINDOW) revert OraclePriceExpired();
        // scale the price to have 18 decimals
        uint256 _scaledPrice = (uint256(price)) * 10 ** (18 - oracle.decimals());
        if (_scaledPrice < 1 ether) revert InvalidOraclePrice();
        return (_scaledPrice, timestamp);
    }
```

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
- Implement Price Threshold Mechanisms: Introduce price threshold mechanisms to detect abnormal price movements and trigger emergency procedures or circuit breakers to prevent the use of incorrect price data during flash crashes.
- Diversify Oracle Sources: Use multiple oracle sources and data providers to aggregate price data from diverse sources and cross-validate information to reduce the risk of relying on a single source of data.
