# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Assets amount smaller than the empty withdraw buffer amount cannot be deposited.

## Proof of Concept

If the deposit amount (`_amount`) is less than `bufferTofill` in #L547, `bufferTofill` returns `_amount`.
As a result, `_amount` returns 0 in #L549.
Therefore, `OperatorDelegator.sol#deposit()` function will be reverted in #L562.
