# Vulnerability details

`xRenzoDeposit` on L2 chains would mint xezETH to users that deposits. After some amount has been accumulated, `sweep` would be called to transfer the `WETH` to L1. On L1, `xRenzoBridge` would deposit the ETH to restakeManager. The provided `ezETH` is locked and the `xezETH` is burnt. It is burnt so that it could be minted when users bridge their `xezETH` from L2 to L1 via connext bridge.

Simplified version of [xReceive](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/main/contracts/Bridge/L1/xRenzoBridge.sol#L139-L201)
```
// Unwrap the WETH
uint256 ethAmount = IWeth(address(wETH)).withdraw(_amount);

// Deposit it into Renzo RestakeManager
uint256 ezETHAmount = restakeManager.depositETH{ value: ethAmount }();

// Approve the lockbox to spend the ezETH
ezETH.safeApprove(address(xezETHLockbox), ezETHAmount);

// Send to the lockbox to be wrapped into xezETH
uint256 xezETHAmount = xezETHLockbox.deposit(ezETHAmount);

// Burn it - it was already minted on the L2
IXERC20(address(xezETH)).burn(address(this), xezETHAmount);
```


However, there's nothing that ensures that the `ezETH` locked by `xRenzoBridge` on L1 is equal or greater than the `xezETH` minted on L2. This should have been a spec or invariant in the protocol.
```
ezETH locked >= xezETH totalSupply
```
If `ezETH` locked is less than `xezETH`, the protocol wouldn't be able to provide `ezETH` if every user withdraws.

In an ideal situation, `ezETH` locked would be less `xezETH` supply. This is mainly because exchange rate is expected to be increasing, and there's a lag between the exchangeRate used for mint on L2 vs the one used for deposit on L1. It might take several hours or days for exchangeRate to move from L1 to L2 and L2 ETH to move to L1 for deposits. A lot of things could change within that period including significant price movement.

This undercollateralization would be made worse with every deposit on every L2 chain.

## Impact
`xezETH` supply could be greater than the `ezETH` locked and that could lead to insolvency. There's not enough `ezETH` backing every `xezETH`

## Proof of Concept
* At hour 0, price of 1.0 is sent from L1
* At hour 4, the price arrives at L2
* At hour 23, the price is used to mint 100 xezETH at 1.0 price
* At hour 40, the tokens are sweeped and sent to L1
* At hour 44, the tokens are deposited to restakeManager at 1.01 price and therefore 99 ezETH is minted and locked
* user bridges 100 xezETH to L1
* user withdraws 99 xezETH for 99 ezETH in lockbox
* There's 1 xezETH that can not be redeemed

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Ensure that ezETH locked is greater than or equal to xezETH minted.
