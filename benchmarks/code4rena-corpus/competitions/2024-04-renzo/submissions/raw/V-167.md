# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The `RenzoOracle` contract employs a `MAX_TIME_WINDOW` of `24 hours` to determine the staleness of all tokens. However, it appears that this setting is intended for use only with specific tokens like ezETH, stETH, and wBETH. If applied to other tokens, this could lead to outdated prices.
