# Vulnerability details

### Impact
The function `queueWithdrawals` converts token amounts to shares for withdrawal, and tracks the shares withdrawn for Total Value Locked (TVL) calculation. However, during completion of the withdrawal in the `completeQueuedWithdrawal` function, there is a discrepancy where the shares deducted for TVL adjustment may exceed the available assets, leading to an error in TVL calculation. This inconsistency arises due to inadequate validation of the available asset balance against the shares converted during withdrawal.
 If the shares converted to assets exceed the available balance, it leads to inaccurate TVL calculations .

### Mitigation
To mitigate the inconsistency in TVL adjustment during withdrawals:
1. **Validate Available Assets**: Implement validation checks to ensure that the shares converted to assets do not exceed the available asset balance.
2. **Prevent Excess Share Burning**: Prevent the burning of excess shares by comparing the value of the assets to the balance available, and ensure that only available shares are deducted for TVL adjustment.
