# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Denial of service for receipients with fallback/receive functions that consumes more than 2300 gas.


## Proof of Concept
The `address.send(...)` function is used to send `gasRefund` `ETH` to recipient on line 485 of `OperatorDelegator.sol`. The `address.send(...)` has a limitation fixed amount of 2300 gas that can be used in the external call. If the receiving contract consumes more than 2300gas in its `receive` or `fallback` function, the transaction will revert causing denail of service.

Quick examples are multisig wallets, most multisig wallet's fallback function consume more than 2300 gas.

Another example is with the innovation of smart accounts. This will most likely cause a denial of service with smart contract accounts.

Note: This issue has not been reported. only the use of `address.transfer()` was reported in the `4naly3er report` not this.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Consider using the `.call(...)` function to send ETH while also preventing reentrancy with check-effect-interaction pattern.
