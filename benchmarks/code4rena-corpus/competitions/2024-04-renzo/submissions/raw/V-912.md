# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In `RestakeManager.sol`, the `calculateTVLs()` function includes the amount of Ether in the `DepositQueue` contract when calculating the TVL. This calculation is used to determine the price of ezETH, providing an opportunity for the protocol's first staker to manipulate the price of ezETH.

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/RestakeManager.sol#L352

The first staker can manipulate the price significantly by depositing 1 wei of ETH to receive 1 ezETH and then transferring ETH into the DepositQueue contract as a donation. This can greatly increase the price per ezETH, potentially causing economic losses for subsequent stakers due to precision losses when the protocol calculates how much new ezETH to mint.

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Oracle/RenzoOracle.sol#L121-L149
