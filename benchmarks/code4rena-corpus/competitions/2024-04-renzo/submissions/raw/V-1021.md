# Vulnerability details

## Impact

Claiming rewards from the `DelayedWithdrawalRouter` fail when the `msg.value` is smaller than the required gas refund, but the contract has enough ETH balance, so the admin will not receive the rewards.

## Proof of Concept

When claiming ETH rewards from the `DelayedWithdrawalRouter`, [here](https://github.com/Layr-Labs/eigenlayer-contracts/blob/dev/src/contracts/pods/DelayedWithdrawalRouter.sol#L100-L105), they will be sent to the `OperatorDelegator`. In the `receive()` function, the gas refund is discounted from `msg.value`, but limited to `address(this).balance`. Thus, an overflow may occur, making the transaction revert. This will likely stop the admin from getting the gas refund.

## Tools Used

Vscode

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Subtract the refund from the `msg.value`, not the balance of the contract.
