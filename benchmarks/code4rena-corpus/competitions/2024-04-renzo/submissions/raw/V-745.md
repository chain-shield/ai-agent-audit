# Vulnerability details

## Impact
When users request a withdrawal, the EigenLayer shares equivalent to their ezETH's value are recorded. During settlement, these EigenLayer shares must be deducted to finalize the cooldown. However, in certain scenarios, the requested EigenLayer shares may be impossible to unwind due to funds idling in the deposit pool.

## Proof of Concept
Let's assume that 1 ezETH equals 1 EigenLayer-stETH, which equals 1 stETH initially.

Alice deposits 5e18 stETH, and her deposits are allocated to operators after rebalancing. Now, Renzo holds 5 EigenLayer-stETH, which is worth 5 stETH.

After some time, Bob deposits 100e18 stETH to Renzo and immediately withdraws it. At the time Bob requests this withdrawal, 100 stETH is worth 100 EigenLayer-stETH, so the shares owed are 100 EigenLayer-stETH. At settlement, 100 EigenLayer-stETH worth of stETH has to be sent to the withdrawal queue to settle this epoch.

Now, assume that the value of EigenLayer-stETH increases, meaning that 1 EigenLayer-stETH is now worth more stETH. This is an expected behavior because EigenLayer-stETH is similar to an ERC4626 vault, and we expect its value to increase over time.

Let's say 1 EigenLayer-stETH is now worth 1.1 stETH.

Now, 100 stETH sits idle in the deposit pool, and there are 5 EigenLayer-stETH in the operators, which means there are a total of 90.9 + 5 = 95.9 EigenLayer-stETH worth of stETH in Renzo. However, Bob's withdrawal request is for 100 EigenLayer-stETH.

This would mean that Bob's withdrawal request will not be settled, and the entire withdrawal flow will be stuck because this epoch can't be settled.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
It's quite difficult to estimate how much the ezETH is worth in the future, so if the protocol intends on allowing yield to be earned even during withdrawal, the protocol could recalculate how much a user will receive at the point when the ezETH is burned, instead of at the point where the ezETH is withdrawn. This will bring about a huge change which may have unexpected consequences.
