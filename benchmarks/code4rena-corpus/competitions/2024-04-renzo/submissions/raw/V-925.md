# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The balance of [LockboxAdapterBlast](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Bridge/Connext/integration/LockboxAdapterBlast.sol#L56-L95) could be drained by specifying large extra data.

## Proof of Concept

LockboxAdapterBlast could be used to for sending assets from Ethereum mainnet to Blast and also for bridging the ERC20 assets.

the function [bridgeTo](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Bridge/Connext/integration/LockboxAdapterBlast.sol#L56-L64) would be called by the user. 

It does follwing.

**Token Resolution**: Get the addresses of the xERC20 token and its associated lockbox contract from the registry.
**Token Transfer and Approval**: Transfer ERC20 tokens from the sender to the contract and approve the lockbox contract to spend them.
**Token Deposit**: Deposit the transferred tokens into the lockbox contract.
**Approval for Bridge**: Approve the blastStandardBridge contract to spend the deposited xERC20 tokens.
**Bridge Invocation**: Call the bridgeERC20To function of the L1StandardBridge contract to initiate the token bridge operation, providing necessary parameters such as token addresses, recipient, amount, gas limit, and extra data.

The function pass the extra data which is sent by the user.

on the blast side, this _extraData  would be used to emit.

https://www.codeslaw.app/contracts/ethereum/0x7a2075519dd9598b62075fd397af0dd34b14619a#:~:text=//%20Emit%20the%20correct%20events.%20By%20default%20this%20will%20be%20ERC20BridgeInitiated,_remoteToken%2C%20_from%2C%20_to%2C%20_amount%2C%20_extraData)%3B

Malicious user could call this function with small amount and specify the large extra data. Since the lockbox call the xcontract call, it pays gas for the transaction sent.

When the extra data is big, the gas incurred could be also high.

## Tools Used

Manual review, blast bridge code flow.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

We suggest to restrict and have check on the extra data size.
