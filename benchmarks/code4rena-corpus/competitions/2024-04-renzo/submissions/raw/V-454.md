# Vulnerability details

## Impact
[DelegationManager.undelegate()](https://github.com/Layr-Labs/eigenlayer-contracts/blob/15b679b08e7a3589ff83a5e84076ca4e0a00ec0b/src/contracts/core/DelegationManager.sol#L211) can be called by a staker to undelegate themselves, or by a staker's delegated operator, where the staker here is the Renzo `OperatorDelegator`. Undelegation lead to [queue withdrawals](https://github.com/Layr-Labs/eigenlayer-contracts/blob/15b679b08e7a3589ff83a5e84076ca4e0a00ec0b/src/contracts/core/DelegationManager.sol#L678) on behalf of the staker for all their delegated shares. They are removed from the StrategyManager while the withdrawal is in the queue. After a delay period, this withdrawal can be completed by the staker by calling the `completeQueueWithdrawal` on `DelegationManager`. 

In Renzo, generally the nativeEthRestakeAdmin calls the `OperatorDelegator.completeQueuedWithdrawal()` function to complete any queued withdrawals. However, the issue is that this function on `OperatorDelegator` is only eligible to complete withdrawals queued from within Renzo. Any withdrawal queued outside of Renzo cannot be completed.

As a result, there will be a significant drop in Renzo's total TVL. The exchange rate, which is dependent on the total TVL, will be further reduced, directly affecting any withdrawal of LRT tokens via the `WithdrawQueue`.

## Proof of Concept
A mapping `queuedShares` is used to [add](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Delegation/OperatorDelegator.sol#L232)/[subtract](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Delegation/OperatorDelegator.sol#L281) respective tokenAmount during queueWithdrawals/completeQueuedWithdrawal, 

```solidity
abstract contract OperatorDelegatorStorageV4 is OperatorDelegatorStorageV3 {
    /// @dev mapping of token shares in withdraw queue of EigenLayer
    mapping(address => uint256) public queuedShares;
}
```

if withdrawal get queued outside the Renzo, the `queuedShares` mapping will left empty for the respective token. And when the nativeEthRestakeAdmin try to `completeQueuedWithdrawal` on `OperatorDelegator` its reverts, since its trying to deduct the `queuedShares` which never got added via the OperatorDelegator. 

```solidty
            // deduct queued shares for tracking TVL
            queuedShares[address(tokens[i])] -= withdrawal.shares[i];
```

The subtraction above will underflow, the `completeQueuedWithdrawal` txn will revert. The nativeEthRestakeAdmin unable to withdraw their stuck funds on the Eigenlayer strategies.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
If it possible anyhow to sync the `queuedShares` with the amount actually queued on EigenLayer protocol, the sync method can be added to OperatorDelegator. If not, we can add a check to deduct queued shares only when `queuedShares[address(tokens[i])] > 0 && queuedShares[address(tokens[i])] > withdrawal.shares[i]`.
