# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The `deposit()` function in the `xRenzoDeposit` contract does not have any explicit slippage protection mechanisms. This leaves users vulnerable to front-running attacks, where an attacker can monitor the mempool and insert their own transactions before and after a user's transaction to manipulate the market price.

The lack of slippage protection can result in users receiving significantly less than the expected amount of xezETH when depositing, or paying more than expected when withdrawing. This can lead to substantial financial losses for users, eroding trust in the protocol and discouraging adoption.


## Proof of Concept
The attacker observes Alice, the victim, attempting to deposit ETH into the protocol using the `deposit()` function.

The attacker quickly submits their own transaction to swap ETH for nextWETH, manipulating the market price before Alice's transaction is executed.

When Alice's transaction is executed, she receives a lower amount of xezETH than expected due to the price change caused by the attacker's front-running transaction.

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Allow users to specify the minimum output amount and revert the transaction if it is not satisfied.
