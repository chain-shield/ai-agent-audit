# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In `OperatorDelegator.sol`, `stakedButNotVerifiedEth` is used to track how much ETH has been sent to the Deposit contract but has not yet been verified by EigenPod. 

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Delegation/OperatorDelegator.sol#L358

The function `verifyWithdrawalCredentials` deducts the effective balance of validators from `stakedButNotVerifiedEth`, typically 32 ETH. 

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Delegation/OperatorDelegator.sol#L381-L389

However, this function does not account for the possibility that a validator's effective balance might be reduced below 32 ETH due to penalties, leading to an inflated calculation of the Total Value Locked (TVL).

## Proof of Concept
1. Assume that 32 ETH are deposited into the Deposit contract, setting `stakedButNotVerifiedEth` to 32 ETH.
2. A validator is penalized by the consensus mechanism, reducing their effective balance to 30 ETH.
3. When updated via `verifyWithdrawalCredentials`, only 30 ETH which is the validator's effective balance are deducted from `stakedButNotVerifiedEth`.
4. As a result, `getStakedETHBalance` function would incorrectly return 30 ETH (Pod Owner Shares) + 2 ETH (stakedButNotVerifiedEth), instead of 30 ETH.

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Delegation/OperatorDelegator.sol#L338-L345

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
In the `verifyWithdrawalCredentials` function, `stakedButNotVerifiedEth` should be deducted by 32 ETH instead of the effective balance.
