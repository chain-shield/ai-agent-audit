# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In `RestakeManager.sol`, when users deposit LST collateral, the protocol first uses this collateral to fill any deficiencies in the `WithdrawQueue`. Only the remaining amount is then deposited into the `OperatorDelegator` contract.

When the deficit in the `WithdrawQueue` is greater than or equal to the amount deposited by the user, the subsequent amount to be deposited into `OperatorDelegator` is reduced to zero. However, the `OperatorDelegator` contract performs a check on the deposit amount, and if it is zero, an `InvalidZeroInput` error is thrown, causing the transaction to fail.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
It is recommended to check whether the amount is zero before executing the deposit function in `OperatorDelegator`.
