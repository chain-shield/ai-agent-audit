## Impact

xzETH will not be minted correctly as intended, therefore causing a precision loss in return of xzETH to the user.

## Proof of Concept
In the depositETH function, there is no price calculation as mentioned in the [inline](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Bridge/L2/xRenzoDeposit.sol#L162) comment and documentation.

Price calculation missing in the function logic and cal cause precision loss or wrong amount of xzETH.

Spans across, [deposiETH](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Bridge/L2/xRenzoDeposit.sol#L168) and [deposit](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Bridge/L2/xRenzoDeposit.sol#L204)

## Tools Used
Manual review, Github lens

Include the price calculation for the two functions.
