# Vulnerability details


## Impact
There's a problem with the gas refund functionality in the `OperatorDelegator` contract when the `NativeEthRestakeAdmin` is a contract account.
The `_recordGas` function is using `msg.sender` to track gas spending.
But `tx.origin` is used to refund gas in the `_refundGas` function.
So when the admin is a contract account (`msg.sender` will be different from `tx.origin`), the gas refund won't work.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
To fix this issue, it's recommended to use the `tx.origin` instead of `msg.sender` to track gas spending.
