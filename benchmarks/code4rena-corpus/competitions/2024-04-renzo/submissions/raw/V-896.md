# Vulnerability details

The `setOperatorDelegatorAllocation` and `addOperatorDelegator` functions do not enforce that the sum of allocations for all OperatorDelegators remains at or below 100%. This oversight could lead to a total allocation exceeding 100%, affecting the protocol's fund distribution and management.

## Impact
An over-allocation can have several consequences:

- **Imbalanced Fund Distribution**: The protocol may not distribute deposits and withdrawals proportionally, leading to some OperatorDelegators handling more funds than their capacity or intended share.
- **Risk Management Issues**: Concentrating too much capital with certain OperatorDelegators could increase systemic risk if those entities fail to manage the funds effectively.
- **Operational Inefficiencies**: The selection process for depositing and withdrawing may become biased, causing operational challenges and affecting liquidity management.

The functions `chooseOperatorDelegatorForDeposit` and `chooseOperatorDelegatorForWithdraw` rely on accurate allocation percentages to make informed decisions. Over-allocation could lead to certain OperatorDelegators being favored or burdened, impacting the protocol's overall functionality.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Modify both `addOperatorDelegator` and `setOperatorDelegatorAllocation` functions to include a check that validates the total allocations do not exceed 100%. This could involve keeping a running total of all allocations and comparing against it whenever a new allocation is added or an existing one is modified.

Example check to include in both functions:

```solidity
uint256 totalAllocations = 0;
for (uint256 i = 0; i < operatorDelegators.length; i++) {
    totalAllocations += operatorDelegatorAllocations[operatorDelegators[i]];
}
totalAllocations += _allocationBasisPoints; // Include the new or updated allocation
require(totalAllocations <= 100 * BASIS_POINTS, "Total allocations exceed 100%");
```

Implementing such a check will prevent the total allocation from exceeding 100%, ensuring that the protocol operates within the intended parameters and maintains a balanced and fair distribution of funds across all OperatorDelegators.
