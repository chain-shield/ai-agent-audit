# Vulnerability details

## Vulnerability Detail
The protocol's accrued rewards are sent to the `RewardHandler` contract.

This will end up calling the `RewardHandler::receive()` function which in turn calls `_forwardETH()`, which forwards the ETH to the DepositQueue.

This will instantly increase the total TVL of the protocol, since the balance of the deposit queue is included in [`RestakeManager::calculateTVL()`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/1c7cc4e632564349b204b4b5e5f494c9b0bc631d/contracts/RestakeManager.sol#L352)

An attacker can take advantage of this to partially steal the ETH rewards using the following steps:

1. Attacker identifies an incoming rewards Tx that will increase the protocol's TVL
2. Attacker frontruns the rewards Tx by minting ezETH at the current TVL level
3. Rewards Tx goes through and increases the TVL of the protocol
4. Attacker backruns the rewards Tx by redeeming all their minted ezETH at the higher exchange rate (since TVL increased), obtaining ether (or LSTs) at a confirmed profit

Consensus layer rewards will be credited to the `RewardHandler` contract without a transaction, so the `NativeEthRestakeAdmin` will have to manually call `forwardRewards()` to send the ETH to the DepositQueue. This call by the `NativeEthRestakeAdmin` will increase the protocol's TVL, therefore it is also vulnerable to the previously described sandwich attack.

## Impact
A portion of the ETH rewards can be stolen from honest users with 0 risk. The attacker makes profit while reducing the yield of honest stakers.

This significantly reduces the incentive for honest users to stake their funds for continuous periods since any non-participant can simply front-run the forwarding of rewards from `RewardHandler` to `DepositQueue` and steal rewards.
