# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Since rewards from EigenLayer are calculated based on shares issued to the operator Delegator, users reward will be diluted because some funds are left dormant and idle in the WithdrawalQueue contrary to the aim of the protocol’s promised utility to users. Hence the protocol does not delivery promised returns.

## Proof of Concept
When a deposits into the Renzo protocol through `RestakeManager::deposit(...)` function, all funds are not immediately forwarded to the Eigenlayer `strategy` . The protocol first fills the `WithdrawalQueue` up to the `withdrawalBufferTarget` and until this target amount of funds is transferred from the `DeposiitQueue` to the `WithdrawalQueue` is met, no funds are transferred to the  `strategy` through the `OperatorDelegator` [and no `strategy` shares are minted to the `OperatorDelegator`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Delegation/OperatorDelegator.sol#L167-L168) while this is going on.

This does not happen to only first deposits but it is also possible when the `WithdrawalQueue` is empty and deposits are queued from EigenLayer.

Notice from `L543` that the function checks if the `WithdrawalQueue` has filled its  `withdrawalBufferTarget` and then proceeds to reduce the original amount user sent by the `bufferToFill` on `L549`. To understand the issue better, consider the example below

### Scenario

For simplicity, I assume there is one `_collateralToken` for use in the system

- `withdrawalBufferTarget` = 100;
- current balance of `_collateralToken` in the `WithdrawalQueue` = 0
- `OperatorDelegator` shares from `strategy` = ~0

- Bob deposits with `RestakeManager::deposit(...)` 40 ether of `_collateralToken` and recieves 40 ezETH
- current balance of `_collateralToken` in the `WithdrawalQueue` = 40 ether
- `OperatorDelegator` send 0 ether and recieves ~0 `strategy` share

- Alice deposits 50 ether of `_collateralToken` and recieves 50 ezETH
- current balance of `_collateralToken` in the `WithdrawalQueue` = 90 ether
- `OperatorDelegator` send 0 ether and recieves ~0 `strategy` share

- Tim deposits 50 ether of `_collateralToken` and recieves 50 ezETH
- current balance of `_collateralToken` in the `WithdrawalQueue` = 100 ether
- `OperatorDelegator` send 40 ether and recieves ~40 `strategy` shares

`totalTVL` = 140 ETH

Notice that despite the protocol having 140 ETH, only 40 ether of `_collateralToken` is put to use in the strategy and it is clear from [`L115` of `EigenLayer.StrategyBase.sol`](https://github.com/Layr-Labs/eigenlayer-contracts/blob/ef2ea4a7459884f381057aa9bbcd29c7148cfb63/src/contracts/strategies/StrategyBase.sol#L115) that the shares  received by a depositor are base on the total supply of assets in the strategy  so in the case of Renzo on 40 ETH worth of collateral tokens will be put to use with the remaining 100 ETH equivalent left in the `WithdrawalQueue`

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
- Modify the `RestakeManager::deposit(...)` function so that funds are sent from the `DepositPool` to the `WithdrawalQueue` only if there is a pending withdrawal _from the protocol_ that has not been queued in EigenLayer already
- Also consider modifying the `RestakeManager::deposit(...)` function as shown below
