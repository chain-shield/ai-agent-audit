# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Depositors may earn more or less profit than expected. Even some profits will be locked in contract and nobody can claim them.

## Proof of Concept
In WithdrawQueue::withdraw(), function will calculate the share's price via `calculateRedeemAmount`. The vulnerability is that total shares `ezETH.totalSupply()` include shares which have already requested withdrawn but not claimed. These shares' price has already calculated and is fixed. If some newly rewards are received, these shares(already request withdraw and not claimed yet) should not be distributed any rewards.

For example, 
- Share's price is 1.
- Alice buys one share with 1 collateral token.
- Bob buys one share with 1 collateral token.
- Cathy buys one share with 1 collateral token.
- Alice requests withdraw and not claim.
- 3 unit rewards are received by the contract. Considering Alice has already requested withdraw, and her claim amount has saved into `claimReserve`. Newly rewards should be distributed to Bob and Cathy. The expected share price is 2.5. But the actual price is 2.
- Bob withdraw and claim 2 collateral token after the cooldown period
- Cathy withdraws and claims 2 collateral token after the cooldown period.
- Alice claim her 1 collateral token.
- 1 reward collateral token is locked in the contract, does not belong to any depositor.
