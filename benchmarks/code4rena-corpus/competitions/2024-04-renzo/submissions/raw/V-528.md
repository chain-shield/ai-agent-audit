# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The `completeQueuedWithdrawal` function only executes for non-native tokens, while native tokens are excluded. However, when deducting queued shares for tracking Total Value Locked (TVL) --  queuedShares[address(tokens[i])]---, the function fails to check or top up the buffer for native tokens. This oversight creates a potential Denial of Service (DOS) scenario when users attempt to withdraw native tokens, as the buffer may not be adequately maintained.

## Proof of Concept
After deducting shares of native tokens, the buffer is not checked or topped up for native tokens, potentially causing a DOS when users attempt to withdraw native tokens.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Consider implementing support for native tokens in the `completeQueuedWithdrawal` function to ensure that the buffer is adequately checked and topped up for native tokens as well. This mitigation measure will prevent potential DOS scenarios when users withdraw native tokens and ensure the stability and reliability of the withdrawal process.
