# Vulnerability details

## Impact
`ezETH` can only be burned when the `paused` flag is set to `true`

- `ezETH` will not be burned from users who make claim their `collateralTokens` from the  `WithdrawalQueue::claim(...)` leading to
    - reentrancy as users can claim multiple times without until the protocol is drained
    - overflow of ezETH `totaSupply`
- this breaks protocol functionality as tokens cannot be minted when the token contract is not paused.

## Proof of Concept
When [`EzETHToken::burn(...)`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/token/EzEthToken.sol#L46-L48) is called to burn ezETH from a user after claiming their `collateralTokens` from the `WithdrawalQueue` contract, the function makes an internal calls to the `ERC20Upgradable::_burn(...)` function which in turn calls the [`EzETHToken::_beforeTokenTransfer(...)`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/token/EzEthToken.sol#L56-L72)` hook.

The problem is that the `EzETHToken::_beforeTokenTransfer(...)` hooks returns and ends execution on `L67` of the `EzETHToken.sol` contract (shown below) when the `EzETHToken.sol` contract is NOT paused without [burning tokens from the user](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/RestakeManager.sol#L571-L572) who has made a deposit

This is contrary to normal behaviour when functions are restricted only when a contract is paused.

`EzETHToken::burn(...)` is used to ezETH token from users who claim their `collateralTokens` from the `WithdrawalQueue` contract and as such users can re-eneter the `WithdrawalQueue::claim(...)` function and drain funds leading to a loss of funds as the burn function will be called without decreasing the balance of the `account` claiming their collateral.

The sponsor makes it clear that only transfers are paused initially, not burning and minting

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Modify the `EzETHToken::_beforeTokenTransfer(...)`  function as shown below

