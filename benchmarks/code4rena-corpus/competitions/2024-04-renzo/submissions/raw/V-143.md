# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Without slippage protection, fewer shares than expected when depositing tokens could be received due to price fluctuations during token swaps.
there could be unexpected losses or receive inadequate shares,

Slippage protection ensures that desired shares are received based on the deposit amount, even in the presence of price fluctuations. This provides users with confidence in the protocol's reliability and predictability.

By setting a slippage tolerance, users can limit the potential losses caused by significant price impacts during token swaps. This protection mechanism reduces the risk of receiving inadequate shares due to unexpected market conditions.

 function deposit(
        IERC20 token,
        uint256 tokenAmount
    ) external nonReentrant onlyRestakeManager returns (uint256 shares) {
        if (address(tokenStrategyMapping[token]) == address(0x0) || tokenAmount == 0)
            revert InvalidZeroInput();

        // Move the tokens into this contract
        token.safeTransferFrom(msg.sender, address(this), tokenAmount);

        return _deposit(token, tokenAmount);
    }
