# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The function attempts to calculate the fee transferred by measuring the contract's balance before and after calling withdraw on the WETH contract. However, this approach assumes that no other transactions affecting the contract's balance will occur concurrently. This could lead to incorrect feeCollected calculations if other functions that modify the contract's balance are called concurrently (race condition).
