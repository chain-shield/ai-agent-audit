# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The primary impact of the concern is the potential for unauthorized manipulation of sensitive functions within the `XERC20` contract. By transferring ownership to the deployer wallet (`msg.sender`), the vulnerability allows the deployer to impersonate validated senders and execute functions intended to be restricted to specific addresses. This could lead to financial losses, reputation damage, and privacy violations, posing significant risks to the security and integrity of the contract and its ecosystem.
## Proof of Concept
The vulnerability arises due to the transfer of ownership of the `XERC20` contract to the deployer wallet (`msg.sender`) within the `_deployXERC20` function of the `XERC20Factory` contract. This transfer of ownership grants the deployer full control over the `XERC20` contract, including the ability to execute functions on behalf of other addresses that are typically validated based on ownership or authorization.
Within the `_deployXERC20` function, after deploying the `XERC20` contract, the ownership is transferred to the deployer wallet (`msg.sender`) using the `transferOwnership` function:
```solidity
XERC20(_xerc20).transferOwnership(msg.sender);
```
If the `XERC20` contract contains functions that validate the sender based on ownership or authorization, such as `onlyOwner` modifiers or sender address checks, these validations may be bypassed by the deployer wallet (`msg.sender`) after the ownership transfer.
After the ownership transfer, the deployer wallet can impersonate any validated sender by directly calling the relevant functions of the `XERC20` contract. This allows the deployer to bypass any sender validation checks implemented within the `XERC20` contract.

Manual
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
 If ownership transfer is necessary, ensure that it is conducted securely and with appropriate checks to prevent unauthorized access post-transfer. Also, Implement robust access control mechanisms within the `XERC20` contract to ensure that sensitive functions can only be executed by authorized addresses.
