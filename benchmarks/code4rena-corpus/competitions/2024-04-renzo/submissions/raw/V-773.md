# Vulnerability details

## Impact
`WithdrawalQueue::withdraw(...)` can be bricked, 

- preventing users from queueing and claiming withdrawals
- users funds stuck will be stuck

## Proof of Concept
Currently, users have to wait for a `cooldown` period to elapse after calling `WithdrawalQueue::withdraw(...)` to withdraw their asset from the protocol by calling  `WithdrawalQueue::claim(...)`. However **due to wrong implementation of checks-effect-interactions** in the `WithdrawalQueue::withdraw(...)` function,  users who are yet to start their cooldown period will not be able to call `WithdrawalQueue::withdraw(...)` and some users  may not be able to [`WithdrawalQueue::claim(...)`]() whether or not  there are enough asset in the withdrawal queue.

The problem exist because available balance is checked against stale `claimReserve[_asset]`before  [updating the `claimReserve[_asset]`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Withdraw/WithdrawQueue.sol#L253) to include the current withdrawal value hence not taking into consideration whether the current [available balance](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Withdraw/WithdrawQueue.sol#L236) of the withdrawal queue can contain amount that is being queued to be claimed after cooldown period

- Assume size of `withdrawalBufferTarget[asset]` was set to 100 ether
- No user has called `WithdrawalQueue::withdraw(...)`  hence `claimReserve[_asset]` = 0
- current balance `asset` in withdrawalQueue = 10 ether
- BOB calls `WithdrawalQueue::withdraw(...)` to withdraw 10 ether, an external call is made to `getAvailableToWithdraw(..)` while  [`claimReserve[_asset]`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Withdraw/WithdrawQueue.sol#L253) = 0
- the call proceeds to increase the `claimReserve[_asset]` = 10 ether as seen on `L253` above
- Alice a heavily invested user calls `WithdrawalQueue::withdraw(...)` to withdraw 110 ether, an external call is made to `getAvailableToWithdraw(..)` while  `claimReserve[_asset]` = 10 ether
- the call proceeds to increase the `claimReserve[_asset]` = 120 ether as seen on `L253` above
- after cooldown period, bob calls [`WithdrawalQueue::claim(...)`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Withdraw/WithdrawQueue.sol#L279) success fully and `claimReserve[_asset]` = 110 ether
- ALICE calls  `WithdrawalQueue::claim(...)`  it [reverts](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/519e518f2d8dec9acf6482b84a181e403070d22d/contracts/Withdraw/WithdrawQueue.sol#L305-L307) because balance is not enough
- `withdrawalBufferTarget[asset]` is now filled up to 100 ether
- TIM attempts to call `WithdrawalQueue::withdraw(...)`  for 10 ether but it reverts because `L159` reverts (since 100 - 110 is not possible)
