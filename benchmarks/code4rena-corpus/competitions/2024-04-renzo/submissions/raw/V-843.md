# Vulnerability details

## Description
Users which interact with the protocol are not enable to specify a min amount of EzETH to mint when they deposit collateral tokens or ETH, in case of frontrunning they can receive less ezETH than they want.

## Impact
Lack of slippage when minting ezETH can lead to users receiving less ezETH than they want.

## POC
Functions `RestakeManager.deposit(IERC20,uint256)`;`RestakeManager.deposit(IERC20,uint256,uint256)`; `RestakeManager.depositETH()`; `RestakeManager.depositETH(uint256)` do not allow users to specify a min amount of EzETH to mint when they deposit collateral tokens or ETH.

## Recommended mitigation
Allow users to specify min EzETH to mint when they call when calling `RestakeManager.deposit(IERC20,uint256)`;`RestakeManager.deposit(IERC20,uint256,uint256)`; `RestakeManager.depositETH()`; `RestakeManager.depositETH(uint256)`
