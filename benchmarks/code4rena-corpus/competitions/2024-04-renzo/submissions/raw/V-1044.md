# Vulnerability details

## Impact

`xRenzoDeposit` claims too much or too litle fees from the users when they deposit.

## Proof of Concept

`xRenzoDeposit::depositETH()` calculates the connext fee based on the hardcoded `bridgeRouterFeeBps` of `5 bps`. However, it may change as seen in the connext code [here](https://github.com/connext/monorepo/blob/main/packages/deployments/contracts/contracts/core/connext/facets/RoutersFacet.sol#L301-L312). This will under or overcharge users for their deposits.

## Tools Used

Vscode

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Get the fee from connext at real time, [here](https://github.com/connext/monorepo/blob/main/packages/deployments/contracts/contracts/core/connext/facets/RoutersFacet.sol#L176-L178).
