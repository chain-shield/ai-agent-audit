# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The inability to directly access the `totalSupply` function of the `EzEthToken` contract within the `BalancerRateProvider` contract could lead to inaccurate rate calculations. This could, in turn, affect the functionality of any systems or applications that rely on these rates for operations such as staking, rewards distribution, or governance voting.

## Proof of Concept
The `BalancerRateProvider` contract, as implemented, lacks a direct method to interact with the `EzEthToken` contract to fetch the total supply of tokens. This omission prevents the contract from accurately calculating the rate of `ezETH` in terms of ETH, which is crucial for its operation.

The issue arises in the `BalancerRateProvider` contract, specifically in the `BalancerRateProvider::getRate` function. The contract attempts to calculate the rate of `ezETH` in ETH but fails to fetch the total supply of `ezETH` tokens from the `EzEthToken` contract. This is evident in the provided code snippet:


To illustrate the concept, consider a scenario where the `BalancerRateProvider` contract is used in a DeFi application for staking `ezETH` tokens. Without the ability to accurately calculate the rate of `ezETH` in ETH, users might receive incorrect staking rewards or face other operational issues.
