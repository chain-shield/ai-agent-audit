# Vulnerability details

## Impact
@dev Comments talk about the lack of slippage protection. This will lead to users losing funds when atackers manipulate transactions or swaps. It is best practice to have slippage protection. Lackof slippage protection will become an issue down the line. Best to protect users from financial losses and attackers.
