# Vulnerability details

## Impact

The `TVL` is calculated incorrectly, resulting loss of funds for some users.

## Proof of Concept

After all `operatorDelegators` are removed, `operatorDelegators.length` will be 0. Then, `totalWithdrawalQueueValue` is not calculated.
However, there would be some collateral tokens left in the `withdrawlQueue`. This leads to incorrect calculation of `TVL` and loss of funds for users.

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/main/contracts/RestakeManager.sol#L274-L358
