# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The lack of implementation for earning rewards undermines the incentive structure of the system. Users who deposit native ETH expect to receive rewards for contributing to securing the network and participating in EigenLayer. Without a mechanism to claim rewards, users may lose out on potential earnings, reducing participation and engagement with the platform. Additionally, this could lead to dissatisfaction among users and diminish the attractiveness of the platform.

## Proof of Concept
The `OperatorDelegator` contract lacks implementation for earning both Ethereum validator rewards and EigenLayer AVS rewards as described in the documentation. Although the contract facilitates depositing and staking native ETH in EigenLayer through the `stakeEth` function, there is no explicit mechanism to claim rewards generated from Ethereum validator activities or EigenLayer AVS rewards.

This vulnerability is reflected in the absence of functions or logic to handle reward distribution or interaction with reward mechanisms on both Ethereum's Beacon chain and EigenLayer.
## Tools Used
Manual
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
The contract needs to incorporate functionality to claim and distribute rewards earned from Ethereum validator activities and EigenLayer AVS rewards. This can be achieved by implementing mechanisms to periodically collect rewards from the Ethereum Beacon chain and EigenLayer, calculate the rewards earned by each user based on their contribution, and distribute the rewards accordingly.
