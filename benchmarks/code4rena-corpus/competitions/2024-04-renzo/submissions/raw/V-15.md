# Vulnerability details

There will be a guarantee of stale price due to the hardcoded `uint256 constant MAX_TIME_WINDOW = 86400 + 60; // 24 hours + 60 seconds`. This mean that pricefeed checks only if the pricefeed is updated 1 day ago instead of the respective token's heartbeat duration.

The heartbeat duration for [STETH/ETH is 24 hour](https://data.chain.link/feeds/ethereum/mainnet/steth-eth)
The heartbeat duration for [BETH/USD is 15 Minutes](https://data.chain.link/feeds/bsc/mainnet/beth-usd)
The heartbeat duration for [ezETH/ETH is 24 hour](https://data.chain.link/feeds/ethereum/mainnet/ezeth-eth)


The staled price from [bots-automation](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/main/4naly3er-report.md#m-6-chainlinks-latestrounddata-might-return-stale-or-incorrect-results) reasoning from stale price is completely different from my report, hence the submission of issue.

## Impact
Staled data due to wrong update timestamp check.

## Proof of Concept
```solidity
        if (timestamp < block.timestamp - MAX_TIME_WINDOW) revert OraclePriceExpired();
```

## Tools Used
Manual Review
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Ensure that latest heartbeat duration is based on token's actual heartbeat from Chainlink.
