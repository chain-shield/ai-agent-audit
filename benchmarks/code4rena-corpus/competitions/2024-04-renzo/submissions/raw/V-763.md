# Vulnerability details

## Description
During the deposit process, the `deposit` function emits a `Deposit` event. It begins by checking if the `withdrawQueue` buffer for the collateral needs to be filled. If so, it populates it with the `_amount` of collateral deposited by the user. Any surplus collateral is then transferred to an `operatorDelegator`. Subsequently, the `Deposit` event is emitted, indicating the `_amount` of collateral transferred to the `operatorDelegator`. However, this event may be misleading as it signifies the `_amount` transferred to the `operatorDelegator`, not the total amount deposited by the user. This discrepancy could cause confusion for users and off-chain systems relying on this event to track deposits.

## Impact
This issue may lead to confusion for users and off-chain systems relying on this event to track deposits. The event may mislead as it reflects the `_amount` transferred to the `operatorDelegator`, rather than the total amount deposited by the user. It's noteworthy that in the worst-case scenario, if the `bufferToFill` exceeds the `_amount`, there will be a zero token approval and transfer to the `operatorDelegator`, resulting in the `Deposit` event being emitted with an `_amount` of zero.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
It is advisable to update the `Deposit` event to reflect the total amount deposited by the user, instead of the amount transferred to the `operatorDelegator`. This adjustment will help mitigate confusion for users and off-chain systems relying on this event to track deposits.
