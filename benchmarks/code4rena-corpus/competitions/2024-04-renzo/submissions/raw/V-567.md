# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The `lookupTokenValue` function in the `RenzoOracle` contract does not include any explicit validation to check if the rollup sequencer is running. If the rollup sequencer goes offline, it could lead to stale prices being used by the protocol, potentially resulting in mispricing of assets and indirect risk to funds.

If the rollup sequencer is offline and stale prices are used, it can lead to mispricing of assets and indirect risk to funds. The impact of this vulnerability can manifest in several ways:

Mispricing of assets: If the protocol relies on stale prices, it may lead to incorrect valuation of assets, causing users to make suboptimal trading or investment decisions.

Indirect financial losses: While the funds may not be directly at risk, users who interact with the protocol based on stale prices may suffer indirect financial losses due to missed opportunities or unfavorable trades.

Reduced liquidity: If users lose confidence in the protocol due to the use of stale prices, it may result in reduced liquidity and trading activity on the platform.

## Proof of Concept
The rollup sequencer responsible for updating the prices in the RenzoOracle contract experiences an unexpected downtime due to technical issues or maintenance.

During the downtime, the prices in the RenzoOracle contract become stale and no longer reflect the current market conditions.

Bob, an attacker, notices the discrepancy between the stale prices in the protocol and the actual market prices on other platforms.

Bob takes advantage of the situation by buying assets from the protocol at the lower stale prices.

Bob then immediately sells those assets on external markets at the higher current market prices, profiting from the price difference.

Alice, an honest user, also interacts with the protocol during the period of stale prices.

Alice makes trading decisions based on the stale prices, unaware that they do not reflect the current market conditions.

As a result, Alice suffers indirect financial losses due to missed opportunities or unfavorable trades executed at the stale prices.

The protocol itself may experience reduced trading activity and liquidity as users lose confidence in the accuracy of the prices provided by the RenzoOracle contract.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Implement a mechanism to detect and handle situations where the rollup sequencer is offline. This could include using backup price feeds, implementing circuit breakers to pause the protocol when stale prices are detected, or using time-based price validity checks to ensure prices are not older than a certain threshold.
