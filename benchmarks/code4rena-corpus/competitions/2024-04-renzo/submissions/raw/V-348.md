# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Using [transfer](https://consensys.io/diligence/blog/2019/09/stop-using-soliditys-transfer-now/) can lead to unexpected reverts due to `out-of-gas` errors if the recipient contract's fallback function consumes more gas than the stipend. This could lock funds or disrupt contract interactions, impacting user experience and trust.
