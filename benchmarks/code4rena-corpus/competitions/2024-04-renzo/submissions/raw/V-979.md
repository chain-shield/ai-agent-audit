# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Protocol insolvency due to not being able to withdraw ETH sent to `DepositQueue` from new users after the deposit count is reached in the Beacon Deposit Contract.

## Proof of Concept

Users mint ezETH through `RestakeManager::depositETH()`, sending the `ETH` to `DepositQueue` to be staked later. However, this does not take into account that the deposit count in the Beacon Deposit Contract may have been reached and staking in the `DepositQueue` will be impossible. As it is currently not possible to withdraw ETH directly from `DepositQueue`, the funds will be stuck, unredeemable. Beacon Deposit contract has this check on [line](https://vscode.blockscan.com/ethereum/0x00000000219ab540356cbb839cbe05303d7705fa#L143) 143.

## Tools Used

Vscode

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Disable `RestakeManager::depositETH()` when the deposit cap is reached.
