# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The protocol does not have any specific mechanisms to address potential inaccuracies in the price feed during flash crash events. The protocol relies on the Chainlink oracle and the price feed received from the xRenzoBridgeL1 contract, but there are no apparent measures to handle the case where these sources return inaccurate prices due to a flash crash. 

During a flash crash event, the price feeds used by the protocol may return inaccurate or unreliable prices. This could lead to the minting of xezETH tokens at incorrect exchange rates, potentially resulting in significant financial losses for users.

For example, if a flash crash causes the price of ezETH to plummet temporarily, the protocol may mint xezETH tokens at an artificially low exchange rate, allowing users to deposit a smaller amount of assets and receive an inflated amount of xezETH tokens. This could be exploited by malicious actors to steal funds from other users.

Additionally, the lack of handling for flash crash inaccuracies could also disrupt the overall functionality of the protocol, as the price feed is a critical component for various operations, such as deposits, withdrawals, and bridging of assets.

A good place to implement checks would be in `RenzoOracleL2::getMintRate`

## Proof of Concept
A flash crash event occurs, causing the price of ezETH to drop significantly.

The Chainlink oracle and the xRenzoBridgeL1 contract reflect this sudden price drop, but the protocol does not have any measures to handle the inaccurate price data.

A user deposits assets into the `xRenzoDeposit.sol` contract, and the protocol mints xezETH tokens based on the artificially low exchange rate.

The user can then withdraw the inflated amount of xezETH tokens, effectively stealing funds from other users who deposited at the correct, pre-crash exchange rate.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Implement checks to ensure that the price returned by the oracle lies within an expected range to guard against potential flash crash vulnerabilities.
