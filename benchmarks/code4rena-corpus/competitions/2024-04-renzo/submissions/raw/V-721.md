# Vulnerability details


## Cause

The [operator that is delegated to by the `OperatorDelegator` can call `undelegate` for the staker](https://github.com/Layr-Labs/eigenlayer-contracts/blob/dev/src/contracts/core/DelegationManager.sol#L211-L221) (Renzo's `OperatorDelegator`) in EigenLayer's `DelegationManager`. If that happens, EigenLayer [removes the shares and starts a **full withdrawal** for the staker](https://github.com/Layr-Labs/eigenlayer-contracts/blob/dev/src/contracts/core/DelegationManager.sol#L247-L253). Crucially, since this action was not done via the `OperatorDelegator`, the shares [were not tracked in its `queuedShares`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/main/contracts/Delegation/OperatorDelegator.sol#L232). As a result, the withdrawal cannot be claimed and is lost. This is because[ `queuedShares` must be subtracted during claiming for each withdrawal](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/main/contracts/Delegation/OperatorDelegator.sol#L281), and in this case, the withdrawal did not previously increment `queuedShares` for the full value of the funds deposited by this OperatorDelegator into EigenLayer. 

## Impact

Notably, in EigenLayer, an operator's undelegation cannot cause any loss of funds and is a safety mechanism. However, in this case, if this safety mechanism is triggered, it will cause a catastrophic loss of funds to Renzo due to the unclaimable withdrawal.

Both the [operator and their nominated `delegationApprover` address can trigger the undelegation](https://github.com/Layr-Labs/eigenlayer-contracts/blob/dev/src/contracts/core/DelegationManager.sol#L218-L219). They may decide to do so in case of an emergency in the course of incident management, due an issue with an AVS they are validating, or due to an operational mistake. For example, if there is a suspected exploit of the operator itself or an AVS they are validating, they have the `delegationApprover` keys to be able to undelegate their stakers and protect their funds. Additionally, the EOA assigned to `delegationApprover` is not explicitly trusted and may also be compromised, possibly calling `undelegate` maliciously.

Several direct losses are caused by this issue:

1. Because `queuedShares` were not incremented for the full amount of the staked funds, subtracting will underflow so withdrawal cannot be completed, causing loss of the entire staking balance. As the withdrawal [must only be completed by the assigned withdrawer](https://github.com/Layr-Labs/eigenlayer-contracts/blob/dev/src/contracts/core/DelegationManager.sol#L574-L577), which is the `OperatorDelegator`, the withdrawal becomes unrecoverable. Currently, Renzo uses a single OperatorDelegator, so almost the entire 3B TVL will be at risk of being lost due a single call to `undelegate` after this upgrade.
2. The drastic reduction in shares, without a corresponding increase of `queuedShares`, [causes a massive TVL change](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/main/contracts/RestakeManager.sol#L302-L304), and corresponding ezETH price change, which is frontrunnable by borrowing, selling via the [Balancer pool](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/main/contracts/RateProvider/BalancerRateProvider.sol#L31), and buying after the price update.
3. Because `queuedShares` were not incremented, if the operator's ETH is also slashed at the same time and the owner shares balance becomes negative,[ `calculateTVLs` will permanently](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/main/contracts/RestakeManager.sol#L329) revert [when calling the `OperatorDelegator`'s `getStakedETHBalance`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-04-renzo/blob/main/contracts/Delegation/OperatorDelegator.sol#L343), which will DoS deposits, withdrawals, and Balancer trades.

## Proof of Concept

1. The operator (or their `delegationApprover`), to which `OperatorDelegator` has delegated, calls `undelegate` in an emergency to protect their stakers.
2. An unclaimable withdrawal of the full `OperatorDelegator`'s TVL is done, reducing its shares to 0 and causing a catastrophic loss of funds.
3. The withdrawal of the full TVL is lost and cannot be claimed by `OperatorDelegator` due to `queuedShares` not having been incremented previously.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Allow for an emergency method that can increment `queuedShares` for claiming a withdrawal that was not triggered by `OperatorDelegator`:

```diff
+	function emergencyIncreaseQueuedShares(
+		address[] calldata assets,
+	    uint256[] calldata addedShares
+	) external onlyOperatorDelegatorAdmin {
+		if (assets.length != addedShares.length) revert MismatchedArrayLengths();
+		for (uint256 i; i < assets.length; ++i) {
+			queuedShares[assets[i]] += addedShares[i];
+		}
+	}
