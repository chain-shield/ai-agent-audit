# Vulnerability details

The `chooseOperatorDelegatorForDeposit` and `chooseOperatorDelegatorForWithdraw` functions in `RestakeManager` incorrectly calculates the allocation threshold by dividing by `BASIS_POINTS` twice, which should only be divided once.

## Impact
This error could lead to the consistent selection of the first OperatorDelegator in the list, regardless of their actual TVL compared to their target allocation. This could result in an imbalance in the distribution of deposits and withdrawals across OperatorDelegators.

## Proof of Concept
The issue is located in the `chooseOperatorDelegatorForDeposit` and `chooseOperatorDelegatorForWithdraw` functions within the `RestakeManager` contract. The relevant code snippet is:

A numerical example is provided to illustrate the issue with the current logic:

- `BASIS_POINTS` is defined as 10000.
- Alice's OperatorDelegator has an allocation of 3000 basis points (30%).
- Bob's OperatorDelegator has an allocation of 7000 basis points (70%).
- The total TVL (`totalTVL`) is 1000 ETH.
- Alice's current TVL (`tvls[Alice]`) is 200 ETH.
- Bob's current TVL (`tvls[Bob]`) is 800 ETH.

The correct allocation threshold calculation for Alice should be:
- (3000 * 1000) / 10000 = 300 ETH

However, with the current logic, the calculation erroneously divides by `BASIS_POINTS` twice:
- (3000 * 1000) / 10000 / 10000 = 0.03 ETH

This incorrect calculation would always result in Alice's TVL being below the threshold, leading to her constant selection for new deposits and withdrawals, regardless of the actual TVLs.

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Modify the calculation to divide by BASIS_POINTS only once to correctly convert the allocation basis points to a proportion. This ensures that the OperatorDelegator's TVL is compared against the correct allocation threshold.
