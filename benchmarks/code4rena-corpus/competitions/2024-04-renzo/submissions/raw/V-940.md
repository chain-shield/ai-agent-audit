# Vulnerability details

depositQueue.sweepERC20 increases the totalTVL of the protocol. The totalTVL determines conversion rate, so an attacker could sandwich this function to steal from the protocol.

## Impact
Theft of funds from the protocol

## Proof of Concept
* There are 1,000 ezETH and 1,000 ETH TVL
* There are 10 LST which is equivalent to 10 ETH in depositQueue
* Alice notices sweepERC20 in mempool and frontrun by depositing 1000 eth for 1000 ezETH
* supply is now 2,000 ezETH amd 2,000 ETH TVL
* depositQueue.sweepERC20 increases TVL from 2,000 to 2,010
* Alice withdraws 1,000 ezETH for 1,005 ETH

Therefore, Alice stole 5 ETH from the protocol.


## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Add balance of collateral token in deposit queue to the protocol TVL in `calculateTVLs` and revisit `depositTokenRewardsFromProtocol` cause TVL logic is different.
