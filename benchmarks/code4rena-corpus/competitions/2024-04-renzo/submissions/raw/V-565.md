# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The `lookupTokenValue` function in the `RenzoOracle` contract relies on the `latestRoundData()` function from the Chainlink oracle to fetch the latest price data. While Chainlink's price feeds are designed to be resistant to flash crashes and provide aggregated prices from multiple sources, there is still a possibility of price deviations during extreme market conditions, leading to mispricing of assets.


## Proof of Concept

Extreme market conditions occur, causing significant volatility and rapid price fluctuations across various assets.

The Chainlink oracle network, despite its decentralized approach and aggregation of prices from multiple sources, experiences some deviation from the true market price due to the unprecedented market turbulence.

The RenzoOracle contract fetches the price data from the Chainlink oracle using the `latestRoundData()` function.

The Chainlink oracle used by the RenzoOracle contract struggles to keep up with the rapid price fluctuations and returns inaccurate prices that significantly deviate from the true market value.

Alice, an unsuspecting user, decides to interact with the protocol during the flash crash, unaware of the ongoing market turbulence.

Alice attempts to buy an asset using the protocol, but due to the mispricing caused by the inaccurate oracle price, she ends up paying a significantly inflated price compared to the actual market value.

Alternatively, Alice tries to sell an asset during the flash crash, but the protocol offers her a severely discounted price based on the inaccurate oracle price, resulting in a substantial loss.

Alice's transactions are executed at the mispriced rates, causing her to suffer significant financial losses.

Bob, an attacker or sophisticated trader, identifies the price discrepancies during the flash crash and takes advantage of the situation by performing arbitrage trades.

Bob buys assets at the discounted prices offered by the protocol and immediately sells them on external markets at the actual market prices, pocketing the difference as profit.

The protocol and its users, including Alice, bear the losses from the mispriced transactions, while Bob benefits from the exploit.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
To mitigate the impact of potential price deviations during extreme market conditions, consider implementing additional safeguards such as:

Using a time-weighted average price (TWAP) over a specified period to smooth out short-term price fluctuations.

Implementing price sanity checks and circuit breakers to prevent the use of prices that deviate significantly from the expected range.

Incorporating multiple oracle sources and using a consensus mechanism to determine the most reliable price.
