# Vulnerability details

According to the documentation, referral rewards should be provided to user address. There isn't any state function that logs the referrals. This means that there isn't referral rewards for any successful deposits in L2.
From [Documentation](https://docs.renzoprotocol.com/docs/product/renzo-faqs/layer-2-faq):

`Am I eligible to receive referral points for referring a user who deposits on an L2?`

Yes, however referral tracking for L2 deposits will not be triggered until an initial deposit is made on Ethereum Mainnet by the referred user. Make sure the user you referred deposits their ETH on Mainnet before depositing ETH anywhere else to ensure Renzo ezPoints are tracking and you are receiving your referral rewards


## Impact
Rewards will not be sent to referred users.

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Implement the referral logic to allow users to claim referral rewards.
