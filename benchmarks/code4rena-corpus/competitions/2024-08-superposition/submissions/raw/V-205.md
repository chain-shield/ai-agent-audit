# Vulnerability details

## Impact
A malicious user could bloat the storage with useless data.

## Proof of Concept
As a user I can create unlimited positions in a pool at a very low cost thus bloating and making the storage more expensive filling it with useless data(empty positions) because the mint_position_B_C5_B086_D and create_position functions do not have any mechanisms against that.

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Consider adding some validation or requiring a minimum liquidity on position creation to prevent such behavior.
