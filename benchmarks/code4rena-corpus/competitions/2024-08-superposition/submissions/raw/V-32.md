# Vulnerability details

## Impact
When the pool is disabled, users cannot remove liquidity.

## Proof of Concept
From the comments above function update_position_internal(), we can get our protocol's thoughts: When pools are disabled, we cannot increase positions' liquidity, but removing liquidity is allowed. 
This design is reasonable that when something wrong happens, we do not wish that LP holders to add more liquidity into the pool, but allow LP holders to remove liquidity from this pool.
The problem is that current pool's `update_position()` function will be reverted if the pool is disabled no matter the operation is increasing liquidity or removing liquidity.
This behavior will block users' removing liquidity when the pool is disabled.
