# Vulnerability details

## Impact

The `create_position` function within the pool library fails to validate that the low tick is less than the up tick when creating a new position. This oversight allows users to create positions where the lower tick is greater than the upper tick. Such a scenario disrupts the liquidity and fee calculation logic, potentially leading to incorrect fee distribution, miscalculated liquidity. Users could exploit this vulnerability to add liquidity less token than expected.

## Proof of Concept

When creating a new position external `mint_position_B_C5_B086_D` function and `pool::create_position` does not validate that low tick is less than up tick. As a result, an invalid position can be created where low > up. Such invalid positions can break liquidity and fee calculation logic, leading to potential discrepancies in the pools operations
