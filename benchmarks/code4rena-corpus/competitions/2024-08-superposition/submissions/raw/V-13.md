# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Users do not get refunded any excess ether supplied during NFT transfer. As such, the stand to lose these funds.

## Proof of Concept
All NFT transfer functions are payable. This means that the caller has to supply ether with the call to complete these operations.
These include:
- [`transferFrom()`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-superposition/blob/4528c9d2dbe1550d2660dac903a8246076044905/pkg/sol/OwnershipNFTs.sol#L123)
- [`transferFrom() with data`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-superposition/blob/4528c9d2dbe1550d2660dac903a8246076044905/pkg/sol/OwnershipNFTs.sol#L133)
- [`safeTransferFrom()`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-superposition/blob/4528c9d2dbe1550d2660dac903a8246076044905/pkg/sol/OwnershipNFTs.sol#L143)
- [`safeTransferFrom() with data`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-superposition/blob/4528c9d2dbe1550d2660dac903a8246076044905/pkg/sol/OwnershipNFTs.sol#L154)

However, there is a possibility that the provided ether may exceed the required amount for these transfer. But since the protocol does not hanlde refunds, these funds are lost.

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
The protocol should refund any excess ether supplied during NFT transfer. This can be achieved by adding a refund logic in these functions.
