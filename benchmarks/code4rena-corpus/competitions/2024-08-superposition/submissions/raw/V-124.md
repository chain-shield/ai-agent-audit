# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Detailed description of the impact of this finding.

The compute_swap_step function performs potentially complex calculations involving liquidity, prices, and fees, depending on the state of the system and input values. In scenarios where the liquidity is high or the number of iterations in computations (loops) becomes excessive, the gas consumption could surpass the block gas limit, causing transactions to fail. This can result in a Denial of Service (DoS) attack by preventing swaps from being executed, especially in large pools or edge cases with extreme input values.

If the gas limit is exceeded during the execution of the compute_swap_step function, the transaction will revert. This can prevent users from executing swaps, leading to denial of service, liquidity disruption, and an overall negative impact on the availability of the system. Attackers could potentially exploit this to prevent swaps from completing, blocking trading activity or preventing specific participants from interacting with the system.
