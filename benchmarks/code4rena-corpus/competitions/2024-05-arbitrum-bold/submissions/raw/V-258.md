# Vulnerability details

## Impact
This can lead to loss of funds for honest validators.


## Proof of Concept
An attacker can frontrun a call to `RollupUserLogic::stakeOnNewAssertion(...)` by calling [`RollupAdminLogic::setLoserStakeEscrow(...)`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-arbitrum-foundation/blob/6f861c85b281a29f04daacfe17a2099d7dad5f8f/src/rollup/RollupAdminLogic.sol#L269-L275) . The frontrun is needed because the DAO may notice and undo the malicious action, however the stake of the user who was front run has already been stolen.

The problem is caused by missing access control in the  `RollupAdminLogic::setLoserStakeEscrow(...)` function.

- Attacker frontruns a call to create a new assertion by changing the `loserStakeEscrow` contract.
- `stakeOnNewAssertion(...)`  succeeds but the funds are already transferred to an attacker controlled account.
- Honest assertion is confirmed but their funds cannot be returned to them because it is stolen

## Tools Used
Manaul review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Add access control to the RollupAdminLogic::setLoserStakeEscrow(...)function

Access Control
