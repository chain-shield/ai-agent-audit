# Vulnerability details

## Impact 
[`SequencerInbox`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-arbitrum-foundation/blob/main/src/bridge/SequencerInbox.sol) contract uses the [`refundsGas`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-arbitrum-foundation/blob/6f861c85b281a29f04daacfe17a2099d7dad5f8f/src/libraries/GasRefundEnabled.sol#L17) modifier in several functions. It is designed to refund the sender for the execution costs of the transaction. 
The calculations for gas refunding rely on `msg.data` which is user-supplied. A malicious batch poster can simply append a tremendously large zero-padded calldata to increase the refund amount from the contract. This is possible because the `calldataSize` variable, which accepts the value of `msg.data.length`, is directly passed to the `onGasSpent()` function without any upper limit or validation. By exploiting this, an attacker can increase the `calldataSize` to an excessive value, causing the contract to refund more gas than what was actually used.  This would result in substantial financial losses for the contract as it over-refunds the gas costs. The inflated refunds could deplete the funds meant for legitimate transactions, potentially disrupting the contract’s operations and causing financial instability.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Implement an upper bound on the length of `msg.data` that can be used in the gas refund calculation. This will prevent excessively large calldata from being processed and limit the potential for inflated gas refunds.
