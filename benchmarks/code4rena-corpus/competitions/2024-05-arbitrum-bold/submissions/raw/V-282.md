# Vulnerability details

## Impact

On RollupUserLogic.sol most functions to be used by validators are access restricted by the modifier `onlyValidator()`. The functions can be called by anyone, or in case of the validatorWhitelist being enabled, can only be called by whitelisted validators. 

When the functions are allowed to be called by anyone, a validator can stake on a new assertion. If the whitelist is then enabled, the validator, now unable to participate, will be unable to withdraw their stake.

## Proof of Concept

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-arbitrum-foundation/blob/main/src/rollup/RollupUserLogic.sol#L20-L23
```solidity
    modifier onlyValidator() {
        require(isValidator[msg.sender] || validatorWhitelistDisabled, "NOT_VALIDATOR");
        _;
    }
```

The withdrawal is done in two steps. First call [returnOldDeposit()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-arbitrum-foundation/blob/main/src/rollup/RollupUserLogic.sol#L222-L225) or [reduceDeposit()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-arbitrum-foundation/blob/main/src/rollup/RollupUserLogic.sol#L241-L245), to inactive the staked amount, and allow it to be withdrawn, and second [withdrawStakerFunds()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-arbitrum-foundation/blob/main/src/rollup/RollupUserLogic.sol#L358-L364) to receive the owed amount.
Both functions on the first step are restricted by the onlyValidator() modifier. Even though the function on the second step is not, this still means that a staked validator will not be able to withdraw their stake if the whitelist is enabled and they are not on it.

## Tools Used

Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Allow a validator to remove their staked amount even if they are not whitelisted.
