# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The lack of access control in the `RollupUserLogic.sol` contract can lead to unauthorized access and manipulation of critical functions within the rollup system. Without proper access control mechanisms, malicious actors could:
- Execute functions meant only for authorized users (e.g., validators), leading to unauthorized state changes.
- Manipulate rollup states and processes, potentially compromising the integrity and security of the rollup protocol.
- Undermine the trust and reliability of the rollup system by enabling unauthorized actions and potential exploits.
