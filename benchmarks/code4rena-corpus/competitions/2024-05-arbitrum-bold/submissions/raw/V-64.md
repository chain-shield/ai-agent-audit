# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The `withdrawFromPool` function in the `AbsBoldStakingPool` contract does not enforce a minimum withdrawal amount beyond zero. This vulnerability is located in the `withdrawFromPool` function, specifically in the absence of a check for a minimum withdrawal amount.

Attackers can exploit this vulnerability by making numerous small or dust withdrawals from the pool, which can lead to network congestion and potentially cause a denial of service. The contract will process these small withdrawals, consuming gas and increasing the cost of using the pool for legitimate users. Additionally, the lack of a minimum withdrawal amount can result in the fragmentation of user balances, making it more difficult to manage and track individual user balances effectively.

## Proof of Concept
An attacker identifies the `withdrawFromPool` function in the `AbsBoldStakingPool` contract.

The attacker creates a script or automated tool to make a large number of small dust withdrawals from the pool, with each withdrawal being just above zero.

The attacker executes the script, sending a flood of small withdrawal transactions to the contract.

The contract processes each withdrawal transaction, consuming gas and increasing the overall cost of using the pool.

As more small withdrawals are made, the network becomes congested, and legitimate users may face higher gas prices, difficulty in having their transactions processed, or a denial of service of the protocol.

The pool's functionality and user experience are compromised due to the fragmentation of user balances and the increased cost of interacting with the contract.

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Disallow transactions below a certain threshold to maintain efficiency and prevent denial of service through dust spamming.

Implement a minimum withdrawal amount requirement in the `withdrawFromPool` function. Add a check to ensure that the withdrawal amount is greater than or equal to a predefined minimum value. If the withdrawal amount is below the minimum, the function should revert with an appropriate error message.
