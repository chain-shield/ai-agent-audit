# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The lack of proper access control on the perform function can lead to several severe consequences:

* Unauthorized Contract Migration: An attacker can pause the old rollup and refund stakes to themselves, resulting in potential financial losses.

* Deployment of Malicious Contracts: An attacker can deploy their own contracts, introducing vulnerabilities and backdoors that can be exploited to steal funds or disrupt the system.

* Permanent Ownership Change: The attacker can change the owner of the rollup to themselves, gaining full control over the rollup and its funds.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
