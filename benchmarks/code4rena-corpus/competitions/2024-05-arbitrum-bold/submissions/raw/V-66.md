# Vulnerability details

## Vulnerability Report: Unauthorized Withdrawal in AbsBoldStakingPool Contract

### Description
The abstract `AbsBoldStakingPool` contract exposes a vulnerability in its `withdrawFromPool(uint256 amount)` function, allowing unauthorized users to withdraw tokens, to withdraw more tokens than they themselves actually possess. The function solely relies on the `depositBalance` mapping to determine the user's balance without performing additional checks on the caller's identity.

### Potential Attack
An attacker with access to a private key of a wallet could manipulate their deposit balance in the `depositBalance` mapping or submit a transaction with a manipulated amount. Since the function only checks the balance against the provided amount without verifying the caller's identity, the attacker could potentially withdraw more tokens than they actually have. The attacker who has access the private key of the wallet can perform any descent based brute force attacks that can be expressed efficiently to the extent of O(T⋅n) or better. In the ideal scenario where the attacker finds that the optimization problem is convex and well-behaved, gradient descent can converge to the optimal solution relatively quickly. 

### Impact
This vulnerability could lead to a loss of funds for the staking pool, as attackers could drain the pool by repeatedly withdrawing more tokens than they should. Additionally, if the pool runs out of tokens due to these malicious withdrawals, it could disrupt the operation of the entire system, potentially leading to a crash or loss of user trust.

### Recommendation
To mitigate this vulnerability, the `withdrawFromPool(uint256 amount)` function should perform additional checks to ensure that the caller is authorized to withdraw the specified amount. This could involve using access control mechanisms such as requiring the caller to be the owner of the tokens or implementing a permission system.
