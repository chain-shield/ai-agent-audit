# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept

* The `RollupUserLogic` contract inherits from `RollupCore`.

* `RollupCore` inherits from `PausableUpgradeable`, which provides internal `_pause` and `_unpause` functions.

* The `RollupUserLogic` contract uses the `whenNotPaused` modifier on multiple functions but does not implement the corresponding `public`/`external` `pause` and `unpause` functions.

* As a result, the contract cannot be `paused` or `unpaused`, negating the intended functionality of the `whenNotPaused` modifier.

## Impact

Functions intended to be paused during certain events cannot be paused.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Implement `public`/`external` `pause` and `unpause` functions in the `RollupUserLogic` contract to call the inherited internal `_pause` and `_unpause` functions from `PausableUpgradeable`.
