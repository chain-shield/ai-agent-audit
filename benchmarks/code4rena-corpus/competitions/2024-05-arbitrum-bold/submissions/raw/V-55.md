# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Several functions in the `RollupAdminLogic` contract lack proper access control mechanisms. Functions such as `setOutbox`, `removeOldOutbox`, `setDelayedInbox`, and others can be called by any address, allowing unauthorized modifications to critical contract parameters. These functions should only be callable by authorized entities, such as the contract owner or a specific role, to prevent unauthorized changes. 

Insufficient access control in the `RollupAdminLogic` contract can have severe consequences:

Unauthorized modifications: Attackers or malicious entities can call the unprotected functions and modify critical contract parameters, such as the outbox, delayed inbox, and other settings, without proper authorization.

Privilege escalation: Unauthorized entities can gain elevated privileges by modifying contract parameters, allowing them to perform actions that should be restricted to authorized roles.

Disruption of contract functionality: Malicious modifications to contract parameters can disrupt the normal operation of the contract, leading to unexpected behavior or system failures.

Loss of trust: If unauthorized changes are made to the contract, it can erode trust in the system and damage the reputation of the project.

## Proof of Concept
The attacker identifies a target function, such as `setOutbox`, that lacks access control.

The attacker calls the `setOutbox` function, passing an arbitrary address as the new outbox.

The function executes successfully, modifying the outbox address without any authorization checks.

The attacker can repeat this process for other unprotected functions, such as `removeOldOutbox` and `setDelayedInbox`, to make unauthorized changes to the contract's state.

The unauthorized modifications can lead to disruption of the contract's functionality, privilege escalation, or other malicious activities.
