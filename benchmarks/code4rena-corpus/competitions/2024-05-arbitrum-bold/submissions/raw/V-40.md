# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The `withdrawStakerFunds` function in the `RollupUserLogic` contract allows users to withdraw their staked funds without enforcing a minimum withdrawal amount. This could result in unnecessary gas costs and potential Denial-of-Service attacks if users repeatedly withdraw small amounts. 

The absence of a minimum withdrawal amount check can have several impacts:

Gas inefficiency: Users can withdraw very small amounts of funds, leading to unnecessary gas costs for processing these transactions. Each withdrawal transaction consumes gas, regardless of the amount being withdrawn.

Potential DoS: Malicious users could spam the contract with a large number of small withdrawals, causing congestion and potentially leading to a Denial-of-Service (DoS) condition. This can impact the contract's performance and availability.

Increased transaction costs: The contract itself may incur higher gas costs for processing a large number of small withdrawals. This can lead to inefficient use of gas and increased operational costs for the contract.

## Proof of Concept
An attacker can repeatedly call the `withdrawStakerFunds` function with small withdrawal amounts, generating a large number of transactions. This can be done programmatically or through a script that automates the withdrawal process.

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Implement a minimum withdrawal amount check in the withdrawStakerFunds function. Reject withdrawals that do not meet the minimum required amount.
