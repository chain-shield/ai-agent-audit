# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The absence of a secure assertion hash calculation mechanism in the MockAssertionChain contract poses a severe risk to the security and integrity of the entire blockchain system. This vulnerability enables malicious actors to potentially manipulate assertion hashes, leading to unauthorized state transitions, double spending, or other critical consensus failures. As assertion hashes are fundamental for ensuring the validity and immutability of the blockchain's state transitions, their incorrect calculation undermines the trustworthiness of the entire system and can result in significant financial losses or disruptions.
