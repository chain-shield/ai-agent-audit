# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Adversary can prevent honest participants from proving an invalid edge.

## Proof of Concept
confirmEdgeByOneStepProof only allows one edge in a group of rivals to be confirmed.
```solidity
    function confirmEdgeByOneStepProof(
        ...
    ){
        ...

        // also checks that no other rival has been confirmed
        setConfirmedRival(store, edgeId);

        // we also check the edge is pending in setConfirmed()
        store.edges[edgeId].setConfirmed();
        store.edges[edgeId].totalTimeUnrivaledCache = type(uint64).max;
    }
```
By submitting two different wrong assertions, where the state in assertionB (that is at the position of wrongness in assertionA) is provable, and the state in assertionA (that is at the position of wrongness in assertionB) is provable, an adversary will be able to prevent an honest party from proving either of the invalid states of assertionA and assertionB

Take for example:
Each letter represents state, The combination is the stateHistoryRoot
- Correct assertion stateHistoryRoot=ABCDEFGH
- Adversary submits a first assertion=ZBCDEFGH
- Adversary submits a second assertion=ABCDEFGY
- Honest party submits assertion=ABCDEFGH
- Adversary bisects his first assertion to prove that G should transition to H, (not Y as submitted in his second assertion)
- Adversary bisects his second assertion to prove that A should transition to B, (not Z to B as submitted in his first assertion)
- Honest participant can't bisect to prove any of the points that was wrong cos [setConfirmedRival](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-arbitrum-foundation/blob/main/src/challengeV2/libraries/EdgeChallengeManagerLib.sol#L826) will revert.
- Note that adversary can prove any of the states inbetween, so If honest participant tries to prove any of the states in between, he can get frontrun by the adversary to prove that state.

Now, honest participant can't prove either points of disagreement
