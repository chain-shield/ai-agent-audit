**Vulnerability Type:** Race Condition

**Description:**
The `isDelayProofRequired` function in the `SequencerInbox` contract references the `isSynced` function from the `DelayBuffer` contract to determine whether a delay proof is required. However, if the `isSynced` function is called from multiple places concurrently, there could be a race condition leading to unwanted consistencies in the returned state or a double spend concern where `isDelayProofRequired` state becomes unreliable. Specifically, if one call to `isSynced` determines that the buffer is not synced, while another call simultaneously determines that it is not synced, subsequent actions based on this information could be incorrect or unpredictable.
Consequently we may assume that `isDelayProofRequired` itself is representative of a or some state without solving any of the race concerns regarded by `isSynced`.


**Impact:**
The race condition in the `isDelayProofRequired` function could lead to incorrect decisions regarding the requirement for delay proofs. This could potentially result in double spends or other unintended consequences, compromising the integrity of the system.

Use appropriate locking mechanisms or access control patterns to prevent concurrent modifications to critical state variables.
Implement event logging or other means of tracking state changes to provide transparency and enable auditing.
Consider atomicity and transactional integrity when designing interactions between contracts that share state.
