# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The `createLayerZeroEdge` function in the `EdgeChallengeManager` contract lacks sufficient input validation for the `prefixProof` and `proof` fields of the `CreateEdgeArgs` struct. This can lead to a potential denial of service (DoS) vulnerability due to excessive gas consumption.

The lack of input validation for the `prefixProof` and `proof` fields can lead to a denial of service (DoS) vulnerability in the following way:

Denial of Service (DoS):

If the attacker can sustain a high rate of transactions with large `prefixProof` and `proof` fields, it can lead to a denial of service situation.

The contract may become overwhelmed with the processing of these transactions, causing it to consume a significant portion of the available gas on the network.

Legitimate transactions may be delayed or blocked due to the increased gas consumption and network congestion caused by the attacker's transactions.

This can effectively render the contract unavailable or difficult to use for legitimate users, disrupting its normal functioning.

Excessive Gas Consumption:

An attacker can provide excessively large `prefixProof` and `proof` fields in the `CreateEdgeArgs` struct when calling the `createLayerZeroEdge` function.

Processing and verifying large proof data can consume a significant amount of gas, especially if the data needs to be decoded and processed by the contract.

If the gas consumption exceeds the gas limit set by the transaction sender, it will cause the transaction to be rejected or fail.

By repeatedly sending transactions with large `prefixProof` and `proof` fields, an attacker can cause the contract to consume excessive amounts of gas, potentially making it difficult or expensive for legitimate users to interact with the contract.

## Proof of Concept

The attacker prepares a `CreateEdgeArgs` struct with excessively large `prefixProof` and `proof` fields.

The attacker calls the `createLayerZeroEdge` function with the crafted `CreateEdgeArgs` struct.

The `createLayerZeroEdge` function passes the `args` parameter to the `store.createLayerZeroEdge` function without validating the length of the `prefixProof` and `proof` fields.

Inside the `store.createLayerZeroEdge` function, the large `prefixProof` and `proof` fields are processed and verified, consuming a significant amount of gas.

If the gas consumption exceeds the gas limit set by the transaction sender, the transaction will be rejected or fail.

The attacker repeats steps 2-5 multiple times, sending a high volume of transactions with large `prefixProof` and `proof` fields.

The repeated processing of these transactions consumes a substantial amount of gas and can congest the network.

Legitimate transactions may be delayed or blocked due to the increased gas consumption and network congestion caused by the attacker's transactions.

The contract becomes difficult or expensive to use for legitimate users, effectively causing a denial of service situation.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
To mitigate this vulnerability, it is recommended to add input validation checks for the `prefixProof` and `proof` fields in the `createLayerZeroEdge` function.
