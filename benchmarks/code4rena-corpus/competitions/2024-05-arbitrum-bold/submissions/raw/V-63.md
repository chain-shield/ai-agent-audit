# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The `depositIntoPool` function in the `AbsBoldStakingPool` contract does not enforce a minimum deposit amount beyond zero. This vulnerability is located in the `depositIntoPool` function, specifically in the absence of a check for a minimum deposit amount.

Attackers can exploit this vulnerability by making numerous small or dust deposits into the pool, which can lead to network congestion and potentially cause a denial of service. The contract will process these small deposits, consuming gas and increasing the cost of using the pool for legitimate users. Additionally, the lack of a minimum deposit amount can result in the accumulation of a large number of small deposit balances, making it more difficult to manage and track individual user balances effectively.

## Proof of Concept
An attacker identifies the `depositIntoPool` function in the `AbsBoldStakingPool` contract.

The attacker creates a script or automated tool to make a large number of small dust deposits into the pool, with each deposit being just above zero.

The attacker executes the script, sending a flood of small deposit transactions to the contract.

The contract processes each deposit transaction, consuming gas and increasing the overall cost of using the pool.

As more small deposits are made, the network becomes congested, and legitimate users may face higher gas prices, difficulty in having their transactions processed, or even a Denial of Service

The pool's functionality and user experience are compromised due to the large number of small deposit balances and the increased cost of interacting with the contract.

Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Disallow transactions below a certain threshold to maintain efficiency and prevent denial of service through dust spamming.
