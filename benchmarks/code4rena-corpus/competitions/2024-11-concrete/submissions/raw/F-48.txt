S-1367
Fee-on-Transfer tokens will cause incorrect accounting and losses/reverts in protocol
Maroutis
37
Sufficient
383d ago
Finding description and impact

The protocol does not properly handle fee-on-transfer tokens, which are tokens that deduct a fee upon transfer. In various parts of the code, such as during asset deposits/mints and reward deposits/claims, the protocol assumes that the amount of tokens sent by the user or received from a strategy is equal to the amount intended or specified. The protocol does not calculate the exact amount of assets deposited or withdrawn by checking the actual balance changes before and after the transfer. This assumption leads to incorrect accounting when interacting with fee-on-transfer tokens, as the actual amount received is less than the amount sent due to the fee deduction.

Impact
The protocol overestimates the amount of tokens it has received, leading to inaccurate total asset calculations.
Rewards may not be properly accounted for or distributed when fee-on-transfer tokens are involved.
Transactions may fail unexpectedly due to insufficient balances or allowances.
Withdrawing funds from strategies can also cause issues.
Proof of Concept

Example for the function deposit in ConcreteMultiStrategyVault :

function deposit(
    uint256 assets_,
    address receiver_
) public override nonReentrant whenNotPaused returns (uint256 shares) {
    _validateAndUpdateDepositTimestamps(receiver_);

    if (assets_ > maxDeposit(receiver_) || assets_ > depositLimit) revert MaxError();

    IERC20(asset()).safeTransferFrom(msg.sender, address(this), assets_);

    uint256 feeShares = shares.mulDiv(uint256(fees.depositFee), MAX_BASIS_POINTS, Math.Rounding.Ceil);

    shares = _convertToShares(assets_, Math.Rounding.Floor) - feeShares;
The function calls IERC20(asset()).safeTransferFrom(msg.sender, address(this), assets_); to transfer assets_ tokens from the user to the contract.
It assumes that the contract will receive exactly assets_ tokens.
However, for fee-on-transfer tokens, the contract will receive less than assets_ tokens due to the transfer fee.
The calculation of shares uses _convertToShares(assets_, Math.Rounding.Floor), which overestimates the amount of assets received.
This leads to minting more shares than appropriate, diluting existing holders and causing accounting inaccuracies.

This applies to many sections of the contracts, withrawing tokens, claiming rewards...

Recommended mitigation steps

Instead of assuming the amount transferred equals the amount specified, calculate the actual amount by checking the balance before and after the transfer.
