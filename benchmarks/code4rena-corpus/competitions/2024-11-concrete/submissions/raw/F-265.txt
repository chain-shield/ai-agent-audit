S-1330
`removeRewardToken()` fails to claim the pending rewards tokens before removing them from the vault
blutorque
65
Sufficient
383d ago
Finding description and impact

The ConcreateMultiStrategyVault contract do support multiple strategies. Rewards accrues to these strategies over time are claimable through a harvesRewards() call on the vault. The issue here is in the method removeRewardToken() which leaves pending reward unclaimed when being called. This method is defined in StrategyBase contract, and the different strategies inherit this method from the StrategyBase. Some strategy such as AaveV3Strategy can have more than one reward tokens,

    /**
     * @dev Retrieves the addresses of reward tokens available for this strategy.
     * @return An array of addresses of reward tokens.
     */
    function getRewardTokenAddresses() public view override returns (address[] memory) {
        return aaveIncentives.getRewardsByAsset(address(aToken));
    }

,removing a single reward tokens without claiming them first, could lead to loss of earned reward tokens,

Also note that, the $RDNT(used in RadiantV2Strategy as reward token), are vested for a 90-day period. Even if the strategy claims them earlier, they cannot be withdrawn immediately.

Proof of Concept

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-11-concrete/blob/c9219f304ed8ce71b8dc8ef67790d76fb42f370d/src/strategies/StrategyBase.sol#L217

Recommended mitigation steps

Some flexible fix required for radiant as mentioned above, however, in other strategies before removing _getRewardsToStrategy can be call to claim any pending rewards, followed by a harvestRewards() call to multi strategy vault to update global rewardIndex for that token.
