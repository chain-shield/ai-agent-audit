`_addTokensToStrategy` of the Claim Router opens up ways to game protect strategies

Finding description and impact

Other strategies using the same token that should get reward tokens added to them would be skipped with more than enough reward tokens added to the strategy being tricked. Hence, reward token loss for the rest strategies.

Let's suppose there are 5 strategies using DAI or USDC or any other token as their reward token. Initially, the caller intends to add 1000 USDC as a reward split across all 5 strategies (200 USDC each)
Another one of those strategies, say strategy 1 frontruns (even without frontruns the issue persists) the addRewards function call and makes a borrow / requestToken of 1 USDC.
Strategy 1 would then have a borrowed amount of 1 USDC
During the calculation here amountToBeSent = amount_.mulDiv(stratBorrowDebt, debtVaults.totalBorrowDebt, Math.Rounding.Floor); of the addRewards function, all 1k USDC will be added as rewards to strategy 1.
Strategy 1 would have successfully accumulated all 1k USDC rewards (instead of 200 USDC) for itself.
Recommended mitigation steps

The addRewards function needs to be reworked to consider how much of the amount being sent should be allocated to the strats which have pending borrows relative to the amount being added to all strategies
