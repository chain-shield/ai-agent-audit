S-118
Deposit limits in ConcreteMultiStrategyVault and StrategyBase are ineffective
muellerberndt
00
Sufficient
383d ago
Finding description and impact

The ConcreteMultiStrategyVault and StrategyBase contracts define deposit limits, e.g. ConcreteMultiStrategyVault in line 53-55:

    /// @notice The maximum amount of assets that can be deposited into the vault.
    /// @dev Public variable to store the deposit limit of the vault.
    uint256 public depositLimit;


As it is implemented now, the limit has practically no effect:

In ConcreteMultiStrategyVault it is only applied on a per-deposit basis. Users can simply make multiple deposits to bypass the limit.
In StrategyBase (as well as in the child strategies) the limit is not considered at all.
Proof of Concept

No PoC required as this is a trivial medium-risk issue.

Recommended mitigation steps

The following fixes are possible:

To implement a global deposit limit, check the projected total balance after adding the deposit in the mint() and deposit() functions.
To add a per-user limit, override maxDeposit(address) to check the sum of the user's current balance in asset tokens plus the deposit amount.
If none of those is required, just remove the ineffective deposit limit checks.
