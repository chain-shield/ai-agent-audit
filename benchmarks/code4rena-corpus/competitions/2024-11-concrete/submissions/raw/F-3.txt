S-1098
takeFees modifier is not used on all functions it should be, which will lead to loss of fess
gesha17
57
Sufficient
383d ago
Finding description and impact

The takeFees modifier calculates the fees generated by the vault so far. It depends on the state of the vault contract, as such the amount of fees calculated is directly proportional to the totalAssets in the vault. As such it is important to calculate fees before any change to totalAssets as otherwise the fee calculations will be incosistent.

However the modifier is not used on some state changing functions like deposit, mint, withdraw and redeem. As a result, users are incentivized to follow certain patterns to avoid paying fees. For example, a user who is about to withdraw has in his best interest to withdraw just before a call to takePortfolioAndProtocolFees() as this way he will avoid paying the fees. On the other hand, a user who is about to deposit/mint is incentivized to make a call to takePortfolioAndProtocolFees(), because otherwise he will unfairly pay fees for the period from the last call to update the fees.

Even without malicios intervention, this will naturally lead to some users unfairly paying more and some users paying less fees.

Proof of Concept

takeFees() modifier calculates fees based on totalSupply and totalAssets:

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-11-concrete/blob/main/src/vault/ConcreteMultiStrategyVault.sol#L104

    modifier takeFees() {
        uint256 totalFee = accruedProtocolFee() + accruedPerformanceFee();
        uint256 shareValue = convertToAssets(1e18);
        uint256 _totalAssets = totalAssets();

        if (shareValue > highWaterMark) highWaterMark = shareValue;

        if (totalFee > 0 && _totalAssets > 0) {
            uint256 supply = totalSupply();
            uint256 feeInShare = supply == 0
                ? totalFee
                : totalFee.mulDiv(supply, _totalAssets - totalFee, Math.Rounding.Floor);
            _mint(feeRecipient, feeInShare);
        }
        feesUpdatedAt = block.timestamp;
        _;
    }

The modifier is not used on deposit(), mint(), redeem() or withdraw().

Consider the following scenario:

Malicios user Mallory deposits 100000e18 assets.
Some time passes and the vault owner decides to collect fees via takePortfolioAndProtocolFees().
Mallory spots that and withdraws her assets, avoiding the protocol fee and the performance fee.

In this case the malicios user would have only paid the deposit and withdrawal fees, so viability of attack depends on fee configuration.

Recommended mitigation steps

Add the takeFees modifier to the above mentioned functions.
