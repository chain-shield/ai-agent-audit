S-690
Reward token swapping economics are flawed, enabling attacks or causing losses to LPs depending on market conditions
muellerberndt
00
Sufficient
383d ago
Finding description and impact

The Swapper smart contract allows users to swap ctAssets (i.e. vault tokens) for reward tokens. The total amount of reward tokens being returned for a given ctAssetAmount is calculated as:

ctAssetAmountInStables(ctAssetAmount) + ctAssetAmountInStables(ctAssetAmount) * (rewardRate / BASISPOINTS)


The reward rate is calculated as:

baseRewardrate + bonusRewardrateUser + bonusRewardrateCtToken + bonusRewardrateSwapToken + progressionFactor


Where progressionFactor is calculated based on the value of the input ctAssets (larger amounts receive a higher reward rate). The calculated stablecoin amount is then converted into reward tokens and sent to the user.

Overall, the reward system disinsentivizes larger/longer deposits because:

Only small amounts of ctAssets can be swapped profitably at a time
Vault deposit fees must be kept high to prevent rewards from being drained instantly

Analysis:

Normally, the reward token will be much less liquid than a stablecoin such as USDC. Therefore, paying the entire amount in reward tokens will cause real losses to large LPs who claim rewards due to slippage when trading the reward tokens back for the underlying asset. Concrete can try to counteract this by reducing vault deposit fees, increasing reward rates, and providing liquidity for the reward token on the market.

However, if vault deposit fees are too low and liquidity of the reward token is high, anyone can instantly drain all reward tokens from the treasury (see proof-of-concept). This will be profitable if there is a vault with a deposit fee rate lower than the percentage gain from baseRewardrate + bonusRewardrateCtToken + bonusRewardrateSwapToken + progressionFactor.

Perhaps the most undesirable property of this system is that it enforces high vault deposit fees, which will disincentivize LPs from depositing in the first place.

Proof of Concept

Let's look at the attack described in point 2 step-by-step with simplified assumptions:

Vault ctAsset deposit fee: 0%
Flash loan fee: 0%
Treasury: 1,000,000 REWARD
REWARD/USDC: 1:1
Total reward rate: 2%

Attack flow:

Take a 980,392 USDC (1,000,000 / 1.02) flash loan from anywhere (e.g. Aave)
Deploy 980,392 USDC into any vault to receive ctAssets
Use the Swapper to trade the ctAssets for 999,999 USDC in REWARD
Swap the REWARD for 999,999 USDC on the open market
Pay back the 980,392 USDC flash loan

After this transaction, we get the following state:

The attacker has gained 19,707 USDC
The treasury holds ctAssets backed by 980,392 USDC in the vault
All REWARD have been sold to buyers on the open market
Recommended mitigation steps

Design a new reward system that fulfills the following requirements:

Allow LPs to claim rewards in reward tokens but get their assets returned as-is
Ideally, calculate rewards based on the time the assets were deployed into the vault (incentivizing LPs to lock their assets for longer).
