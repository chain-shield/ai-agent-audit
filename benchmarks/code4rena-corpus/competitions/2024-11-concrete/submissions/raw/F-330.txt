Finding description and impact

Fee recipient will be charged a fee in a certain case

Proof of Concept

Upon a user withdrawing, we have the following code:

        uint256 feeShares = msg.sender != feeRecipient
            ? shares.mulDiv(MAX_BASIS_POINTS, MAX_BASIS_POINTS - withdrawalFee, Math.Rounding.Floor) - shares
            : 0;

If the msg.sender is the fee recipient, then he is not charged a fee. The issue is that the withdraw functionality allows for withdrawing for someone else by using a typical approval mechanism. Thus, if Bob who is approved by a fee recipient, withdraws 100 tokens for the fee recipient, then they will be charged a fee.

Recommended mitigation steps
+uint256 feeShares = msg.sender != feeRecipient && receiver_ != feeRecipient
-uint256 feeShares = msg.sender != feeRecipient
            ? shares.mulDiv(MAX_BASIS_POINTS, MAX_BASIS_POINTS - withdrawalFee, Math.Rounding.Floor) - shares
            : 0;
