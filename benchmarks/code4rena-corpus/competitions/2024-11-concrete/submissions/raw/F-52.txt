Blockage attack on the withdrawal queue can freeze users' assets permanently
serial-coder
78
Sufficient
383d ago
Finding Description

Note: per the contest's readme, any ERC20 tokens with Blocklists are in scope.

The ConcreteMultiStrategyVault's withdrawal queue can be DoSed by an attacker (or a regular user accidentally) if their account has been blocked by the asset token's operator, rendering all pending withdrawal requests in the queue stuck. From the attacker's viewpoint, exploiting this attack requires low cost and low complexity.

Please refer to the Proof of Concept and Coded PoC sections for details.

Finding Impact

All pending withdrawal requests in the queue will be blocked, locking other users' assets.

Proof of Concept

To attack the vault, an attacker can transfer their vault shares to another account blocked from transfers by the asset token's operator through the ERC20Upgradeable::transfer() (see @1 in the snippet below).

@1: https://github.com/OpenZeppelin/openzeppelin-contracts-upgradeable/blob/723f8cab09cdae1aca9ec9cc1cfa040c2d4b06c1/contracts/token/ERC20/ERC20Upgradeable.sol#L131-L135

Then, the attacker can employ the blocked account to initiate the attack by executing the withdraw() or redeem() of the ConcreteMultiStrategyVault contract, which in turn calls the _withdraw() to burn their shares to withdraw assets (@2).

However, if available assets are less than the expected amount, the _withdraw() will execute the withdrawalQueue::requestWithdrawal() to enqueue the request for later withdrawal (@3).

Per the implementation of the batchClaimWithdrawal(), withdrawal requests in the queue must be processed sequentially (@4). Thus, one stuck request can block all the following requests.

The claimWithdrawal() will get the withdrawal-request details (e.g., recipient, amount, etc.) via the withdrawalQueue::prepareWithdrawal() (@5). Then, the _withdrawStrategyFunds() (@6) will be triggered to transfer assets to the recipient, in this case, the attacker's blocked account.

Since the recipient was blocked, the asset transfer to them will be reverted (refer to @7.1, @7.2, and @7.3).

Please refer to the Coded PoC section below for programmatic details.
