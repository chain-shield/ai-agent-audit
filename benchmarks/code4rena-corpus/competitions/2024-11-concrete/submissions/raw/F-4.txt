S-128
Vault users can cause performance fees to always be calculated based on the lowest tier, reducing fees for the feeReceiver
muellerberndt
00
Sufficient
383d ago
Finding description and impact

The ConcreteMultiStrategyVault accrues performance fees based on the performance of the vault's share value. The fee rate is selected based on a tier system. Vault users can ensure that the fee receiver receives the lowest possible fees (using the lowest tier) by triggering payouts frequently.

The fee is calculated as:

fee = (Price Increase × Total Supply) × Fee Rate Fraction


Where "price increase" is the increase since the last application of the fee, and the fee rate is selected from GraduatedFee[] performanceFee in the storage variable VaultFees private fees. An example fee structure from tests:

        graduatedFees[0] = GraduatedFee({lowerBound: 0, upperBound: 500, fee: 300});
        graduatedFees[1] = GraduatedFee({lowerBound: 501, upperBound: 2000, fee: 1000});
        graduatedFees[2] = GraduatedFee({lowerBound: 2001, upperBound: 5000, fee: 2000});
        graduatedFees[3] = GraduatedFee({lowerBound: 5001, upperBound: 10000, fee: 5000});


To the detriment of the fee reveiver, anybody can trigger fee payouts by calling takePortfolioAndProtocolFees() in the vault which is not permissioned. Ideally, users should always call this function before the second fee tier is reached to significantly reduce the fees.

Proof of Concept

Given that this is a medium-risk, trivial issue, I am not providing a proof-of-concept here. The issue can be reproduced simply by calling takePortfolioAndProtocolFees() during normal vault operation.

Recommended mitigation steps

As an immediate mitigation step, limit access to takePortfolioAndProtocolFees() to the vault owner.
