S-1215
Users will miss Morpho Strategy last epoch reward
ChainProof
00
Sufficient
383d ago
Description

Concrete strategies include a function to claim rewards from specific strategies. However, the MorphoVaultStrategy handles reward claiming differently from the others, as evident in the implementation below.

On the display side:
The reward calculation logic is block-based and the displayed amount is updated at different periods. The displayed amount of MORPHO rewards corresponds to the amount earned up to 00:00:00 UTC. For other rewards, the displayed amounts correspond to the amounts earned up to 30 minutes ago.

On the claim side:
Rewards work with an epoch system. When an epoch ends, rewards accrued during the epoch become available to claim. Currently epochs last 2 weeks, current one should end around November 5. Goal is to make this epochs short asap.

As shown above, rewards can only be claimed every two weeks.
With the current implementation in Concrete, users depositing into the vault with the Morpho strategy may miss out on receiving rewards.

Proof of Concept

Consider the following scenario:

Reward amount is updated by the admin. But Morpho strategy reward amout not received due to epoch withdrawal system. (it will be received after 2 week period as shown above)
Use withdraw assets. Due to user withdrawal of assets his reward index(serRewardIndex[userAddress][rewardAddresses[i]]) is set to current gloabl reward index(tokenRewardIndex) as here. On this time, Last epoch reward amount is not send to the MorphoVaultStrategy contract yet.
So user unable to claim those last Epoch reward.
Impact

User will lose last epoch rewards(here assume Owner called harvestRewards function at least 1 time for every two weeks) they are entitled to and will never have the opportunity to claim it.

Also user will lose rewards acquired time between harvestRewards to withdraw functions calling period.(this is applicable for all the strategy rewards)

Recommended mitigation steps

Implement a distinct handling mechanism for the Morpho strategy to ensure that users remain entitled to rewards, even after withdrawing, once the rewards become available.
