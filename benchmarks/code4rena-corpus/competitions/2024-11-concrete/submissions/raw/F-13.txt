Wrong rounding direction when applying fees
samuraii77

Finding description and impact

Wrong rounding direction when applying fees causing a potential difference of 0.01-0.02$ on every redeem/withdrawal

Proof of Concept

Upon redeeming, we round up the fee shares:

        uint256 feeShares = shares_.mulDiv(uint256(fees.withdrawalFee), MAX_BASIS_POINTS, Math.Rounding.Ceil);

Upon withdrawing however, we round them down:

shares.mulDiv(MAX_BASIS_POINTS, MAX_BASIS_POINTS - withdrawalFee, Math.Rounding.Floor) - shares

This results in a different amount of fees upon withdrawing compared to when redeeming. Depending on the intended design, this results in a difference of 1-2 wei between the 2 flows. For tokens such as GUSD which are intended to be used, this is a 0.01-0.02$ difference on each redeem/withdrawal that causes a floating number result. Over a long enough timeframe, the fee difference could reach hundreds and thousands of dollars.

Recommended mitigation steps

Round up or round down in both scenarios depending on your intended design
