## Brief Summary

The RadiantV2Strategy contract interacts with the rToken (Radiant's AToken implementation), specifically using its getIncentivesController() function to fetch the incentives controller. However, the incentiveController is stored in memory during initialization instead of dynamically fetched whenever required. Since rToken is part of an upgradeable system, its incentives controller can be updated, leading to potential inconsistencies in reward distribution.
