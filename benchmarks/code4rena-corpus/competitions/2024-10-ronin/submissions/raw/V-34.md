# Vulnerability details

Within the `_updatePosition` function, this function responsible for updating liquidity positions, can lead to an inconsistent state where the `liquidityNet` value for a tick becomes greater than `liquidityGross` or less than `-liquidityGross`. 

Because the `ticks.update` function, called within `_updatePosition`, does not properly maintain the relationship between `liquidityGross` and `liquidityNet` when updating tick liquidity. When positions are modified, especially when crossing tick boundaries, the `liquidityDelta` is applied without sufficient checks to ensure the resulting `liquidityNet` remains within the valid range of `±liquidityGross`.

In the `_updatePosition` function [#L353-L383](https://github.com/ronin-chain/katana-v3-contracts/blob/03c80179e04f40d96f06c451ea494bb18f2a58fc/src/core/KatanaV3Pool.sol#L353-L383)

This call can potentially result in an invalid state if the liquidityDelta pushes liquidityNet outside its valid range.

## Impact
Incorrect liquidity calculations affecting swap prices.

## Proof of Concept
1. A pool is initialized with a large liquidity position spanning ticks -10 to 10.
2. A user creates a smaller position from ticks -5 to 5.
3. The larger position is completely removed.
4. An attempt is made to remove the smaller position.

In step 4, the `ticks.update` function may incorrectly calculate `liquidityNet`, potentially allowing it to become less than `-liquidityGross` for ticks -5 and 5.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Implement strict checks in the `ticks.update` function to ensure `liquidityNet` always remains within `±liquidityGross`
