# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept
Balance Manipulation in BALANCE_CHECK_ERC20: This function could incorrectly assume token balances for checks without accounting for tokens with non-standard behaviors, like rebasing tokens, callback-enabled tokens, or fee-on-transfer tokens.

Proof of Concept: An attacker could use a token with a callback function that inflates their balance before the balance check, passing the validation under false conditions.
Attacker’s balance inflates temporarily due to token manipulation.
The contract validates based on a false balance, allowing unauthorized operations.

Impact:Unauthorized operations or bypassing intended validations.



## Recommended Mitigation Steps:
1. Avoid reliance on token balances for security-sensitive logic.
2. Require more explicit validations (e.g., validating specific amounts rather than entire balances).

Example: The AMPL rebasing issue demonstrated unexpected behavior with balance manipulation, especially in tokens with rebasing or fee-on-transfer mechanics.
