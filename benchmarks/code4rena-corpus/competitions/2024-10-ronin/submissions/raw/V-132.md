# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept
Explain and rationalize the potential impact. Provide:
this mint function is a crucial part of a liquidity provision mechanism, allowing users to provide liquidity to a pool and receive a token that represents their position in that pool.
 the amount of token minted per address is not increased for the caller.
So this introduces a reentrancy risk where the caller could be a contract that could call this function multiple times to reenter the mint function
