# Vulnerability details

## Issue Description: The _modifyPosition() function in the pool contract is responsible for updating liquidity, but there is a potential issue where imprecise calculations during liquidity withdrawals can lead to an attacker withdrawing more liquidity than entitled. This could result in slow but steady liquidity draining over time.

##Proof of State: In _modifyPosition():


amount0 = SqrtPriceMath.getAmount0Delta(
    TickMath.getSqrtRatioAtTick(params.tickLower),
    TickMath.getSqrtRatioAtTick(params.tickUpper),
    params.liquidityDelta
);
If tickLower and tickUpper values are not properly validated, this could lead to inaccurate liquidity adjustments, allowing attackers to withdraw incorrect amounts.

## Mitigation: Perform additional validation checks on the tickLower and tickUpper parameters to ensure that liquidity adjustments are always accurate. Additionally, enforce strict bounds on liquidity deltas to prevent imbalances.
