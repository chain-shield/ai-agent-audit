# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept
There is no approval for liquidity removal from V2pool to V3Migrator.sol, although safeApprove is called before INonfungiblePositionManager(nonfungiblePositionManager).mint is called. safeApprove is needed to see if msg.sender is related to liquidityToMigrate, since there is no check if the caller is the owner of the position in V2.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
add safeApprove before call safeTransferFrom
