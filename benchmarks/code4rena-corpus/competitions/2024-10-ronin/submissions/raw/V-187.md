# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept
Authorization Bypass in Permit Functions: The permit2TransferFrom functions rely on permissioned signatures but could be subject to forgery or invalid allowances if misused.

Proof of Concept: If permit2TransferFrom is used without validating the allowance or the function caller, an attacker could invoke this function to transfer more than intended.
Attacker uses a forged permit.
Contract executes based on a false authorization, leading to loss of funds.

Impact:Unauthorized transfers or allowance abuse.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
1. Validate signatures and allowances rigorously.
2. Use the latest EIP-712 compliant libraries for permits and allowances.

Example:A compound-like lending protocol faced similar authorization risks with permit-based allowances.
