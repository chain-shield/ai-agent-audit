# Vulnerability details

## Summary  
The contract `AggregateRouter.sol::AggregateRouter` is a contract that has a `receive()` function, a couple of functions, `execute(bytes calldata commands, bytes[] calldata inputs)` and `execute(bytes calldata commands, bytes[] calldata inputs, uint256 deadline)`, that are payable but the contract has no functions to withdraw the funds sent to it.  
  
## Proof of Concept  
The contract has no functions to withdraw the funds sent to it. This would lead to the funds being locked in the contract forever, not to be accessed by anyone, not even the deployer/owner.  
  
## Tools Used  
Aderyn, Manual review  
  
## Mitigation Steps  
Add a `withdraw()` function so that the funds can be accessed and taken out of the contract whenever necessary.
