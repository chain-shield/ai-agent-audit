# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept
The Katana V3 Migrator is responsible for transferring liquidity from a V2 pool to a V3 pool. During the migration, the liquidity is burned in V2, and new liquidity is minted in V3 using the corresponding token amounts. However, the current implementation does not account for slippage, which can result in significant discrepancies between the amounts burned in V2 and minted in V3, particularly under volatile market conditions.

The lack of slippage checks means that users may migrate fewer tokens than they initially burned, leading to loss of value. This issue is especially problematic in low-liquidity pools or during high market volatility, where price changes between the two pools can be substantial.

Vulnerable code:
The migration logic assumes that the liquidity minted in V3 will closely match the liquidity burned in V2. However, no checks are in place to ensure this.
https://github.com/ronin-chain/katana-v3-contracts/blob/03c80179e04f40d96f06c451ea494bb18f2a58fc/src/periphery/V3Migrator.sol#L53-L67
```solidity
(,, uint256 amount0V3, uint256 amount1V3) = INonfungiblePositionManager(nonfungiblePositionManager).mint(
    INonfungiblePositionManager.MintParams({
        token0: params.token0,
        token1: params.token1,
        fee: params.fee,
        tickLower: params.tickLower,
        tickUpper: params.tickUpper,
        amount0Desired: amount0V2ToMigrate,
        amount1Desired: amount1V2ToMigrate,
        amount0Min: params.amount0Min,
        amount1Min: params.amount1Min,
        recipient: params.recipient,
        deadline: params.deadline
    })
);
```
This code does not verify that `amount0V3` and `amount1V3` are within a reasonable range of the amounts burned in V2 (`amount0V2` and `amount1V2`). As a result, users can suffer losses if the amounts minted in V3 are significantly lower than those in V2.

Example scenario:
1. A user migrates 1000 USDC and 1 WETH from a V2 pool to a V3 pool.
2. During the migration, the price of WETH increases significantly, leading to a smaller WETH amount being minted in the V3 pool.
3. The user receives only 0.95 WETH in the V3 pool, leading to a loss of 0.05 WETH without any warning or protection.

describe("Katana V3 Migrator - Slippage Issue", function () {

    // User approves migrator to transfer tokens
    await token0.connect(accounts[0]).approve(migrator.address, amount0ToMigrate);

The test will show that after migration, the user's WETH balance is less than 1 WETH due to the lack of slippage protection. This demonstrates the vulnerability to price changes during the migration process.

## Impact
1. Users migrating liquidity without slippage protection can suffer significant losses, particularly during periods of high volatility. In volatile markets, liquidity providers may end up with far fewer tokens in V3 than they had in V2.
2. The absence of slippage checks can lead to dissatisfaction among users, damaging the reputation of the protocol and reducing user trust in the system.

Example impact:
If a user migrates 1000 USDC and 1 WETH during a volatile market and receives only 0.9 WETH in the V3 pool, they have lost 0.1 WETH (which could be worth several hundred dollars, depending on the price).

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
1. Add slippage protection to the migration process to ensure that the token amounts received in V3 are within a reasonable range of the token amounts burned in V2. For example, you could enforce a minimum amount of tokens that must be received to complete the migration.
```solidity
require(amount0V3 >= amount0V2.mul(98).div(100), "Slippage too high for token0");
require(amount1V3 >= amount1V2.mul(98).div(100), "Slippage too high for token1");
```
This ensures that the amounts received in V3 are at least 98% of the amounts burned in V2, protecting users from excessive slippage.
2. Allow users to specify a slippage tolerance during migration, similar to how decentralized exchanges handle slippage. Users can choose their acceptable slippage level (e.g., 1%, 2%) when migrating liquidity.
```solidity
uint256 minAmount0 = params.amount0Desired.mul(100 - params.slippageTolerance) / 100;
uint256 minAmount1 = params.amount1Desired.mul(100 - params.slippageTolerance) / 100;

require(amount0V3 >= minAmount0, "Slippage too high for token0");
require(amount1V3 >= minAmount1, "Slippage too high for token1");
```
