But `increaseLiquidity` has no access control, and anyone can increase a position's liquidity by any amount, no matter how little.

A malicious user in a griefing attack can continously increase liquduity by as low as 1 wei to continously dos attempt to burn a position

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Ensure only a position owner can increase his liquidity.
