# Vulnerability details

### Issue Summary
The vulnerability lies within the `mint` function's balance check conditions:

```solidity
if (amount0 > 0) require(balance0Before.add(amount0) <= balance0(), "M0");
if (amount1 > 0) require(balance1Before.add(amount1) <= balance1(), "M1");
```

This implementation intends to ensure that the contract receives sufficient `amount0` and `amount1` tokens during the callback (`katanaV3MintCallback`). However, the current logic may cause unexpected reverts due to the `<=` condition, which incorrectly assumes `balance0Before + amount0` and `balance1Before + amount1` will always be less than or equal to the post-callback `balance0()` and `balance1()`.


### Impact
This bug causes an unintended revert when balances do not exactly meet the required condition, potentially disrupting contract functionality and preventing legitimate minting operations. If left unresolved, users may be unable to mint tokens due to this issue, reducing the contract’s usability and reliability.


## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Replace the `<=` condition with `>=` to ensure the contract has received sufficient funds without requiring an exact balance match:

```solidity
if (amount0 > 0) require(balance0() >= balance0Before.add(amount0), "M0");
if (amount1 > 0) require(balance1() >= balance1Before.add(amount1), "M1");
```

### Rationale
Using `>=` checks that the balance has increased by at least `amount0` and `amount1`, respectively, preventing unnecessary reverts and ensuring successful mint operations while maintaining the security of the contract’s balance validation.
