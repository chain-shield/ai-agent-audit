# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept
Return values `(uint256 amount0V2, uint256 amount1V2)` from call `IKatanaV2Pair(params.pair).burn(address(this))` lack of slippage check. 
The absence of a slippage check means that the function does not verify the price impact of the migrate, leaving it susceptible to manipulation by external actors.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
add slippage control as in `decreaseLiquidity()` and `addLiquidity()`
`require(amount0 >= params.amount0Min && amount1 >= params.amount1Min, "Price slippage check");`
