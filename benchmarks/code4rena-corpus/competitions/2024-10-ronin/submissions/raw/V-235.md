# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept
Potential Misuse of Hardcoded Constants (ADDR_SIZE, V3_FEE_SIZE, V3_POP_OFFSET)
Vulnerability Explanation:
The constants ADDR_SIZE, V3_FEE_SIZE, and V3_POP_OFFSET are hardcoded as 20, 3, and 43, respectively, representing encoded offsets and sizes. Although they save gas, they may become a risk if the encoding structures change or if developers rely on these values across varying data formats. Any misalignment could lead to silent failures, with offsets leading to incorrect data.

Proof of Concept (PoC):
Consider a scenario where ADDR_SIZE is used in encoding but doesn’t match the expected size. A function may read a buffer incorrectly, leading to logic errors:
bytes memory data = abi.encodePacked(addr, fee);
require(data.length >= Constants.V3_POP_OFFSET, "Data too short");
If ADDR_SIZE or V3_POP_OFFSET are incorrect, it could lead to data misalignment, potentially resulting in partial or failed transactions.

Impact: While less of a direct security threat, these constants can lead to incorrect logic or data misinterpretations, especially if used across evolving contract designs.
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Use encoding functions that dynamically compute offsets and sizes rather than relying on hardcoded values. Review all encoding/decoding logic when upgrading contracts to ensure alignment with data expectations.
