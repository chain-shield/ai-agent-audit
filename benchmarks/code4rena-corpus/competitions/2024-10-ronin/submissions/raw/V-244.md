# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept
Reentrancy Vulnerability: The pay, payPortion, sweep, and unwrapWETH9 functions use safeTransferETH to transfer ETH to an arbitrary address (recipient). If this recipient address is a contract, it could contain a malicious fallback or receive function, allowing it to re-enter the Payments contract before the initial transfer finishes. If the contract state changes after the transfer, the attacker could execute reentrancy attacks to exploit repeated fund transfers, potentially draining assets.

PoC: Deploy a malicious contract that has a receive function, which calls back into the Payments contract to call pay or payPortion again, re-entering and repeating the transfer process.
Execute a transaction from the malicious contract as the recipient in the Payments contract, triggering reentrancy and repeated fund withdrawals.

Impact: If exploited, reentrancy vulnerabilities can result in unauthorized fund withdrawals and total asset depletion of the Payments contract.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
1. Use a reentrancy guard: Add the nonReentrant modifier to all vulnerable functions to prevent reentrant calls.
2. Order of operations: Perform all state updates before making external calls (e.g., fund transfers), ensuring no vulnerable state remains accessible during the external call.
