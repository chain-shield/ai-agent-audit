# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept
Unchecked External Call Failures: When making external calls, token transfers can fail due to non-standard implementations. Failing to check return values can lead to unexecuted transfers or unexpected state inconsistencies.

Proof of Concept: If the Payments.sweep or ERC20.transfer functions do not revert on failure and simply return false, the contract might assume the transfer succeeded and proceed with incorrect data or state.

Attacker initiates a transfer with a non-standard ERC20 token.
The transfer fails (returns false) but does not revert.
The contract assumes the transfer succeeded, leading to loss or inconsistencies.
Impact: Inconsistent contract state or fund loss due to unacknowledged transfer failures.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
1. Verify the return value of all token transfers and other external calls.
2. Use helper libraries (like OpenZeppelin’s SafeERC20) that handle non-standard ERC20 tokens.

Example:The MakerDAO Black Thursday incident was partially attributed to unhandled transfer failures, leading to a liquidation crisis.
