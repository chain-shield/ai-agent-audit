# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept

The `KatanaV3Factory::initialize` function allows the owner to be set to address(0), effectively enabling anyone to become the contract's owner. This vulnerability grants unauthorized individuals access to critical functions and, if unnoticed, could lead to the theft of funds.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Use modifier onlyOwner in `initialize`.
