# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept

Although the function [v2SwapExactInput](https://github.com/ronin-chain/katana-operation-contracts/blob/27f9d28e00958bf3494fa405a8a5acdcd5ecdc5d/src/aggregate-router/modules/katana/v2/V2SwapRouter.sol#L54-L75) protects the user from slippage, by letting them specify the `amountOutMinimum` - it also has to allow the user to specify by what time the transaction should get executed, therefore the deadline parameter.

A call to `v2SwapExactInput` is made in [dispatch](https://github.com/ronin-chain/katana-operation-contracts/blob/27f9d28e00958bf3494fa405a8a5acdcd5ecdc5d/src/aggregate-router/base/Dispatcher.sol#L123-L139), however, there is no additional validation that compensates for the missing `deadline` parameter.

Without the `deadline` parameter, each call to this function is exposed to the risk of getting executed much later than the caller's intention, resulting in the price of the token changing and thereby receiving a worse deal. This applies to both higher and lower prices after the initial call.

Furthermore, because Ronin's mempool is publicly available, this opens up another opportunity for MEV attacks, where a bot can easily sandwich the user's transaction due to its long pending state.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Consider adding a `deadline` parameter, and compare the current `block.timestamp` to the `deadline` specified by the caller.
