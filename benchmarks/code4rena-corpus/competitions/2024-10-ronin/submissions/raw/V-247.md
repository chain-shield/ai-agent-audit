# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept
Arbitrary Token Transfers without Validation: The pay, payPortion, and sweep functions allow the transfer of any token specified by the token parameter. If a non-ERC20-compliant address is passed, or if the token doesn’t follow the standard transfer mechanisms, it could result in failed transactions, unexpected behavior, or even the inability to recover assets. Additionally, arbitrary recipient addresses could accidentally send funds to a black hole or inaccessible address.

PoC: Pass an invalid or incompatible ERC20 address to the pay function.
Attempt the transaction, resulting in either failed transfers or token loss if misconfigured.

Impact: While it may not directly impact security, misconfigured or incorrect token addresses can cause contract failures or loss of user assets if tokens get locked.
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
1. Add validation to check the token address to ensure it conforms to the ERC20 standard, e.g., through an IERC20 interface check.
2. Consider validating the recipient address to reduce the risk of sending to black hole addresses.
