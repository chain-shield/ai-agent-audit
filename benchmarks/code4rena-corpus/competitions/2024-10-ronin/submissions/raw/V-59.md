# Vulnerability details

## Issue Description: The tick system, which is responsible for determining price points and liquidity ranges, can be manipulated by forcing unintended tick transitions. Attackers can exploit this by shifting liquidity unexpectedly, impacting the fair price calculation and draining liquidity providers.

## Proof of State: In the swap() function:

(state.sqrtPriceX96 == step.sqrtPriceNextX96)
This condition checks if the price has reached a new tick. An attacker could manipulate trades to force multiple tick transitions, allowing them to drain liquidity or take advantage of incorrectly priced assets.

## Mitigation: Introduce more stringent checks around tick transitions to ensure that large liquidity shifts don't occur unexpectedly. Enforce minimum time or liquidity thresholds for triggering tick movements.
