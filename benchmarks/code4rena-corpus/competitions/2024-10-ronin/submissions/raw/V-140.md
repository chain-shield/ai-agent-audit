# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept
```solodity
      if (paid0 > 0) {
        uint256 fees0 = FullMath.mulDiv(paid0, slot0.feeProtocolNum, slot0.feeProtocolDen);
        if (fees0 > 0) TransferHelper.safeTransfer(_token0, treasury, fees0);
        feeGrowthGlobal0X128 += FullMath.mulDiv(paid0 - fees0, FixedPoint128.Q128, _liquidity);
      }
      if (paid1 > 0) {
        uint256 fees1 = FullMath.mulDiv(paid1, slot0.feeProtocolNum, slot0.feeProtocolDen);
        if (fees1 > 0) TransferHelper.safeTransfer(_token1, treasury, fees1);
        feeGrowthGlobal1X128 += FullMath.mulDiv(paid1 - fees1, FixedPoint128.Q128, _liquidity);
      }
```

The fee accounting is incorrect because fees from flash loans are transferred directly to the treasury after each flash loan, which also results in an increase in the global liquidity tracker. This poses a problem, as users may be unable to withdraw the fees they are entitled to due to a lack of liquidity, since the fees have exited the protocol. All fees recorded in the global tracker should remain within the protocol.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
If the protocol intends to send all fees to the treasury, it should do so without updating the feeGrowthGlobal tracker. This approach will ensure accurate accounting, as the fees generated from flash loans will be allocated to the treasury rather than to users.
