# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept
Lack of Access Control in deposit and withdraw Functions: The deposit and withdraw functions allow any user to deposit Ether and receive wrapped Ether (WETH) or withdraw WETH for Ether. Since there are no access control mechanisms, any malicious actor can exploit these functions.

Proof of Concept
Scenario: An attacker creates a contract that calls the deposit function to deposit Ether without any checks.
Attack Execution: The attacker could repeatedly call the deposit function, flooding the contract with transactions, causing potential DoS or liquidity issues.

Impact
Financial Loss: Users could lose funds if the contract becomes unusable or if the attacker manipulates the liquidity.
Denial of Service: A high volume of transactions could block legitimate users from interacting with the contract.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
1. Access Control: Implement access control mechanisms, such as requiring certain roles to interact with these functions.
2. Rate Limiting: Introduce limits on how often a user can call the deposit/withdraw functions to prevent abuse.
