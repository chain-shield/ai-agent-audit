# Vulnerability details

## Issue:
The PoolV3 contract uses the "push" pattern for transferring funds directly to a user in the _withdraw function, such as in the following line:


IERC20(underlyingToken).safeTransfer({to: receiver, value: amountToUser}); // U:[LP-8,9]


Using the "push" pattern to transfer funds directly to users can lead to potential risks such as reentrancy or failed transfers. It’s generally safer to adopt the "pull over push" pattern, where users can explicitly request to withdraw their funds, reducing the likelihood of vulnerabilities during the transfer process.

## Proof of Concept:
In the _withdraw function, funds are directly transferred to the receiver. This can be replaced with a mechanism that allows the receiver to withdraw the funds themselves by calling a function when needed.

## Mitigation:
To prevent potential issues, switch to the "pull over push" pattern by storing the user's funds in the contract and allowing them to withdraw their funds by calling a separate withdrawal function.
