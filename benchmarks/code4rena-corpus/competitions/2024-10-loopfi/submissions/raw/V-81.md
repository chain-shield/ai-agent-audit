# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept
`calcDecrease` handles the case where `debtToDecrease == maxRepayment`, but it didn't explicitly check if `debtToDecrease > maxRepayment` and revert if true.
This might lead to accounting errors and loss of funds.
Without the overpayment check, the contract's totalDebt would be reduced by more than the actual debt owed.
The user's position would show a negative debt balance, which is not logically correct.
This could lead to inconsistencies in calculations of interest, collateralization ratios, and other metrics, potentially affecting the overall accounting of the vault.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Inside `modifyCollateralAndDebt`, add a check to prevent overpaying the debt.
