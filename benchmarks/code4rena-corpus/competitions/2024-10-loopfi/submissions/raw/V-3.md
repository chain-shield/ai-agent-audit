# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept

updateQuotaRevenue() and setQuotaRevenue() are the same kind of process, used to _setQuotaRevenue, setQuotaRevenue() is poolQuotaKeeperOnly, but updateQuotaRevenue() is creditManagerOnly.
