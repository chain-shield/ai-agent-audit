# Vulnerability details

## Summary
The `deposit` function assumes that the amount of tokens sent by the user (`_amount`) will be fully received by the contract, which is incorrect for tokens that have a transfer fee mechanism. This results in an inaccurate balance being recorded in the `deposits` mapping, causing a mismatch between the user's deposit balance and the actual tokens received by the contract.

## Vulnerability Detail
In the `deposit` function, the contract tracks the amount of tokens deposited by the user in the `deposits` mapping, adding the entire `_amount` to the user's balance. However, for tokens that implement a transfer fee (i.e., tokens that reduce the transferred amount due to a fee), the contract will receive fewer tokens than `_amount`.

For example:
- The user deposits 100 tokens.
- Due to a 6% transfer fee, only 94 tokens are received by the contract.
- However, the `deposits[msg.sender].amount` records 100 tokens, which is inaccurate.

This results in a discrepancy between the user's recorded deposit and the actual amount of tokens in the contract, potentially leading to incorrect withdrawal amounts or other logic errors.

## Impact
- **Incorrect accounting**: The user's balance in the `deposits` mapping will reflect the pre-transfer-fee amount, leading to an overestimation of the user's actual balance.
- **Withdrawal issues**: When users attempt to withdraw, the contract may not have enough tokens to fulfill their full balance, causing potential transaction failures.



## Recommended Mitigation Steps
To handle tokens with fee on transfer, use balanceOf checks before and after the transfer to accurately track the amount of tokens received by the contract. Update the `deposits[msg.sender]`.amount with the actual amount of tokens received, as shown below:

```diff
function deposit(uint256 _amount) external {
    if (_amount == 0) revert AmountIsZero();

-    deposits[msg.sender].amount += actualReceived;
+    uint256 balanceBefore = token.balanceOf(address(this));
    token.safeTransferFrom(msg.sender, address(this), _amount);
+    uint256 balanceAfter = token.balanceOf(address(this));
+    uint256 actualReceived = balanceAfter - balanceBefore;

+    deposits[msg.sender].amount += actualReceived;
    emit Deposited(msg.sender, actualReceived);
}
```
