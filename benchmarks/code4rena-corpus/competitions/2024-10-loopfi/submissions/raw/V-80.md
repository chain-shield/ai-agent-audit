# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept
Your pause check only applies when `deltaDebt > 0` or `deltaCollateral != 0`.  This means someone could increase collateral in a paused vault. 
1. Allowing collateral increases during this paused state undermines the purpose of the pause, as it potentially exposes the system to further risk.
2. Those who can add collateral might be able to manipulate their positions to gain a better outcome when the vault resumes, potentially at the expense of other users.
3. It becomes harder to determine the accurate state of the vault before the issue occurred, potentially hindering the recovery process.
4. Users might believe that everything is functioning normally, even though the vault is paused for a reason. This could lead to further deposits and losses if the underlying issue is not resolved.


## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Modify the condition to:
```
if (deltaDebt > 0 || deltaCollateral > 0){ 
    _requireNotPaused();
}
```
