# Vulnerability details

## Description:
The `moveFunds` function allows the funds administrator to transfer all funds to any specified address (`treasury`). However, there is no verification to ensure that the new treasury address is a legitimate contract or owned by a trusted party. This could lead to accidental or malicious transfer of the entire balance to an unintended or malicious address.

## Impact:
A compromised funds administrator account could transfer all funds to an attacker-controlled address, permanently locking the assets out of the treasury and resulting in the total loss of the contract's funds.

## Proof Of Concept:
Assume the administrator’s account is compromised.
The attacker calls `moveFunds` with an arbitrary address they control.
All funds in the contract are sent to the attacker’s address without verification.
The funds are irreversibly lost from the original treasury.

## Tool used:
Manual Review.

## Recommended Mitigation:
Implement a whitelisting mechanism for the new `treasury` address or require an additional confirmation step before the funds are moved. For example, verify that the new treasury address is a contract and optionally restrict it to only approved contracts.
