# Vulnerability details

## Issue
The _cumulativeIndexSince() function calculates the interest index using block.timestamp, which can be slightly manipulated by miners. This creates a risk of interest rate manipulation, especially in a financial system where even small changes in time can lead to significant discrepancies over long-term calculations or high-value transactions.

## Proof of Concept
The function calculates the time delta using block.timestamp:

(block.timestamp - lastQuotaRateUpdate) * rate
A miner could manipulate the timestamp to influence the interest accrual, leading to potential financial gains or losses.

## Mitigation
Replace block.timestamp with a reliable source of time, such as an oracle, to prevent miner manipulation.

##Risk Level
High – Given the potential financial impact of interest rate manipulation, the lack of protection could lead to significant losses or imbalances in the system.
