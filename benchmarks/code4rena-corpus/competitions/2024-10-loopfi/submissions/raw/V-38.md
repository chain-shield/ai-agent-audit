# Vulnerability details

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

## Issue
In the Permit2 system, the signature (v, r, s) is manually concatenated using bytes.concat(params.r, params.s, bytes1(params.v)) without ensuring proper validation. This can lead to signature malleability, allowing an attacker to alter the signature while keeping it valid, potentially leading to replay attacks or unauthorized transactions.

## Mitigation
Use OpenZeppelin’s ECDSA library for safe signature verification to prevent signature malleability.
