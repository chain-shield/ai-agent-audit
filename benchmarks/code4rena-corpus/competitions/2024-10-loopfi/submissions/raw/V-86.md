# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept
The liquidation penalty is applied as uint256 deltaDebt = wmul(repayAmount, liqConfig_.liquidationPenalty); (line 588).
However, the penalty is subtracted as uint256 penalty = wmul(repayAmount, WAD - liqConfig_.liquidationPenalty); (line 589), which implies that the penalty is calculated in two different ways: once as repayAmount * liquidationPenalty and again as repayAmount * (1 - liquidationPenalty).
This inconsistency in how the penalty is applied can lead to over-penalization or under-penalization, depending on how deltaDebt and penalty are handled in subsequent calculations. Specifically, there seems to be a risk that the liquidation penalty may be incorrectly applied, leading to either:

Over-penalizing the user and draining more collateral than intended.
Under-penalizing the user and leaving the protocol exposed to bad debt.
```
uint256 deltaDebt = wmul(repayAmount, liqConfig_.liquidationPenalty);
uint256 penalty = wmul(repayAmount, WAD - liqConfig_.liquidationPenalty);
```

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Ensure that the liquidation penalty is applied consistently. For example, the penalty should likely be calculated using deltaDebt rather than repayAmount * (1 - liquidationPenalty), or the logic of deltaDebt should be revised to avoid confusion.
