# Vulnerability details

## Issue
The calcQuotaRevenueChange() function performs arithmetic with int256 and uint256 values. The multiplication of change (an int256) by rate (cast to int256 from uint256) could lead to an integer overflow or underflow if the result exceeds the bounds of an int256.

## Proof of Concept
The vulnerable code is:

return (change * int256(uint256(rate))) / int16(PERCENTAGE_FACTOR);
If change is large enough, multiplying it by the cast rate value could result in an overflow or underflow.

## Mitigation
Use SafeMath functions or Solidity’s default overflow protection (enabled in Solidity 0.8+), ensuring that the arithmetic operations are safely handled without risking overflow.
