# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Consider the following scenario:

Cap : 250k
Reserve : 20%
APR : 10%
Penalty : 20%
Grace : 5 days
Protocol Fee : 10%

Lenders A and B deposit 50k each. Borrower withdraws 80k. Lender A withdraws 20k. Reserve is empty and after 5 days delinquency kicks in. Borrower cures it after 10 days.

Lender B deposits 100k. For the next 10 days, the penalty will be applied (among others) to those 100k, which makes no sense.

Penalty (or curing) can be abused by lenders if the supply is not maxed out. This can happen unintentionally, if Lender B has depositing schedule and is not aware of the penalty state.
