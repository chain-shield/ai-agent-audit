# Vulnerability details

## Summary

The Wildcat protocol, a decentralized lending platform, prioritizes security and regulatory compliance. It incorporates a Sanctions Sentinel to enforce sanctions, preventing sanctioned addresses from participating in markets. While the protocol implements checks at various interaction points, a crucial oversight exists during the initial market creation process. This omission could potentially allow sanctioned borrowers to establish markets, undermining the protocol's integrity and compliance.

## Details

The Wildcat protocol utilizes the WildcatSanctionsSentinel contract to manage sanctions. This contract determines whether an address is flagged by Chainalysis and allows borrowers to override sanction statuses.

The protocol allows for the creation of new lending markets through the deployMarket and deployMarketAndHooks functions within the HooksFactory contract. These functions configure the market's parameters and deploy it using a create2 method.

The critical omission lies within the _deployMarket function called during market origination. While this function validates fees and ensures the uniqueness of the market, it lacks a verification step to check if the borrower's address is flagged by the Sanctions Sentinel.

## Impact

The absence of a sanction check during market origination can have significant repercussions:

Sanctioned Market Creation: The most direct consequence is the possibility of sanctioned entities creating and operating markets within the protocol. This undermines the effectiveness of the sanctions framework and poses regulatory risks.

Unintentional Participation: Lenders, unaware of the borrower's sanctioned status, might deposit funds into these markets, unknowingly engaging in transactions that violate sanctions.

Reputational Damage: Allowing sanctioned entities to create markets can severely harm the protocol's reputation, deterring legitimate users and investors.

## Scenario

- A borrower, whose address is flagged by the Sanctions Sentinel, initiates the deployment of a new market using the deployMarket or deployMarketAndHooks function.
- The _deployMarket function is called to create the market. It verifies parameters like fees and market uniqueness but fails to check the borrower's address against the Sanctions Sentinel.
- The market is successfully deployed, despite the borrower being a sanctioned entity, due to the missing check.
- Unaware of the borrower's sanctioned status, lenders deposit funds into the market, potentially violating sanctions.

## Fix

Integrate a sanction check within the _deployMarket function to prevent sanctioned borrowers from creating markets:

Sanction Check Integration: Within the _deployMarket function, before the market is deployed, add a check that calls the isSanctioned function of the WildcatSanctionsSentinel contract. This check should verify if the borrower's address is flagged as sanctioned.

Conditional Market Deployment: Allow the market deployment to proceed only if the isSanctioned check returns false, indicating the borrower is not sanctioned. If the borrower is flagged, revert the transaction, preventing the market creation.
