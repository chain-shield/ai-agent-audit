The contract contains loops within the write and readToPointer functions that iterate over data based on the length of the byte array. For large byte arrays, these loops could consume an excessive amount of gas, which may lead to transaction failures due to the block gas limit being exceeded.

	•	High Gas Costs: Handling large arrays would lead to a significant increase in gas consumption.
	•	Denial of Service: If an attacker or even a legitimate user attempts to store or retrieve excessively large byte arrays, the gas required could exceed the block gas limit. This would result in a denial of service (DoS) for the function call, potentially blocking users from interacting with the contract.
	•	Limited Functionality: The contract may fail to function as intended if the byte arrays become too large, limiting the scalability of the contract.
