# Vulnerability details

## Summary
The Wildcat Protocol presents an architectural vulnerability in its borrower registration system, as borrower management is centrally handled by the onlyOwner. In line with the decentralized nature of the protocol, borrower registration should be restricted to individual market owners, ensuring that no market can interfere with the borrower management of another market.
## Vulnerability Details
Currently, all borrower registrations are controlled centrally by the onlyOwner. This design contradicts the decentralized structure of the Wildcat Protocol, where each market should have autonomy. Moreover, a borrower from Market A should not be able to register on Market B through Market A’s owner. Instead, each market should only manage its own borrower registrations.
## Impact
The current centralized borrower registration system introduces:
•	Cross-Market Interference: Market owners could potentially interfere with other markets by registering borrowers outside their own market.
•	Single Point of Failure: The centralized registration system increases the risk if the onlyOwner is compromised, potentially affecting all markets.
•	Decentralization Issues: This system goes against the principles of decentralization, where markets should operate independently and govern their own borrowers.

##  Proof of Concept
Borrowers are centrally registered by onlyOwner:
solidity
function registerBorrower(address borrower) external onlyOwner {
    if (!_borrowers.add(borrower)) {
        revert BorrowerAlreadyExists();
    }
    emit BorrowerAdded(borrower);
}
This allows any borrower to be registered centrally, without market-specific restrictions.

•	Manual code review and system architecture analysis.
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
To maintain decentralized governance, borrower registration should be restricted to market owners, and a market owner should only be able to register borrowers for their own market. This can be enforced by creating a custom modifier such as onlyActiveMarketOwner, ensuring that no owner can register borrowers for another market:
solidity
modifier onlyActiveMarketOwner() {
    require(isMarketActive(msg.sender), "Market is not active");
    require(isMarketOwner(msg.sender), "Not the market owner");
    _;
}

function registerBorrowerInMarket(address borrower) external onlyActiveMarketOwner {
    require(isValidBorrower(borrower), "Invalid borrower");
    // Register borrower for this specific market
}
## Key Points:
•	onlyActiveMarketOwner: This modifier ensures that only the current, active owner of a market can register borrowers.
•	Market-Specific Registration: Borrowers can only be registered for the market they belong to, preventing cross-market interference.
•	Autonomy: Each market manages its own borrower registration process without the risk of interference from other market owners.
This approach aligns with the protocol’s decentralization goals and eliminates the risk of one market registering borrowers for another.
