# Vulnerability details

## Summary

The borrower has the ability to change market parameters such as annualInterestBips (annual interest rate) and reserveRatioBips (reserve ratio) at any time using the setAnnualInterestAndReserveRatioBips() in WildcatMarketConfig.sol. If the borrower changes these parameters after lenders have initiated withdrawals but before the withdrawals are processed, this can lead to discrepancies between the burned tokens and the assets claimable.

This behavior can result in users receiving less (or more) assets than they expected based on the calculations at the time of withdrawal initiation.

## Proof of Concept

	1.	A user initiates a withdrawal by calling the withdraw function.
	2.	The user’s market tokens are burned based on the current market state parameters (annualInterestBips, reserveRatioBips).
	3.	The withdrawal request is added to a pending batch to be processed at a later time.
	4.	Before the withdrawal batch is processed, the borrower calls the setAnnualInterestAndReserveRatioBips() to change annualInterestBips and/or reserveRatioBips.
	5.	The market state is updated with the new parameters.
	6.	When executeWithdrawalBatch is called to process the withdrawals, the calculations are based on the newly updated market parameters.
	7.	As a result, the amount of assets the user receives differs from what was expected when they initiated the withdrawal.


## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Modify WildcatMarketWithdrawals.sol to include snapshots of market parameters at withdrawal initiation
