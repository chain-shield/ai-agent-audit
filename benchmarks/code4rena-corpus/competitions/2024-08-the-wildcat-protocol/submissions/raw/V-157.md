# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept
According to the docs 
https://docs.wildcat.finance/using-wildcat/protocol-usage-fees

protocolFee is a percent increase in the annual rate that borrower is meant to pay seperate from the annual rate 

using the example in the docs, assuming a protocol fee rate of 5% and annual rate of 10%, the % increase is meant to be 10.5% 
however this condition doesn't hold as protocol rate is calculated as a percentage of (supply + accrued interest) 

example using above rate 
for a supply of 10,000 usdc, the total debt owed after exactly one year should be 10,000 * 10.5 / 100 = 11050 

however due to the way it is calculated 
it will end up being protocolFee 10,000 * 10 / 100 * 0.5 / 100 = 55 
so the borrower would end up paying 11,000 + 55 = 11,055
