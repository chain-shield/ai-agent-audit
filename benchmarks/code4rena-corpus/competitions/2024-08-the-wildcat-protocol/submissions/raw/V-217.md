# Vulnerability details

A critical vulnerability was identified in the AccessControlHooks contract of the Wildcat Protocol. The issue stems from the contract's improper validation of credentials returned by external role providers. Specifically, expired credentials returned by the FaultyRoleProvider are improperly accepted by the AccessControlHooks, allowing unauthorized access to privileged functions.

The system relies on external role providers to validate users, and by returning an expired credential, a malicious role provider can bypass the access control check, thus compromising the system's security.
