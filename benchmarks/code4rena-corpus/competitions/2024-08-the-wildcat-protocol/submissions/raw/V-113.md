# Vulnerability details

In `WildcatSanctionsSentinel.sol` , the function `overrideSanction()` and  removeSanctionOverride() are used to overide and remove override of sanction respectively .  There is no access control allowing anyone to call the functions

This allows for race conditions where an attacker can immediately negate a legitimate user's attempt to override their sanction by calling `removeSanctionOverride` right after the user calls `overrideSanction`

## Impact

1. Sanctioned accounts that are not supposed to override sanction their sanction could use `override sanction` to whitelist themselves, allowing them to interact with markets they should be blocked from.

2. Dos as follows :

    - Alice overrides her sanction using the `overrideSanction` function.
    - Bob the attacker monitors for  function  calls of alice and quickly 
      executes the opposite function, `removeSanctionOverride`, to deny Alice 
      access to other features of the protocol.
