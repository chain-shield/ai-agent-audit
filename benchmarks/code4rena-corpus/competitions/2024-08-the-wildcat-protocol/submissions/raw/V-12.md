# Vulnerability details

## Impact
`mcopy` and `mstore` (used in most contracts in the `src/libraries` directory) instructions are low level instructions used to store data in memeory and copy data between memory locations respectively. They require a cancun compatible EVM due to optimization and features only available in the cancun EVM upgrade. The canun-compatible EVM require higher solidity version of 0.8.21 and above. This can cause compatiblity issues, deployment failures and increased risk of errors or vulnerabilities.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
 Consider changing the solidity version to a cancun compatible one, (eg; `pragma solidity ^0.8.21`) or higher and specify the following the foundry.toml file. The version 0.8.25 is not yet released though.
```

[profile.lib]
solc_version = '0.8.21'
evm_version = 'cancun'

```
