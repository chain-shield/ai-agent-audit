# Vulnerability details

## Impact

[convertAllETH()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L315C1-L330C6) is used to convert all the locked ETH in the contract to lpETH and give the start to the 2nd epoch after the gap of 7 days user have to withdraw their tokens in case they changed their mind, or they detected a malicious contract being set, between the epochs has passed. 

This conversion works correctly, but there's a problem: It doesn't update the  [user's ETH balances](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L50) used to calculate their [userStake](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L244) in the [_claim()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L240C5-L267C1) function.

Resulting in users been able to claim twice the amount of lpETH they should.



## Proof of Concept

Consider the following scenario, which is tested in the runnable PoC:

1. Simo locks 1 ETH in the contract through [lockETH()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L124C2-L126C6).

2. The owner calls [setLoopAddresses()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L348C1-L358C6) setting the loop and the vault addresses. This sets loopActivation which ends the 1st epoch and the 7 days period users have to withdraw their locked tokens starts.

3. The 7 days passed and the owner calls [convertAllETH()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L315C1-L330C6) converting simo's 1 ETH to 1 lpETH, but without setting the [ETH simo balances](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L50) to 0. The function also sets [startClaimDate](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L327), allowing simo to claim his lpETH tokens.

4. Simo claims his lpETH through [claim()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L211C4-L216C6) with lpETH as `_token`, resulting in him having now 1 lpETH token.

5. Simo have solidity knowledge, so he previously checked the smart contract of the protocol and noticed the [ETH user balances](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L50) not set to 0 problem in [convertAllETH()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L315C1-L330C6). As he previously locked ETH in the contract he knows that his ETH balances still reflect the old balance(1 ETH), so he calls [claim()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L211C4-L216C6) with ETH as the `_token`.

6. [claim()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L211C4-L216C6) calls [_claim()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L240C5-L267C1) setting the [userStake](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L244) to the old simo's ETH balances(1 ETH), the `if (_token == ETH)` check will pass converting simo's 1 ETH to 1 lpETH, resulting in simo having now 2 lpETH tokens.

8. Simo with 1 ETH got 2 lpETH tokens, getting the reward twice.

https://gist.github.com/cholakovvv/87cfcfa6f425da59b0b78fc396125058

## Tools Used
Manual review, Foundry


## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Ensure when converting all ETH to lpETH that the ETH balances of all users are nulled.
