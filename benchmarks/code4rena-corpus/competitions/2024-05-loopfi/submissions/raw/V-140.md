# Vulnerability details


#### Vulnerability details:
The vulnerability lies in the `PrelaunchPoints::withdraw` function. This function allows users to withdraw their locked tokens from the contract. However, the function lacks proper checks to prevent denial of service attacks. Specifically, there is no limit on the amount that can be withdrawn in a single transaction, nor is there a pull-based approach to withdrawals. This exposes the contract to the risk of being overwhelmed by large-scale withdrawals initiated by malicious actors.

#### Proof-of-Concept:
An attacker could exploit this vulnerability by repeatedly calling the `withdraw` function with a large amount of tokens, effectively draining the contract's balance and making it unavailable for legitimate users.

### Impact
If exploited, this vulnerability could lead to a denial of service (DoS) attack on the `PrelaunchPoints` contract. The contract could become inaccessible to legitimate users, resulting in financial loss, damage to user trust, and reputational harm to the project.

### Tools Used
VsCode

### Recommended Mitigation
To mitigate this vulnerability and prevent denial of service attacks, the following steps are recommended:

1. Implement withdrawal limits or throttling mechanisms to prevent large-scale withdrawals that could overwhelm the contract.
2. Adopt a pull-based approach for withdrawals, where users initiate the withdrawal process, reducing the risk of attackers exploiting the contract to overload the system.
3. Implement emergency measures or circuit breakers to temporarily halt withdrawals in case of unexpected load or suspicious activity, providing time to investigate and mitigate the attack.
