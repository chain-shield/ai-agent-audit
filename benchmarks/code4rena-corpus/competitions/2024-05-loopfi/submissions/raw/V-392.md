# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Whales could exit the protocol completely since no partial withdraw supported in the protocol.

## Proof of Concept
No partial withdraw in the protocol, assuming a whale locked a big amount in PrelaunchPoints contract address, the whale decided after a while to invest a half of that deposit, but he can't withdraw 50% of it, unfortunately he has to withdraw all of it and then lock it again 50%.

it's very important to have partial withdraw since it goes in the favor of the protocol, that will allow whales and users to withdraw a partial of their amounts and leave the rest invested in Loop protocol.

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Allow users to pass a percentage in [withdraw](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/0dc8467ccff27230e7c0530b619524cc8401e22a/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L274) method the same as it is in claim method.
