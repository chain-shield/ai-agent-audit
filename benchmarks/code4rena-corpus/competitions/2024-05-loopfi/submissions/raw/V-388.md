# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The _claim function in the smart contract is currently unable to process claims for amounts less than 1% of a user's total balance due to the lack of a scaling factor. This limitation prevents users from claiming fractional amounts, which could lead to dissatisfaction and reduced usability, 
## Proof of Concept
The _claim function takes a uint8 _percentage parameter, which only allows whole number percentages for claims.
When _percentage is set to less than 1, the resulting calculation for userClaim becomes zero due to integer division truncation.
