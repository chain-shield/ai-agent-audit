# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Lockings shorter than 120 days will have to wait until loopActivation date to withdraw earnings with earning no rewards during the extra waiting period.

## Proof of Concept

1. Assume Bob decided to lock dLP for 3 months.
2. Bob locks his dLPs for 3 months, with expecting 4x on the real yield earnings.
3. Bob earns real yield for 3 months.
4. At the end of the locking period, Bob tries to withdraw his staking rewards.
5. Due to the timing logic error in the withdraw function, Bob has to wait 30 days more to withdraw his earnings.
6. Bob will be unable to use his staking rewards for 30 days.
