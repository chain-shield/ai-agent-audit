# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Users can keep on withdrawing locked funds even after the proposed 7 days period up until the `convertAllETH` is called.

## Proof of Concept
The checks in the `withdraw` function focus on the set variables for `loopActivation` and `startClaimDate` during non-emergencies. This implies that the 7 days are not enforced and allows users to still withdraw after 7 days. The impact of this is loss of funds of 0x protocol as locked funds are still withdrawn even after 7 days and before `convertAllETH` is called, even though `convertAllETH` might be in the mempool to be executed.

## Tools Used

Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Implement a similar logic of TimeLock to withdraw as done in the `convertAllETH` function, to invalidate all withdrawal txns after 7 days have passed.
