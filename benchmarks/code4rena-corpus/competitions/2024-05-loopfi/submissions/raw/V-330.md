# Vulnerability details

## Impact

The [_claim](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L240C1-L266C6) function is called by users to claim their converted lpETH tokens after the [global ETH to lpETH conversion](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L315C5-L330C6) is completed.

In addition to the previously reported from me vulnerability in [convertAllETH()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L315C5-L330C6) where ETH user balances are not reset to 0, another issue exists. This issue, combined with the previous one, could allow malicious users to claim more lpETH than they are entitled to.

The problem lies in the handling of `totalLpETH`, which during the [claim process](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L240C1-L266C6) with ETH as the `_token` argument the protocol does not subtract. Additionally, `totalSupply` isn't substracted during the conversion process within [convertAllETH()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L315C5-L330C6).

Both `totalSupply` and `totalLpETH` are used to calculate the [claimedAmount](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L249) using the OZ [mulDiv()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/lib/openzeppelin-contracts/contracts/utils/math/Math.sol#L123) function, which is mathematically equivalent to:

$$ \frac{userStake * totalLpETH}{totalSupply} $$

Since totalLpETH and totalSupply are not updated based on the actual ETH balances, users claiming with ETH across multiple wallets can receive a higher amount of lpETH tokens than intended.

## Proof of Concept

Consider the following scenario, tested with a runnable PoC:

1. Simo and Pesho, 2 friends, create 3 wallets each and lock 1 ETH per wallet using the [lockETH()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L124C3-L126C6) function. This locks a total of 6 ETH in the contract. 

2. The owner calls [setLoopAddresses()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L348C1-L358C6) setting the loop and the vault addresses. This sets [loopActivation](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L355) which ends the 1st epoch and the 7 days period users have to withdraw their locked tokens starts.

3. The 7 days passed and the owner calls [convertAllETH](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L315C1-L330C6), converting all 6 ETH to 6 lpETH, but without setting the ETH balances to 0. The function also sets [startClaimDate](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L327), allowing both friends to claim their lpETH tokens.

4. Simo and Pesho claim their lpETH through [claim()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L211C3-L216C6) with lpETH as _token, resulting in they having now 6 lpETH token.

5. Both have solidity knowledge, so they previously checked the smart contract of the protocol and noticed the ETH user balances not set to 0 problem in [convertAllETH](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L315C1-L330C6), together with the not updating of `totalLpETH` and `totalSupply` problems. 

6. Since they previously locked ETH in the contract, they know that their total ETH balances still reflect the old balance(1 ETH per wallet, 6 ETH in total).

7. They exploit this vulnerability by calling [claim()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L211C3-L216C6) with each wallet using the following parameters:

| **Parameters** | **Values** |
|----------------|------------|
| userStake      | 1 ETH == 1e18      |
| totalLpEth     | 6 ETH == 6e18     |
| totalSupply    | 6 ETH == 6e18     |

8. The [claim()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L211C3-L216C6) function calls [_claim()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L240C4-L266C6) which sets userStake to the old ETH balances (1 ETH). The [if (_token == ETH)](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L248) check passes. 

9. The `claimedAmount` is calculated using the following formula:
 $$ \frac{userStake * totalLpETH}{totalSupply} $$ 

10. Since `totalLpETH` and `totalSupply` remain fixed due to the lack of updates, the total claimed amount calculation called with different addresses will look like this:

| **Nº**    | **Calculation**                                        | **Claimed amount** |
|-----------|--------------------------------------------------------|-------------------|
| 1st claim | 1e18(userStake) * 6e18(totalLpEth) / 6e18(totalSupply) | 1e18              |
| 2nd claim | 1e18(userStake) * 6e18(totalLpEth) / 6e18(totalSupply) | 1e18              |
| ...       | ...                                                    | ...               |
| **Total** |                                                        | 6e18 + 6e18             |

However, if `totalLpEth` were updated correctly it would look like this:

| **Nº**    | **Calculation**                                        | **Claimed amount** |
|-----------|--------------------------------------------------------|-------------------|
| 1st claim | 1e18(userStake) * 6e18(totalLpEth) / 6e18(totalSupply) | 1e18              |
| 2nd claim | 1e18(userStake) * 5e18(totalLpEth) / 6e18(totalSupply) | 1e18              |
| ...       | ...                                                    | ...               |
| **Total** |                                                        | 3.5e18 + 6e18                |

This results in Simo and Pesho claiming much more lpETH than they should have claimed.
