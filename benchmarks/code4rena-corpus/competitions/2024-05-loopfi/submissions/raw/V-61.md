# Vulnerability details

## Summary
 A single owner controls critical functions in the contract, creating a central point of trust and potential failure.

## Vulnerability Details
The `PrelaunchPoints` smart contract designates an owner address with exclusive authority over several important administrative functions. This introduces risks associated with centralized governance models, including potential misuse of power or loss of control if the owner's private key is compromised.
## Impact
Such a high level of centralization compromises decentralization principles inherent to blockchain systems. It can lead to unilateral decision-making that may not be in the best interest of all stakeholders, potentially undermining trust in the protocol and posing security threats.

## Proof of Concept
```solidity
function setOwner(address _owner) external onlyAuthorized {
    owner = _owner;
    emit OwnerUpdated(_owner);
}

modifier onlyAuthorized() {
    require(msg.sender == owner, "Not authorized");
}
```
