# Vulnerability details


## Impact
The current implementation of the `claim` function violates the 1:1 ETH to LPETH conversion invariant in the contract. 

doc  >>      Users that deposit ETH/WETH get the correct amount of lpETH on claim (1 to 1 conversion)
doc  >>       Users that deposit LRTs get the correct amount assuming a favorable swap to ETH 

https://code4rena.com/audits/2024-05-loopfi#top:~:text=Users%20that%20deposit,swap%20to%20ETH.


When swapping ERC20 tokens for ETH and subsequently converting the ETH to LPETH, the total supply of ETH remains unchanged, while the total supply of LPETH is incremented. This discrepancy breaks the intended 1:1 conversion ratio between ETH and LPETH, leading to inaccuracies in the representation of funds within the contract.

## Proof of Concept
The violation of the 1:1 ETH to LPETH conversion invariant can be observed in the following code snippet:

```solidity
// Swap token to ETH
_fillQuote(IERC20(_token), userClaim, _data);

// Convert swapped ETH to lpETH (1 to 1 conversion)
claimedAmount = address(this).balance;
lpETH.deposit{value: claimedAmount}(_receiver);
```

This code converts the claimed ETH to LPETH without updating the total supply of ETH in the contract, resulting in an incorrect representation of the ETH to LPETH conversion ratio.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
To address the violation and ensure adherence to the 1:1 ETH to LPETH conversion ratio, the following mitigation steps are recommended:

 **Update Total Supply**: Update the total supply of ETH in the contract with the claimed amount that will be converted to LPETH before the conversion takes place. This ensures that the total supply of ETH accurately reflects the amount converted to LPETH.

```solidity
// Update total supply of ETH
++          totalSupply += claimedAmount;
            lpETH.deposit{value: claimedAmount}(_receiver);
```
