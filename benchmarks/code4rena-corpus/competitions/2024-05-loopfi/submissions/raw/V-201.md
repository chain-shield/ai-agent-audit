# Vulnerability details

## Impact
**`_claim()`** is an internal function utilized by various functions to claim  the LP tokens to users. However, a issue arises when tokens other than ETH are involved. In such cases, a token-to-ETH swap is executed without any slippage protection, potentially resulting in financial losses for users.
## Proof of Concept
**`_claim()`** is used to calim the LP for the users.
