# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The ``convertAllETH`` function currently lacks a check to verify if the contract has sufficient ETH balance before attempting the conversion to ``lpETH`` tokens. This could lead to failed transactions if the balance is zero. It's crucial to ensure the contract has enough ETH to fulfill the conversion, especially considering the protocol offers a 7-day grace period for withdrawals after ``loopActivation``.
