# Vulnerability details

## Description
The `withdraw` function in the `PrelaunchPoints` contract fails to update the contract's internal record of the user's locked balance `(balances[msg.sender][_token])` when withdrawing a token other than ETH `(_token != ETH)`. This means that, after a successful withdrawal, the user's balance in the contract remains unchanged. As a result, an attacker could potentially call withdraw for the same non-ETH token multiple times and siphon off all the tokens they previously locked, even though the contract still shows their balance as locked.
## Impact
Users could lose their locked tokens if an attacker exploits this vulnerability

## Proof of Concept
An attacker deposits 100 units of Token A into the contract.
The attacker calls withdraw for Token A, successfully receiving 100 units.
However, the contract's internal record of the attacker's balance for Token A remains at 100 units (vulnerability).
The attacker can now call withdraw again for Token A, even though they have already withdrawn all their tokens. This could potentially be repeated until the contract's total supply of Token A is depleted

Manual 
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Update User Balance Before Transfer
