# Vulnerability details

## Impact
This implementation leads to a situation where ETH depositors always receive rewards that do not accurately reflect their share of the total staked value.i.e they always receive less rewarads than they deserve


## Proof of Concept
The contract differentiates between ETH/WETH deposits and other allowed tokens in two critical areas: how totalSupply is affected by deposits and the calculation of rewards during the claim process.
Deposits:
For ETH and WETH deposits, the contract increments totalSupply, which represents the total locked/staked value within the contract.
For deposits of other allowed tokens, totalSupply is not incremented

Claim:
The contract calculates rewards for ETH depositors based on their stake's proportion to totalSupply, directly linking their rewards to the total staked value i.e totalSupply.
but here `totalSupply is ETH+WETH` deposited due to this the user stake proportion to totalsupply calculates incorrectly.here it should be only ETH totalSupply otherwise Eth depositers will always get less claimAmount due to the totalSupply i.e ETH+WETH

For other tokens, including `WETH`, rewards are calculated based on the market value of the tokens at the time of conversion to ETH and then to lpETH, without a direct proportionality to totalSupply.
