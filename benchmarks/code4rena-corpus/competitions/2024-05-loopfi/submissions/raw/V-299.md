# Vulnerability details

## Impact

[convertAllETH()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L315C1-L330C6) is used to convert all the locked ETH in the contract to lpETH and give the start to the 2nd epoch after the gap of 7 days user have to withdraw their tokens in case they changed their mind, or they detected a malicious contract being set, between the epochs has passed. 

This conversion works correctly, but there's a problem: It doesn't update the  `totalSupply` which tracks all the ETH/WETH deposited. In the [convertAllETH()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L315C1-L330C6) function the ETH in the contract goes to 0, because of being converted to lpETH, so after the conversion, the totalSupply still holds the old value (total ETH deposited before conversion), which is incorrect. 

This can result in wrong calculations.

## Proof of Concept

https://gist.github.com/cholakovvv/5466b51cd5002e82ab618ccd6864e538

## Tools Used

Manual review, Foundry

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Ensure when converting all ETH to lpETH that the `totalSupply` is updated.
