# Vulnerability details

## Impact
A user can intentionally provides their own address as the _referral parameter. This could lead to incorrect referral tracking and reward distribution.

## Proof of Concept
In the lock and lockFor functions including the _processLock function, there is no validation or check to ensure that the msg.sender (the address calling the function) is not the same as the _referral address provided as a parameter. This lack of validation can lead to potential issues and unintended behavior.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Check that msg.sender is not referral address


## Assessed type

Access Control
