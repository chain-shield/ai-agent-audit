# Vulnerability details

## Impact

The current ownership transfer process involves the current owner calling ```PrelaunchPoints::setOwner```. This function does not checks the new owner is not the zero address and proceeds to write the new owner's address into the owner's state variable. If the nominated new owner is not a valid account, it is entirely possible the owner may accidentally transfer ownership to an uncontrolled account, breaking all functions with the onlyAuthorized() modifier.

## Proof of Concept

1. Alice is the owner of the contract
2. Alice may mistakenly choose a new owner with address(0), or set an inappropriate owner, e.g. Bob, who may not know he is the new owner, or be malicious, or just a random address.

In both scenarios of point 2, functions that have the modifier onlyAuthorized will become unusable and this will cause the protocol to behave inappropriately. 

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Implement zero address check and consider implementing a two step process where the owner nominates an new owner and the nominated new owner needs to call an acceptOwnership() function for the transfer of ownership to fully succeed. This ensures the nominated PrelaunchPoints owner is a valid and active address.
