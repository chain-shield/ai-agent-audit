# Vulnerability details

## Summary
The `emergencyMode` feature in the contract can override `timelocks`, potentially enabling premature actions that could lead to fund loss if misused.
## Vulnerability Details
The smart contract includes an `emergencyMode` `boolean` flag which, when activated by the owner via the `setEmergencyMode()` function, disables certain time-based restrictions. This allows for immediate withdrawal of funds irrespective of predefined lock periods or claim schedules.
## Impact
If used properly during genuine emergencies, this feature can help protect user assets. However, if misused or activated maliciously due to a compromised owner account or poor governance decision-making processes it poses significant risk allowing unexpected asset transfers leading potential financial losses for stakeholders involved within protocol operations
