# Vulnerability details

## Impact
According to the document, tokens like `WETH, weETH, ezETH, rsETH, rswETH, uniETH, pufETH` are used by the protocol. However, some tokens like `ezETH` are volatile in price. Under such conditions, user could suffer from extreme marketing conditions.

## Proof of Concept
Tokens like `ezETH` are volatile in price and could [depeg unexpectedly](https://coinmarketcap.com/community/articles/662f51228576ff40e27bae36/).

Using such token as LRT could expose user to extreme marketing conditions and cause unexpected user fund loss.

Especially, consider the following case:
1. User A lock 100 `ezETH` into the contract.
2. Currently the claim process has started and user A can't withdraw.
3. The price of `ezETH` has dropped, user A does not want to trade it for lpETH at the moment, but he cannot withdraw his funds. This could lead to the inefficient of fund-management.

## Tools Used
Manual

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Use more stable `wrapped-eth` as the allowed-token.
