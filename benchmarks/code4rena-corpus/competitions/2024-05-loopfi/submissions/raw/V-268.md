# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The  ETH is forever locked in the contract after the `convertAllETH` function is called

## Proof of Concept
After all the locked ETH is converted to `lpETH` any ETH send to the contract intentionally or not will be locked forever.
It is always bad to have locked ether somewhere not for the protocol safety, but just for the missed opportunities of it.

## Tools Used
manual review

## Recommended Mitigation
consider adding a function, which can be called only by the owner, where he can withdraw the ETH, just so it can be actually used or returned and not stuck in the contract forever without any use of it.
