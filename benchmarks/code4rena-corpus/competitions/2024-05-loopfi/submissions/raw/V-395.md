# Vulnerability details

## Impact
LpETH doesn't apply 1:1 ratio with ETH as stated in the docs.

## Proof of Concept
A ratio is defined as a comparison of two quantities. The general form of representing a ratio between two quantities say 'a' and 'b' is a: b, which is read as 'a is to b'. 1: 1 means one part of 'a' is exactly equal to one part of 'b'.

1 : 1 = 2 : 2 = 3 : 3 = ...... = a : a

Therefore, a 1: 1 ratio means the compared items are equal in quantity with respect to each other.

According to docs, this should be 1:1 ratio .
>All deposits are converted to ETH to be liquidity for Loopers (borrowers). For every ETH deposited, you get lpETH at a 1:1 rate. 

https://docs.loopfi.xyz/the-launch/the-points-program#what-happens-after-the-points-program-has-concluded

The [current formala](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/0dc8467ccff27230e7c0530b619524cc8401e22a/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L249-L250) is used in the protocol:
`claimedAmount = userStake.mulDiv(totalLpETH, totalSupply)` ,  this calculation doesn't guarantee full precision.
Instead, the `claimedAmount` (lpETH) should equal `userStake` (ETH) amount since totalLpETH equals totalSupply
