# Vulnerability details

## Impact
A user will not be able to withdraw ETH due to insufficient gas if that user stakes very little ETH/dust amount ex., 1 wei by calling ```lockETH``` and calls the ```withdraw``` function.

## Proof of Concept
Due to the lack of a minimum amount to stake or lock ETH, there is a chance of a possible denial of service if a user calls ```withdraw``` after staking a dust amount like 1 wei.

Exploit Scenario:
Alice calls ```lockETH``` and stakes 1 wei.
Alice calls ```withdraw``` to get back 1 wei.
```withdraw``` fails due to insufficient gas => DOS 

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Implement a minimum ETH amount that can be staked.


## Assessed type

DoS
