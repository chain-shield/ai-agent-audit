# Vulnerability details

## Impact

[_fillQuote](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L491-L505) is used to get the LRT converted into ETH by using the 0x protocol.

However, there is an issue with how the [boughtETHAmount](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L493) variable is calculated. At the beginning of the function it's set to the [current contract's balance (address(this).balance)](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L493)). After the swap call, it's then [subtracted from the contract's balance to determine the amount of ETH acquired](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L503). This subtraction results in `address(this).balance - address(this).balance`, which will always be 0.

This incorrect calculation of [boughtETHAmount](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-loop/blob/main/src/PrelaunchPoints.sol#L493) could lead to inaccurate calculations of the swapped tokens, because of the usage the protocol do with the events demonstrated in the Proof of Concept section.
