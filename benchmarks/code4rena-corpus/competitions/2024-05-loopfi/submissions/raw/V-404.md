# Vulnerability details

## Impact

Users earn points based on how many lpETH they hold, since 1 lpETH equals 1 ETH, this can be calculated through the amount deposited in lock method. The issue is, when users claim their lpETH, the received ETH is likely not 1:1 plus some fees go to the 0xProtocol. This diff difference is not tracked, leading to incorrect tracking of how many points the user should earn.


## Proof of Concept

The Scenario

- Bob locked X amount of an LRT token
- Lock method emitted event with X amount
- The backend use this amount as a base to calculate their earned points
- Claim date is active
- Bob claimed their X amount
- On claim, X amount is changed to ETH
- The ETH received is likely lower than X. because of a price change in the market and the fee taken from 0xProtocol
- Bob now receives lpETH less than X
- However, Bob now earn points based on X amount, although he contributed with less than that. 


## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Track fees, let the backend handle the difference. This will be fair to other users
