# Vulnerability details

## Description 

In the smart contract, the startClaimDate is initialized to the maximum value for a uint32, which corresponds to the year 2106. This makes it impossible for users to claim or convert ETH to lpETH as intended.

The functions affected are:

`PrelaunchPoints:claim`
`PrelaunchPoints:claimAndStake`
`PrelaunchPoints:convertAllETH()`

Each of these functions has a modifier that either restricts execution before or after the startClaimDate. Due to the initialized value of startClaimDate, these functions are essentially unusable.

## Impact
Users are unable to claim their tokens or convert ETH to lpETH as intended. This impacts both user experience and the intended functionality of the contract. Additionally, it prevents users from withdrawing ETH through the withdraw function, leading to potential loss of funds.
