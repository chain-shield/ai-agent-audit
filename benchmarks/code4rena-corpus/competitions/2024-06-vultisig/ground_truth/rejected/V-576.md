## Brief Summary

When another user opens a buy position for a given `recipient`, the `raiseAmount` is transferred from the user's account to the contract. However, when `claimRefund()` is called, all raise tokens are sent to the `recipient` i.e `tokenOwner`, not the user who supplied them.
