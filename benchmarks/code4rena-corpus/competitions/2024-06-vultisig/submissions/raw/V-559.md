# Vulnerability details

## Impact
`initProject` is permissionless and allows the caller to specify the `params.launchTime`

If the attacker provides a `params.launchTime` that is 100 years in the future then `refundDeadline = params.launchTime + DEFAULT_DEADLINE_OFFSET` which will also be > 100 years later.

Any investors that buy in will not be able to refund or launch for > 100 years. They have essentially lost their funds


## Proof of Concept
Described above

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
The launchTime param must be restricted to reasonable values
