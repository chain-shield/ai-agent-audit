# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The uniswapV3MintCallback function in the LiquidityManagement contract does not check the contract's token balances before attempting to transfer tokens to the Uniswap pool. This can lead to transaction failures if the contract does not have sufficient tokens to cover the transfer amounts (amount0 and amount1).

If the contract does not have sufficient balance of token0 or token1, the safeTransfer call will fail, causing the entire uniswapV3MintCallback function to revert. This will halt the minting process initiated by the Uniswap pool.

Functions that rely on successful minting operations with Uniswap pools could be disrupted. For example, the [lanching of uniswap pool may halt](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-vultisig/blob/cb72b1e9053c02a58d874ff376359a83dc3f0742/src/ILOPool.sol#L269)

## Proof of Concept
The payment to the univ3 pool is done through the [pay function](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-vultisig/blob/cb72b1e9053c02a58d874ff376359a83dc3f0742/src/base/PeripheryPayments.sol#L21), which has 3 check blocks . If the token is not WETH it will enter the 2nd/3rd block where it may revert due to the following reasons

> Insufficient balance in user's account
> Insufficient approval for the contract to spend the tokens
> Being blacklisted or frozen or paused tokens
