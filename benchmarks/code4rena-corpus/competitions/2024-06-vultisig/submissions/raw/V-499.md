# Vulnerability details

## Description
In the readme, its mentioned: Anyone can launch the pool when all conditions met.
Consider these possible scenarios:

- A denial of service attack on the blockchain has prevented transactions occurring for a significant period of time.
- An extreme spike in gas prices has prevented transactions occurring for a significant period of time.
- Some unforeseen technical error takes the blockchain down, perhaps during an upgrade or fork.

Now due to any of the above scenarios the [oldest observation](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-vultisig/blob/cb72b1e9053c02a58d874ff376359a83dc3f0742/hardhat-vultisig/contracts/oracles/uniswap/UniswapV3Oracle.sol#L39) is more than 30 minutes due to which default [PERIOD](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-vultisig/blob/cb72b1e9053c02a58d874ff376359a83dc3f0742/hardhat-vultisig/contracts/oracles/uniswap/UniswapV3Oracle.sol#L41) will be used to get TWAT(time-weighted average tick).
These 30 minutes give a perfect time window for a malicious act to be perform as you can see here in the [chart](https://x.com/bantg/status/1455151835828867079) a moment where DUCK/WETH@1% went to zero in a 30min TWAT oracle
## Impact
Malicious actors could use the current [PERIOD](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-vultisig/blob/cb72b1e9053c02a58d874ff376359a83dc3f0742/hardhat-vultisig/contracts/oracles/uniswap/UniswapV3Oracle.sol#L16) time window to swap at an inflation rate
## Proof of Concept
If, due to any scenario, the next call to observation is after 30 minutes, the attacker could use this timeframe window and just after 30 minutes tick calculated value, swap the affected tokens at a high inflation rate.

## Tools Used
Manual review
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
The advice would be to increase the period time to make sure the protocol oracle is more secure in high volatility time or calculate avg observations time and if the obs value is above this simply reject it or put a warning flag
