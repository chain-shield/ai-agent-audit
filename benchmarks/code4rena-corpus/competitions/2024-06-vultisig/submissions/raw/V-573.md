# Vulnerability details

## Vulnerability Analysis


I would like to hint that i do not know if this is a vulnerability or an inherent risk that's not documented by the Vultisig Team yet. But i wanted to draw attention to it anyways, so i report it here.


1. In the [claimRefund] function, the investor's tokenId is cleared from the positions array, and then burnt. 

2. If an investor later calls [claim] in an attempt to pull his or her liquidity, it reverts as there is no liquidity associated with a non-existent tokenId.






## Impact
INVESTORS LOSE THEIR LIQUIDITY
Investors who had mistakenly called `[claimRefund]` will lose their associated liquidity.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
I recommend that, when an investor calls [claimRefund], he be sent his liquidity in addition to his raiseAmount automatically...ie, before clearing user's position, and burning his or her tokenId, call [claim] as it is independent of a position.raiseAmount value.  That is refactor the code to include `claim(tokenId)` as shown below.

```
+ claim(tokenId);
+ delete position(tokenId);
+ delete positionsVests(tokenId);
+ burn(tokenId);

```


2. However, if this is by protocol design, then I recommend that the Vultisig Team documents this inherent risks so that Investors become aware of this danger.
