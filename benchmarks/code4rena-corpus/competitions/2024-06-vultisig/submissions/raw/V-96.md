# Vulnerability details

# [M-01] Whitelisted Addresses Late to Purchase Receive Fewer VULTs for Same ETH Paid, Potentially Leading to Gas Race and Front-running Issues
## Impact
The design of Uniswap liquidity pools inherently reduces the amount of VULT tokens allocated to users as more participants engage in purchasing. Although this reduction is balanced by the fluctuating price, it may inadvertently introduce a competitive dynamic known as a "gas race." Users eager to secure VULT tokens at the earliest opportunity might rush to complete their transactions, leading to higher transaction fees and potential front-running issues.
This discrepancy is not negligible and can be substantiated through a proof of concept demonstrating the decreasing allocation of VULT tokens to late purchasers.
## Proof of Concept
Consider a scenario ( current `Whitelist.ts test` scenario) where the pool initially contains `10,000,000 VULT` tokens and `26 ETH`,
disregarding transaction fees for simplicity.
Also note that this is probably an exterme Scenario and if the intial ETH amount is higher the discrepancy wont be this wide but the problem will still persist.
The first participant receives `1,034,482.76 VULT` tokens for a `3 ETH` deposit,
calculated like this:
Given the Uniswap Invariant Without fees for simplicity:
\[x \times y = K\]

Where:
- \(x\) represents the initial amount of VULT tokens in the pool.
- \(y\) represents the initial amount of ETH in the pool.
- \(K\) is a constant representing the product of \(x\) and \(y\).

For the first transaction, we calculate:
\[10,000,000 \times 26 = x_1 \times 29\]

To find \(x_1\), the amount of VULT tokens remaining after a certain amount of ETH (\(y\)) has been swapped for VULT tokens, we solve for \(x_1\):
\[x_1 = \frac{10,000,000 \times 26}{29}\]

Then, we calculate the user's balance (\(user_b\)), which represents the amount of VULT tokens a user receives for swapping ETH for VULT tokens:
\[user_b = 10,000,000 - \frac{10,000,000 \times 26}{29} = 1,034,482.76\]

and the second person will get  `840,517.24 VULT tokens` (8.9M - 8.9M*29/32). Subsequent participants receive fewer VULT tokens, with the hundredth participant receiving less than `7,400 VULT tokens`.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
1. **Implement a Fair Launch Mechanism**: Consider launching the token sale in a way that ensures equal access to all participants, regardless of their timing. This could involve setting a fixed price or using a airdrop system with a fixed payment fee (3 ETH) to allocate tokens.
2. **Review and Adjust Pool Parameters**: Regularly review the performance of the Uniswap pool and adjust parameters as needed to mitigate the impact of increasing participation on token allocations. e.g. increase the intial ETH balance in contrast with purchase per address cap. so purchase cap to initial ETH balance ration is a small number.
3. **Explore Alternative Distribution Methods**: Investigate alternative methods for distributing tokens, such as direct sales or auctions, to provide more control over the allocation process and potentially reduce the incentive for early participation.
