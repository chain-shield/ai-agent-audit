# Vulnerability details

The claim function includes sending the ILO platform and performance fees to the feeTaker address for every claim call. 

According to the readme, tokens with blacklists (eg. USDC is even used in tests) are in scope. 

When investors call claim() to get their unlocked liquidity back, every call will try to push the fee amounts to the ILO feeTaker address. But if the raiseToken is for eg. USDC and the feeTaker address has been blacklisted by USDC, these token transfers will fail. 

This will prevent investors from getting their portion of unlocked liquidity.

## Impact
This will cause a temporary DOS on the claim functionality. When the ILOManager owner notices this, they can change the feeTaker address and the claim calls would start working correctly again. 

But unlocked liquidity will be inaccessible for some time, and we cannot guarantee how much time the team can take to notice this. This breaks an important contract functionality. 


## Proof of Concept
ILOPool.claim() function has this logic at the end :

```
address feeTaker = IILOManager(MANAGER).FEE_TAKER();
        // transfer fee to fee taker
        TransferHelper.safeTransfer(_cachedPoolKey.token0, feeTaker, amountCollected0-amount0);

```

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Use a pull over push approach. Collect all fees and store its value in a variable, add a new collectFees() function accessible by the ILOManager owner.
