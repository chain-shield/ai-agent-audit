# Vulnerability details

# Small buy can round down to zero liquidity
## Lines of code
https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-vultisig/blob/cb72b1e9053c02a58d874ff376359a83dc3f0742/src/ILOPool.sol#L161-L167

## Description
When a small amount of tokens is bought, the amount of liquidity associated with the raised amount can be rounded down to zero. This can lead to a situation where the investor is not allocated any liquidity. In this way, the investor would be losing money.

## Impact
* An investor can lose money when buying a small amount of tokens.
* The team and treasury can censor whitelisted addresses by buying a small amount of tokens in their name.

## POC
While the amount of liquidity provided by a user is [checked to ensure it does not round to zero](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-vultisig/blob/cb72b1e9053c02a58d874ff376359a83dc3f0742/src/ILOPool.sol#L161), this does not happen when liquidity is added to the user.

The math underlying the liquidity claim right for a user is:
$$Liquidity Right = \frac{\Delta L \times vestingConfig[0].shares}{BPS}$$

If $\Delta L \times vestingConfig[0].shares < BPS$, the function `ILOPool.buy(uint256, address)` won't revert.

On the other hand, the team and treasury can censor a whitelisted investor by buying in their name (assigning the recipient to a whitelisted address), [given that the position's raised amount is increased](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-vultisig/blob/cb72b1e9053c02a58d874ff376359a83dc3f0742/src/ILOPool.sol#L152).
