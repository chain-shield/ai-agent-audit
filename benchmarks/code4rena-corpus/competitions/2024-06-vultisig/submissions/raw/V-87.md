# Vulnerability details


## Impact
When the softCap is greater than hardCap, the project launch will always fail.
The `initILOPool` function does not have a validation to ensure that softCap is less than hardCap in the initILOPool function.

## Proof of Concept
User can't buy more than hardCap in the `buy` function. [buy](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-vultisig/blob/main/src/ILOPool.sol#L136)
So, the `totalRaise` can't exceed the `hardCap` meaning the `totalRaise` can't exceed the `softCap` as well.
The launch function always revert if the `totalRaise` is less than the `softCap`. [launch](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-vultisig/blob/main/src/ILOManager.sol#L274)
User funds will be locked in the contract until the `refundDeadline` passes.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Add the validation to check if the softCap is greater than the hardCap in the `initILOPool` function to prevent the project launch from failing.
