# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Tokens purchase through whitelist could be more expensive than purchase through Uniswap (or other DEX)

## Proof of Concept
5% slippage is considered to be a high amount for token conversions. Most non-exotic pairs on Uniswap can be converted using 0.3% to 1% slippage value.

For this reason, users could prefer to use Uniswap to buy tokens instead of using the whitelist to be allocated <x> tokens.

## Tools Used
NA

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
I would recommend removing slippage value or reducing it to a value between 0.3% to 1% to ensure buying tokens through whitelist is relatively cheaper (/competitive) than buying tokens off of Uniswap (or any other DEX)
