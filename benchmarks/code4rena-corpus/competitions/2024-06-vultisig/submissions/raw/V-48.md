## Impact
The require() check does not verify that initialPoolPriceX96 is less than sqrtRatioUpperX96. Instead, it checks if the upper bound is greater than the lower bound, which can be true even if initialPoolPriceX96  > upper bound. Without this condition, there is no guarantee that the initial pool price is below the upper tick ratio, which could lead to invalid configurations where the initial price is outside the specified range. Adding this condition ensures the initial pool price is properly constrained within the defined tick range.
