# Vulnerability details

## Summary
## Summary
The vulnerability in the Uniswap math libraries (`FullMath`, `Oraclelibrary` and `TickMath`) stems from the absence of the unchecked modifier in Solidity versions prior to 0.8.0. This oversight would lead to unintended reverts, affecting critical functionalities such as token swaps and liquidity management in UniswapV3.
## Impact
The absence of the `unchecked` modifier in these libraries could inadvertently cause transactions to revert when performing arithmetic operations that result in an overflow, despite the libraries' intention to handle "phantom overflow". This exist in the libraries `fullmath`, `oraclelibrary` and `tickmath` and will break the functionalities of the vultisig contracts dépending on them.
