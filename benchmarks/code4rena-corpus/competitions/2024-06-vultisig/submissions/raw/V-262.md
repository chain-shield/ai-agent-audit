# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Due to slippage, users may receive fewer tokens than expected.

## Proof of Concept
Function `claim` will burn users' liquidity and return some tokens(including token0 and token1). However, the returned amount is not checked, which may lead to user receive fewer tokens than expected.

## Tools Used
VScode

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Add slippage protect for amount0 and amount1.
