# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The `AddLiquidityParams` is used when adding liquidity as seen in the [Launch function](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-vultisig/blob/cb72b1e9053c02a58d874ff376359a83dc3f0742/src/ILOPool.sol#L296). In times of high network congestion, transactions can be delayed. Without a deadline, users transaction might remain pending for an extended period, during which market conditions can change. This could result in user's liquidity being added at an unfavorable price. 


## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Introduce a deadline in the `AddLiquidityParams` struct in `LiquidityManagement.sol` and use block.timestamp as value when deploying liquidity
