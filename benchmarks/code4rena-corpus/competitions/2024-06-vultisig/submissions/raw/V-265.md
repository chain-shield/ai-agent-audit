# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Some liquidity will be locked.

## Proof of Concept
In the function `launch`, the liquidity will be distributed to `projectConfig.recipient`s according to the `projectConfig.shares`. The sum of projectConfig.shares is 10000(i.e., BPS). However, the sum of `liquidityShares` will not be `liquidity` because of the remainder of division. Therefore, part of the liquidity will be locked.
```solidity
uint128 liquidityShares = uint128(FullMath.mulDiv(liquidity, projectConfig.shares, BPS));
```
## Tools Used
VScode

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Distribute the remaining liquidity to the owner or the last recipient.
