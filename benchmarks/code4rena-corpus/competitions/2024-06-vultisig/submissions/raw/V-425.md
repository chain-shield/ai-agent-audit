# Vulnerability details

## Impact
`tx.origin` can make your contract vulnerable to attacks 

## Proof of Concept

It is advised by industry experts to avoid the use `tx.origin` for authentication and authorization because of how insecure it is.

[here are some articles from solidity](https://docs.soliditylang.org/en/develop/security-considerations.html#tx-origin)
[Here are some articles from consensys](https://consensys.github.io/smart-contract-best-practices/development-recommendations/solidity-specific/tx-origin/)

## Tools Used
manual review 
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
use `msg.sender` instead
