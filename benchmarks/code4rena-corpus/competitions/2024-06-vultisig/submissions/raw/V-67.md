# Vulnerability details


## Impact
UniswapV3's `TickMath` library was updated to support Solidity version 0.8.
However, adjustments to handle the new overflow behavior introduced in Solidity 0.8 were not made in these `TickMath`, `FullMath` and `OracleLibrary`.
The `Whitelist` contract, which is compiled with Solidity 0.8.24, indirectly uses this library through the `UniswapV3Oracle`.
In the worst-case scenario, the library may always revert (due to the lack of overflow behavior handling) instead of overflowing as it did in previous versions, potentially leading to a broken `checkWhitelist` contract.

## Proof of Concept
The `pragma solidity >=0.5.0;` directive, instead of `pragma solidity >=0.5.0 <0.8.0;`, has been applied to the `OracleLibrary`, `FullMath` and `TickMath` contracts.
However, these contracts do not rely on implicit overflow behavior.
