# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The initProject function in the contract is susceptible to griefing vulnerability due to the manipulable nature of its parameter data. Malicious actors can exploit this vulnerability by copying parameter data from mempool transactions, enabling them to become administrators of existing Uniswap pools without authorization. This unauthorized access could lead to the manipulation of pool parameters and other critical functions.
