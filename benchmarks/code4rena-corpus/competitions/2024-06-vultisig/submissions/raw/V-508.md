# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The documentation says that only the project admin can launch project(aka launch all ilo pool).

[Link to the documentation 1](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-vultisig/blob/cb72b1e9053c02a58d874ff376359a83dc3f0742/README.md?plain=1#L224)

[Link to the documentation 2](https://ilo-docs.krystal.app/for-project-creators/run-a-private-sale)

However, the `launch(address uniV3PoolAddress)` function found in the `ILOManager.sol` contract is missing the `onlyProjectAdmin(uniV3PoolAddress)` modifier. Without the `onlyProjectAdmin(uniV3PoolAddress)` modifier, the `launch` function can be called by any address, regardless of their role or permissions within the project, without the control of the project admin. This bypasses essential access controls, allowing unauthorized addresses to launch projects and associated ILO pools. This lack of control can lead to unauthorized activities, misuse of the protocol, and potential fraud.
