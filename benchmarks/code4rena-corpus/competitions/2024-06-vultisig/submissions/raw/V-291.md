# Vulnerability details

In the `ILOPool.sol` contract the protocol may wrongly assume that it is a EIP-721 compliant. However it does not implement EIP-165 interface .

## Impact
It might cause integration problems in the future that can lead to wide range of issues for both parties.

## Proof of Concept
The protocol intended to check EIP-721 compliance.

> EIP compliance checklist
> Question	        Answer
> src/ILOPool.sol	EIP721 (or ERC721)

All official EIP-4626 requirements can be found on it's [official page](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-721#:~:text=Every%20ERC%2D721%20compliant%20contract%20must%20implement%20the%20ERC721%20and%20ERC165%20interfaces%20(subject%20to%20%E2%80%9Ccaveats%E2%80%9D%20below)%3A).

## Tools Used
Manual reveiw.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Consider adding the EIP-165 implementation as mention in their official page.
