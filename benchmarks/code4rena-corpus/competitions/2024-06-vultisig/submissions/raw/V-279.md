# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The absence of validation checks to prevent the pool price from being set above the upper limit could lead to several potential issues:

- Market Manipulation: The pool price could be used to artificially inflate or deflate the market price of the token.
- Arbitrage Exploitation: Arbitrageurs could exploit the price discrepancy between the pool and the market, leading to potential loss for other participants.
- Loss of Trust: Users may lose trust in the system if they notice that the pool price can exceed its supposed limit, potentially affecting the liquidity of the token.
- Unexpected Contract Behavior: Violation of the assumption that the pool price will never exceed the upper limit could lead to unexpected behavior and contract failure.
