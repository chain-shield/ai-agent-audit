# Vulnerability details



## Impact
Someone can effectively DoS every project launch.

## Proof of Concept
We have this validation:

    require(_cachedProject[uniV3PoolAddress].initialPoolPriceX96 == sqrtPriceX96, "UV3P");

[Line#190](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-vultisig/blob/cb72b1e9053c02a58d874ff376359a83dc3f0742/src/ILOManager.sol#L190)
which makes sure that the initialPoolPriceX96 set during the creation of the UniswapV3 pool in the _initUniV3PoolIfNecessary() is the same at the time the  ILOManager.launch() function is called.

ILOManager.launch() can only be called after launchTime has passed, and it is expected that launchTime would be at least a few days. This would give plenty of time for someone to just add liquidity to the UniswapV3 pool of the project and alter the sqrtPriceX96, which is retrieved from slot0:

    (uint160 sqrtPriceX96, , , , , , ) = IUniswapV3Pool(uniV3PoolAddress).slot0();

thus effectively breaking the launch.

Usually, the malicious actor would have the incentive to do it a little before the launchTime, so people would have to wait for nothing. This won't result in losing funds, because when the refundDeadline passes, people can take their **RAISE tokens back**. 

However, besides the reputation loss of the people who launch the project, with enough effort, the malicious actor could actually do this every time, thus **preventing every project launch.**

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
One approach I could think of is refactoring the logic so the UniswapV3 pool is created with the launch.
