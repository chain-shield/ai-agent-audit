# Vulnerability details

## Impact

Attackers can disrupt the launch by manipulating the price of the Uniswap V3 pool.

## Proof of Concept

As shown in the code at `L190`, the launch requires the price of the Uniswap V3 pool to remain unchanged. However, an attacker could exploit this by adding a small amount of liquidity to the Uniswap V3 pool and then executing a swap, which would change the pool price. This manipulation could potentially trigger the reversal of the launch.
