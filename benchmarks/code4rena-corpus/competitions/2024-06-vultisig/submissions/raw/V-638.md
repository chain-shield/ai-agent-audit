# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Given LOWER & UPPER ticks are not checked properly to ensure they include the spot price in-between. This can lead to honeypot like behaviour or just failing liquidity deposit attempts by the ILOPool.sol.

## Proof of Concept
In the contract ILOManager.sol (ln: 90) we see the following:
```
require(sqrtRatioLowerX96 < _project.initialPoolPriceX96 && sqrtRatioLowerX96 < sqrtRatioUpperX96, "RANGE");
```
The require checks that the lower tick is lower than the spot price & for the lower tick to be less than the upper tick. Missing the fact that the upper tick can be less than the current price. 

For simplicity choosing 1 as our lower tick, 5 as the spot price and 3 as our upper tick this would be a satisfactory range for the given require statement. The issue here, is that, given range is bellow the current spot price, which means the entire liquidity deposit will be in token1. So, on successful presales, the collateral accumulated will be the only thing to be deposited, or it will revert due to the first liquidity deposit not being able to be outside the range.

Possible Honeypot:
Since the ILOManager, optimistically trusts users inputs all over the contract, a user who mints a truthful ERC20 token, can previously make a liquidity deposit with a range of 3-6 of very small amounts, lets say 1e5 WETH & xe5 TOKENS.
This creates a bridge, which would NOW allow for the ILOPool.launch() to deposit liquidity, only the entire deposit would be in WETH. Now because most the liquidity remains in 1-3, the creator of the project can sell his reserves of the remaining ERC20 token, which are ALL the remaining tokens given to the project, since none were used in the liquidity deposit & sell them, while investors are kept waiting for vesting time to finish.

Regular reverts:
Due to uniswap, not accepting liquidity deposit outside of ranges or that dont connect to currently initialized ticks, the liquidity deposit would fail.

Note that these scenarios were including the IDEAL scenario of truthful ERC20's and creators following the sponsor's flow of use in their contracts.

## Tools Used
Manual analysis.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Users in web3 cant be trusted. You cant accept a random ERC20 from users, nor for them to pass correct parameters.
Also Uniswapv3 pools are very vulnerable if only 1 entity holds the tokens to deposit liquidity into them & shift the initial price of said pool, in this case manipulating where the WETH would go. Since the attack can be re-created by the admin just pushin the price above the given range by users.
