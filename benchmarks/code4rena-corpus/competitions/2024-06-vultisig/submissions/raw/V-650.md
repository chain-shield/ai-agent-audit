# Vulnerability details

## Impact

There is no check if the sequencer is still active in `UniswapV3Oracle`. The sequencer is responsible for ordering transactions and ensuring they are processed in a timely manner.

## Proof of Concept

The protocol will use EVM compatible chains that includes also L2 chains like Arbitrum. But in the L2 chains the sequencer is very important to order and process transactions. Verifying the sequencer's operational status is essential for maintaining data reliability and accuracy, especially for applications that require real-time information.

If the sequencer is an L2 chain, the time-weighted price will be extrapolated from the last known price once the sequencer is back online. This creates an opportunity to execute transactions at the outdated price before it updates. Even when the new price is observed, the sequencer will assume the previous price remained valid until it resumed operation, leading to a gradual, time-weighted adjustment back to the current price.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Check if the sequencer is active or use Chainlink Oracle for L2 chains.
