# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Gas limitation when using the ```transfer```

When calling the ```transfer```  function to send ETH from a contract to the sender's address, there is a limit on the amount of gas (2300 gas) that is allocated to perform the transaction. This limit may not be sufficient if the sender is a smart contract that requires more gas to perform its actions, resulting in an error and transaction failure.

## Proof of Concept

If the sender is a smart contract, a call to ```payable(_msgSender()).transfer(msg.value)``` may fail due to insufficient gas. This will result in a loss of funds and an inability to complete the operation, which compromises the integrity and reliability of the system.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

To avoid gas limitation problems, it is recommended to use the ```call``` method instead of ```transfer```. The ```call``` method does not have a strict limitation on the amount of gas and allows transferring the entire remaining gas quota, which ensures successful execution of the transaction.

The use of ```call``` in conjunction with call success verification allows for reliable refunds even if the recipient is a smart contract.
