# Vulnerability details

##Vulnerability Details
The `peek` function in the `UniswapV3Oracle` hard-codes a 5% slippage protection, which may not optimally adapt to various market conditions such as high volatility or liquidity imbalances.

## Impact
The primary concern is the potential for inaccuracies in price estimations due to the fixed 5% slippage protection, which could lead to for example reduced market efficiency.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
There are 2 ways to mitigate this issue. First workaround is to implement a slippage protection that is set by the operator/manager of the contract and the second more preferable way is to allow users to determine the maximum slippage they are willing to take. Thic can be further implemented on the front-end.

The latter approach is the better choice since the market could change fast before the operator can apply the necessary slippage changes.
