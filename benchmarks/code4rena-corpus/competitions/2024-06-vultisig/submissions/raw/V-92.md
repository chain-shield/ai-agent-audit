# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The Ownable contract is not initialised in the Whitelist code. This lead to not having an owner hence all functions which are requiring it, will not work. 

## Proof of Concept
All functions with onlyOwner modifier are not working. 

## Tools Used

Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Initialise the Owner in the constructor of the Whitelist.sol when deploying it.

Access Control
