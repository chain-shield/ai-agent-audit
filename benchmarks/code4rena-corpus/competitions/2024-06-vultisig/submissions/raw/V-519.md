# Vulnerability details

## Impact
`ILOPool` is an `ERC721` token that can be traded freely on the secondary markets (example Opensea). These NFTs are tied to vesting balances in the `ILOPool` contract. Since the owner of the nft can call the `ILOPool::claim()` to claim his vested amount at any moment (after launch), it exposes buyers of NFTs on secondary markets to the risk of acquiring devalued assets.

## Proof of Concept
Consider the following scenario :

- Alice mints an NFT tied to a vested position in the pool after calling `buy` function.
- Alice decides to sell the NFT on a secondary market like OpenSea after discussing the terms with a buyer.
- Prior to the sale, Alice claims the accrued vested tokens associated with the NFT, significantly reducing the value tied to the NFT.
- At the same time the buyer sends buying TX and receives the NFT with much less vested amounts than he expected, overpaying for the the asset.
## Tools Used

Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Consider adding functionality that pauses claims or refunds when the NFT is for sale.
