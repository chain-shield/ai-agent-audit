# Vulnerability details

## Impact
It is possible for an investor to have two NFT tokenIds if someone sold their position or whitelist spot to them. But the current implementation, hardcodes 0 when retrieving the tokenId for the investor through tokenOfOwnerByIndex [here](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-vultisig/blob/cb72b1e9053c02a58d874ff376359a83dc3f0742/src/ILOPool.sol#L147). 

This forces the investor to only be able to use their first tokenId instead of the second one they received. 

## Proof of Concept

1. Investor B provides A with their tokenId by transferring it.

2. Investor A decides to add liquidity to the tokenId they just received using the [buy()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-vultisig/blob/cb72b1e9053c02a58d874ff376359a83dc3f0742/src/ILOPool.sol#L126) function. But since A cannot pass in index 1 (representing the 2nd tokenId they have) as a parameter, they are forced to use the first ID at index0.
