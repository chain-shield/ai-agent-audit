# Vulnerability details

When a project is launched, all associated ILOPools get launched. This process requires an ILOPool to mint the pre-calculated liquidity in the uniswap v3 pool and sending the required amount of tokens to the uniswap pool from within the uniswapV3MintCallback(). 

This will fail if the ILOPool contract does not have enough SALE_TOKEN because the transfer of this token will fail. There is no check anywhere to force the project admin to send required SALE_TOKEN amount to the ILOPools while initializing an ILOPool.

This means while the project and the ILOPools have been initialized and investors are locking their raise tokens in the contracts, the ILOPool contract might not even have any amount of SALE_TOKEN, because that is completely dependent on the project admin's wish. 

Eventually, when the launch time arrives and the project has not sent any SALE_TOKEN, launching will fail. 

## Impact
Project can intentionally not send any SALE_TOKEN to any deployed ILOPools, which will cause launch to fail. Investors who had locked their raiseToken liquidity will need to wait for the whole "pre-launch investing duration" + refund deadline until they can finally get back their tokens.

The default refund deadline is 7 days, and if we assume 1 month of pre-launch duration, the investors' funds get locked for 37 days, in return for nothing.

Investors face opportunity loss, and lose gas money, while they could have simply invested these funds to earn yield somewhere else. 

Project launch can also fail due to other reasons, like not reaching softCap, but that's a reasonable risk already known to the investors and independent of possible rugs. While the issue presented here is unreasonable. 

## Proof of Concept
launch() calls [addLiquidity()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-vultisig/blob/cb72b1e9053c02a58d874ff376359a83dc3f0742/src/ILOPool.sol#L296) => calls [pool.mint()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-vultisig/blob/cb72b1e9053c02a58d874ff376359a83dc3f0742/src/base/LiquidityManagement.sol#L48) => this will initiate a callback to the uniswapv3mintcallback function [here](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-vultisig/blob/cb72b1e9053c02a58d874ff376359a83dc3f0742/src/base/LiquidityManagement.sol#L27) => which involves [transferring tokens to the pool](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-vultisig/blob/cb72b1e9053c02a58d874ff376359a83dc3f0742/src/base/PeripheryPayments.sol#L33).

The token transfer here will fail if there are no Sale tokens in the ILOPool contract. 


## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Force the project admin to transfer at least maxSaleAmount of SALE_TOKEN to every ILOPool they initialize. This can be done in the ILOManager.initILOPool() function. 

Any remaining amount of SALE_TOKEN will anyways get refunded to the admin at launch via _refundProject().
