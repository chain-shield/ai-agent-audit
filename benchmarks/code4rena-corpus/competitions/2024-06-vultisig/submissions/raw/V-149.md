# Vulnerability details

## Impact
There is no check when initILOPool that initialPoolPriceX96 must be less than sqrtRatioUpperX96. As a result, initialPoolPriceX96 may be larger than initialPoolPriceX96. This may cause all ilopools corresponding to this uniV3pool to be unable to launch or cause users to receive fewer tokens and have liquidity stuck in the protocol.

## Proof of Concept
Because initialPoolPriceX96 may be greater than initialPoolPriceX96. This may cause [liquidityDelta to be incorrectly calculated](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-vultisig/blob/cb72b1e9053c02a58d874ff376359a83dc3f0742/src/ILOPool.sol#L155-L159) when [buying](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-vultisig/blob/cb72b1e9053c02a58d874ff376359a83dc3f0742/src/ILOPool.sol#L126) is calculated in the pool. Finally, during launch, [totalRaised](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-vultisig/blob/cb72b1e9053c02a58d874ff376359a83dc3f0742/src/ILOPool.sol#L288C58-L288C69) and [liquidity](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-vultisig/blob/cb72b1e9053c02a58d874ff376359a83dc3f0742/src/ILOPool.sol#L288C27-L288C36) could not correspond, resulting in failure to add liquidity or stranded funds.
