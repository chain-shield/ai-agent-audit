# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Prevention of buyers from participating in the sale at the exact start and end times of the sale

## Proof of Concept
`require(block.timestamp > saleInfo.start && block.timestamp < saleInfo.end, "ST");`

This requirement ensures that the current block timestamp is strictly greater than the saleInfo.start time and strictly less than the saleInfo.end time. As a result, buyers cannot call the buy() function at the exact start and end times of the sale.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
To allow buyers to participate at the exact start and end times of the sale, the require statement should be modified as follows:

`require(block.timestamp >= saleInfo.start && block.timestamp <= saleInfo.end, "ST");`
