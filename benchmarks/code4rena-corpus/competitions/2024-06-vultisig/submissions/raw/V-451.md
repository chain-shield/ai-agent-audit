# Vulnerability details

## Impact

Users who are part of the `ILOPool whitelist` can exceed their `saleInfo.maxCapPerUser` and transfer their NFTs to users without a whitelist, effectively allowing them to buy from others indefinitely.

## Proof of Concept

ILOPool.t.sol:

```
    function testBuyForOtherUser() external {
        _prepareBuy();
        (uint256 tokenId, uint128 liquidity) = _buy(SALE_START+1, 60000 ether);
        vm.expectRevert();
        _buy(SALE_START+1, 1);
        vm.startPrank(INVESTOR);
        IILOPool(iloPool).transferFrom(INVESTOR, makeAddr("user"), tokenId);
        vm.stopPrank();
        _buy(SALE_START+1, 1);
    }
```

## Tools Used

Foundry

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Track Individual Purchases: Keep a record of each whitelist user's purchases to ensure they do not exceed their allowed cap.
