# Vulnerability details

## Impact
[ILOManager.sol#L90](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-vultisig/blob/cb72b1e9053c02a58d874ff376359a83dc3f0742/src/ILOManager.sol#L90)
```solidity
require(sqrtRatioLowerX96 < _project.initialPoolPriceX96 && sqrtRatioLowerX96 < sqrtRatioUpperX96, "RANGE");
```
The condition `sqrtRatioLowerX96 < _project.initialPoolPriceX96` checks if the lower price tick is less than the initial pool price. However, more than this condition is needed to ensure the initial pool price falls within the specified price range.

This means that `sqrtRatioUpperX96` could be greater than `_project.initialPoolPriceX96`, allowing the ILO to accept prices outside the intended range.

## Proof of Concept

To illustrate the issue, let's consider an example:
   - Lower price tick (`sqrtRatioLowerX96`): 1000
   - Upper price tick (`sqrtRatioUpperX96`): 2000
   - Initial pool price (`_project.initialPoolPriceX96`): 500

In this case, the current condition would pass because `1000 < 500 and 1000 < 2000`. However, the initial pool price (500) is actually outside the specified price range [1000, 2000].
