# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Upon analysis, the `LiquidityAmounts` library employs arithmetic operations to compute liquidity amounts from token quantities and price ranges. The library relies on imported functions such as `FullMath.mulDiv`, which are critical for accurate calculation of liquidity based on Uniswap V3 price ranges (sqrtRatioAX96 and sqrtRatioBX96).

The FullMath library was taken from Uniswap v3-core. However, the original solidity version that was used was < 0.8.0, meaning that the execution didn't revert when an overflow was reached.

## Proof of Concept
The concern arises from the potential similarity to a known vulnerability found in the FullMath library. The vulnerability in FullMath stems from its inability to handle intermediate overflows correctly, specifically in scenarios where an overflow is intended but not reached ("phantom overflow"). This oversight can lead to incorrect results or unexpected transaction reverting, particularly when precise arithmetic is pivotal for financial calculations.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Consider wrapping critical arithmetic operations in unchecked blocks where appropriate. This approach clarifies the intended handling of overflows, aligning with best practices to prevent unexpected reverting due to arithmetic exceptions.
