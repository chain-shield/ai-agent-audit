# Vulnerability details

## Impact
`feeTaker` could receive more than the actual fees when claim is called leading to a leak of value and loss of funds. 

## Proof of Concept
Users call `claim(...)` to collect their tokens associated with a `tokenId` . As explained below excess fees can be sent to the `feeTaker` due to the logic implementation of the `claim(...)` function.

- On `L205` the claimable liquidity (`liquidity2Claim`) for the position is burnt and `amount0, amount1` are made available to collect.
- on `L208` platform fees are removed from `amount0, amount1` respectively
- also, on `L227` performance fee are removed from the accrued `feeGrowthInside{0/1}Last128` 
- notice that the platform fees deducted from the `amount0, amount1` returned from burnt liquidity and the performance fee deducted from  the `feeGrowthInside{0/1}Last128` are not cached
- on `L252` - `L253` the burnt amount and earned feeGrowth less the deducted fees are sent to the claimer
- on `L259` - `L260` whatever is left of the `amount0` and `amount1` is sent to the fee taker and this could be in excess of the actual fee owed to the `feeTaker`

The problem arises if the `burn(...)` has previously been called on the position, then the asset received into `ILOPool` on `L242` will consist of the amount in excess of the previously burnt liquidity and as such the fees received by the `feeTaker` will be in excess this breaks protocol accounting and also a leak of value.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

- cache the deducted fees when they were removed from the burnt amount add it to the fees deducted from the fee growth
- collect the sum of the final amounts (including fee growth) and the deducted fees only.
- send the exact cached fees to the fee taker
- check if there is an excess of `amount0` and `amount1` left and perhaps add it is as liquidity to the position (this is at the discretion of the sponsor)


Modify the `pool.collectCall(...)` in the `claim(...)` function using the snippet below as a guidance as shown below and also the team should `deduct(...)` function to include the deducted amounts in its return tuple not only the amounts left after deduction.
