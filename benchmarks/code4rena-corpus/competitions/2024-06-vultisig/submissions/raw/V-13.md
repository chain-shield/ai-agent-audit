# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Uniswap's math libraries depend on wrapping behavior for arithmetic operations. However, Solidity version 0.8.0 introduced checked arithmetic by default, meaning that operations causing an overflow will revert. Since the original code was adapted from Uniswap and written in Solidity version 0.7.6, these arithmetic operations should be enclosed within an unchecked block to maintain the intended functionality. Considering the critical role that the FullMath and TickMath libraries play in calculating liquidity and pricing within the protocol I believe this should be a high. The functions - `getLiquidityForAmount0`, `getLiquidityForAmount1`, `getAmount0ForLiquidity`, and `getAmount1ForLiquidity` provided are fundamental to determining the value of liquidity positions and the amounts of tokens corresponding to those positions. If these calculations are incorrect due to unhandled overflows or underflows, it could lead to significant issues. Incorrect calculations in these areas can lead to mispricing of tokens, which could result in users receiving incorrect amounts of tokens upon trading or withdrawing liquidity. This could potentially lead to loss of funds for users or the protocol itself.
