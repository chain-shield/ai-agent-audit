# Vulnerability details

## Vulnerability details

To launch a project there are some requirements that need to be fulfilled. One of those conditions is that the current price in the pool is the same as the initial price provided by the admin project.

```javascript
(uint160 sqrtPriceX96, , , , , , ) = IUniswapV3Pool(uniV3PoolAddress)
            .slot0();
        require(
            _cachedProject[uniV3PoolAddress].initialPoolPriceX96 ==
                sqrtPriceX96,
            "UV3P"
        );
```

This situation might not always be true. Protocols use different strategies to distribute their tokens. They often do it partialy. In a scenario where some amount of sale token is already distributed (airdrop for example) there is a risk that someone could add liquidity and the price will change overtime.

## Impact

Project will not be launched thus raised token and sold token will not be added as liquidity to the UniswapV3 pool. Even worse when refund deadline is past, the tokens will be stuck in ILOPools as the can't be refunded after that time and can't be claimed because they are not in Uniswap pool.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

To prevent any tokens from being stuck do not allow projects which already have distributed any amount of their token as they can be used to lock remaining tokens in ILOPools.
