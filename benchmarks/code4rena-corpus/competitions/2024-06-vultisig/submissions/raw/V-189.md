# Vulnerability details

## Impact

Investors will not participate in the project because there is no incentive to do so.

## Proof of Concept

When an investor is whitelisted for a particular pool, they can call `ILOPool.buy()` to buy some liquidity in order to exchange for the sale_token in the future. The investor has to provide raise_token, and the function will calculate the amount of `liquidityDelta` raised. 

```
        if (RAISE_TOKEN == _cachedPoolKey.token0) {
            liquidityDelta = LiquidityAmounts.getLiquidityForAmount0(SQRT_RATIO_X96, SQRT_RATIO_UPPER_X96, raiseAmount);
        } else {
            liquidityDelta = LiquidityAmounts.getLiquidityForAmount1(SQRT_RATIO_LOWER_X96, SQRT_RATIO_X96, raiseAmount);
        }
        require(liquidityDelta > 0, "ZA");
        // calculate amount of share liquidity investor recieve by INVESTOR_SHARES config
>       liquidityDelta = uint128(FullMath.mulDiv(liquidityDelta, _vestingConfigs[0].shares, BPS));
```

Then, the investor will only get a percentage of the `liquidityDelta`, subjected to the `shares`. So, for example, if the shares percentage is 20%, and the investor deposits 1 WETH worth of raise_token, then they will only get 1/5 of the liquidity that they should get.

Compared this when the pool is launched, if another user simply adds 1 WETH worth of liquidity, they will get the full liquidity that they should get instead of 1/5.

This means that the investors are paying for the remaining 80% of liquidity that will be sent to the treasury, project, and developers in the vesting config struct.

Also, since their token will be locked under a vesting schedule, it will be much better for the pool to launch already and then add the liquidity.

Investors will have no incentive to participate in the ILO because they are getting shortchanged, by a factor of `shares` and also getting their liquidity locked.

## Tools Used

Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Give all the liquidity to the investors. If the development team / treasury wants a portion of the rewards, they have to also participate in the protocol by providing raise tokens. Another way is to allocate the sale tokens to the project team outside of the ILO.
