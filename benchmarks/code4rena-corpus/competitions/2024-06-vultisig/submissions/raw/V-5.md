# Vulnerability details

## Impact

Any investor can request a refund before the protocol launches, and the pool cannot be launched if a refund is requested. **This means that even a single user who has staked the smallest amount of tokens can cancel the entire pool launch, leading to a potential Denial of Service (DoS) attack**. To successfully launch the pool, trust must be placed in every single investor, including those on the whitelist. Any whitelist member has the power to prevent the pool launch by requesting a refund too.

## Proof of Concept
`_refundTriggered` was updated to public just to read its value for the test.
