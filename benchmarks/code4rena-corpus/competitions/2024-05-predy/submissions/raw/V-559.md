# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Storage of the child contracts of `BaseMarketUpgradable`/`BaseHookCallbackUpgradable` might be corrupted during an upgrade.

## Proof of Concept
Contract `BaseMarketUpgradable`/`BaseHookCallbackUpgradable` and their child contracts are designed to be upgradeable. However, these contracts implement no gap storage for future use, and thus not upgrade-safe. Without storage gaps, adding new storage variables in future versions of these contracts might potentially overwrite their child's storage layout, which could cause serious misbehaviors in the protocol.

## Tools Used
VSCode

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Add storage gaps for `BaseMarketUpgradable`/`BaseHookCallbackUpgradable` contract, and their child contracts.
