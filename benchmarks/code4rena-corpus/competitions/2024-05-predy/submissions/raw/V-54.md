The `BaseHookCallbackUpgradable` contract introduces upgradeability but relies on proper initialization to ensure security. If the initializer function `__BaseHookCallback_init` is not called, the contract remains in an uninitialized state, potentially leading to unintended and insecure behavior. Additionally, improper implementation of the `predyTradeAfterCallback` function in derived contracts can introduce security risks such as reentrancy attacks and logic errors.

## Proof of Concept
The `BaseHookCallbackUpgradable` contract requires proper initialization through the `__BaseHookCallback_init` function. If this function is not called during the initialization phase, the `_predyPool` variable remains uninitialized. This can lead to issues when the `onlyPredyPool` modifier is used, as it will not correctly verify the caller.
