# Vulnerability details

## Impact
A vault can be liquidated when there is open position in the vault and its vaultMargin falls below its vaultValue. This helps prevent bad debt from accumulating within the system. However, it is possible for a vault to not be completely liquidated which would cause a vault with unsafe positions to persist.

The remainingMargin is the sum of the trade fee, perpPayoff and sqrtPayoff. The vault.recipient is the original creator or  of the vault. Also, one of the quotetokens used in the system is USDC. It is possible for the vault recipient to be blacklisted by USDC at any point before the vault becomes unsafe. Also, a malicious vault owner may update the vault recipient to a blacklisted address.
