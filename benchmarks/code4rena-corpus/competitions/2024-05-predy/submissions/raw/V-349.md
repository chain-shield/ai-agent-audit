# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The current implementation allows users to supply assets to the protocol and receive bond tokens in return. users might receive zero bond tokens despite supplying assets. This issue could lead to users losing their supplied assets without receiving the corresponding bond tokens, resulting in a loss of assets.

## Proof of Concept

The vulnerability lies in the function responsible for minting bond tokens when users supply assets to the protocol. If the numerator is less than the denominator in the calculation of bond tokens minted, or any issue occurs during computation, users might receive zero bond tokens. This could occur during the first supply or subsequent supplies, leading to users not receiving bond tokens despite supplying assets based on the following function.


Users might experience a loss of assets as they supply assets to the protocol but receive no bond tokens in return.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Consider adding a check to revert the transaction if `zero` bond tokens are minted. This check is a common practice in similar protocols and helps prevent users from losing their assets due to incorrect calculations.

```javascript
    require(mintedAmount != 0, "Zero bond tokens minted");
 
```
