# Vulnerability details

## Impact
An attacker could potentially exploit the incorrect-equality vulnerability in the GammaTradeMarket contract by manipulating the comparison of the user's vaultId to 0. 

For instance in "https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-predy/blob/main/src/markets/gamma/GammaTradeMarket.sol#L375-L386",  by altering the value of vaultId or providing a malicious input, the attacker could bypass intended logic or gain unauthorized access to certain functionalities within the contract. This could lead to unauthorized trades, fund manipulation, or other malicious activities within the trading market, posing a significant risk to the security and integrity of the system.
