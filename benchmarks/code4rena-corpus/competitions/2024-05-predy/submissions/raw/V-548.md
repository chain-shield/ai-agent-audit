# Vulnerability details

## Impact
`borrowedAmount` does not contributes to the `_assetStatus.fee{0/`}Growth` and `borrowPremium{0/1}Growth` owing to calculation of spread parameter (`spreadParam`) becuase of a rounding error and as such 

- the protocol does not deliver promised returns
- this breaks core protocol functionality

## Proof of Concept
The protocol is designed such that whenever `utilization` is more that `Constants.SQUART_KINK_UR` which is 10%, the borrowedAmount contributes to the `_assetStatus.fee{0/1}Growth` and `borrowPremium{0/1}Growth` of the Squart perp asset. However, this not always true because due to a rounding error when calculating the `spreadParam` from the `PremiumCurveModel.sol`.

As seen on L21, the spread parameter is dependent on the excess of the `utilization` over 10%. 

As shown in the explanation below, the problem arises when sometimes due to rounding error the spread parameter is still be zero when `utilization > Constants.SQUART_KINK_UR`. In this scenarios, if the `utilization` stays in that region for long then the `borrowedAmount` will never contribute toward the calculation of `_assetStatus.fee{0/1}Growth` despite having a plausible utilisation.
