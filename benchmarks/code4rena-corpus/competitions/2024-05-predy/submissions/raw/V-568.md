# Vulnerability details

## Impact
An outdated price may be used to calculate the price of the quote token as the staleness is not checked in any way in the pricefeed. This would allow for arbitrage opportunities by any user.

## Proof of Concept
https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-predy/blob/2fb1e0ec7a52fc06c2e9c8e561bccba84302e4bb/src/PriceFeed.sol#L48-L51

The PriceFeed oracle within Predy makes use of both Chainlink and Pyth oracles in retrieving the price of a token pair. Usually, the chainlink oracle returns the price of the quotetoken while the base price is returned from the Pyth oracle.

```
function getSqrtPrice() external view returns (uint256 sqrtPrice) {
        //audit: Doesn't check staleness of quote price
        (, int256 quoteAnswer,,,) = AggregatorV3Interface(_quotePriceFeed).latestRoundData();

        
        IPyth.Price memory basePrice = IPyth(_pyth).getPriceNoOlderThan(_priceId, VALID_TIME_PERIOD);
.....................................................................

```
The basePrice returned from Pyth is checked for staleness during retrieval, however the price returned from Chainlink is not checked in any way for staleness. 
It is recommended even by Chainlink to always confirm the price returned from their oracles is not older than a given threshold and chainlink oracle prices may occasionally fall out date or become stale. What happens when a stale price is returned and is used to get the sqrtPrice is that arbitrage opportunities become possible.

1. Check the `quoteAnswer` returned falls within a threshold ideally 1 - 24 hours.
