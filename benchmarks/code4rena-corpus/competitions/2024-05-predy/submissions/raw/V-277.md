# Vulnerability details

Predy's current TWAP has a 30-minute period. This is fine in most situations and protects against atomic price manipulation in many cases. However, it is also an ineffective tool when determining a position's health in volatile times. Any large price swing would not be immediately recognized, making liquidations impossible. During times like this, timely liquidations are critical to maintain the health of the protocol. Any lag (especially the 30-minute TWAP being used) risks bad debt accruing and jeopardizes the viability of the protocol.

With pool creation being permissionless, there are a variety of tokens that will be used, many of which will have volatile price swings. When this happens, the protocol will not be prepared to liquidate on time, and the unhealthy positions will directly impact liquidity providers.

## Impact
Delayed liquidation will lead to bad debt accruing, hurting liquidity providers.


## Proof of Concept
Alice creates a 10x long position of 1 Doge with the price of Doge at 4000
Price of Doge drops rapidly 6%, 6 * 10x leverage => 60% loss
Alice's position is underwater and should be liquidated, but the returned value of Uniswap's TWAP lags any rapid price change
Price of Doge continues to drop rapidly another 6%, 6 * 10x leverage => 60% loss => 120% total loss
Bob is finally able to liquidate; however, the position has taken on so much bad debt that Bob has to pay the difference to put the protocol back into a healthy state.

Manual analysis

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Consider using an oracle instead of Uniswap when determining if a position is liquidatable.
