# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Each pair acts as a liquidity pool, where there is a tick range `lowerTick` to `upperTick`.

When reallocations occur, the `Uniswap current tick` is fetched for the respective pool.

If the `current tick` is out of range with the `tick range` of the pair, the pair's `tick range` is rebalanced and a swap is executed to ensure the pair has the correct amount of liquidity for the new range.

However, even if the `current tick` is in range, the range is still rebalanced to a new range. But in this case, there is no swap that will reflect the amount of liquidity in the new range.

This will cause the pairs to have an incorrect amount of liquidity, potentially causing DoS on trades.

## Proof of Concept
When reallocation occurs, the tick of the pool is fetched:

[Perp.sol#L206](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-predy/blob/main/src/libraries/Perp.sol#L206)
```javascript
(uint160 currentSqrtPrice, int24 currentTick,,,,,) = IUniswapV3Pool(_sqrtAssetStatus.uniswapPool).slot0();
```

`currentTick` is used to determine if the tick is in range of the pair's `tick range`.

[Perp.sol#L232-L257](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-predy/blob/main/src/libraries/Perp.sol#L232-L257)

Regardless of where `currentTick` is in range or not, `rebalanceForInRange` is executed, which updates the `tick range` for the pair:

[Perp.sol#L276-L277](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-predy/blob/main/src/libraries/Perp.sol#L276-L277)
```javascript
    (_sqrtAssetStatus.tickLower, _sqrtAssetStatus.tickUpper) =
        Reallocation.getNewRange(_assetStatusUnderlying, _currentTick);
```

If it is out of range (`isOutOfRange = true`), then `swapForOutOfRange` is executed to ensure that the `pair` has the correct amount of liquidity for the new `tick range`.

However, since `rebalanceForInRange` is called regardless if the tick is out of range or in-range, the `tick range` will be updated on each call.

In the case where the `currentTick` is in range, there will be no swap to adjust for the liquidity of the new `tick range`. Therefore, the pair will have an incorrect amount of liquidity for the new tick range.

As mentioned above, the pair will have an incorrect amount of liquidity, potentially causing DoS to trades due to insufficient liquidity
