# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In all the Optimistic L2 oracles, Chainlink recommends consulting the Sequencer Uptime Feed to ensure that the sequencer is live before trusting the data returned by the Price Feed.


## Proof of Concept
If the Arbitrum Sequencer goes down, oracle data will not be kept up to date and thus could become stale. As a result, users may be able to use the protocol while oracle feeds are stale. Some users may be liquidated unfairly, while others may be able to perform undercollateralized borrows.

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
It is recommended to consult the [Sequencer Uptime Feed](https://arbiscan.io/address/0xFdB631F5EE196F0ed6FAa767959853A9F217697D) before validating any price returned by a Chainlink Price Feed. The Chainlink documentation contains an example for how to check the sequencer status:
https://docs.chain.link/data-feeds/l2-sequencer-feeds
