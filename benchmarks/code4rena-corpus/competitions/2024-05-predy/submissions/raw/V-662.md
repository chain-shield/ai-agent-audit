# Vulnerability details

## Impact
`PriceFeed::getSqrtPrice(...)` is susceptible to returning stale price due to  missing check for sequencer downtime on L2s

## Proof of Concept
This will create an opportunity to push through transactions at the old price before it is updated. Even when the new price is updated, it will be assumed by the sequencer that the previous price held up until the moment it came back online, which will result in use of stale price to perform transactions. An exploiter can notice this and use it to his advantage.

While `IPyth::getPriceNoOlderThan(...)` function will revert if the price is not updated within the last 5 minutes, this is not a fool proof mitigation to the sequencer issue

Assume the sequencer downtime starts within a 5 minute period (because at the end of 5 minutes, `Pyth` should revert is prices are not updated

- price is updated at t = 0s, `quoteAnswer` = 1e18 and `basePrice.price` = 2e18.
- L2 sequencer goes offline t = 30s.
- at t = 45s `quoteAnswer` = .9e18 and `basePrice.price` = 2.1e18.
- Alice performs a trade at t = 60s but price are still `quoteAnswer` = 1e18 and `basePrice.price` = 2e18 respectively.
- at t = 5mins transactions start reverting because of `IPyth::getPriceNoOlderThan(...)`  However because the sequencer went offline within the last 5 minutes that the price was updated, this was not caught.

## Tools Used

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Chainlink recommends that all Optimistic L2 oracles consult the Sequencer Uptime Feed to ensure that the sequencer is live before trusting the data returned by the oracle. 

Chainlink recommends devs to take heed from [this](https://docs.chain.link/data-feeds/l2-sequencer-feeds#example-code) example
