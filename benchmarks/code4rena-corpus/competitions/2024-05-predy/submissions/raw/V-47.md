# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The deployment script assumes the successful deployment of the Trade contract and uses its address in subsequent contract deployments (__TradeLogic__ and __LiquidationLogic__). If the Trade contract deployment fails or returns an incorrect address, the dependent contracts will be linked to an invalid address. This can lead to:

-Functional Failures: The __TradeLogic__ and __LiquidationLogic__ contracts may not function correctly, causing the entire system to fail or behave unexpectedly.
-Security Vulnerabilities: Incorrect address linking can be exploited by malicious actors, potentially leading to unauthorized access or control over critical functionalities.
-Deployment Inconsistencies: Without proper validation, the deployment process becomes unreliable, increasing the risk of partial or corrupted deployments.

## Vulnerabilities Detail
The script does not validate the successful deployment and correct address retrieval of the Trade contract before proceeding to deploy the TradeLogic and LiquidationLogic contracts. This critical oversight means that any issue with the Trade contract deployment propagates to the dependent contracts, compromising their integrity and functionality.
