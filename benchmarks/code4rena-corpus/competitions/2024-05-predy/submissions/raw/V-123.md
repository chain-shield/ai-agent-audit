This function is used to liquidate a vault that is now in danger as the name suggests and it takes in several parameters, including the vault ID, a close ratio, global data, and settlement data, issue however is with the acceptance of the range of the `closeRatio`.

The close ratio parameter determines how much of the vault's position will be closed during the liquidation process. However there is a potential issue with allowing the close ratio to be very close to the maximum possible value (up to 99.2% of 1e18 in this case). This is because this flexibility would lead to situations where a liquidator closes a significant portion of a vault's position, leaving behind a small amount ("dust") that might not be attractive enough for other liquidators to act upon. Now accumulating such "dust" positions would lead to the protocol accumulating to "bad debts."

## Impact

The close ratio logic is a critical aspect of the liquidation process, however in this case the current implementation allows for protocol to end up having multiple potential dust positions that wouldn't be enticing for other liquidators to liquidate which then accumulates up as bad debts/ acceptance of the existence of vaults that are in danger.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

To mitigate this issue, consider implementing a stricter limit on the close ratio, specifically between 95% and 100%. This adjustment would encourage liquidators to either liquidate the entire position or at least a substantial portion (up to 90%) of it, thereby reducing the likelihood of ending up with numerous small, unattractive positions. By enforcing a higher minimum threshold for the close ratio, the protocol aims to maintain a healthier balance sheet and reduce the risk of accumulating bad debts.
