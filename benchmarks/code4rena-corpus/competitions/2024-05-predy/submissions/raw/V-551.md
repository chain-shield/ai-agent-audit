# Vulnerability details

## Impact
This will lead to a situation where

- LP that constitutes a squart position will earn less fees because the liquidity can be reallocated to a range smaller than the defined `rangeSize` and even `rangeThreshold` as shown in the POC section
- tick can move out of range before moving out of threshold breaking core protocol design as shown in the POC section

## Proof of Concept
During reallocation, the `Reallocation::calculateUsableTick(...)` is used to calculate the usable tick for the reallocated squart position.

However, the `Reallocation::calculateUsableTick(...)` function is implemented wrongly and this leads to incorrect tick rounding causing, 

- the reallocated liquidity to earn less fees because it is reallocated into an uneven and narrower tick range
- the position to go out of range before going out of threshold, thus hitting the range boundary before threshold boundary breaking core protocol functionality.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Modify the `Reallocation::calculateUsableTick(...)` function as shown below to eliminate the rounding error

