# Vulnerability details

## Impact

The `PerpMarketV1.sol` contract, specifically the `executeOrderV3` function, does not implement sufficient price validation checks. This lack of validation could allow malicious actors to manipulate the Time-Weighted Average Price (TWAP) and oracle price, leading to significant financial consequences for users and the protocol itself.

The absence of robust price validation mechanisms creates the following risks:

1.  **Price Manipulation:** Malicious actors could manipulate the TWAP and oracle price by executing trades at extreme prices. This could trigger liquidations of other users' positions at unfair prices, leading to substantial losses.
2.  **Front-Running Attacks:** Attackers could front-run legitimate trades by manipulating the TWAP and oracle price, allowing them to profit at the expense of other users.
3.  **Loss of Funds:** The protocol could suffer financial losses if liquidations occur at manipulated prices, as the protocol may need to cover the difference between the liquidation price and the actual market price.

## Proof of Concept

The `executeOrderV3` function in `PerpMarketV1.sol` does not include any explicit checks to ensure that the trade price is within a reasonable range of the TWAP or oracle price. This allows attackers to execute trades at any price, potentially manipulating the TWAP and oracle price in the process.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

1.  **Implement Price Validation:** Add price validation checks to the `executeOrderV3` function to ensure that the trade price is within a reasonable range of the TWAP or oracle price. This could involve setting a maximum allowed deviation from the TWAP or oracle price, or using a price slippage mechanism.
2.  **Consider Circuit Breakers:** Implement circuit breakers that temporarily halt trading if the price deviates significantly from the TWAP or oracle price. This can help prevent extreme price manipulation and protect users from excessive losses.
3.  **Enhance Oracle Security:** Consider using multiple price oracles or implementing more robust oracle security measures to mitigate the risk of oracle manipulation.
