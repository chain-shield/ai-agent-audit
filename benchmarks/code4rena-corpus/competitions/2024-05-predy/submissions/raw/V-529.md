# Vulnerability details

## Summary
The `trade` function in the smart contract is vulnerable to MEV (Miner Extractable Value) attacks due to its reliance on the current price from Uniswap's `slot0`. This vulnerability can lead to price manipulation and front-running, resulting in financial losses for users and reduced fairness in the trading process.
## Impact
- Price Manipulation:
Attackers can manipulate the pool price using flash loans or large trades, causing trades to execute at unfavorable prices for legitimate users.
- Front-running:
MEV bots can submit transactions with higher gas fees to execute just before the victim’s transaction, exploiting predictable price movements.
