# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The 'updateFeeRatio' function does not validate the input 'feeRatio' before passing it to 'AddPairLogic.updateFeeRatio'. This could lead to an exploitable situation where an attacker can set an high fee ratio that could potentially clause too high FreeRation

## Proof of Concept

1. **Identify the Target Pair**: The attacker identifies a pair in the system where they have significant holdings or influence.

2. **Call `updateFeeRatio` with an Extreme Value**: The attacker calls the `updateFeeRatio` function with a `pairId` corresponding to the identified pair and a `feeRatio` set to the maximum value (255).

3. **Impact on Trading Fees**: Since the fee ratio is set to an extremely high value, any token swaps involving this pair will incur a disproportionately high fee. This high fee can disrupt normal trading activities and discourage users from trading in this pair.

4. **Manipulation of Market Dynamics**: The attacker can manipulate the market dynamics by strategically using this high fee to their advantage. For instance, they can buy or sell tokens at a lower volume, knowing that the high fees will deter others from entering the market, thus allowing the attacker to control the price movements.

5. **Economic Impact**: The economic impact of this manipulation can be significant. The attacker can potentially profit from price discrepancies caused by the high fees, while other users might suffer losses due to the inability to trade efficiently.
