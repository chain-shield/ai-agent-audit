# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Unsafe vaults would not be liquidated which would result to bad debts accrual with multiple opened unsafe vaults.

## Proof of Concept
Unsafe vault are to be closed from the protocol.

The issue is that there's no incentive to liquidate an unsafe vault, since MEV bot or liquidator are not incentivized to liquidate the vault bad debt could be accrued or unsafe vaults would remain open for extended period.

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Implement a mechanism to incentivize liquidators or share a small percentage of positive `remainingMargin` with liquidators.
