# Vulnerability details

## Vulnerability Details

During liquidation, the remaining margin is transferred back to the trader's specified recipient this is not safe as the recipient might be a blacklisted address.

Tokens like USDC have a blacklist, and any blacklisted account cannot transfer or receive tokens.



## Impact
Impact, loss of funds to the protocol as Liquidatable positions can't be liquidated, they will continue losing until they become insolvent.


## Proof of Concept

1. Alice Opens a position.
2. Alice's Position is losing and she will soon become liquidatable.
3. Alice calls the `updateRecepient`  function and changes the recipient to a blacklisted address.
4. Once Alice's position is liquidatable, the liquidation operation will fail, because of the blacklisted address.
