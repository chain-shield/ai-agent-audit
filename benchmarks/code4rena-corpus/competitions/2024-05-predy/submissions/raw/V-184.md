# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Users can lose a portion of their funds if some of their funds haven't been fully utilized in the underlying Uniswap pools. There's always a chance of such event since Uniswap pools take balanced token amounts when liquidity is added.

When interacting with Uniswap pools, if the tokens provided for a trade are not fully utilized, the surplus tokens remain unaccounted for, potentially leading to Users  losing a portion of their funds if some of their funds haven't been utilized to the underlying Uniswap pools.

There's always a chance of such event since Uniswap pools take balanced token amounts when liquidity is added. Users might not receive the full value of their traded tokens due to unspent tokens remaining in the contract.

Token balances in the contract may not reflect the actual holdings, leading to discrepancies during swaps.

Consider a scenario where a user initiates a trade with an amount that does not perfectly match Uniswap's required token ratios. If Uniswap returns unspent tokens, they might remain in the contract, affecting subsequent transactions. For instance:

User intends to swap 100 Token A for Token B.
Uniswap requires 150 Token A and 200 Token B for the trade.
After the trade, Uniswap returns 50 unspent Token A to the contract.
