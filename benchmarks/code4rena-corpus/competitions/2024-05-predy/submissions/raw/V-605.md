# Vulnerability details


## Impact: 
This is a popular bug Case: where if a protocol were to allow  users to borrow their LTV exactly they might be liquidated in the next block.

In cases where a protocol allows users to borrow their exact Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio, there is a risk of immediate liquidation in the following block. Currently, in Predy, this scenario seems feasible, leading to potential unfair liquidation, financial losses, and diminished trust in the protocol.


## Proof of Concept:
The protocol's liquidation logic permits borrowing conditions where the user's liquidatable state matches their liquidation threshold, resulting in potential immediate liquidation.

Similar issues have been documented in the past, such as in this code4rena report:
- https://github.com/code-423n4/2023-02-sturdy-findings/issues/16

## Tool Used: 
Manual Review.
## Recommended Mitigation Steps: 
Incorporate a buffer to the liquidation threshold, ensuring users have a safety margin and reducing the risk of immediate liquidation in the subsequent block.

Adding this buffer will provide a safety net, preventing immediate liquidation due to minor fluctuations in collateral value.
