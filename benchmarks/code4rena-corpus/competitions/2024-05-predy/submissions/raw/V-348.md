# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Unintended division before multiplication in Solidity arithmetic can lead to precision loss and unexpected outcomes, particularly when dealing with large or small numbers. This issue arises due to the left-to-right evaluation of expressions and the absence of parentheses to explicitly define the order of operations. Without careful consideration, such code can introduce inaccuracies, affecting the reliability and correctness of smart contracts.

```javascript
uint256 price = uint256(int256(basePrice.price)) * Constants.Q96 / uint256(quoteAnswer);
        price = price * Constants.Q96 / _decimalsDiff;
```
