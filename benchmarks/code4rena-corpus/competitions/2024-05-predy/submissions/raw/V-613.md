# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In the `PriceFeed` contract, the calculation of `price` by the `getSqrtPrice` function involves a division before a multiplication, which can lead to precision loss.

Given that the purpose of this function is to provide an accurate square root price of the base token quoted in the quote token, maintaining precision is crucial.

## Proof of Concept
In the calculation of `price`, `basePrice.price` is converted to `int256` and then `uint256`, multiplied by `Constants.Q96`, and then divided by `uint256(quoteAnswer)` to calculate an initial `price` value. Subsequently, this `price` is multiplied by `Constants.Q96` again and divided by `_decimalsDiff`.

The sequence of these operations means that the division by `uint256(quoteAnswer)` in the first line occurs before the multiplication by `Constants.Q96` in the second line, potentially leading to a loss of precision.

```solidity
        uint256 price = uint256(int256(basePrice.price)) * Constants.Q96 / uint256(quoteAnswer);
        price = price * Constants.Q96 / _decimalsDiff;
```

Precision loss in the price calculation can lead to inaccurate pricing, which might affect the reliability of the `getSqrtPrice` function.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
To ensure that precision is maintained, reorder the operations to perform multiplication before division to minimize intermediate rounding.

```solidity
uint256 price = (uint256(int256(basePrice.price)) * Constants.Q96 * Constants.Q96) / (uint256(quoteAnswer) * _decimalsDiff);
```

This approach ensures that multiplications are done first, minimizing the impact of division truncation. By multiplying the numerators together before dividing by the product of the denominators (`quoteAnswer` and `_decimalsDiff`), you reduce the chance of losing precision.
