# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Performing division before multiplication in Solidity can lead to precision loss due to the way integer division truncates. This vulnerability, known as divide-before-multiply, arises when a calculation involves dividing two integers and then multiplying the result. The issue lies in the fact that the division operation truncates any remainder, potentially leading to inaccuracies in the final result. To mitigate this vulnerability, it is recommended to carefully consider the order of operations in mathematical calculations to ensure accurate and precise results.
