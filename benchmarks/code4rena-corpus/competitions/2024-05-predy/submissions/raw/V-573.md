# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In gamma trading, the `sqrtRebalanceEntryUpdateStable` and `sqrtRebalanceEntryUpdateUnderlying` are used to make a LP position into Quart asset.

First the the `sqrtRebalanceEntryUpdateStable` and `sqrtRebalanceEntryUpdateUnderlying` are added to `sqrtPerp`

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-predy/blob/a9246db5f874a91fb71c296aac6a66902289306a/src/libraries/Perp.sol#L503-L504

Then they are also added to `basePool.tokenStatus` and `quotePool.tokenStatus`

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-predy/blob/a9246db5f874a91fb71c296aac6a66902289306a/src/libraries/Perp.sol#L511-L523

By doing this, the rebalance interest will be counted twice. First time is from the `basePool.tokenStatus` and `quotePool.tokenStatus`

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-predy/blob/a9246db5f874a91fb71c296aac6a66902289306a/src/libraries/PerpFee.sol#L21-L22

Second time is from the `sqrtPerp`

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-predy/blob/a9246db5f874a91fb71c296aac6a66902289306a/src/libraries/PerpFee.sol#L25-L27

As a result, it would be favorable for the buyer because `sqrtRebalanceEntryUpdateStable` and `sqrtRebalanceEntryUpdateUnderlying` are considered asset in `basePool.tokenStatus` and `quotePool.tokenStatus`, so they will pay less interest fee. It would be unfavorable for the seller `sqrtRebalanceEntryUpdateStable` and `sqrtRebalanceEntryUpdateUnderlying` are considered debt in `basePool.tokenStatus` and `quotePool.tokenStatus`, so they have to pay more interest fee.

## Tools Used
Manual Review.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Do not count `sqrtRebalanceEntryUpdateStable` and `sqrtRebalanceEntryUpdateUnderlying` in `basePool.tokenStatus` and `quotePool.tokenStatus`.

`src/libraries/Perp.sol`

        _pairStatus.basePool.tokenStatus.updatePosition(

-           _updatePerpParams.tradeAmount + payoff.sqrtRebalanceEntryUpdateUnderlying,
