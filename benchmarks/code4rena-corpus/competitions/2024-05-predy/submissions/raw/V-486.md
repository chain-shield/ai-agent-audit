# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The wrong price range will be consumed by the protocol, affecting the trading activity with incorrect prices.

## Proof of Concept
To understand this issue better, please read this section from [Pyth](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-predy/blob/a9246db5f874a91fb71c296aac6a66902289306a/src/libraries/logic/LiquidationLogic.sol#L39) docs:

>The aggregate confidence interval interpolates between the two cases above as publishers’ prices begin to diverge. In situations closer to case 1 where there is significant overlap of the individual publishers’ intervals, the aggregate interval (μ-σ, μ+σ) will capture most of the spread of the individual publishers. In the situation where the prices look more like case 2 with greater disjointness due to different views of the price across different venues, that aggregate interval may be in some eyes an imperfect measure of spread because there may be a number of individual price intervals that lie outside the aggregate interval. In this case, a protocol has a couple of options:

>2.It can decide that there is too much uncertainty when σ/μ exceeds some threshold and choose to pause any new activity that depends on the price of this asset.

During extremely volatile market conditions, the aggregate interval returned by Pyth might not reflect accurate prices. In such circumstances, the publishers do not agree on the aggregate price interval and hence there could be a number of individual price intervals that lie outside the aggregate interval. In such cases, the price interval being consumed will be incorrect.

As the 2nd point suggests, new activity should be paused when such a case happens. Transactions should revert when there is too much uncertainty (when σ/μ exceeds some threshold)

Another protocol consuming Pyth has rightly implemented this [check](https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2023-12-flatmoney-NishithPat/blob/5db084c27199bb2b07cb005f545802c5641e9287/flatcoin-v1/src/OracleModule.sol#L178) -

```solidity
// Check that Pyth price confidence meets minimum
if (priceData.price / int64(priceData.conf)< 
 int32(offchainOracle.minConfidenceRatio)) {
   invalid = true; // price confidence is too low
}
```

## Tools Used
Manual review, Pyth docs

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Use the recommendation as suggested above. Check that when σ/μ exceeds some threshold, the transactions do not execute.
