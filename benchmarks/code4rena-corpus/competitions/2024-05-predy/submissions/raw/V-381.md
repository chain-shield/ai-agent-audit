# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Significant revenue loss occurred due to round off error even with the high utilization. Its happened with less
decimals token like WBTC, WETH..etc. Here its considered WBTC-DAI Pair. Since WBTC is having 8 decimals , it caused significant reduction of accumulatedProtocolRevenue and accumulatedCreatorRevenue values. Normally users needs to pay at least base interest. But here users no need to pay any fees up to some extent of utilization. On current implementation , protocol revenue is significantly less compared to actual revenue that it should be earned(If round off error negligible) even with the high utilization conditions.

## Proof of Concept
Root cause of round off error occurred here. Round off error happened other steps also when calculating the
protocol revenue but here is the most significant one.
```solidity
uint256 protocolFee = FixedPointMathLib.mulDivDown(
            FixedPointMathLib.mulDivDown(_interestRate, getTotalDebtValue(tokenState), Constants.ONE),
            _reserveFactor,
            100
        );
```


This case scenario considered WBTC-DAI pool which is having 1WBTC and 70,000 DAI. User open with a trade amount is 1e5 WBTC (1e5 WBTC = 0.001 WBTC). Another user open a trade after 5min. For the simplicity it's assumed same amount of trade is done by every user for every 5min. After 250 trades , accumulatedProtocolRevenue and accumulatedCreatorRevenue are zero. (Protocol should earned at least base interest but here its not happened.). [_reserveFactor](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-predy/blob/main/test/pool/Setup.t.sol#L131) is set as 12.


From last trade, 

_interestRate = 877035514381

getTotalDebtValue(tokenState) = 16438914


[protocolFee](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-predy/blob/e96f378007e3dc56b184079f0c0e4fe48a72efaa/src/libraries/ScaledAsset.sol#L194C17-L198C11) = ((877035514381 * 16438914)/1e18)*12/100 = 1

But when calculating the [accumulated protocol revenue](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-predy/blob/e96f378007e3dc56b184079f0c0e4fe48a72efaa/src/libraries/ApplyInterestLib.sol#L71) and [accumulated creator revunue](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-predy/blob/e96f378007e3dc56b184079f0c0e4fe48a72efaa/src/libraries/ApplyInterestLib.sol#L72) this protocolFee is divided by 2 so finally those revenues are becomes zero.

Here , [utilizationRatio](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-predy/blob/main/src/libraries/ApplyInterestLib.sol#L58) = 164387946543508094 (i.e. 16 % of the total WBTC is used for the trading)

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
There could be so many scenarios which accumulated protocol and accumulated creator revenues goes to zero or either its loss some portion due to round off error. So add more domical places for [Constants.ONE](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-predy/blob/e96f378007e3dc56b184079f0c0e4fe48a72efaa/src/libraries/ScaledAsset.sol#L236C43-L236C57). Currently its used 1e18. But its not enough to eliminate round off error.
