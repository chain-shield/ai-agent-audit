# Vulnerability details


At any point, the owner of a pool can call `updateAssetRiskParams` and change the `riskRatio` and `debtRiskRatio` to values that greatly raise the amount of margin a position needs to be healthy. By doing so, they will be able to liquidate and profit off many of the existing positions. This does not have to be a malicious act or even an accident; merely changing the params at any point puts all positions at risk of an unexpected liquidation.


## Impact
Unexpected liquidation and loss of funds for traders.

## Proof of Concept
Alice opens a position with a 100 margin while the minimum margin is 90. At the moment, Alice's position is healthy.
Bob, the pool owner, calls `updateAssetRiskParams` and changes the parameters so that the minimum margin increases for current positions.
Alice's position now requires 110 margin while Alice only has 100.
Charlie, the bot, sees the unhealthy position and liquidates Alice, leading to a loss of funds.
## Tools Used
Manual analysis.
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Implement a timelock for the `updateAssetRiskParams` where changes are initially pending for X amount of time and then set. This gives users a chance to modify their positions.
