## Brief Summary

`operator` variable is a state variable that represents the address of the contract operator. It is initialized with the value of `msg.sender` in the `initialize` function. However, the line `operator = msg.sender;` is executed after the call to `AddPairLogic.initializeGlobalData(globalData, uniswapFactory);`.
