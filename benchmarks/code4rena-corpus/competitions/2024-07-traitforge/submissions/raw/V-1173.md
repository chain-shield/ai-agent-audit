# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In function `deriveTokenParameters` the nukeFactor is the division between entropy and 4 000 000 which is 7 digits leading to a round down and it being  0 everytime.

## Proof of Concept
- entropy : 999999
- nukeFactor : 999999 / 4000000 = 0

## Tools Used

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
fix the divisor to be 6 digits.
