# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Some key functions will be dos when `totalDevWeight` in DevFund is 0.

## Proof of Concept
In DevFund, if the `totalDevWeight` is zero, the DevFund will return back the funds. 

When we trade or forge NFT, some funds will be transferred to the NukeFund. And the NukeFund contract will split 10% to the DevFund.
The problem is that when NukeFund contract transfer some funds to DevFund and the totalDevWeight is zero, DevFund will transfer funds back to the NukeFund, and NukeFund will split funds again and resend splited funds to the DevFund. Even if the funds is splited to 0, we still transfer fund. The transaction will be trapped into one infinite loop and will be reverted because of out-of-gas.

## Tools Used
Manual

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
In NukeFund, if the `devShare` is 0, don't transfer funds to the DevFund to break the infinite loop.
