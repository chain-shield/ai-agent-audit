# Vulnerability details

## Impact

The `EntropyGenerator` contract has an issue where the `performanceFactor` derived from entropy values at `currentNumberIndex=0` is always zero, for any slot number. More precisely, the entropy value results in a number within range [0,73663], which is always zero-padded on the right by multiplication with 10 :

```code
10**78 - 8*(2**256-1) = 7.36632861e76
```

**Impact:**

Tokens that rely on entropy values from `currentNumberIndex=0` will always have a `performanceFactor` of zero. This creates an unfair and unintended discrepancy among tokens, since they have no ability of ageing.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

To address this issue, revise the entropy calculation method to ensure that the derived values do not result in zero. This may involve adjusting the modulus operation or padding logic to account for the specific limitations.
