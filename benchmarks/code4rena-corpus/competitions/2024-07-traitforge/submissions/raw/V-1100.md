# Vulnerability details

## Impact
There's a probability that the underlying chain may reformed, causing previously commited states change. 
If player1 wants to forge with a forger listed for forge, and send a transaction with value greater than forge fee. Before he send his transaction he checked the forge fee and thought it was acceptable. At this moment the chain reformed, the listed forge fee may become higher than his expection, but the transaction was already sent, causing financial loss.

## Tools Used
Manual audit

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
change the >= in the value require in forgeWithListed to ==
