# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The forging capacity dictates how many times a forger can actually forge, and the times forged is tracked by the `forgingCounts` mapping, due to an insufficient check users can actually forge more than what their forging capacity permits.

## Proof of Concept

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Allow the listing without the forge potential check, then make that check during the forging process, after the update of the `forgingCounts` for the forgers to avoid the error
