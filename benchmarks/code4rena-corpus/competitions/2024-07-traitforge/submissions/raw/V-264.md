# Vulnerability details

## Impact

If  mergerTokenId’s owner calls the function forgeWithListed with more msg.value than expected forgingFee, then excess eth is not refunded to mergerTokenId’s owner and excess eth will be stuck in the contract EntityForging.


## Proof of Concept
1. Let’s assume, 0.02 ether is listed as forgingFee for a  forgerTokenId.

2. A mergerTokenId’s owner calls the function forgeWithListed with 0.03 ether mistakenly, then 0.01 ether will not be refunded and 0.01 ether will be stuck in the contract EntityForging.

## Tools Used
manual review
## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Make sure to refund excess eth to  mergerTokenId’s owner at the end of the function execution.




## Assessed type

ETH-Transfer
