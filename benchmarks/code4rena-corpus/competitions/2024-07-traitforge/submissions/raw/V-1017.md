# Vulnerability details

The `lastForgeResetTimestamp` is currently updated only when an entity is listed for forging or engages in a forging activity.

This is incorrect as the intention is to allow the entities to forge upto `forgingCounts` times within a 1 year window and reset `forgingCounts` to 0 when the 1 year window has passed.

The `lastForgeResetTimestamp` should be initialized during the minting process to ensure the one-year countdown for resetting the forging potential starts immediately. 

Additionally, to ensure the forging counts reset exactly after one year, an automated mechanism such as an external service or keeper should periodically check and reset the forging counts.

## Impact
The impact of this issue is that it encourages players to engage in forging activities as soon as possible after minting to start the one-year countdown for resetting the forging potential. 

This design may limit strategic gameplay options and cause players who delay their forging activities to be at a disadvantage, as their one-year timer starts later than those who forge early. 

Additionally, without an automated reset mechanism, the forging potential may not reset exactly after one year, leading to inconsistencies in gameplay.

The owners of the forger nfts can benefit by listing their nfts for forger at appropriate times just to reset the 1 year window.
(The listing can be done at unrealistic forgefee or be immediately cancelled if they do not wish to forge their nfts)

## Proof of Concept
UserA mints a forger token at time T with `forgingCount` of 2.
UserB also mints a forger token at time T with `forgingCount` of 2.
UserA waits for 364 days without `listingforforging` or forging.
UserB immediately calls the `listForForging` and `_cancelListingForForging ` functions.
UserA's 1 year window starts from T+364 days.
UserB's 1 year window starts from T.
Similarly when the 1 year window ends, the `forgingCount` is not automatically reset unless `listForForging`  or `forgeWithListed` is called.
UserA can only forge 2 times as on T + 364 + 365 days.
UserB can forge 4 times as on T + 364 + 365 days.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Initialize `lastForgeResetTimestamp` During Minting:
Ensure that the `lastForgeResetTimestamp` is initialized at the time of minting to start the one-year countdown immediately

Automated Reset Mechanism:
Implement an external service or keeper (e.g., Chainlink Keepers) to periodically call a function that checks and resets the forging counts based on the `lastForgeResetTimestamp`.
