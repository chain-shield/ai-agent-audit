# Vulnerability details

## Impact
When users list their Forger NFTs or Mergers NFTs, if they do not delist their NFTs, they may accidentally repurchase their own listed NFTs in subsequent purchase operations and incur a `nukeFundContribution` fee.

In addition to the absence of `buyer` and `seller` address verification in the `buyNFT` function of `EntityTrading.sol`, I noticed that on https://traitforge.game/trading, there is no clear indication of short address display for users, such as 0x1abc...abc or 0x2bde...fg. Additionally, there is no feature to hide NFTs listed by the user's connected wallet address. As a result, users are very likely to make the mistake of purchasing NFTs they have already listed, resulting in the loss of a portion of their `msg.value`.

Let's consider the following scenario:

Regular User:
1. Suppose the buyer lists a tokenId 1 NFT at a price of 1 ETH and transfers the NFT authorization to the `EntityTrading.sol` contract.
   
2. In a subsequent purchase, the user mistakenly buys their own listed tokenId 1 NFT.
   
3. The user pays 1 ETH as msg.value during the purchase, and according to the initialized `uint256 public taxCut = 10;`, a certain percentage of the fee is sent to the `NukeFund.sol` contract.

4. Based on `uint256 nukeFundContribution = msg.value / taxCut;`，the calculation is nukeFundContribution = (1 ETH / 10) = 0.1 ETH.

5. According to `uint256 sellerProceeds = msg.value - nukeFundContribution;`, the calculation is sellerProceeds = (1 ETH - 0.1 ETH) = 0.9 ETH.

6. `EntityTrading.sol` sends the tokenId 1 NFT back to the user, completing all purchase operations.
7. Buyer lost 0.1eth of `msg.value`


Batch Purchase NFT Bot:
1. Since batch purchase NFT bots may lack verification of the actual seller of the NFT, suppose it includes the functionality of listing NFTs and lists 10 NFTs [TokenId: 0...10], repeating the above operation, the loss of `msg.value` would be nukeFundContribution * 10 or nukeFundContribution * x.
2. EntityTrading.sol sends the tokenId[0...10] NFT back to the bot, completing all purchase operations.
3. bot lost total 0.1eth * 10 or 0.1eth * x eth of `msg.value`


## Proof of Concept

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-traitforge/blob/main/contracts/EntityTrading/EntityTrading.sol#L65-L69

The buyNFT function only validates the `listing.price` and seller being an empty address, lacking the verification of `msg.sender == listing.seller`, allowing buyers to purchase their own listed NFTs
```
require(
    msg.value == listing.price,
    'ETH sent does not match the listing price'
);
require(listing.seller != address(0), 'NFT is not listed for sale.');
```

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-traitforge/blob/main/contracts/EntityTrading/EntityTrading.sol#L72-L74
```
//transfer eth to seller (distribute to nukefund)
uint256 nukeFundContribution = msg.value / taxCut;
uint256 sellerProceeds = msg.value - nukeFundContribution;
transferToNukeFund(nukeFundContribution); // transfer contribution to nukeFund
```

## Tools Used
Manual Review and VSCode

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
It is recommended to add a check to ensure the NFT buyer is not the same person as the seller
```
require(msg.sender != listing.seller, "Seller cannot buy operation");
```
