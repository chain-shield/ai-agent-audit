# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In `TraitForgeNft::burn`, the function uses the `isApprovedOrOwner` function to check whether `msg.sender` is actually approved or the owner of the given tokenId. This `TraitForgeNft::isApprovedOrOwner` is using `_isApprovedOrOwner` internal function which is not declared in any of the inherited contract or not in `TraitForgeNft` contract. And burn function allow a participants to burn their NFT and nuke their funds but `_isApprovedOrOwner` this function doesn't declared and participants can't take out money and burn NFT ever. Locking of funds and NFT in the `TraitForgeNft` and `NukeFund` contracts, participants can't burn their NFT and nuke their funds.

## Proof of Concept
If a participant wants to nuke their funds and burn NFT they call `NukeFund::nuke` function, and this function has burn functionality also `nuke` function itself uses the `TraitForgeNft::isApprovedOrOwner` function which has not declared the `_isApprovedOrOwner`.

`TraitFOrgeNft::isApprovedOrOwner` function compilation error:
`Undeclared identifier. Did you mean "isApprovedOrOwner"?`
