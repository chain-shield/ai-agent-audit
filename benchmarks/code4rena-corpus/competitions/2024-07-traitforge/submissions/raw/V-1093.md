# Vulnerability details

## Description
In the `TraitForgeNft` contract, the mint price for NFTs increases incrementally based on the number of tokens minted in the current generation. The pricing structure encourages early minting, where the price per token is lower at the start of each generation and increases with each subsequent token minted. Users can take advantage of this pricing structure to manipulate the mint price in their favor.
## Impact
- Users who can mint tokens early in each generation will benefit from lower prices, as the mint price increases with each token minted. This allows early minters or whitelisted users to acquire NFTs at a lower cost.
- Malicious users can deploy bots to rapidly mint the first tokens in each generation, thereby securing the cheaper tokens before other users can access them. This results in a significant advantage for the bot operator and prevents other users from benefiting from the lower initial prices.
- There is a risk of race conditions between multiple users attempting to mint tokens at the same time. This can cause unpredictable outcomes where users may compete to acquire the cheapest tokens, potentially resulting in failed transactions or unfair distribution.
## Proof of Concept
Malicious users can create bots to mint tokens immediately as a new generation starts. These bots can quickly mint the initial tokens, securing them at the lower price before other users have a chance.

## Tools Used
Manual Review
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Implement rate limiting to restrict the number of tokens that can be minted by a single address or within a specific timeframe. This would prevent bots from monopolizing the minting process.
