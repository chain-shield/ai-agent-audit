# Vulnerability details

## Impact
`maxAllowedClaimDivisor` is used to determine the portion of nuke fund that can be claimed by entity holder. It can be updated by owner by calling `setMaxAllowedClaimDivisor`. However, the update to it may cause the issues to user and incentives.

Since `nukeFactor` of user increases as days passes, it may be possible that user is not nuking his entity in hope that he can receive higher portion of nuke fund at later stage. But if `maxAllowedClaimDivisor` is updated(ex: from `2` to `3`), the user was earlier hoping for 50% of nuke fund will now only get `33%` of nuke fund. Due to this, all their incentives for waiting can be useless because of decrease in incentive. It may be the case that user could have gotten similar reward even if they had nuked earlier when `maxAllowedClaimDivisor` was `2`. 

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Not a simple solution. Maybe add a timelock with sufficient time for an update to `maxAllowedClaimDivisor` parameter or make it fixed.
