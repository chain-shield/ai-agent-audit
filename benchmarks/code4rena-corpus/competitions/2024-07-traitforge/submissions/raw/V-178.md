# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The receive() function in the NukeFund contract is vulnerable to a Denial of Service (DoS) attack through the flooding of small Ether transactions. An attacker can exploit this vulnerability by sending a large number of transactions with small amounts of Ether (e.g., 100 Wei per transaction) to the contract. Due to the way the receive() function processes each transaction, the contract could become overwhelmed with numerous transactions, leading to significant delays or failures in processing legitimate transactions.

This DoS attack can prevent honest users from interacting with the contract efficiently, particularly when they attempt to send or claim Ether. The issue stems from the fact that each transaction triggers the receive() function, which may become clogged with the high volume of transactions, causing delays and increasing the gas costs for processing legitimate transactions.

## Proof of Concept
Attack Scenario:
Step 1: An attacker sends 100 Wei in each of 10,000 transactions to the NukeFund contract.
Step 2: Each transaction triggers the receive() function, leading to a substantial amount of processing.
Step 3: The high volume of small transactions can clog the contract’s processing, leading to delays for legitimate users who may struggle to send Ether or perform other actions efficiently.

## Tools Used
Manual code review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Add a Mechanism to Filter Transactions: Implement a filter to reject or delay transactions that come in rapid succession, especially small amounts, to prevent abuse.
