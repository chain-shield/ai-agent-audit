# Summary 

* info.pendingRewards += (totalRewardDebt - info.rewardDebt) * info.weight;

* This formula calculates the pending rewards based on the previous weight `(info.weight)`. If `totalRewardDebt - info.rewardDebt` is large, multiplying it by `info.weight` could potentially lead to overflow (though unlikely in practical terms with 256-bit integers).

* More importantly, this calculation might not accurately reflect the intended rewards if `totalRewardDebt` is updated frequently, potentially leading to stale or incorrect reward calculations.
