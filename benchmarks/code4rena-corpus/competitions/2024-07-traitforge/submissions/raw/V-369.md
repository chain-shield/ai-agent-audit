# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Users might be forced to sell their NFT in a certain case

## Proof of Concept
Users can put up their NFT for sale using `EntityTrading::listNFTForSale()`. If they decide to, they can also cancel their listing using `cancelListing()`. The `cancelListing()` function has the `whenNotPaused` modifier:
```solidity
function cancelListing(uint256 tokenId) public whenNotPaused nonReentrant
```
Imagine the following scenario:
1. Bob lists his NFT for 1 ETH as he believes that is a fair price
2. The contract gets paused
3. The average price of the protocol NFTs grow to 2 ETH and Bob wants to cancel his listing as the price has put up is outdated and too low
4. He tries to cancel the listing but he is not allowed due to the modifier
5. The contract gets unpaused and Alice buys the NFT before Bob could cancel it as that price was too good of a deal

Even worse, Bob has almost no chance to cancel his listing in this case as if someone has put up an NFT for 1 ETH when the price should actually be 2 ETH, there will be many people trying to buy it before he cancels the listing, effectively making this a many versus one scenario where Bob has very little chance.

## Tools Used
Manual Review
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Allow cancelling of listings when contract is paused
