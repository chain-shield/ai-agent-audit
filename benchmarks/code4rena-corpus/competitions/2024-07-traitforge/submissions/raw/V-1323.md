# Vulnerability details

## Impact
- Wrong calculation of `nukeFactor` and `forgePotential` inside `deriveTokenParameters`.
- Nuke factor and forgePotential are important data which are calculated wrongly in the function.
- Nuke factor would always be zero due to round down.
- And forgePotential has been calculated wrongly as they are taking 1st digit of entropy but rather 5th digit of entropy should have been taken. In EntityForging.sol 5th digit of entropy is used as forgePotential.
