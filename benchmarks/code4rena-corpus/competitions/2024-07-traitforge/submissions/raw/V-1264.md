## Impact
It is like the seller can blacklist certain addresses to make the call for `buyNFT()` on his listed NFT. Although this shouldn't be the case, as any address is free to buy the NFT if they are willing to pay the listed price.

The seller has the control to revert this buyNFT() call made by multiple addresses, which should not be the case, as it being centralized to the seller to whom he wishes to sell his NFT.
