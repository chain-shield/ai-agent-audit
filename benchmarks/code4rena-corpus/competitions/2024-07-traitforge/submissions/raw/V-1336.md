# Vulnerability details

## Impact

The `EntityForging.listForForging`, `EntityForging.forgeWithListed` and `EntityForging.cancelListingForForging` functions all have the `whenNotPaused` modifier implemented in the `EntityForging` contract. But the `EntityForging.setTaxCut` function does not have the `whenNotPaused` modifier implemented thus can be called to update the `taxCut` amount even when the `EntityForging` contract is paused.

Now let's consider the following scenario:

1. User A lists his `forgerTokenId` for `forging` by calling the `EntityForging.listForForging` function for a fee of `1 Eth`. Currently the `taxCut` is `10%` and he is expecting a `forgerShare` of `90%` of the `forging fee` which is `0.9 Eth` after the New NFT has been forged. 
2. There is sudden emergency in the protocol and the `EntityForging` contract is paused by the `owner` of the `EntityForging` contract.
3. Now the `User A` can not call the `cancelListingForForging` to cancel the `forging listing` or neither can another user call the `EntityForging.forgeWithListed` function to forge the `forgerTokenId` NFT since above functions use the `whenNotPaused` modifier and the `EntityForging` contract is currently paused.
4. After resolving issue of the `EntityForging` contract the `owner` of the `EntityForging` contract calls the `EntityForging.setTaxCut` function to update the `taxCut` amount to `20%`, and unpause the contract.
5. User B who is merger immediately calls the `EntityForging.forgeWithListed` function to forge the `forgerTokenId` NFT.
6. As a result now the `User A who is the forger` will get only `80%` of the `forging fee` which is `0.8 Eth` which is a loss of `0.1 Eth` to the `User A`.
7. Since `User A` did not anticipate this loss of `0.1 Eth` in the `forgerShare` to him at the time of listing the forgerTokenId for forging, it can be considered this is loss of funds to the `User A (forgerOwner)`.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Hence it is recommended to implement the `whenNotPaused` modifier in the `EntityForging.setTaxCut` function as well such that the `taxCut` amount will not be updated when the `EntityForging` contract is paused. This will prevent the `User A` from incurring loss of funds unexpectedly.
