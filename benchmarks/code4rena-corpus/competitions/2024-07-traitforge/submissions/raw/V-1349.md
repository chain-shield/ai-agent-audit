# Vulnerability details

## Impact
When we forge to get a new Nft, the `entropy` of that nft is the average of the parent Nft. But when this is done again and again through out the different generations, the entropy of the next generations will be the very similar to each other and the nft will have the similar traits as we are averaging the averages.
This will lead to a lot of duplicates in the later generations and killing the whole dynamics of the Nft game and the protocol need was to minimize as duplicates as it can.

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Implement a different approach to generate the `newEntropy` like generating the `newEntropy` based on parents `entropy` and a random number.
