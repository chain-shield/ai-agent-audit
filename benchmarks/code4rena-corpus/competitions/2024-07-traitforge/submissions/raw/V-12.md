# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Many entities with same entropy with different prices can be minted to affect integrity and player perception of the game.

An attacker can call `forgeWithListed` function in EntityForging contract multiple times with same parameters (forgerTokenId and mergerTokenId) to mint entities with same entropies (identical traits) but different prices which will lead to questions about fairness, integrity and player perception of the game.
