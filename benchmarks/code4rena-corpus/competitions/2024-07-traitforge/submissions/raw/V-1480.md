# Vulnerability details

## Impact
There is an arbitrage opportunity in the protocol's forging mechanism that a malicious actor can exploit. By listing their forger token and then forging it with another merger token from a new address at the minimum fee (0.01 ether), they can gain significant profits.

Reasons for Arbitrage Opportunity:
- NFT mint prices increase with each mint and across every generation, while the forging minimum fee remains constant. Since the merger only needs to pay the forging fee listed by the forger, this creates an ever-increasing opportunity gap between obtaining NFTs through minting and forging.
- A forger can list their token at any price they choose, regardless of the token generation, due to this line of code in the `listForForging` function of the `EntityForging` contract:
   ```javascript
   require(fee >= minimumListFee, 'Fee should be higher than minimum listing fee');
   ```
This creates an opportunity for malicious actors to gain huge profits depending on the new token generations and high-end token indexes in each generation.

## Proof of Concept
Consider this scenario:

1. The malicious actor lists a forger token with minimum allowed forging fees.
2. Using another address, the actor forges the listed forger token with a merger token, paying only 0.01 ether.
3. The actor ensures this process is done when the minting price of new NFTs is significantly higher due to the progression in generation and token count.
4. The forged NFT token, obtained at minimal cost, is then sold via the `EntityTrading` contract or other markets at a price lower than the original minting price but still high enough to yield substantial profits.

Malicious actors can consistently exploit this loophole to generate profits without bearing the true costs of obtaining NFTs. This puts legitimate traders at a disadvantage because they must either lower their prices to compete or face reduced sales. Lowering prices to match those offered by arbitrageurs often means selling at a loss, which is unsustainable for legitimate traders.

This creates a market disruption, lowering the overall value of NFT tokens and making it difficult for legitimate traders to compete without incurring losses.

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
- Dynamic Forging Fee Structure: Implement a forging fee that depends on the generation and index/position of the token being forged. This will minimize the profit malicious actors can obtain.
- Limiting Mechanism: Introduce a fixed period before the same forger and merger can forge again.
