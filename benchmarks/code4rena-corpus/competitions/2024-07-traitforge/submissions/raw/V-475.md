# Untimely mint price calculation charges users too much 

## Vulnerablity details
After the final Entity in one generation is minted, `generationMintCounts[currentGeneration] = 10000`. Then when `mintToken()` or `mintWithBudget` is executed to mint the first Entity in the next generation, `mintPrice` is first calculated by `calculateMintPrice()` based on `generationMintCounts[currentGeneration]`, which is still `10000` instead of `0` because the new gereration is not yet generated by `_mintInternal()`. Only when `msg.value >= mintPrice` is true, `_mintInternal()` can be executed.

## Impact
`culculateMintPrice()` is executed to calculate the estimated mint price however internally in contract or externally for UI. However, the retrieved mint price is actually of the 10001st (if possible) Entity in the current out-of-date generation, which is greater than the initial price of Entity in the next generation due to price increment. The user must pay more than or equal to the wrong `mintPrice`. The excessive `mintPrice` are then distributed to funds instead of refunded to `msg.sender`.
```solidity

@>      require(msg.value >= mintPrice, 'Insufficient ETH send for minting.');
```
