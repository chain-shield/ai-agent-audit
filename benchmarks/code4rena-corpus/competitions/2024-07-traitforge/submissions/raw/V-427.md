# Vulnerability details

## Impact
User can sell NFTs through third-party marketplaces to bypass NukeFund tax set in the EntityTrading contract.

## Proof of Concept
One revenue stream is the fee taken on every sale done through the EntityTrading contract. But, there's no way to force the users to sell through that contract.

Since there's no royalty specs you can trade on third-party marketplaces and only pay the platform fee. On OpenSea that's 2.5% way less than the 10% you'd pay with the EntityTrading contract. That will cause sellers to move to those platforms causing the NukeFund's revenue to go down significantly. That impacts one of the core mechanisms of the game.

## Tools Used
none

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Add royalty specs to your NFT as described in [EIP-2981](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-2981) so that you receive your fees if the token is sold on other marketplaces as well.
