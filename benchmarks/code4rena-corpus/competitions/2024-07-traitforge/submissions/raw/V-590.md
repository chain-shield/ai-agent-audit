# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Excessive ether send by users who want to forge with listed NFTs will not get refunded, and stuck in contract forever.

## Proof of Concept
Here in this line in the `forgeWithListed` function, we see that it's possible that user may send excessive ethers:

```solidity
    require(msg.value >= forgingFee, 'Insufficient fee for forging');
```

However, in the entire function, excessive amount is not calculated and refunded at the end of the function. This makes those amounts to be lost forever.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Either asking users to provide the exact amount, or refund the excessive value back to user.

ETH-Transfer
