# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Pausing of one contract will lead to an unintended pause in certain functionalities of other contracts.

## Proof of Concept
Both the Airdrop.sol and TraitForgeNft.sol contract have their own indivdual pausing state. Certain functions in the TratForgeNft such as `forge()`, `burn()` and a variety of the minting functions all implement either `airdropContract.addUserAmount()` or `airdropContract.subUserAmount()`. 

The issue here is that, because both the above airdrop function has the `whenNotPaused` modifier, and the `claim()` function of the airdrop contract also has the same `whenNotPaused` modifier, it will become impossible for the owner to decouple intended contracts that are to be paused.

**For instance, if the owner wants to stop users from claiming from the airdrop contract, they will have to pause the contract. But doing so will also pause both the `addUserAmount()` and `subUserAmount()`. As a result, the owner and users will not be able to mint, forge or burn on the TraitForgeNft contract as well even though the contract may not be paused.**

As it currently is, there is no way to decouple the pausing of the 2 contracts.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Consider decoupling the pausing mechanism such that pausing of one contract will not affect another uintended contract.
