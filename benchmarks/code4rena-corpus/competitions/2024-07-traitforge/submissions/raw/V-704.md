# Vulnerability details

## Vulnerability Details

Entropy values are pre determined and stored in `EntropyGenerator::entropySlots`. At the time of minting a new entity, `getEntropy` is called internally which simply returns the entropy value corresponding to the current `slotIndex` and `numberIndex`.
Users can use this information to calculate and predict the characteristics/parameters of the token such as its `nukeFactor`, `performanceFactor` and `forgePotential` which directly influence to its perceived value.

10% of all tokens may have 0 `performanceFactor` and `forgePotential` or very less `nukeFactor` values or all at the same time. This will discourage users from minting such tokens as there would be no ROI and since minting is accomplished sequentially, if a dud token comes up next and nobody mints it, the coming tokens cannot be minted either.

## Impact

Tokens with low or zero `nukeFactor`, `performanceFactor`, or `forgePotential` may become less appealing, discouraging users from minting them. Given the sequential nature of minting, a lack of interest in a particular token could halt the entire process, impacting the availability of NFTs affecting market dynamics and investor confidence.

## Recommended Mitigation

1. Update the entropy generation logic to avoid 0 values for critical token parameters.
2. Include rewards for minting diverse tokens or mechanisms to upgrade token attributes post-minting.
3. Use Chainlink VRF to generate verifiably random entropy values dynamically at the time of minting by following the [docs](https://docs.chain.link/vrf) and not store them on chain.
