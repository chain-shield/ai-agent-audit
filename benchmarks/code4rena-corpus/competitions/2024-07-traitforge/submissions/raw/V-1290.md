# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The `TraitForgeNft` contract contains a critical issue with token ID management and generation constraints, which can significantly impact the contract’s integrity and functionality. The `maxTokensPerGen` parameter is intended to limit the number of tokens minted per generation. However, due to incorrect management of the `_tokenIds` counter in the `mintWithBudget` function, the contract can potentially exceed this limit. The `_tokenIds` counter continues to increment regardless of the generation, allowing the minting of more tokens than allowed per generation. this will cause forever dos of the function `mintWithBuget`
