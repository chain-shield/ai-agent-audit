# Vulnerability details

## Impact
This can lead to the complete drain of the contract's funds with each execution of the `claim` function, resulting in significant financial losses for the rest of the developers. Each developer will withdraw the entire contract balance, disrupting the intended distribution of funds and leading to inequities among developers.

In the `DevFund` contract, the owner sets the weight value for each dev to claim their corresponding amount of funds. However, the contract incorrectly multiplies the weight value by the total amount of funds raised in the `claim` function, instead of dividing it. Consequently, each execution of the claim function transfers all the contract's funds to the caller.


## Proof of Concept
To illustrate this, consider the following scenario:
1. The owner calls the addDev function passing userA, and weight equal to 50. 
2. The owner calls the addDev function passing userB, and weight equal to 200. 
3. The contract receives 1 ETH of funds, updating the `totalRewardDebt` to 1 ETH.
4. userA, with a weight of 50, calls the `claim` function. The pending reward is calculated as follows:

`
pending = info.pendingRewards +
      (totalRewardDebt - info.rewardDebt) *
      info.weight;
`

Resulting in `pending = 0 + ( 1 ETH - 0 ) * 50 = 50 ETH`.

As a result, the contract attempts to transfer 100 ETH to userA, which would drain the contract's total funds of 1 ETH.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
The contract should divide the amount by `info.weight` instead of multiplying by `info.weight`. This change will ensure that the distribution of funds represents a proportionate share of the total funds.
