# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Valuable entropies can be frontrun and the original minter may suffer financial losses.
## Proof of Concept
The minting process of the traitForgeNFT shows that the tokenId is incremented by 1 for each minting. 

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-traitforge/blob/279b2887e3d38bc219a05d332cbcb0655b2dc644/contracts/TraitForgeNft/TraitForgeNft.sol#L181

And once the entropy slots are set the entropy for each tokenId is determined.
