# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Users can farm airdrop points by using two good NFTs and forging them as much as possible. This allows them to accumulate a large number of airdrop points for a very low cost.

## Proof of Concept
1. Users can generate airdrop points by minting or forging NFTs. The minting price starts at `0.005` ETH and goes up to `0.25` ETH for the first generation, with each subsequent generation adding an additional `0.05` ETH.

   However, forging costs only `0.01` ETH, with 10% (`0.001` ETH) taken as a fee and the rest returned to the seller. This price is fixed for all generations.

2. NFTs have a forging factor that allows them to merge with another NFT 0 to 9 times per year. Each merge creates a new NFT with entropy as the average of its parents.

These two factors enable users to farm airdrop points by forging two good NFTs for a very low fee.

Example:
1. Alice has two good NFTs, one for forging and one for merging
2. Both NFTs have a forging factor of 9, allowing them to merge 9 times a year
3. Alice lists her forger for the minimum price
4. She merges from another account 9 times, generating 9 new NFTs, while only paying `0.001 * 9 = 0.009` ETH

Alice has managed to generate 9 NFTs worth of airdrop points while only paying the price of 2 (or less if the first few hundred NFTs are minted).

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Rethink the forging design in relation to the airdrop.
