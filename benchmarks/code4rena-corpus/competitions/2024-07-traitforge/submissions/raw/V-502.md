# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In TraitForgeNft.sol, there's a mapping that tracks NFTs minted per generation `generationMintCounts` and a uint256 that uniquely identifies every NFT `_tokenIds`. At Gen 1, `generationMintCounts[currentGeneration]` and `tokenIds` are equal, but after there's 10000 NFTs minted/forged, a new generation starts;
`currentGeneration` is incremented and `generationMintCounts[currentGeneration]` is now 0, but tokenIds is 10000.
The function mintWithBudget() has an incorrect check in the loop header, it compares `_tokenIds < maxTokensPerGen`, where maxTokensPerGen is always 10000. As you can see, after Gen 1 ends, this check will always fail, rendering the function useless.
