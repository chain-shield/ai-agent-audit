# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Massive loss of funds due to improper management of interacting with Uniswap router.

## Proof of Concept
Whenever funds are received in the DAOFund.sol contract, they are swapped immediately the corresponding Trait token of the contract via Uniswap.

The `minAmount` is harded coded to 0 and `deadline` is hardcoded to `block.timestamp`. As a result, MEV bots will be able to sandwich the contract, profitting at the expense of the DAOFund.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Consider changing the way ETH is swapped for `_token` and introduce slippage to prevent MEV bots from stealing from the DAOFund.
