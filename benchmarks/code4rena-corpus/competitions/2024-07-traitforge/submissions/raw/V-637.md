# Vulnerability details

## Impact

Users cannot use the function past gen 1 mints (tokenId > 10000).

## Proof of Concept

Users can call `mintWithBudget()` to mint more than 1 token at a time. Users put up an arbitrary amount of `msg.value` first, and the function calculates however much NFTs they can afford, and return the remainder.

The problem lies in the check `_tokenIds < maxTokensPerGen`

`_tokenIds` increase every time an NFT is minted, and `maxTokensPerGen` is 10,000. 

```

    bytes32[] calldata proof

    uint256 mintPrice = calculateMintPrice();

```

There are 10 generations, so there should be a total of 100,000 NFTs capable of being minted. After the 10000th mint, mintWithBudget doesn't work anymore as the tokenId will be greater than `maxTokensPerGen`.

## Tools Used

Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Recommend changing `maxTokensPerGen`(10,000) to `maxTokens` in total (100,000).
