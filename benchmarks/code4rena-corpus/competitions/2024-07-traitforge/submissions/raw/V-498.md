# Vulnerability details

## Impact

The more powerful entities with high entropies will be populated, causing game imbalance.

## Proof of Concept

When forging a new entity, the entity digits is calculated by taking the two entities that are being merged, adding them together and then dividing them by 2.

```
  // Calculate the new entity's entropy
    (uint256 forgerEntropy, uint256 mergerEntropy) = getEntropiesForTokens(
      parent1Id,
      parent2Id
    );
    uint256 newEntropy = (forgerEntropy + mergerEntropy) / 2;
```

Players can source for a pool of high entropies (999,998 / 999,997) and consistently breed them together for continuous supply of high level entities. They can just wait 1 year and merge them once all the forging chances are finished.

## Tools Used

Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Recommend not resetting the forging chances for fairness.
