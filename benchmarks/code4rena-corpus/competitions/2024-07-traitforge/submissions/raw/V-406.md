# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In the `NukeFund` contract, the [receive()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-traitforge/blob/main/contracts/NukeFund/NukeFund.sol#L40) function for distributing developer shares of ETH sends ETH to the contract owner when certain conditions are met. If the ownership of the `NukeFund` contract is renounced, ETH will be sent to the zero address.

## Proof of Concept
ETHs will be send to the `owner()` of NukeFund if airdrop has been started and DAO fund is not allowed:

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

The ETH distribution for this scenario is up to protocol's requirement.

A possible workaround is sending ETH to `devAddress`.

Alternatively, send ETH to a new receiver address.
