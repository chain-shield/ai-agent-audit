## Impact
We can exceed the 10th generation which is not intended
in the current state of the game

## Proof of Concept

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-traitforge/blob/main/contracts/TraitForgeNft/TraitForgeNft.sol#L35

In the mint logic, the minting itself is in _mintInternal function that is called from mintWithBudget, we still do not see a check for the max generation: https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-traitforge/blob/main/contracts/TraitForgeNft/TraitForgeNft.sol#L280-L309
