# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Eth fund will get stuck inside EntityForging Contracct since the excess fund is not transferred back to the msg.sender when `forgeWithListed()` is executed.

## Proof of Concept

User calls `forgeWithListed()` with eth to forge. If the user has sent the eth > forgingFee mentioned by the owner of `forgerTokenId` the remainng amount will get stuck inside the contract.
