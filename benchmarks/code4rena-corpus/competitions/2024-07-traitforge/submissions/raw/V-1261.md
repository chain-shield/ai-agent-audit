# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The `TraitForgeNft` contract uses the `whenNotPaused` modifier from the OpenZeppelin Pausable contract, but lacks an implementation for the pausing mechanism itself. This results in the `whenNotPaused` modifier being ineffective, as the contract cannot be paused. This lack of pausing functionality could prevent the contract from being halted in case of critical vulnerabilities or emergencies, potentially leading to irreversible damage or loss.
