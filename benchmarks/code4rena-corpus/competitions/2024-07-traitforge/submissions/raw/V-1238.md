# Vulnerability details

## Impact
When NFT is bought via buyNFT in EntityTrading.sol, the seller gets a fee, however, this results in a contract call to an arbitrary address, i.e., the seller. Currently the buyer is not protected from overspending gas.

## Proof of Concept
User story: A seller decides to put his NFT for sale, and buyer decides to buy it. However, seller is malicious.

Step 1: A Seller lists his NFT via `listNFTForSale()` in EntityTrading contract.

Step 2: Buyer calls `buyNFT()` in EntityTrading.sol. 
However, due to missing gas limit set on the call itself, a malicious Seller, can have code in its `receive();` function which's goal is to just increase gas spent, until OOG hits. This will make the buyer exhausts all the provided gas for the transaction, and as well will stop the buy action itself.

## Recommendation Steps
Set the gas limit to `23,000`, to have some buffer above 21,000.
Its also recommended to extract this value as field and add setter, as in the future, its not impossible that base intrinsic gas could change.
