# Vulnerability details

## Impact
- DOS of `mintWithBudget()` when _tokenId > 10000.
- Due to wrong check in `mintWithBudget()` the function would revert .
- Lets suppose total 10,001 NFT gets minted and the geneartion is incremented to 2nd generation.
- Putting value in equation `while (budgetLeft >= mintPrice && _tokenIds < maxTokensPerGen)` , here _tokenIds = 10,001 and maxTokensPerGen = 10,000.
- Due to this condition `NO NFTS gets minted when _tokenId > 10000 through mintWithBudget()`.
- In short this function would work in Generation 1 only and would revert in other generations.
