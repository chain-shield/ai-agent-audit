# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Those functions will revert due to out gas error when executing.



## Proof of Concept
1. Let’s assume, function writeEntropyBatch1 is called.

2. Here lastInitializedIndex = 0; batchSize1 = 256; so  endIndex = 0+256 = 256.

3. Now when storing pseudoRandomValue in entropySlots from 0 to 256 times, this function execution will revert due to out of gas error.

4. Similarly, writeEntropyBatch2/writeEntropyBatch3 will revert due to out of gas error.

manual review
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Implement properly, so that those functions don’t revert due to out of gas error.
