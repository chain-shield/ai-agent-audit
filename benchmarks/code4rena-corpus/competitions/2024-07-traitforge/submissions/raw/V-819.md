# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The `listForForging` function in the EntityForging contract contains a logic error that allows users to list their NFT for forging one more time than its forgePotential. This discrepancy between the intended behavior and the actual implementation could lead to:

Unfair advantage for some players who can breed their entities more times than designed.
