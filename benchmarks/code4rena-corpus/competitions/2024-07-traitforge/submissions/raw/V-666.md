# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The reward distributed to the DAOFund contract may be sandwiched.
## Proof of Concept
Every time the DAOFund contract receives native tokens as rewards, it will swap the native tokens via Uniswap-like pairs. But there is no slippage control in this swapping, making it vulnerable to MEV attack.
