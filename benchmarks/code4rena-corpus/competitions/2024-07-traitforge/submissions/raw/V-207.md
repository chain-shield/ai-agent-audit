# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Users would be able to merge their own NFTs, by just transferring them to another address.

## Proof of Concept
[forgeWithListed](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-traitforge/blob/main/contracts/EntityForging/EntityForging.sol#L111-L118) has the bellow segment of code, preventing users to merge their own NFTs.

```solidity
    require(nftContract.ownerOf(mergerTokenId) == msg.sender, "Caller must own the merger token");
    require(nftContract.ownerOf(forgerTokenId) != msg.sender, "Caller should be different from forger token owner");
```
However that is easily passable if the token is just transferred into anther address controlled by our user.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Implement other verification methods.
