# Vulnerability details

### Description
The `EntityForging::forgeWithListed` function in the smart contract currently validates that the amount of Ether sent (msg.value) is at least equal to the required forging fee. However, this validation allow msg.value to be greater than and does not account for any excess funds paid by the user, resulting in overpayments that are not refunds

### Impact
Users are consistently overcharged due to the lack of a refund mechanism in `EntityForging::forgeWithListed.`
The following require condition expects the fee to be either equal to or greater than the expected fee without accounting for the excess to be transferred back to the player
`require(msg.value >= forgingFee, "Insufficient fee for forging");`
This leads to financial imbalances and erosion of user trust.
Transferring the excess back to the player will enhance trust and ensure total accountability.

### Tool used
manual review

### Recommended Mitigation:
Accounts for both excess funds in the check  and implement a validation that will be able to transfer excess back to the player when needed
