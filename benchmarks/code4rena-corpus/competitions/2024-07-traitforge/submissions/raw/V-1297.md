# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The owner of the contract could mistakenly set the nuke fund address to any contract's address thus sending funds to an unwanted location when calling _distributeFunds

## Proof of Concept
The variable nukeFundAddress is of type address and in the setter function no checks are done on the passed value:
https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-traitforge/blob/279b2887e3d38bc219a05d332cbcb0655b2dc644/contracts/TraitForgeNft/TraitForgeNft.sol#L66-L71
There are also no checks in the _distributeFunds function:
https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-traitforge/blob/279b2887e3d38bc219a05d332cbcb0655b2dc644/contracts/TraitForgeNft/TraitForgeNft.sol#L358-L365

## Tools Used
manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Consider making nukeFundAddress of type INukeFund instead of just address.
