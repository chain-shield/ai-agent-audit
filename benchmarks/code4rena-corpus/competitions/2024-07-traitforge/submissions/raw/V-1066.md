# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Can cost the first minter of generation 2 to generation10 more price and the following minter less price than they should pay.

The mintPrice in function mintToken() and mintWithBugget() is calculated before _mintInternal(), where generation increasing is handled in the _mintInternal(). The problem arises when mint the first token(suppose in generation2), the price will be calculated as startPrice+10000*priceIncrement. The right price amout should for the first minter of each generation(except gen1) is startPrice. The generation increment is updated behind the price query. As a consequence, the 2nd minter will pay startPrice to mint, which should be the first minter price.
