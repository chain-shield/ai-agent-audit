# Vulnerability details

## Details
- The `EntityForging::listForForging` function allows a forger to list their NFT defined by the 5th digit in their entropy every year.
- But due to an incorrect forge count limit check inside the function allows forgers to list their NFT for an additional one more time every year.
- So, if the forge potential for a forger is `4`, then they will be allowed to list and forge for `5` times due to that incorrect check.


## Impact
Allows forgers with forgePotential > 0, to list their NFT, an additional one more time every year than what was allocated.

## Proof of Concept
The check inside the `EntityForging::listForForging` is mentioned below:
```
    require(
      forgePotential > 0 && forgingCounts[tokenId] <= forgePotential,
      'Entity has reached its forging limit'
    );
```
- Here, we are only considering the case where `forgePotential > 0`.
Here, the forgers current forge count is checked with their max forge count and is allowed to forge if their current forge count is less than or equal to forge potential
- But if the forge count of a forger is equal to their forge potential that means that they have forged for their full potential and can't forge until next year.
- But as it allows for equal check too, will make the forger to list their entity one additional time per year.
- Also, the `forgeWithListed` function doesn't check for forger's potential limit as it mentions it was already checked but actually it was checked incorrectly.

### Dry Run:
forgingCounts[userA_tokenId] = 0
forgePotential (userA) = 1 (let)

Step I:
userA lists their NFT, check (forgingCounts[userA_tokenId] <= forgePotential (userA)) = (0 <= 1)  true, therefore listed
now, some merger forges with them forgePotential[userA_tokenId] = 1

Step II:
user A lists their NFT, check (forgingCounts[userA_tokenId] <= forgePotential (userA)) = (1 <= 1)  true, therefore listed
now, some merger forges with them forgePotential[userA_tokenId] = 2

We, can see that userA was able to forge 2 times, even though their forgePotential was 1

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Correct the check as below:
```diff
    require(
-    forgePotential > 0 && forgingCounts[tokenId] <= forgePotential,
+    forgePotential > 0 && forgingCounts[tokenId] < forgePotential,        
      'Entity has reached its forging limit'
    );
```
