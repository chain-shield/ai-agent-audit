# Vulnerability details

## Vulnerability details
In the `nuke` function of the `NukeFund` contract, the protocol assumes that `msg.sender` is the owner of the token and transfers the claim amount to them. However, the function allows either the owner or an approved operator to call it. This means if an operator initiates the `nuke` function, the `claimed amount` is mistakenly sent to the operator instead of the actual owner.

## Impact
This vulnerability allows any approved operator to call the nuke function and receive the Ether claimed from the fund. As a result, the rightful owner of the token does not receive the funds, leading to potential financial loss for the owner.


## Proof of Concept
"The nuke function allows the owner of an entity to claim a portion of the fund based on the entity's finalNukeFactor. First, it verifies that the caller owns the entity and that the entity is eligible to be nuked. The function calculates the entities finalNukeFactor using its entropy and age. The potential claim amount is determined by the finalNukeFactor, capped at 50% of the current fund size. The appropriate claim amount is then transferred to the entity owner, the fund balance is updated, and the entity is burned." 

these lines are found in the documentation of the protocol which indeed states that the claim amount should be transferred to the owner
