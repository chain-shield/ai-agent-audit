# Vulnerability details

## Impact:
In a paused state, critical operations should be halted to prevent unintended behavior or unauthorized modifications. The absence of the whenNotPaused modifier in dev management functions (`addDev`, `updateDev`, and `remove`) allows these operations to proceed regardless of the contract's pause status.
This could lead to the following:
1.State Inconsistency: Unauthorized addition, update, or removal of developers during a pause.
2.Security Risks: Potential for malicious actors to exploit this loophole.
3.Operational Issues: Unintended updates can lead to incorrect reward calculations and distribution.
