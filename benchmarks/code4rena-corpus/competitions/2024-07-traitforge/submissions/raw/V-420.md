# Vulnerability details

## Impact

Nuke factor is not synced with the document.

## Proof of Concept

[Document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1pihtkKyyxobFWdaNU4YfAy56Q7WIMbFJjSHUAfRm6BA/edit#heading=h.h7aaliqlummz) states:

> Every Entity ages by blocktime from their mint date, at a speed proportional to their Performance Factor. The fastest should mature fully after 30 days, with the slowest taking almost 2 years, an entity with performanceFactor of 0 won’t age at all. Full maturity simply means their Nuke Factor is computed to be the maximum of 50%. Mature Entities would be either extremely lucrative to forge with or to Nuke, because they would be able to claim 50% of the Nuke Fund. 

Basically it means that the best entity should only need 30 days to reach 50% nuke factor. Let's look at the calculation:

Take entity 999999. It's `performanceFactor is 9 (highest)

In calculateAge(), `daysOld` is 30. Assume `ageMultiplier` is 1

```
  uint256 age = (daysOld * perfomanceFactor * MAX_DENOMINATOR * ageMultiplier) / 365; // add 5 digits for decimals
  age = 30 * 9 * 100000 * 1 / 365 = ~73972
```

In calulateNukeFactor(), 
- `initialNukeFactor` is 999999 / 40 = ~24999
- finalNukeFactor is ~25183, which is not > 50,000

```
uint256 finalNukeFactor = ((adjustedAge * defaultNukeFactorIncrease) / MAX_DENOMINATOR) + initialNukeFactor;
finalNukeFactor = 73972 * 250 / 100000 + 24999 = ~25183
```

After 30 days, the nukeFactor of entity 999999 only increase by ~200 (24999 -> 25183). To increase it to 50,000, it will take more than a decade.

Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

It seems that the division by 365 is part of the issue. Recalculate the `nukeFactor` to make sure it is on point with the documentation.
