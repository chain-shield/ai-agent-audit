# Vulnerability details


### Impact
In the `EntityForging::forgeWithListed` function, the protocol ensures that msg.value is at least equal to or greater than the forging fee. However, this check does not account for any excess funds paid by the player, leading to overpayments that are not refunded. This results in a gradual depletion of user funds without their awareness, affecting the game's economic balance.
