# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Loss of eth for users that sent excess eth through `EntityForging.forgeWithListed()` as they are not refunded the remaining eth at the of the transaction also the contract has no withdraw function so the eth is stuck there forever.

## Proof of Concept
When forging there is a check that ensures adequate eth is sent(1st link/code snippet above), and it is the actual forging fee that is sent to the owner of the token being forged and the remaining ether sent with the fucntion call isn't refunded to the user causing loss of funds for user and the remaining of the eth is stuck in the contract witout anybody being able to retreive it because of no withdrawal function.

## Tools Used
Manual review 
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Ensure the remaining eth is refunded back to msg.sender or only accept the exact `forgingfee` as msg.value.

ETH-Transfer
