# Vulnerability details

## Impact
TraitForgeNft doesn't enforce the maximum number of generations allowing users to mint more tokens than they should be able to.

This breaks one of the core invariants of the contracts.

## Proof of Concept
In `incrementGeneration()` it doesn't check whether it has reached `maxGeneration`. That's only checked in `forge()` where a user is prevented from forging a new NFT that surpasses the max generation.

Thus, users are able to just keep minting even after 10,000 tokens have been minted in the 10th generation. It will just increment to the 11th and so on.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
In `incrementGeneration` revert if `currentGeneration == maxGeneration`.
