# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In `forgeWithListed` merger token can be combined with already listed forger token. Everyone should be allowed to forge with listed tokens however the owner of the forger token can choose how can forge with his token and if he doesn't like the merger, he can make the transaction to revert.

## Proof of Concept
When `forgeWithListed` is executed the merger should pay the needed fee to the owner of the forger token. The fee is payed in ETH and it is sent to the forger owner through a low level call.  

```solidity
  // EntityForging::forgeWithListed
  (bool success_forge, ) = forgerOwner.call{ value: forgerShare }('');
```
That call allows the owner to implement a malisious code in his recieve function and only accept payment by a certain addresses. If the merger is not his desired address, he can simply make the transation to revert.

## Tools Used
Manual Review
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Don’t send the fee to the forger owner in the `forgeWithListed` function. Create a mapping which containa forger owners and their accumulated rewards, and send the fees in a different function. That way his token will not be able to manipulate who can forge with his token.
