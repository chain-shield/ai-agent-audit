# Vulnerability details

All contracts in scope inherit from OpenZeppelin's Pausable, but don't implement external functions that will invoke the internal _pause() and _unpause() functions. In case of emergency or attack the admins won't be able to pause the system.

## Impact
Multiple contracts of the protocol cannot be paused

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Add external functions restricted to admins that invoke _pause() and _unpause()
