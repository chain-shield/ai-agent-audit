# Vulnerability details

## Impact

Description: In both the receive and safeRewardTransfer functions, external calls are made without specifying a gas limit, which may consume all gas and cause the transaction to revert.

Impact: An external call consuming all gas could revert the transaction, causing it to fail unexpectedly.

## Tools Used
Slither

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Recommendation: Specify a gas limit when making external calls.
