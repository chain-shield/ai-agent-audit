# Vulnerability details

## Impact

Users may get lesser value than intended when nuking their NFT if they are frontrunned.

## Proof of Concept

Users can nuke their NFT in exchange for ETH through NukeFund.nuke(). 

However, there is no slippage in the function. Users can be frontrunned and will get lower value than expected.

For example, if there is 10ETH in the nuke fund and Alice wants to Nuke her NFT to get 5 ETH (her nuke factor is 50%). She calls `nuke()`, expecting to get 5 ETH. 

Other players also calls nuke at the same time, but since they have paid more gas than Alice, their transaction happens first before Alice. If there are 5 people ahead with 50% nuke factor as well, Instead of getting 5 ETH from the nuke fund, Alice will only get ~0.3 ETH, which is way less than expected.
