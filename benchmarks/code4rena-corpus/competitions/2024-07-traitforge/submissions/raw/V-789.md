# Vulnerability details

## Impact
User loses guaranteed NukeFund funds and matured NFT for way cheaper than intended when nuking NFTs.

Guaranteed funds because nuking is the only section of the game that rewards you for your strategy and maturing your NFT whilst burning the NFT during the process, therefore, shouldn't be front runnable.

## Proof of Concept

With the understanding that the developers built the game with frontrunning allowed in mind for certain areas of the game. Allowing frontrunning on nuking NFT's would lead to a guaranteed loss of funds for the user alongside losing the NFT (normal occurrence with nuking, burning).

Take for instance a user has a very high nuke factor after maturing, meaning the owner has held the NFT for a long time, depending on the performance and initial nuke factor this could be longer. (Some mature faster than others, with the fastest maturing in around 30 days, and slowest 600 days.). The user has decided to finally nuke the NFT to earn 50% of the nuke fund and sends his transaction.

Other users can see that the user is about to take a large portion of the pool (maximum allowed) and then front run his transaction if they have a lesser share to secure some funds for themselves before his transaction goes through, leaving the user at a very unfair position and rendering his matured NFT and strategy useless.

For instance if the Nuke Fund has a balance of 1 ETH to give out and the user's NFT has a nukeFactor of 50% (maximum) and then decides to nuke his NFT to gain 0.5 ETH guaranteed funds, another user whose NFT had just a 25% nukeFactor and wasn't intending to nuke his NFT can see that the user with 50% nuke Factor is nuking and would reduce his share in the nukeFund, then decides to front run him and claim a 0.25 ETH from the fund, leaving the user who had an initial 0.5 ETH guaranteed fund down to 0.375 ETH regardless of whatever strategy he might have had.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Making use of slippage protection when nuking NFTs.
