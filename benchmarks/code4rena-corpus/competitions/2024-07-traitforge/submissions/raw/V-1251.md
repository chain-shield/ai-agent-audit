# Vulnerability details

The nuke factor of an entity is calculated as:
((adjustedAge * defaultNukeFactorIncrease) / MAX_DENOMINATOR) + initialNukeFactor,
where adjustedAge = (daysOld * perfomanceFactor * MAX_DENOMINATOR * ageMultiplier) / 365,
performanceFactor is a number from 0 to 9,
MAX_DENOMINATOR = 100000,
defaultNukeFactorIncrease = 250,
initialNukeFactor = entropy/40.
As stated in the game's whitepaper, `"Every Entity ages by blocktime from their mint date, at a speed proportional to their Performance Factor. The fastest should mature fully after 30 days, with the slowest taking almost 2 years, an entity with performanceFactor of 0 won’t age at all. Full maturity simply means their Nuke Factor is computed to be the maximum of 50%."`. However currently this isn't true. For example, let's count how many days would the best entity (The Golden God) need to mature.
It's entropy is 999999, so it's performanceFactor is 9 and initialNukeFactor is 24999. The max nuke factor (50%) means finalNukeFactor of 50000. The equation would be:
(adjustedAge * 250) / 100000 + 24999 = 50000
((daysOld * 9 * 100000 * 1) / 365 * 250) / 100000 + 24999 = 50000
((daysOld * 9) / 365 * 250) + 24999 = 50000
daysOld = 4055.71
As you can see, it takes the best possible entity in the game a little over 4055 days to mature, but should take around 30 days. Entities with lower entropies and performanceFactors will take even longer to mature.

## Impact
Currently maturing takes much longer and users that nuke don't the expected share of the fund.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Change the nukeFactor calculations so that entities actually take from 30 to 600 days to mature.
