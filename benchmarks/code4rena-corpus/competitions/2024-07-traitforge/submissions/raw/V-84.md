# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The `TraitForgeNft.sol` contract uses the `Pausable` extension from the `OpenZeppelin` libraries allowing the contract owner to pause the functionalities of the contract in case of an emergency. Currently, all `public` and `external` functions that alter the contract state have the `whenNotPaused` modifier. However, 
`TraitForgeNft` is an `ERC721` and the transfer function does not have the modifier, meaning the users can still transfer tokens even when the contract has been paused.


## Proof of Concept
An example where stopping transferring tokens was actually very helpful:
https://x.com/flashfish0x/status/1466369783016869892.
