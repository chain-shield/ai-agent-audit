# Vulnerability details

In `EntityForging::forgeWithListed` The require statement ensures that the sent Ether (msg.value) is at least equal to the forgingFee, 
but the actual amount being processed is just the forgingFee. 
This discrepancy can lead to situations where any excess Ether sent by the user remains stuck in the contract, as there is no mechanism to refund or withdraw this excess amount. 
Over time, these stuck funds can accumulate, resulting in a significant loss for users.
