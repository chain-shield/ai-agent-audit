# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The `_incrementGeneration` function overwrites the `generationMintCounts` of new generation to `0`. However, the new generation may have mint counts due to forging done by previous generation entities. Due to this,  it is very much possible case to have entities more than `maxTokensPerGen` in each generation.
`calculateMintPrice` is also affected since it relies on `generationMintCounts`

## Proof of Concept
* 10000 entities are minted in first generation.
* due to forging, let's assume 5000 entities are produced in generation 2.
* on calling `mintToken`, generationMintCounts[1] will be 0 but should be 5000.
* 10000 entities can be minted in second generation again. But due to forging which is not considered, it can exceed `maxTokensPerGen`

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Do not overwrite next generation mint count to 0
