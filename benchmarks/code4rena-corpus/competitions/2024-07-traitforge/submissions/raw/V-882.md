# Vulnerability details

## Impact
It seems like formula used in `calculateNukeFactor` is wrong. Users doesn't have much incentive to hold the entity instead of directly nuking the entity and claiming the portion of nuke funds. 

First reason is inconsistency with docs. The docs say that fastest mature will be in 30 days. When the entity is matured, it's `nukeFactor` should be more than or equal to `nukeFactorMaxParam`. However, even if we consider `golden god` entity which has the best performance factor, it is not maturing in 30 days. 
Let's run the calculation for golden god whose entropy = 999999.
```solidity
his performance factor = 9
his adjusted after 30 days is 
age = (30 * 9 * MAX_DENOMINATOR) / 365; 
his fiinal nukeFactor is  finalNukeFactor = (( (30 * 9 * MAX_DENOMINATOR) / 365 * 250) /MAX_DENOMINATOR) + 999999/40; 
= 184 + 24999 = 25183
```

The other reason is incentive mechanism of calculating `nukeFactor`. The `nukeFactor` is calculated as following:
```solidity
 uint256 initialNukeFactor = entropy / 40;
 uint256 finalNukeFactor = ((adjustedAge * defaultNukeFactorIncrease) /
      MAX_DENOMINATOR) + initialNukeFactor; 
```
`performanceFactor` ranges from `0` to `9`.

The `nukeFactor` heavily depends upon `initialNukeFactor` which depends on `entropy`. `defaultNukeFactorIncrease` is only `250` so `nukeFactor` only increases by maximum `performanceFactor / 365` per day which is comparably very small value compared to `entropy / 40`.  Due to this, users don't have any incentive to wait for their increase in nuke factor but directly claiming the nuke fund.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
The formula to calculate `nukeFactor` should be designed better by considering incentives.
