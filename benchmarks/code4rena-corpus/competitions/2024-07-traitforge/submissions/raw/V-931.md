# Vulnerability details

## Impact
`taxCut` variable in `EntityTrading` and `EntityForging` determines how much percentage of value needs to sent to `nukeFund`. The remaining is set to `seller` in `EntityTrading` and `forger` in `EntityForging`. However, `taxCut` variable can be updated by `owner`. 

The issue arises when `taxCut` is updated by owner. In `EntityTrading`, the holder of the entity would have listed the entity(by calling `listNFTForSale`) for sale considering the current `taxCut`. If the previous `taxCut` was `10%` and  holder wants to get `9 ether` after `tax deduction`, he would list it for `10 ether`. But if `taxCut` is increased to `20%` after listing but before someone buys it, the holder of entity will get only `8 ether`. Due to this, seller will get less price than anticipated. 

The similar issue will arise in `EntityForging`. It's explained in below poc with example.

## Proof of Concept
* `taxCut` = 10
* The seller lists the NFT(tokenId = 100) for forging by calling `listForForging(100, 1 ether)`.
* The seller put the price as 1 ether previously hoping that he will receive 0.9 ether when someone forges with his entity.
* owner updates `taxCut` to 20
* When someone forges with listed NFT of tokenId = 100, seller will receive 0.5 ether and not what he anticipated.

## Tools Used
Manual

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
`taxCut` used in deduction should be the same at which the seller or forger lists the entity and not the updated one.
