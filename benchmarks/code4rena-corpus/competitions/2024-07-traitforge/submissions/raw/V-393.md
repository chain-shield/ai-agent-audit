# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The DevFund contract contains a critical vulnerability in its reward distribution mechanism. This vulnerability allows early participants (developers) to receive a disproportionately large share of rewards compared to later participants, despite having equal weights.
Technical Details:

The issue is rooted in the rewardDebt calculation and update mechanism.
When a new developer is added, their rewardDebt is set to the current totalRewardDebt.
As new funds enter the contract, totalRewardDebt increases, but individual developers' rewardDebt remains unchanged until they update their weight or claim rewards.
This discrepancy allows early developers to claim rewards from funds that entered the contract before they were added.

Affected Functions:

addDev(address user, uint256 weight)
updateDev(address user, uint256 weight)
claim()
pendingRewards(address user)


Early developers can claim more rewards than intended based on their weight.
Later developers receive fewer rewards than they should, based on their weight.
The overall reward distribution becomes unfair and does not accurately reflect the intended weight-based allocation.

## Proof of Concept
Dev A is added with weight 100 when totalRewardDebt is 0.
The contract receives 1000 ETH, increasing totalRewardDebt to 10.
Dev B is added with weight 100 when totalRewardDebt is 10.
The contract receives another 1000 ETH, increasing totalRewardDebt to 20.
When both claim:

Dev A can claim: (20 - 0) * 100 = 2000 ETH
Dev B can claim: (20 - 10) * 100 = 1000 ETH
Despite equal weights, Dev A receives twice the rewards of Dev B.

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Implement a global accumulator for rewards per weight unit.
Update all developers' rewardDebt whenever new funds are added to the contract.
