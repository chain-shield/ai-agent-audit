# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Executing the `claim` function, by a developer, completely depletes the contract's funds each time, resulting in significant financial losses and disruption of intended fund distribution among developers.

## Proof of Concept
In the `DevFund` contract, the `claim` function improperly calculates the distribution of funds by multiplying the weight value by the total amount of funds raised, instead of dividing it. This leads to the entire contract balance being transferred to the caller on each execution.

Scenario illustrating the issue:
1. The owner calls `addDev` with userA and weight of 100.
2. The owner calls `addDev` with userB and weight of 300.
3. The contract receives 1 ETH, updating `totalRewardDebt` to 1 ETH.
4. userA calls `claim` with weight 100, resulting in:
    
`pending = info.pendingRewards +
          (totalRewardDebt - info.rewardDebt) *
          info.weight;
 `

Calculation: 

`pending = 0 + (1 ETH - 0) * 100 = 100 ETH`

This results in an attempted transfer of 100 ETH to userA, depleting the contract’s 1 ETH balance.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Adjust the `claim` function to divide the amount by `info.weight` instead of multiplying, ensuring proportional distribution of funds.
