# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Users might burn their NFTs for a significantly lower amount than expected

## Proof of Concept
NFT owners can call `nuke()` in order to burn their NFT for an amount of ETH based on multiple factors. There, we have this line which caps the amount of ETH the user can receive for burning his NFT at 50% of the fund:
```solidity
uint256 maxAllowedClaimAmount = fund / maxAllowedClaimDivisor;
```
Imagine the following scenario:
1. There is 1 ETH in the fund
2. There are 2 users with NFTs, that can earn 0.5 ETH each for nuking their NFT
3. UserA and UserB both nuke their NFTs at very similar times
4. UserA's transaction goes through first and he gets 0.5 ETH for nuking his NFT (that is also the maximum allowed claim amount as explained above)
5. UserB's transaction goes through and now the maximum allowed claim amount is 0.25 ETH (50% of the 0.5 ETH left in the fund)
6. He burned his NFT and earned only 0.25 ETH when he rightfully expected 0.5 ETH

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Allow users to specify the minimum expected amount and revert if that amount goal isn't met
