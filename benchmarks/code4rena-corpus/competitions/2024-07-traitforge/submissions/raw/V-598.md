# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Breaks the core assumption and intention that there would be only 1 golden god in a generation.

## Proof of Concept
In the writeEntropyBatch3() function, there is no check to make sure that only a single golden god is present (there could be 0-3330 golden gods). In each iteration the `pseudoRandomValue` variable can result in 1 or more golden gods.

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Ideally there should be no golden gods written in the writeEntropyBatch functions, since the golden god indices are calculated in the initializeAlphaIndices() function. Also keep a counter of the number of golden gods written.
