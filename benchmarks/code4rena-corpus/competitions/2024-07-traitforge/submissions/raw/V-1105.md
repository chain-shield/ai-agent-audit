# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Variables must have true randomness to ensure that predatory users cannot snipe the rarest entities at the expense of less sophisticated users. It is therefore highly recommended to find a source of randomness to set key variables. The block.number isn’t random at all; it only incrementally increases, allowing anyone to easily compute and find the Golden God entity.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Consider using a decentralized oracle for the generation of random numbers, such as Chainlinks VRF.
