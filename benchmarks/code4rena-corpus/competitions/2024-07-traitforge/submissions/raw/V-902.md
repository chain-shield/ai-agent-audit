# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The codebase set the `slotIndex` and `numberIndex` of golden god nft in random way. But the minting of it is deterministic. Since golden god is considered the most valuable nft, smart users can try to monitor the parameters of `currentSlotIndex` and `currentNumberIndex`. When the `slotIndex` of golden god is near to `currentSlotIndex`, smart users can call `mintBatch` minting multiple NFTs to them(golden god being one of them). So, in this way, innocent users may not be aware of such tricks and thus it is not truly random.
