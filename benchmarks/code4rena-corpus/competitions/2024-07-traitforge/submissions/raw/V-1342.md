# Vulnerability details

## Impact
- As entropy has been generated on chain any attacker would mint NFT whose entropy is 999999.
- The number 999999 is considered to be luckiest and would be able to majority of nuke funds while nuking.
- Generation of onchain random numbers is risky as malicious attacker can take advantage of it.
