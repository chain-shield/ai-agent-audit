# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Improper access control implemented will lead to the `TraitForgeNft.sol` contract to not be able to function correctly.

## Proof of Concept
Both the `addUserAmount()` and `subUserAmount()` of the airdrop contract has the `onlyOwner()` modifier. Since the `TraitForgeNft.sol` contract calls both this functions of the Airdrop contract, it MUST be the owner of it.

However, this is a contradiction as it cannot call other `onlyOwner` functions such as `allowDaoFund()`, suggesting that it is not meant to be the owner of the Airdrop contract.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Consider setting the access control of the 2 contracts to correct ones.
