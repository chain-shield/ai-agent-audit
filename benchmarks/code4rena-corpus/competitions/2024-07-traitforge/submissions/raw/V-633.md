# Vulnerability details

## Impact

Loss of funds for users. An attacker with multiple entities ready to nuke can frontrun every attempt to nuke.

## Proof of Concept

As the generation of entities with ideal characteristics is allowed and the call to nukeFund can be frontrunned ( allowed in Base), an malicious user could prevently nuke before every normal user. 

## Tools Used

Manual review.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Add a queue mechanism or a listing for nuke mechanism.
