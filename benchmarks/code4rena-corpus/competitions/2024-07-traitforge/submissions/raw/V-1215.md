However, when the NFT is listed in `EntityTrading.listNFTForSale` and sold, the `initialOwner` is not updated to the new address.

## Impact

The new NFT owner can burn the NFT, harming the initial minter by removing their eligibility for airdrop claiming.

## Tools Used

Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Update the `initialOwner` when the NFT is transferred to a new account.
