# Vulnerability details

The receive function is designed to distribute incoming Ether among developers based on their weights (totalDevWeight). 
However, there are several issues in the distribution logic that can lead to claim discrepancies and unfair reward allocation. 
When the incoming Ether amount (msg.value) is very small or not an exact multiple of totalDevWeight, the calculation of amountPerWeight can leave a remainder (remaining), which is not correctly handled, leading to two major problems

The _incrementGeneration function in TraitForgeNft will fail when trying to call initializeAlphaIndices, potentially halting the contract's intended operations.
