# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Every generation is supposed to have a maximum of 10_000 NFTs, that seems not to be the case because `generationMintCounts` is reseting back to zero

## Proof of Concept
When we forge an NFT (form the same generation that we are now) we create an NFT of the next generation. When the current generation mints out  the minting is supposed to begin at the point of how many entities exist in the next generation(if 10 gen 2 have been minted in gen1 then gen 2 starts from 11).But `_incrementGeneration()` which is responsible for changing generations resets the `generationMintCounts` mapping value back to 0
