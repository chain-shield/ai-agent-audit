# Vulnerability details

In DevFund.sol, there is a functions claim(). This function have `whenNotPaused` modifier. This means that the function will not execute during a suspended state. However, there is no functionality to retrieve dev rewards if the contract is paused.

## Impact
Devs may permanently lose their rewards as they will be not able to claim due to paused state of the contract.


## Proof of Concept
https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-traitforge/blob/279b2887e3d38bc219a05d332cbcb0655b2dc644/contracts/DevFund/DevFund.sol#L61

1. Bob have generated some rewards.
2. By any reason the contract gets temporary/permanently paused.
3. Bob rewards are lost for the duration of the pause. It may be forever.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Consider a backup plan for this situation with which devs will be always able to claim their rewards, or remove `whenNotPaused` modifier from claim().
