# Vulnerability details

## Impact
_incrementGeneration() resets generationMintcount to 0 even if the gen already has forged entities in it. This will result in minting/forging more than 10000 nft per gen.
It will happen for every gen and the impact could be very high. If forges 20 percent of the gen tokens are due to forge the protcol may have 20000 nft's more than expected. 

Impact - high (breaks game restrictions)
Likelihood - high (once there is a forge with gen1 tokens it has happened and will happen to every gen that has forges in it)
Overall - high

## Proof of Concept
```solidity
function _incrementGeneration() private {

    currentGeneration++;
    generationMintCounts[currentGeneration] = 0; //@audit sets to 0 while they could be many more essentialy losing the count
```
