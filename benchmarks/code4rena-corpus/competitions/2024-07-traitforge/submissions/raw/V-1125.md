# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Players can exploit the `Forge`, `mintToken`, and `mintWithBudget` functions to exceed the intended limit of 10 generations for tokens. This violates the protocol's design, which specifies a maximum of 10 generations.

The `_incrementGeneration` function, which is invoked whenever a new generation token is minted via the `Forge`, `mintToken`, and `mintWithBudget` functions, does not include a check for reaching the maximum generation limit. This omission allows players to mint tokens beyond the 10th generation. Theoretically, players could continue minting tokens up to the 20th or 30th generation, as there is no logic in place to restrict this.

According to the protocol, strategy of keeping a limited number of tokens in each generation and overall is aimed at maintaining stable and upward prices and value for the NFT tokens. That is, increasing scarcity to stably maintain the price. Allowing more than 10 generations could undermine this strategy, potentially diminishing the value of the NFTs and adversely affecting the protocol.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Add this condition in `_incrementGeneration` function to limit the maximum generations:
```javascript
    require(generationMintCounts[currentGeneration] >= maxTokensPerGen,'Generation limit not yet reached');
++  require(currentGeneration + 1 <= maxGeneration, 'Maximum Generation limit reached');
```
