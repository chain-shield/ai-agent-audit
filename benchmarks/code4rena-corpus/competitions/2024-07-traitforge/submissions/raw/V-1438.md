# Vulnerability details

The current implementation of the require statements checking the forgePotential and mergerForgePotential has an off-by-one error. 
This error results in either allowing the forgeTokenId to forge one more time than intended or restricting the mergeTokenId to merge one less time than intended. 
The issue arises from the <= comparison in the require statements, which does not correctly enforce the forging and merging limits.

## Impact
forgeTokenId can forge one more time than intended, leading to potential overuse of forging capabilities.
