# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Sellers can front-run buyers and change the value of their NFT, essentially selling a low value NFT for a high price.

## Proof of Concept
NFTs have different stats, one of which is forging. The forging stat ranges from 0 to 9, indicating the number of times per year the NFT can be merged with another one. NFTs with higher forging stats are more valuable and therefore cost more.

Sellers can exploit this by front-running buyers and using up the forging potential of their NFTs. This allows sellers to benefit significantly by selling essentially worthless NFTs for high prices.

Example:
1. Alice has an NFT with a forging potential of 9
2. She lists it for a high price
3. Bob sees that the NFT has good stats and decides to buy it
4. Alice front-runs Bob by using up all 9 forging potential before re-listing the NFT
5. Bob's transaction executes, and he buys the NFT, but its forging potential is exhausted

Bob ends up purchasing an expensive NFT that cannot be forged again for a year.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Implement a check or allow users to specify parameters to control for forging potential and other changeable stats.
