# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The `finalNukeFactor` and maturation duration in the `NukeFund` contract differ significantly from the documentation. The documentation states that the fastest maturation duration is 30 days and the slowest is 2 years.

However, the actual calculations in the code show that the fastest maturation takes at least 11 years, and the slowest takes 190 years. This discrepancy will result in a significant yield loss for players and drastically affect the protocol's functionality.

## Proof of Concept

1. According to the documentation, the fastest maturation duration is supposed to be 30 days, and the slowest is 2 years.
2. When calculating the `finalNukeFactor` based on the actual code present in `NukeFund` contract's `calculateAge` and `calculateNukeFactor` functions:
   - The fastest maturation (for 999999 entropy) takes 11.1 years to reach the maximum NukeFactor (50%).
   - The slowest maturation (for 100001 entropy) takes 190 years to reach the maximum NukeFactor (50%).
3. This discrepancy will result in players experiencing significantly reduced yields and affect the overall protocol performance.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Correct the calculation of `finalNukeFactor` and maturation duration in the code to align with the values specified in the documentation ensuring that the fastest maturation duration is 30 days and the slowest is 2 years as intended.
