# Vulnerability details


## Impact

The vulnerability allows the `claimAmount` ETH to be refunded to `msg.sender` instead of the NFT owner. This could result in ETH being sent to an approved entity, such as a relayer, which forwards transactions on behalf of users who do not have enough gas to perform certain actions. If the ETH is sent to an entity that incorrectly handles the funds, it could be lost.

## Proof of Concept

The root cause of the vulnerability is that the `nuke` function refunds the `claimAmount` to `msg.sender` rather than the NFT owner. 

- Found in contracts/NukeFund/NukeFund.sol at [Line 177](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-traitforge/blob/main/contracts/NukeFund/NukeFund.sol#L177)

177:@>     (bool success, ) = payable(msg.sender).call{ value: claimAmount }('');

Let us walk through the issue with the following scenario:

1. Alice owns an NFT and wants to nuke it to claim the associated ETH.
2. Bob, a relayer, is an approved entity who forwards the transaction on behalf of Alice because Alice does not have enough gas to perform the action herself.
3. Bob calls the `nuke` function, which refunds the `claimAmount` ETH to `msg.sender` (Bob) instead of Alice.
4. If Bob handles the funds incorrectly, the `claimAmount` ETH is lost, and Alice does not receive the funds she is entitled to.



## Tools Used

Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Cache the token owner via `nftContract.ownerOf(tokenId)` before burning the token and send ETH to the `owner` address accordingly.
