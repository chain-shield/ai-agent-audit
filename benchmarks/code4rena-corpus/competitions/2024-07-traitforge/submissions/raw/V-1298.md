# Vulnerability details



## Vulnerability Details

The NukeFund:calculateAge function calculates the age of an entity, which is then used to determine the nukeFactor. This nukeFactor is crucial when a user decides to nuke their entity.

The calculation of the age is performed as follows:

solidity
daysOld = (block.timestamp - nftContract.getTokenCreationTimestamp(tokenId)) / 60 / 60 / 24;


However, this calculation method is prone to rounding errors. For instance, if the time difference (block.timestamp - nftContract.getTokenCreationTimestamp(tokenId)) equals 4406396 seconds, which is just 4 seconds short of 51 hours, the daysOld will be calculated as:

solidity
daysOld = 4406396 / 60 / 60 / 24 = 50 days


In reality, this should represent nearly 51 hours (or about 2.125 days). Due to the integer division, it results in a total loss of 1 day, which can negatively impact the user's claim amount. This discrepancy can cause significant issues in the calculation of the nukeFactor, leading to potential financial losses for the user.

Moreover, this issue can affect anyone staking for different durations. Users who stake for 10 days or even 365 days can lose up to a maximum of 1 day due to this rounding error. Additionally, everyone will lose some hours, as it is highly improbable for anyone to end their staking period at an exact total hour. This inaccuracy can compound over time, leading to systematic underestimation of the staking period and consequently lower nukeFactor values.
