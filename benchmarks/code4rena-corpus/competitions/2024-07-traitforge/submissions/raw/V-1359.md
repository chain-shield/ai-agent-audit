# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Attackers can maximize their claim of the NukeFund without fear of it being claimed by others. Normal users may only receive a fraction of what they hoped to receive from the transaction, while also burning their token.

## Proof of Concept
Due to time-sensitive nature of the function an attacker scans mempool for any transaction calling 'Nukefund::nuke', once a user calls it, the attacker front runs it; burning their own token(s) for a claim of the fund before the user burns and receives their claim of a smaller (already-claimed) fund.
