# Vulnerability details

When the funds are sent to the DevFund contract either by the NukeFund contract or directly, a portion is reserved for the devs & the rest is transferred to the owner.

However, if the `totalDevWeight` is greater than the amount being received, none of it would go to `totalRewardDebt` from which the devs claim their portion.

## Proof of Concept

```solidity
File: DevFund.sol

      if (remaining > 0) {
        (bool success, ) = payable(owner()).call{ value: remaining }('');
        require(success, 'Failed to send Ether to owner');
      }
    } else {
    ...
```
From above we can see that if `totalDevWeight` is greater than `msg.value`, the `amountPerWeight` becomes `zero`. There is no increase in the value of  `totalRewardDebt`, sending all the `remaining` (msg.value) to the owner.

Although this situation is valid in cases where the funds being received are in small amounts. 
But due to the fact that **only 10% of the total amount** is being sent from the NukeFund contract to the DevFund & also that **the amount from NukeFund is not sent out all at once but whenever someone sends fund to the contract**, the likelihood of the above described situation taking place is more likely.

## Impact
Loss to devs because the reward pool does not increase despite funds being sent to the contract.

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Maybe add a check in case where if the total weight exceeds the msg.value, the amount can be divided by the total number of devs.
