# Vulnerability details

## Vulnerability Details
User can forge the forger entity one time more than their forge potential.  Due to the check in the listForForging function : forgingCounts[tokenId] <= forgePotential, the forge nft can still be listed when their forge potential is at their max. Forge with listed function does not check the correctness of the forgeCount of the forger so users can forge over the forge potential.

## Proof of Concept
Create a new test file, paste the code below and run it. The tests shows that user can forge their forger entity over their forge potential

## Impact
The core functionality of the protocol is broken, also the economy of the game could be broken making lower entrope nfts even less valuable due to the fact that nfts with higher entropy values can forge over their forge potential, this could mean that they will be less likely to be forged with.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Change the ≤ to < in the require statement
