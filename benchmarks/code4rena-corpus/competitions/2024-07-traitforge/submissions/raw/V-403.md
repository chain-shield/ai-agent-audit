# Vulnerability details

## Impact

The NFT pricing structure disincentivizes users from purchasing NFTs when the `currentGenMintCount` nears `maxTokensPerGen`, causing users to wait for the price reset in the next generation. This can lead to a potential bottleneck at the end of each generation.

## Proof of Concept

Given:
```solidity
File: TraitForgeNft.sol
22:   uint256 public maxTokensPerGen = 10000;
23:   uint256 public startPrice = 0.005 ether;
24:   uint256 public priceIncrement = 0.0000245 ether;
25:   uint256 public priceIncrementByGen = 0.000005 ether;
```

The last NFT price of generation 1 is [startPrice + priceIncrement * currentGenMintCount](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-traitforge/blob/main/contracts/TraitForgeNft/TraitForgeNft.sol#L227-L232) = `0.005 ether + 10000 * 0.0000245 ether` = 0.25 ether.

[In the next generation](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-traitforge/blob/main/contracts/TraitForgeNft/TraitForgeNft.sol#L351), the NFT price will be set to the `startPrice`, which is 0.005 ether and much cheaper (1/50) than the previous NFT sale price. 

The price of NFTs within a generation increases incrementally [based on the `priceIncrement`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-traitforge/blob/main/contracts/TraitForgeNft/TraitForgeNft.sol#L227). However, once the `maxTokensPerGen` is reached, as the [minted token amount of the new generation is zero](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-traitforge/blob/main/contracts/TraitForgeNft/TraitForgeNft.sol#L351), the price resets to the `startPrice` for the next generation. This significant price discrepancy creates a disincentive for users to purchase NFTs as the `currentGenMintCount` approaches `maxTokensPerGen`, leading to potential stagnation in sales at the end of each generation.
