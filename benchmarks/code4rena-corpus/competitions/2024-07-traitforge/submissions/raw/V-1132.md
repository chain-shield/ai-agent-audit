# Vulnerability details

## Description
The `deriveTokenParameters` function in the `EntropyGenerator` contract calculates the `nukeFactor`, `forgePotential`, `performanceFactor`, and `isForger` from entropy values. However, the current implementations of `nukeFactor` and `forgePotential` may be incorrect. Specifically, `nukeFactor` is calculated as entropy / 4000000 when it might need to be entropy / 40, and `forgePotential` is derived from the first digit of entropy instead of the 5th digit as documented.
## Impact
If the calculations for `nukeFactor` and `forgePotential` are incorrect, it could lead to improper game mechanics, affecting the balance and fairness of gameplay. Players might experience unintended gameplay outcomes, and the overall game dynamics could be compromised.

## Tools Used
Manual Review
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Review and update the `nukeFactor` and `forgePotential` calculations to ensure they conform to the documented requirements.
