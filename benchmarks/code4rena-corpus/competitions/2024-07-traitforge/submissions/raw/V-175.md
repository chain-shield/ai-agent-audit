# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Forge can steal some ETH from the merger by front-running him and increasing his forging fee.

## Proof of Concept
Currently [forgeWithListed](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-traitforge/blob/main/contracts/EntityForging/EntityForging.sol#L102) check if the merger has supplied more or equal `msg.value` than the selected `forgingFee`

```solidity
    require(msg.value >= forgingFee, "Insufficient fee for forging");
```

However in cases where the `msg.value` is more than the fee the forger can front-run the TX, un-list and list again his NFT for the higher price, matching the increase `msg.value`.

Example:
1. Alice list and NFT for 1 ETH
2. Bob merges his NFT to Alice's one for 1.2 ETH
3. Alice FR the TX and re-lists here NFT for 1.2 ETH
4. Bob merges for 1.2 ETH instead of 1 ETH.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Modify the require statement to check for exact amount.
