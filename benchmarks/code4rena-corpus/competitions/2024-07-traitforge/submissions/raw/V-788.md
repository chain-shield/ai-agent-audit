# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In TraitForgeNft.sol we have option for continuous minting with certain budget, via `mintWithBudget()` function.
However, the `mintWithBudget() `function will not work after Generation 1 gets incremented to Generation 2, due to incorrect require check in its while loop.
This applies for other Generations too, the function will only be able to mint in Generation 1.

## Proof of Concept
This check in mintWithBudget() function in TraitForgeNft.sol is wrong, as if the first generation has passed, the tokenIds would always be > maxTokensPerGen, so the `mintWithBudget`, won't be working after Gen 1.

        assertTrue(nftContract.generationMintCounts(2) == mintedCountBefore);
