# Vulnerability details

## Details
The smart contracts of TraitForge are intended to halt their operations when the owner deems it necessary. This is evidenced by the inheritance of the Pausable contract from OpenZeppelin and the application of the whenNotPaused modifier on key functions, such as :
```solidity
@> function buyNFT(uint256 tokenId) external payable whenNotPaused nonReentrant {
// ...
 }
```
[Link to code](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-traitforge/blob/279b2887e3d38bc219a05d332cbcb0655b2dc644/contracts/EntityTrading/EntityTrading.sol#L63)
However, in the current implementation, the required pause and unpause functions are absent. These functions are essential to trigger the internal _pause and _unpause methods of the Pausable contract, effectively pausing or resuming the protocol. As a result, there is no mechanism in place to pause the contracts, undermining their intended functionality.

## Impact
The lack of a functional pausing mechanism poses a significant risk to the security and operational integrity of the TraitForge contracts. Without the ability to pause the protocol, the contracts are vulnerable to exploitation during emergencies or unforeseen events, potentially leading to financial losses or other critical issues.
