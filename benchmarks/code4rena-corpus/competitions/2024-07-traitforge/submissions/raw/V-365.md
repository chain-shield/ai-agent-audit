# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Users might mint themselves a different generation that expected

## Proof of Concept
Users can call `mintToken()` in order to mint themselves an NFT. The issue is that they have no way to provide the generation they are expecting. 

Imagine the following scenario:
1. Bob wants to mint himself an NFT from the first generation as he knows there is a good chance those would be the most valuable in the future
2. He calls `mintToken()` to mint himself an NFT
3. He expects an NFT from the first generation but due to the very high demand (or someone frontrunning him), 10000 NFTs get minted and we end up in the next generation before Bob's transaction goes through
4. Now, Bob has an NFT from the second generation even though he wanted one from the first

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Allow users to provide the expected generation and revert if they are minted a different one
