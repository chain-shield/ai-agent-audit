# Vulnerability details

 ## Description
In the cancelListing function of the EntityTrading.sol contract, the nftContract.transferFrom(address(this), msg.sender, tokenId) method is used to transfer an NFT back to the seller when they cancel a listing. However, the function does not check the boolean return value of transferFrom. This oversight can lead to a scenario where the transfer fails (e.g., due to lack of approval or other conditions), but the listing is still deleted from the listings mapping, potentially leading to inconsistencies in the contract state and loss of track of the NFT ownership within the contract.

## Impact
f the NFT transfer fails but the listing is deleted, the seller might lose access to their NFT without any record in the contract, leading to asset loss. This inconsistency can undermine the trust in the contract's reliability and potentially be exploited to remove listings without actual transfer of NFTs.
