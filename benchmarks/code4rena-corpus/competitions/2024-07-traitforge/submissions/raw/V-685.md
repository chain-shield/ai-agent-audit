# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Wrong value of a variable makes the nuke factor of an NFT 10 times lower than expected

## Proof of Concept
As seen in the docs, the nuke factor should increase at 2.5% per year (https://github.com/TraitForge/docs/blob/main/user-actions/nuking.md):
>Nuke Factor: Each entity has a unique nukeFactor that determines the percentage of the HoneyPot you can claim. nukeFactor increases over time (2.5% per year * performanceFactor).

If we see the value of the `defaultNukeFactorIncrease` which is the variable responsible for that 2.5% increase, we see the following:
```solidity
uint256 public defaultNukeFactorIncrease = 250;
```
Then, we see it used in the following calcultaion:
```solidity
uint256 finalNukeFactor = ((adjustedAge * defaultNukeFactorIncrease) / MAX_DENOMINATOR) + initialNukeFactor;
```
`MAX_DENOMINATOR` is equal to 100_000. 250 out of 100_000 is only 0.25%. The nuke factor increase variable should be equal to 2500 in order to reach 2.5%. That makes the final nuke factor 10x lower than expected, leading to users receiving a much smaller ETH amount.

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Use 2500 instead of 250
