# Vulnerability details

### [H-1] TITLE `TraitForgeNft::_incrementGeneration()` reverts due to  permission mismatch

**Description**
* In the `TraitForgeNft` contract, the`_incrementGeneration()` function is responsible for advancing to the next generation when conditions are met. This function includes a call to `entropyGenerator.initializeAlphaIndices();`, which is defined in the EntropyGenerator contract.
  **Permission Mismatch**
* The `initializeAlphaIndices()` function in `EntropyGenerator.sol` is restricted to `onlyOwner`. However, the current design intends for a multisig wallet to be the owner of EntropyGenerator, not the TraitForgeNft contract.

**Proof of Concept**

* `EntropyGenerator.sol`is deployed with the multisig wallet as owner.
* Minting occurs normally until `maxTokensPerGen` is reached.
* The next mint attempt triggers `_incrementGeneration().`
* The call to `initializeAlphaIndices()` fails due to ownership mismatch.

**Impact**
* Minting process will be halted after reaching the maximum tokens for the initial generation, also The protocol will be unable to progress beyond the first generation.

## Tools Used
* manual review


## Recommended Mitigation Steps

* Make TraitForgeNft.sol the owner of EntropyGenerator.sol instead of sponsors initial plan of the multisig been the initial owner so that it will be able to call `initializeAlphaIndices()`  when the time is right.
