# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In the _incrementGeneration(), we call initializeAlphaIndices() from the
entropyGenerator contract, but the initializeAlphaIndices() function has the onlyOwner modifier which is the one who has deployed the contract, not the
entropyGenerator, thus it will always revert due to not being called by the owner
