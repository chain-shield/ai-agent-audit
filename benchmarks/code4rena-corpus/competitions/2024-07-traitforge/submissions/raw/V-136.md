# Vulnerability details

## Impact
With current impementation of calculating `claimAmount` the `NukeFund::nuke()` is vulnerable to MEV attacks. Malicious user can front-run other user to gain more share from `fund`

## Proof of Concept
When performing `NukeFund::nuke()` user take percent of total funds accumulated in contract. Current good strategy would be waiting as long as they can to receive percentage share of bigger value.

Take a look at scenario with 2 users that waits for funds to accumulate:
UserA - has NFT with `nukeFactor` that has maximum possible claimAmount = 50%
MaliciousUserB - has NFT with `nukeFactor` that has claimAmount = 20% 
Current funds accumulated in contract is 1 ether


1. UserA decides to nuke his NFT. His expected share should be 0.5 ether
2. UserB front-run UsersA transaction and take his 20% share = 0.2 ether
3. Current funds at contract `1-0.2=0.8 ether`
4. UsersA transaction goes through and he claims 50% of the remaining 0.8 ether
`0.8*0.5=0.4 ether`
5. UserA end up with lower claimed amount than he expected

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Consider changing calculations for `claimAmount`. For example use fixed value in wei instead of percentage of total funds
