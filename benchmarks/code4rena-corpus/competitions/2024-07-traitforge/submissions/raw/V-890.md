# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In `writeEntropyBatch1` and `writeEntropyBatch2`, the following require condition is added.
`require(pseudoRandomValue != 999999, 'Invalid value, retry.');`

However, there is no such condition in `writeEntropyBatch3`. So, if `pseudoRandomValue` equals to `999999`, then it will correspond to golden god. However, there will be another slot that will correspond to golden god which is determined by calling `initializeAlphaIndices` by owner. Due to this, the invariant of game of only having one golden god entity per generation may be incorrect.

The probability of 6 consecutive `9` in 78 digit number is `0.0073%`. Since we are generating `258` numbers, the total probability is `1.88%`.

Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Add the require condition in `writeEntropyBatch3` also
