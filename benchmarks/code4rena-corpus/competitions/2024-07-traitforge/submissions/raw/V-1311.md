# Vulnerability details

## Impact
- In `listForForging` function `forgePotential` is calculated as 5th digit of entropy of NFT.
- While in `deriveTokenParameters` function `forgePotential` is calculated as 1st digit of entropy of NFT.
- So there is mis-match in calculating `forgePotential` in different functions.
