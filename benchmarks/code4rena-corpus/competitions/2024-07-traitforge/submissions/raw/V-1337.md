# Vulnerability details

## Impact
**`totalDevWeight` is too large, resulting in reduced precision and unfair distribution of funds:**

- `totalDevWeight` represents the total weight of all developers. When `totalDevWeight` is too large, it may cause the calculation precision of `amountPerWeight` to be reduced.
- Specifically: `amountPerWeight = msg.value / totalDevWeight`. If `totalDevWeight` is very large and `msg.value` is relatively small, the calculation result may cause `amountPerWeight` to become very small or even zero due to integer division.

**`msg.value` is too small, resulting in reduced precision and developers being unable to obtain rewards:**

- Similarly, when `msg.value` is too small, since integer operations in Solidity do not retain decimals, `amountPerWeight` may become too small or zero.
- This means that some developers may not be able to obtain the rewards they deserve from this transfer.

      uint256 amountPerWeight = msg.value / totalDevWeight;
      uint256 remaining = msg.value - (amountPerWeight * totalDevWeight);
      totalRewardDebt += amountPerWeight;
      if (remaining > 0) {
        (bool success, ) = payable(owner()).call{ value: remaining }('');
        require(success, 'Failed to send Ether to owner');

      (bool success, ) = payable(owner()).call{ value: msg.value }('');

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Use a more precise numeric type or increase the precision preserved when assigning amounts, such as storing amountPerWeight as a larger unit and converting to ether units when needed.
