# Vulnerability details

## Description
The entire EntropyGenerator uses prng that decides how much the user will gain when calling `nuke()`, for this reason, the users will be able to predict how much they will gain for minting and wait until `currentNumberIndex` and `currentSlotIndex` are good enough to output an entropy that will result in better gains, and then send the transaction or even frontrun to get the NFT in the desired entropy.

## Impact
High, users can predict the entropy and get the better NFTs.

## Proof of Concept
1. Bob wants to mint an NFT from the TraitForgeNFT
2. Bob has an idea and clones the contract in his machine, inputing the block.number that was deployed, and also using future `currentNumberIndex` and `currentSlotIndex`
3. Bob decides to wait a little until `currentNumberIndex` and `currentSlotIndex` outputs a good entropy
4. After a some mintings, Bob sees that he will gain a good NFT if he mints now, he sends a transaction with high priority getting that NFT

## Tools Used
Foundry

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Use chainlink VRF in the `EntropyGenerator` to make the numbers more random and unable to predicted
