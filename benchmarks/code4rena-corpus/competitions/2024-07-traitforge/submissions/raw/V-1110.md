# Vulnerability details

## Impact
initialOwners mappings are used to store owners of minted nft and their airdrop allocation based on their entropy, but when that nft is sold on `EntityTrading` their allocations does not get reduced and They can still receive the airdrop either ways, but since they are incentives for users to nuke and receive funds from the nuke funds especially if it a entity with high performance factor, when this new owner nukes the initialOwner allocation for the airdrop gets removed, due to factors they cant control, causing loss of funds.

## Proof of Concept
When a new Entity is minted their allocation for airdrop is tracked using this 
_mintInternal
```solidity
initialOwners[newItemId] = to;
```
When users sell their Entities in `EntityTrading` it does not reduce their allocations and the new owner buys it as shown below ,

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-traitforge/blob/279b2887e3d38bc219a05d332cbcb0655b2dc644/contracts/EntityTrading/EntityTrading.sol#L63C2-L92C4
