# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Users will get much less ETH than planned for nuking their entity

## Proof of Concept
The issue starts from NukeFund#calculateAge() when we calculate the age of an entity.
In the whitepaper, it's specified that this should be in around 30 days for the fastest and 600 days for the slowest entity to reach full maturity.

The minimum of around 30 days can be reached theoretically with the Golden God (since it is the perfect entity), so I will use the Golden God as an example in our calculation.

I made a calculation about the best scenario, that is, 9 performance factor.
(Golden God has all the params maxxed out) 
Which as, specified in the whitepaper, should be around 30 days for full maturity: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1pihtkKyyxobFWdaNU4YfAy56Q7WIMbFJjSHUAfRm6BA/edit#heading=h.45lywoxkn9oi

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-traitforge/blob/main/contracts/NukeFund/NukeFund.sol#L128-L133

uint256 age = (daysOld *
      perfomanceFactor *
      MAX_DENOMINATOR
    ) / 365;
 
(30 * 9 * 100000) / 365 = 73972

We use the result in NukeFund#calculateNukeFactor(), take note that with Golden God initialNukeFactor is going to be number of 24999 (24.99% of the nuke fund)


uint256 finalNukeFactor = ((adjustedAge * defaultNukeFactorIncrease) /
      MAX_DENOMINATOR) + initialNukeFactor;

((73972 * 250) / 100000) + 24999 = 25183

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-traitforge/blob/main/contracts/NukeFund/NukeFund.sol#L147-L151

After 30 days, we have 25183 as a final nuke factor:(25.18% of the nuke fund)
This is nowhere near the full maturity, which is 50 000: (50% of the nuke fund).

I tried to calculate how many days it will take for full maturity as the Golden God. Remember, the Golden God is the best entity so this is the #1 best scenario from 10 000 possibilities (number of entities per generation).


In order to reach full maturity with the best entity, it would over 4000 days... or above a decade!

So this is very disastrous and users will get much much less funds
and maximum nuke factor of 50 000 (50% of the nuke fund) will be reached for decades for a big portion of the entities (remember if it's below the entropy of 50 000, it will take at least more than 2 decades ).

Rendering the entities less valuable due to nuke factor increasing very slowly, directly affecting user's entities price as well as his ability to get money from the nuke fund in case he wants to destroy his entity.
