# Vulnerability details

## Impact
TraitForgeNft will not be able to mint NFT beyond the first generation.

## Proof of Concept

The bug lies in `_incrementGeneration()`. It calls `entropyGenerator.initializeAlphaIndices()`. However, the `initializeAlphaIndices()` has the `onlyOwner` modifier. This modifier represents that only one owner can be owner of the entropyGenerator contract at a time. This also means that the TraitForgeNft contract MUST be the owner of entropyGenerator contract for this function to work correctly.

However, the TraitForgeNft contract cannot be the owner of the entropyGenerator contract as it does not implement functions to call `onlyOwner` functions such as `setAllowedCaller()`.

As a result, once the first generation has maxed out on the number of mints, further mints will be bricked.
