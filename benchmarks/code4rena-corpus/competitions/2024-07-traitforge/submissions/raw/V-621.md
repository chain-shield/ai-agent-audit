# Vulnerability details

## Impact
`EntropyGenerator::initializeAlphaIndices` has wrong 'onlyOwner' access modifier. This function is called in the constructor but also when incrementing generation. In second case it is called by `TraitForgeNft.sol` contract and will fail.  

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-traitforge/blob/279b2887e3d38bc219a05d332cbcb0655b2dc644/contracts/EntropyGenerator/EntropyGenerator.sol#L30-L33
