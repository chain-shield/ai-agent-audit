# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The `listNFTForSale()`, `buyNFT()` and `cancelListing()` functions in the `EntityTrading` contract do not check the return value of the `transferFrom` function. In the mentioned function, the contract transfers the NFT from the user to the contract address but does not verify if the transfer was successful. This can lead to scenarios where the NFT remains with the user/contract while the contract assumes it has been transferred. This can lead to scenarios where the NFT remains with the contract while the contract assumes it has been transferred back to the user.

According to OpenZeppelin, ["Usage of this method is discouraged, use safeTransferFrom() whenever possible"](https://docs.openzeppelin.com/contracts/3.x/api/token/erc721#IERC721-transferFrom-address-address-uint256-). The safeTransferFrom method provides safety checks that ensure the transfer is successful and mitigates the risk of losing NFTs. 


## Proof of Concept
Following scenario is for listing NFT for sale: 

1. Alice calls the `listNFTForSale` function to list her NFT with `tokenId = 1` at a price of `1 ETH`.
2. The `transferFrom` function is called to transfer the NFT from Alice to the contract.
3. Due to an issue (e.g., insufficient gas, etc), the `transferFrom` function fails, but this failure is not checked. The contract assumes the NFT has been transferred to it.
4. Alice's NFT remains in her wallet, but the contract records that the NFT is listed for sale.

The same scenario applies for buying NFT and while canceling NFT for listing
