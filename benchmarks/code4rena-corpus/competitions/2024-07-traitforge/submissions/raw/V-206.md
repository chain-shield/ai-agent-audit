# Vulnerability details

## Impact
On nuking of the NFT Token, the claimable amount should be transferred to the `owner` of the NFT and not to the caller of `nuke(...)` function. This is because the approver could call `nuke(...)` function on behalf.

Approval should be limited to acting on behalf, and hence approved user should be able to initiate the nuke, but the final benefit should be available only to the owner of the NFT Token. 

## Proof of Concept
In the `nuke(...)` function, the caller is validated to be approved user/owner so that the caller can initiate the nuke. But, the `claimAmount` should be transferred back to the owner of the NFT only.

In the below implementation, it is being transferred to the caller(msg.sender) which could be owner or the approved user.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Transfer the `claimAmount` to the owner of the NFT as below.

```
address tokenOwner = nftContract.ownerOf(tokenId);
(bool success, ) = payable(tokenOwner).call{ value: claimAmount }('');
```
