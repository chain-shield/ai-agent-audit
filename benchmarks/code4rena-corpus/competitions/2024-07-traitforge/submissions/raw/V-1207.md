# Vulnerability details

## Impact
It is assumed that callers who are approved by the owner but do not own a specific NFT are allowed to call the listNFTForSale() function. Currently, the listNFTForSale() function will revert unless the sender is the owner of the NFT.

## Proof of Concept

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-traitforge/blob/279b2887e3d38bc219a05d332cbcb0655b2dc644/contracts/EntityTrading/EntityTrading.sol#L43-L46

listNFTForSale() requires msg.sender to be the owner of the NFT. In this case, approved addresses cannot list tokens for which they are approved.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Consider adding the ability for approved operators to call the listNFTForSale() function.
