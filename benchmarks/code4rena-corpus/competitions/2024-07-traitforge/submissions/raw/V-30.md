# Vulnerability details

## Impact
When `forgeWithListed` in `EntityForging` the contract ensures that enough amount of native token is sent through the following check
`require(msg.value >= forgingFee, 'Insufficient fee for forging');`. Basically, requiring the caller to have enough `forgingFee` and it allows excess amount of ether to be sent with the transaction. 

However, the contract does not refund the excess ETH sent with the transaction to the caller. Therefore, if a user calls the function `forgeWithListed` with excess ether, the excess ether will stay in the contract. Moreover, the contract has no mechanism to withdraw the excess ETH sent to it.
