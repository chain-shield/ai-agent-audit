# Vulnerability details

The forging mechanism allows creation of multiple identical entities by repeatedly forging with the same parent combination, limited by the lower of the two parents forging counts.

## Impact
Reduced Entity Diversity: Players can create multiple copies of particularly powerful or desirable entity combinations, reducing overall diversity in the game.

Economic Imbalance: The ability to replicate successful combinations could lead to localized market saturation of certain entity types.

Strategy Limitation: The game's strategic depth may be reduced as players focus on replicating known successful combinations rather than experimenting with new ones.

Fairness Concerns: Players who discover powerful combinations early can gain a significant advantage by replicating them multiple times.

## Proof of Concept
Player acquires a forger NFT (ID: 100, forgePotential: 5) and a merger NFT (ID: 200, forgePotential: 3) with desirable traits.

Player forges these entities, producing a new entity (ID: 300) with specific, desirable attributes.

Player can re-forge using the same forger NFT (ID: 100) and merger NFT (ID: 200) two more times.

This results in three identical entities (IDs: 300, 301, 302) before reaching the forging limit of the merger NFT.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Implement a forging history tracker to prevent repeated combinations:
```
require(!hasForged[forgerTokenId][mergerTokenId], "These entities have already forged");
```
