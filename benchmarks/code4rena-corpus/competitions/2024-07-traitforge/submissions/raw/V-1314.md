# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Description: In the receive function, there's a division operation that could result in a division by zero if totalDevWeight is zero.

Impact: A division by zero would cause the transaction to revert, leading to unexpected behavior.

## Tools Used

Sliter

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Recommendation: Ensure that totalDevWeight is greater than zero before performing the division.
