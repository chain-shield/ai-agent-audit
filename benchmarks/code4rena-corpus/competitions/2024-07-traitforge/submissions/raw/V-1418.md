# Vulnerability details

## Impact
NukeFactor calculations are significantly skewed for tokens in the later generations, particularly affecting the 10th generation. This results in two out of the four main player strategies, Hold to Age and Forge till Infertility, becoming ineffective. Consequently, forging as a whole loses its value in the 10th generation. Players with high `performanceFactor` (pF) will either nuke their entity or sell it shortly after minting, leading to a disparity in the value of entities based on their pF.

For tokens in the 10th generation:
1. **High `performanceFactor` Players:**
   - Players with high pFs will either nuke or sell their entities as soon as possible (typically after 3 days), due to the entity's value primarily stemming from its nuking potential and `performanceFactor`.
   - Entities with higher pFs will have a significantly higher trading value.

2. **Low `performanceFactor` Players:**
   - Players with low pFs (especially those with pFs of 3, 2, and 1) will face substantial difficulties. They will not be able to recover their minting costs due to the high minting costs in later generations and the low funds in the NukeFund.
   - The strategy of Hold to Age becomes unviable because the entity's value in the later generations is mainly derived from its nuking capability and `performanceFactor`.
   - The Forge till Infertility strategy, viable in earlier generations for lower pF players, becomes ineffective as these players cannot forge their NFTs to a valuable level or nuke them to obtain a significant return.

As a result, the Nuke to Win strategy heavily favors players with higher pFs in the later generations, creating an imbalance in the game and diminishing the viability of lower pF entities.

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
- Reevaluate the NukeFactor calculation to ensure it remains balanced across all generations, such as converting low `performanceFactor` (pF) to a high `performanceFactor` (9 - pF). This adjustment would create a competitive market where the deciding factor is the speed at which players take actions.
- Consider adjusting the minting costs and NukeFund distribution to prevent a disproportionate advantage for high pF entities and to maintain a balanced value for lower pF entities.
