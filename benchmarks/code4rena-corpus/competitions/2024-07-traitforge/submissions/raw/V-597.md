# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The users of the protocol will be misinformed greatly.

## Proof of Concept
In the documentation it is specified that the fastest that an entity can mature is 30days. 
`The fastest should mature fully after 30 days, with the slowest taking almost 2 years. Full maturity simply means their Nuke Factor is computed to be the maximum of 50%`
(https://github.com/TraitForge/GitBook/blob/4b5c7835e65f8862e94675d2f04ccd3664aba905/GamePlay/Aging.md?plain=1#L7-L9)

In NukeFund.sol, the fastest entity to age and reach a nukeFactor of 50% would be having the following properties:
perfomanceFactor = 9
initialNukeFactor = 24999 (calculated with an entropy of 999999)

(https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-traitforge/blob/279b2887e3d38bc219a05d332cbcb0655b2dc644/contracts/NukeFund/NukeFund.sol#L118-L167)
By performing the calculations done, we get that to achieve a finalNukeFactor of 50,000 (corresponding to 50%) the age(not the adjusted aage) of the entity must be approximately 4076 days or 11years.

As this is drastically different from the 30days mentioned (130times longer), it is indeed a very big misinformation to the users of the game.

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Make the `defaultNukeFactorIncrease` as ~32,500 instead of the current 250.
