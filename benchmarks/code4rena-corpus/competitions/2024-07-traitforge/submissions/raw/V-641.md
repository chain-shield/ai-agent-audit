# Vulnerability details

## Impact
TraitForgeNft::mintWithBudget() will always fail after maxTokensPerGen 
tokens mint finished.

## Proof of Concept
In function TraitForgeNft::mintWithBudget(), "_tokenIds < maxTokensPerGen" is checked before mint. 
maxTokensPerGen is the max token number, that could be minted in each generation. However, _tokenIds is not reset at the beginning of each generation, instead, it  increase after each mint.
After maxTokensPerGen tokens minted, _tokenIds >= maxTokensPerGen will always be true, then TraitForgeNft::mintWithBudget() will do nothing, only consume some gas.

## Tools Used
manually reviewed

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
change "_tokenIds < maxTokensPerGen" to 
generationMintCounts[currentGeneration] < maxTokensPerGen
