# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In the EntityForging::forgeWithListed function, the protocol ensures that `msg.value >= forgingFee`. However, this check does not account for any excess funds paid by the player, leading to overpayments that are not refunded. Those funds will be locked for even in `EntityForging.sol`

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Implement a proper refund mechanism to ensure that any excess payments are refunded to the player.
An example of such code can be seen in `TraitForgeNft::mintToken`.
