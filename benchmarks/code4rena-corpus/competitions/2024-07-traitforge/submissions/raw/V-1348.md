# Vulnerability details

## Impact
When calling the `forgeWithListed` function, if `msg.value` is greater than the listing `fee`, the remaining amount of ETH will become trapped in the `EntityForging` contract with no way to recover it.

## Proof of Concept
- Alice calls `listForForging(1, 100)` on the `EntityForging` contract. This transaction is mined.
- Bob calls `forgeWithListed(1,2)` on the `EntityForging` contract, and sends a `msg.value` of `100` for the `fee`. This transaction is in the mempool.
- Alice calls `cancelListingForForging(1)` and `listForForging(1, 50)` to reduce the `fee` amount. These two transactions are mined (sequentially) in front of Bob's transaction.
- Bob's transaction is executed, but the fee for that token is now only 50, not 100 like he initially thought. Bob's 50 wei is now locked in the contract.

*I chose this scenario instead of the more obvious one where `msg.value > fee`, because this is a case where no one did anything "wrong" and no one made a mistake.*

## Tools Used
Manual review.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
At the end of the `forgeWithListed` function, calculate the remaining ETH and send it back to the caller.
