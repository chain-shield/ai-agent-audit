# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In `EntityForging`, the `forger` will receive native ETH when the `EntityForging#forgeWithListed` function is called

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-traitforge/blob/279b2887e3d38bc219a05d332cbcb0655b2dc644/contracts/EntityForging/EntityForging.sol#L158

In `EntityTrading`, the `seller` will receive native ETH when the `EntityTrading#buyNFT` function is called

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-traitforge/blob/279b2887e3d38bc219a05d332cbcb0655b2dc644/contracts/EntityTrading/EntityTrading.sol#L77

An attacker could use a contract, which has `receive()` function with a `while(true)` loop, to list a good NFT with a cheap price. When a merger/buyer forge/buy that NFT, they will waste their gas.

## Tools Used
Manual Review.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
There are two approach to mitigate:
1. Do not allow a contract to call `EntityForging#listForForging` and `EntityTrading#listNFTForSale`.
2. Sending native ETH with gas limit. If it is failed then native ETH is wrapped to send to the forger/seller.
