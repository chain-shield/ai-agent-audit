# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In `EntityForging.sol::forgeWithListed` If the msg.value is bigger than the forger fee the difference is lost.

## Proof of Concept
In `EntityForging.sol::forgeWithListed` there are no checks if msg.sender sent more funds than the forge fee amount, which leads to loss of funds.

1. Forger owner wants 0.05 ETH for using his token.
2. Merger owner calls forgeWithListed and sends 0.1 ETH
3. Forger will get 90% of 0.05 (10% goes to NukeFund)
4. The difference of 0.05 ETH will be lost

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Check if the user sent more ETH than needed, and send back the difference.
