# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The `TraitForgeNft::_incrementGeneration()` resets the `generationMintCounts` for the new generation. This reset allows for the possibility of minting more than the intended maximum of 10,000 tokens per generation. Specifically, if tokens are forged into the next generation, it can lead to an accumulation of tokens across generations, allowing up to 20,000 tokens to be minted in one generation.

1. **Exceeded Token Cap**: The token cap for each generation is intended to be 10,000 tokens. By resetting the mint count for new generations, it is possible to mint more than this cap, thus violating the cap and potentially causing issues with token distribution, values and pricing.

2. **Price Increment Impact**: The intended transition between generations is delayed, leading to a situation where tokens are minted at the lower price of the current generation while the next generation's price increment should have been applied. As the price increment for the next generation is not immediately applied due to the delay in incrementing generations, there is a potential for loss of funds for the protocol. So, tokens are minted at a lower price, even though they should be minted at the increased price of the subsequent generation.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

To address this issue, consider modifying the `_incrementGeneration` function to prevent minting more than the maximum allowed tokens per generation.

Remove unnecessary line in the `_incrementGeneration` function:
