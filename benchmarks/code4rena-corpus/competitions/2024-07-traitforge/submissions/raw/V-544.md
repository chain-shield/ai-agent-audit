# Vulnerability details

### [H-3] Ownership Restriction in `EntropyGenerator::initializeAlphaIndices()` Prevents Generation Increment in `TraitForgeNft::_incrementGeneration`

### Vulnerability Details
The `TraitForgeNft::_incrementGeneration()` function is responsible for incrementing the generation when the conditions are met. Within this internal function, there is a call to `entropyGenerator.initializeAlphaIndices()`, a function from `EntropyGenerator.sol`. This function in `EntropyGenerator.sol` is restricted by the onlyOwner modifier, meaning it can only be called by the owner of the `EntropyGenerator` contract. The sponsor's plan is for their multisig wallet to be the owner of `EntropyGenerator.sol.` Consequently, if `TraitForgeNft` is not the owner of `EntropyGenerator.sol`, it will be unable to call `initializeAlphaIndices()`.

### Impact
The protocol will be unable to start the next generation after the first generation is completed. This will halt the minting process and prevent the progression of the contract's intended functionality.

### Proof of Concept
* Deployed  `EntropyGenerator`.sol Contract with a wallet that is not TraitForgeNft
* Mint tokens until reaching `maxTokensPerGen` for the current generation.
* Attempt to mint one more token.
* Observe the transaction reverting due to the failed `initializeAlphaIndices()` call.
* Check that the currentGeneration in TraitForgeNft remains unchanged.

### Recommended Mitigation
Ensure that `TraitForgeNft` is the owner of the `EntropyGenerator` contract. This will allow `TraitForgeNft` to successfully call `initializeAlphaIndices()` when necessary, enabling the protocol to progress to the next generation without issues.
