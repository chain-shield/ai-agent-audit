# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Users who buy NFT are excluded from airdrop

## Proof of Concept
Here's the documentation:

```
TraitForge will receive an airdrop of $TRAIT tokens, which can be initiated at any time. Each instance of direct minting or forging to mint is recorded in the Airdrop Contract, along with the entropy of the minted entity. This process establishes a comprehensive tracking system for the total number of entities minted by each player and their corresponding entropy. In the event that a player 'nukes' an entity, the entropy allocated by that entity is removed. The cumulative entropy accrued by a player determines their final share of the airdrop. It is anticipated that the airdrop will be initiated within the first year of gameplay to facilitate a broader distribution of $TRAIT tokens.
```
https://github.com/TraitForge/GitBook/blob/main/GamePlay/%24TRAIT%20Token%20Airdrop.md

Based on the above, it is only users who minted directly or forge to mint that are eligible to airdrop. And that a user can be deprived of an airdrop only when a user nukes his token.

Now, the issue is a user who calls the buyNFT() and bought NFT is not recoreded in the airdrop contract or entitled to airdrop.

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-traitforge/blob/279b2887e3d38bc219a05d332cbcb0655b2dc644/contracts/EntityTrading/EntityTrading.sol#L63-L92

Note that a user can mint and immediately list the minted token for sale. In this instance, the token is not nuked but rather transferred. And the token entropy is sitll in existence and calculated right from the time the token was minted.

The new owner of the token ought to be eligible for airdrop (if airdrop has not been done). But this is not the case.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
The airdrop eligibility right of the previous token owner should be transferred to the new owner upon calling buyNFT().
