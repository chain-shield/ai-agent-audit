# Vulnerability details

## Impact
If the `taxCut` variable is set to `1`. 100% of the `msg.value` will go to the `nukeFundContribution` and `devShare`.

## Proof of Concept
- In the `NukeFund` and `EntityForging` contracts, the contract owner calls `setTaxCut(1)`.
- In the `EntityForging` contract, a user calls `buyNFT(1)` and sends along a `msg.value` of `100` (the listing price amount).
- On line 72 of `EntityForging`, `nukeFundContribution = 100 / 1`, which calculates to `100`.
- On line 73 of `EntityForging`, `sellerProceeds = 100 - 100`, which calculates to `0`.
- The `nukeFundContribution` amount is sent to the `NukeFund` contract.
- On line 41 of `NukeFund`, `devShare = 100 / 1`, which calculates to `100`. That amount is now claimable by the contract developers.
- Back in `EntityForging.buyNFT`, NFT token `1` is successfully transferred to the buyer.
- The contract developers have effectively stolen the seller's proceeds.

## Tools Used
Manual review.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
In all instances of the `setTaxCut` function, enforce a requirement that `_taxCut` be greater than `1`.
