# Vulnerability details


## Impact

The `initializeAlphaIndices` function is currently protected by the `onlyOwner` modifier, requiring an ownership transfer from the current owner/DAO to the `TraitForgeNft` contract. This contradicts the design, as the `TraitForgeNft` contract is not equipped to manage `EntropyGenerator` functionalities such as emergency pause/unpause or ownership transfers. 

This incorrect access control can lead to improper management and potential misuse of the function, impacting the overall protocol's governance and security.
