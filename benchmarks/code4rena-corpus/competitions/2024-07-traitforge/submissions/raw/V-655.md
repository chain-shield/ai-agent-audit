# Vulnerability details

## Summary
The `EntropyGenerator` use of keccak256 with block.number, block.timestamp, and other predictable values to generate randomness is vulnerable to prediction and manipulation by attackers. This lack of true randomness can compromise the integrity and security of the contract's operations, leading to potential exploitation.
## Impact
1. Predictable Outputs: Attackers can predict the pseudo-random values generated by the contract, allowing them to manipulate or game the system.
2. Exploitation: An attacker could exploit the weak randomness to ensure certain outcomes, potentially gaining unfair advantages or causing financial losses.
3. Integrity and Security Risks: The integrity and security of the contract are compromised as the randomness is not truly random, leading to potential vulnerabilities in any functionality that relies on these values.
