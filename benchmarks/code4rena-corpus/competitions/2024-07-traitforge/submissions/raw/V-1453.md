# Vulnerability details

The inputs to the keccak256 hash function (block.number and i) are predictable and can be known to anyone observing the blockchain. 
This predictability makes it possible for an attacker to pre-compute the pseudoRandomValue.
Without true randomness, the integrity of the system is at risk, and users can lose trust in the fairness and security of the application

## Impact
Systems relying on this randomization for security, fairness, or randomness can be manipulated by attackers, leading to unfair advantages or compromised security.
