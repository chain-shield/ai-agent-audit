# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Amount paid by users fully goes to developers instead of nuke(fund)

## Proof of Concept
when ever a user mints a new NFT the amount paid by him goes to NukeFund contract 
https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-traitforge/blob/279b2887e3d38bc219a05d332cbcb0655b2dc644/contracts/TraitForgeNft/TraitForgeNft.sol#L361-L362
In receive function of NukeFund, `uint256 devShare = msg.value / taxCut;`
   ` uint256 remainingFund = msg.value - devShare;`
   ` fund += remainingFund;`
https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-traitforge/blob/279b2887e3d38bc219a05d332cbcb0655b2dc644/contracts/NukeFund/NukeFund.sol#L40-L57
If owner sets taxCut as 1, then entire amount paid by buyer goes to devFund contract and eventually goes to developers instead of storing in NukeFund contract so users will get far more less amount than what they deserve when they Nuke their NFT.



## Tools Used
manual review
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
set a maximum limit to taxcut, example
`function setTaxCut(uint256 _taxCut) external onlyOwner {`
`require(_taxCut <= 15, taxcut exceed maximum value);  ` 
`taxCut = _taxCut;`
  `}`
