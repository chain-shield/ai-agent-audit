S-456
Double Spending Due to Optimism Commitment Challenge
joaovwfreire
34
Sufficient
383d ago
Description

Messages sent from Optimism to Ethereum Mainnet rely on state commitments from the Optimism rollup. These commitments are subject to fault proof games during a 7-day challenge window. If a commitment is successfully challenged and the Optimism state is reverted, a user may reclaim their tokens on Optimism while the corresponding message is still processed on Mainnet. This creates a double-spending vulnerability.

Critically, the system does not account for the possibility of a state rollback during the challenge window, allowing messages to be processed on Mainnet even though the corresponding state on Optimism may later be invalidated. Relying solely on block finality for readiness ignores the risk of Optimism's 7-day challenge mechanism invalidating the underlying transaction.

Impact

Double Spending: A user reclaims their tokens on Optimism after a rollback while still benefiting from the processed message on Mainnet.

Malicious actors can also deliberately trigger invalid states, causing financial imbalances and undermining trust in the cross-chain messaging system.

Proof of Concept

Consider the following scenario:

Message Sending: Alice initiates a cross-chain transfer of 100 tokens from Optimism to Mainnet.
State Commitment: The message is included in a state commitment on Optimism and relayed to Mainnet.
Message Execution on Mainnet: Mainnet processes the message, transferring 100 tokens to Alice’s wallet.
Challenge Raised: A challenge is raised against the Optimism state commitment during the 7-day window, proving it invalid.
State Rollback: Optimism reverts its state, and Alice’s 100 tokens are returned to her wallet on Optimism.
Double Spending: Alice now has 100 tokens on both Optimism and Mainnet due to the invalidated state not propagating back to Mainnet.
Tools Used
Manual code review
Conceptual modeling of fault-proof games and cross-chain messaging mechanisms
Recommended Mitigation Steps

To address this issue, implement measures to ensure messages sent from Optimism to Mainnet are only executed after the 7-day challenge window has passed.
This may not necessarily fit the business needs of the protocol. In such case, the protocol should be aware of the risks.
