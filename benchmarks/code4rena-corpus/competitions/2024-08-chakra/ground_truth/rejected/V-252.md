## Brief Summary

The `cross_chain_erc20_settlement` function, crucial for initiating token transfers from Ethereum to Starknet, lacks a minimum gas requirement setting. This omission can lead to transaction failures in the cross-chain messaging process, potentially resulting in stuck or failed token transfers.
