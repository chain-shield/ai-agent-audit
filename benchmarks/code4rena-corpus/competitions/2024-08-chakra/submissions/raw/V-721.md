# Vulnerability details

## Impact
ERC20 tokens with different decimals on different chain can lead to users benefitting by minting more tokens OR losing by minting less tokens on the destination chain.

## Proof of Concept
There exists ERC20 tokens those who have different decimal places on different chain.

For example USDC, On Ethereum has 6 decimal places & And Binance Smart Chain(BSC i.e in scope) has 18 decimal places, you can check below
https://bscscan.com/address/0x8ac76a51cc950d9822d68b83fe1ad97b32cd580d#readProxyContract#F3

And in current implementation, there's no scaling of decimal places based on the destination chain.

The parameters are being encoded into a payload, and then decoded on the destination chain directly, there's no scaling involved.

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-chakra/blob/d0d45ae1d26ca1b87034e67180fac07ce9642fd9/solidity/handler/contracts/ChakraSettlementHandler.sol#L180-L188

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-chakra/blob/d0d45ae1d26ca1b87034e67180fac07ce9642fd9/solidity/handler/contracts/ChakraSettlementHandler.sol#L320-L351

Hence any user can benefit from this, or can get into loss.

For example : 
On BSC , Alice inputs amount = 1e18, i.e 1 USDC.
Hence then while `cross_chain_erc20_settlement`, 1 USDC will be locked/burned based on mode of settlement.

Then on Ethereum, the payload will be decoded, and `payload.amount` will be unlocked/minted .
Hence 1e18 amount will be minted, i.e 1e12 USDC tokens.

Because of which easily ALICE was able to benefit more tokens than expected.

Same if done vice-versa, a user can be in great loss.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Scaling of amount should be done on the destination chain, based on the decimals places before unlocking/minting.

OR

The use of such tokens should be PROHIBITED, which have different decimal places on different chains
