# Vulnerability details

## Impact
When a user's cross-chain fails, the user cannot obtain any refund and the user's funds will be locked.

## Proof of Concept
background:
1. Users can cross-chain tokens from chain A to chain B through the protocol.
2. After chain A sends a request, chain B may fail to execute. [The user's entire cross-chain request will be marked as Failed](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-chakra/blob/abef77d95866f2fec93270491fc5abc9ab14f86d/solidity/handler/contracts/ChakraSettlementHandler.sol#L391-L393).
3. After the marking is completed, [no refund operation will be initiated for the user](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-chakra/blob/abef77d95866f2fec93270491fc5abc9ab14f86d/solidity/handler/contracts/ChakraSettlementHandler.sol#L391-L393).
4. Even if the protocol wants to implement manual refunds through the mintTo and burnFrom functions of the token, [there are cross-chain forms such as Lock and UnLock](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-chakra/blob/abef77d95866f2fec93270491fc5abc9ab14f86d/solidity/handler/contracts/ChakraSettlementHandler.sol#L123-L131), and the protocol does not have the authority to perform mint/burn. User funds will still be locked.

In summary, if cross-chain execution fails, the funds transferred by the user to the handler of chain A will be locked in the contract.
Since the funds may be locked, I judge it to be High.

## Tools Used
manual

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
When cross-chain execution fails, the user will be refunded in the handler.
