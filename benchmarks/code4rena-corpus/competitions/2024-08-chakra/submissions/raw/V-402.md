# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In `settlement.cairo::check_chakra_signatures`, the assert compares if `pass_count` is greater than or equal to `required_validators_num`. In this scenario, `pass_count` are the valid signatures verified after the looping. The function should only work  is the `validate signatures` is greater than the `required_validators_num`. The assert instead allows a condition where the `pass_count >= required_validators_num`. The malicious user will take advantage of this one moment where the variables are equal and perform a pass check on a malicious signature.    

## Proof of Concept

This ommission could also introduce a bug in `settlement.cairo::receive_cross_chain_msg`, a function which depends on it to be safe.


## Recommended Mitigation Steps
At the assert level, use only the greater than operator and not the greater than or equal to:

```javascript
 @>   assert(pass_count > self.required_validators_num.read(), 'Not enough validate signatures');
```
