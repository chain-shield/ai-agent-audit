# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The `ckr_btc.cairo::mint_to` function does not put a check to confirm whether the `to` is a zero address. This omission can introduce different sets of vulnerabilities into the contract such as burning of tokens by mistake. Malicious users can take advantage of other unexpected behaviors brought about by this challenge to exploit the contract.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Assert that the `to` is not a zero address

```javascript
    fn mint_to(ref self: ContractState, to: ContractAddress, amount: u256) -> bool {
            let caller = get_caller_address();
        @>  assert(!to.is_zero(), 'ERC20: mint to 0');
```
