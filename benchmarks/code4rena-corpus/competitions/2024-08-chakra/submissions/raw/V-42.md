# Vulnerability details


## Summary

The `BaseSettlementHandler` contract does not invoke the `_disableInitializers` function in its constructor. This omission exposes the implementation contract to unauthorized initialization, allowing potential attackers to gain control over the contract and disrupt its intended functionality.

## Impact

Without `_disableInitializers`, an attacker could initialize the implementation contract and take control over it. This vulnerability could be exploited to delegatecall a malicious contract containing a self-destruct opcode, which could destroy the implementation contract. Consequently, this action would permanently lock any assets within the proxy, rendering them inaccessible and causing significant financial loss and disruption of services.

## Recommendation

To mitigate this risk, it is recommended to include the `_disableInitializers()` call in the constructor of the `BaseSettlementHandler` contract. This measure will prevent unauthorized initialization by locking the implementation contract immediately upon deployment. By doing so, even if someone attempts to initialize the contract directly, they will be unable to gain ownership or execute critical functions, safeguarding the contract against potential attacks.
