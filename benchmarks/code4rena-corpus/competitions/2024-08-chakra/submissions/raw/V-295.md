# Vulnerability details

## Impact

Tokens may behave differently on different chains, especially decimal. Tokens of different decimals use the same quantity when crossing chains, which leads to an imbalance in funds. Eventually, the tokens will be issued infinitely for arbitrage and become worthless.       

## Proof of Concept

Taking the mainstream USDC token as an example, its decimal on different chains is also different ([ETH - 6 decimals](https://etherscan.io/token/0xa0b86991c6218b36c1d19d4a2e9eb0ce3606eb48#readProxyContract#F11) \ [BSC - 18 decimals](https://bscscan.com/address/0x8ac76a51cc950d9822d68b83fe1ad97b32cd580d#readProxyContract#F3)).    
From the audit documents, Chakra supports other ERC20 and ERC777 tokens, and even [ChakraToken](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-chakra/blob/d0d45ae1d26ca1b87034e67180fac07ce9642fd9/solidity/handler/contracts/ChakraToken.sol#L35) itself supports arbitrary decimal tokens.
Therefore, when crossing chains, it is necessary to consider the decimal issue, rather than assuming that the two tokens have the same decimal. 

## Tools Used

Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

The cross-chain quantity should be divided by the decimalA and then multiplied by the decimalB
