# Vulnerability details

## Impact
On zkSync, MSTORE has a different meaning and as result applies differently as compared to ethereum. this can cause further complications or misinterpretations on these chains 

## Proof of Concept
From zkSync documentation;
[Unlike EVM, where the memory growth is in words, on zkEVM the memory growth is counted in bytes. For example, if you write mstore(100, 0) the msize on zkEVM will be 132, but on the EVM it will be 160. Note, that also unlike EVM which has quadratic growth for memory payments, on zkEVM the fees are charged linearly at a rate of 1 erg per byte...](https://docs.zksync.io/build/developer-reference/ethereum-differences/evm-instructions#mstore-mload)
