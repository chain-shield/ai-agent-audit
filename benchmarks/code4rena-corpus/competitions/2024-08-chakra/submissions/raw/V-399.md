# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The constructors in `settlement.cairo::constructor`,and `handler_erc20.cairo::constructor`, fails to confirm if the owner is a zero address. This could be consequential if an attacker tries to access the contracts through the zero address and potentially exploiting the protocol.   

## Proof of Concept
The lines of code shown below have their constructors lacking a zero address check;

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-chakra/blob/d0d45ae1d26ca1b87034e67180fac07ce9642fd9/cairo/handler/src/settlement.cairo#L162

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-chakra/blob/d0d45ae1d26ca1b87034e67180fac07ce9642fd9/cairo/handler/src/handler_erc20.cairo#L35

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
In the constructors assert that the owner is not a zero address.

```javascript
 @>   assert(owner.is_non_zero(), errors::OWNER_IS_ZERO);
```
