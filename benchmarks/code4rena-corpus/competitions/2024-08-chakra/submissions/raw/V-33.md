# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The `ChakraSettlement` contract is missing a check to ensure below main invariant holds:

> In Settlement Contract
> - Only valid settlement handlers can process received cross-chain messages and callbacks.

The main invariants should not be broken to ensure all validations are applied to prototect the cross-chain protocol.

## Proof of Concept

The `ChakraSettlement` contract does not have a handler whitelist to ensure that the `to_handler` in the `receive_cross_chain_msg()` and `receive_cross_chain_callback()` functions is valid.

## Tools Used
vscode

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Ensure the `to_handler` in the `receive_cross_chain_msg()` and `receive_cross_chain_callback()` functions is whitelisted.
