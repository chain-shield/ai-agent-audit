# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The txid in ChakraSettlementUpgradeTest and ChakraSettlementHandler cannot correspond, resulting in a revert when the callback is executed in the handler. All cross-chain transactions cannot be completed normally.

## Proof of Concept
background:
1. The txid in [ChakraSettlementUpgradeTest](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-chakra/blob/abef77d95866f2fec93270491fc5abc9ab14f86d/solidity/settlement/contracts/ChakraSettlementUpgradeTest.sol#L98-L111) and [ChakraSettlementHandler](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-chakra/blob/abef77d95866f2fec93270491fc5abc9ab14f86d/solidity/handler/contracts/ChakraSettlementHandler.sol#L139-L150) cannot correspond.
2. In the cross-chain protocol from chain A to chain B, if the cross-chain is successfully executed on chain B, [the callback function of chain A will also be called to complete the cross-chain](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-chakra/blob/abef77d95866f2fec93270491fc5abc9ab14f86d/solidity/settlement/contracts/ChakraSettlementUpgradeTest.sol#L212).
3. [Since the cross-chain message ID is calculated based on the ChakraSettlementUpgradeTest contract](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-chakra/blob/abef77d95866f2fec93270491fc5abc9ab14f86d/solidity/settlement/contracts/ChakraSettlementUpgradeTest.sol#L125). So when this id is passed to the handler, the [corresponding transaction cannot be found](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-chakra/blob/abef77d95866f2fec93270491fc5abc9ab14f86d/solidity/handler/contracts/ChakraSettlementHandler.sol#L378-L381). The handler's callback will revert.

In summary, due to inconsistent txid calculations, the ChakraSettlementUpgradeTest contract cannot be completed cross-chain. Seriously affects the functionality of the protocol.

## Tools Used
manual

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
It is recommended that when updating the Settlement, the calculation method of txid in the handler should be updated simultaneously.
