## Impact
Detailed description of the impact of this finding.
Title: ChakraSettlementUpgradeTest contract has Typographical Error in Enum Definition and Usage
•	Severity: Medium
•	Impact: A typographical error was found in the CrossChainMsgStatus enum, where the status Unknow should likely be corrected to Unknown.
•	Status: Unresolved
•	File: ChakraSettlementUpgradeTest.sol

A typographical error was found in the CrossChainMsgStatus enum, where the status Unknow should likely be corrected to Unknown. This typo appears in both the enum definition and its usage throughout the contract. Additionally, it may cause misunderstandings or mistakes when the contract is integrated with other systems or when its output is interpreted by external tools or users.

The main impact of this issue is related to code readability and maintainability. Developers who are not aware of the typo might misinterpret the status, leading to potential bugs or miscommunication. Furthermore, if the contract interacts with external systems that rely on the status text, it could cause unexpected behavior or require unnecessary modifications in those systems.

The same typographical error is in the Cairo structure in the settlement.cairo contract as follows:
```sol
        pub const UNKNOW: u8 = 0;
```
