# Vulnerability details

## Impact
When the callback receives a successful call with `SettlementMode.MintBurn` it will burn the previous locked tokens. If the call was not successful, there is not way to unlock the locked tokens and they will stay in the hander contract.

N/A

## Tools Used
manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Transfer the tokens to the user if the message was not successful.
