# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Detailed description of the impact of this finding.
in this add_operator() the owner of the contract has the ability to assign the operator_role to any address including itself. this means that indirectly the owner could give themselves or any other address the ability to mint token using the mint_to() function
as the owner has too much control it could raise concerns about the centralization of power, expecially in decentralized ecosystem where trustlessness is the key
for an example where the owner's private key is compromised, an attacker could take control of the owner's address and perform malicious action, such as assigning them selves the operator_role and minting excessive tokens, since there is no immutable limits or caps in the mint_to function
