# Vulnerability details

## Discord 

The `ChakraSettlement` contract handles cross-chain messages, which are critical for ensuring the integrity and consistency of transactions across different blockchain networks. The [`send_cross_chain_msg()` function](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-chakra/blob/abef77d95866f2fec93270491fc5abc9ab14f86d/cairo/handler/src/settlement.cairo#L284-L314) is responsible for creating and sending these messages, while the [`receive_cross_chain_msg()` function](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-chakra/blob/abef77d95866f2fec93270491fc5abc9ab14f86d/cairo/handler/src/settlement.cairo#L325-L383) processes incoming messages. However, the current implementation lacks a nonce or unique identifier for each cross-chain transaction, which can lead to replay attacks.

In the `send_cross_chain_msg()` function, the `cross_chain_settlement_id` is generated using the transaction hash and a transaction count. This approach does not guarantee uniqueness across different chains or prevent replay attacks:

## Impact
The lack of a nonce or unique identifier in cross-chain messages allows an attacker to replay valid messages, leading to double-spending of assets, repeated execution of critical operations, and inconsistent state across different chains. 


## Proof of Concept
1. Alice initiates a cross-chain transfer of 100 tokens from Chain A to Chain B.
2. The message is successfully processed on Chain B, and Alice receives her tokens.
3. An attacker observes this transaction and replays the exact same message on Chain B.
4. Without a proper nonce check, Chain B processes the message again, giving Alice another 100 tokens.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Implement a nonce system for cross-chain messages to ensure each message is unique and processed only once.
