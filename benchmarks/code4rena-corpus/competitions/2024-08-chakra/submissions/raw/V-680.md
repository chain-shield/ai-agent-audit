# Vulnerability details


## Impact

The bridging process will not correctly handle ERC20 tokens that have different decimals across chains, which can result in significant miscalculations of token amounts.

## Proof of Concept

As per Readme,

> ERC20 used by the protocol:	any

And for chain in which the protocol is going to be deployed on:

> Chains the protocol will be deployed on: Ethereum,Base,Arbitrum,BSC,Optimism,Polygon,zkSync

Some ERC20 tokens have different decimals on different chains. Even some popular ones like USDT and USDC have 6 decimals on Ethereum, and 18 decimals on BSC for example:

- [USDT on Ethereum](https://etherscan.io/token/0xdac17f958d2ee523a2206206994597c13d831ec7#readContract#F6) - 6 decimals
- [USDC on Ethereum](https://etherscan.io/token/0xa0b86991c6218b36c1d19d4a2e9eb0ce3606eb48#readProxyContract#F11) - 6 decimals
- [USDT on BSC](https://bscscan.com/address/0x55d398326f99059ff775485246999027b3197955#readContract#F6) - 18 decimals
- [USDC on BSC](https://bscscan.com/address/0x8ac76a51cc950d9822d68b83fe1ad97b32cd580d#readProxyContract#F3) - 18 decimals

A more exhaustive list can be found in this [tokens-decimals](https://github.com/magnetto90/tokens-decimals) repository.

**Example Scenario:**

1. Alice wants to bridge 100 USDC from Ethereum to BSC with settlement mode: `LockUnlock`:
    * On Ethereum, USDC has 6 decimals, so Alice’s 100 USDC will be represented as 100e6.

2. Locking USDC on Ethereum:
    * The protocol locks `100e6` USDC on Ethereum as part of the `LockUnlock` settlement mode.

3. Unlocking USDC on BSC:
    * When the cross-chain message is processed on BSC, the protocol attempts to unlock USDC.
    * On BSC, USDC has 18 decimals, so the `100e6` amount is interpreted as `10**-10` USDC on BSC (because BSC expects amounts in 18 decimal format).

This leads to a significant loss of funds for Alice. 

If this process were reversed (bridging USDC from BSC to Ethereum), a small amount locked on BSC could unlock a disproportionately large amount on Ethereum, which could allow someone to drain the entire balance on the Ethereum side.

## Tools Used

VS Code

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Standardize to 18 Decimals on Bridging and then Adjust to Destination Chain Decimals.
