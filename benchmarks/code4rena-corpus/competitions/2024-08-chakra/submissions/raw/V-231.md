# Vulnerability details

## Impact
For the BurnUnlock mode, tokens/funds might be burned but if the unlock part fails, the funds will be unrecoverable and lost forever, leading to an invariant broken that the same amount of tokens exist in both chains, as one chain's amount will be loss due to burning.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Although it might be a feature, I believe that burning tokens first before confirming transfer succeeded would lead to problems, and especially since there are a lot of reasons why even after burning the transaction might fail, burning in a later stage is recommended.
