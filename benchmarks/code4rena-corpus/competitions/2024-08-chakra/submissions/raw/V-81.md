# Vulnerability details

The send_cross_chain_msg function does not perform any validation on its inputs, which include to_chain, to_handler, payload_type, and payload. These inputs are crucial for ensuring that cross-chain messages are sent correctly and securely.

AFFECTED LINE OF CODE:

Impact: Without proper validation, invalid or malicious inputs could cause the function to behave unexpectedly. This could result in cross-chain messages being sent to incorrect handlers or chains, leading to potential loss of assets or failed transactions. Malicious actors could exploit this lack of validation to disrupt cross-chain communication or direct messages inappropriately.
