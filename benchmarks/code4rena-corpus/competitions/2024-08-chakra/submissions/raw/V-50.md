# Vulnerability details

## Impact

The upgrade from the `ChakraToken` to the `ChakraTokenUpgrade` contract introduces an incompatibility, where the `ChakraTokenUpgrade` contract lacks the `mint_to()` and `burn_from()` functions. As a result, the settlement handler is unable to mint or burn tokens,

## Proof of Concept

The `ChakraTokenUpgrade` contract is the next version of the `ChakraToken` contract. However, athe `ChakraTokenUpgrade` contract does not include the `mint_to()` and `burn_from()` functions, which are utilized by the settlement handler to mint or burn tokens.

## Tools Used
vscode

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

The `ChakraTokenUpgrade` contract should include the `mint_to()` and `burn_from()` functions, ensuring compatibility with the existing settlement handler.
