# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In the `ChakraSettlementHandler` contract, when a cross-chain transaction fails, there is no mechanism to automatically refund the funds locked or transferred during the transaction. This exposes users to the risk of losing their tokens or funds if a transaction fails. As cross-chain transactions involve high stakes and complexity, the lack of a refund mechanism can result in financial losses for users and reduce the reliability of the system.

## Proof of Concept
The contract manages cross-chain settlements using modes such as `MintBurn`, `LockUnlock`, `LockMint`, and `BurnUnlock`. For instance, in the `cross_chain_erc20_settlement` function, tokens are locked or burned depending on the mode, and the transaction is recorded. However, if the cross-chain message or callback fails, the contract updates the status to `Failed`, but does not initiate any refunds or reversals of the locked/burned tokens.
Here is the critical section of the code where failure handling is missing

The contract updates the status to Failed``, but no additional logic is implemented to return the tokens to the user. As a result, the tokens remain locked or burned with no possibility of recovery unless additional off-chain mechanisms are in place.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Implement a refund mechanism to handle failed cross-chain transactions. When a transaction status is updated to Failed, the contract should unlock and transfer the locked tokens back to the sender or mint an equivalent amount of tokens and transfer them back to the sender.
