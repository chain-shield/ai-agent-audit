# Vulnerability details

## Impact
When the handler of the target chain has insufficient funds, the cross-chain cannot be completed, and the user's funds will be locked and cannot be returned.

## Proof of Concept
background:
1. The protocol can cross-chain funds from chain A to chain B through events.
2. When cross-chaining, chain A will lock the user's funds and give the user the corresponding funds in chain B.
3. Under normal circumstances, [if an error is encountered in chain B, the contract will return false](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-chakra/blob/abef77d95866f2fec93270491fc5abc9ab14f86d/solidity/handler/contracts/ChakraSettlementHandler.sol#L312) and [mark the cross-chain as Failed](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-chakra/blob/abef77d95866f2fec93270491fc5abc9ab14f86d/solidity/settlement/contracts/ChakraSettlement.sol#L232). The order may be refunded later through cross-chain status.
4. When the handler of chain B has insufficient funds, [it will revert directly instead of returning false](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-chakra/blob/abef77d95866f2fec93270491fc5abc9ab14f86d/solidity/handler/contracts/ChakraSettlementHandler.sol#L272-L275). Chain B cannot fully execute cross-chain transactions.

In other words, when the handler has insufficient funds, chain B cannot complete the cross-chain. It cannot be marked as Failed either. The contract cannot refund users. User funds are locked in the contract.

Since the funds may be locked, I judge it to be High.

## Tools Used
manual

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Use try to call transfer to transfer, and return false if the transfer fails.
