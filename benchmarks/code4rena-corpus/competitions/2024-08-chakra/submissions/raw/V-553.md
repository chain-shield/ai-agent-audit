# Vulnerability details

## Impact
This can lead to possible loss of funds and possibly break accounting for the protocol.

## Proof of Concept
When `settlement::receive_cross_chain_callback()`  is called, the `handler_erc20::receive_cross_chain_callback(...)` burns token amount in the message payload and then a `created_tx` is updated for the `cross_chain_msg_id` being processed. 
However, the `created_tx` is done wrongly and even the `CrossChainResult` event used by the validators is emitted with the wrong parameters

- this breaks accounting for the protocol considering that the state is wrong
- and validators output will as well be wrong and as the input received are wrong as well
