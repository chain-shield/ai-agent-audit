# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The lack of proper access control in the `receive_cross_chain_msg` and `receive_cross_chain_callback` functions presents a severe vulnerability. This allows unauthorized parties to call these functions, leading to potential exploitation such as replay attacks where malicious actors resend valid transactions to manipulate the system, griefing attacks where spammed or invalid messages could disrupt the normal operation of the system, potentially freezing or halting cross-chain communication or unauthorized modifications to the state of cross-chain transactions, resulting in financial losses or corruption of data on the destination chain.

## Proof of Concept
The [`receive_cross_chain_msg`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-chakra/blob/d0d45ae1d26ca1b87034e67180fac07ce9642fd9/solidity/settlement/contracts/ChakraSettlement.sol#L170-L244) function can be called by any external party without restriction. Malicious actors can bypass signature verification or even attempt denial-of-service (DoS) attacks by flooding the contract with invalid or repetitive messages, affecting system performance and potentially leading to state corruption.

Similarly, [`the receive_cross_chain_callback`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-chakra/blob/d0d45ae1d26ca1b87034e67180fac07ce9642fd9/solidity/settlement/contracts/ChakraSettlement.sol#L256-L283) function has no restriction on who can invoke it.
Without access control mechanisms, any attacker could invoke this function to manipulate the status of a cross-chain transaction or disrupt the settlement process.

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Use access control modifiers such as `onlyAuthorized` or `onlyTrustedRelay` to restrict these functions to be callable only by trusted entities or contracts.
