# Vulnerability details


The `settlement.cairo::receive_cross_chain_msg()` and `settlement.cairo::receive_cross_chain_callback()` functions contain errors in the parameters used for emitting events. The incorrect parameters are highlighted in the code snippets below, marked with `@>`.

In cross-chain projects, the accuracy of emitted events is as critical as the correctness of the underlying logic. These events are often utilized to verify the integrity of cross-chain transactions, trigger operations in other contracts, or notify off-chain systems. Any discrepancies in the event data can lead to a cascade of errors, potentially jeopardizing the entire cross-chain operation.

## Impact
The impact of this issue is significant because it undermines the reliability and accuracy of event logs, which are crucial for cross-chain communication. Inaccurate event data can cause:

1. Misalignment of Chain Data: Off-chain systems or other contracts that rely on these events to track cross-chain operations may act on incorrect information, leading to failures in executing transactions or validating operations.
2. Security Risks: Incorrect event parameters could be exploited by malicious actors to disrupt the integrity of cross-chain transactions, potentially leading to financial losses or denial of service.
3. Operational Failures: Systems designed to monitor cross-chain events may fail to detect or respond to transactions correctly, resulting in incomplete or failed operations.
