# Vulnerability details

## Impact
txid recorded by ChakraSettlement and recorded by ChakraSettlementHandler are different for the same transaction. ChakraSettlementHandler::receive_cross_chain_callback() will always be reverted because of the unmatched txid.

## Proof of Concept
When we bridge tokens, one unique txid will be generated based on msg.sender's nonce. And the ChakraSettlementHandler and ChakraSettlement will generate the txid dependently based on recorded users' nonce for this transaction.
The problem is that users' nonce `nonce_manager[msg.sender]` in ChakraSettlementHandler and ChakraSettlement might be different. Because `ChakraSettlement` is designed to process multiple handler's messages, and `ChakraSettlementHandler` aims to process one kind of token's transfer. In normal scenario, multiple `ChakraSettlementHandler` will interact with `ChakraSettlement`. This will cause nonce in `ChakraSettlement` will be larger than the nonce in `ChakraSettlementHandler`.

This will cause the generated txids in `ChakraSettlementHandler` and `ChakraSettlement` are different. When the `receive_cross_chain_callback` is triggered in the source chain, this transaction will always be reverted, because 
`ChakraSettlementHandler` cannot recognize this `txid` and `create_cross_txs` status will be unknown.
