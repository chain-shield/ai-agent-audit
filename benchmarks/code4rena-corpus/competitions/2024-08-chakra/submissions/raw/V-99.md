# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In contracts such as BaseSettlementHandler, gap variables are not used, nor are designated storage slots used for storage. During subsequent upgrades, variable overwriting may occur.

## Proof of Concept
In the upgradeable contract BaseSettlementHandler, the _gap variable is not used to reserve a storage location, and variable overwriting may occur during subsequent upgrades.
For similar examples, please refer to https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2024-05-midas-judging/issues/109
In contracts such as its parent contract OwnableUpgradeable, hash is used to determine the storage location, but none of the contracts implemented by the project itself does this. The storage structure will be destroyed during subsequent upgrades.

manual

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Add gaps for non pure-function contracts
