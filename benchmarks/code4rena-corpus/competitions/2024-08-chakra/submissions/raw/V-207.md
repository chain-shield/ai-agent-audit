# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The txid calculated by ERC20Handler and ChakraSettlement may be different. When `receive_cross_chain_callback` is triggered in source chain, ERC20Handler cannot match the txid from ChakraSettlement. If we use mintBurn mode, we cannot burn the tokens as expected.

## Proof of Concept
When users bridge tokens, one unique txid will be generated based on msg_count. ERC20Handler and ChakraSettlement will generate the txid dependently. 
The problem is that the txid generated by ERC20Handler and ChakraSettlement may be different. Because txid is generated based on msg_count. And msg_count may be different in ERC20Handler and ChakraSettlement. Because multiple handlers will interact with one settlement, in the normal case, the settlement's msg_count will be larger than handler's msg_count.
