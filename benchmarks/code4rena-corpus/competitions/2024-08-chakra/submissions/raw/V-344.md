# Vulnerability details

## Impact
`ChakraSettlement.receive_cross_chain_msg` and `settlement.receive_cross_chain_msg`'s signatures don't include `block.chainid`. If hardfork happens, the tx can be replayed on the forked chain.

## Proof of Concept
I'll take [ChakraSettlement.receive_cross_chain_msg](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-chakra/blob/d0d45ae1d26ca1b87034e67180fac07ce9642fd9/solidity/settlement/contracts/ChakraSettlement.sol#L170-L244) as an example
as shown in [ChakraSettlement.sol#L183-L192](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-chakra/blob/d0d45ae1d26ca1b87034e67180fac07ce9642fd9/solidity/settlement/contracts/ChakraSettlement.sol#L183-L192), the `message_hash` doesn't contain `block.chainid`.
