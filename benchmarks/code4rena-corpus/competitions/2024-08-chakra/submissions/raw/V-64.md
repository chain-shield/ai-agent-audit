# Vulnerability details

## Impact
When a cross-chain message is received on the settlement contract on Starknet, `receive_cross_chain_msg` is triggered by one of the validators after the signatures are signed to process the message. The settlement contract [interacts with the handler contract](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-chakra/blob/abef77d95866f2fec93270491fc5abc9ab14f86d/cairo/handler/src/settlement.cairo#L354) to process the ERC20 transfer, [either minting or unlocking](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-chakra/blob/abef77d95866f2fec93270491fc5abc9ab14f86d/cairo/handler/src/handler_erc20.cairo#L125-L132) the tokens to the recipient

The issue is that the function does not check whether the cross-chain message has already been received. As a result, a malicious actor can reuse the function arguments from the first transaction to repeatedly call the function, leading to a continuous unlocking or minting of tokens to the recipient. This could ultimately drain the protocol's funds.
