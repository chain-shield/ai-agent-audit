# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The incorrect naming of the settlement mode as `MintBurn` instead of `BurnMint` introduces potential confusion among developers, auditors, and users interacting with the contract. This discrepancy can lead to misunderstandings about how the cross-chain settlement process functions, which may cause improper usage, errors in system integration, and vulnerabilities in future contract upgrades.

In the `MintBurn` settlement mode, the intended process is to burn tokens on the source chain and mint them on the destination chain, but the naming suggests the opposite—minting first, then burning. While this does not directly affect the functionality of the contract, it can result in operational issues, misconfigurations, or even financial losses due to incorrect assumptions about the system's behavior.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Change the name of the settlement mode from `MintBurn` to `BurnMint` to accurately reflect the actual process of burning tokens on the source chain and minting tokens on the destination chain. This will improve clarity and reduce the risk of misunderstanding.
