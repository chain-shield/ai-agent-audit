# Vulnerability details

## Impact

CCTX with LockUnlock and BurnUnlock types will mostly be unsuccessful due to no liquidity

## Proof of Concept
For BurnUnlock types, user's tokens get burned on source chain, and tokens get transferred on dest chain.
For LockUnlock types, user's tokens get locked on source chain, and tokens get transferred on dest chain.

The problem is, there will always be liquidity issues. There will almost always not be enough tokens to transfer on dest chain.

Let's take LockUnlock for instance:

- Alice wants to lock 100 tokens on chain A to receive 100 tokens on chain B
- Protocol collects the 100 tokens on source chain
- Alice cannot receive her tokens on dest chain because it simply is not available.

The only scenario that can allow Alice to receive tokens on dest chain is if another user simultaneously wants to send exactly 100 tokens from chain B to chain A.

The above logic applies for BurnUnlock type.

Since tokens are not refundable on source chain, Alice(and other competitors) has to hope for someone else to make a matching CCTX(which has a very low chance of happening)

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

There should be a function that allows liquidity provisioning to facilitatae successful CCTX
