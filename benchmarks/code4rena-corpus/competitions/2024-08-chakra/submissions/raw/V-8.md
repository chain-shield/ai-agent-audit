# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The absence of gap storage increases the risk of storage layout corruption during contract upgrades.

## Proof of Concept

The `ChakraSettlementHandler` and `ChakraSettlement` contracts rely on the upgradability pattern provided by the `UUPSUpgradeable` contract. These contracts inherit state variables from their base contracts, `BaseSettlement` and `BaseSettlementHandler`. However, the base contracts do not implement gap storage.

Gap storage is a mechanism used in upgradable contracts to reserve storage slots for future variables, ensuring that new variables introduced during an upgrade do not conflict with existing ones. In the absence of such gaps, any new storage variables added to the contracts during an upgrade could overwrite existing variables in the inherited contracts, leading to unintended and potentially critical behavior.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Implement gap storage in the base contracts `BaseSettlement` and `BaseSettlementHandler`.
