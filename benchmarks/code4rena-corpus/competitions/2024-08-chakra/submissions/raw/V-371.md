# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Some ERC20 tokens have different decimals on the different chains. This means that they need to be handled correctly when they are bridged, otherwise this can cause serious problems like inability to bridge some of the ERC20's or bridge much less tokens to the user. Such example are the USDT AND USDC, they have 6 decimals on Ethereum but 18 decimals on Binance Smart Chain (both of this chains are in the scope of this audit). This problem is present in both the `solidity` and the `cairo` files and the fixes have to be applied in both of the files. For simplicity we will show just the problem in the `solidity` (handler and settlement) files

Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Implement a functionality that standardizes the decimals. For example when a user sends his tokens you should write them with 18 decimals in the message (no matter if the token has 18 decimals or not), and then on the destination chain you should also implement a logic that sets the correct decimals of the amount before being sent to the user. Apply this changes in the `cairo` files as well
