# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Funds can be stuck in the contract without a way to retry them.

## Proof of Concept
In both the EVM based chains and starknet, there are possibilities of cross chain messages failing. However there are no functions or mechanisms implemented to ensure these failed transactions can be retried.
Also, when a message fails, since the validators depend on events, the events are emitted with the status of the failed messages

This can lead to losses for the users and unexpected behaviour of the validators.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Either revert on failure or implement functions to retry failed messages and ensure to update the state of the messages in the contracts


## Assessed type

Access Control
