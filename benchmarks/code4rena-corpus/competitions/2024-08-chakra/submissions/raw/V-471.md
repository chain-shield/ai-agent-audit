# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Detailed description of the impact of this finding.
1. the initialiser function in the base contract does not include sufficient access control, and may be invoked again.

## Proof of Concept

1. The bad actor can take complete ownership, or perform upgrades (change owner, create weird token address etc).

## Tools Used

1. manual review.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
1. The initialiser may be coded to be securely invoked via singleton function pattern, or via internal only i.e from a constructor, or via designated initialise deinitialise pattern.
