# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In Cairo implementation, the txid's generated with get_tx_info and msg_count. This breaks one invariant: `Cross-chain transaction IDs are uniquely generated based on the source and destination chain information, sender, handler addresses, and nonce.`

## Proof of Concept
In cairo handler implementation, the txid will be generated based on get_tx_info() and msg_count. But from readme, we can find one invariants:
`Cross-chain transaction IDs are uniquely generated based on the source and destination chain information, sender, handler addresses, and nonce.`.
The txid generation in cairo handler implementation will break the invariant.
