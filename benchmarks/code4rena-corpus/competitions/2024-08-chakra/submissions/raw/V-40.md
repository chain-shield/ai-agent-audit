# Vulnerability details

## Impact

The `ChakraSettlementHandler::cross_chain_erc20_settlement()` function locks or burns users' tokens and sends a cross-chain message. However, if the user provides incorrect `to_chain` or `to_handler` inputs, the validators are unlikely to sign the message. The settlement contract on the target chain never receives the cross chain message, resulting in permanent loss of the user's tokens.

## Proof of Concept

The only validation in `cross_chain_erc20_settlement()` for user-provided `to_chain` and `to_handler` is `to_handler != 0`:
