This can happen with both addition and subtraction when handling liquidity or swap amounts.

Impact: if unchecked operations allow overflow on crucial parameters like liquidity, amount_in, and amount_out, potentially causing inaccurate calculations and giving attackers opportunities for manipulation.
The result could be incorrect price movements, liquidity miscalculations, or drained liquidity pools.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Use Rust’s checked arithmetic functions to prevent overflow. For example, use checked_mul, checked_sub, or checked_div, and handle None cases for any overflows.
Example Fix: Replace unchecked arithmetic operations with their checked counterparts:
