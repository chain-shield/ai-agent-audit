# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept
 Lack of Precision Handling (Rounding Errors in mul_div and mul_div_rounding_up): Division operations often involve rounding errors, especially in cases of fractional division. mul_div performs truncation by default, while mul_div_rounding_up attempts rounding up but may still yield inaccurate results due to limitations in handling near-overflow conditions.

Proof of Concept: When fractional results are expected, truncation or incorrect rounding can lead to inaccuracies:
let a = U256::from(10);
let b = U256::from(3);
let result = mul_div(a, b, U256::from(4)).unwrap(); // Expected result may vary due to truncation
Here, 10 * 3 / 4 should yield 7.5, but without proper handling, the result might be truncated to 7.

Impact: In financial or cryptographic operations, even minor rounding errors can significantly affect downstream calculations.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
1. Add precision-aware calculations where needed, such as through improved rounding logic.
2. Document the expected rounding behavior so users are aware of any limitations.
Example:
Enhance rounding accuracy by tracking fractional parts and adjusting the final result where appropriate:
let precise_result = result + if rem { U256::one() } else { U256::zero() };
