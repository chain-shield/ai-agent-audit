## Proof of Concept
Unsafe Casting Vulnerability: Unchecked or unsafe casting from a larger integer to a smaller integer type occurs without handling potential overflow or truncation. This might happen when converting U256 to smaller integer types like u128.

Impact:medium-high if improper casting could cause data loss, which might lead to incorrect calculations.
Casting errors can result in lost precision or negative values, which might cause the function to misbehave.
Proof of Concept: In code that requires liquidity calculations, any direct cast from U256 to u128 without bounds checks could lead to unexpected results:
let liquidity = some_large_value as u128;
If some_large_value exceeds the range of u128, the above cast will lead to an incorrect liquidity value.
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Use safe casting methods like .try_into() to handle potential overflow, and gracefully handle errors when the casting isn’t possible.
Example Fix:

let liquidity: u128 = some_large_value.try_into().map_err(|_| Error::CastOverflow)?;
