# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Loss of funds for users due to less amounts of RTokens redeemed 

## Proof of Concept
in `RToken::issueTo` there is not Slippage protection or minimum Amount issued variable
This is a problem due to the fact that Chain-link oracles has a deviation of 2-5% associated with bigger market movements the price may deviate more
```solidity
File: RToken.sol
110:         assetRegistry.refresh();
```  

since we refresh the price of assets on issuing (which is a good thing) 
- This will cause issuing with the _new_ price of the asset, causing substantially less RToken than what he expected
  
Leading to loss of funds
## Tools Used
Manaul Review
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Introduce a new variable to have minimum tokens issued to guard against volatile movements and unintended user harm during those times
