# Vulnerability details

## Impact

Through out the important contracts of the `reserve` protocol the `meta transactions` are supported by inheriting from the `OZ ContextUpgradeable`. We can see that in the `BasketHandler.sol` and `BackingManager` contracts.

The `DutchTrade` is an important contract in the `reserve` protocol since it enables the functionality to `initiate auctions`, `initiate bids` and `settle the auctions`.

The issue here is this contract uses `msg.sender`  thus not supporting the `metaTransactions`. Hence there is a discrepancy in how the `reserve` protocol supports the `metaTransactions`. This could lead to broken operations in the `reserve protocol` when the transactions are executed via a `relayer` for meta transactions.
