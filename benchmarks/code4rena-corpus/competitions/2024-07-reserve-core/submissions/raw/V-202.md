# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Staking at a low stake rate and unstaking at a high stake rate in the same block results in the extarction of value. Although the withdraw of funds is done after `availableAt`, the staking is not subjected to risk.

## Proof of Concept 
The attack happens when there's an opportunity to rebalance on backing manager.
[BackingManagerP1::settleTrade](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-reserve/blob/3f133997e186465f4904553b0f8e86ecb7bbacbf/contracts/p1/BackingManager.sol#L85) >> [BackingManagerP1::rebalance](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-reserve/blob/3f133997e186465f4904553b0f8e86ecb7bbacbf/contracts/p1/BackingManager.sol#L107) >> [stRSR::seizeRSR](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-reserve/blob/3f133997e186465f4904553b0f8e86ecb7bbacbf/contracts/p1/StRSR.sol#L424)

1. currently stake rate is 0.95e18 and draft rate = 1e18. So user stakes amount A RSR.
2. Then immediately calls `trade.bid` which will call through the flow mentioned above to `rebalance` >> then calls `seizeRSR`, which will increase the stake rate or even better stake rate = 1e18 (FIX_ONE) is assigned on `beginEra` internal call. 
3. It doesn't matter if a new draft era begins,  because it's already 1e18. So, in this flow ists still 1e18
4. Now after the successful rebalance and seizing RSR, which led to the pumping stake rate from 0.95e18 into 1e18 (5% pump), the user calls unstake the whole amount A in the same block, and a new draft is pushed for him.
5. If the stake rate is high, then more rsrAmounts can be withdrawn after unstaking 
6. Hence complete the transaction so unstaking at the same block will have to wait till `availableAt` to call withdraw, completing a 5% gain without being subject to staking risk.

[stRSR::seizeRSR](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-reserve/blob/3f133997e186465f4904553b0f8e86ecb7bbacbf/contracts/p1/StRSR.sol#L424) && [stRSR::unstake](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-reserve/blob/3f133997e186465f4904553b0f8e86ecb7bbacbf/contracts/p1/StRSR.sol#L259-L281) && [stRSR::pushDraft](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-reserve/blob/3f133997e186465f4904553b0f8e86ecb7bbacbf/contracts/p1/StRSR.sol#L640-L664) && [stRSR::withdraw](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-reserve/blob/3f133997e186465f4904553b0f8e86ecb7bbacbf/contracts/p1/StRSR.sol#L304-L342)

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

A simple recommendation is to not allow staking when its time to rebalance, so that the opportunity of staking at staking rate 0.95e18 is not possible.
