# Vulnerability details

vulnerability details

Total Weight: Represents the cumulative weight of all assets in the basket
It’s used to determine how much weight is actually achieved with the assets present in the basket.

Good Weights: This is the total weight of assets that are considered good (valid and non-zero) 
 
according to the docs and comments During the basket selection process, if the total weight is less than good weights (required target weight) backup weights are considered. however due to a vulnerability when the total weights is less than good
weights in the backup weights wont be considered


The condition totalWeights[i].lte(goodWeights[i]) checks whether the total weight for the target is less than or equal to the weight provided by good collateral. If this is true, it means that the weight requirement for the target is already satisfied or exceeded by the prime collateral which is wrong

impact

When the total weights do not meet the good weights, backup weights will not be added to ensure that the total weight meets the target weight needed. This can lead to issues for the protocol.


mitigation

if the total weights are less than good weights ensure that backup weights are considered
