# Vulnerability details

## Impact

Dust of stETH could be lost in the router which could lead to unexpected behaviors in case they accumulate over time.

## Proof of concept

After adjusting or closing a CDP, the contract is supposed to sweep the potential remaining eBTC and stETH in the router so it does not hold any asset using the `_sweepStEth()` and `_sweepEbtc()` internal functions.

In both the internal functions `_closeCdpOperation()` and `_adjustCdpOperation()` it is stated that "sweepStEth happens outside of this call" however it is not case.

Indeed, after the CDP has been adjusted or closed, only the amount of stETH received by the operation is transferred to the user.

[https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-badger/blob/main/ebtc-zap-router/src/EbtcLeverageZapRouter.sol#L459-L466](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-badger/blob/main/ebtc-zap-router/src/EbtcLeverageZapRouter.sol#L459-L466)

```solidity
if (_zapStEthBalanceDiff > 0) {
    _transferStEthToCaller(
        _cdpId,
        EthVariantZapOperationType.AdjustCdp,
        _params.useWstETHForDecrease,
        _zapStEthBalanceDiff
    );
}
```

*where `_zapStEthBalanceDiff` is the difference between the amount of stETH the router had before and after the operation*

This does not represent a risk right away but accumulating stETH without being swept could lead to inconsistencies in the protocol and produce unexpected behaviors.

---

In order to demonstrate the issue, we can modify the `test_ZapCloseCdp_WithStEth_LowLeverage` test file like such

## Tools used

Manual analysis

## Recommended mitigation steps

Add a call to `_sweepStEth()` at the end of the `_closeCdpOperation()` and `_adjustCdpOperation()` functions
