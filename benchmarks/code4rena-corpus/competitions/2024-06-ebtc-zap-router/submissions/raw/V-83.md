# Vulnerability details

## vulenrbaility details 
is the use of the low-level call to transfer ETH without verifying if the call succeeded is make a bug that The call function, when used without checks, it's can lead to silent failures, where the intended operation depositing ETH to receive stETH does not occur, but no error is thrown to indicate the issue.
```solidity
payable(address(stEth)).call{value: _initialETH}("");
```
An attacker or user could exploit this bug by calling the function with conditions that cause the call to fail as exceeding gas limits. 
The transaction would proceed without performing the intended ETH transfer, potentially leading to inconsistencies in the system.
## Impact
the silent failure of the ETH transfer can lead to Incorrect accounting of assets, where the expected amount of stETH is not received.

need a check that the call's success, and reverting on failure.
