# Vulnerability details

## Impact

The absence of slippage protection in trading operations exposes users to significant financial risks. Without proper checks on the minimum output of trades, users are vulnerable to front-running and sandwich attacks, potentially resulting in substantial losses. This vulnerability affects critical functions such as opening, closing, and adjusting CDPs, which are core operations of the protocol.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
1. Implement slippage protection by adding a `minReceived` parameter to functions that involve trading operations.
2. In the internal trading functions, add checks to ensure the received amount meets or exceeds the `minReceived` value.
3. If the received amount is less than `minReceived`, revert the transaction.
4. Update the `TradeData` struct to include slippage parameters if necessary.
