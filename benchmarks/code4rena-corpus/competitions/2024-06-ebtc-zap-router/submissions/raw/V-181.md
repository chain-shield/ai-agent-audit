# Vulnerability details

## Impact
`_doSwapChecks` will revert when there is sufficient balance due to requiring an amount greater than `expectedMinOut`, specifically in the case that they are equal.

## Proof of Concept
<https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-badger/blob/9173558ee1ac8a78a7ae0a39b97b50ff0dd9e0f8/ebtc-protocol/packages/contracts/contracts/LeverageMacroBase.sol#L490-L494>
```solidity
require(
    IERC20(swapChecks[i].tokenToCheck).balanceOf(address(this)) >
        swapChecks[i].expectedMinOut,
    "LeverageMacroReference: swap check failure!"
);
```

## Tools Used
Manual review

Change `>` to `>=`
