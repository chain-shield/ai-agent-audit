# Vulnerability details

## Impact
It fails to effectively ensure that the actual collateral share is greater than or equal to the expected minimum collateral share. This could lead users to open positions that are on the verge of liquidation, resulting in the user being liquidated (which is not the user’s intention, but merely a consequence of the parameter check not functioning as intended).

It can be seen that since Operator.gte is used in the code, the requirement is for the expected value: stETH.getSharesByPooledEth(_cdp.stETHToDeposit) * _collValidationBuffer / BPS to be greater than or equal to the actual collateral amount in the position cdpInfo.coll.

However, what we actually need to ensure is that the actual collateral amount in the position cdpInfo.coll is greater than or equal to the expected collateral amount.
