# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The function `LeverageZapRouterBase::_getSwapOperations` is designed to handle swap operations for either opening a new CDP or closing an existing one. Currently, it includes a safeguard called `expectedMinOut` to protect users from receiving less than the minimum expected amount of output tokens (`tokenOut`). However, it does not explicitly check or limit the maximum amount of input tokens (`tokenIn`) that a user can deposit, especially during sudden price increases.

To ensure comprehensive protection against slippage, the function should be updated to include checks for both the maximum acceptable input amount (`tokenIn`) and the minimum acceptable output amount (`tokenOut`). Without these checks, users could end up paying more than necessary if prices suddenly rise, whether due to natural market shifts or artificial manipulation.


## Proof of Concept
Below is the potential scenario how an user got affected with this issue:

A user wants to swap a large amount of stEth for eBTC. Assume the current exchange rate is 1 stETH = 0.0667 eBTC (or 15 stETH = 1 eBTC).
1. User (A) submits their transaction to swap 1500 stETH for 100 eBTC.
2. This transaction is broadcasted to the Ethereum network's mempool, where a malicious node operator (NP) see A's transaction.
3. NP submits his own transaction with a higher gas fee, selling a large amount of stETH for eBTC to drive down the price of stETH. Resulting NP's transaction mined first and decreasing the price of stETH.
4. A's transaction gets processed next, but because the price of stETH is lower, they end up paying more to receive 100 eBTC instead of the originally expected 1500 stETH
5. Meanwhile, NP profits by selling at a high price and buying at a low price, while A experiences a loss in this transaction.

Manual Audit

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Adding a new paramter `uint256 maxInputAmount;` in `TradeData` struct and enforcing the maximum input amount logic in `_getSwapOperations` and `_getSwapChecks ` functions would help to mitigate this issue.
