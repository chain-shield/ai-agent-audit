# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Users who opens a position with leverage router with some self-provided collaterals may not be able to fully get his collaterals back when closing a position.

## Proof of Concept
The leverage router offers a way for users to create a leveraged position when they don't necessarily have the assets to do so, with the help of flashloans. The logic is quite simple. When creating a position, users can provide some margin from his own pocket, and combined with flashloan, to open a higher position in the protocol. With the debt coming back to users, the router will try to swap the eBTC to stETH to payback flashloans, and if there are some excessive stETH left, it will be transferred back to users as profit.

The logic for closing a position is quite similar, but the opposite routine, which the router first borrows eBTC from flashloan,payback the debt and get collateralized stETH, to swap for eBTC and payback flashloan.

However, the `_closeCDP` and `_adjustCDP` functions don't provide an option for users to pay debt back with their own eBTC assets. They can only choose to use flashloan to payback debt.

Suppose Bob wants to create a leveraged position, he has 1e18 stETH to offer, and wants to borrow 3e18 stETH from flashloan, and in total the 4e18 stETH as collateral would grant him, say, for example, 2e18 eBTC as debt. Bob doesn't want to swap all his debt eBTCs back to stETH, he wants to keep a quart of those, which is 0.5e18 eBTC for his own, so he swaps 1.5eBTC for 3e18 stETH. This makes Bob to own 0.5e18 eBTC, at the cost of his own 1e18 stETH. After a while, Bob wants to adjust his position a bit, he wants to payback 0.2e18 out of 0.5e18 eBTC of his total eBTC assets, to get some stETH back. Only for him to realize that there is no option for him to payback debt with his own eBTC assets. His only option to adjust or close the position is through flashloan. For sure, Bob could have just swapped the eBTC to stETH somewhere else, but the leverage router should have such feature for users to adjust or close position with their own assets. 

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Offer an option for users to adjust or close positions with their own assets.
