# Vulnerability details

## Issue
When performing token swaps or conversions, it's crucial to implement slippage protection to guard against unexpected price movements and front-running attacks. This is typically done by specifying a minimum amount of tokens to receive from the conversion. Without such protection, users may receive significantly fewer tokens than expected if the market price shifts unfavorably during the transaction's execution.

## Vulnerability Details
In the ZapRouterBase contract, there are several functions that convert between different forms of ETH-based collateral (ETH, WETH, stETH, wstETH). However, these conversion functions lack checks for minimum received amounts, which could lead to potential losses for users due to slippage or unfavorable price movements.

## Affected functions:
1. _convertWrappedEthToStETH
2. _convertRawEthToStETH
3. _convertWstEthToStETH

These functions perform token conversions without any slippage protection mechanisms. They calculate the received amount after the conversion but do not compare it against any minimum expected value.

For example, in _convertWrappedEthToStETH:

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-badger/blob/main/ebtc-zap-router/src/ZapRouterBase.sol#L43

```solidity
uint256 _wETHReiceived = wrappedEth.balanceOf(address(this)) - _wETHBalBefore;
uint256 _rawETHBalBefore = address(this).balance;
IWrappedETH(address(wrappedEth)).withdraw(_wETHReiceived);
uint256 _rawETHConverted = address(this).balance - _rawETHBalBefore;
return _depositRawEthIntoLido(_rawETHConverted);
```

There's no check to ensure that the final amount of stETH received meets a minimum threshold.

## Impact
Without minimum received amount checks, users are exposed to the following risks:

1. Unexpected slippage: If the exchange rate between the token pairs changes unfavorably during the transaction's execution, users may receive significantly fewer tokens than anticipated.

2. Front-running attacks: Malicious actors could exploit this vulnerability by front-running user transactions, manipulating the exchange rate, and causing users to receive fewer tokens.

3. Financial losses: In extreme cases, users could experience substantial financial losses due to receiving far fewer tokens than expected from their conversions.

## Mitigation

Implement slippage protection by adding minimum received amount parameters to these conversion functions and validating the actual received amounts against these minimums.
