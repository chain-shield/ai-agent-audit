# Vulnerability details


## Proof of Concept
When a CDP is opened, the contract checks that the initial collateral deposited meets the minimum requirement of 2 stETH. This is done through the [_requireAtLeastMinNetStEthBalance](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-badger/blob/9173558ee1ac8a78a7ae0a39b97b50ff0dd9e0f8/ebtc-zap-router/src/EbtcLeverageZapRouter.sol#L217) function, which compares the total `stETH` deposit amount minus a predefined reward (`LIQUIDATOR_REWARD`) against a minimum threshold. However, when adjusting a CDP, the contract does not perform a similar check to ensure that the remaining collateral after the adjustment still meets this minimum requirement.

The [adjustCdp](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-badger/blob/9173558ee1ac8a78a7ae0a39b97b50ff0dd9e0f8/ebtc-zap-router/src/EbtcLeverageZapRouter.sol#L390) functions allow for various operations that might change both the collateral and debt of a CDP. These operations include increasing or decreasing the collateral, changing the debt, and possibly withdrawing some of the collateral as margin. Since there is no check to ensure that the remaining collateral after such adjustments is at least 2 stETH, a user could theoretically reduce their collateral below this threshold through these adjustments. This vulnerability could lead to scenarios where the collateral is insufficient to cover the debt, potentially leading to liquidation events.
### Example Scenario
Consider a scenario where a user opens a CDP with $`4 stETH`$ as collateral, meeting the minimum requirement. Later, the user decides to adjust their CDP by reducing their collateral to $`1.5 stETH`$. Without the enforcement of the minimum collateral requirement during adjustments, the contract would not prevent this action, even though it results in a violation of the minimum collateral rule.
## Impact
Users can reduce their collateral below the required threshold. This could lead to a situation where the collateral is insufficient to cover the debt, posing a significant risk to the stability and security of the CDP mechanism.
## Tools Used
Manual
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
The contract should implement checks in the `adjustCdp` functions to ensure that the remaining collateral after an adjustment is at least equal to the minimum required collateral. This can be achieved by recalculating the total collateral after the adjustment and comparing it against the minimum requirement. If the new total is below the threshold, the transaction should revert with an appropriate error message.
