# Vulnerability details

## Impact

`EbtcLeverageZapRouter` is missing the ability to both perform debt decrease and collateral increase, which will prevent users from having granular control over their CDP positions.

## Proof of Concept

Due to the limitation of `EbtcLeverageZapRouter` to perform only `stETH` or `eBTC` flash loans, not both at the same time, users have no native way to both decrease their debt (burn `eBTC`) and increase their collateral (add `stETH`). There can be scenarios when `EbtcZapRouter` is not enough to perform these 2 actions in unison. 

Consider the example of `stETH` price crash and the system has entered recovery mode. Alice has CDP with 1 `eBTC`, 21 `stETH`, and her CR has fallen below 103%. now she has 3 options:

1. `Decrease her debt`
    1. Pros
        1. Improved CR, avoiding liquidation
    2. Cons
        1. Receive collateral tokens at a very low price
2. `Increase her collateral`
    1. Pros
        1. Improved CR, avoiding liquidation
    2. Cons 
        1. Provide all the needed collateral from herself, since no eBTC can be borrowed against it, or simply has to add a lot of `stETH`, to get `eBTC` which will be used to repay the flash loan, but this will make her pay higher rebase fee due to the increased CDP stake.
3. `Decrease her debt + increase her collateral` **atomically with flash loans** through `EbtcLeverageZapRouter`
    1. Pros
        1. in a single transaction she will both be able to burn less `eBTC` and use the flash loan functionality to minimize the `stETH` margin that she will have to provide, saving her CDP position from liquidation with minimum costs.

However since a flash loan can be performed with only one of the 2 tokens, option 3 is not possible in the leverage contract.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Consider extending the leverage router to support both operations atomically, this will provide more granular control to the users, also lowering the barrier of protecting their positions from liquidation.
