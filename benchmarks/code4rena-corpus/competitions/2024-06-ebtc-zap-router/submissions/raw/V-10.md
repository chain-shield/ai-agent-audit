# Vulnerability details

## Impact
It is possible that `_zapStEthBalanceBefore` will be greater than `_zapStEthBalanceAfter` when `_closeCdp()` is called. This can occur if the operations performed during the closure of the CDP result in a net decrease of the stETH balance of the contract. For instance, if stETH is used to pay off debts or fees during the closure process, this could reduce the stETH balance held by the contract, making `_zapStEthBalanceAfter` less than `_zapStEthBalanceBefore`.

In such a scenario `_stETHDiff` can be negative, so a check is needed before calling `_transferStEthToCaller`.
 

## Proof of Concept
```
uint256 _zapStEthBalanceBefore = stEth.balanceOf(address(this));
_closeCdpOperation({ _cdpId: _cdpId, _debt: debt, _tradeData: _tradeData });
uint256 _zapStEthBalanceAfter = stEth.balanceOf(address(this));
```
`_closeCdpOperation` could involve transactions that decrease the stETH balance, such as repaying borrowed amounts or associated transaction fees that might be paid in stETH.
