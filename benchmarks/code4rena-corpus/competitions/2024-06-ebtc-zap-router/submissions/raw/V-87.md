However a look at the implementation of _openCdpOperation(...) function in the LeverageZapRouterBase contract as pointed out above shows that caller is allowed to completely sweep contract StEth Balance as noted in the _sweepStEth() function provided below from the same contract. The complete share balance of contract is transferred to msg.sender. The problem is at no point throughout the chain of this function calls all the way from openCdpWithEth(...) down to _sweepStEth() was there any restriction to caller, a bad actor simply needs to monitor the contract balance and steal the protocol fund in the contract

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Protocol should ensure a validation to make sure unauthorized callers cant sweep away fund from contract balance by preventing any possible calls of this sensitive functions.
