# Vulnerability details

## Impact

### 1- Loss of Funds:
Depositing assets to the zero address results in a permanent loss of these funds, as they become irrecoverable. 

### 2- Operational Disruption:
The contract may not function as intended if funds are misdirected to the zero address, leading to failures in subsequent operations that rely on the deposited assets.

### 3- Reputation Damage:
If users lose funds due to this vulnerability, it can damage the reputation of the smart contract developers and the platform it operates on, reducing trust among users and investors.

## Proof of Concept

To illustrate the vulnerability, consider the following scenario:

A user accidentally or maliciously calls the deposit function with to set to address(0).
The function does not revert, and the assets are deposited to the zero address.
These assets are now lost permanently, as the zero address cannot be accessed or recovered.
