# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The current implementation allows a malicious operator to exploit the staking and redemption mechanisms. By staking a significant portion of their own vault (e.g., 50%) and then initiating the `startRedeem` process. Suppose after day 8 a DSS wants to slash about 80% of the vault but will not be able to do that because of the `SLASHING_VETO_WINDOW`. Till the veto period ends the operator can withdraw its stake and system will not be able to slash 80% but will be able to slash only 50% tokens.
This loophole allows the operator to withdraw funds before the slashing can be executed, effectively reducing the penalty. Add a mechanism where if the slashing request is in pending then don't allow to withdraw from that vault until slashing happens or Veto period finishes.

1. Malicious Operator Stake: The operator stakes a significant amount in their own vault, say 50%.
2. Initiate Redemption: The operator calls `startRedeem()` to begin the redemption process.
3. Malicious Activity Found: After 8 days, the operator found in malicious activity.
4. DSS Attempts Slashing: The DSS identifies the malicious behavior and initiates a slashing request for 70%.
5. Redemption Completion: However, due to the SLASHING_VETO_WINDOW, the DSS cannot execute the slashing immediately.
6. Withdrawal of Funds: The operator completes the redemption calling `finishRedeem` and withdraws their staked amount, leaving insufficient funds for the full slashing amount.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Block Withdrawals During Slashing Pending: Implement a mechanism that blocks withdrawals from the vault when a slashing request is pending. This would ensure that operators cannot withdraw funds once malicious activity is detected, allowing the DSS to execute the required slashing.
