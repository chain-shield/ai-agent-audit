# Vulnerability details

## Details
- The finalize slashing involves slashing of a certain number of vaults of the operator by the DSS.
- The `SlasherLib::finalizeSlashing` involves calling the `slashAssets` for every vault which in turn calls the `handleSlashing` function on the `SlashingHandler`.
- The vulnerability occurs when for a vault for which the slashing amount becomes `0` will lead to DoS, thus failing the whole txn and DSS would not be able to perform the slashing, while the operator would be able to withdraw all their stakes as time would be passed till then.


## Proof of Concept
The reason behind slashing amount becoming 0 can be due to the following cases:

1. Case I
- The malicious operator has their own malicious DSS (having max slashable percentage set to 100%) which either front-runs the genuine DSS slashing request or is active enough to perform their own slashing before in time for their vault and performs it only for a single vault that they can sacrifice and is also contained in the genuine DSS slashing.
- As well as the malicious operator also places the vault unstake request before the above step.
- Then, the malicious operator would be able to finalize their own slashing request first as it was placed before in time, leading to amount to be reduced to 0 on a vault, and then when the genuine DSS tries to finalize their slashing request it would revert their whole txn as for a vault the amount was reduced to 0 (sacrificed by the operator to be able to unstake their other vaults).
- Finally the operator unstakes all of their vaults.

&nbsp;  

2. Case II
- The other case would be that the operator have leveraged their vault to multiple DSS, and this time consider the malicious DSS `B` to be a genuine DSS with same max slashing percentage set to 100%.
- Consider the case that they have stakes multiple vaults to DSS A, and single vault to DSS B, and that single vault is also in DSS A, and now both DSSs detects the malicious behavior of operator and they passes all the respective staked vault for slashing, and consider that the DSS B's finalize slashing happens before DSS A, resulting in failure of DSS A slashing due to a revert due to `0` amount of a particular vault.
- And the DSS would be able to unstake their vaults (note: they placed their unstake request as soon as they saw the slashing request).


## Impact
DSS would face a DoS due to the revert in `handleSlashing` for the amount being 0 for the case discussed in this finding and thus would not be able to slash the operator, thus giving the operator the opportunity to unstake their vaults.

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Remove the revert condition in `SlashingHandler::handleSlashing` where it reverts for the case when amount is 0.
