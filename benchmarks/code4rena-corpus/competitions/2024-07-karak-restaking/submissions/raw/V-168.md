# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The unregisterOperatorFromDSS function in the Core contract allows an operator to unregister from a Delegated Staking Service (DSS). However, the current implementation does not check whether the operator has any remaining delegated assets before allowing the unregistration. This oversight can lead to potential inconsistencies and security issues within the system, especially given the critical role operators play in managing stakers' assets who can lose an asset if the operator who manages the asset is unregistered.
