# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In `Vault` contract, the redeem process is split into 2 steps.In steps, user call `startRedeem()` to start a redeem process and transfer their shares to the vault.https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-karak/blob/f5e52fdcb4c20c4318d532a9f08f7876e9afb321/src/Vault.sol#L146
However, to get assets, they have to wait a certain period of time (`Constants.MIN_WITHDRAWAL_DELAY = SLASHING_WINDOW + SLASHING_VETO_WINDOW = 9 days`) then call `finishRedeem()`, however in `finishRedeem()` it has the following check: `if (shares > maxRedeem(address(this))) revert RedeemMoreThanMax();`
9 days is not exactly a short time, anything could happen during this period, like the project get hacked which is the worst.
Anyway if `maxRedeem(address(this))` < `shares`, then the user can never redeem again because his shares already transfer to the contract in step 1.
