# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Operators need to specify the address of the `SlashStore` which will receive any funds that are slashed from Native Vaults. The issue is that as operators are not trusted (they are not included in the `All trusted roles in the protocol` section of the contest page) there is a high danger of malicious operators setting the slash store to an address they control in order to be able to steal all assets that are slashed from their vaults.

## Proof of Concept
When Native Vaults are deployed in `Core.sol` the caller/operator specifies the address of the `SlashStore` in the `extraData` field. Currently, there is no validation on what that address could be, allowing malicious operators to specify a contract that they have full control over. Thus, when the DSS slashes their Native Vaults they will retain possession of the slashed funds.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Perhaps, making validations that the `SlashStore` address, specified in the `extraData` field, is a secure contract would be a sufficient fix. This can be achieved by implementing a whitelist of secure `SlashStore` addresses.
