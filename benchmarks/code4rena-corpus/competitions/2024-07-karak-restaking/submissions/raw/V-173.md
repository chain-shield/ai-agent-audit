# Vulnerability details

## Impact
1. The slashed amount can be less than intended amount, the amount can be less more in case of a substantial increase in assets.

2. Meaning, a vault operator can maliciously deposit ( asked validators to) in order to reduced the amount substantially and ask them to withdraw queued their deposits after the actual slashing ( ETH out of node) happened.

## Proof of Concept
When a vault is slashed, the actual slashed ( ETH out of node) can be more or **less** than what was actually slashed, this  is because the `totalAssets()` used during the calculations of the slashing amount can change:
```solidity
for (uint256 i = 0; i < slashingMetadata.vaults.length; ++i) {
            earmarkedStakes[i] = Math.mulDiv(
                slashingMetadata.slashPercentagesWad[i],
                IKarakBaseVault(slashingMetadata.vaults[i]).totalAssets(),
                Constants.MAX_SLASHING_PERCENT_WAD
            );
        }
```

lets say the `slashingPercentagesWas` is `10e18` and the `totalAssets()` at the time is `100e18` 

The slashable amount should be:
```solidity 
10e18 * 100e18 / 100e18 which is 10000000000000000000
```

meaning, the vault should be slashed `10e18`

but in a situation where the vault assets has  increase to lets say `2000e18` due to new `nodeOwners` or `validators` pointing to the node addresses, the amount to be slashed after calling [startSnapShort](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-karak/blob/f5e52fdcb4c20c4318d532a9f08f7876e9afb321/src/NativeVault.sol#L456) by  a `nodeOwner`  who has a share of `1000e18` would be be:
```solidity
1000e18 * 1990e18 + 1 / 2000e18 + 1 = 995002498750624687656
```

meaning the vault would be slashed 1000e18 - 995002498750624687656= `4997501249375312344`


this is not actually the intended slashed amount, it is supposed to be 10e18

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

By removing the slashed amount immediately during the slashing will mitigate this issue, removing the risk of increasing of the assets , thus, preventing the values to fluctuate.
