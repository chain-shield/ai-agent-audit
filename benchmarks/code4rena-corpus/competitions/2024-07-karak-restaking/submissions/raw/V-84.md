# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The `Vault` inherits solady's `ERC4626` implementation, which is not compatible with fee-on-transfer tokens.

`Vault`'s `deposit()` and `mint()` functions do not work well with fee-on-transfer tokens as the `amount` variable is the pre-fee amount, including the fee, whereas the `totalAssets` do not include the fee anymore.

This can be abused to mint more shares than desired:

A deposit(1000) should result in the same shares as two deposits of deposit(500) but it does not because amount is the pre-fee amount. Assume a fee-on-transfer of 20%, and ignore the virtual share in ERC4626 for simplicity. Assume current totalAmount = 1000, totalShares = 1000.

- `deposit(1000) = 1000 / totalAmount * totalShares = 1000 shares`
- `deposit(500) = 500 / totalAmount * totalShares = 500 shares`. Now the totalShares increased by 500 but the totalAssets only increased by (100% - 20%) * 500 = 400. Therefore, the second deposit(500) = 500 / (totalAmount + 400) * (newTotalShares) = 500 / (1400) * 1500 = 535.714285714 shares.

In total, the two deposits lead to 35 more shares than a single deposit of the sum of the deposits.

Mannual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
`amount` should be the amount excluding the fee. Consider override `previewDeposit` and `previewMint` to support fee-on-transfer tokens.
