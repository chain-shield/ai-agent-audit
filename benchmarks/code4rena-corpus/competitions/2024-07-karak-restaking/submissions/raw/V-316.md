# Vulnerability details

## Vulnerability Detail
In Vault.sol,  The Inflation problem faced traditionally in ERC4626 follows here as well. 
The Target transaction for a deposit can be front-run by an attacker who mints 1 share by depositing 1 asset, and then transfers greater asset amount than the target's deposit. 

This allows the victim to get 0 shares. While redeeming can take days, the damage is done where the victim gets 0 shares despite depositing a large amount and the attacker can potentially walk away with most of the profits,.

This can be amplified by the intermediate depositors who deposit in oblivion of the situation during the redeeming interval, if they fail to make use of the `deposit` which has `minSharesOut`, everyone else who uses deposit walks away with 0 shares.

### Example

Alice deposits 1 asset to get 1 share.
totalAssets in contract ~ 1
totalSupply in contract ~ 1

Alice see's Bob's transaction of 1000 and front-runs it with a transfer to the vault contract of around ~ 10000

totalAssets in contract ~ 10001
totalSupply in contract ~ 1

Bob deposits the 1000 but he gets no shares as the formula to compute the shares he's owed is 
~ deposited asset * ( totalSupply/totalAssets)
~ 1000 * 1 / 10000
~ 0

## Impact
Most depositors funds are lost and the attacker likely walks out with the profit.
