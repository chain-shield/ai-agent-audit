# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Although the protocol uses ERC4626 from Solady, which makes the first deposit attack not profitable, it leaves room for a griefing attack.

Since Solady's ERC4626 implementation adds an extra `one` to `totalSupply()` in `convertToAssets`, the first depositor attack will lose half of the `donate` token amount. However, the attacker can gain 25% or more, and create `dead` assets, leading to inaccurate calculations in any other share calculations.

If all shares are withdrawn, these `dead` assets will create a gap for the first depositor in the vault.

Attack flow:

- Attacker sees in the mempool a transaction from Alice for 500 tokens.
- Attacker deposits 1 wei and donates 1000 tokens (2x Alice's deposit).
- Alice will get 0 shares and lose her funds.
- Bob deposits 1000 tokens but receives shares equivalent to only 833.33 tokens due to a calculation error.
- An attacker withdraws 1 share and gets back about 833 tokens (~83% of the deposited amount, more deposits will increase this amount).
- During the `MIN_WITHDRAWAL_DELAY` period, Bob deposits his funds.
    - Due to the `dead` assets, Bob loses some amount of assets.
    - `Dead` assets will increase over time (see PoC: after the attacker withdraws his one share - 750 `dead` assets left, and after Bob's deposit, the `dead` assets increase).
- After the `MIN_WITHDRAWAL_DELAY` period, there will be no deposits (Bob won't deposit).
    - Now the vault has `totalSupply() == 0` and about 750 `dead` tokens in `totalAssets()`.
    - If any other user deposits fewer tokens than this `dead` amount:
        - The user will lose their funds.
        - This gap will increase.

With minimum effort and monetAttacker can infect any newly created Vault.
Which will lead to the user's funds being lost.

        console2.log("--- Initial state ---");

        console2.log("Vault total supply:", vault.totalSupply());

        console2.log("\n--- Final state ---");
        console2.log("Dead assets:", vault.totalAssets() - vault.convertToAssets(vault.totalSupply()));
    }
