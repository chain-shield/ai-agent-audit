# Vulnerability details

## Impact
`_disableInitializer()`, `initializer()` and other important components won't work as expected. 
This will lead to loss of protection on the contract.

## Proof of Concept
in `NativeVault` we can see that the contract imports `initializable`

`import {Initializable} from "@openzeppelin-upgradeable/proxy/utils/Initializable.sol";`

but moving forward we will see that the contract doesn't inherit the imported contract.

`contract NativeVault is ERC4626, IBeacon, Pauser, INativeVault, OwnableRoles, ReentrancyGuard {...}`

but it uses [modifiers](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-karak/blob/f5e52fdcb4c20c4318d532a9f08f7876e9afb321/src/NativeVault.sol#L53) and [functions](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-karak/blob/f5e52fdcb4c20c4318d532a9f08f7876e9afb321/src/NativeVault.sol#L36) from `initializable`.
