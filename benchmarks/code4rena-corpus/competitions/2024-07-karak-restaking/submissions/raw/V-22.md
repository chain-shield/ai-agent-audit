# Vulnerability details

The vulnerability stems from a timing issue in the slashing mechanism, where the amount to be slashed is calculated at the time of request but not updated at the time of finalization. This can lead to inconsistencies in the slashed amounts due to changes in the vault's total assets during the delay period.

### Vulnerable Process Flow

1. **Slashing Request:**
   - `earmarkedStakes` are calculated based on the current `totalAssets()` of each vault.
   - These values are stored in the `QueuedSlashing` struct.

2. **Time Delay:**
   - A minimum delay of `Constants.SLASHING_VETO_WINDOW` exists between request and finalization.
   - During this period, vault assets can change due to:
     - Deposits (`deposit` function)
     - Mints (`mint` function)
     - Withdrawals (`startRedeem` and `finishRedeem` functions)

3. **Slashing Finalization:**
   - The pre-calculated `earmarkedStakes` are used without rechecking the current `totalAssets()`.

### Potential Impacts

1. **If total assets increase:**
   - The slashed amount will be less than intended as a percentage of current assets.
   - This could lead to under-punishing of malicious actors.

2. **If total assets decrease:**
   - The slashed amount could be a higher percentage than initially intended.
   - While `Math.min()` in `slashAssets` prevents slashing more than available, it could still result in over-punishing.

### Root Cause

The core issue is that the slashed amount doesn't accurately reflect the intended percentage of the vault's assets at the time of slashing execution. This discrepancy arises from the time delay between calculation and execution, during which the vault's total assets can change.

### Recommendation

To address this vulnerability, consider implementing one of the following solutions:

1. Recalculate the slash amount during finalization based on:
   - The current `totalAssets()`
   - The originally requested slash percentage

2. Implement a mechanism to freeze deposits and withdrawals for the affected vault once a slash is requested, until it's either finalized or cancelled.

These changes would ensure that the slashed amount accurately reflects the intended percentage of the current vault assets.
