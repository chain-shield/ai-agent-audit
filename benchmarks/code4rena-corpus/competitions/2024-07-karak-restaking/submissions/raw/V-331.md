# Vulnerability details

## Impact
As mentioned in the README, the Karak protocol will work with all possible ERC20 tokens. 
Some tokens (e.g. stETH) revert when attempting to transfer to `address(0)`.

`SlashingHandler` performs slashing by transferring tokens from `msg.sender` to `SlashingHandler` and then to the `address(0)`. This approach fails for tokens that revert on transfer to `address(0)`.
## Proof of Concept
https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-karak/blob/f5e52fdcb4c20c4318d532a9f08f7876e9afb321/src/SlashingHandler.sol#L58

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Ensure changing the burn address to `address(1)` or other when creating a `SlashingHandler` wich will work with tokens that revert on transfer to `address(0)`
