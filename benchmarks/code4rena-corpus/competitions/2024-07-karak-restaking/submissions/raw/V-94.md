# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Someone could be waiting in the native vault's withdrawal queue to front-run a slashing event.

## Proof of Concept
There is a comment on the finishRedeem method in Vault.sol that states that the DSS should not consider stakes that are queued for withdrawal https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-karak/blob/f5e52fdcb4c20c4318d532a9f08f7876e9afb321/src/Vault.sol#L154
However there are no such checks in the slash method of the Native Vault.

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Consider checking the amounts of the pending withdrawals before slashing the assets
