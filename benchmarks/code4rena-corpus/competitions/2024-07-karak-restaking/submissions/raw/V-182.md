# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Initializer won't work as expected.

## Proof of Concept
from the [contract declaration](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-karak/blob/f5e52fdcb4c20c4318d532a9f08f7876e9afb321/src/NativeNode.sol#L17) we will observe that the contract does not inherit openzeppelin's initializable but  uses the initializer modifier from openzeppelin as seen below.

`    function initialize(address _owner, address _nodeOwner) external initializer {...}`

## Tools Used
Manual Review(VsCode)

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
inherit `initializable` from openzeppelin
