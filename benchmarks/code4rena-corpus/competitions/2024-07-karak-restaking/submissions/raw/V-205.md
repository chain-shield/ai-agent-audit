# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Validators are able to completely avoid DSS slash events by withdrawing their entire stake on the Beacon Chain right before the slashing occurs, causing a loss of funds for the protocol.

## Proof of Concept
Here, I will explain why the attack is possible and how it can be performed:
For simplicity, we will assume that the validator, escaping the slash event, has a `NativeNode` with a balance of 0 and a stake on the Beacon Chain of 32 ETH, therefore their `snapshot.nodeBalanceWei = 0` and `ownerToNode[validator].totalRestakedETH = 32 ETH`.

The validator notices that a slash event will occur soon, the slashing is intended to reduce the `totalAssets` by 50%. In order to not suffer any losses the malicious validator calls `startSnapshot`, if needed, and after that exits their position on the Beacon Chain, withdrawing the 32 ETH of stake to their native node.

The withdrawn 32 ETH is yet to be recorded and `snapshot.nodeBalanceWei` is still 0. Now the validator makes a call to `validateSnapshotProofs`. As the validator has exited their stake, `balanceDeltaWei` will be -32 ETH and `_updateSnapshot` will burn the entirety of the validator's shares, also reducing their `totalRestakedETH` to 0. Therefore, the validator currently has a node with `snapshot.nodeBalanceWei = 0`, while in reality there are 32 ETH in the native node contract.

Now after the slashing occurs and `totalAssets` are reduced, the validator calls `startSnapshot`, which does not transfer any funds to the slash store as `totalRestakedETH` would still be 0. Furthermore, the function increases their `snapshot.nodeBalanceWei` to 32 ETH, allowing them to withdraw their funds without any harm from the slash events.

It is important to note that even if the DSS notices this malicious behaviour and performs another slash, other validators will be harmed and lose funds from the actions of the attacker.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
In `validateSnapshotProofs` update appropriately the validator's node `snapshot.nodeBalanceWei` if funds have been withdrawn from the Beacon Chain to the native node.
