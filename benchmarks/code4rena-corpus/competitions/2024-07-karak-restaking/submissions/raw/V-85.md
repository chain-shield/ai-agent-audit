## Impact

`SlashingHandler.handleSlashing` transfers tokens to address(0). However, some tokens (e.g. openzeppelin) revert when attempting to transfer to address(0).

As a result, the DSS might be unable to slash a vault with tokens that revert when transferred to the zero address.

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Implement a compatible version of `SlashingHandler` for tokens that revert when transferred to the zero address.
