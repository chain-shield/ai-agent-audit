# Vulnerability details

## Impact
DSS is still registered to the operator even though the interfaces are not implemented in the DSS or the low level call failed.

## Proof of Concept


We are calling `callHookInterfaceImplemented()` to call the DSS.
For functions like `requestUpdateStake` , `registerToDSS` we shuoldnt ignore the failure case when the hook functions are called.

But since we are simply returning false  when the call is failed , invalid DSS can be used to be register and stake.
