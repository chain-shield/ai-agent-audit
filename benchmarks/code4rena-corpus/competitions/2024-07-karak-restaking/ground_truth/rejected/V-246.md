## Brief Summary

The code currently lacks a check for whether a validator has been [slashed](https://ethresear.ch/t/slashing-proofoor-on-chain-slashed-validator-proofs/19421). This omission could allow a slashed validator to be treated as active or valid, potentially leading to incorrect behavior in the system. Specifically, a slashed validator may be erroneously considered for withdrawals or staking operations, which could undermine the protocol's integrity and security.
