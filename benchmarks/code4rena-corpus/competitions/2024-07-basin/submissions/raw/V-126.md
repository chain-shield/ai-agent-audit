# Vulnerability details


## Impact
The `divide-before-multiply` issue involves performing a multiplication on the result of a division operation. This can lead to precision loss and unexpected results in smart contract calculations, particularly in financial contracts where accurate arithmetic operations are crucial. 

The impact is medium because while it may not always result in catastrophic failures, it can cause significant inaccuracies in financial calculations, leading to potential loss or improper distribution of funds.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
1. **Reorder Operations:** Ensure that multiplications are performed before divisions to maintain precision. Review all arithmetic operations in the code to identify and correct any instances where divisions precede multiplications.
2. **Test Thoroughly:** Implement extensive testing and validation, including edge cases and high-value inputs, to verify the accuracy of calculations and catch any potential issues.
