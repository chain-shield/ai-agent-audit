## Recommended Mitigation Steps
To protect the `initializer` functions (`init` and `initNoWellToken`), you can use the `onlyOwner` modifier provided by the `OwnableUpgradeable` contract. This ensures that only the contract owner can call these functions. Additionally, you should use the `initializer` and `reinitializer` modifiers to ensure these functions can only be called once or under specific conditions.
