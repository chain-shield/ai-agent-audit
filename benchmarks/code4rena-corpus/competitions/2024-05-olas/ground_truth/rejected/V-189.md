## Brief Summary

The use of staticcall in smart contracts, particularly when interacting with other contracts, poses a significant security risk. According to the solidity docs, if a staticcall encounters a state change, it burns up all gas and returns. If the called contract contains state-changing operations, it could potentially consume all available gas during the execution of the staticcall, leading to a Denial of Service (DoS) condition.
