# Vulnerability details

The function performs multiple arithmetic operations on totalIncentives which can potentially exceed the uint96 data type's maximum limit, leading to integer overflow. This occurs particularly when calculating rewards based on system parameters that could escalate, such as totalTopUpsOLAS and topUpUnitFraction.

## Impact
An overflow could result in erroneously low rewards being recorded and distributed, undermining the integrity of the incentive system and potentially causing financial discrepancies.

## Proof of Concept
Suppose you have the following typical values in a blockchain context:

- pendingRelativeTopUp = 5000; // High but realistic amount for a staking contract
- totalTopUpsOLAS = 5 * 1e18; // Total staked amount normalized to ether
- topUpUnitFraction = 1e18; // Normalized fraction for top-up calculations
- sumUnitTopUpsOLAS = 10 * 1e18; // Total unit top-ups for normalization
During the execution:

`totalIncentives = (5000 * 5 * 1e18 * 1e18) / (10 * 1e18 * 100);`

Here, multiplication of pendingRelativeTopUp, totalTopUpsOLAS, and topUpUnitfraction could exceed the limits of uint96 if not properly managed or if an unexpected spike occurs due to market behavior or a burst of transactions. The calculation could wrap around, resulting in erroneous, smaller incentive values and potentially affecting reward distributions improperly.
