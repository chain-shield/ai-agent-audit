# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Tokens that have balance changes outside of transfers need to be supported based on https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-olas?tab=readme-ov-file#erc20-token-behaviors-in-scope. When `stakingToken` is such token, its rebasing event can be triggered to make the `StakingToken` contract's balance of such token lower than before. If such rebasing event has been triggered, when the following `_claim` or `unstake` function, which further calls the `_withdraw` function below, is called, the `StakingToken` contract's balance of such token can be lower than the reward amount to be transferred from the `StakingToken` contract to the service owner; for instance, the reward can accumulate to equal `balance` but the `StakingToken` contract's balance of such token can become less than `balance` when such rebasing event has been triggered. In this case, executing `SafeTransferLib.safeTransfer(stakingToken, to, amount)` in the `_withdraw` function reverts, which causes the service owner to be unable to receive her or his deserved reward.

## Proof of Concept
The following steps can occur for the described scenario.
1. `stakingToken` is a token that has balance changes outside of transfers for the `StakingToken` contract.
2. 100e18 `stakingToken` is deposited to the `StakingToken` contract.
	- `balance` and `availableRewards` are increased to 100e18.
3. `stakingToken`'s rebasing event is triggered to decrease the `StakingToken` contract's balance of `stakingToken` to 99.5e18.
4. A service owner unstakes after staking for a long enough time.
	- The reward for such service owner has accumulated to equal 100e18 at this moment.
5. Transferring 100e18 `stakingToken` from the `StakingToken` contract to the service owner reverts since the `StakingToken` contract only owns 99.5e18 `stakingToken`.
	- Thus, the service owner is unable to receive her or his deserved reward of 100e18 `stakingToken`.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Like many other protocols, this protocol can consider to not support tokens that have balance changes outside of transfers. If this protocol wants to support these tokens, it needs to communicate with its users about the shortcomings of these tokens in this protocol, such as these tokens' airdrops can be lost. Alternatively, if `stakingToken` is such token, the `StakingToken` contract can be updated to keep track of `stakingToken`'s underlying asset amount held by it in order to transfer the underlying assets instead of the balances of `stakingToken`.
