# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Users with a staking time less than the minStakingDuration cannot unstake when rewards are insufficient.
## Proof of Concept
In the StakingBase contract, when rewards are insufficient, users can still unstake even if their staking time is less than the minStakingDuration. An attacker can transfer 1 token or 1 wei to the contract, making availableRewards greater than 0 (which costs almost nothing). Consequently, the funds of users whose staking time is less than minStakingDuration will be locked in the contract, and they will only receive minimal returns (essentially almost no returns).
## Tools Used
Manual Audit

Set a deposit minimum
