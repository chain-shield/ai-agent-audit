# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The calculation of `newBias` in the `voteForNomineeWeights` function can lead to incorrect voting weight calculations and distributions, which can have significant implications for the fairness and integrity of the voting system.

**Calculation of `nextTime`:`** [voteForNomineeWeights#L489](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-olas/blob/e2a8bc31d2769bfb578a06cc64919ad369a82c08/governance/contracts/VoteWeighting.sol#L489)
```solidity
uint256 nextTime = (block.timestamp + WEEK) / WEEK * WEEK;
```
This line calculates the next week boundary by adding `WEEK` (a constant representing one week in seconds) to the current block timestamp and then rounding up to the nearest week boundary. This ensures that `nextTime` is always aligned with the week boundary.

**Calculation of `newBias:`**  [voteForNomineeWeights#L520](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-olas/blob/e2a8bc31d2769bfb578a06cc64919ad369a82c08/governance/contracts/VoteWeighting.sol#L520)
```solidity
uint256 newBias = newSlope.slope * (lockEnd - nextTime);
```
The `newBias` is calculated by multiplying the `newSlope.slope` with the difference between `lockEnd` (the end time of the user's veOLAS lock) and `nextTime`. However, `lockEnd` is not necessarily aligned with the week boundary, which can lead to incorrect calculations.

**Impact on `pointsWeight` and `pointsSum` mappings:** [voteForNomineeWeights#L532-L533](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-olas/blob/e2a8bc31d2769bfb578a06cc64919ad369a82c08/governance/contracts/VoteWeighting.sol#L532-L533)
```solidity
pointsWeight[nomineeHash][nextTime].bias = _maxAndSub(_getWeight(account, chainId) + newBias, oldBias);
pointsSum[nextTime].bias = _maxAndSub(_getSum() + newBias, oldBias);
```
The incorrect calculation of `newBias` directly affects the updates to the `pointsWeight` and `pointsSum` mappings, which store the voting weights and the sum of weights for each nominee and the overall system, respectively.

## Proof of Concept
**Incorrect voting weight distribution** If `newBias` is calculated incorrectly, the voting weights assigned to nominees will be inaccurate, leading to an unfair distribution of voting power.
**Skewed voting results** Incorrect voting weight calculations can skew the voting results, potentially favoring certain nominees or causing the overall voting outcome to be inaccurate.

## Tools Used
Vs

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
We should ensure that both `nextTime` and `lockEnd` are aligned to the same time boundary (e.g., week boundary) before calculating `newBias`.
```solidity
uint256 alignedLockEnd = lockEnd / WEEK * WEEK;
uint256 newBias = newSlope.slope * (alignedLockEnd - nextTime);
```
