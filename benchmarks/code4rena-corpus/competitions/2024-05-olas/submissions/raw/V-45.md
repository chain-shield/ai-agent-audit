# Vulnerability details

## Impact

Any NFT sent to `StakingNativeToken` and `StakingToken` contracts is irrecoverably lost. Both of these contracts inherit from `StakingBase`, which in turn inherits from Solmate's `ERC721TokenReceiver`. This indicates an explicit intent for `StakingBase` to receive NFTs, even when they are sent via `safeTransferFrom()`. However, there is no functionality within these contracts to manage, transfer, or withdraw the NFTs once they are received.

Consequently, any NFT sent to these contracts is irrecoverably lost, leading to potential loss of valuable assets and user funds.

## Proof of Concept

The vulnerability can be verified by reviewing the inheritance structure and the absence of NFT management functions within the `StakingBase`, `StakingNativeToken`, and `StakingToken` contracts:

- The `StakingNativeToken` and `StakingToken` contracts inherit from `StakingBase`.
- `StakingBase` inherits from Solmate's `ERC721TokenReceiver`, which means it is designed to receive NFTs.
- There are no functions implemented in `StakingBase`, `StakingNativeToken`, or `StakingToken` to manage, transfer, or withdraw NFTs.

## Tools Used

Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Implement functionality in `StakingBase` to manage or withdraw NFTs. This can include functions such as `withdrawNFT` that allow the owner to recover the NFTs sent to these contracts.
