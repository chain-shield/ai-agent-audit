# Vulnerability details

## Description
The migrate function at the DefaultTargetDispenserL2 contract migrates funds to a new specified L2 target dispenser contract address. It also removes the owner of the contract and makes it useless as it transfers away all its OLAS balance - therefore staking incentives cannot be redeemed.

The issue with that is that even though L2 Dispenser contracts may already have been migrated, nothing stops receiveMessage calls from being done. In the specific case in which the messageRelayer relays a received message right after the contract owner has called migrate, the 
processData internal function will add the staking incentives to the stakingQueueingNonces mapping-based queue but it cannot ever be redeemed. 

To make matters worse, the migrate function doesn't ensure the previously queued staking incentives are processed or migrated.
## Impact
Previously queued staking incentives are not sent nor migrated to their targets.
Messages relayed after a migrate function call may never be redeemed either.

Severity is high as it leads to the loss of whole incentives.
Likelihood is medium as staking queues are expected to have pending incentives.
## Tools Used
Manual review
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Ensure the stakingQueueingNonces is fully paid or fully added to the new dispenser before removing OLAS funds from the contract.
Make sure the contract is not allowed to receive messages once its paused and/or has no owner - as it signals the migration has been completed.
