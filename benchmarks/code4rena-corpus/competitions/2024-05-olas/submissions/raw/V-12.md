# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The userSlope check does not check for equality 0. This further results in a null value of `newBias`. 

This also causes `pointsWeight[nomineeHash][nextTime].bias`, `pointsSum[nextTime].bias` to be calculated incorrectly if the values returned by `_getSum()` and `_getWeight` are less than or equal to `oldBias`.

Also, if the condition `oldSlope.end > nextTime` is true or not, `pointsWeight[nomineeHash][nextTime].slope` and `pointsSum[nextTime].slope` will be incorrectly calculated or equal to zero

Also the values of 
`changesWeight[nomineeHash][newSlope.end]`, `changesSum[newSlope.end]`, `voteUserSlopes[msg.sender][nomineeHash]` will be with a null `newSlope`.
