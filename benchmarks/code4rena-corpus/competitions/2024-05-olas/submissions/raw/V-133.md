# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The ArbitrumDepositProcessorL1.recievemessage() doesn't have the payable modifier, hence it can't receive ether, which will lead to funds stuck in the contract.

## Proof of Concept
A user Alice sends a message from L2 alongside ether, the L1 receive message doesn't accept ether, hence Alice's funds are stuck in the contract.

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Consider adding the Payable modifer to ensure the function is able to receive ether.
