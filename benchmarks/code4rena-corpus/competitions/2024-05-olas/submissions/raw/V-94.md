# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Fee-on-transfer tokens may mess up the accounting in the staking mechanism

## Proof of Concept
After conversation with the sponsor I came to the conclution, that the `stakingToken` address in the logic of `StakingToken` contract, can be a token different than `Olas`. This means that a token with Fee-On-Transfer functionality, can mess up the tokens accounting in the staking mechanism

## Tools Used
Manual review 
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
After importing the `IERC20` interface in the contract, change the transfer in the deposit function like this:
```javascript
  uint256 balanceBefore = IERC20(stakingToken).balanceOf(address(this));
        SafeTransferLib.safeTransferFrom(
            stakingToken,
            msg.sender,
            address(this),
            amount
        );
        amount = IERC20(stakingToken).balanceOf(address(this)) - balanceBefore;
```
