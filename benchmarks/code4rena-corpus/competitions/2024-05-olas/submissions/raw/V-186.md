# Vulnerability details

## Impact
A user's vote may take effect at an unexpected time.

## Detail
The protocol voting interval is one week, which means that the current vote will take effect next week. One situation is that a user votes on a weekend to get a quick effect, but due to congestion or delays, the actual voting time may exceed the current week, causing the user's vote to take effect in the the week after next.

## Tools Used
VScode

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Add a deadline to the voting function to prevent unintended voting.
