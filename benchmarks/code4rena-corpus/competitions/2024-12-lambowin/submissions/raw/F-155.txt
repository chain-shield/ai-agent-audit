S-717
LamboRebalanceOnUniwap lacks mechanism to execute unprofitable but necessary rebalances
Evo
52
Sufficient
383d ago
Impact

The protocol cannot maintain its core 1:1 VETH/WETH peg during market conditions that require unprofitable rebalancing, as the current implementation only allows profitable rebalances. This limitation could lead to sustained depegging events, undermining the protocol's primary purpose.

Proof of Concept

The root cause:

The contract's rebalance() function requires a profit (profit > 0) for all rebalancing operations, No alternative mechanism exists for admin/owner to execute necessary but unprofitable rebalances

This contradicts the protocol's design goal stated in the Protocol readme:

"The RebalanceOnUniswap contract is designed to maintain the VETH/WETH pool ratio at 1:1 rather than for profit. Gas costs are intentionally omitted to increase rebalancing frequency, accepting gas losses as a trade-off for improved price stability."

Tools Used

Manual Review

Recommended Mitigation Steps

Add an admin-only function that can execute rebalances using existing profit if necessary.
