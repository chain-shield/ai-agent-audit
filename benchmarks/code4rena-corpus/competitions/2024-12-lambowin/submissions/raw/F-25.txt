S-744
`VirtualToken` accounting might break if `USDT` or `USDC` fees get activated
Infect3d
72
Sufficient
383d ago
Finding description and impact

While USDC and USDT fees are not activated right now, they can be activated at anytime.
The issue is that cashIn and cashOut functions rely on the amount parameter to decide how much virtual counterpart to mint/burn to/from msg.sender.

Doing so will break accounting.

Proof of Concept

As a simple scenario:

USDT fees are activated to 5%
100 USDT are deposited (cashIn), in response 100 vUSDT get minted
Because of fees the VirtualToken only received 95 USDT
It is not possible to withdraw 100 USDT as only 95 USDT are available
95 USDT are withdrawn, now there are 5 vUSDT left in VirtualToken
Recommended mitigation steps

Measure how much token have been received and mint the same quantity of virtual tokens.
