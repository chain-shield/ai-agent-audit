## Brief Summary

The `_handleTrade` function is responsible for managing trading operations within the contract. It is a critical function that likely involves transferring tokens, updating balances, and interacting with other contracts. However, the absence of the `nonReentrant` modifier in this function opens up the possibility of a reentrancy attack, where a malicious actor could exploit the function to repeatedly call itself (or another vulnerable function) before the initial execution completes.
