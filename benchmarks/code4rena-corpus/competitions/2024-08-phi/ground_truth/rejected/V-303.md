## Brief Summary

The `supportsInterface` function in the `PhiNFT1155` contract does not explicitly check for the `IERC2981` interface, which is the standard interface for NFT royalties. This could lead to a situation where marketplaces or other contracts that rely on `supportsInterface` to determine royalty support may incorrectly conclude that the contract does not support royalties. As a result, they may not collect or forward royalty fees to the creators, leading to a potential loss of revenue.
