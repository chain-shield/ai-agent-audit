# Vulnerability details

## Impact
`CuratorRewardsDistributor::distribute` is used to distribute the awards to the Cred involved Curators, based on their shares number.
A missing check allows anyone outside of the Phi ecosystem to receive reward for triggering the `CuratorRewardsDistributor::distribute` function. The only prerequisite for this to happen is for the attacker to find existing `CredId`.
This vulnerability could lead to unauthorized distribution of rewards, impacting the integrity and fairness of the reward system.
