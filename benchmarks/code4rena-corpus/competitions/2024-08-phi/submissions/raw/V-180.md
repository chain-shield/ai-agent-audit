## Impact
If exploited, these vulnerabilities could lead to:
 - Transactions reverting due to division by zero, potentially blocking critical functionality.
 - Incorrect price calculations due to overflow or underflow, which could be exploited for unfair token minting or economic damage to the 
   protocol.
 - Potential denial of service if the function consistently reverts.
