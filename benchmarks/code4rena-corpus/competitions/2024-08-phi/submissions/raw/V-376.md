# Vulnerability details

The `PhiFactory` contract includes two important functions: `createArt`, which is responsible for generating new artwork, and `updateArtSettings`, which modifies the settings of existing pieces. 

These functions has different pause controls which could lead to inconsistent behavior.

## Impact

The `createArt` function is protected by the `whenNotPaused` modifier, which blocks the creation of new artworks while the contract is paused. On the other hand, the `updateArtSettings` function does not have this modifier, permitting updates to existing artworks even during a contract pause. This discrepancy could result in unintended behavior and introduce potential security vulnerabilities.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

To maintain consistent behavior and enhance security, it's advisable to apply the `whenNotPaused` modifier to the `updateArtSettings` function as well.
