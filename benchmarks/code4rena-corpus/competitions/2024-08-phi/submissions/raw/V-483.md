# Vulnerability details

`depositRewards` function in the `PhiRewards` contract,

The bug is in the handling of the `referral_` address and its associated rewards:

```solidity
if (referral_ == minter_ || referral_ == address(0)) {
    artistTotalReward_ += referralTotalReward_;
    referralTotalReward_ = 0;
} else if (referral_ != address(0)) {
    balanceOf[referral_] += referralTotalReward_;
}
```

The issue is that when the `referral_` address is the same as the `minter_` or is the zero address, the `referralTotalReward_` is added to the `artistTotalReward_`. This means that the artist (represented by the `receiver_` address) gets both their own reward and the referral reward.

This is problematic for several reasons:

1. It creates an incentive for artists to set themselves or the zero address as the referrer, effectively doubling their reward.

2. It goes against the principle of rewarding different participants in the ecosystem separately, as outlined in the project documentation.

3. It could lead to an imbalance in the reward distribution, potentially undermining the incentive structure of the platform.


The impact on the project could be significant:

- It could reduce the effectiveness of the referral system, as there's no incentive to use genuine referrals.
- It could lead to an unfair distribution of rewards, favoring artists over other participants.

## PoC

Triggering the Bug:

1. The `depositRewards` function is internal and is called by `handleRewardsAndGetValueSent`.
2. `handleRewardsAndGetValueSent` is an external function that can only be called by the `PhiFactory` contract (as per the `onlyPhiFactory` modifier in `PhiNFT1155.sol`).
3. The `PhiFactory` contract calls this function during the minting process, specifically in the `_processClaim` function.

Impact and Exploitation Path:

1. Setup:
   - An artist creates a new art piece using the `createArt` function in `PhiFactory`.
   - The art is associated with a specific credential (cred).

2. Minting Process:
   - A user (minter) decides to mint this art.
   - The minter calls the `claim` function in `PhiFactory`.
   - `PhiFactory` processes the claim and calls `claimFromFactory` on the `PhiNFT1155` contract.
   - `PhiNFT1155` then calls `handleRewardsAndGetValueSent` on `PhiRewards`.

3. Exploitation:

   - The critical point is when the `ref_` (referral) address is passed to `claimFromFactory`.
   - If the `ref_` address is set to either the minter's address or the zero address, the bug is triggered.

4. Result:

   - The artist (represented by `receiver_`) receives both the artist reward and the referral reward.
   - The total reward received by the artist is increased by `referralReward` (0.00005 ether) per minted token.


summary : 

1. Artist creates art with credId 1.
2. Minter decides to mint 10 tokens of this art.
3. Minter sets their own address as the referral address.
4. Minter calls `claim` function with this setup.
5. In `PhiRewards.depositRewards`:
   - `artistTotalReward_` = 10 * (0.0001 ether + mintFee)
   - `referralTotalReward_` = 10 * 0.00005 ether
   - Since `referral_ == minter_`, `artistTotalReward_` becomes:
     10 * (0.0001 ether + mintFee) + 10 * 0.00005 ether

The artist ends up receiving an extra 0.0005 ether (10 * 0.00005) in this transaction.


While the individual amounts are small, the cumulative effect over many transactions could be significant, especially for popular artworks with many mints.


## mitigation

To fix this, the function should probably handle the case of an invalid referral address differently. For example, it could either not distribute the referral reward at all in these cases, or it could redirect it to a community fund or back to the protocol itself.
