# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The RewardControl.sol contract has a withdrawWithSig() function, which executes a withdrawal of protocol rewards via signature. This function can be used to steal funds.

## Proof of Concept
The attacker scans the mempool to detect a transaction with the withdrawWithSig() function. After the transaction appears, he reads the sig parameter. He sends his transaction with increased gas with his to parameter and the read sig parameter. The funds are successfully stolen.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Add msg.sender to the from parameter:
_withdraw(msg.sender, to, amount)
