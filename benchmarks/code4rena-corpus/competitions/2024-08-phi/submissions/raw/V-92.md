# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In flow of creating a new Cred, the caller has to buy the [first `1` amount of cred share](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-phi/blob/8c0985f7a10b231f916a51af5d506dd6b0c54120/src/Cred.sol#L570)
If there is excess ether value sent by the caller, then there will be a [refund to the caller](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-phi/blob/8c0985f7a10b231f916a51af5d506dd6b0c54120/src/Cred.sol#L641-L643), effectively give caller execution control. At this execution step, the last trade timestamp has not updated yet and the caller can reenter the contract to sell Cred share, which can bypass the share lock duration
