# Vulnerability details

One of the important concepts in the protocol is Cred. Anyone can buy shares of a Cred using a credId, if the credId exists. Buying a shares comes with two positives for a user: 
1. If the user buy the shares earlier, shares value will increase if the number of issued shares is increasing.
2. And if the Cred is used for art, every time an art is claimed, share holders will receive a reward.

The problem with the second point is that if malicious user see that reward is issued, it can frontrun the `distribute` function and he will claim also rewards and his funds will be locked just for 10 minutes. 

## Impact

Users who are holding shares for days, weeks or months, the current reward distribution model will be not fair for them. Because new users could come buy shares just before the reward distribution, receive a reward and this will lead the long term share holders not satisfied. Also having a just 10 minute ban for withdrawing their funds it's not enough. 

## Proof of Concept

Let's have the following situation
1. There a 50 shares issued to different users for a cred. 
2. Minter claims an artId and pays a fee. Part of this fee is send to the cred of this art id, if both are on the same chain.
3. Malicious user see that the current reward for a cred is big enough and front runs the call and buy's 50 shares. 
4. After that the distribute function and the malicious user will receive 50% of the reward.

User who are having shares for longer period of time will receive less reward.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
The proposal here is to add additional logic to `Cred::getCuratorAddresses` to return only the eligible contracts for reward using a boolean flag. Also I think the `SHARE_LOCK_PERIOD` should be at least 1 day or more, in that situation users will be eligible for rewards after longer period.
