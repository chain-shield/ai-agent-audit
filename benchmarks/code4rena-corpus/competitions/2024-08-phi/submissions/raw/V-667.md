# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The mintProtocolFee and artCreateFee are incorrectly bounded to 10000 wei. These fees do not use basis points model when calculating fees and will thus receive extremely small amounts of fees. 

## Proof of Concept

The mintProtocolFee and artCreateFee cannot be more than 10000 wei. The issue is that although the fees are being interpreted in basis points, the actual fees charged are not being divided by 10000. The model is broken since there is no price attached when claiming nfts through this contract, thus there cannot be a fee percentage applied to a specific value.
