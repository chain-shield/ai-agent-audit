# Vulnerability details

## Impact
```buyShareCredFor()``` allows any user to buy share on behalf of another curator with no restrictions. Whenever a curator buys some amount of credId, their ```lastTradeTimestamp[]``` mapping for that credId is updated, letting them sell any amount at least 10 minutes after last purchase.

A malicious attacker could use a script with their EOA which periodically calls ```buyShareCredFor()``` on behalf of the victim target, they would pay such a low value (some wei) enough to get as few amount of credId share as possible and get the ```lasTradeTimestamp[]``` updated, so that victim will never be able to sell their share.


## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Use a mapping variable that allows a user to operate on behalf on another user, this way no malicious attacker can prevent other from selling.
