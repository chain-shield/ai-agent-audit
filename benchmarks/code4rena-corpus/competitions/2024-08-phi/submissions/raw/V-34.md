# Vulnerability details

## Impact
PhiFactory allows minting of NFT using signatureClaim(), which will verify the minter is
eligible to mint validating the claim state on _validateAndUpdateClaimState() function.

However, it fails to validate the verifier_ in this case, allowing the minter to set it to his own
address and avoid paying verifier the verifierFee during minting.

This will cause verifiers to lose their verifierFee as it can be easily exploited by anyone.
