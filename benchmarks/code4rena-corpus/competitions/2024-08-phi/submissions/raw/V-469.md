# Vulnerability details

## Impact
When a user wishes to call `PhiFactory::createArt` they must pass a `signature_` from `phiSignerAddress` that signs off on a signature deadline, the proposed `_uri` of the newly created art as well as certain information about the `credId` the art will be linked to.

## Proof of Concept
There are a few problems that stem from how the signatures are designed within this function:
1. The signature can be repeated infinitely until the signature deadline has passed.
2. The signature can be repeated by anyone, not just the person who received the signature.
3. Even though the art's initial `_uri` is validated within the signed data, anyone using this signature to call `createArt` can later call `updateArtSettings` without a signature and change the art's uri to anything they desire.
4. Additionally the signature will be replayable cross-chain on any of the different instances of the `PhiFactory`

All of these lead to ways in which new art pieces can be created in ways that have not been permissioned first via the `phiSignerAddress`.

## Recommended Mitigation
To avoid signatures being repeated by non-permissioned actors the protocol should consider adding information about the whom the `creator` must be to the signed data so that only the designated `creator` can use that signature. Additionally the protocol should consider adding a creator nonce to the signature to avoid the signature receiver being able to repeatedly create art (either for themselves or acting as a middle man to use their signature to create art for others).
