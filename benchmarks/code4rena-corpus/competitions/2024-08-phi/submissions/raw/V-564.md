# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The identified vulnerability allows an artist to reuse the same artwork across multiple credentials on the platform, potentially leading to unfair advantages, reduced effort, and grievances among other artists.

Duplication of Art: Artists can create a piece of art once and then reuse it for multiple credentials. This practice significantly reduces the amount of effort required to participate in the protocol, allowing an artist to profit from the same artwork repeatedly without creating new content for each credential.

Unfair Competitive Advantage: By reusing the same artwork, an artist can quickly flood the platform with credentials tied to the same visual content. This diminishes the value of originality and creativity, giving artists who reuse art an unfair advantage over those who invest time and effort into creating unique pieces for each credential.

Undermining the Artist Ecosystem: The practice of reusing art can lead to grievances among other artists who adhere to the norms of creating unique art for each credential. It can create an imbalance in the ecosystem, where originality is devalued, and the platform becomes oversaturated with repetitive content.

Erosion of users Trust: Collectors may begin to lose trust in the platform if they realize that the credentials they are collecting feature duplicate or recycled artwork. This could lead to a decrease in the perceived value of the NFTs, as collectors might feel they are not getting unique or exclusive content.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
1. Artwork Hashing: Use hashing algorithms to generate a unique identifier for each piece of art uploaded to the platform. The system can then check against existing hashes to prevent duplication.

2. Artist Guidelines: Establish clear guidelines and rules that artists must adhere to, including a mandate that each credential must feature unique artwork.

3. Verification by Curators or Verifiers: Introduce a step where curators or verifiers must approve the uniqueness of the art before it is associated with a new credential, ensuring that the content is original and not reused.

4. Whitelisting of Artist Addresses: Implement a whitelisting mechanism where only certain approved addresses are permitted to create or submit artwork for credentials. This would maintain the sanctity of the platform's art content by ensuring that only trusted and verified artists can contribute, thereby reducing the likelihood of art reuse and maintaining high standards of creativity and originality.
