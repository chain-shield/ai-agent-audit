# Vulnerability details

#### Description:
   The function relies on `IBondingCurve(cred.bondingCurve).getPriceData` to get price information. If the bonding curve contract or the data it relies on can be manipulated (e.g., through a compromised oracles), the price data could be skewed, leading to incorrect pricing.

   **Recommendation:**  
   - Ensure that the bonding curve contract is secured and that price data is resistant to manipulation, possibly by using multiple oracles or price sources.
   - Implement sanity checks or price bands to ensure the returned price data falls within an expected range.
