# Vulnerability details

there is a potential issue in the overall system design that could lead to incorrect supply calculations and pricing. This issue stems from the interaction between the `Cred` and `PhiFactory` contracts. Let's break it down:

1. The `Cred` contract manages the buying and selling of shares for credentials.
2. The `PhiFactory` contract manages the creation and minting of NFTs associated with these credentials.

The potential issue arises because these two processes are not directly linked, which could lead to a mismatch between the number of shares and the number of minted NFTs. Here's how this could manifest:

Potential Impact:

1. If the current supply in the `Cred` contract doesn't accurately reflect the number of minted NFTs, it could lead to incorrect pricing calculations for buying and selling shares.

2. This discrepancy could be exploited by savvy users to buy shares at a lower price than they should be or sell them at a higher price.


Proof of Concept:

Let's consider a potential flow that could exploit this issue:

1. A credential is created in the `Cred` contract with an initial supply of 1 (the creator buys the first share).

2. Several users buy shares of this credential through the `Cred` contract, increasing the `currentSupply`.

3. However, no one has actually minted an NFT for this credential through the `PhiFactory` contract yet.

4. Now, when someone wants to mint an NFT, they interact with the `PhiFactory` contract, which doesn't check or update the `currentSupply` in the `Cred` contract.

5. This leads to a situation where the `currentSupply` in the `Cred` contract is higher than the actual number of minted NFTs.

6. This discrepancy could be exploited by users who understand this mechanism. They could potentially buy shares at a lower price (based on the actual number of minted NFTs) and sell them at a higher price (based on the inflated `currentSupply`).


Code Example:

```solidity
// In Cred contract
function buyShareCred(uint256 credId_, uint256 amount_, uint256 maxPrice_) public payable {
    _handleTrade(credId_, amount_, true, _msgSender(), maxPrice_);
}

// In PhiFactory contract
function claim(bytes calldata encodeData_) external payable {
    // ... (minting logic)
    // Note: This doesn't update the currentSupply in the Cred contract
}
```

Why this could be triggered:

1. Separate Contracts: The `Cred` and `PhiFactory` contracts operate independently, without direct synchronization of supply information.

2. No Cross-Contract Checks: The `PhiFactory` contract doesn't verify or update the `currentSupply` in the `Cred` contract when minting NFTs.

3. User Behavior: Users might buy shares without minting NFTs, or vice versa, leading to discrepancies.


Mitigation:

To address this issue, consider implementing the following:

1. Synchronization Mechanism: Implement a way for the `PhiFactory` contract to update the `currentSupply` in the `Cred` contract when NFTs are minted or burned.

2. Supply Checks: Add checks in both contracts to ensure that the number of shares and minted NFTs always match.
