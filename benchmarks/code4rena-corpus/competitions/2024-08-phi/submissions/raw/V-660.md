# Vulnerability details


## Description
The `CuratorRewardsDistributor` and `PhiNFT1155` contracts include mechanisms to accept Ether via payable functions and the receive function. However, they lack corresponding functions to withdraw or transfer this Ether from the contract. This design flaw can result in Ether being permanently locked within the contract, rendering it inaccessible to anyone, including the contract owner. 

The constructor of the `CuratorRewardsDistributor` contract is marked as payable, allowing the contract to receive Ether at deployment. Additionally, the `deposit` function is also payable, allowing users to deposit Ether into the contract. Despite these functionalities to accept Ether, the contract does not include any method (such as a withdraw ) that allows the owner or any authorized user to withdraw the deposited Ether. As a result, any Ether sent to the contract will be permanently locked, leading to potential financial loss.

The `PhiNFT1155` contract contains a receive function that allows it to accept Ether. However, similar to the `CuratorRewardsDistributor` contract, there is no withdraw function to recover any Ether that is accidentally sent to the contract. This omission can cause the same issue of permanently locked funds. While the PhiNFT1155 contract facilitates the transfer of tokens, it does not provide any method to withdraw Ether, which could lead to the contract holding a balance of Ether that cannot be accessed or utilized.
## Impact
Any Ether sent to the contract, whether during deployment, via deposit, or accidentally through the receive function, will be permanently locked within the contract. This results in a financial loss, as these funds cannot be recovered.
## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommendation
To mitigate the risk of locked funds, it is recommended to implement a `withdraw` function in both the `CuratorRewardsDistributor` and `PhiNFT1155` contracts. This function should allow the owner or an authorized entity to withdraw any Ether held by the contract.
