# Vulnerability details

## Centralization Risk in Signature Verification

**Severity: High**

**Description:**
The contract relies heavily on a single `phiSignerAddress` for signature verification. If this address is compromised, it could lead to unauthorized art creation and claims.

**Vulnerable Code:**
```solidity
if (_recoverSigner(keccak256(signedData_), signature_) != phiSignerAddress) revert AddressNotSigned();
```

**Recommendation:**
Consider implementing a multi-signature scheme or using a decentralized oracle for verification to reduce the centralization risk.
