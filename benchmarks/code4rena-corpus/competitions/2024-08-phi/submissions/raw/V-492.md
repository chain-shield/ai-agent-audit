# Vulnerability details

## Description
In `PhiFactory::createArt` artist could set a `mintFee` of `X`, and in `PhiFactory::updateArtSettings` he may change it to `Y`, where `Y > X`.
Now the `minter` saw the initial amount of `X` as the `mintFee` & executed the `claim` function, and sent the `E` amount of ether, where `E > mintFee`, expecting `mintFee - E` amount of
ether to be returned baack to him.
Now to make this a favorable situation, malicious artist `FRONT-RUNS` the minter's tx & updates his `mintFee to Y` which is way more than what `minter` agreed to pay for which was `X`.
This would force minter to pay more for an art while `claim` function is executed, because when the minter tx reads the mintFee, due to frontrun its now `Y` not `X`.
Forcing minter to not receive the amount back.

## Impact
- This griefing attack could hinder the protocol's ability to attract a larger audience, as minters may be discouraged from claiming the art.
- Artist will start benefiting more than what was expected from them.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Add some sort of `slippage protection` in `claim()` function as shown below :
```diff
+   function claim(bytes calldata encodeData_, uint256 maxMintFee){
          uint256 mintFee = getArtMintFee(artId, quantity_);
+         if(mintFee > maxMintFee){  revert(); }
          this.merkleClaim{ value: mintFee }(proof, claimData, mintArgs, leafPart_);
    }
```
