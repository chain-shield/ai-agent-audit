# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The withdrawFor function does not perform any checks to ensure that the caller (msg.sender) is authorized to initiate a withdrawal on behalf of the from address. This means that any external address can call withdrawFor and withdraw funds from another user's account to that same user's address.

Although the funds are ultimately withdrawn to the rightful owner's account (from), this action can be taken without the owner's consent or intention. If the owner has specific plans for those funds (e.g., using them for a particular purpose at a future time or keeping them in the contract for security), an unauthorized withdrawal can disrupt these plans and create unexpected consequences.

## Proof of Concept
Any external actor can call withdrawFor with any user's address as the from parameter. Although the withdrawal is sent back to the original owner (from), it might not be their intention to withdraw the funds at that moment.

Repeated unauthorized withdrawals by external actors could cause inconvenience or prevent the owner from managing their funds as planned. This could be especially problematic if the owner is relying on the balance within the contract for future transactions or if there are gas implications.

If an owner is performing a sequence of actions that depends on the balance in the contract, and a withdrawal is triggered unexpectedly by a third party, it can lead to failed transactions or errors.
