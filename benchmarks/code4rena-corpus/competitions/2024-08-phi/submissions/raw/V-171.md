# Vulnerability details

## Impact

In the getRoyalties function, you revert if the contract is not initialized. 

However, if royaltyInfo is called before initialization, it could also fail unexpectedly. Consider handling the NotInitialized case directly in royaltyInfo to ensure consistent behavior.
