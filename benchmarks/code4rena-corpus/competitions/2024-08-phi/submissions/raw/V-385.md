# Vulnerability details


#### Description
   The check `block.timestamp <= lastTradeTimestamp[credId_][curator_] + SHARE_LOCK_PERIOD` in the sell operation might not be sufficient to prevent certain forms of market manipulation, especially if the `SHARE_LOCK_PERIOD` is too short. This could allow users to buy and sell shares rapidly, potentially manipulating prices or causing instability.

   **Recommendation:**  
   - Review the length of the `SHARE_LOCK_PERIOD` to ensure it provides adequate protection against rapid trading.
   - Consider adding additional checks or cooldowns between trades if the system is susceptible to high volatility.
