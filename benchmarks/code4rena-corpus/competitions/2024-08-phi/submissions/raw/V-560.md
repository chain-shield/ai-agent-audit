# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The identified vulnerability allows the curator to update the credURL associated with a credential (cred) without a subsequent verification process, which could lead to the potential falsification of the entire credential.

1. Lack of Verification: After the initial verification and creation of the NFT linked to a credential, the curator has the ability to update the credURL. However, this update process does not require any re-verification by the original artist or a third party. This means that after an NFT is created and users have claimed it, the underlying data associated with the NFT can be altered without their knowledge or consent.

2. Potential for Fraud: This loophole can be exploited to falsify credentials. The original credURL verified by users can be replaced with a different URL, leading to misinformation or fraudulent claims regarding the NFT. Users who have previously claimed the NFT based on the original data could find that the cred they own now represents something different than what was originally verified.

3. Trust Erosion: The lack of a re-verification mechanism could erode trust in the platform, as users and artists may feel insecure about the integrity of the credentials associated with their NFTs.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
To mitigate this risk, it is recommended to implement a re-verification process whenever the credURL is updated. This would ensure that any changes to the underlying data of a credential are validated by the original artist or a trusted third party, maintaining the integrity of the credentials and the associated NFTs.
