# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Loss of either funds or potential profit, because the block execution is delegated to the validator

## Proof of Concept
Inside `Cred.sol` the functions for buying and selling shares have a slippage protection parameter but lack a deadline parameter, which is very important to avoid unexpected trades.
Without a deadline, the transaction might be hanging inside the mempool and be executed at an unexpected time.
The validator might keep such buy & sell transactions and execute them at a time in which users will get a worse price

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Add a deadline parameter in `buyShareCred`, `sellShareCred`, `buyShareCredFor`,
`batchBuyShareCred`, `batchSellShareCred`
