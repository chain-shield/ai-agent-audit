# Vulnerability details

## Impact

This can be exploited to:
1. An attacker repeatedly buys small amounts of shares for a target user, constantly resetting their SHARE_LOCK_PERIOD and preventing them from selling.
2. Just before a large sell order is about to be executed, a front-runner buys a minimal amount of shares for the seller, forcing them to wait and potentially miss out on favorable market conditions.

The impact of these attacks can be significant as users may be unable to sell their shares at desired times, leading to potential financial losses.


## Proof of Concept
https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-phi/blob/8c0985f7a10b231f916a51af5d506dd6b0c54120/src/Cred.sol#L186

Anyone can call for a curator `buyShareCredFor` resetting their trade timestamp
`lastTradeTimestamp[credId_][curator_] = block.timestamp;`

Resulting in actual curator failing to sell on desired time: 
`
if (block.timestamp <= lastTradeTimestamp[credId][curator] + SHARE_LOCK_PERIOD) {
    revert ShareLockPeriodNotPassed(
        block.timestamp, lastTradeTimestamp[credId][curator] + SHARE_LOCK_PERIOD
    );
}
` 

## Tools Used
Manual Review
## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Implement separate rate control for `buyShareCredFor`, where other users can only trade with larger cooldown period
Implement separate lock periods for buying and selling as selling cannot be done by attackers.
