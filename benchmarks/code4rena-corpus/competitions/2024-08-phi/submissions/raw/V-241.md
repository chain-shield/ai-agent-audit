# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The `distribute` function in the `CuratorRewardsDistributor` externally callable without any access restrictions, allowing any address to trigger the distribution of rewards for any credId. while this isn't a vulnerability per say but the function sends the royaltyFee to `msg.sender` in https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-phi/blob/8c0985f7a10b231f916a51af5d506dd6b0c54120/src/reward/CuratorRewardsDistributor.sol#L120.
this logic allows users even with no relation to the phi protocol to observe the mempool and wait for large deposits to trigger the distribution of rewards. This could lead to a situation where funds are consistently drained from the contract by opportunistic actors.

## Proof of Concept
- Whale decides to buy huge amount of shares
- Malicious user with no legitimate connection to the protocol monitoring the mempool will call distribute frontrunning other honest believers in the protocol.
- Malicious users get the royaltyFee.
## Tools Used
Manual Review
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Ensure that the royaltyFee is distributed to the appropriate beneficiaries 'royaltyRecipient' rather than the caller. Consider implementing a separate function for claiming royalty fees, which includes checks to ensure that only eligible parties can claim these fees.
