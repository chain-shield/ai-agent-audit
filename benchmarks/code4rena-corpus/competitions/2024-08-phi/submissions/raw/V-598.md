# Vulnerability details


## Impact

If the `distribute` function is called with a large number of curators (e.g., 999), the gas cost could exceed the block gas limit on certain chains such as Optimism and Berachain, leading to an out-of-gas (OOG) error and making the function impossible to execute. This would result in a denial of service (DoS) for the reward distribution process, effectively causing loss of funds for dedicated curators.

## Proof of Concept

Edge case scenario: `credContract.getCuratorAddresses(credId, 0, 0)` call could return 999 `distributeAddresses`. Attempts to allocate and iterate over a potentially large array of curator addresses within one transaction without considering the gas cost implications could lead to OOG. 

Recent figures for the block gas limit on chains the protocol will be deployed on are Base at [120 million](https://basescan.org/blocks?p=1), Optimism at [30 million](https://optimistic.etherscan.io/blocks), and Berachain at [30 million](https://bartio.beratrail.io/blocks). According to the results of the Proof of Concept (PoC), the `distribute()` call costs approximately 32 million gas, which is too high. This will obviously revert due to out-of-gas (OOG) errors on Optimism and Berachain.
