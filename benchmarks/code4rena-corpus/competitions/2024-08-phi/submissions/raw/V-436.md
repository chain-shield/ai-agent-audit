# Vulnerability details

## Impact
-Denial of Service (DoS) attacks can significantly impact the functionality and reliability of smart contracts. The primary concerns associated with the identified issues are:

-Increased Gas Costs: The loops identified in the contract code iterate over potentially large arrays or mappings, which can result in high gas consumption. If an attacker is able to trigger these loops with large inputs, they can cause transactions to fail or exceed the gas limit, effectively rendering the contract functions unusable.

-Contract Unavailability: By exploiting the high gas consumption of these loops, an attacker could prevent legitimate users from interacting with the contract. This can lead to situations where critical functions cannot be executed, affecting the availability of the contract’s services.

-Economic Impact: If functions fail due to excessive gas usage, users may experience financial loss or disruption. For example, if a function handling transactions or updates fails, users might lose funds or miss important updates.

-Operational Disruption: High gas costs and failed transactions can lead to operational issues for dApps or services relying on this contract. This can disrupt the normal operation and trustworthiness of the application, potentially affecting its reputation and user base.

-Exploit Potential: An attacker aware of these vulnerabilities could design specific inputs or attacks to maximize gas usage and cause denial of service. This can be especially harmful if the contract handles sensitive or high-value transactions.
