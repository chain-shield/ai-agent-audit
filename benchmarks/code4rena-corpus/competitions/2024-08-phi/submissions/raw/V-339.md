# Vulnerability details

## Impact
If the reward accumulated is not small, we can buy the whole remaining shares, call distribute() and **lock the other users from selling when we've bought the entire remaining amount** through gifting the major shareholders one share using buyShareCredFor()

## Proof of Concept
Each credential can have a max of 999 shares (MAX_SUPPLY constant). 
Let's imagine that there is a cred with currently 400 shares that are being held from 3 users with a good reward accumulated. I can freely buy the remaining shares for myself and leave 3-4 shares to be bought by the buyShareCredFor() in next step, call distribute(), **use buyShareCredFor() to lock the 3 other holders their funds**, because this function runs _handleTrade() which has this check, this is regardless if I buy or I gift, it will run through here: https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-phi/blob/main/src/Cred.sol#L625-L629,
and then sell the shares after my own lock had passed. 

This will be exacerbated if the cred creator is us or a friend of ours, so we don't pay this fee.

Steps:
1. I see a cred with good amount of rewards accumulated, it will be a plus if shares are centralized to 2-4 users, so I can lock them easily.

2. I buy the remaining shares (except a small amount to be used for gifting in step 3) and call distribute().
3. I call N times buyShareCredFor() with the curator as the user I want to lock, I will gift one share to each major shareholder.
4. I wait for my own lock period to expire and sell my shares.
5. Other users have their lock expired, but I've already sold so **they can't profit on my big purchase** and I get away with the majority of the rewards.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Make gifting unable to lock other people's funds.
