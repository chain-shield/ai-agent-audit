# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The PhiFactory contract contains a critical vulnerability due to an improper check of msg.value. This flaw can result in either ETH becoming stuck in the contract or users minting NFTs for less than the intended price. The issue arises because the contract fails to properly verify that the full minting price is paid by the user, potentially allowing ETH already present in the contract to be used to cover part of the cost.
in the `Claim` the funciton:
```javascript

```
this function doesn't handle the `msg.value` instead it calls either `merkleClaim` or `signatureClaim` with `mintFee` to execute everything else.
this opens 2 possibilities:
- Excess Eth sent by the caller won't be refunded and will be stuck in the PhiFactory contract, the logic of refund in `_processClaim` won't be executed as it'll take in arguments `mintFee` for `_etherValue`.
- if there's any Eth stuck in the contract, a user can send underpayment just enough to give the contract the balance of `mintFee` to execute the function.
