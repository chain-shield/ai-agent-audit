# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Observing the `claim()` we see that the mintFee is being calculated and sent to the `MerkleClaim()` and `signatureClaim()` respectively and in these functions the refund is calculated and attempted to be sent to the msg.sender. However, upon proper analysis we can detect that this method is wrong.
Reason that since refund is being calculated in the `merkleClaim()` and `signaureClaim()` the entirety of the msg.value should be sent to the these functions and then the calculation of the `mintFee` and the refund process should follow prompt respectively
