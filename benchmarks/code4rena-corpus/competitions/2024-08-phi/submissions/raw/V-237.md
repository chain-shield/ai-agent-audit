# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Fees `PhiFactory::artCreateFee` and `PhiFactory::mintProtocolFee` are very low, lower than transaction cost. They were probably meant to be percentage values but currently they are sent as flat wei values.

## Proof of Concept
https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-08-phi/blob/8c0985f7a10b231f916a51af5d506dd6b0c54120/src/PhiFactory.sol#L742
`PhiFactory::mintProtocolFee` in this line is sent as flat wei value. Maximum is 10_000 which is 1e-14 ether. This is much less than 0.01$

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Increase maximum allowed value or
Change calculations for these fees
