# Vulnerability details


## Description
The `updateArtSettings` function in the `PhiFactory` contract allows the creator of an art to update various settings related to an existing art, including the URL, receiver address, maximum supply, mint fee, and time bounds for minting. While this function provides flexibility for the art creator, it does not impose any constraints or limits on the value of the `mintFee_` parameter.

The `mintFee_` parameter represents the fee that users must pay to mint an NFT from the art collection. However, the function does not include any validation to ensure that this fee remains within reasonable limits. Without a maximum limit on the mint fee, the art creator has the ability to set this value to an arbitrarily high amount, potentially leading to excessive costs for users who wish to mint NFTs from the art collection. 

The absence of a cap on the `mintFee_` could be exploited by malicious or careless art creators to impose exorbitant fees on users, effectively locking them out from participating in the minting process. Additionally, if the mint fee is set too high, it could deter users from minting NFTs altogether, negatively impacting the utility and popularity of the art collection.

Users who are unaware of the updated mint fee could be caught off guard by the increased costs, leading to a negative experience and potential loss of trust in the platform. High mint fees could also reduce the overall participation in the minting process, which might be detrimental to the success and adoption of the art collection.
## Impact
Users may be forced to pay unexpectedly high fees to mint NFTs, leading to financial strain and dissatisfaction with the platform. Excessive mint fees could result in decreased participation in the minting process, reducing the overall success and reach of the art collection. Allowing unchecked mint fee increases can harm the reputation of the platform, as users may perceive it as exploitative or poorly managed.

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommendation
Introduce a validation check within the `updateArtSettings` function to ensure that the `mintFee_` does not exceed a predefined maximum value. This limit should be set at a level that balances affordability for users with the needs of the art creator.
