# Vulnerability details

During the execution of a DoS (Denial of Service) test on the PhiNFT1155 contract, it was discovered that increasing the batch size and number of iterations resulted in excessive gas consumption, leading to an out-of-gas error. This indicates that the contract might not handle large-scale operations efficiently, which could be exploited in a real-world scenario to disrupt the contract's functionality.

Observed Gas Consumption: The test with the high batch size and iterations consumed significantly more gas than the typical Ethereum block gas limit (around 8 million gas). The excessive gas consumption in this test suggests that the contract is vulnerable to DoS attacks that exploit gas limits.
Impact
Service Disruption: High gas consumption could render the contract unusable under certain conditions, causing service disruption for all users.
Denial of Service: Attackers could exploit this vulnerability to overload the contract with high gas transactions, effectively denying service to legitimate users.
Increased Costs: Excessive gas consumption could lead to increased transaction costs for all users interacting with the contract.

Gas Limits Exceeded:

The total gas usage exceeding the block gas limit causes the test to fail. The Ethereum Virtual Machine (EVM) cannot process the transaction because it runs out of gas, which is necessary for executing smart contracts.
