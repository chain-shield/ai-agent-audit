# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The `protocolFeeDestination` is not listed as a trusted role, so this could be a contract that reverts
on ETH received. 
This could potentially block essential functions of the protocol because on the essential protocol's actions a fee is taken and send to the protocol.
The fee is directly transferred to `protocolFeeDestination` in `_handleTrade`, `_executeBatchTrade`, `_processClaim` and `createArtFromFactory`. 
A similar issue is reported [here](https://solodit.xyz/issues/m-06-denial-of-service-contract-owner-could-block-users-from-withdrawing-their-strike-code4rena-putty-putty-contest-git)

Users cannot do essential operations with the protocol, buy & sell shares, create art or claim tokens


## Proof of Concept
To reproduce the issue, you may create a contract with a receive function that directly reverts.
Next set this contract as a `protocolFeeDestination` and try to buy a share for example 
You will see that the function will revert because the fee is directly sent to the `protocolFeeDestination`

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Use the pull-based approach or just deposit the fee inside the `PhiRewards.sol`

An example is inside the `PhiFactory.sol`s `_processClaim`. 
This way the `protocolFeeDestination` can withdraw his rewards without risking the protocol's functionality
