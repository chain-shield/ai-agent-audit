# Vulnerability details

## Impact
This vulnerability allows malicious actors to copy another artist’s artwork and use it in a credential (cred) by paying a minimal fee. This act can be used to undermine or grieve the original artist, leading to potential harm to their reputation and financial loss.

Artistic Plagiarism: Malicious users can duplicate an artist’s original work and create credentials using the copied art. Since the fee to create a credential is minimal, this can be done repeatedly, flooding the platform with plagiarized content.

Reputation Damage: The original artist’s reputation may suffer as their work could appear across multiple credentials without their consent. This dilution of their creative output can lead to confusion among collectors, who may not be able to distinguish between the original and copied versions.

Financial Harm: As copied art circulates within the platform, the original artist may experience a decrease in demand for their work. This can result in financial losses, especially if the plagiarized credentials are sold at lower prices or given away, devaluing the original art.

Erosion of Trust: The platform’s integrity could be questioned if artists feel their work is not protected against plagiarism. This could lead to a decrease in the number of artists willing to contribute their work, ultimately reducing the platform's appeal to collectors.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
This may involve implement one or more below mitigation steps

Artwork Hashing: Implement an artwork hashing mechanism to create a unique digital fingerprint for each piece of art. This can be used to detect and prevent the reuse or copying of existing artwork within new credentials.

Copyright Protection Mechanism: Introduce a system to check for and enforce copyright protection for artists. This could include automated checks to identify potential plagiarism and flag it for review before the credential is published.

Manual Review Process: Implement a manual review process for credentials where curators or verifiers review the submitted artwork to ensure it is original and not a copy of existing work. This step would add an additional layer of protection against art theft.

Penalty System for Plagiarism: Establish a penalty system for users who are found to have copied another artist’s work. This could include fines, account suspension, or banning users from creating further credentials if they engage in plagiarism.

Whitelisting of Artist Addresses: Introduce a whitelisting system where only verified and trusted artist addresses can submit artwork for credentials. This will help maintain the platform’s artistic integrity by ensuring only reputable artists contribute.
