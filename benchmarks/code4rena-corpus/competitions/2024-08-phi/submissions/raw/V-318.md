# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Wrongful calculation of price of share, users and protocol might lose money.

## Proof of Concept
In handleTrade getPriceData is set to get the price data as isBuy, even when the user is selling instead.

Buy Operation (isSign_ == true):
The price is calculated using the current supply and the amount being purchased.
This means that the price reflects the cost of acquiring new shares based on the current market conditions and the bonding curve.


Sell Operation (isSign_ == false):
The price is calculated using the supply minus the amount being sold.
This reflects the market price for shares that are being sold back into the market, taking into account the reduced supply after the sale


This shows that the price should actually be situational, depends on whether it is selling or buying.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Add an if statement, if selling then use this isntead:
        (uint256 price, uint256 protocolFee, uint256 creatorFee) =
            IBondingCurve(cred.bondingCurve).getPriceData(credId_, supply, amount_, isSell);

and if buy then use this:
            IBondingCurve(cred.bondingCurve).getPriceData(credId_, supply, amount_, isBuy);
