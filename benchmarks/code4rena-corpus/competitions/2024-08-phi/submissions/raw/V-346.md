# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Loss of rewards for curator's. Curators can be target on purpose and whenever they buy shares they would lose value. This can cause a potential loss of users for the protocol as someone with malicious intent can purposefully target and cause the loss of curator's, making curator's afraid of buying any shares.


## Proof of Concept


1.Initial Conditions:
Cred ID: 69
Total Shares: 900
Shares Owned by A: 100
Shares Owned by B: 0 (B is about to buy shares)
Total Balance in Cred ID 69: 9 ETH (this is the amount available for distribution)
Royalty Fee: 1% (this will be deducted from the total balance before distribution)



2.B's Intent:
B intends to buy 100 shares in credId 69  which costs around 1 ETH in total, which means he is expecting to get at least 10% of the rewards back or at least 1 ETH back after investing. B expects to receive a portion of the curator rewards based on the shares owned after the purchase.


3.B Initiates Buy Shares:
B submits a transaction to buy 100 shares for a total of 1 ETH. This transaction is pending in the mempool.


4.A sees B's pending transaction and quickly submits a transaction to call the distribute function for credId 69 before B's transaction is confirmed.


The distribute function checks the total balance in credId 69, which is 9 ETH, and resets  the balanceOf[credId69].


        balanceOf[credId] -= totalBalance;
