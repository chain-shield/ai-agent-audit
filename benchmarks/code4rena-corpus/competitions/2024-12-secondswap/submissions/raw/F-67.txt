S-233
Lost of `SecondSwap::claim` rewards when Arbitrum L2 sequencer is down
00
Sufficient
383d ago
Finding description and impact

The chains the protocol will be deployed on is defined in the "Question" section in the readme file and are: Ethereum, Base, zkSync, Arbitrum.

On the Arbitrum chain when the sequencer is down the state changes can still happen on L2 by passing them from L1 through the Delayed Inbox. During periods when the Arbitrum sequencer is unavailable, transactions are rerouted through the Delayed Inbox, which applies address aliasing to the sender's address.

When the sequencer is down on Arbitrum, the msg.sender of a transaction from the Delayed Inbox is aliased in this way:

L2_Alias = L1_Contract_Address + 0x1111000000000000000000000000000000001111

If the user that initiates a claim during the sequencer downtime, is a smart contracts, this aliasing prevents to transfer the claimed tokens to the correct address because the aliased address on the destination does not match expectations. The user lost their tokens irretrievably.

Impact: high. Users lose their claimed tokens.

Likelihood: Low. Arbitrum is a widely-used Layer 2 scaling solution, with many users relying on it for their transactions. The downtime of the Arbitrum sequencer, while infrequent, is unpredictable in terms on when and how. For example, the recent down was on Dec15, 2023 during a significant surge in network traffic with a major outage lasted over 4 hours and affected multiple components of the network, including the sequencer, batch poster, and feed (ref. here https://status.arbitrum.io/clq6te1l142387b8n5bmllk9es). Arbitrum is a widely used Layer 2 scaling solution with a large user base. Nothing can’t exclude that during a bull period and a lot of new incoming users, it can happen again with a longer downtime period.

Recommended mitigation steps

Incorporating an uptime feed to mitigate issues arising from sequencer downtime.
