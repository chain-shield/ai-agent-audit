Lack of proper support for tokens with decimals that are less than 6
056Security
00
Sufficient
383d ago
Finding description and impact

From the contest README, the protocol has stated that they will handle tokens with decimals that are less than 6. This is not properly handled, as such tokens could lead to the vesting plan's releaseRate to be 0 due to how Solidity rounds down.

Proof of Concept

The main issue with tokens with low decimals is that every time the vesting plan's releaseRate is calculated, it divides the totalAmount and the numSteps. If a token with low decimals is used the total amount could easily fall below the numSteps, leading to releaseRate = 0.

Let's take the following example:

A token with 5 decimals is used -> Token issuer wants to add 500e5 tokens.
Token issuer wants to create a vesting plan for 3 years (94608000 seconds) and have tokens distributed every second.
The release rate calculation will be -> 500e5 / 94608000 = 50000000 / 94608000 = 0
Users won't be able to claim anything until the end of the vesting plan, as every claim in an intermediary step will be claimableAmount = vesting.releaseRate * claimableSteps = 0
Recommended mitigation steps

Set a bare minimum of 1 token per second, or a require statement that the token totalAmount is > numSteps.
