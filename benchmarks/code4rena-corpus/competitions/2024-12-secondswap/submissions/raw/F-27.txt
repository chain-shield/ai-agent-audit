unlistVesting() is susceptible to frontrunning leading to loss of funds to the seller.
dhank
77
Sufficient
383d ago
Finding description and impact

When a user calls unlistVesting( _vestingPlan, _listingId) before a minListingDuration, he should also pay a fixed penaltyFee in USDT.

That means user X calls this functions only if he is aware that reclaiming all the vesting tokens they listed for sale is worth more than the penaltyfee.

But an attacker can frontrun the above execution by calling the spotPurchasing from the X's _listingId leaving only 1 token remaining in the _listingId. (if attacker takes the entire amount ,listingId is marked as soldOut and the attcker cannot explait since the unlistingexecution reverts)

So when the X's unlistVesting() takes place he is actually paying the penaltyFee for 1 vesting token , which puts the X in grief.

Proof of Concept

seller decides to reclaim his listed vestedtokens by calling unlistVesting() before the minListingDuration.(Lets say the seller has 100e18 tokens listed)code
Attacker seeing the txn in the mempool, decides to frontrun the txn by purchasing tokens from that listingId , leaving 1 token behind.
When the sellers execution happens he is paying the penaltyFees only to receive 1 vested token.code

Recommended mitigation steps

Seller should be able to mention a minAmountOut to prevent such attacks while calling the unlistVesting().
