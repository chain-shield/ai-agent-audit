# Vulnerability details

## Impact
An attacker can take out a flashloan to mint more collateral tokens to take out a large debt position. When opening the debt position the attacker would then be eligible to receive LoopFi emissions relative to the amount of debt requested. The attacker can then pay the Loan with no interest as loan amounts are only charged after the timestamp has done. With this the attacker would have left with there collateral tokens to pay flashloan and extra LoopFi emissions.

`Note: ` Loop emissions have a lock on them however they can be exited early with 90% penalty.
