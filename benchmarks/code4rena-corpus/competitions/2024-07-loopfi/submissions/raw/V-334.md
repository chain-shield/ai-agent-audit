# Vulnerability details

## Impact

A critical vulnerability has been identified in the `CDPVAULT` contract that allows users to bypass the paused state of the contract and continue to deposit and withdraw funds. Although the `deposit` and `withdraw` functions are designed to be disabled when the contract is paused, this restriction can be circumvented by directly calling the `modifyCollateralAndDebt` function. 

## Proof of Concept
The vulnerability lies in the `modifyCollateralAndDebt` function, which is marked as `public`. This allows users to call it directly, even when the contract is paused, bypassing the restrictions on deposits and withdrawals.

This demonstrates that users can continue to interact with the contract despite the intended pause
