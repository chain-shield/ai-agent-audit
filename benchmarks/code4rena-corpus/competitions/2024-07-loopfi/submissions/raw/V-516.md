# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Chainlink aggregators have a built-in circuit breaker if the price of an asset goes outside of a predetermined price band. The result is that if an asset experiences a huge drop in value (i.e. LUNA crash) the price of the oracle will continue to return the minPrice instead of the actual price of the asset and vice versa. The wrong price could lead to incorrect collateral valuation and wrongly determine the health of leveraged positions in this protocol.
