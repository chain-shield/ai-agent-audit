# Vulnerability details

## Vulnerability details
When liquidating a position, liquidator must enter the `repayAmount`, this amount is then multiplied by the spot price (minus discount), which will return the amount of collateral the liquidator will receive from the operation.

As this price is subject to variation, liquidator can suffer slippage.
The caller should have an additional parameter `minCollateralOut` to protect himself against slippage.

## Impact
Liquidator can suffer slippage on the received collateral amount.
