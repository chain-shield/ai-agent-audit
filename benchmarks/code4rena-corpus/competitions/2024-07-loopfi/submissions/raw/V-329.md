# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In balancer oracle, the final price is calculated based on all underlying token prices. These underlying token's price comes from chainlink aggregator. Some underlying tokens in balancer pools does not have one feed in chainlink.


## Proof of Concept
In BalancerOracle, the BPT price will be calculated based on underlying token's price. Underlying tokens' price will be fetched from chainlink's aggregator.
The problem is that some balancer pool's underlying token does not have one chainlink aggregator. This will cause that the BalancerOracle is not applicable for some pools.
Some unsupported weighted pools in Balancer:
1. wstETH/sUSDe
2. Temple/DAI
3. WETH/NOTE
4. tBTC/T
...
After checking balancer pool's list, unsupported ratio is quite high. The balancerOracle can work based on all tokens in this balancer pool is quite common and have a feed in chainlink.
