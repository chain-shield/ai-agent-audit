# Vulnerability details

## Impact
By front-running any collateral allocation or position closure of a legitimate user that became under-collateralized during the paused state, a liquidator can unfairly liquidate user positions and collect liquidation profit as soon as the protocol is unpaused. This results in a loss of funds for the user.
Additionally, upon unpausing, it is unlikely that any human users will be able to secure their positions before MEV or liquidation bots capture the available profit. Consequently, the loss is almost certain.

## Proof of Concept
The `CDPVault.sol` can pause certain functions like:
- [deposit()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-loopfi/blob/57871f64bdea450c1f04c9a53dc1a78223719164/src/CDPVault.sol#L223)
- [withdraw()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-loopfi/blob/57871f64bdea450c1f04c9a53dc1a78223719164/src/CDPVault.sol#L239)
- [liquidatePosition()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-loopfi/blob/57871f64bdea450c1f04c9a53dc1a78223719164/src/CDPVault.sol#L509)
- [liquidatePositionBadDebt()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-loopfi/blob/57871f64bdea450c1f04c9a53dc1a78223719164/src/CDPVault.sol#L579)

However, there is a risk that when these functions are unpaused, liquidators could front-run and liquidate user positions before those users have a chance to stabilize their positions.
Note that the repay function is not pausable, but this may not be ideal, as users might have locked the borrowed tokens in another protocol and are unable to redeem them at the moment.

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Consider adding a grace period after unpausing during which liquidation remains blocked to allow users to avoid unfair liquidation by closing their positions or allocating additional collateral. The protocol team can also think of any other way which mitigates the unfair liquidations of users.
