# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The last user who withdraws their `lpETH` might suffer a loss of up to `1e16 - 1` wei of `lpETH`
## Proof of Concept
When `sLP-ETH` is redeemed for `lpETH`, one of following functions might be called:
- `StakingLPEth#withdraw()`
- `StakingLPEth#redeem()`
- `StakingLPEth#cooldownAssets()`
- `StakingLPEth#cooldownShares()`

[`_checkMinShares()`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-loopfi/blob/main/src/StakingLPEth.sol#L141-L144) will be invoked in the above functions to ensure that the total supply of `sLP-ETH` does not fall below `1e16` unless it is `0`.

However if one user left `1` wei `lpETH` in `StakingLPEth`, the last user has no way to withdraw all their `lpETH` because the total supply of `sLP-ETH` will be `1` wei, which is not allowed in `_checkMinShares()`.  

## Tools Used
Manual review
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Remove function `_checkMinShares()`.
