# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The position in `CDPVault` could be at high risk of being liquidated.
## Proof of Concept
Anyone can deposit collateral and borrow `WETH` from `CDPVault`. It will check if the borrower's position is under collateralized at the end of `modifyCollateralAndDebt()`: 
```solidity
451:        if (
452:            (deltaDebt > 0 || deltaCollateral < 0) &&
453:            !_isCollateralized(calcTotalDebt(_calcDebt(position)), collateralValue, config.liquidationRatio)
454:        ) revert CDPVault__modifyCollateralAndDebt_notSafe();
```
As we can see, any action that causes the position to become under-collateralized will revert.

While `config.liquidationRatio` is used for under-collateralization check, it is also used for liquidation check:
```solidity
535:        if (_isCollateralized(calcTotalDebt(debtData), wmul(position.collateral, spotPrice_), config.liquidationRatio))
536:            revert CDPVault__liquidatePosition_notUnsafe();
```
If a borrower takes out the maximum amount of `WETH`, they are at high risk of being liquidated. Any small price fluctuation of the collateral could make their position liquidatable. In such a scenario, the borrower has almost no chance to increase their collateral ratio in time to avoid being liquidated.

## Tools Used
Manual review
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Introduce a `borrowRatio` variable for over-collateralization/under-collateralization check, which should be greater than `liquidationRatio`:
- A borrower can borrow `WETH` as long as the collateral ratio of their position is no less than `borrowRatio`
- The position can be liquidated once their collateral ratio is less than `liquidationRatio`
- When the collateral ratio of a position is between `borrowRatio` and `liquidationRatio`, it is not liquidatable, its owner can deposit collateral or repay debt to increase their collateral ratio
