# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Deposits and withdrawals are rounded down which is incorrect.

## Vulnerability Details
There are rounding errors in the cdp vault. Deposits and withdrawals are rounded down in the same direction, which is incorrect as they are two different operations. Deposits adds collateral to the vault, withdrawals removes collateral from the vault. Hence, they cannot be rounded in the same direction. Deposits should be rounded down and withdrawals should be rounded up.

## Proof of Concept
As seen below, deposits and withdrawals are rounded down in the same direction, which is wrong because they are two different operations.
