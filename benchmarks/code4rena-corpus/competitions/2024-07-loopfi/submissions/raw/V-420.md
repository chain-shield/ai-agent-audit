# Vulnerability details

## Vulnerability details
The `PoolV3::repayCreditAccount` is called by [`CDPVault::repay`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-loopfi/blob/main/src/CDPVault.sol#L274)>>[modifyCollateralAndDebt](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-loopfi/blob/main/src/CDPVault.sol#L428) when users want to repay part of their loan to close or make their position healthier.
The same function is also [called by `CDPVault::liquidatePosition`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-loopfi/blob/main/src/CDPVault.sol#L563) when liquidator want to liquidate a unhealthy position.
The issue with the fact that `PoolV3::repayCreditAccount` [can be paused](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-loopfi/blob/main/src/PoolV3.sol#L537), is that as soon as the pause is removed, the users who couldn't make their position healthier (because of pause) will risk liquidation my MEV bots which can act way faster.

Scenario:
1. Alice deposit funds to CDPVault and borrow ETH (which is borrowed by CDPVault from PoolV3) such as her health-factor is 1
2. PoolV3 is paused, `PoolV3::repayCreditAccount` cannot be called
3. during the pause, asset prices are updated such as Alice H-F is now liquidatable
4. liquidator see that, but cannot liquidate Alice as the liquidation calls `PoolV3::repayCreditAccount` too
5. Alice cannot repay her loan to make it healthy because of the pause
6. pause is removed, Alice can finally send a tx to repay her loan and make it healthy
7. Alice a regular user is frontrun by liquidator who liquidate her, she couldn't do anything

## Impact
Users who get liquidatable during a pause will not be able to repay until pause is removed, and when they will be able to do so they will likely get front-run by MEV bot liquidating their position.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
2 solutions: 
1) Allow users to make their position healthier during pauses
2) Disallow liquidations for a delay after unpause to allow users to manage their position before liquidators
