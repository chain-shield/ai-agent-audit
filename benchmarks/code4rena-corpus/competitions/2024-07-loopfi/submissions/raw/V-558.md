# Vulnerability details

## Vulnerability details

When protocol call `BalancerOracle#update` to get the price by [`EligibilityDataProvider#updatePrice`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-loopfi/blob/4f508781a49ffa53511e7e5ed6cda0ff0eb5bdc5/src/reward/EligibilityDataProvider.sol#L264) or [`ChefIncentivesController#claim`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-loopfi/blob/4f508781a49ffa53511e7e5ed6cda0ff0eb5bdc5/src/reward/ChefIncentivesController.sol#L549), it has no balancer oracle reentrancy protection, so any users can gain profit from the protocol. Although the balancer oracle is updated by keeper role but it's not fully trusted role in general.

## Impact

Protocol may be vulnerable to balancer read-only reentrancy attack.

## Tools Used

vscode

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Use [`VaultReentrancyLib.ensureNotInVaultContext`](https://github.com/balancer/balancer-v2-monorepo/blob/36d282374b457dddea828be7884ee0d185db06ba/pkg/pool-linear/contracts/LinearPool.sol#L136-L146) to protect the balancer oracle from reentrancy attack.
