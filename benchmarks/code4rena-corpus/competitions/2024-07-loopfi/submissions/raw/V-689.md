# Vulnerability details

## Title
`PositionAction::onFlashLoan()` is missing initiator check when trigger flashloan.

## Impact
When the users call the function `Flashlender::flashloan()`, it calls the `pool.lendCreditAccount()` or `PositionAction::onFlashLoan()` but the `initiator` address isn't checked in the `PositionAction::onFlashLoan()` function. 

This allows malicious users to trigger flashloan and `PositionAction::onCreditFlashLoan()` to inflate and credit debt to `leverParams.vault` and then start unfair liquidation leading of loss of collateral of honest users.

If there are fee charged to trigger flashloan, the attacker can trigger the flashloan repeated to inflate the debt because the fee will be accumuated as debt.
