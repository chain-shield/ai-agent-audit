# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The Pendle swap does not check for a deadline, unlike other swaps such as BalancerSwap and UniV3Swap, which do enforce a deadline. Since the Pendle swap does not include this safeguard, it is vulnerable to front-running attacks.

## Proof of Concept
1. User A decides to perform a `pendleJoin` or `pendleExit` transaction after noticing an advantageous price.
2. They submit their transaction.
3. An attacker front-runs the transaction, altering the prices.

## Tools Used
Manual review.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Introduce a deadline variable and enforce a deadline check before submitting transactions to the Pendle protocol, particularly in `pendleJoin` and `pendleExit`.
