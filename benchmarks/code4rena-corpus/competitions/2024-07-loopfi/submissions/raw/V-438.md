# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Positions liquidated with badDebt leading to loss of protocol shares should be excluded from reward, but the current implementation does not ensure this.

## Proof of Concept
```liquidatePositionBadDebt()``` is used to liquidate a position with bad debt, which leads to burning part of treasury shares in PoolV3. This signifies loss of protocol/stakers/dLpLockers rewards.

For such positions, reward should not be recorded for them if they were previously eligible for rewards due to loss they have caused to the treasury.

Currently, there is no way to ensure that positions that led to loss forfeit their rewards in chefIncentive contract.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
If protocol experiences a loss when a position was liquidated, no reward should be recorded for such position in ChefIncentivesController, ```userBaseClaimable[_user]``` should also be set to zero.
