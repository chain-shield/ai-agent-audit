# Vulnerability details

## Vulnerability details
`CDPVault::withdraw` has a `whenNotPaused` modifier which can prevent users from withdrawing their assets.
Users should always be able to manage their position (repay) or get their assets back (withdraw) 

## Impact
Users will not have access to their funds during pauses but are still able to deposit, leading to potential locked funds during an extended

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Common practice is to prevent users from depositing/borrowing during pauses, but to enable withdrawing and repaying. 
That way, if vault get exploited, users are able to save their funds, but also are prevented to put their funds at risk into the vault.
