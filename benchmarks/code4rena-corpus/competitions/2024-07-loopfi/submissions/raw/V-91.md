# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The _isCollateralized is used by the CDP Vault to ensure whether collateral value is equal or greater than the debt. its used by the modifyCollateralAndDebt that can create new or modify existing positions to check if position is sufficiently collateralized:

````
        if (
            (deltaDebt > 0 || deltaCollateral < 0) &&
            !_isCollateralized(calcTotalDebt(_calcDebt(position)), collateralValue, config.liquidationRatio)
        ) revert CDPVault__modifyCollateralAndDebt_notSafe();
````

notice how its passed the total debt of the position, the collateral value based off collateral amount in position and current spot price, and finally the liquidation ratio.

Its also used in liquidations to determine if position is liquidatable or not with similar logic as above:

````

        // verify that the position is indeed unsafe
        if (_isCollateralized(calcTotalDebt(debtData), wmul(position.collateral, spotPrice_), config.liquidationRatio))
            revert CDPVault__liquidatePosition_notUnsafe();
````

Notice how in both scenarios it relies on the liquidationRatio, which means there is no safety buffer for the user. If they take on maximum debt, the smallest change in price can cause them to be instantly liquidated.

Near Instantaneous liquidations are discouraged due to multiple reasons including and not limited to:
1. Protection Against Volatility.
2. Protocol stability and avoiding cascading liquidations.
3. Avoiding unnecessary forced liquidations by MEV bots.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Consider implementing a safety buffer for the users position. For example, if the liquidation threshold is at 80%, the borrower’s max loan is at 75% of that ratio.
