# Vulnerability details

Contract: CDPVault
Function: liquidatePosition
During the review of the liquidatePosition function in the CDPVault contract, we identified a high-risk issue that could prevent the system from performing normal liquidation operations.  The specific issue is found in the following line of code:
```
if (calcTotalDebt(debtData) > wmul(position. collateral, spotPrice_)) revert CDPVault__BadDebt();
```
Risk Description:
This code is intended to prevent liquidation when the collateral value falls below the debt.  However, this approach may lead to the following serious problems:
​Liquidation Failure: In rapidly declining markets, it may become impossible to liquidate any positions as all positions requiring liquidation would trigger the BadDebt error.
Accumulation of System Risk: Due to the inability to liquidate, the system may accumulate a large amount of bad assets, threatening overall solvency.
Reduced Market Efficiency: Preventing liquidations may cause market pricing mechanisms to fail, increasing overall system risk.
Potential Economic Model Collapse: In the long term, this could lead to a collapse of the entire lending system's economic model.
​Impact:
Under extreme market conditions, this issue could lead to:
​System's inability to maintain expected collateralization ratios
Accumulation of a large amount of bad debt
Decrease in user confidence
Compromised overall protocol stability
​Recommendations:
We strongly recommend redesigning the liquidation mechanism.  Consider implementing the following improvements:
​Tiered Liquidation Mechanism: Adopt different liquidation strategies based on varying collateralization levels.
Partial Liquidations: Allow liquidation of partial positions rather than an all-or-nothing approach.
Dynamic Liquidation Parameters: Automatically adjust liquidation thresholds and discounts based on market conditions.
Introduce Risk Buffer Mechanisms: Such as insurance funds to handle bad debt in extreme scenarios.
Improve Price Oracles: Utilize Time-Weighted Average Price (TWAP) to mitigate the impact of instantaneous price fluctuations.
Enhance Liquidation Incentives: Design more attractive liquidation reward mechanisms to ensure sufficient liquidation motivation even in extreme situations.
