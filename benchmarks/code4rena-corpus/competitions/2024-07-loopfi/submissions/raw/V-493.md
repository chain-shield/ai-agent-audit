## Impact
This will result in users receiving fewer rewards than they are entitled to.

In the _newRewards function, the pool’s newReward amount is updated. However, the total rewards in ChefIncentivesController are not only influenced by rewardsPerSecond and duration but also by how much reward is currently available in the contract. 

The available rewards are determined by depositedRewards. In an extreme scenario, if depositedRewards are depleted and there is no timely call to registerRewardDeposit to increase the rewards, or if the call to registerRewardDeposit is delayed, no one will be able to receive rewards. 

Moreover, these missed rewards cannot be retroactively distributed.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Instead of using depositedRewards to limit the reward distribution accounting, you can modify the system so that when the contract runs out of reward tokens, users are simply not allowed to withdraw their rewards. Once additional reward tokens are added to the contract, users can continue to withdraw their rewards. This approach ensures that the amount of rewards users are entitled to is not affected, and they can still receive the full amount once the reward tokens are replenished.
