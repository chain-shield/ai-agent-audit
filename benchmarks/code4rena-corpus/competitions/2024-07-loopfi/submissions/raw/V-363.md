# Vulnerability details

## Impact

Having liquidation penalty may backfire as it can incur bad debt from undercollaterized (CR>100%) positions.

## Proof of Concept

- Assume collaterization ratio is 125%, and Alice has 100 ETH worth of collateral (100 aETH worth 1 ETH each) and borrows 50 ETH.
- Some time passes and Alice's collateral becomes 55 ETH, which is liquidable (CR=110%)
- Bob wants to repay 50 ETH and get back some extra (maximum of 55 ETH).
- liquidationPenalty is 20%

Bob calls `liquidatePosition()`

- `spotPrice()` is derived. Assume no `liquidationDiscount` 

```
        // load price and calculate discounted price
        uint256 spotPrice_ = spotPrice();
        uint256 discountedPrice = wmul(spotPrice_, liqConfig_.liquidationDiscount);
```

The spot price is now 0.55 ETH per 1 aETH. The `discountedPrice` is 0.55 ETH

The total debt is 50 ETH, and the collateral is worth 55 ETH (100 aETH * 0.55 ETH), so there's no bad debt.

```
        // Enusure that there's no bad debt
        if (calcTotalDebt(debtData) > wmul(position.collateral, spotPrice_)) revert CDPVault__BadDebt();
```

`takeCollateral` is calculated.

```
        uint256 takeCollateral = wdiv(repayAmount, discountedPrice);
        uint256 deltaDebt = wmul(repayAmount, liqConfig_.liquidationPenalty);
        uint256 penalty = wmul(repayAmount, WAD - liqConfig_.liquidationPenalty);
        if (takeCollateral > position.collateral) revert CDPVault__tooHighRepayAmount();
```

`takeCollateral` = 50 ETH * 1e18 / 0.55e18 (no discount at play) = 90.90 ETH, which is greater than 60 ETH (the collateral). This means the liquidator can take 90.90 out of the 100 collateral amount, leaving the borrower with ~9.1 collateral left (9.1 * 0.55) = ~5 ETH value.

`deltaDebt` = 50e18 * 80% = 40e18, `penalty` = 10e18. This means that although the liquidator is taking 90.90 collateral away, he is only paying 40 ETH worth of debt, with the 10 as penalty. `deltaDebt` is 40e18, while `debtData.debt` is 50e18.

```
  } else {
            (newDebt, newCumulativeIndex, profit, position.cumulativeQuotaInterest) = calcDecrease(
                deltaDebt, // delta debt
                debtData.debt,
                debtData.cumulativeIndexNow, // current cumulative base interest index in Ray
                debtData.cumulativeIndexLastUpdate,
                debtData.cumulativeQuotaInterest
            );

```

Before: Alice has 50 ETH DEBT, 55 ETH worth of collateral (100 aETH)
After: Alice has 10 ETH DEBT (only 40 is paid off), 5 ETH worth of collateral (~9.1 aETH). 

This bring Alice into bad debt, although she can perfectly clear her debt before the penalty as her collateral ratio was still >100% at time of liquidation.

## Tools Used

Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Consider not using the penalty as it may backfire, and all the penalty (profits) will end up paying the bad debt anyways. Simplify the liquidation process.
