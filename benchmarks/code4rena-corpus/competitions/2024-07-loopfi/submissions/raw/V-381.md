# Vulnerability details

## Impact
when liquidating a position , user needs to repay the debt + interest accrued to get the discounted collateral, 
but the interest is not added to user who repay only the partial amount .
``The loss would be the users who repay partial will get more collateral than who repay  full repayment because they need to pay the interest accrued also.``

 ## Proof of Concept
here , user is not paying the interest accrued when partial liquidation.
