# Vulnerability details

## Impact

The OpenZeppelin's modifier `whenNotPaused` used in `CDPVault` is to ensure that core functions are only callable when the protocol's kill switch isn't active. Functions such as `deposit`, `withdraw` and `liquidation` functionalities have the modifier. But, other vital functions such as  `CDPVault::_modifyPosition`, `CDPVault::modifyCollateralAndDebt`, `CDPVault::borrow` all lack this modifier. The impact of this is that even when the protocol is paused users can still borrow collateral against their credit and modify their positions as they see fit. If they accumulate bad debt they can't face liquidation as all liquidation functions will be paused. The ussers do need to have deposited `collateral` before the protocol pause.
