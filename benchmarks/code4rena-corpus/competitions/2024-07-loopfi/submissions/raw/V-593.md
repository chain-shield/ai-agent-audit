# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Attackers steal other users' tokens

## Proof of Concept

`StakingLPEth` is an `ERC4626` contract with a `MIN_SHARES` check added to `ERC4626`, but this does not protect against an inflation attack,

An attacker can donate the asset token in the pool's initial state, then `mint/deposit` 0.01 ETH share, avoid the `MIN_SHARES` check, and conduct a inflation attack.

1. The pool is in the initialization state, and no other user has mint token.
2. The attacker discovers that the victim bob deposits 1 ETH of assets.
3. The attacker uses frontrun to perform the following operations:
    3.1 The attacker donated an asset token of 100 wei to inflate the pool.
    3.2 The attacker mint 0.01 ETH share and skip the check of `MIN_SHARES`, about 1 ETH asset is required.
4. Bob's transaction is executed, at this time, the amount of share is 0.01 ETH, the amount of asset is 1 ETH, and the amount of share bob gets is 0 due to division overflow. share = assets * totalSupply / totalAssets.
5. Since only the attacker has a share at this point, all assets belong to the attacker.
6. The `MIN_SHARES` check allows `_totalSupply = 0`, so the attacker can withdraw all the assets.

The quantities used above are estimates.
About the erc4626 inflation attack: https://docs.openzeppelin.com/contracts/4.x/erc4626
