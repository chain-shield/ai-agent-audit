# Vulnerability details

## Impact

The `_chainlinkSpot()` fetches the latestRoundData() from a Chainlink oracle feed, without checking the round completeness or the timestamp to ensure that the reported price is not stale. Using a stale price feed can result in wrong calculations in the vault shares math which can lead to an exploit from an attacker.

## Proof of Concept

No checks for `_chainlinkSpot()` latestRoundData to validate if the value returned indicates stale data. Reasons for a price feed to stop updating are listed [here](https://ethereum.stackexchange.com/questions/133242/how-future-resilient-is-a-chainlink-price-feed/133843#133843)

 This could lead to stale prices according to the Chainlink [documentation](https://docs.chain.link/data-feeds/historical-data)

Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Validate `updatedAt` of `latestRoundData()` and check that the price age is not above a threshold and validated.
