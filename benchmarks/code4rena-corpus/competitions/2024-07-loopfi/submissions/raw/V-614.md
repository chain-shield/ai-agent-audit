# Vulnerability details

## Description
The AuraVault contract is open to early inflation attacks. 

On its deployment, it doesn't mint any sort of dead shares.
It doesn't require a minimum amount of shares to be minted.
Its totalAssets are determined by the current balance of the contract.

With those premises in mind, a user is free to deposit 1 wei worth of rewardPool tokens, mint exactly 1 wei share then donate an arbitrarily high amount of rewardPool tokens (let's consider 1k ether worth of rewardPool tokens here).
If the next depositor deposits less than 1k ether + 1 wei worth of rewardPool tokens, the minting process will round down the shares minted and the second depositor will end up with 0 shares.
## Impact
The AuraVault contract can be attacked with the early-inflation attack vector.
Unsuspecting users that deposit after a malicious actor may lose their entire deposit.
## Tools Used
Manual review
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Make sure to mint 1e9 dead shares in the construction of the contract so that the early inflation attack vector is not appliable anymore.
Check out Euler's article on ERC4626 vaults safety: [Exchange Rate Manipulation in ERC4626 Vaults - Euler Finance](https://www.euler.finance/blog/exchange-rate-manipulation-in-erc4626-vaults) ; on it, the protocol discusses different mechanisms of protecting the vault - the  "How not to get rekt" topic has some interesting suggestions to prevent early inflation attacks.
