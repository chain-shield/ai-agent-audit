# Vulnerability details

## Impact
- after repaying portion of interest, the borrower can hold debt for a period of time without paying any interest.
- The user who repay portion of interest will be blocked to deposit/withdraw collateral, borrow/repay asset for a period. If the collateral price drops, the user could be at risk of being liquidated after the blocking period ends because they might not be able to increase their collateral ratio in time by depositing more collateral or repaying their debt.
- The user who are unable to increase their collateral ratio could repay portion of interest to avoid being liquidated.

## Proof of Concept
A borrower can repay their debt and accumulated interest by calling `CDPVault#repay()`. 
The quota interest will be repaid first, followed by the accrued interest. Any remaining assets will then be used to repay the debt.

If accrued interest can be fully repaid, `newCumulativeIndex` will be set to `cumulativeIndexNow`:
```solidity
679:                newCumulativeIndex = cumulativeIndexNow;
``` 
If accrued interest can only be partially repaid, `newCumulativeIndex` will be calculated following below formula:
```math
\begin{align*}
debt * (\frac{index_{now}}{index_{last}} - 1) - interest_{repaid} &= debt * (\frac{index_{now}}{index_{new}} - 1)\\
\Downarrow \\
index_{new} &= \frac{debt*index_{now}*index_{last}}{debt*index_{now} - index_{last}*interest_{repaid}}
\end{align*}
```
$interest_{repaid}$ should be the partially repaid amount. However, `profit` is used as $interest_{repaid}$:
```solidity
703:                newCumulativeIndex =
704:                    (INDEX_PRECISION * cumulativeIndexNow * cumulativeIndexLastUpdate) /
705:                    (INDEX_PRECISION *
706:                        cumulativeIndexNow -
707:@>                      (INDEX_PRECISION * profit * cumulativeIndexLastUpdate) /
708:                        debt); // U:[CL-3]
``` 
`profit` is the sum of `cumulativeQuotaInterest` and partially repaid interest. The incorrect value could result in `newCumulativeIndex` being significantly larger than `cumulativeIndexNow`.
The wrong `newCumulativeIndex` value will be stored as `cumulativeIndexLastUpdate` of borrower:

The accrued interest will be calculated by calling CreditLogic.calcIncrease(). The formula will be like as below:
```math
\begin{align*}
interest_{accrued} &= debt*(\frac{index_{now}}{index_{last}} - 1)
\end{align*}
```
 `cumulativeIndexLastUpdate` of the borrower is used as $index_{last}$.

As we can see, the above calculation might be negative because  $index_{last}$ could be larger than $index_{now}$
