# Vulnerability details

When updating the position using the [_modifyPosition()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-loopfi/blob/57871f64bdea450c1f04c9a53dc1a78223719164/src/CDPVault.sol#L305-L342) internal function. The issue is that collateral and debt updates are performed independently, without ensuring that they maintain a consistent collateral-to-debt ratio.

The `_modifyPosition()` function is called with the `newDebt` and `deltaCollateral` values, which are calculated separately based on the `deltaDebt` and `deltaCollateral` inputs, respectively. However, there is no adjustment made to ensure that the resulting collateral and debt values are in sync and maintain the desired collateralization ratio.

This independent update of collateral and debt can lead to positions with imbalanced collateral-to-debt ratios.

## Impact
The current implementation of modifyCollateralAndDebt() can result in the following consequences:

* The function directly sets position.collateral to add(position.collateral, deltaCollateral) without considering the corresponding debt change. This can cause discrepancies between the actual collateral balance, the sum of position collaterals, and the total collateral tracked by the vault.

* By allowing independent updates to collateral and debt, the function may create positions with imbalanced collateral-to-debt ratios. This can lead to positions that are undercollateralized or overcollateralized, deviating from the intended vault mechanics.

* If a position's collateral and debt are not updated in sync, it may trigger unintended liquidations or prevent necessary liquidations from occurring. This can result in financial losses for users and disrupt the stability of the vault.

## Proof of Concept
* Alice creates a position in the vault with an initial collateral of 100 tokens and a debt of 50 tokens.
* Alice calls `modifyCollateralAndDebt()` with the following parameters:

   * `deltaCollateral = -50` (withdrawing 50 tokens of collateral)
   * `deltaDebt = 0` (no change in debt)

* The function calculates the new debt based on `deltaDebt` (which is zero) and updates `position.debt` accordingly.
* However, the function directly updates `position.collateral` to `add(position.collateral, deltaCollateral)`, resulting in a new collateral balance of 50 tokens.
* The vault's total collateral and the sum of position collaterals are not updated consistently with the debt change.

Because of that, Alice's position now has a collateral of 50 tokens and a debt of 50 tokens, deviating from the expected collateral-to-debt ratio.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
The modifyCollateralAndDebt() function should be revised to ensure that collateral and debt updates remain in sync and the position remains properly collateralized after the updates.

* Instead of updating `position.collateral` directly, calculate the new collateral value based on the new debt and the target collateral ratio.
* Ensure that any update to a position's collateral takes into account the corresponding debt change, maintaining a consistent collateral-to-debt ratio.
* Update the vault's total collateral and the sum of position collaterals in a manner that reflects the consistent changes in both collateral and debt.
