# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Storage of [BalancerOracle](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-loopfi/blame/57871f64bdea450c1f04c9a53dc1a78223719164/src/oracle/BalancerOracle.sol#L53) contract might be corruptible during upgrade.

## Proof of Concept
The vulnerability arises from the mismatch in the size of the storage gaps between the following contracts contracts [BalancerOracle](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-loopfi/blame/57871f64bdea450c1f04c9a53dc1a78223719164/src/oracle/BalancerOracle.sol#L53), [ChainlinkOracle](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-loopfi/blame/57871f64bdea450c1f04c9a53dc1a78223719164/src/oracle/ChainlinkOracle.sol#L32), [PendleLPOracle](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-loopfi/blame/57871f64bdea450c1f04c9a53dc1a78223719164/src/oracle/PendleLPOracle.sol#L40).

Inconsistencies in storage layout can lead to challenges in maintenance and future upgrades.  This inconsistency deviates from the common practice observed in the codebase, where storage gaps typically align to a fixed number, often 50 (https://docs.openzeppelin.com/contracts/3.x/upgradeable).

The storage gap in the mentioned contracts we can see here:

[BalancerOracle](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-loopfi/blame/57871f64bdea450c1f04c9a53dc1a78223719164/src/oracle/BalancerOracle.sol#L53)
```js
   uint256[47] private __gap;
```
[ChainlinkOracle](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-loopfi/blame/57871f64bdea450c1f04c9a53dc1a78223719164/src/oracle/ChainlinkOracle.sol#L32)
```js
    uint256[50] private __gap;
```
[PendleOracle](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-loopfi/blame/57871f64bdea450c1f04c9a53dc1a78223719164/src/oracle/PendleLPOracle.sol#L40)
```js
    uint256[50] private __gap;
```

The impact of this inconsistency lies in potential difficulties during code maintenance and upgrades. Inconsistent storage layouts can lead to storage collisions and make the codebase harder to understand and maintain.
The probability of something going wrong with future upgrades is low to medium, and the impact of mistakes would be medium to high.

This is already known vulnerability found on other contests you can check it [here](https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2024-05-midas-judging/issues/30).

## Tools Used
Manual Review.
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Make storage gap in `BalancerOracle` same as in other oracles:
```diff
-    uint256[47] private __gap;
+    uint256[50] private __gap;
```
