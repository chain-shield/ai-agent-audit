# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In the callback function `PositionAction#onCreditFlashLoan()`, the flashloan fee is not added to the final allowance from PositionAction contract to FlashLender contract.
So in case the flashloan fee it not zero, users can not decrease the leverage of the position by taking out a credit flashloan
