# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Interest is not accrued properly

## Proof of Concept
assume withdrawal fee is 0.1%
assume at any point in time due to borrows the available liquidity is 10 weth, 
bob who has 20 weth worth of shares calls withdraw of 10.01 weth , the amount actually going to bob is 10weth so the transfer will go through, however in updateBaseInterest, the available liquidaity would be calculated as 10e18 - 10.01e18 , because the operation is done as int256, there wont be a revert on underflow, hence the base interest rate would be calculated using the wrong availableLiquidity  

## Tools Used
manual analysis

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
a check to ensure availableliquidity did not underfiow
