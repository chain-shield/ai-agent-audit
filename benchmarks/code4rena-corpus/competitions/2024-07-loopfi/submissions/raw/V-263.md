# Vulnerability details

## Impact
CDPVault.sol can accumulate bad debt when paused because liquidation functions are  pausable

## Proof of Concept
The `liquidatePosition(...)` and `liquidatePositionBadDebt(...)` functions of the `CDPVault.sol` have the `whenNotPaused` modifier. This will make liquidations to be paused when the contract is paused. In event that the contract is paused and there is unexpected market fall, the protocol may accrue bad debt that are not attractive to liquidate anymore when the market is unpaused. This also can affect user's trust in the stability of the protocol that also pauses liquidations when the contract is paused.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Consider removing the `whenNotPaused` modifier on the `liquidatePosition(...)` and `liquidatePositionBadDebt(...)` functions of the `CDPVault.sol` contract.
