# Vulnerability details

## Impact

The decentralized stablecoin protocol will be unable to operate without fallback solutions, if Chainlink's aggregators fail to update price data which may lead to denial of service.

Consider the scenario that Chainlink's aggregators fail to update price data and collateral tokens' prices dramatically go down, the decentralized stablecoin protocol will refuse to liquidate users' positions. The protocol will become insolvent eventually, leading to the protocol's disruption.

## Proof of Concept

Since the `_fetchAndValidate()` liquidation and borrowing operations rely on price feed reporting, if Chainlink's aggregators fail to update the price data, the protocol will not be able to operate and transactions will revert as well when the minimum price disparity between the oracles exceeds.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Consider using  fallback solutions, such as using other off-chain oracle providers and/or on-chain Uniswap's TWAP, for feeding price data in case Chainlink's aggregators fail.
