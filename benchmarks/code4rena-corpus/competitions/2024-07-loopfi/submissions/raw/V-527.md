# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Positions could be uncollaterized with debt that cannot be repayed 

## Proof of Concept
cdpvault::liquidatePosition calculates delta debt with the assumption that the position owner pays a penalty for allowing themselves to become liquidatable, however this could be exploited such that a malicious actor would receive all their collateral and still have some debt left 
using a simple example 
,,,
assume liquidation ratio is 1.1, penalty is .5%,current price is 1 lpeth:1 weth
discount is 3.5%
however due to some conditions the price is now 1:0.9409090909
a malicious actor can frontrun the oracle by depositing 110 lpeth and borrowing 100 weth 
then backrun the oracle and liquidate themself, at this point, accounting for discount and price, the take collateral would be exactly 110 lpeth , hence all checks would be passed and bobs collateral can cover debt accounting for discount  so no bad debt revert,delta debt accounting for penalty would be 99.5weth 
leaving bob with 0.5 weth in debt however bob would get all his collateral back 
leaving his position completely uncollaterized and impossible to liquidate even if cdp::liquidate with bad debt is called as the take collateral as well as the repay amount would be zero which would revert when token transfer occurs(zero amount)
,,,

manual analysis 

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
if debt is left after liquidate call, revert if the debt is not collaterized and treat as bad debt
