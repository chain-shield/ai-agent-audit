# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The omission of calling disableInitializers in the constructor of a proxy upgradeable contract's logic contract introduces a significant risk, as it leaves the implementation contract vulnerable to being initialized by potential attackers.


## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Call disableInitializers: Include a call to disableInitializers in the constructor of the logic contract as recommended by OpenZeppelin

Upgradable
