# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The `AuraVault::_chainlinkSpot` is used to return the `BAL/USD` price in 8 decimals. However, the only validation done on the returned price, `answer`, is a `bool isValid` to check that the calculated `price > 0`. 

The `AuraVault::_getAuraSpot` is used to get the price of `AURA` reward tokens. However, there is no validation whatsoever on the returned `answer` used to calculate the `ethPrice`.
