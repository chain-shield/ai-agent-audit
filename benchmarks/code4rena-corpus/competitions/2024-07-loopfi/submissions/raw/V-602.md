# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The liquidator might receive less collateral than expected

## Proof of Concept
When a position become liquidatable, anyone can call `CDPVault#liquidatePosition()` to liquidate this position. The liquidator can pay `repayAmount` of payment token to buy the collateral in `discountedPrice `. 

However, there is no slippage protection to ensure the minimum amount of collateral the liquidator can receive. The liquidator might receive far less collateral than expected due to network congestion or high fluctuations in collateral price.
## Tools Used
Manual review
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Introduce slippage protection to `liquidatePosition()`. The liquidator should be able to specify the minimum amount of collateral they are willing to receive.
