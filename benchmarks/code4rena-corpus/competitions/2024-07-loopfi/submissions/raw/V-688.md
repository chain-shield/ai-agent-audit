# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Both the `AuraVault::_chainlinkSpot` and `AuraVault::_getAuraSpot` funcitons don't check if the prices returned by the Chainlink aggregators are out of date.
The user can claim more or even less funds than the WETH corresponding to what has been deposited.

As they are used in the `AuraVault::_previewReward` function, it can result in an incorrect rewards price which is used when users are claiming the rewards. Meaning that users can get more or even less rewards than they should, based on the amount of WETH they deposited in exchange.

## Proof of Concept
-`_getAuraSpot` function:
