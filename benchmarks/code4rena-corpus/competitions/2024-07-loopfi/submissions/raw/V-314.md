# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In PoolAction, users need to approve poolAction and then call transferAndJoin() function to transfer and join pool. Malicious user can make use of the victim's approval and frontrun the victim's transferAndJoin() to transfer victim's funds to join pool.

## Proof of Concept
In PoolAction, the contract provides one method transferAndJoin() to help users to transfer and join pools. Before users call transferAndJoin(), they need to approve poolAction, or sign one signature. The malicious users can frontrun the victim's transferAndJoin() to join the pool for himself.
I've already confirmed with the sponsor, users can use this contract directly to transfer and join pools.
