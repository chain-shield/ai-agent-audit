# Vulnerability details

During withdrawals, a user can leave behind 1 wei of LP shares, preventing the last user from withdrawing their shares below the `MIN_SHARES` threshold.

## Impact

The last user will be unable to withdraw all their shares from the vault, causing a denial of service (DoS) and a potential loss of funds up to the value of `MIN_SHARES`.

## Proof of Concept

1. The StakingLPEth vault is no longer supported, and all users need to withdraw their shares.
2. One user withdraws all their shares except for 1 wei.
3. The last user attempting to withdraw needs to withdraw all their shares to bring the totalSupply to 0. However, this is impossible because 1 wei of shares always remains, causing the following code to revert: `if (_totalSupply > 0 && _totalSupply < MIN_SHARES) revert MinSharesViolation();`. The _totalSupply will always be `> 0`.
4. The last user is stuck with their LP shares up to the `MIN_SHARES` amount, unable to withdraw them until another user deposits, who will then also be stuck.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Mint non-withdrawable dead shares to the vault itself, amounting to `MIN_SHARES`. This will prevent donation attacks and also mitigate the potential griefing of the last user.
