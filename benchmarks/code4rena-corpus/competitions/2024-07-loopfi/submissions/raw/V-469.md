# Vulnerability details

## Vulnerability Details 
The `ChefIncentivesController` contract offers two approaches for reward distribution:

1. A fixed `rewardsPerSecond` value
2. A dynamic `rewardsPerSecond` value based on an emission schedule

For the emission schedule method, regular checks are necessary to compare the current rewards per second against the schedule, determining when adjustments are needed. This functionality is implemented in the `setScheduledRewardsPerSecond` function, which is called by `_updateEmissions`.

However, an inconsistency exists in the current implementation. The `_updateEmissions` function is only utilized in `addPool` and `claim` operations. It is notably absent in `_handleActionAfterForToken` and `afterLockUpdate`. These functions call `_updatePool` to recalculate pool rewards using `rewardsPerSecond`, but they don't account for potential changes in the emission schedule.

This oversight could result in inaccurate reward emissions until either a new pool is added or a claim is processed. To address this issue, it's advisable to invoke `_updateEmissions` whenever reward recalculations are necessary. This approach would ensure that the `rewardsPerSecond` value consistently aligns with the current scheduled emission rate, maintaining accuracy in reward distribution across all relevant contract functions.

## Impact
Rewards distributed to users may be inaccurate, potentially leading to over- or under-payment of incentives. This discrepancy could persist until `addPool` is called or a claim is processed, potentially affecting the fairness of the reward system and the economic balance of the protocol.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
To mitigate this issue, call _updateEmissions in all functions that update rewards, ensuring consistent alignment with the current emission schedule.
