# Vulnerability details

## Vulnerability details
`CDPVault::borrow` doesn't have the `whenNotPaused` modifier, but `CDPVault::liquidatePosition` and `CDPVault::liquidatePositionBadDebt` have this modifier. 

## Impact
This can lead to users opening borrow positions, potentially creating bad debt but being unliquidatable until pause is removed.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Common practice is to prevent users from depositing/borrowing during pauses, but to enable withdrawing and repaying.
That way, if vault get exploited or any other market issues, users can get back their funds but also avoid bad debt situations, which would be bad for users and the protocol.
