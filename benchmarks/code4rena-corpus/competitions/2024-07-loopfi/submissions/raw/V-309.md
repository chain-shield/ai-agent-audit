# Vulnerability details

## Impact
If some bad debt happens in PoolV3, share's price in PoolV3 keeps 1:1. It means that earlier withdrawal can withdraw asset with price 1:1 and later withdrawal can withdraw nothing because of insufficient assets.

## Proof of Concept
In PoolV3, credit managers can borrow and repay funds from PoolV3. If some funds are lost in one credit manager, the `treasury` will try to absorb this fund loss. 
The problem is that when the `treasury`'s fund is not enough to cover the loss, some bad debt will happen and leave in this PoolV3. And the share's price in PoolV3 keeps 1:1 compared with the underlying asset WETH. The earlier withdrawal can still withdraw WETH with share's price 1:1 WETH. However, the later withdrawal may fail to withdraw WETH because of insufficient funds left in the PoolV3.
 
In PoolV3, when some bad debt happens, all depositors should cover this bad debt together. But according to current implementation, the later withdrawer has to take the loss. This is unfair.
