# Vulnerability details


## Impact
When the entire protocol incurs a loss, the users who withdraw their assets first are protected, while those who withdraw later bear all the losses.

However, lending can potentially lead to bad debt and result in losses. In the repayCreditAccount() function, when sharesToBurn > sharesInTreasury, it indicates that the entire protocol is in a loss state.

Loss Scenario Example:

	1.	Alice deposits 1000 ETH and receives 1000 shares.
	2.	Bob deposits 1000 ETH and receives 1000 shares.
	3.	The credit manager loans out 1000 ETH, which results in bad debt, and only 800 ETH is actually returned.
	4.	Bob withdraws his 1000 shares and receives 1000 ETH.
	5.	When Alice tries to withdraw her 1000 shares, she will fail (because at this point, there are only 800 ETH left in PoolV3), so she can only withdraw 800 shares, equivalent to 800 ETH. At this point, the remaining 200 shares have a value of 0.

Because other users will notice that the shares exceed the assets, they will not risk depositing into this pool, and Alice will never be able to recover her 200 ETH.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Modify the calculation of shares and assets so that everyone shares the loss.
