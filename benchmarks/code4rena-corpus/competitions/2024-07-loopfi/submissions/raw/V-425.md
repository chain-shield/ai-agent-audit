# Vulnerability details

## Summary
PendleLP generate revenues through fees (auto-compounded into LP) but also PENDLE tokens rewards, the latter cannot be claimed by the vault, meaning they are lost.

## Vulnerability details
Users can deposit PendleLP tokens into `CDPVault` to borrow ETH. These PendleLP are held by `CDPVault` until user withdraw them.
Users are then incentivized to borrow ETH in order to buy more PendleLP, deposit and borrow again, leveraging the amount of deposited PendleLP.

Those PendleLP generate revenues that are for most of them auto-compounded into the LP token value (Swap fees generated by the pool), but the $PENDLE incentive must be claimed.

To claim the PENDLE reward, the holder must [call `redeemRewards`](https://etherscan.io/address/0x17be998a578fd97687b24e83954fec86dc20c979#writeContract#F6) from the PendleMarketV3 contract, to get the reward in tokens returned [by `getRewardTokens`](https://etherscan.io/address/0x17be998a578fd97687b24e83954fec86dc20c979#readContract#F11)
The contract provided in the links above (0x17be998a578fd97687b24e83954fec86dc20c979) are from the PendleLP-pufETH market, [which is a supported strategies](https://docs.loopfi.xyz/the-protocol/looping-leveraged-restaking/supported-strategies) in LoopFi.

The issue is that the rewards are based on the active balance of the user it can be seen in Pendle contracts [from the `PendleGauge::_redeemRewards` function](https://github.com/pendle-finance/pendle-core-v2-public/blob/7b3d0eb9362fd7f4e861d58856e2ac2262367c3d/contracts/core/Market/PendleGauge.sol#L38-L48)

```solidity
    /**
     * @dev Since rewardShares is based on activeBalance, user's activeBalance must be updated AFTER
        rewards is updated
     * @dev It's intended to have user's activeBalance updated when rewards is redeemed
     */
    function _redeemRewards(address user) internal virtual returns (uint256[] memory rewardsOut) {
        _updateAndDistributeRewards(user);
        _updateUserActiveBalance(user);
        rewardsOut = _doTransferOutRewards(user, user);
        emit RedeemRewards(user, rewardsOut);
    }
```

This means that when users will deposit PendleLPs in the vault, the reward will be assigned to the vault for the time being until users withdraw the PendleLPs.
If the Vault has no function to retrieve the \$PENDLE rewards, then these rewards are lost, representing a loss of revenue.

More informations can be found in the Pendle Finance documentation:

https://docs.pendle.finance/Developers/Oracles/HowToIntegratePtAndLpOracle#handling-of-pendle-lps-rewards
>Handling of Pendle LP's rewards:
>Holding Pendle LP tokens will generate PENDLE incentives and potential reward tokens (like WETH for LP for GLP pool)
>**The money market contracts will need to redeem these rewards by calling the `redeemRewards` function and implement logic to distribute these rewards to their users**

https://docs.pendle.finance/FAQ#do-i-have-to-stake-my-lp-to-earn-rewards
> Do I have to stake my LP to earn rewards?
> **No, you do not have to stake LP tokens**. All LP token holders will receive their fair share of the rewards.

https://docs.pendle.finance/FAQ#what-do-i-receive-in-return-for-providing-liquidity
>What do I receive in return for providing liquidity?
> - Swap fees generated by the pool
> - **PENDLE incentives**
> - Protocol incentives emitted by the underlying asset (e.g. $COMP, $AAVE)

## Impact
[Loss of matured yield](https://docs.code4rena.com/awarding/judging-criteria/severity-categorization#loss-of-yield-as-high) that could have been earned by protocol through PENDLE claiming, and shared to users and treasury.
[Example here](https://app.pendle.finance/trade/pools/0xa54fc268101c8b97de19ef3141d34751a11996b2/zap/in?chain=ethereum&zpi=true&view=info), for an active PufETH pool with \$44M TVL, the daily \$PENDLE rewards are estimated at ~512 PENDLE, or \$1500USD at PENDLE current price ($2.83)
LoopFi will probably not hold a entire pool, but this gives a good estimate for a specific TVL amount. Also, as the mechanism are based on leveraging LP, we can imagine achieving pretty high TVLs for LoopFi.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Implement a function to claim reward tokens from PendleLP holding [as explained here](https://docs.pendle.finance/Developers/Oracles/HowToIntegratePtAndLpOracle#handling-of-pendle-lps-rewards)
You could take inspiration from Equilibria which implemented such mechanism in its [PendleProxy](https://github.com/eqbtech/equilibria-contracts/blob/1359a56853df014b960eb8c2d645b9e76c33ca97/PendleProxyBaseUpg.sol#L96) where users claim their share of rewards through the [PendleBooster](https://github.com/eqbtech/equilibria-contracts/blob/1359a56853df014b960eb8c2d645b9e76c33ca97/PendleBoosterBaseUpg.sol#L411-L424) contract.
Another solution would be to automatically swappe PENDLE rewards to the underlying token, but this still require to redeem the rewards as show before.
