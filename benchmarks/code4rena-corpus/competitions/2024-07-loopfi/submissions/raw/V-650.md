# Vulnerability details

## Impact

`borrow()` is an external function of CDPVault.sol without whenNotPaused. In case where the governance wants to stop all activity, they still can't stop transferring tokens. Without the whenNotPaused modifier, attackers can continue to borrow assets, potentially draining the protocol's reserves leading to irreparable financial damage.

## Proof of Concept

Even after the protocol is paused, an attacker can continue to call the borrow() function because it does not have the whenNotPaused modifier and it is a public function. They repeatedly borrow more assets, draining the protocol’s reserves.
