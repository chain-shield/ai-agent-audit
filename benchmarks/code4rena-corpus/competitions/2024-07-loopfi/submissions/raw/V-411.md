# Vulnerability details

## Impact
No slippage protection upon liquidating

## Proof of Concept
Users can liquidate others by calling `liquidatePosition()`. The issue is that there is no slippage protection as to the amount they would actually receive from the liquidation. The price received from the oracle is the thing responsible for determining the collateral the liquidator will receive. Imagine the following scenario:
1. Bob calls `liquidatePosition()` when price of collateral is 1000$
2. Price grows to 1200$ which makes the collateral he can take smaller
```solidity
uint256 takeCollateral = wdiv(repayAmount, discountedPrice);
```
3. The user is still liquidatable and Bob's transaction goes through after a delay causing him to receive a smaller collateral than expected

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Add slippage
