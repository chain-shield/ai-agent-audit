## Brief Summary

`repayCreditAccount()` logic can lead to incorrect debt reduction when the `repaidAmount` parameter exceeds `type(uint128).max`. This problem arises from the conversion of `repaidAmount` to `uint128` before updating the `_totalDebt.borrowed` and `cmDebt.borrowed` variables.
