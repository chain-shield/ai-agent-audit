S-297
Lack of expiration check
levi_104
00
Sufficient
383d ago
Finding description and impact

According to the annotation, after the farm expires, anyone can close it and create a new farm.

Only the farm creator or the owner of the contract can close a farm, except if the farm has expired, in which case anyone can close it while creating a new farm.

But there is no check here to see if it has expired. It must be the owner of the farm or the owner of the contract

    ensure!(
        farm.owner == info.sender || cw_ownable::is_owner(deps.storage, &info.sender)?,
        ContractError::Unauthorized
    );
Proof of Concept
The expiration date of a farm is the 15th of this month；
On the 16th of this month, a non owner user attempted to close this farm and create a new farm, but failed because he was not the owner.
Recommended mitigation steps

Consider adding time expiration checks
