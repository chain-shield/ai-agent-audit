Risk of Unclaimed Rewards Misappropriation on Farm Closure
won
00
Sufficient
383d ago
Finding Description and Impact
Root Cause

The close_farms function in the Mantra Dex farm manager contract contains a critical vulnerability in how it handles unclaimed rewards during the farm closure process. Specifically, the function deducts claimed rewards from the total farm assets and transfers the remaining unclaimed rewards directly to the farm owner. This behavior creates a structural flaw with significant security implications:

Transfer of Unclaimed Rewards:

Upon closure, all rewards that users have not claimed are transferred directly to the farm owner instead of being allocated for users. This leads to permanent loss of user rewards.

Exploit Potential:

A farm owner can close the farm prematurely and misappropriate user rewards. The exploit is inherent in the current implementation and does not rely on external conditions or speculative scenarios.
Impact

Financial Loss for Users:

Users who have not claimed their rewards lose their rightful assets.

Rug Pull Risk:

Farm owners can exploit the system by deliberately closing farms prematurely to misappropriate unclaimed rewards, effectively executing a rug pull.

Centralization Risk:

The farm owner gains undue control over unclaimed assets, enabling abuse.

Protocol Trust Erosion:

User trust erosion could lead to reduced liquidity and lower engagement with the protocol.
Proof of Concept
Vulnerable Code

The issue lies in the close_farms function, as seen in the repository:

FARMS.remove(storage, &farm.identifier)?;

farm.farm_asset.amount = farm.farm_asset.amount.saturating_sub(farm.claimed_amount);

if farm.farm_asset.amount > Uint128::zero() {
    messages.push(
        BankMsg::Send {
            to_address: farm.owner.into_string(),
            amount: vec![farm.farm_asset],
        }
        .into(),
    );
}

All unclaimed rewards are transferred to the farm owner without any mechanism for users to retrieve them later. The function makes no effort to preserve or reallocate these rewards for the users.

Pseudocode Description
Pre-Condition:
another (pre-existing position) and another2 (post-farm creation position) hold unclaimed rewards.
Farm is funded by the owner with 4,000 uusdy.
Farm Closure:
close_farms transfers all unclaimed rewards to the farm owner.
Post-Condition:
Both another and another2 cannot claim their rewards.
Farm owner absorbs the rewards meant for users.
Recommendation and Mitigation

Implement a pending_reward system to ensure unclaimed rewards remain accessible to users after farm closure. Key features include:

During closure, unclaimed rewards are stored in a pending_rewards mapping.
Users can claim rewards via a claim_pending_rewards function within a configurable grace period (e.g., 30 days).
Post-grace period, unclaimed rewards can be allocated to protocol reserves or managed by governance.

By adopting this mechanism, Mantra Dex can ensure fairness, mitigate risks of misappropriation, and align with best practices in Dex protocols.

Conclusion

The close_farms function creates a severe risk of financial loss for users due to mismanagement of unclaimed rewards. The recommended fixes address this issue by ensuring unclaimed rewards are either distributed to rightful owners or safely stored for future claims. Implementing these measures will significantly enhance the protocol's security and fairness.
