Impact

The lack of invariant enforcement allows malicious actors to manipulate prices within the pool by providing liquidity in imbalanced proportions.

The issue mentioned in F-119 (and its duplicates), judged as Medium, is basically an impact of the issue reported here. The only difference is that #F-119 didn't catch the true root cause entirely.
The root cause in #F-119 according to the report is:

When tokens are provided in different proportions relative to the pool's current reserves, the excess tokens from the higher proportion are effectively donated to the pool. This way, users can lose tokens when providing liquidity with disproportionate amounts.

That's basically another formulation of saying that XYK invariant is not enforced, just less accurate.

In ideal implementation, like implemented in Uniswap and as this report highlights, there can be no excessive tokens donated to the pool since the pool is the one that calculates the amounts in of both reserves and returns any excessive to the user.

So, the true root cause of F-119 is that XYK invariant is not enforced, as this report highlights.

I believe the severity of this report and its duplicates should be Medium, following the same reasoning of #F-119
