S-156
Improper penalty distribution logic caused farm owners to not receive their rightful share of profits.
Usagi
00
Sufficient
383d ago
Finding description and impact

When handling scenarios where users trigger an Emergency Unlock, the protocol charges a penalty fee (total_penalty_fee) to the user, which is expected to be distributed as follows:

50% (owner_penalty_fee_commission) allocated to the respective farm owners (farm_owners).
The remaining 50% allocated to the contract's fee collection address (fee_collector).

However, when calculating the profit share for each farm owner (penalty_fee_share_per_farm_owner), the program applies to_uint_floor(), truncating the fractional part of the share to an integer.

If the penalty fee amount is small and the number of farm owners is large, this can result in each owner’s share being less than 1, which gets truncated to 0.
In such cases, no distribution is made to farm owners, and the entire penalty fee is allocated to the fee_collector.

Proof of Concept

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-11-mantra-dex/blob/main/contracts/farm-manager/src/position/commands.rs#L366-L370

Consider the following scenario:

total_penalty_fee = 100
PENALTY_FEE_SHARE = 50%
farm_owners.len() = 60

The calculation for owner_penalty_fee_commission is as follows:
owner_penalty_fee_commission = 100 * 50% = 50

let penalty_fee_share_per_farm_owner = Decimal::from_ratio(
    owner_penalty_fee_commission,
    unique_farm_owners.len() as u128,
)
.to_uint_floor();

penalty_fee_share_per_farm_owner = 50 / 60 ≈ 0.833...

Using to_uint_floor(), penalty_fee_share_per_farm_owner becomes 0.

When penalty_fee_share_per_farm_owner = 0, the program enters the else branch:

penalty_fee_fee_collector = total_penalty_fee;

The entire total_penalty_fee is allocated to the fee_collector, and farm owners receive no penalty fee distribution.

This issue arises when the penalty fee amount is too small or the number of farm owners is too large.
