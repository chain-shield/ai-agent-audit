S-274
`PoolFees.extra_fees` are stucked in the contract
Egis_Security
67
Sufficient
383d ago
Finding description and impact

Pool creator can define extra_fees:

   /// A list of custom, additional fees that can be defined for specific use cases or additional
   /// functionalities.


Later on the swapped they are decreased from the swapped amount in the pool.

fn get_swap_computation(
    return_amount: Uint256,
    spread_amount: Uint256,
    fees_computation: FeesComputation,
) -> Result<SwapComputation, ContractError> {
    let return_amount = return_amount
        .checked_sub(fees_computation.swap_fee_amount)?
        .checked_sub(fees_computation.protocol_fee_amount)?
        .checked_sub(fees_computation.burn_fee_amount)?
        .checked_sub(fees_computation.extra_fees_amount)?;


However, there is no logic to transfer them to any account, or to save them in the contract storage, so an authorized party can withdraw them later. This results in having those fees frozen in the contract.

Recommended mitigation steps

Introduce mechanic to transfer additional fees, or remove them from the swap calculation.
