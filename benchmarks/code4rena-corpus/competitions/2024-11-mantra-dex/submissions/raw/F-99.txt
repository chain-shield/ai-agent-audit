The issue is that this config.farm_manager_addr can be modified,
if the user sees a high reward farm_manager_addr and submits provide_liquidity(),
but when executed, it is changed to a lower rewards, then the user's LP will be locked for an unlocking_duration and the reward will be much lower than expected.

Example:
Suppose currently: config.farm_manager_addr = 0x123 = [ 5000 usdc rewards / per epoch]

alice see config.farm_manager_addr = [ 5000 usdc / per epoch] , then submit transaction provide_liquidity(unlocking_duration = 100 days)
owner change config.farm_manager_addr = 0x456 = [ 100 usdc rewards / per epoch]
alice's transaction executes, and succeeds.
alice's LP is locked for 100 days ,but only gets a very small reward, much less than `[5000 usdc rewards / per epoch]'
Impact

User's LP is locked for a long time, but the rewards are very small, not at all as expected.

Recommended mitigation steps
provide_liquidity() with the argument expect_arm_manager_addr.
Check that it is equal to config.farm_manager_addr.
