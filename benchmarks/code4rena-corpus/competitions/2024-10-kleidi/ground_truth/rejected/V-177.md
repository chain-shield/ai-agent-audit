## Brief Summary

The **InstanceDeployer** contract is a crucial component of the Kleidi protocol, responsible for creating new wallet instances. However, a critical bug in the `createSystemInstance` function prevents the successful deployment of system instances. This issue lies in the construction of the signature used in the Safe's `execTransaction` function, which fails due to incorrect encoding, thereby blocking instance creation. Without functional instance creation, the Kleidi system is rendered unusable, as it heavily relies on this process to onboard users and create secure wallets.
