## Brief Summary

Cleanup operation modifies critical state variables (`_liveProposals`) after making external calls, violating the checks-effects-interactions pattern. The `isOperationExpired()` check and subsequent state modifications are not atomic, creating a window for reentrancy. An attacker can reenter during the cleanup process before state variables are updated, potentially executing multiple cleanups on the same operation.
