## Brief Summary

Timelock contract fails to track and validate cumulative value transfers across execution paths, enabling potential arithmetic overflow attacks through multiple transaction executions, as the contract executes value transfers without maintaining or validating the total accumulated value. In `_execute()`, each call transfers value independently, without considering previous transfers. This design allows the total transferred value to silently overflow when multiple transactions are executed sequentially or in batch.
