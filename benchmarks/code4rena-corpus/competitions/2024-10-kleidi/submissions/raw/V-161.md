# Vulnerability details

## Description

In the `Timelock` contract, there is no check to ensure that the `hotSigners` are the same as the owners of the `safe`.

According to the comment on [#L:52](https://github.com/solidity-labs-io/kleidi/blob/0d72b6cb5725c1380212dc76257da96fcfacf22f/src/Timelock.sol#L52), "only safe owners can execute whitelisted calldatas," but the constructor and the `grantRole()` functions do not verify that hot signers are also safe owners.

The separation of the `owners` array for the safe contract and the `hotSigners` array for the timelock contract introduces the risk that non-safe owners could be granted the `HOT_SIGNER_ROLE` and have ablity to execute whitelisted calldatas.

This weakens the intended security model of the system by allowing external or unauthorized addresses to gain the ability to execute privileged actions.

## Impact

Non-safe owners may be granted the `HOT_SIGNER_ROLE`, enabling them to execute whitelisted calldatas without being authorized.

## Proof of Concepts

- In [Timelock constructor](https://github.com/solidity-labs-io/kleidi/blob/0d72b6cb5725c1380212dc76257da96fcfacf22f/src/Timelock.sol#L264-L309), hot signers are granted `HOT_SIGNER_ROLE`, without any checks to ensure they are also safe owners as it's stated in [#L:52](https://github.com/solidity-labs-io/kleidi/blob/0d72b6cb5725c1380212dc76257da96fcfacf22f/src/Timelock.sol#L52).

- the safe `owners` array and `hotSigners` array [are separate](https://github.com/solidity-labs-io/kleidi/blob/0d72b6cb5725c1380212dc76257da96fcfacf22f/src/TimelockFactory.sol#L6-L18), and nothing ensures in [TimelockFactory](https://github.com/solidity-labs-io/kleidi/blob/0d72b6cb5725c1380212dc76257da96fcfacf22f/src/TimelockFactory.sol#L36-L55) that `owners[i] == hotsigner[i]` when Deploying Timelock.

- The [grantRole()](https://github.com/solidity-labs-io/kleidi/blob/0d72b6cb5725c1380212dc76257da96fcfacf22f/src/Timelock.sol#L759-L769) function does not validate the `account` against the list of safe owners to ensure only safe owners can have `HOT_SIGNER_ROLE`, this allows an arbitrary address to be assigned `HOT_SIGNER_ROLE`, even if they may not be one of the safe owners.

## Recommended mitigation

- Add a check in the [Timelock constructor](https://github.com/solidity-labs-io/kleidi/blob/0d72b6cb5725c1380212dc76257da96fcfacf22f/src/Timelock.sol#L271C5-L309) to ensure that the account we are assigning `HOT_SIGNER_ROLE` to, is one of the safe owners.
  
- remove `hotSigners` array from [DeploymentParams](https://github.com/solidity-labs-io/kleidi/blob/0d72b6cb5725c1380212dc76257da96fcfacf22f/src/TimelockFactory.sol#L11) struct as member and simply pass safe `owners` array on deployment of Timelock, to assign `HOT_SIGNER_ROLE` to each safe owner.
  
- add a check in `Timelock::grantRole()` function to ensure [account](https://github.com/solidity-labs-io/kleidi/blob/0d72b6cb5725c1380212dc76257da96fcfacf22f/src/Timelock.sol#L763) is one of the current owners of safe.
