Issue Title: Reuse of Fixed Salt in CREATE2 Deployments Leading to Predictable Contract Addresses

Issue Type: Design/Implementation Flaw

Severity Level: Medium

Impact:

The SystemDeploy contract utilizes a fixed salt value (0x3afe...) for deploying multiple distinct contracts using the CREATE2 opcode. While CREATE2 allows for deterministic contract addresses based on the deployer’s address, the provided salt, when reused across different contract deployments, can lead to predictable contract addresses. This predictability can facilitate targeted attacks, such as address precomputations or front-running, especially if the deployed contracts hold significant value or control critical system functionalities. Moreover, if the bytecode of any of the deployed contracts can be influenced or is not sufficiently unique, it may increase the risk of address collisions, potentially leading to contract replacement or takeover scenarios.

Vulnerability Details:

The contract reuses the same salt for deploying multiple distinct contracts via CREATE2. Although CREATE2 considers both the deploying address and the salt to determine the contract address, reusing the same salt across different contract deployments can lead to address predictability. If an attacker can predict or influence the bytecode of the contracts being deployed, they might exploit this predictability for malicious purposes.

Proof of Concept (PoC):
[PoC](https://gist.github.com/0xtr3/285d162616583402c533d429134a3cae)

In the above example, an attacker deploys a MaliciousContract using the same salt as the SystemDeploy contract. If the bytecode is predictable or can be influenced, the attacker can precompute the address and deploy their contract to clash with legitimate deployments.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
	1.	Use Unique Salts: Generate unique salts for each contract deployment, possibly incorporating nonces or other unique identifiers to ensure that each deployment has a distinct salt.
	2.	Dynamic Salt Generation: Instead of using a fixed salt, derive salts dynamically based on contextual data, making it harder for attackers to predict or reproduce the salt values.
	3.	Audit Bytecode Uniqueness: Ensure that each contract deployed with CREATE2 has unique and non-overlapping bytecode to prevent address collisions.
	4.	Access Control: Restrict the deployment functions to authorized entities to minimize the risk of malicious deployments.
