# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept
A hardcoded salt makes it easier for an attacker to predict or repeat the contract deployment across different chains or environments. If a malicious actor can predict the contract addresses, they can front-run or preempt future deployments. You could add some chain-specific entropy or a dynamic salt generation method (such as incorporating the current chain ID or a timestamp).

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
1.Use a Dynamic Salt: Consider generating the salt based on dynamic data, such as the block timestamp, sender address, or a nonce. For example:
bytes32 public salt = keccak256(abi.encodePacked(block.timestamp, msg.sender));
2. Replay Protection: Additionally, implementing mechanisms to prevent replay attacks across different chains could help mitigate this risk. You can add chain-specific data to the salt generation.
