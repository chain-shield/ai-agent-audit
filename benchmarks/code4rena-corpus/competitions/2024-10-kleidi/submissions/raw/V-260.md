# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept

AddressCalculation.sol uses the CREATE2 opcode to calculate the address of the proposed wallet instance.

However, this approach imposes risks due to the fact that the CREATE2 allows anyone to anticipate the address to be occupied by a contract and, in a front-running scenario, occupy it with a malicious contract.

`calculateAddressUnsafe` function can potentially make the whole setup vulnerable. 

By front-running such a transaction a malicious actor could deploy their own contract at the anticipated address, which might expose the system to a range of possible security risks.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Consider taking measures to prevent potential address pre-computation attacks. 

For example, introduce a mechanism by which on-chain contracts need to confirm the identity of the contract created at the precomputed address before any operations can be performed with it. 

Additionally, have a process in place that allows the contract at the precomputed address to be securely updated if it doesn't meet expected conditions. 

Also, consider making the `calculateAddressUnsafe` function internal or private to not expose its functionality.
