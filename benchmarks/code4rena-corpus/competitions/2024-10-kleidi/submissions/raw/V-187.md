# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept
Incorrect Validation Risk: The contract attempts to validate the bytecode of deployed contracts, but this validation fails for contracts with immutable variables. If the system relies on incorrect or incomplete validation, it could miss critical issues with deployed contracts.

Risk:
False Confidence in Validation: The system might assume the deployment was successful and the contracts are correct when, in fact, critical security contracts could have been incorrectly deployed or manipulated.

Impact: This could lead to broken security mechanisms, unvalidated contracts, or incorrect contract addresses, opening up the system to potential attacks or failures.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
1. Use Alternate Validation Methods: For contracts with immutable variables, instead of checking the bytecode, you can validate critical immutable variables or perform interface checks to ensure the contract behaves as expected. Example:
require(factory.timelockDuration() == expectedDuration, "Incorrect timelock duration");
2. Use Event Logs: Consider emitting specific events during contract deployment and validate those event logs to ensure the contract deployment process completed successfully.
