# Vulnerability details

## Description

calldata removal related functions like [removeCalldataCheck()](https://github.com/solidity-labs-io/kleidi/blob/0d72b6cb5725c1380212dc76257da96fcfacf22f/src/Timelock.sol#L857-L869), [removeCalldataCheckDatahash()](https://github.com/solidity-labs-io/kleidi/blob/0d72b6cb5725c1380212dc76257da96fcfacf22f/src/Timelock.sol#L871-L934) and [removeAllCalldataChecks()](https://github.com/solidity-labs-io/kleidi/blob/0d72b6cb5725c1380212dc76257da96fcfacf22f/src/Timelock.sol#L936-L952), do not currently inherit the `onlySafe` modifier, instead they inherit `onlyTimelock` modifier.

This means that, if a malicious calldata is uncautiously added by safe owners through timelock, the Safe owners must wait for the minimum timelock delay before they can delete the calldata since they have to schedule an operation for Timelock for calldata removal.
During this waiting period, a hot signer could potentially use the malicious calldata to harm the system before the safe/timelock is able to act.

## Impact

In the event of a malicious or unintentional calldata addition due to safe owners carelessness, safe owners are unable to delete the calldata immediately due to the timelock's delay. This leaves the system exposed to potential exploitation by hot signers, who could use the calldata and cause harm to the system before it is removed.

## Proof of Concepts

1. A malicious calldata is added to the system uncautiously by safe owners through timelock with `addCalldataCheck()` function.
2. Safe owners schedule a operation to timelock to delete the calldata but they are forced to wait for the timelock delay.
3. During this waiting period, a hot signer exploits malicious calldata and cause harm to the system (e.g., by draining funds) by executing it with `executeWhitelisted()` function.

## Recommended mitigation

Ensure that all functions responsible for deleting calldata inherit the `onlySafe` modifier, allowing safe owners to delete potentially harmful calldatas immediately without waiting for the timelock delay. 

also introduce new process where calldatas must get approved by timelock after they get added. meaning hot signers cannot use those newly added calldatas until they're approved. this creates 2 step process for calldata authorization and enhances the security of Timelock.
