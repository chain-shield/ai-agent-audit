# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept
The executeRecovery() function relies on block.timestamp for critical time-based comparisons to check whether the recovery process is ready to proceed. Since miners can manipulate block.timestamp, this introduces the risk of artificially delaying or hastening the recovery.

### Impact
An attacker, potentially a miner, could manipulate the block timestamp to initiate a recovery too early or delay it beyond the intended timeframe, resulting in either unauthorized recoveries or undue delays in legitimate recoveries.

### Code Snippet

'''solidity

require(block.timestamp > recoveryInitiated + delay, "Recovery not ready");
'''

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Use block.number for timing comparisons or rely on an off-chain oracle for more accurate and tamper-resistant time checks. Consider adding multiple conditions to mitigate potential timestamp manipulation.
