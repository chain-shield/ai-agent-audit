# Vulnerability details

## Impact
During the transfer of Gas assets or ERC20 for EVM outbound txn in [_transferOutV5](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-thorchain/blob/e3fd3c75ff994dce50d6eb66eb290d467bd494f5/chain/ethereum/contracts/THORChain_Router.sol#L216) if the token isn't ETH it will update the `vaultAllowance` mapping first before actually transferring the function, tho it doesn't check the proper amount got transferred or not.
As mentioned in the [docs](https://dev.thorchain.org/concepts/delays.html#outbound-delay) THORChain throttles which could potentially lead to halting a txn up to 720 blocks approx for 1 hour.
In this time period, the ERC20 token value could change hence leading to the wrong accounting of assets internally.
