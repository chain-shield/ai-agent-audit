# Vulnerability details

## Impact
When `transferOutAndCallV5`,`transferOutAndCall` or their batch versions are called, internal functions will be executed. Then they will call aggregator's `swapOut` or `swapOutV5`. However they pass `type(uint).max` as deadline to the `swapRouter`.

## Proof of Concept
Passing `type(uint).max` or `block.timestamp` as a deadline, means that swap can happen at any time in the future. There is a `amountOutMin` which will prevent slippage, but still there should be a proper deadline to prevent loss of tokens.

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Add another parameter `deadline` that will be used by `swapOut` and `swapOutV5`
