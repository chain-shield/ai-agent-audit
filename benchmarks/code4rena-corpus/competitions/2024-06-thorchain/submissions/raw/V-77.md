# Vulnerability details


## Impact
After `swapOutV5` fails, the assets are transferred to the aggregator contract.
Since anyone can call the `THORChain_Aggregator.swapOutV5` function, an attacker could exploit this by swapping the failed assets by changing the `recipient`, `amountOutMin`, or `toAsset`.

## Proof of Concept
An attacker can monitor transactions for `swapOutV5` and initiate a frontrun to cause the swap to fail.
Once the swap fails and the assets are transferred to the aggregator, the attacker can call `swapOutV5` to steal the assets without transferring the asset.

## Tools Used

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Use token approvals instead of direct transfers for `swapOutV5` and update the relative contract.
After it fails, reset the approval to zero.
