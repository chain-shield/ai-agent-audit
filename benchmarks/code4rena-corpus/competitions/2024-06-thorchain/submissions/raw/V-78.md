# Vulnerability details


## Impact
There is no validation to ensure that the sum of transferOutPayload.amount matches `msg.value`.
Consequently, both `transferOutV5` and `batchTransferOutV5` functions do not refund any overpaid native ETH to the `msg.sender`.
The attacker can steal this overpaid ETH by calling the `transferOutV5` function.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
It's recommended to validate if there is any remaining Ether and send it back to the `msg.sender` in the both of `transferOutV5` and `batchTransferOutV5` function.
