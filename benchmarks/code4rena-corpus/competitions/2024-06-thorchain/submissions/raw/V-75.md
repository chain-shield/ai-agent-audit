# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Since the `send` and `transfer` functions only forward 2300 gas - using them to send ETH could cause a receiving smart contract to be unable to receive it depending on the contents of their `fallback` or `receive` function. Additionally with EIP-2929 the gas cost of certain opcodes has increased which means they could increase again in the future and make this issue even more severe.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Use the `call` function with an appropriate amount of gas, CEI pattern and/or reentrancy protection instead of `send` or `transfer`.


## Assessed type

ETH-Transfer
