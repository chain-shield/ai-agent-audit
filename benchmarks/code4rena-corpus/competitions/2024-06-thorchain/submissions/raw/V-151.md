# Vulnerability details

## Impact
All the transfer out and call functions inside `THORChain_Router` are missing the deadline parameter. Deadline provides users the option to limit the execution of their transaction.
Without a deadline parameter, users can execute their transactions at unexpected times when market conditions are unfavourable.
Refer to [this issue](https://solodit.xyz/issues/m-03-missing-deadline-check-in-swap-functions-pashov-none-kekotron-markdown)

Users will miss positive slippage

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Add deadline parameter to all transfer out functions as well as in the `THORChain_Aggregator`
