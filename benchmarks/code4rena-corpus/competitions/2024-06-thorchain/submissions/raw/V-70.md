# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Some of the functions can accept both ERC20 and ETH at the same time, but you can send ETH even in the case of an ERC20 transfer, and there's no check to ensure that `msg.value == 0` - this could lead to losing ETH if the user accidentally sends it when trying to send ERC20.

## Proof of Concept
https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-thorchain/blob/e3fd3c75ff994dce50d6eb66eb290d467bd494f5/chain/ethereum/contracts/THORChain_Router.sol#L198-L205
Example:
1. Alice tries to transfer from ERC20
2. Because she previously specified msg.value > 0 for another trade - she saved her value and accidentally sent transferOut with msg.value greater than 0.
3. Even though the ERC20 transfer was successful - she lost her ETH that she sent by mistake.

## Tools Used
Manual review.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Add `require(msg.value == 0)` for ERC20 transfers.
