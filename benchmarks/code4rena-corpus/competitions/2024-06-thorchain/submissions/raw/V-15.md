# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Usage of deprecated send() can revert.
https://consensys.io/diligence/blog/2019/09/stop-using-soliditys-transfer-now/

## Proof of Concept
The .send() function intends to transfer an ETH amount with a fixed amount of 2300 gas. If the recipient implements a fallback function containing some sort of logic, this may inevitably revert, meaning the ETH forwarding will fail

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Use .call() instead to send ETH

ETH-Transfer
