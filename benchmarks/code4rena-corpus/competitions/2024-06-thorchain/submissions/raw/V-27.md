# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Anyone can call vulnerable methods that do ETH transfers/ERC20 transfers (transferOut/transferOutV5/transferOutAndCall), but according to the project's backend and overall logic, this violates its intentions to only parse logs related to logs. Otherwise you can create many fake event logs that would be parsed on the backend and affect the whole system.

  // Aggregator is matched to the last three digits of whitelisted aggregators
  // FinalToken, To, amountOutMin come from originating memo
