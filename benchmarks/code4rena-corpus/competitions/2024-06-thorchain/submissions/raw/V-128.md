# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In the whitelist of tokens its already mentioned that THORChain supports [UNI](https://gitlab.com/thorchain/thornode/-/blob/develop/common/tokenlist/ethtokens/eth_mainnet_latest.json?ref_type=heads#L2179), [COMP](https://gitlab.com/thorchain/thornode/-/blob/develop/common/tokenlist/ethtokens/eth_mainnet_latest.json?ref_type=heads#L1139).
These tokens have some weird behaviors when it comes to approvals and sending transactions:
- If the amt is more than `type(unit96).max` in case of UNI approval gets reverted.
- If the amt is more than `type(unit96).max` in the case of COMP, both transactions and approvals get reverted.
## Proof of Concept
[Weird-erc20(UNI & COMP)](https://github.com/d-xo/weird-erc20#revert-on-large-approvals--transfers)

## Tools Used
Manual review
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Either remove these tokens from whitelists or put custom modifiers which limits these token approvals and txn value.
