# Vulnerability details

## Impact
[transferOut](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-thorchain/blob/e3fd3c75ff994dce50d6eb66eb290d467bd494f5/chain/ethereum/contracts/THORChain_Router.sol#L185) lacks an expiry parameter due to which an EVM outbound txn can be throttled by the THORChain itself as mentioned in [thorchain docs](https://dev.thorchain.org/concepts/delays.html#outbound-delay) due to which malicious actor could potentially gain a decent arbitrage over the outbound txns, not to mention this will ruin the market value of THORChain among its users if they keep losing or gaining random funds due to throttling.

>Also not using a deadline in a [amm-interactive protocol](https://dev.thorchain.org/aggregators/evm-implementation.html#swapout) while using transfer is a bad practice as already mentioned [in this finding discussion.](https://github.com/code-423n4/2022-06-canto-v2-findings/issues/90#issuecomment-1214244667)
