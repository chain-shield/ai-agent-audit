# Vulnerability details

## Impact
If at some point a contract with logic inside of the fallback is used and consumes more than 2300 gas, the `.send()` will revert and make the `THORChain_Router` unusable for this contract.
This makes a recipient unable to receive its funds if his fallback uses more than 2300 gas.
Since there is a `nonreentrant` modifier on the functions, you can use `.call()`
[Refer to here](https://solodit.xyz/issues/m-05-use-of-send-may-revert-if-the-recipients-fallback-function-consumes-more-than-2300-gas-code4rena-forgotten-runes-forgotten-runes-warrior-guild-contest-git)

Inability to use `THORChain_Router` with fallback that consumes more gas or receive gas asset if recipient

## Proof of Concept
