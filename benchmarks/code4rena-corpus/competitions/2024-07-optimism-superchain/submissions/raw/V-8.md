# Vulnerability details

## Impact
If the array lengths are not verified to ensure equality of lengths, operations performed by the user may not be executed completely due to a mismatch in the number of items iterated over compared to the number of items provided in the second array. This can lead to inconsistencies in the execution of functions, which bad actors can exploit to disrupt or manipulate processes within the contract.

## Proof of Concept
1. Deploy contract *'PreimageOracle.sol'*.
2. Call the *'challengeLPP'* function with *'_preStateProof'* and *'_postStateProof'* arrays of different lengths.
3. Observe that functions may fail or not behave as expected due to unverified array length mismatches.
