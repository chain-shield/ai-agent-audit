# Vulnerability details

## Impact
A valid claim could be considered invalid 

## Proof of Concept
the affected code works with the assumption that is an attack against the parent is valid then the parent must be invalid, however is there is a no attack claim but there is a defence claim the the logic fails 
taking an example 
```
bob makes an invalid root claim x 
alice make a valid claim attack at y
alice is not attacked because her claim is valid
charlie makes a valid defence claim to alice attack at z 
assuming no claim is made against z 
z will resolve with its counteredBy still be address(0)
hence when y is being resolved the countered by of y will be set to the claimant of z and the bond will be distributed to the countered by hence charlie 
also as a result bobs invalid claim can now resolve validly 
```
these should not be possible as a defence claims the parent claim is valid
