# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The incentive to challenge a wrong proposal in the PreimageOracle.sol system is undermined due to frontrunning. Malicious actors can monitor the mem-pool and front-run challengers' calls to `challengeLPP()` or `challengeFirstLPP()` by monitoring and submitting the challenger's own correct inputs by increasing the gas fee. This allows them to claim the bond intended for the challenger who incurred off-chain costs for generating such proofs. The problem is exacerbated when the malicious proposers themselves front-run these challenges, using the challenger's valid input proofs to submit their call and receive the bond(since the bond is sent to the `msg.sender`). This disincentivizes legitimate challenges and allows malicious proposers to continue their harmful activities unchecked, as they can repeatedly thwart any opposition.

You can see that in the end the bond is sent to the person that calls the function regardless of who it is, given challengers are incentivized to generate valid proof and challenge an invalid proposal(which will incur costs), with the incentive being the bond, however with the state of the blockchain being visible/decentralized anyone can siphon the challenger's TX proof against a proposal and paste it with a higher gas fee to gain the bond, however, the system will still work as the invalid proposal is always attacked, and optimism is kept secure, but given that the malicious proposer can also siphon the challenger's TX and gain back their bonds, in such a case the malicious proposers can never be challenged since anyone can take the incentive away from the challengers and if they malicious proposers are challenged, they can smartly gain back their bonds back to avoid any consequence of submitting an incorrect proposal, thereby breaking every incentive to challenge an invalid proposal in PreimageOracle.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
The mitigation is a bit difficult given the state of the preimage however one good idea would be to ensure that a proposer can never challenge their own proposal but the proposer can use another address to take the bonds, so the best solution here:
-  Use a commit-reveal scheme for the challengers and their proof.
