# Vulnerability details

## Summary

0xaliyah
arabgodx

title: Unsafe Admin Control in DisputeGameFactory

1. the `setImplementation` and `setInitBond` functions in the `DisputeGameFactory` contract can have significant impacts on the protocol's governance and functionality and lacking sufficient safeguards

## Vulnerability Detail

Attack Path
1. attacker with owner privileges can arbitrarily change the implementation of dispute games `setImplementation` and potentially deploying concerning changes
2. the initial bond required for dispute games was set to an arbitrary value `setInitBond` and disrupting the games' economics

## Impact

1. High Impact, Medium Likelihood
2. the lack of safeguards (no time-lock or two-step process) allows immediate and potentially malicious changes, affecting the protocols' stability and trust
