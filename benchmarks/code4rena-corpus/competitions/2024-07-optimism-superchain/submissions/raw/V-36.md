# Vulnerability details

As per the [specs](https://specs.optimism.io/fault-proof/stage-one/fault-dispute-game.html#resolution), one of the invariants:
- `truth percolates upwards towards the root claim by resolving each individual sub-game bottom-up.

The outcome of subgames moves upwards in the gametree by resolving the subgames from bottom to up.

This invariant, however, does not always hold up.

Let's first look at the first scenario where this invariant does hold up, a regular resolution of a FDG:
- Alice plays vs Bob and Alice makes the final step that is successfully executed on `MIPS.sol`, which makes her the winner.

Now,  let's look at the second scenario, where this invariant does **not** hold up:
- Alice plays vs Bob, Alice made the last move and it has **not** been countered.
- If we take a look at the `resolveClaim()` function, we see the following check:
```javascript
        // INVARIANT: Cannot resolve a subgame unless the clock of its would-be counter has expired
        // INVARIANT: Assuming ordered subgame resolution, challengeClockDuration is always >= MAX_CLOCK_DURATION if all
        // descendant subgames are resolved
        if (challengeClockDuration.raw() < MAX_CLOCK_DURATION.raw()) revert ClockNotExpired();
```
- This means that if clock is expired, the game is resolved.
- Alice calls `resolveClaim(8,0)` - the subgame has successfully been resolved.
- Now, we can move upwards and resolve the other subgames.
- However, Charlie enters the party and calls `step()`, this call should **not** be able to succeed, the deepest subgame has already been resolved.
- Now, the game result becomes skewed, Bob will end up winning some subgames, even though Alice was the winner.

As we can see, even though Alice was the winner, the payout got skewed, she got played out of:
- `1074074857400000000000-1014074857600000000000=59999999800000000000` rewards stolen by Bob because of Charlie stepping.

This means that the variant that is set in the specs does not hold up. Furthermore, it should not be possible for a party to step when a game has been resolved on the most bottom depth, since from that resolve on, we go upwards and resolve the rest.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
When the lowest level is resolved, do not allow steps.
