## Brief Summary

When the game reaches MAX_DEPTH the last claim in the game can be challanged with a step. If it's an honest challanger's turn in the game, they submit a step transaction with state data that matches the prestate commitment. If the state data indicates that the step requires a local preimage, they need to also call addLocalData before they call Step, so that the data is available for the Mips Step function.
