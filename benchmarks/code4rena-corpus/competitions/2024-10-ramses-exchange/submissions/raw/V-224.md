# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept
cachePeriodEarned calculates and optionally caches the rewards a specific liquidity position earned during a given period by determining its in-range time and multiplying this by the total reward allocation for that period, relative to all positions' in-range times. If caching is enabled, it records the calculated in-range time to save gas on future calculations.


[https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-10-ramses-exchange/blob/4a40eba36bc47eba8179d4f6203a4b84561a4415/contracts/CL/gauge/GaugeV3.sol#L278-L335](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-10-ramses-exchange/blob/4a40eba36bc47eba8179d4f6203a4b84561a4415/contracts/CL/gauge/GaugeV3.sol#L278-L335)

However, using WEEK << 96 as the denominator instead of the sum of all positions' periodSecondsInsideX96 values does introduce a problem in accurately calculating rewards.

[https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-10-ramses-exchange/blob/4a40eba36bc47eba8179d4f6203a4b84561a4415/contracts/CL/gauge/GaugeV3.sol#L321-L325](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-10-ramses-exchange/blob/4a40eba36bc47eba8179d4f6203a4b84561a4415/contracts/CL/gauge/GaugeV3.sol#L321-L325)

Scenario
Assume:

Position A is in range for only 1 day out of the 7-day week.
Position B is in range for 2 days out of the week.
Position C is in range for 1 day out of the week.

Current implementation:  (Using WEEK << 96 as Denominator):

Position A: 143 tokens
Position B: 286 tokens
Position C: 143 tokens
Total Distributed: 572 tokens
Unallocated: 428 tokens

Correct implementation: 
(Using Total In-Range Time as Denominator):

Position A: 250 tokens
Position B: 500 tokens
Position C: 250 tokens
Total Distributed: 1,000 tokens


Impact: 1） wrong calculation for the earning for each position; 2) no tokens might not be allocated to any staker at all. 

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

To ensure a fair distribution of rewards, we should:

Accumulate Total In-Range Time: Track the sum of periodSecondsInsideX96 for all positions within a period. Use This Sum as the Denominator: When calculating rewards, replace WEEK << 96 with this accumulated total in-range time. This ensures that each position’s rewards are proportional to its in-range time relative to all positions.

Revised Code Example
Here's how to adjust the code:

```javascript
amount = FullMath.mulDiv(
    tokenTotalSupplyByPeriod[period][token],
    periodSecondsInsideX96,
    totalPeriodSecondsInsideX96 // Use the total in-range time for all positions
);
```

This adjustment makes the reward calculation more accurate and fair, as each position’s rewards are based on its actual contribution to the pool's liquidity within the period.
