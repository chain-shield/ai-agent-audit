# Vulnerability details

**Description**: The contract heavily relies on the `AccessManaged` contract for access control. This means that a single entity (the access manager) has significant control over critical functions, posing a centralization risk that goes against the principles of decentralization in DeFi.

**Vulnerable Code**:
```solidity
function setFeeProtocol(uint8 _feeProtocol) external override restricted {
    require(_feeProtocol <= 100, FTL());
    uint8 feeProtocolOld = feeProtocol;
    feeProtocol = _feeProtocol;
    emit SetFeeProtocol(feeProtocolOld, _feeProtocol);
}
```

**Recommendation**: 

1. **Short Term**:
   - Implement a combination of timelock and multi-signature wallet (e.g., 2/3 or 3/5) to mitigate risks:
     - Add a time-lock with a reasonable latency (e.g., 48 hours) for all privileged operations.
     - Assign privileged roles to multi-signature wallets to prevent single points of failure.
   - Publish a medium/blog post sharing the timelock contract and multi-signer addresses for transparency.

2. **Long Term**:
   - Implement a combination of timelock and DAO to enhance decentralization and transparency:
     - Maintain the time-lock with a 48-hour latency for privileged operations.
     - Introduce a DAO/governance/voting module to increase user involvement in decision-making.
   - Publish a medium/blog post detailing the timelock contract, multi-signer addresses, and DAO information.

3. **Permanent**:
   - Consider renouncing ownership permanently and never reclaiming privileged roles.
   - Alternatively, remove risky functionalities entirely from the contract.
