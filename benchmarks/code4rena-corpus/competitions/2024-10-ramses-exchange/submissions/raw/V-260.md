# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept
**DESCRIPTION:**
In the `RamsesV3Pool.sol` contract, the `flash` function allows users to perform flash loans. However, there is no check to ensure that the liquidity pool has a sufficient balance (liquidity) before allowing a `flashloan`. Flash loans enable users to borrow assets without collateral, as long as they repay the loan within the same transaction. If the pool's liquidity falls below a certain threshold and flash loans are still allowed, this could result in:

**IMPACT:**
- Depletion of Pool Liquidity: Allowing flash loans without checking the pool's liquidity could deplete the pool’s liquidity, leaving the pool with an insufficient balance for handling regular swaps or liquidity provider (LP) withdrawals.
- Financial Exploits: Malicious actors could exploit this vulnerability by draining liquidity through repeated or excessively large flash loans, leaving little liquidity for genuine users.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Introduce a Liquidity Threshold Check:
Implement a check in the flash function to ensure that the liquidity in the pool is above a minimum threshold before a flashloan can be taken. This ensures that the pool retains enough liquidity for normal operations.
