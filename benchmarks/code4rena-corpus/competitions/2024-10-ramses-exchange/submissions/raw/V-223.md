# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept

GaugeV3.notifyRewardAmount() does not roll over unclaimed emission due to no stakers from previous period, these emission will be stuck in the gauge forever.


The Voter-Gauge mechanism is used to incentivize users to provide liquidity to different pools with the following workflow: 

1) Voting on Gauges: Users vote on their preferred gauges, determining how much of the total emissions each gauge will receive based on the votes cast.

2) Emissions Allocation by Voter: The Voter contract calculates each gauge’s share of emissions based on its vote weight relative to the total votes. The Voter contract transfers the allocated emissions to each gauge and calls notifyRewardAmount to set up each gauge’s reward rate for the period.

3) Gauge Streaming Rewards to Stakers: Each gauge calculates a reward rate based on the emissions received, setting a fixed rate for the entire period. Stakers accumulate rewards proportionally to their stake in the gauge over time, with their rewards increasing based on how long and how much they’ve staked.

4) Claiming Rewards:
Stakers can claim their accumulated rewards at any time.
The Gauge contract calculates and transfers the earned emissions to the staker, streaming rewards continuously based on their share of the total stake.
This system incentivizes stakers and aligns reward distribution with voting preferences, ensuring that emissions flow from governance decisions to active participants.

In its current implementation, a gauge will still receive emissions even though there is no staker for a particular period. When new emissions are allocated for a new period, the previous unclaimed emissions (due to no stakers) will not be rolled to the new period. These emissions will be stuck in the gauge forever.

```javascript
function notifyRewardAmount(
        address token,
        uint256 amount
    ) external override pushFees lock {
        require(isReward[token], "!Whitelisted");
        IRamsesV3Pool(pool)._advancePeriod();
        uint256 period = _blockTimestamp() / WEEK;

        uint256 balanceBefore = IERC20(token).balanceOf(address(this));
        IERC20(token).safeTransferFrom(msg.sender, address(this), amount);
        uint256 balanceAfter = IERC20(token).balanceOf(address(this));

        amount = balanceAfter - balanceBefore;
        tokenTotalSupplyByPeriod[period][token] += amount;
        emit NotifyReward(msg.sender, token, amount, period);
    }
```

For example, suppose for the previous period = 100, 10,000 ether emissionTokens are allocated to GuageA and there is no stakers for that period. No the Voter calls notifyRewardAmount() to allocate rewards 10,000 ether for the new period 101, unfortunately, the unclaimed 10,000 ether emissionTokens will not roll over to period 101. Period 101 will have emission tokens in the amount of 10,000 ether not 20,000 ether!


## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Consider the case of no stakers period and roll over leftover emissionTokens to the new period as follows (need to consider the boundary case of first period):

```diff

    uint256 balanceAfter = IERC20(token).balanceOf(address(this));
```
