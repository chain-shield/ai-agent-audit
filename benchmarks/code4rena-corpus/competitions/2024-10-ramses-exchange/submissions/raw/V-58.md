### Impact
The RamsesV3Pool contract is vulnerable to a reentrancy attack because the _advancePeriod function is declared as public and can be called externally. 
Since _advancePeriod is also used in the advancePeriod modifier, an attacker can exploit this by reentering functions that use this modifier, such as mint, burn, and swap. 
This can lead to inconsistent state changes, unexpected errors, and potentially allow the attacker to manipulate the pool’s state or extract funds improperly.

### Proof of Concept
An attacker can exploit this vulnerability by reentering the contract through the public _advancePeriod function during a callback.
