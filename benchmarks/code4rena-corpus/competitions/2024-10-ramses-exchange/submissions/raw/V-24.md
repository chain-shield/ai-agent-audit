# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept

In the [swap](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-10-ramses-exchange/blob/236e9e9e0cf452828ab82620b6c36c1e6c7bb441/contracts/CL/core/RamsesV3Pool.sol#L544) function, the following line of code poses a risk of integer overflow when calculating protocol fees:

```solidity
state.protocolFee += uint128(delta);
```

#### Explanation
- The variable `delta` is derived from the calculation of the protocol fee based on the `feeAmount` and `cache.feeProtocol`. Specifically, it is computed as:

```solidity
uint256 delta = (step.feeAmount * cache.feeProtocol) / 100;
```

- Since `delta` is of type `uint256`, it can hold larger values than `uint128`. If the resulting `delta` exceeds `2^128 - 1`, casting it directly to `uint128` can lead to an unexpected overflow. This means that instead of incrementing the `protocolFee` correctly, the calculation may wrap around, resulting in a negative or incorrect fee value.

### Impact
- **Financial Discrepancies**: If the protocol fee is miscalculated due to overflow, it could lead to incorrect distributions of fees among liquidity providers or the protocol itself. This could ultimately result in financial losses for users and damage to the protocol's integrity.

### Summary
- **Bug**: The risk of integer overflow when casting `delta` to `uint128`.
- **Cause**: The calculation of `delta` can exceed the maximum value of `uint128` if `step.feeAmount` and `cache.feeProtocol` produce a large enough product.
- **Likelihood**: This issue is likely to occur under conditions of high trading volume or high fees, where `step.feeAmount` may reach significant values.
- **Impact**: Miscalculation of protocol fees can lead to financial discrepancies, impacting users and the overall trust in the protocol.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Utilize the SafeCast library from OpenZeppelin, which provides safe type conversion functions that automatically revert the transaction when the conversion is not possible.
