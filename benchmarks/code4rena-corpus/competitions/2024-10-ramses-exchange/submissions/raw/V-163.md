# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept
Lack of Parameter Validation: The initialize() function lacks validation checks for the _treasury and _voter addresses. Without these checks, the contract could be initialized with zero addresses or addresses that don’t meet protocol standards.
Here’s how this vulnerability could manifest:
feeCollector.initialize(address(0), address(0)); // Invalid initialization, breaking functions that use `voter` and `treasury`

Impact: Initialization with invalid addresses could disrupt treasury fee collection, making the contract ineffective or leading to contract malfunction.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Add non-zero address checks within initialize():
require(_treasury != address(0), "Invalid treasury address");
require(_voter != address(0), "Invalid voter address");
