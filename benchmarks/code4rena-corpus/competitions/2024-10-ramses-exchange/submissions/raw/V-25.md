# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept

The vulnerability in the [swap](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-10-ramses-exchange/blob/236e9e9e0cf452828ab82620b6c36c1e6c7bb441/contracts/CL/core/RamsesV3Pool.sol#L512-L524) function stems from the reliance on the `sqrtPriceX96` value obtained from the `slot0` of the Uniswap V3 pool. This value can be manipulated through various attack vectors such as MEV and **Flash Loans**, particularly via sandwich attacks. This manipulation can lead to substantial losses for users executing swaps, as the actual price at which the swap occurs may deviate significantly from the expected price.
- **Functionality**: The `swap` function is designed to execute a token swap while adhering to certain price constraints. Specifically, it ensures that the `sqrtPriceLimitX96` lies within specified bounds relative to the current `sqrtPriceX96`.

- **Code Snippet**:
  ```solidity
  require(
      zeroForOne
          ? sqrtPriceLimitX96 < slot0Start.sqrtPriceX96 && sqrtPriceLimitX96 > TickMath.MIN_SQRT_RATIO
          : sqrtPriceLimitX96 > slot0Start.sqrtPriceX96 && sqrtPriceLimitX96 < TickMath.MAX_SQRT_RATIO,
      SPL()
  );
  ```
  In the above code, the function checks if the `sqrtPriceLimitX96` falls within acceptable bounds. However, since `sqrtPriceX96` is sourced directly from `slot0`, it is susceptible to manipulation before the execution of the swap.

- **Risk**: An attacker can exploit this vulnerability by manipulating the price data via MEV strategies. They could place their transactions strategically to push the price in their favor before executing their own swaps, thereby incurring losses for the users who rely on the `sqrtPriceX96` pulled from the pool.

### Impact
- **Financial Loss**: Users may execute swaps at manipulated prices, resulting in unfavorable trade outcomes. The attacker profits by buying at a lower price and selling at a higher price immediately after the user’s transaction, leading to a financial loss for the user and potentially significant market distortions.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
It is advisable to implement a **Time-Weighted Average Price (TWAP)** mechanism to retrieve the price. The TWAP function smooths out the price over a defined period, making it less susceptible to manipulation and sudden spikes due to MEV strategies.
