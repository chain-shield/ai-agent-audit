# Vulnerability details

## Impact
UniswapV3 slot0 should not be used to get price as it can be manipulated

## Vulnerability Details
The use of slot0 to obtain sqrtPrice is heavily discouraged as it is easy to manipulate. slot0 represents the current price rather a time weighted price. An attacker can therefore use flash loans to shift the slot0 by doing large swaps on Uniswap.

## Proof Of Concept
As seen below, slot0 is used to get the price of the amount when modifying a position, which is easy to manipulate.
[RamsesV3Pool.sol#L241](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-10-ramses-exchange/blob/236e9e9e0cf452828ab82620b6c36c1e6c7bb441/contracts/CL/core/RamsesV3Pool.sol#L241)
