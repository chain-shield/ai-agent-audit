# Vulnerability details

## Summary 
In the `NonFungiblePositionManager::increaseLiquidity.sol` function allows any user to increase the liquidity of a position without verifying whether the caller is the owner of the position's `poolId` or has been approved for that position. This opens the contract to a security vulnerability where unauthorized users can increase the liquidity by 0 and claim the accrued fees of the position, potentially allowing them to steal fees that rightfully belong to the owner of the position.
## Proof of Concept
Vulnerability Overview:
The increaseLiquidity function does not check whether the caller owns the position (identified by poolId) or is an approved account for that position.
- An attacker can trigger a liquidity increase of 0, which does not modify the liquidity but still allows the attacker to collect any accrued fees for that position via the PoolManager

**Exploitation Scenario:**
- Joe holds a position with poolId 10 and has accrued fees from trading activity.
- Eve (an attacker) calls the increaseLiquidity function, specifying poolId 10 and setting the liquidity amount to 0.
The NonFungiblePositionManager calls modifyLiquidity on the PoolManager, which calculates the liquidity delta (positive due to the accrued fees).
Eve receives the positive liquidity delta, effectively stealing Joe’s accrued fees

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Ownership or Approval Check:
Ensure that only the owner of the poolId or an approved account can call increaseLiquidity for a given position. This prevents unauthorized users from manipulating other users' positions and stealing their fees.
