# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept
 Insecure Reward Tracking Mechanism in collect Function: The collect function calculates the tokensOwed0 and tokensOwed1 based on the feeGrowthInside0LastX128 and feeGrowthInside1LastX128 variables. An attacker could manipulate these variables by repeatedly calling the function to artificially increase their reward balance without contributing additional liquidity.

Proof of Concept: An attacker could call collect multiple times to update the tokens owed calculation without actual liquidity contributions, resulting in unearned rewards.

Impact: This vulnerability could lead to significant financial losses for other liquidity providers as the attacker would be rewarded disproportionately, draining the reward pool.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
1. Rate-Limiting or Accrual Frequency Controls: Limit the frequency with which users can claim rewards or base reward accruals on validated liquidity.
2. Ensure Accurate Reward Calculations: Use a robust mechanism for reward accrual that cross-verifies with actual liquidity deposited.

Example Code with Fix
