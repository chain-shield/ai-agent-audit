# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept
 Insecure Use of periodSecondsInsideX96 Calculation: periodSecondsInsideX96 is calculated based on data from IRamsesV3PoolState, an external contract. If the data is manipulated, the rewards could be skewed unfairly.

Proof of Concept: If periodSecondsInsideX96 returns manipulated data, it will impact the cachePeriodEarned calculation:
// External contract manipulates periodSecondsInsideX96
await externalContract.modifyPeriodSecondsInsideX96();
Impact: This could lead to an incorrect distribution of rewards, either under or over-paying users.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Add validations to ensure data accuracy when relying on external contracts or add fallback calculations.
