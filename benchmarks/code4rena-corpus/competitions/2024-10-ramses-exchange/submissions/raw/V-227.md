# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept
- Miners can manipulate `block.timestamp` within a range of around ±15 seconds, enough to alter the period value in calculations involving liquidity or fee growth.
= By adjusting the timestamp, an attacker could potentially influence the `secondsPerLiquidityOutside` values, impacting the liquidity and fees within the pool.
- For example, a miner could manipulate timestamps to force a liquidity calculation into a different period, causing rewards to skew in favor of a certain participant.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
- To replace `block.timestamp` with `block.number`, you can calculate the period based on block numbers and approximate the weekly period calculation by assuming an average block time. For example, if the blockchain has an average block time of 13 seconds, a week can be approximated by dividing the current block number by 46523 (7 days × 24 hours × 60 minutes × 60 seconds / 13).
