# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept
In the `cross` function in the `Tick` library, there is an arithmetic operation between the variables `feeGrowthGlobal0X128` of type `uint256` and `info.feeGrowthOutside0X128` of type `uint128`. This operation is performed without ensuring that the two variables have compatible data types, which can lead to calculation errors and transaction failures.

## Impact
Since Solidity 8.0+ has fixed the underflow/overflow issue, users trying to perform a swap will experience transaction failure, resulting in service disruption to the affected functions.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Use `SafeCast` to convert `feeGrowthGlobal0X128` to `uint128` before performing the reduction.
```
-       info.feeGrowthOutside0X128 = feeGrowthGlobal0X128 - info.feeGrowthOutside0X128;
+       info.feeGrowthOutside0X128 = SafeCast.toUint128(feeGrowthGlobal0X128) - SafeCast.toUint128(info.feeGrowthOutside0X128);
```
