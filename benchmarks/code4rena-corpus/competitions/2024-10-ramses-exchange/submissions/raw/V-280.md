# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept
When you use the `mint` function of a pool, it calls the `_modifyPosition` function, which in turn calls the `Position._updatePosition` function to update the position accordingly. 

The `_updatePosition` is external without any verification on who called it. Hence, anyone can call it and change positions. This can cause the following issues: 

* Changing `tickLower`, `tickUpper`, or `liquidityDelta` can claim, or reduce fees by creating fake positions or adjusting existing ones to benefit from incorrect fee calculations
* Changing `secondsPerLiquidityPeriodX128` could allow the manipulation of reward distribution. 
* It can also disrupt the pool's behavior, by incorrect liquidity accounting. 

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Verifying whether the account that calls the function has the correct permissions to do so.
