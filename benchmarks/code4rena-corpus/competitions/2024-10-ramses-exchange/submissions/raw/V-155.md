# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept
 Potential for Address Collision: The use of create2 with a salt based on the token addresses and tickSpacing could lead to address collisions if the same parameters are reused. for example,
Assume two users, Eva and David, want to create a liquidity pool for the same token pair with identical tickSpacing. If both invoke the deploy function concurrently or in quick succession, the last deployment will take precedence, causing David's pool to overwrite Eva's, leading to confusion and potential financial losses for both users.

Impact: Address collisions can lead to unintended consequences where a new pool replaces an existing pool, potentially disrupting liquidity and trading activities.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
1. Implement logic to ensure unique parameters are used for each deployment.
2. Consider using additional parameters or a unique identifier in the salt.
