# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept
When we try to initialize one pool, we will initialize the new periods and update the `startTick`.
But the problem is that we call _advancePeriod() at first and then initialize `$.slot0`. It means that the $.slot0 is not initialized when we call _advancePeriod(). This will cause the `_newPeriod.startTick` is zero.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
We should initialize $.slot0 and then trigger `_advancePeriod`.
