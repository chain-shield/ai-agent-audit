# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept
Unchecked Arithmetic in Reward Calculation: Arithmetic operations within increaseLiquidity and decreaseLiquidity are not safeguarded against overflow and underflow, potentially allowing for unpredictable results when managing token balances. Solidity 0.8+ does revert on overflow/underflow, but unchecked blocks still exist in parts of the code.

Proof of Concept: Suppose there is an overflow or underflow when updating tokensOwed0 and tokensOwed1. This could allow attackers to mint rewards or affect liquidity amounts improperly, causing loss or incorrect accounting for other users.

Impact: Unchecked arithmetic can result in unexpected token balances, ultimately leading to a lack of integrity in the liquidity positions and rewards distribution.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
1. Remove unchecked blocks: Ensure that any block where arithmetic operations are performed does not use unchecked.
2. Use SafeMath for specific complex operations: Although Solidity 0.8+ has safe arithmetic by default, use SafeMath explicitly if calculations are complex or easy to misinterpret.
