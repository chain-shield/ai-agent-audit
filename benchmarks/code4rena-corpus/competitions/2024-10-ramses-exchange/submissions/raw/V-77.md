# Vulnerability details

**Title**: Vulnerability in Fee Calculation Due to Liquidity Manipulation

**Description**:
The `update` function in the Position contract allows users to modify their liquidity positions. This function is also responsible for calculating and updating accumulated fees. However, the current implementation may allow users to manipulate their liquidity just before fee calculations to minimize their fee obligations.

**Impact**: 
1. Users could potentially reduce their fee obligations by manipulating their liquidity just before fee calculations.
2. This could lead to unfair fee distribution among liquidity providers.
3. The protocol might experience reduced fee collection, affecting its sustainability and revenue model.

**Proof of Concept**:
1. A user observes that fee calculation is imminent.
2. They quickly reduce their liquidity to a minimal amount.
3. Fees are calculated based on this reduced liquidity.
4. Immediately after fee calculation, the user restores their original liquidity.

**Recommendation**: 
1. Implement a time-weighted average liquidity calculation for fee assessments.
2. Add a cooldown period for significant liquidity changes before they affect fee calculations.
3. Consider implementing a minimum time lock for liquidity positions to prevent rapid manipulations.


This vulnerability is rated as medium severity because it could potentially impact the fairness and economic stability of the protocol. While it requires specific timing and may not always be profitable to exploit, the potential for fee evasion warrants careful consideration and mitigation.
