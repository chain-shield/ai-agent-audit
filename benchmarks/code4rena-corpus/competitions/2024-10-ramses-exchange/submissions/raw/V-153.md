# Vulnerability details

## Proof of Concept
Access Control Vulnerability: The contract uses a simple require statement to check whether the caller is the RamsesV3Factory. If the factory address is compromised or misconfigured, unauthorized users could potentially deploy pools.

Impact: An attacker could exploit this vulnerability to deploy malicious or invalid pools, leading to potential financial losses for users who interact with these pools. They could also cause confusion within the ecosystem by creating pools that are not legitimate.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
1. Implement a modifier to encapsulate the access control logic.
2. Regularly audit the factory contract to ensure its integrity.
