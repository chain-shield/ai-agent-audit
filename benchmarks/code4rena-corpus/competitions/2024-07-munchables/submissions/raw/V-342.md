# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Owners of NFTs (Munchables) can stake their tokens inside the `LandManager` contract and receive rewards in the process. Through the `transferToUnoccupiedPlot()`, they can change the `plotId` for their token. The function however does not correctly update the struct stored in the storage inside the `toilerState` mapping. Inside the function, the only variable updated from the `ToilerState` struct is `latestTaxRate`. The `plotId` is not updated, which could lead to a loss of rewards/schnibbles for the owner of the NFT/s.

## Proof of Concept
Let's say a landlord by the name of Alice has 10 plots available based on their `LockedWeightedValue`. The owner of a NFT with id of 3 (Bob) decided to stake his token to plot №10 (plotId = 9).

1. Bob calls `stakeMunchable(0xAl1ce, 3, 9)`.

2. After one week, Alice withdraws some of her locked funds and based on the calculation inside `_getNumPlots()`, she now has only 5 available plots -> a reduction from 10 to 5. Let's also assume that Bob called `farmPlots()` and got his rewards for the 1 week during he had staked.

3. Bob decides to change the plot for his NFT from 9 to 4, because plot №5 is still NOT occupied and has the lowest taxes of the newly available plots. In the process of calling `transferToUnoccupiedPlot()`, he will claim schnibbles before the fn executes (Due to the `forceFarmPlots` modifier). However only `toilerState[tokenId].latestTaxRate` storage variable will be updated and NOT `toilerState[tokenId].plotId`. The change (The update of `plotId`) will be reflected only for the `plotOccupied` mapping on the following lines.

4. After one more week, Bob calls `farmPlots()`, he will claim once again the according rewards for staking for one week, however because `toilerState` mapping has NOT been updated properly, the `plotId` will still be 9 and the condition on line 258 (https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-munchables/blob/main/src/managers/LandManager.sol#L258) will pass, marking the `ToilerState` as "dirty".

5. After 2 more weeks, Bob decides to withdraw his `Munchable`. The `forceFarmPlots` will be executed prior to the function body, but since the `toiler` is dirty, the condition on line 251 (https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-munchables/blob/main/src/managers/LandManager.sol#L251) will pass and no rewards will be given/awarded for the last 2 weeks.

As a conclusion, Bob received only rewards for the 2 out of the total 4 weeks during which he had staked his token, leading to loss of rewards.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Update the `plotId` storage variable -> `toilerState[tokenId].plotId = plotId;`
