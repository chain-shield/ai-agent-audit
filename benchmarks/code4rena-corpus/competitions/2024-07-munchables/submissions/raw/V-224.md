# Vulnerability details

## Impact
transferToUnoccupiedPlot() does not reset the toiler.dirty flag whenever we occupy a new "clean" slot deeming the stake void of future rewards even though the conditions are met.

## Proof of Concept
If we have Alice as staker
Bob as landlord:

1. Bob has 15 plots to begin with.
2. Alice calls stakeMunchable(landlord = Bob, tokenId = 1, plotId = 10) and stakes on plotId 10.
3. Bob's plots get reduced to 8
4. Alice farmPlots() gets "dirty"
5. Alice transferToUnoccupiedPlot() plotId:5, but toiler.dirty is still set to true.
6. After some time Alice decides to farmPlots and sees that no rewards are accrued on her account.
7. Alice loses rewards even though conditions are met.

```solidity
  function _farmPlots(address _sender) internal {
  ```
