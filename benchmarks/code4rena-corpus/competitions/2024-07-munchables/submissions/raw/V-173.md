# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Basic premise landlords lock certain amount of funds and because of that based on the amount they locked they they get plots that they can rent to other users that want to stake and therefore they payback tax to the landlords.

If landlords at some point withdraw their locked funds they should not be landlords anymore therefore users that stake on their plots should unstake and not pay any taxes to the past landlords.

Or if they withdraw certain amount they should have fewer plots available.

The issue arise in [_farmPlots](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-munchables/blob/94cf468aaabf526b7a8319f7eba34014ccebe7b9/src/managers/LandManager.sol#L258) which is used by renters to pay their landlords amount the other thing, specificially with this lines of code :
```js
            if (_getNumPlots(landlord) < _toiler.plotId) {
                timestamp = plotMetadata[landlord].lastUpdated;
                toilerState[tokenId].dirty = true;
            }
```
If landlord withdraw his funds and decide not to lock anymore this will always be true and he would still receive taxes even if he should not because of the function design and because his address would still remain in `plotMetadata` mapping, it will just use last known information when metadata is updated in `lastUpdated`.
## Proof of Concept
Bob lock his funds and became a landlord. His plot metadata is created and stored in `plotMetadata` mapping with his landlord address. Alice decides to stake on Bob's plot. She start staking and paying taxes regulary and everything is in order.
After some time Bob withdraw his locked funds and is not landlord anymore. So this will always be true even if `_getNumPlots` return 0:
```js
            if (_getNumPlots(landlord) < _toiler.plotId) {
                timestamp = plotMetadata[landlord].lastUpdated;
                toilerState[tokenId].dirty = true;
            }
```
It will just use timestamp with `lastUpdated` time and Alice would pay to Bob even if he is not the landlord anymore and that plot don't exist (or should not).

After that the `lastToilDate` would get updated with timestamp as we can see here:
```js
            toilerState[tokenId].lastToilDate = timestamp;
```
 The specific value for this specific case would be: `timestamp = plotMetadata[landlord].lastUpdated` so in the next attempt for Alice to pay she would not pay anything to Bob but also she would not earn any `schnibbles` for their munchables. Basically she would stake for nothing.

After that she can unstake from Bob's plot but she will be at loss one tax she payed to Bob that is not landlord anymore and she would not get her staking reward (schnibbles) next time.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Track when user is not landlord anymore, the renters should automatically be unstacked from his plots, they should pay what they owe him for that time and his plot metadata and his address should be removed from `plotMetadata` mapping. In that way the protocol will prevent renters to pay taxes to non existing landlords and to not receive rewards for staking their munchables.
