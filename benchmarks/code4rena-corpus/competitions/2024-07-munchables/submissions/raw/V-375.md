# Vulnerability details

## Impact
If LandLord have larger `taxRate` than `MAX_TAX_RATE` this will make him get more `taxes` than the currently Set Max which will benefit him beneficialy leading to more schnibbles taken for him from users staked at him

If LandLord have smaller `taxRate` than `MIN_TAX_RATE`, this will give him more advantages to attract other users to stake on him and not on other new LandLords that are forced by the new `MIN_TAX_RATE`


## Proof of Concept

The problem lies in the fact that the way the `TaxRate` is reconfugered doesn't backward affect the LandLords who already have set their `TaxRate` 

This makes it like the LandLord now has the choice to update his `currentTaxRate` to `MAX` or `MIN` if it only benefates him directly or indirectly. This even can be grouped with the fact that LandLord can frontRun the `_reconfigure` to his intent

**For example:**
- LandLord is having his taxRate at 75e16 `MAX`
- trusted entity updates the `MAX_TAX_RATE` to 60e16
- `plotMetadata[landlord].currentTaxRate` is yet still have 75e16 as its value.

Same thing for `MinTaxRate` that can be gamed as mentioned in Impact section

In fact the LandLord can have another scenario:
- LandLord is having his taxRate at 50e16(not MAX) while `Max` is at 75e16
- trusted party sumbit a txn to updates the `MAX_TAX_RATE` to 60e16
- LandLord sees this as an opportunity that won't be made again and frontruns that trusted entity txn and set his `taxRate` at 75e16 `MAX`
- The txn of the trusted entity gets executed and update the `MAX` to 60e16
- `plotMetadata[landlord].currentTaxRate` is yet still have 75e16 as its value.



## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
to prevent the LandLord from beneficially having his rate to be outdated we can make condition `if` (which is `?`) whenever we use the `plotMetadata[landlord].currentTaxRate` in any function

when ever we set the `currentTaxRate` to any staker during `stakeMunchable` or `_farmPlots` we check if `currentTaxRate` < `MIN_TAX_RATE`? if true then we set `latestTaxRate` of that staked `tokenId` to `MIN_TAX_RATE` and also the same check for if > `MAX_TAX_RATE`
