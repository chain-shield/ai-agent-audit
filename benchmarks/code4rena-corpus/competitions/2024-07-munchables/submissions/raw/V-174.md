# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In [stakeMunchable](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-munchables/blob/94cf468aaabf526b7a8319f7eba34014ccebe7b9/src/managers/LandManager.sol#L131) there is no check if the NFT is already staked which will allow users to stake same munchables to different landlords and exploit protocol for their gains.

There is no check that will check if user NFT is already staked and that will allow it to stake same NFT multiple times with different landlords.
## Proof of Concept
Bob stake to Alice and provide her `landlord` address and `plotId` and his NFT `tokenId`. Bob being the exploiter of the protocol decide to stake to Jon with the same `tokenId` as the previous landlord Alice, he just now provide new (Jon's) `landlord` address and `plotId`. Bob repeats that again and again...
