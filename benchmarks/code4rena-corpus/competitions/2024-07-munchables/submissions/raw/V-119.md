# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Any token holder can stake their token on one of the plots owned by landlords. Landlords have a taxation system that is bounded by the `MIN_TAX_RATE` and `MAX_TAX_RATE` variables. A landlord can change the tax rate at any time using the `updateTaxRate` function. The problem arises when a user wants to stake their token on a plot owned by a landlord. The landlord can frontrun the stake transaction by updating the tax rate (which could be set to the `MAX_TAX_RATE`, for example), and because the update tax rate transaction is in the block before the stake transaction, the user will stake their token at a higher tax rate than they initially expected, losing potential rewards from staking. As we can see, there's no protection against this attack - (https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-munchables/blob/94cf468aaabf526b7a8319f7eba34014ccebe7b9/src/managers/LandManager.sol#L135-L143)

## Proof of Concept
Let's look at the example:

1. Alice wants to stake her token and decides to stake it on the plot with a low tax rate (it has the MIN_TAX_RATE at the moment).
2. Alice sends the stake transaction, thinking that she will get a good deal because the tax rate is really low.
3. The landlord sees the stake transaction in the mempool and frontruns it by sending an `updateTaxRate` transaction with the MAX_TAX_RATE value.
4. Alice's transaction is mined, and she stakes her token with the highest tax rate.

## Tools Used
Manual review.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Add slippage protection to `stakeMunchable` function.
