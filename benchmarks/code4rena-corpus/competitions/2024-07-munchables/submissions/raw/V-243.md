# Vulnerability details

## Vulnerability details
A landlord can monitor toiler staking activities. Upon detecting a toiler's intention to stake, the landlord can front-run the toiler's `stakeMunchable()` transaction by quickly executing `updateTaxRate()` to set the tax rate to its maximum value. This action deceives the toiler into staking under unfavorable conditions. After the toiler's `stakeMunchable()` transaction is completed, the landlord can again execute `updateTaxRate()` to restore the tax rate to its original value, continuing to deceive subsequent toilers.  

## Impact
Financial Loss: Toilers may incur significant financial losses by staking under manipulated, unfavorable tax rates.  

## Proof of Concept
Prerequisites:  
- Assume the current tax rate is 10%  
- The maximum allowed tax rate is 50%  
- A Toiler is preparing to stake 1 NFT on a Landlord's plot  

Attack Steps:  

1. The Landlord monitors the mempool and detects the Toiler's `stakeMunchable()` transaction.  

2. The Landlord immediately sends an `updateTaxRate()` transaction to increase the tax rate to the maximum of 50%.  
   This transaction is set with a higher gas price than the Toiler's to ensure it's processed first by miners.  

3. Miners process the transactions in the following order:  
   a. The Landlord's `updateTaxRate()` transaction is executed, changing the tax rate to 50%.  
   b. The Toiler's `stakeMunchable()` transaction is executed, staking based on the 50% tax rate.  

4. The Toiler's stake is completed, but they only receive rights to 50% Schnibbles instead of the expected 90%.  

5. The Landlord immediately sends another `updateTaxRate()` transaction to restore the tax rate to 10%.  

6. Final Result:  
   - The Toiler loses rights to 40% Schnibbles
   - The Landlord gains an additional 40% Schnibbles in revenue  
   - The system's tax rate returns to normal, ready to deceive the next Toiler  

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

1. Implement a time-lock mechanism for tax rate changes, ensuring that any modifications to the tax rate take effect after a set period (e.g., 24 hours).  

2. Modify the stakeMunchable() function to include an expectedTaxRate parameter. If the landlord's current taxRate is greater than this expected value, the transaction should revert. This change would look something like:  

```solidity
   function stakeMunchable(
        address landlord,
        uint256 tokenId,
        uint256 plotId，
        uint256 expectedTaxRate) external override forceFarmPlots(msg.sender) notPaused {
            .....
            require(plotMetadata[landlord].currentTaxRate <= expectedTaxRate, "Tax rate higher than expected");  
            // Existing staking logic  
   }  
```
   This modification allows toilers to specify their maximum acceptable tax rate, providing protection against unexpected rate increases.
