# Vulnerability details

## Impact
When a staker farms plots using the farmPlots() function, it sets the toilerState[tokenId].latestTaxRate to the landlord's updated tax rate [here](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-munchables/blob/94cf468aaabf526b7a8319f7eba34014ccebe7b9/src/managers/LandManager.sol#L292C4-L293C33). The issue is that a landlord can increase the tax rate once stakers are realized, thus charging them more when they farm. Since the tax rate is not fixed on stake, the staker would receive a higher tax rate than what they agreed upon before staking. This way the landlord can provide certain users with preferential treatment by charging higher or lower tax rates.

## Proof of Concept

1. User A stakes munchable [here](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-munchables/blob/94cf468aaabf526b7a8319f7eba34014ccebe7b9/src/managers/LandManager.sol#L131) after viewing the landlord's tax-rate to be 10e16 (10%). 

2. Landlord subsequently increases the tax-rate to 50e16 (50%) [here](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-munchables/blob/94cf468aaabf526b7a8319f7eba34014ccebe7b9/src/managers/LandManager.sol#L92).

3. User A farms plots and earns schnibbles. It also sets the latestTaxRate to toil on to be the currentTaxRate [here](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-munchables/blob/94cf468aaabf526b7a8319f7eba34014ccebe7b9/src/managers/LandManager.sol#L292C4-L293C33).

4. Next time the user A farms plots, they are charged 50% of their earned schnibbles. This was not expected by user A since the tax-rate agreed upon was 10% on stake.
