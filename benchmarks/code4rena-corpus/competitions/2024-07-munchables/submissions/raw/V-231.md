# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The `stakeMunchable` function in the `LandManager` contract currently checks `if (munchablesStaked[mainAccount].length > 10)` during staking. However, according to the whitepaper, each account can have a maximum of 6 Munchables, as stated in [“You can input up to 6 Munchables at any given time but can obtain more slots in your Snuggery by converting MUNCH points to slots.”](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-munchables/blob/main/guides/ARCHITECTURE.md). Therefore, it is recommended to modify this check to `if (munchablesStaked[mainAccount].length > 6)`.

## Proof of Concept
The `stakeMunchable` function in the `LandManager` contract checks `if (munchablesStaked[mainAccount].length > 10)` and throws an error if there are more than 10.

According to the whitepaper, each account can have a maximum of 6 Munchables, as stated in [“You can input up to 6 Munchables at any given time but can obtain more slots in your Snuggery by converting MUNCH points to slots.”](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-munchables/blob/main/guides/ARCHITECTURE.md). Therefore, a main account cannot stake more than 6 Munchables at any given time. As such, the current check `if (munchablesStaked[mainAccount].length > 10)` is invalid.
