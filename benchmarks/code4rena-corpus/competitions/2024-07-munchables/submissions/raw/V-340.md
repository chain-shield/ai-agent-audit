# Vulnerability details

## Impact
External call to updatePlayer() will fail causing most operations to fail

## Proof of Concept
At the end of the `_farmPlots()` function, the contract makes an external call to the updatePlayer() function in the accountManager contract:
```solidity
accountManager.updatePlayer(mainAccount, renterMetadata);
```
However, the updatePlayer() has an onlyConfiguredContract3() modifier which LandManager hasn't been added to yet, this will cause the call to updatePlayer() to revert causing most operations to fail.

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Either remove the call to the updatePlayer or add the LandManager to the onlyConfiguredContract3() modifier in the accountManager.
