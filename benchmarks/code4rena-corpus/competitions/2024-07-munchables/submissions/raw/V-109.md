# Vulnerability details

## Impact
When a user stakes their NFT with a certain tax rate, they expect to be charged that rate for the duration of their staking period. However, if the landlord can update the tax rate after the NFT has been staked, it can lead to unexpected and unfair charges. Specifically, if a user decides to unstake their NFT, they might be charged a higher tax rate than the one they initially agreed to. This can result in financial losses for users and reduce trust in the platform.

## Proof of Concept
1. Initial Stake:
Bob stakes his NFT (TokenID 1) on Alice's plot (Plot ID 120) with an initial tax rate of 10% (1e17).
2. Rewards Accumulation:
Time passes, and rewards accumulate.
3. Tax Rate Change:
Alice intentionally changes the tax rate for her plot to a higher value, such as 50% (5e17).
4. Bob Unstakes His NFT:
Bob decides to unstake his NFT and is charged the new, higher tax rate.
Alice ends up winning higher rewards due last minute change of tax rate.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Record Initial Tax Rate:
Record the tax rate at the time of staking in the toilerState structure.
```javascript
toilerState[tokenId].initialTaxRate = plotMetadata[landlord].currentTaxRate;
```
and use this initial tax in `_farmplots`
