# Vulnerability details

## Impact
This could lead to much higher fees paid by the user than anticipated and hence he is going to receive less rewards when farming

## Proof of Concept
1. Consider a current plot of a landlord with currentTaxRate = MIN_TAX_RATE (plotMetadata[landlord].currentTaxRate = MIN_TAX_RATE)
2. A user knows of its existence and decides to stake a Munchable on this specific plotId
3. The owner of the landlord address observes the mempool & sees the stake transaction
4. Then landlord frontruns it, updating taxRate (updateTaxRate) setting it to MAX_TAX_RATE

## Tools Used
- Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
- Consider implementing a mechanism that does not allow such big increases of the TAX_RATE (e.g. put some threshold value 5e16 or 10e16 max increase per transaction)
