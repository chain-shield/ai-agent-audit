# Vulnerability details

## Lines of Code
LandManager.sol#L299
SnuggeryManager.sol#L232-L233

## Description
`_farmPlots` is an internal function called to calculate and distribute rewards (called "schnibbles") for landlords and renters (who stake Munchable NFTs on landlord plots). It is used as a modifier (`forceFarmPlots`) in various functions within the `LandManager` contract, including: `farmPlots`, `stakeMunchable`, `unstakeMunchable`, and `transferToUnoccupiedPlot`.

In the current `_farmPlots` logic of `LandManager` contract, after calculating the `unfedSchnibbles` bonus for renters and landlord, the landlord's `lastPetMunchable` state will be updated to `block.timestamp`.

```
            landlordMetadata.lastPetMunchable = uint32(timestamp);
```

However, the `lastPetMunchable` state is also related to the logic of the petting feature, which is defined in the `SnuggeryManager` contract. And in the `pet` function of this contract, there is a condition for a player's petting behavior to be considered valid, which is that it must occur after a period of time > 10 minutes since the last petting.
```
        if (_petterPlayer.lastPetMunchable + 10 minutes > block.timestamp)
            revert PetTooSoonError();
```

Farm/stake/unstake/transfer_to_unoccupied logics (all of them use the `forceFarmPlots` modifier) in `LandManager` are active actions on the side of the renter, which makes landlords passive in petting another munchables because they have no control over when their own `lastPetMunchable` state is updated. Passive here means that, when the activities of a landlord's renters are performed too frequently (<10 minutes / action), all of that landlord's petting another munchable activities will be reverted with `PetTooSoonError` with no control over when they will succeed.

## Impact
Landlords will be unable to pet another player's munchable if `_farmPlots()` is invoked more frequently than every 10 minutes. This restricts the functionality and usability of the petting feature, preventing landlords (who have overactive renters) from gaining the fully benefits associated with petting.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Remove `lastPetMunchable` state update from `_farmPlots`:
Modify the `_farmPlots` function to avoid updating `lastPetMunchable` unnecessarily, ensuring that it only updates in `SnuggeryManager` contract logic, when required by certain conditions or intervals.
