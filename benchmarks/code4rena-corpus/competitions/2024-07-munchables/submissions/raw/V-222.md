# Vulnerability details

## Impact
When landlord's plots are reduced such that staker's munchable is staked outside of the availabilty zone, staker is not affected and could proceed with the game without farming plots, not losing rewards. If Bob's plots gets increased back to the staker's availabilty zone he can farm his rewards without being penalized.

## Proof of Concept
If we have Alice as staker
Bob as landlord:

1. Bob has 15 plots to begin with.
2. Alice calls stakeMunchable(landlord = Bob, tokenId = 1, plotId = 10) and stakes on plotId 10.
3. Bob's plots get reduced to 8
4. Alice proceeds to stake on plotId:10 without any penalties because she is not calling farmPlots
5. After some time Bob's plots are increased above 10
6. Alice unstakes with all the rewards accrued for the period.
   
With this scenario it is evident that we have timespan in which Alice is accruing rewards without that being part of the game mechanic.

Likelihood = low
Impact = high
Severity = medium

## Tools Used
Manual review
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Whenever there is an update of plot data we should check whether plots outside of the availability zone are affected and properly unstaked or toiler "dirtied".
