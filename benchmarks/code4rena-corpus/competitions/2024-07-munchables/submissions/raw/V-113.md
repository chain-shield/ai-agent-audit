# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Users can occupy the plot for a long time but do not allow the landowner to receive Schnibbles.

## Proof of Concept
background:
1. The user will occupy the landowner's plot, and then receive Schnibbles through the `_farmPlots` function. The Schnibbles will be distributed to the user and the landowner in proportion.
2. In the `_farmPlots` function, [whether a piece of land is dirty is determined based on the landlord's pledge. If it is dirty, Schnibbles will not be updated after this time](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-munchables/blob/94cf468aaabf526b7a8319f7eba34014ccebe7b9/src/managers/LandManager.sol#L258-L261).

There are two situations where users can prevent landowners from receiving Schnibbles.
1. The user never receives Schnibbles but always occupies this plot.
2. After the landlord withdraws the funds, the polt becomes dirty, and the landlord stakes again. The land will not become non-dirty. Schnibbles no longer spawn. Users can occupy the plot without taking out the NFT.

User failure to claim may be malicious or unintentional.
Since there is a risk of funds being locked, it satisfies High, but the possibility is low because users do not get rewards. So I think the risk is Med.

## Tools Used
manual

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
It is recommended that landowners also call the `farmPlots` function to receive Schnibbles.
