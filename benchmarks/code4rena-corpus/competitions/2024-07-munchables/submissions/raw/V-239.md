# Vulnerability details

## Description
The `_farmPlots` function updates the landlords' unfedSchnibbles and its lastPetMunchable:
```solidity
landlordMetadata.unfedSchnibbles += schnibblesLandlord;
landlordMetadata.lastPetMunchable = uint32(timestamp);
```

The issue with that is, the lastPetMunchable variable is used in the context of the SnuggeryManager's contract pet function. It prevents petters who have pet for less than 10 minutes from petting again:
```solidity
function pet(address _pettedOwner, uint256 _tokenId) external notPaused {
...
if (_petterPlayer.lastPetMunchable + 10 minutes > block.timestamp)
            revert PetTooSoonError();
...
}
```

Since the farmPlots call updates the landlord's lastPetMunchable to the timestamp when a farm action has been processed to his/her land, the landlord will have a much harder time attempting to pet other munchables and may not be able to pet at all.
## Impact
Landlords may have their capacity to pet other munchables denied by the farming activity of users that stake on their land.

Users that lock tokens in the system with the intent of only getting bonuses on their pet calls at the SnuggeryManager contract may not be able to do so: as the creation of plots at the LandManager doesn't rely on any call by the landlord and can be done in a permissionless manner as long as the landlord has enough locked tokens - as the `_getNumPlots` function relies solely on the lockedWeightedValue of the possible landlord.
## Tools Used
Manual review
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Make sure the `_farmPlots` function does not update the landlord's lastPetMunchable timestamp, as the landlord did not execute such action.
