# Vulnerability details

A player can farm [schnibbles](https://munchables.gitbook.io/munchables/explorers-guide/glossary/schnibbles) for his Munchable by staking his NFT on a landlord’s plot. 

Whenever a renter calls [`LandManager::farmPlots`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-munchables/blob/94cf468aaabf526b7a8319f7eba34014ccebe7b9/src/managers/LandManager.sol#L228-L230) , he collects schnibbles that he can use to feed his Munchable. The landlord receives a small cut from the total amount of schnibbles that the renter collects in the form of a tax rate that he has set for his plots.

The schnibbles to be collected are calculated in the following way:

```diff
schnibblesTotal = (timestamp - _toiler.lastToilDate) * BASE_SCHNIBBLE_RATE;
```

Here, `timestamp` is `block.timestamp` at the moment of the function call, and `_toiler.lastToilDate` is the `block.timestamp` of the last time the renter farmed schnibbles. By subtracting `_toiler.lastToilDate` from `timestamp`, we get the duration since the last schnibble collection, which is then multiplied by `BASE_SCHNIBBLE_RATE` to determine the total schnibbles earned.

According to the sponsor, the game is designed to be played actively and it should discourage inactivity. However, due to the way schnibbles to be received is calculated, a renter who hasn’t farmed for a long time will receive more schnibbles than one who is actively playing the game and farming regularly.

## Impact

A renter who has staked his Munchable NFT and hasn’t farmed since the staking of his NFT will earn more schnibbles than a renter who is actively playing the game. This discrepancy undermines the game’s intended reward system, as it favors inactive players over those who are consistently engaging with the game.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps:

To encourage renters to actively play the game and farm regularly, consider adding an `INACTIVITY_PERIOD` variable. This variable would define a maximum period of inactivity, after which the renter would no longer be eligible for rewards. Then proceed to add the following check in the [`LandManager::_farmPlots`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-munchables/blob/94cf468aaabf526b7a8319f7eba34014ccebe7b9/src/managers/LandManager.sol#L228-L230) function:


By incorporating the `INACTIVITY_PERIOD` variable, the system can automatically detect and flag inactive players who have not farmed within the specified period. This prevents inactive players from receiving schnibbles, thereby promoting consistent engagement with the game. The inactivity check within the `_farmPlots` function ensures that any `ToilerState` entries exceeding the inactivity period are marked as `dirty` and skipped in the reward distribution process. This approach helps maintain fairness and balance in the game, ensuring that active players are appropriately rewarded for their participation, while simultaneously discouraging inactivity.

Additionally, this mitigation aims to address the edge case described in the “Automated Findings/Known Issues", which can occur if a renter, whose `plotId` is no longer valid due to a landlord unlocking funds, can deliberately postpone interacting with the protocol until his `plotId` becomes valid again, effectively avoiding the plot being marked as `dirty` and as a result, the protocol continues to treat the entire duration as valid farming time, even though it was not.

By adding the `INACTIVITY_PERIOD` check, the renter cannot wait indefinitely for his `plotId` to become valid again. If the renter’s last toil date exceeds the inactivity period, their position will be marked as `dirty`, ensuring the protocol does not incorrectly reward inactive periods.
