# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In the `LandManager contract`, certain constraints are enforced on accounts, such as ensuring that `landlord != mainAccount` and limiting `munchablesStaked[mainAccount].length` to a maximum of 10. However, it's still possible to create multiple accounts to bypass checks: these checks can be easily bypassed by creating and registering another account or by transferring NFTs to another account to perform staking.


## Proof of Concept
The contract enforces the following constraints, such as ensuring that `landlord != mainAccount` and limiting `munchablesStaked[mainAccount].length` to a maximum of 10.

```solidity
@=>     if (landlord == mainAccount) revert CantStakeToSelfError();
        if (plotOccupied[landlord][plotId].occupied) // @note front-run to take?
            revert OccupiedPlotError(landlord, plotId);
@=>     if (munchablesStaked[mainAccount].length > 10)
            revert TooManyStakedMunchiesError();
        if (munchNFT.ownerOf(tokenId) != mainAccount)
            revert InvalidOwnerError();
```

It's still possible to create multiple accounts to bypass checks:

1. Creating and Registering Another Account: A user can create a new account and register it to avoid the `CantStakeToSelfError`.
2. Transferring NFTs: Users can transfer their NFTs to another account and perform staking from that account, bypassing the `TooManyStakedMunchiesError`.
