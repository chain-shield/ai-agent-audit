# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Not farming the plot while updating the landlord fee will cause a portion of the time with the new fee to be charged by the old one.

## Proof of Concept
[updateTaxRate](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-munchables/blob/main/src/managers/LandManager.sol#L92-L101) does not farm the plots that it is used in, meaning they still keep the old value inside `toilerState[tokenId].latestTaxRate`.

When farming them [_farmPlots](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-munchables/blob/main/src/managers/LandManager.sol#L287-L293) will always use the old tax to calculate the current `schnibblesLandlord` tax and then update the tax to the latest one.

```solidity
   schnibblesLandlord = (schnibblesTotal * _toiler.latestTaxRate) / 1e18;

   toilerState[tokenId].lastToilDate = timestamp;
   toilerState[tokenId].latestTaxRate = plotMetadata[_toiler.landlord].currentTaxRate;
```

This will cause the tax to be outdated. Users can use this accidentally or maliciously to pay lower tax (or higher):

1. At T1 tax is 10% and our user farms the plot.
2. At T2 the landlord changes the tax to 20%
3. At T3 our user farms the plot again, only paying 10% tax for the time T1 to T3.

In the example above our user paid the old tax for T2-T3, although the landlord had updated it.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Farming all of the plots when the tax is changed may be too hard, however a formula can be made that will calculate the weight of old and new tax depending on the time it was changed. 

An example is if tax is 10% for 1 day and 20% for 1 day, then when the total tax collected for the 2 days would be 15%.
