# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In function `transferToUnoccupiedPlot()`, the `toilerState[tokenId].plotId` and `toilerState[tokenId].dirty` are not updated to reflect plot transfer. Users transfer `dirty` NFT to a new slot would become ineffective and cannot participate in farming activities.

## Proof of Concept
The `transferToUnoccupiedPlot()` function does not properly update the `toilerState[tokenId]` struct associated with the `tokenId` of the transferred NFT. The `toilerState[tokenId].plotId` is not updated to reflect the new plot ID, and `toilerState[tokenId].dirty` is not reset to `false` after the transfer.

Therefore, users cannot transfer the `dirty` NFT to a new slot to activate the farming of this NFT, as it will be skipped in [`_farmPlots()`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-munchables/blob/main/src/managers/LandManager.sol#L251).
