# Vulnerability details

## Impact

As AccountManager.addSubAccount() can arbitrarily overwrite anyone’s main account, an attacker can prevent a chosen victim from updating their tax rate, which results in unfair game restrictions for the victim.


## Proof of Concept

Consider the following scenario:

1. Assume Alice is a registered landlord.
2. Bob calls `AccountManager.addSubAccount(alice)` which sets `mainAccounts[alice]` to Bob’s address.
3. Alice wants to update her tax rate, so she calls `LandManager.updateTaxRate()`. However, this call will update the tax rate of Bob’s plot instead because `landlord` (the return value of `_getMainAccountRequireRegistered(msg.sender)`) is Bob’s address at this point. Hence, Alice will never be able to update her tax rate

Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

The AccountManager logic must be reworked to ensure that no authorized party can arbitrarily modify someone’s main account address. The simplest fix would be to remove the entire concept of subaccount. If that’s not feasible, one potential mitigation is to require an approval signature from the original main account’s address in order to override the original main account address.

It is also important to ensure that `updateTaxRate()` offers the original landlord a way to update their tax rate, even if the main account changed.
