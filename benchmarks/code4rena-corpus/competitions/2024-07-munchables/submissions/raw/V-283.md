# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The `transferFrom()` method is used instead of `safeTransferFrom()`. [Openzeppelin Documentation](https://docs.openzeppelin.com/contracts/4.x/api/token/erc721#IERC721-transferFrom-address-address-uint256-) discourages the use of `transferFrom()`, but rather use `safeTransferFrom()` whenever possible. This helps to ensure that the recipient is capable of receiving ERC721s, hence preventing loss of nfts 

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Use `safeTransferFrom()` instead `transferFrom()` whenever possible
