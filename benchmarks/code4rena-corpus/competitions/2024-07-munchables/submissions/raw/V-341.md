# Vulnerability details

The main purpose of the `LandManager` is to allow munchable players to farm additional schnibbles. Two types of players are needed: landlords and toilers. Landlords lock tokens in the `LockManager` in exchange for plots in the `LandManager`. Toilers stake their `munchableNFT` on some of the landlords' plots. Once a plot is occupied by a munchable, it starts to farm, providing rewards to both the landlord and the toiler.

It's important to note that one address cannot be both a landlord and a toiler, but it can be a toiler on someone else's plots. However, it cannot be ensured that two addresses are not controlled by one person.

The reward formula can be simplified to this: the more the landlord has locked and the longer the toiler stakes at their plot, the more schnibbles they will receive. The rewards are distributed to both after [`LandManager.farmPlots()`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-munchables/blob/94cf468aaabf526b7a8319f7eba34014ccebe7b9/src/managers/LandManager.sol#L228-L230) is called. The goal of `LandManager` is to give fair rewards to pairs of landlords and toilers. So, if the landlord unlocks their funds, the reward that both should receive is up to that moment. Also, if the toiler unstakes, the reward should stop accumulating.

Here's the issue: it's possible for a landlord to lock funds, receive a plot, have someone stake on that plot, and then the landlord unstakes. At this moment, if the toiler calls [`LandManager.farmPlots()`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-munchables/blob/94cf468aaabf526b7a8319f7eba34014ccebe7b9/src/managers/LandManager.sol#L228-L230), they will receive the reward for the past period but will stop accumulating future rewards because the plot they are staked on is invalid.

If the toiler does not trigger [`LandManager.farmPlots()`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-munchables/blob/94cf468aaabf526b7a8319f7eba34014ccebe7b9/src/managers/LandManager.sol#L228-L230), they will not receive rewards, but the reward can continue accumulating. After some time, if the landlord re-locks the funds and the `plotId` becomes valid again, the toiler can call [`LandManager.farmPlots()`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-munchables/blob/94cf468aaabf526b7a8319f7eba34014ccebe7b9/src/managers/LandManager.sol#L228-L230). This way, both will receive the full reward for the period, even for the time when the funds were not staked.

## Impact

- The scenario gives an incentive for the landlord and toiler to perform that strategy and get free rewards.
- Someone can be a landlord from one account and a toiler from another, using this strategy to receive almost free (which are not backed by staked funds) schnibbles.

## Proof of Concept

Consider the following scenario:

- Alice locks funds in the `LockManager` and receives 10 plots at timestamp 100.
- Bob stakes munchable at Alice’s `plotId` 8 at timestamp 100. At this point, it starts farming schnibbles.
- After some time, Alice decides that her funds will be more lucrative elsewhere and unlocks half of them. As a result, her plots decrease, leaving her with only 5 plots.
- At this stage, Bob’s staked NFT remains on an invalid plot and should stop receiving rewards. If he calls `unstake` or `transferToUnoccupiedPlot`, the reward that Alice and Bob should split is only for the 150 seconds (300 - 150).
- However, Bob doesn’t unstake and leaves his NFT on `plotId` 8.
- After a long time (let's say 30 days), Alice decides to participate again and stakes the same amount.
- Now, Bob’s NFT is on a valid plot, and he can [`LandManager.farmPlots()`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-munchables/blob/94cf468aaabf526b7a8319f7eba34014ccebe7b9/src/managers/LandManager.sol#L228-L230). For simplicity, let's say this happens immediately after Alice's lock.

The amount that both should receive is just 300 - 150, the time that the munchables have been farming on an active plot. However, they will receive the schnibbles amount for 30 days - 150 seconds, which is an extremely large difference.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps:

I don’t see an easy way to fix the issue. One solution could be to track the active staking period and remove the period when the user has unlocked part or all of their funds from the `LockManager`.

Another alternative is to encourage renters to actively play the game and farm regularly. Consider adding an `INACTIVITY_PERIOD` variable. This variable would define a maximum period of inactivity, after which the renter would no longer be eligible for rewards. Then, add the following check in the `LandManager._farmPlots` function:

```diff
contract LandManager is BaseBlastManagerUpgradeable, ILandManager {
    uint256 MIN_TAX_RATE;
    uint256 MAX_TAX_RATE;
    uint256 DEFAULT_TAX_RATE;
    uint256 BASE_SCHNIBBLE_RATE;
    uint256 PRICE_PER_PLOT;
    int16[] REALM_BONUSES;
    uint8[] RARITY_BONUSES;
+   uint256 INACTIVITY_PERIOD = 14 days;

    function _farmPlots(address _sender) internal {
        (
            address mainAccount,
            MunchablesCommonLib.Player memory renterMetadata
        ) = _getMainAccountRequireRegistered(_sender);
        uint256[] memory staked = munchablesStaked[mainAccount];
        MunchablesCommonLib.NFTImmutableAttributes memory immutableAttributes;
        ToilerState memory _toiler;
        uint256 timestamp;
        address landlord;
        uint256 tokenId;
        int256 finalBonus;
        uint256 schnibblesTotal;
        uint256 schnibblesLandlord;

        for (uint8 i = 0; i < staked.length; i++) {
            timestamp = block.timestamp;
            tokenId = staked[i];
            _toiler = toilerState[tokenId];

            if (_toiler.dirty) continue;
            landlord = _toiler.landlord;
            
	    // Encouraging renters to actively farm, otherwise they get illegible for staking rewards
+           if (timestamp >= _toiler.lastToilDate + INACTIVITY_PERIOD) {
+               toilerState[tokenId].dirty = true;
+               continue;
+           }

	    // Discouraging landlords from unlocking tokens, otherwise they lose rewards on invalid plotIds
            if (_getNumPlots(landlord) < _toiler.plotId) {
               timestamp = plotMetadata[landlord].lastUpdated;
               toilerState[tokenId].dirty = true;
            }
            ...
            toilerState[tokenId].lastToilDate = timestamp;
            ...
        }
        accountManager.updatePlayer(mainAccount, renterMetadata);
    }
}
```
