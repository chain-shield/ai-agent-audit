# Vulnerability details

## Impact
 Users end up staking under less favorable conditions than they intended potentially leading to financial losses

## Proof of Concept
The `updateTaxRate` function allows landlords to instantly modify their tax rates `(currentTaxRate)` without any cooldown period or pending state. Concurrently, the `stakeMunchable` function processes staking actions using the `currentTaxRate` at the `time of execution`. This creates a time-of-check to time-of-use  issue where the tax rate a user bases their staking decision on may differ from the rate applied when their transaction is processed.

The underlying race condition persists due to potential delays between transaction submission and execution which can be influenced by network latency, block times and the validator's transaction ordering mechanism. This non-atomic operation in a concurrent environment creates a window of opportunity for rate changes that could be exploited by strategic landlords or lead to unintended economic outcomes for stakers.

## Tools Used
Manual Review
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Allow users to lock in a rate for a certain period when they initiate a stake transaction.
