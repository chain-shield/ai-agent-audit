# Vulnerability details

The [`LandManager.farmPlots()`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-munchables/blob/94cf468aaabf526b7a8319f7eba34014ccebe7b9/src/managers/LandManager.sol#L228-L230) function lets stakers farm schnibbles, which are split between the landlord and staker based on the landlord’s tax rate. The schnibbles amount is calculated by multiplying the time difference since `_toiler.lastToilDate` by `BASE_SCHNIBBLE_RATE`. After farming, `_toiler.lastToilDate` and `_toiler.latestTaxRate` are updated.

Landlords can update their tax rate via `LandManager::updateTaxRate`, but changes only apply to future `farmPlots` calls. When a staker stakes an NFT, their `ToilerState` uses the current tax rate. If the landlord updates it later, the staker might avoid `farmPlots` and instead call `LandManager::unstakeMunchable`, which indirectly triggers `_farmPlots()` via `forcedFarmPlots`. This calculates schnibbles with the old tax rate before updating it. Since the staker has already unstaked, future `farmPlots` calls don't affect them, allowing them to benefit from the old tax rate.

## Impact

A staker may exploit a tax rate update by postponing their interaction with the protocol, thus continuing to farm rewards based on the old tax rate. When a landlord increases their tax rate, the staker can avoid using the `farmPlots` function, which would otherwise update their tax rate, and instead use `unstakeMunchable`. This allows the staker to collect rewards at the outdated, more favorable tax rate before fully disengaging. As a result, the effectiveness of tax rate adjustments is undermined, and landlords may find their efforts to increase tax rates ineffective, as stakers may have little to no incentive to keep farming under less advantageous conditions. Additionally, stakers are incentivized to remain inactive, instead of farming actively, so they can maximize their rewards, which contradicts the main idea of the protocol.

## Proof of Concept

During this process, schnibbles are distributed between the landlord and the staker according to a ratio determined by the tax rate specified in the landlord’s `plotMetadata` struct. The amount of schnibbles awarded is calculated by subtracting `_toiler.lastToilDate` from the current `block.timestamp`, and then multiplying the result by `BASE_SCHNIBBLE_RATE`. At the end of the function call, `_toiler.lastToilDate` is updated to the current `block.timestamp` to reflect the most recent farming activity, and `_toiler.latestTaxRate` is updated to match any changes in the landlord’s `plotMetadata`.

A landlord can update their tax rate by calling `LandManager::updateTaxRate`. However, the new tax rate will only affect stakers on subsequent calls to [`LandManager.farmPlots()`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-munchables/blob/94cf468aaabf526b7a8319f7eba34014ccebe7b9/src/managers/LandManager.sol#L228-L230), not the current call. This means that while [`LandManager.farmPlots()`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-munchables/blob/94cf468aaabf526b7a8319f7eba34014ccebe7b9/src/managers/LandManager.sol#L228-L230) will update the tax rate at the end of the function call, it does not retroactively apply to the schnibbles calculated before this update.

As a result, when a staker stakes an NFT, their `ToilerState` struct is initialized with the landlord’s current tax rate. If the landlord subsequently updates their tax rate, the staker might notice this change and choose to become inactive to maximize rewards. By avoiding calls to [`LandManager.farmPlots()`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-munchables/blob/94cf468aaabf526b7a8319f7eba34014ccebe7b9/src/managers/LandManager.sol#L228-L230)and instead opting to call `LandManager::unstakeMunchable`, the staker indirectly triggers [`_farmPlots()`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-munchables/blob/94cf468aaabf526b7a8319f7eba34014ccebe7b9/src/managers/LandManager.sol#L232) via the `forcedFarmPlots` modifier. This function calculates schnibbles using the outdated tax rate stored in `ToilerState` before updating it. Consequently, since the staker has already unstaked their NFT, future calls to `farmPlots` will not affect them, allowing the staker to benefit from the old tax rate while avoiding the new one by remaining inactive.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps:

I don’t see an easy way to fix the issue. What I suggest is to calculate the tax rate dynamically, based on the time-weighted cumulative tax rates during the period, and use it to determine the ratio to split the reward.

Another alternative is to encourage renters to actively play the game and farm regularly. Consider adding an `INACTIVITY_PERIOD` variable. This variable would define a maximum period of inactivity, after which the renter would no longer be eligible for rewards. Then, add the following check in the `LandManager._farmPlots` function:
