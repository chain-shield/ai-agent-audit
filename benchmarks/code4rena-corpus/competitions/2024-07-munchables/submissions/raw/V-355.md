# Vulnerability details

## Description
The calculation of the landlord's share of schnibbles may result in significant precision loss due to integer division, particularly when dealing with small values of `schnibblesTotal`.


## Code snippet

```solidity
schnibblesLandlord = (schnibblesTotal * _toiler.latestTaxRate) / 1e18;
```

The use of integer division in Solidity truncates any remainder. When `schnibblesTotal` is small relative to the tax rate (which is scaled by 1e18), the result of this calculation could be zero, even when a non-zero amount should be allocated to the landlord.

For instance, if `schnibblesTotal` is 1000 and `_toiler.latestTaxRate` is 1e16 (1%), the calculation would result in:
(1000 * 1e16) / 1e18 = 0

This means the landlord receives nothing, even though they should receive 1% of 1000, which is 10.

## Impact
1. Landlords may not receive their fair share of schnibbles, especially for small amounts or short time periods.
2. This could disincentivize landlords from participating in the system, particularly those with lower tax rates.
3. Over time, this could lead to a significant cumulative loss for landlords.

## Suggested Fix
Use fixed-point arithmetic to maintain precision:
   
```solidity
uint256 constant PRECISION = 1e6;
schnibblesLandlord = (schnibblesTotal * _toiler.latestTaxRate * PRECISION / 1e18) / PRECISION;
```
