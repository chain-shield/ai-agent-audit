# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In `LandManager::initialize(address)` is missing access control and after deployment of the contract, everyone can call the function with arbitrary '_configStorage' address. As an effect, the deployer of the contract will be able to see the malevolent interaction and will have to redeploy otherwise the '_configStorage' will be using incorrect data. The attacker may use a bot to frontrun and call the function every time the contract is deployed and immediately passing, for instance, a 0x0 address to the `LandManager::initialize(address)`, thus making it unusable and requiring another deployment.

MEDIUM - make contract unusable immediately after deploy and requiring a costly redeploy

## Proof of Concept
1. Deploy contract 'LandManager'
2. The Attacker calls `LandManager::initialize(address)` with an arbitrary address

## Tools Used
Solidity

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Add access control to the `LandManager::initialize(address)` so only the deployer of the contract or a specific set of owners/guardians can call the function.
