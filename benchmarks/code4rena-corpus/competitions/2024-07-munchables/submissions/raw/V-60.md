# Vulnerability details

## Impact
https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-munchables/blob/94cf468aaabf526b7a8319f7eba34014ccebe7b9/src/managers/LandManager.sol#L283-L286
here ``` 
schnibblesTotal = uint256(
                (int256(schnibblesTotal) +
                    (int256(schnibblesTotal) * finalBonus)) / 100
            );```
we are dividing sum of both schibblesTotal and (schnibblesTotal * finalBonus) by 100. which was not the correct way to calculate the schnibblesTotal as it was not only dividing (schnibblesTotal * finalBonus) by 100 as intended but dividing sum of schnibblesTotal and (schnibblesTotal * finalBonus) by 100 . Finally it leads to decreased(very much) schibblesTotal.

## Tools Used
vscode, manual review

## Recommended Mitigation
divide only (schnibblesTotal * finalBonus) by 100, not the sum of schnibblesTotal and (schnibblesTotal * finalBonus) by 100.
`schnibblesTotal = uint256(
                int256(schnibblesTotal) +
                   (int256(schnibblesTotal) * finalBonus) / 100
            );`
