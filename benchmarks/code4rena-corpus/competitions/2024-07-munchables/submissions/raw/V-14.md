# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Landlords can incentivize other users to stake on their plot with a low tax rate and then frontrun their staking transaction in order to set the tax rate to the maximum possible value. Most users will not go to check the tax rate after staking their tokens and will likely only realize that it has been increased when they try to farm the plot in order to earn rewards.

## Proof of Concept

Let's say that Alice has a plot with `currentTaxRate = MIN_TAX_RATE`. Bob sees that she has a very low tax rate and decides to stake on her plot in order to earn the most rewards possible and puts forward a transaction by calling `LandManager::stakeMunchable`. Alice sees that and fruntruns his transaction by calling `LandManager::updateTaxRate` and setting `currentTaxRate = MAX_TAX_RATE`. That way when Bob's transaction goes through he will be putt on the maximum possible tax rate and may not realize that until he receives less rewards than he should have.

Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Pass in `uint256 taxRate` as a parameter in `LandManager::stakeMunchable` and `LandManager::transferToUnoccupiedPlot` in order to ensure the user is put on the tax rate that they intended to get and revert the transaction if the tax rates do not match.
