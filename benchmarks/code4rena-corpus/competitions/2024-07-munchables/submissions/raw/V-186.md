# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Calculates share of landlord when farming plots incorrectly.

## Proof of Concept
For calculating any percentage of an given value, let's say x% of y value, we calculate it as:
```solidity
  (y * x) / 100
```

But in the given codebase:
```solidity
            schnibblesLandlord =
                (schnibblesTotal * _toiler.latestTaxRate) /
                1e18;
```
1e18 is used in the calculation to calculate the value of percentage.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Change 1e18 in the division to 100 for correct percentage calculation.
