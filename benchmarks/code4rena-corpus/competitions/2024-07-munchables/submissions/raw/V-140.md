# Vulnerability details

In LandManager.sol, two of the functions are stakeMunchable() and unstakeMunchable(). Both have the `notPaused` modifier. This means that the functions are not executed during a suspended state. However, there is no functionality to retrieve staked MUNCH tokens for users if the contract is paused.

## Impact
Users may permanently lose their MUNCH tokens as they will be not able to unstake due to paused state of the contract.

## Proof of Concept

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-munchables/blob/94cf468aaabf526b7a8319f7eba34014ccebe7b9/src/managers/LandManager.sol#L175

1. Bob stake MUNCH tokens.
2. By any reason the contract gets temporary/permanently paused.
3. Bob MUNCH tokens are lost for the duration of the pause. It may be forever.

In this case all staked MUNCH tokens are going to be blocked temporary/permanently.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Consider a backup plan for this situation with which MUNCH token owners will be always able to unstake their tokens.
