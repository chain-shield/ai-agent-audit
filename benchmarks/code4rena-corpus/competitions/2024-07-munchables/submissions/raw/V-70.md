# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Loss of Player Fund value due to deflation of Total Schnibbles as a result of Schnibbles bonus addition math operation which should have increase Total Schnibbles but reverse is the case as total Schnibbles would be deflated by approximately 100 times over when _farmPlots(...) function is called in the LandManager contract

The code above shows how this bonus is implemented and added to the total schnibbles for players in the _farmPlots(...) function in the LandManager contract. It can be noted that final bonus is calculated by adding RealmBonuses & RarityBonuses after which it is used to calculate a bonus addition to the total Schnibbles in other to increase or decrease it depending on the bonus value as bonus can also be negative. 
Item 35 and 36 of this munchables documentation at https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-munchables/blob/main/guides/CONSTANTS.md shows that bonus are represented with a direct value that represents the percentage of bonus in relation to 100%, as can be noted in the doc 1 is equivalent to 1%, 2 to 2% and so on
[RealmBonuses & RarityBonuses Doc ScreenShot](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/32c3af69-5019-404d-b791-48f50d30ddd2)
This can also be verified in the pointer in the code quoted by this report which shows a division by 100 i.e 100%.
In summary the problem this report is trying to point out is that the 100 is wrongly placed, it is understandable that multiplication of the bonus should first be carried out before division by 100 to avoid precision loss which is why the 100 is outside the bracket, but the 100 was mistakenly placed outside the wrong bracket, it is no long acting on just the schnibblesTotal bonus calculation but now acting on the overall calculation which divides not just the bonus schnibblesTotal by 100 but all schnibblesTotal.
#### Scenario Proof
lets assume schnibblesTotal is to be increase 50%
if schnibblesTotal = 1000
The correct calculation should be
(1000 + (1000 * 50) / 100 ) = 1500
However the wrong calculation in contract would show:
(1000 + (1000 * 50)) / 100  = 510
there by deflating the initial schnibblesTotal from 1000 to 10
