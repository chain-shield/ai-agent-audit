# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Users can withdraw funds during the staking process without affecting the final number of Schnibbles. This goes against the logic of staking rewards.

## Proof of Concept
background:
1. There are two types of accounts, landlords and NFT pledgers. Users can impersonate both accounts through different addresses.
2. Landowners need to pledge funds to develop land. NFT pledgers can pledge NFT on the corresponding land. In this way, both landowners and NFT stakers can receive rewards.
3. The rewards are settled through the `farmPlots` function. During settlement, it will be checked whether the landlord's stake is sufficient. However, during the period from the beginning to the settlement, no matter what the stake amount is.

Assume the following:
1. The user holds two addresses: the landlord and the NFT pledger.
2. The landowner opens up 5 pieces of land through stake.
3. NFT pledgers pledge 5 NFTs.
4. The landowner withdraws substantially all of the pledged funds.
5. After waiting for a long time, the landowner’s funds can be used as he wishes during this period.
6. The landowner stakes enough funds. NFT stakers call the [`farmPlots` function](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-munchables/blob/94cf468aaabf526b7a8319f7eba34014ccebe7b9/src/managers/LandManager.sol#L228) to settle rewards.

In this way, users can receive rewards without staking funds.

Due to breaking the principle of stake rewards. Similar to stealing funds from the protocol, so I judge it to be high.
## Tools Used
manual

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
It is recommended that dirty plots be forced to be settled when the landlord withdraws the staked funds.
