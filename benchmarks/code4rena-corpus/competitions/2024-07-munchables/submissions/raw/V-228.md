# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Subaccounts can change the landlord's tax rate via the `updateTaxRate` function in the `LandManager` contract. However, the audit contest details specify that [“Landlord can change their own tax rate”](https://code4rena.com/audits/2024-07-munchables#top). Additionally, when a landlord sets up subaccounts through the `addSubAccount` function in the `AccountManager` contract, these subaccounts could be untrusted third parties. Therefore, it is recommended to include a check to ensure that only the main account of the landlord can modify the tax rate.
