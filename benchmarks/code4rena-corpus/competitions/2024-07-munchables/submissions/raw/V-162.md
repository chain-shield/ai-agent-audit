# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Landlord get their "rent" or their tax payed by user when these two functions are called [farmPlots](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-munchables/blame/94cf468aaabf526b7a8319f7eba34014ccebe7b9/src/managers/LandManager.sol#L228) and [forceFarmPlots](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-munchables/blame/94cf468aaabf526b7a8319f7eba34014ccebe7b9/src/managers/LandManager.sol#L40) . The `farmPlots` is used by renter manually, the `forceFarmPlots` is used when the user (renter) stake, unstake or transfer munchables to different plots - [stakeMunchable](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-munchables/blame/94cf468aaabf526b7a8319f7eba34014ccebe7b9/src/managers/LandManager.sol#L135), [unstakeMunchable](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-munchables/blame/94cf468aaabf526b7a8319f7eba34014ccebe7b9/src/managers/LandManager.sol#L175), [transferToUnoccupiedPlot](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-munchables/blame/94cf468aaabf526b7a8319f7eba34014ccebe7b9/src/managers/LandManager.sol#L202). These are all functions that are called by the renter (user who stake) not the landlord. 
The issue is that renter can by neglecting or malicious intents make landlord lose money and potential gains that are his right when he became landlord (when he get plot by locking funds).

## Proof of Concept
Imagine the following scenario:
Bob become landlord and lock funds to get the plot and want to rent it. Alice decides to stake munchable on the Bob's plot. Bob's plot get occupied and Alice start staking. But Alice decide never farm or unstake or transfer her munchables. Her munchies just sit on Bob's plot and making him occupied, but Bob never get his tax that he expect and also he can't move Alice munchies from his plot and free it for other person who wants to stake. This could go indefinitely theoretically which will make Bob the loser by not gaining desired rent(his TAX), not be able to get new user who want to stake and pay him and by that means his investment in plot will be unworthy.

The similar real world scenario:
Bob rents apartment to Alice, but Alice decides not to pay Bob the rent, and Bob not to be able to evict Alice from his apartment he rent and replace her with new residents. That would not work and Bob's investment would not be worth and he will lose money.

## Tools Used
Manual Review.
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Make function that will check if user(renter) do not farm for a certain time period and give the landlords permission to remove them from his plot so it is not occupied so he can replace it with new staker. That can be manually by landlord or automatically done by protocol.
