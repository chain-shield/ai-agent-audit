# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The formula used to calculate `schnibblesTotal` in the `_farmPlots` function is incorrect because `finalBonus` is the total of ` REALM_BONUSES` and `RARITY_BONUSES` which are percentages. This can lead to incorrect calculations of the total schnibbles earned, potentially resulting in under-rewarding the players. This will affect the main functionality of the function `_farmPlots`.

## Proof of Concept

According to the guides,

[CONSTANTS.md](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-05-munchables/blob/main/guides/CONSTANTS.md)

` REALM_BONUSES` and `RARITY_BONUSES` are percentages which means that 1=1%. 

The `finalBonus` is calculated as the sum of these two bonuses which denote the total percentage of the initial `schnibblesTotal` earned by the player as bonus. The final schnibblesTotal should be the sum of the initial `schnibblesTotal` and the percentage bonus earned.

The formula is found in the `_farmPlots` function in the `LandManager` contract:

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-07-munchables/blob/94cf468aaabf526b7a8319f7eba34014ccebe7b9/src/managers/LandManager.sol#L283-L285

```solidity
schnibblesTotal = uint256(
    (int256(schnibblesTotal) +
        (int256(schnibblesTotal) * finalBonus)) / 100
);
```

This formula incorrectly applies the `finalBonus` as a percentage increase. It divides the sum of the Initial `schnibblesTotal` and percentage bonus by 100 instead of just the `finalBonus`. for example,


Assume `schnibblesTotal = 1000` and `finalBonus = 20` (representing a 20% increase):

Using the incorrect formula:
```solidity
schnibblesTotal = uint256(
    (int256(1000) +
        (int256(1000) * 20)) / 100
);
// schnibblesTotal = (1000 + 20000) / 100 = 21000 / 100 = 210
```

But a player who has 1000 schnibbles and a finalBonus of 20% should have:
1000 + (1000*20)/100 = 1200

The incorrect formula results in `210` instead of the correct `1200`.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

**Correct the Formula**: Update the formula to correctly apply the `finalBonus` as a percentage increase.

```Solidity
   schnibblesTotal = uint256(
                int256(schnibblesTotal) +
                    (int256(schnibblesTotal) * finalBonus) / 100
            );
```
