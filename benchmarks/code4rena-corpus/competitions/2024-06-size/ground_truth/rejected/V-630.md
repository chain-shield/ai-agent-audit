## Brief Summary

The current implementation of the `isDebtPositionLiquidatable` function in the `Size` protocol does not account for fees when determining if a debt position is liquidatable. This oversight could lead to scenarios where positions that should be liquidated (due to accumulated fees making the borrower effectively underwater) are not liquidated, resulting in potential financial losses for the protocol and its participants.
