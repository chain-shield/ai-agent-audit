# Vulnerability details

## Description:

When the value of USDC depreciates, users can repay their debt at a reduced cost in USD, while still holding debt tokens that were minted at the original, higher value of USDC. This discrepancy can result in significant losses for the platform and undermine the stability of the borrowing and lending system.

## Impact:

Users who have borrowed USDC can take advantage of a market depreciation in its value to repay their debts at a reduced cost in USD, effectively underpaying their debt. This discrepancy arises because the debt tokens are minted based on the initial value of USDC at the time of borrowing, but repayment is based on the current, depreciated value of USDC. As a result, the platform could lose the difference between the borrowed amount and the repaid amount, leading to instability in the overall financial system.

**Example Scenario:**

Initial Borrowing:

1. Alice borrows 100 USDC from the platform.
2. The platform mints 100 debt tokens to Alice, representing her $100 debt.
   USDC Depreciation:
3. USDC depegs and its value drops to $0.70 per USDC.
4. Alice's 100 USDC is now worth only $70 in USD terms.
5. Debt Repayment: Alice repays her debt by returning 100 USDC to the platform.
6. Since USDC is now worth $0.70 each, Alice effectively repays only $70 in USD value, despite borrowing $100 initially.
   Note: Intrest is not considered in this scenario.

This scenario is rated as medium severity due to the likelihood is low and the impact is high.

## Tools Used:

Manual Review

## Mitigation:

1. **Implement a Dynamic Debt Token Valuation:** Adjust the valuation of debt tokens to reflect the current market value of the underlying asset (USDC) rather than the value at the time of borrowing. This ensures that users repay the equivalent value in USD, regardless of USDC's current market price.

2. **Introduce Collateralization Adjustments:** Regularly update the required collateralization ratios to account for fluctuations in the value of USDC. This could involve setting dynamic collateral requirements based on the volatility of the underlying asset.

3. **Utilize Price Oracles:** Integrate reliable price oracles to fetch real-time price data for USDC. Use this data to dynamically adjust the amount required for debt repayment, ensuring that repayments are made based on the current market value of USDC.
