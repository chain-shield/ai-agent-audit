# Vulnerability details

## Impact
- Unattractive liquidation rewards for small loans will lead to these loans not getting liquidated, resulting in bad debts and loss of funds for lenders. This issue is further exacerbated by the fact that lenders have no control over the debt position's future value since borrowers can split their debt into multiple smaller debt positions through the compensate function. This makes the issue exploitable by borrowers to avoid liquidation, causing financial instability and increased risk of loss for lenders.
## Proof of Concept
- in the size protocol,a user’s debt position can become liquidatable under two scenarios:
   1.  the user’s collateral ratio (CR) drops below the `crLiquidation` threshold (set to130%), making all debt positions of that user liquidatable
   2.  that user fails to repay their debt position at maturity, making that specific debt position liquidatable(Overdue Liquidation).

- The protocol has a minimum credit amount set to `50USDC` means the minimum future value a  `debtPosition` can have is also `50$`. In case of liquidation, a percentage of the future value of the debt position is taken as a reward for the liquidator, based on the type of liquidation:
- **Overdue Liquidation**: If the borrower does not repay on time, the reward is 1% of the future value.
- **CR Liquidation**: If the borrower’s CR drops below the threshold, the reward is 5% of the future value.
- The rewards are calculated in the protocol as follows:
```js
if (assignedCollateral > debtInCollateralToken) {
    uint256 liquidatorReward = Math.min(
        assignedCollateral - debtInCollateralToken,
        Math.mulDivUp(debtPosition.futureValue, state.feeConfig.liquidationRewardPercent, PERCENT)
    );
    liquidatorProfitCollateralToken = debtInCollateralToken + liquidatorReward;
}
```

- The issue arises with small-value loans, such as those with a future value of `50USDC` for example ,in this case : 

  - The maximum reward for overdue debts is:
    \[
    50 \times 0.01 = 0.5 \text{ USDC}
    \]
  - The maximum reward for CR Liquidation is:
    \[
    50 \times 0.05 = 2.5 \text{ USDC}
    \]

- These rewards are insufficient to attract liquidators, especially given the high gas price on the mainnet. Liquidators would incur higher costs in gas fees than the rewards they receive, making it economically unviable for them to liquidate these small positions.
> **Notice**: This issue is different from the issue of using `futureValue` which is in `debtToken` with 6 decimals instead of `debtInCollateralToken` to calculate the rewards. This assumes a valid calculation of liquidator rewards
- Moreover, a malicious user could exploit this by splitting their debt position into multiple small debts to avoid liquidation during high market volatility for example. This can be achieved using the `compensate` function. By splitting a large debt into many smaller debts, the borrower can ensure that the liquidation rewards are too low to incentivize any liquidator to act, effectively avoiding liquidation
