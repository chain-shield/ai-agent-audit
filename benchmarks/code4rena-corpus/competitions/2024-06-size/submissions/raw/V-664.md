# Vulnerability details

## Impact

The `AccountingLibrary.getCreditAmountIn` function is used to calculate the `creditAmountIn` when the `cashAmountOut` is provided. This function is called by the `SellCreditMarket.executeSellCreditMarket` during its execution.

In the event of a fragmentation the `creditAmountIn` is calculated as follows:

```solidity
                creditAmountIn = Math.mulDivUp(
                    cashAmountOut + state.feeConfig.fragmentationFee, PERCENT + ratePerTenor, PERCENT - swapFeePercent
                );
```

But the issue here is that the `fragmentationFee` is added on to the `cashAmountOut` and then the `interest (PERCENT + ratePerTenor) and the swap fee is calculated` on top of that.

Due to the above issue the `seller (current lender) of the credit position` will have to sell more `credit` than he should to receive the required `cashAmountOut`. Hence this is loss of funds to the `seller`.

A similar issue is found in the `AccountingLibrary.getCreditAmountOut` function. This function is used by the `BuyCreditMarket.executeBuyCreditMarket` to get the credit amount out for a given cash amount in.

In the `getCreditAmountOut` function when credit fractionalization happens while buying credit, the `creditAmountOut` is calculated as follows:

```solidity
            uint256 netCashAmountIn = cashAmountIn - state.feeConfig.fragmentationFee; //@audit-info - account for hte fragmentation fee
            //@audit-info - account for the fragmentation fee in the form of creditAmountOut
            creditAmountOut = Math.mulDivDown(netCashAmountIn, PERCENT + ratePerTenor, PERCENT);
```
Here the `netCashAmountIn` is used to calculate the `creditAmountOut` and the `netCashAmountIn` is derived by deducting the `fragementation fee` from the `cashAmountIn`. Hence it is evident the `interest accrues on top of the fragmentationFee` which makes the `buyer` to `receive less creditAmountOut` than he deserves. Hence this is loss of funds to the `buyer` FragmentationFee is  a constant and it should not accrue fee during internal accounting.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

When calculating the `creditAmountIn` in the `AccountingLibrary.getCreditAmountIn` function the `fragmentationFee` should be added after accounting the `interest and swapFee` on top of the `cashAmountOut`. Because the `fragmentatioFee` is a constant fee which is not suppose to accrue interest over it.

When calculating the `creditAmountOut` in the `AccountingLibrary.getCreditAmountOut` function the `fragmentationFee` should be deducted from the `creditAmountOut` after accounting interest on top of the provided `cashAmountIn` value (This interest accrual gives the `creditAmountOut` from which we can deduct the fragmentationFee). 

The above steps will ensure `fragmentationFee` (which is a constant) will not accrue interest on it and would not break the internal accounting of the credit and cash calculations.
