# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The extra rewards earned by liquidity providers are locked within the protocol (address(size)) and are not able to be claimed, resulting in a loss of potential yield for users.

## Proof of Concept
Like many other lending protocols, Aave distributes additional rewards to users to incentivize supplying liquidity and, in some cases, borrowing. This is especially prevalent on L2 chains, where the chain itself may offer significant rewards (high APYs) for liquidity providers.

However, the size protocol does not provide any mechanism to claim these earned rewards, effectively locking them within the protocol.

According to the Aave documentation (https://docs.aave.com/developers/whats-new/multiple-rewards-and-claim), extra rewards are managed by the Aave RewardsController (https://docs.aave.com/developers/periphery-contracts/rewardscontroller). And users can call `rewardcontroller.claimRewards(assets, amount, to, reward)` to claim a single reward type.

## Recommendation
We understand that adding the functionality to distribute rewards to users may introduce significant complexity to the protocol's internal accounting and require substantial development effort. 

However, we believe the protocol should, at a minimum, implement a function to claim the rewards and transfer them to the `feeRecipient`. These claimed rewards could then be either distributed as a protocol fee or used to bolster the protocol's insurance fund to handle bad debt.

Adding an admin function like `claimRewardsFromAAVE(address Rewardstoken)` would be relatively straightforward to implement. It would ensure that the rightfully earned rewards do not remain locked and unusable within the protocol.
