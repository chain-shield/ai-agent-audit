# Vulnerability details

## Impact

In `executeSellCreditMarket`, if the borrower specifies an exact cash amount, the `creditAmountIn` is calculated such that the fees that are transferred from the are accounted for. Basically, to achieve the exact `cashAmountOut`, the lender lends extra funds which later have to be paid back by the borrower with interest.

However, the fees are incorporated into the `creditAmountIn` incorrectly as the `swapFee` is not accounted for.

## Proof of Concept

Let's say Alice wants to borrow 100 USDC from Bob. Alice chooses cashAmountOut = 100. We have:

cashAmountOut = 100
Rate = 10%
Swap fee = 5%
Fragmentation fee = 10 USDC

Bob pays 100 USDC to Alice and 20 USDC in fees. According to the formula in the code, his credit is currently calculated as:

```
CreditAmountIn = (cashAmountOut + state.feeConfig.fragmentationFee) * ((PERCENT + ratePerTenor) / (PERCENT - swapFeePercent))

= (100 + 10) * (110 / (100 - 5)) = 127.368
```

Since Bob has paid 120 USDC, his effective interest rate is only 5.8%. A calculation to approximate the correct account would be:

```
swapFee = cashAmountOut * swapFeePercent / PERCENT

CreditAmountIn = (cashAmountOut + state.feeConfig.fragmentationFee + swapFee) * ((PERCENT + ratePerTenor) / (PERCENT - swapFeePercent))

swapFee = 100 * 0.05 = 5

CreditAmountIn = (cashAmountOut + state.feeConfig.fragmentationFee + swapFee) * ((PERCENT + ratePerTenor) / (PERCENT - swapFeePercent))
= (100 + 10 + 5) * (110 / (100 - 5)) = 133.1578
```

This now gives Bob an interest rate of 9.99%.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Update the calculation of `CreditAmountIn` to account for the swap fee.
