# Vulnerability details

## Impact
CreditSeller's CollateralRatio can be brought down by CreditBuyers, which, depending on the market's activity, could help to deteriorate the CR faster and have CreditSellers liquidated.

## Proof of Concept
When CreditSellers wants to SellCredit via a LimitOrder, they post the YieldCurve specifying the APR and Tenors they'd like to SellCredit at. Then, a CreditBuyer can request a BuyCredit MarketOrder to lend/sell Credit to a BorrowOffer from the Borrow OrderBook. When buying credit with a market order, [the CreditBuyer can specify if he would like to buy the credit from an existing CreditPosition, or if he would like to buy the credit from a new CreditPosition, a.k.a borrowing to a lender.](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-size/blob/main/src/libraries/actions/BuyCreditMarket.sol#L179-L193)

The CreditSellers can specify if their positions are for sale, or only certain positions could be sold, but, **CreditSellers can't specify if they would like to sell credit via a new CreditPosition (borrowing)**. 
- This poses a risk because CreditSellers who have active borrows (DebtPositions) most likely have a healthy CollateralRatio to avoid getting liquidated. But, if a CreditSeller places a SellCredit LimitOrder to sell credit from one of his existing CreditPositions, **a CreditBuyer can buy the Credit by creating a new Debt/Credit Position, a.k.a lending to the credit seller.** ***This will bring down the collateral ratio of the CreditSeller, which poses a risk of getting liquidated.***


## Tools Used
Manual Audit

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Similar to how CreditSellers can control which of their CreditPositions are for sale, allow CreditSellers to have the ability to specify if they'd like to Sell Credit by lending or by selling credit from an existing CreditPosition.
