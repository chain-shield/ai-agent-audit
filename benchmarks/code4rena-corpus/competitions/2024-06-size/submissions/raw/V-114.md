# Vulnerability details

## Impact
A attacker can fill up the whole block at near the end of a loan to prevent the borrower from repaying and then profit from the liquidation process

## Proof of Concept
Consider this scenario, Alice's debt is near the due time, so she deposit USDC to repay the debt. Since she could benefit from the interest rate from AAVE, so she is planning to pay the debt as late as possible. An attacker, Bob, can exploit this by filling the whole block to prevent Alice's repay transaction from minting, and then he will submit a transaction to liquidate Alice's debt and benefit from 5% face value of the loan.

The larger value of the debt, the later the borrowers want to pay their debt to benefit more from the interest rate, which makes this attack more profitable for the attacker.

## Tools Used
Manual Review.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Since the payment of the overdue debt is not urgent in the sense that there are no assets at risk, the protocol can remove the incentives for the liquidators when liquidating overdue loan. The protocol and lenders are expected to liquidate the overdue debt. This approach is still opened to griefing attack, but the impact on the borrowers is small.

Another approach is when liquidating an overdue debt, if the borrowers have enough AToken to pay the debt, then the AToken is paid back to the lenders and the borrowers are not charged for penalty.
