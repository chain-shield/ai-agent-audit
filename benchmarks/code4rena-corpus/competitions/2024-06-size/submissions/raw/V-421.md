# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Since Size functions most times are dual usage functions, check should be implmented for validations at each process, but this does not happen in  `sellcreditMarket::validateSellCreditMarket`. the check to make sure that the apr returned is not greater than what they are willing to pay for the loan but since users can also sell existing credit positions this check does not do the intended work in the opposite, because a lender selling credit position should be check if the apr being returned from the `getAPRByTenor` is not smaller than a minimum at which they will sell their credit position since the main incentive to sell is to sell to people that are willing to pay higher rates.

## Proof of Concept
```solidity
// validate maxAPR
        uint256 apr = loanOffer.getAPRByTenor(
            VariablePoolBorrowRateParams({
                variablePoolBorrowRate: state.oracle.variablePoolBorrowRate,
                variablePoolBorrowRateUpdatedAt: state.oracle.variablePoolBorrowRateUpdatedAt,
                variablePoolBorrowRateStaleRateInterval: state.oracle.variablePoolBorrowRateStaleRateInterval
            }),
            tenor
        );
        if (apr > params.maxAPR) {
            revert Errors.APR_GREATER_THAN_MAX_APR(apr, params.maxAPR);
        }
```
Here checking the apr against the maxApr wont protect an exisiting credit seller, to protect them a check that checks against the minimum is needed to prevent selling at a lower rate.

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
a inverse check that protects the existing credit seller to make sure the apr returned is not less than what the seller is willing to sell at.
