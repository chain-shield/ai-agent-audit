# Vulnerability details

## Impact

The constant `Math::Year` is used in the calculation of swap fee percent in the `AccountingLibrary::getSwapFeePercent` function. Also, the `getSwapFeePercent` function is called in the `getSwapFee` function to calculate the swap fee. This swap fee is used in different functions of the protocol (some of them: `SizeView::getSwapFee`, `AccountingLibrary::getCreditAmountOut`, `AccountingLibrary::getCashAmountIn`, `AccountingLibrary::getCashAmountOut`). The problem is that the `YEAR` is set to be `365 days`. But that is not correct for the leap years that have `366 days`. The protocol may lose fees due to the incorrect duration of the year when the year is leap, leading to  inaccuracies in interest and fee calculations during leap years:

- Interest rates calculated on a 365-day basis will be inaccurate during leap years. Borrowers may pay less interest than intended, leading to reduced revenue for the protocol.
- Fees calculated annually will be off by one day every four years. Over a long period, this can accumulate to a noticeable shortfall in collected fees.
- Loans spanning a leap year may have minor discrepancies in duration. The protocol may not accurately capture the intended interest and fees for the extra day in leap years.
- Annual Percentage Rate (APR) and Annual Percentage Yield (APY) calculations will be skewed. This can lead to misrepresentation of the actual cost or yield of loans and investments.

## Proof of Concept

In the `Math` contract there is a constant `YEAR` that is defined as `365 days`. But this constant is not always correct, because not every year has 365 days.
In `Math.sol`:
```javascript
uint256 constant YEAR = 365 days;

```
The constant `YEAR` is used in `AccountingLibrary::getSwapFeePercent` function.
In `AccountingLibrary.sol`:
```javascript
    function getSwapFeePercent(State storage state, uint256 tenor) internal view returns (uint256) {
@>      return Math.mulDivUp(state.feeConfig.swapFeeAPR, tenor, YEAR);
    }

    function getSwapFee(State storage state, uint256 cash, uint256 tenor) internal view returns (uint256) {
@>      return Math.mulDivUp(cash, getSwapFeePercent(state, tenor), PERCENT);
    }
```
Also, the `getSwapFeePercent` function is called to calculate the swap fee in the `AccountingLibrary::getSwapFee` function. Moreover, the `getSwapFee` function is used in different important parts (contracts) of the protocol. Therefore the return value from this function is important to be correct and reliable.

Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Take into account that the leap years have 366 days and use the correct value for the `YEAR` variable.
