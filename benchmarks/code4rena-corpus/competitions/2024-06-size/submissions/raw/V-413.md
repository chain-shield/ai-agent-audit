# Vulnerability details

## Impact

A trader(credit seller) who lends with a higher interest rate and early exit using ```executeBuyCreditMarket``` with lower interest rate to earn profit could be forced to borrow incase he liquidate a position.

The trader pays interest + swap fees and also got exposed to being underwater and liquidated, when his intention was to never borrow but instead sell his credit. 

## Proof of Concept
A lender can sell his credit to another lender to exit position earlier. And this can be done in two ways, ```SellCreditMarket``` or ```BuyCreditMarket```. Incase there is no order the seller likes then he puts up an order to sell his credit and wait for another lender to call ```BuyCreditMarket``` with his Id. 

The problem here is in the fact that to sell credit through ```BuyCreditMarket``` the seller needs to put up a ```borrowOffer``` which is the same struct used by a borrower. 
```javascript
} else {

```

This alone is not a probem because if try to let the seller borrow it will revert because the seller(trader) does not have any collateral at this point. 
```javascript
    function buyCreditMarket(BuyCreditMarketParams calldata params) external payable override(ISize) whenNotPaused {
        state.validateBuyCreditMarket(params);
        uint256 amount = state.executeBuyCreditMarket(params);
        if (params.creditPositionId == RESERVED_ID) {
            state.validateUserIsNotBelowOpeningLimitBorrowCR(params.borrower);
        }
```
```validateUserIsNotBelowOpeningLimitBorrowCR``` will revert the transaction in this case.


But if the seller wants to earn by liquidating a position(which is very likely considering the user(trader)'s intention is to earn) then this attack becomes possible.

After liquidation he recieves a collateral from the liquidated psoition:
```state.data.collateralToken.transferFrom(debtPosition.borrower, msg.sender, liquidatorProfitCollateralToken);```

Now, since he has collateral the ```borrowOffer``` he put up for selling his credit can be used to make him borroww instead of buying his credit. 
This can be done by calling ```buyCreditMarket``` with RESERVED_ID and the trader's address as the borrower. 
All checks will pass because the same offer struct is used. 

The trader's whose intention was to sell his credit is now forced to borrow and pay interest and also swap fees. 


Example:
* Bob is a trader that earns profit by buying credit(lending with higher rate) and selling it early with lower rate.
* Bob's collaterlaTokens = 0, credit = 1000000
* Bob finds no order that interest him to ```executeSellCreditMarket```.
* So bob puts up a ```borrowOffer``` since thats the only way to sell his credit so that a lender migh match him through ```buyCreditMarket```. 
* Bob finds a profitable liquidation so he wants to earn extra profit from it.
* Bob executes liquidation and he receives collateralTokens back.
* Bob's borrowOffer suddenly becomes a two way offer, one for selliing his credit and one for letting him borrow tokens.
* Alice quickly execute ```BuyCreditMarket``` with Bob as the borrower but not to buy his credit, instead to let him borrow. Alice doesn't necessarily need to be a malicious attacker here. Alice might simply think bob wants to borrow and executed that and it is valid.
* Suppose the amount made to borrow was 1000 and interest was 10% then Bob loss 100 + swap fees, he will be liquidated if he doesn't repay, paying more.

* Since borrowOffers are never cleared even after fulfilled, it increases the likelyhood of this issue. 

Reporting this as a medium severity because, every action Bob and Alice does here is a valid action and since it is not a know issue that Bob could be made to borrow incase he liquidate a position while having an open borrowOffer to sell credit(with an intention to sell existing credit not to borrow tokens).

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
consider using a different struct order for selling credit when a lender sells his credit using ```executeBuyCreditMarket```.

The same way we have functions to put up a loanOffer and borrrowOffer, there should be another one for credit owner to put an offer that indicates they want to sell their credit. Which is separated from the borrowOffer, so that a seller with an intention to sell credit does not need to use borrowOffer.
