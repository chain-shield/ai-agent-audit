# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The size protocol offer fixed interest loan that are due in some point in the future. In the case that the `creditPosition.duedate` is reached and the borrower didn't pay then the loan, the `debtPosition becomes elligible for liquidations and the liquidators will get  incintivised to liquidate the positon.
The protocol uses the oz pause library to allow for pausing the prtocol, in the case of a risk or a security incident.  

The problem however arises, from the fact that the protocol doesn't put a safeguard in place in the case that during the period of the pause, that some debtPosition have matured and became elligible for liquidations. 
## Proof of Concept
Protocol admins are able to pause and unpause the protocol by calling `pause()` and `unpause()`. and in the case that the protocol is paused all the user facing functions would be unavailable and will fail (they are all equipped with `whenNotPaused` which will revert if protocol paused). This would block users from being able to repay their debt, before they become overddue and raced on to be liquidated by MEV Bots

**Example Scenario**

1. Alice takes out a loan of 1000 USDC from the Size protocol, with a due date set for June 30th.
2. On June 29th, the protocol is paused by the admins due to a potential security risk.
3. Alice's loan becomes overdue on June 30th while the protocol is still paused.
4. On July 1st, the protocol is unpaused.
5. Immediately after the protocol is unpaused, liquidators (potentially MEV bots) identify Alice's overdue debt position and liquidate it.
6. Alice loses her collateral and faces a liquidation penalty, despite being unable to repay her loan during the pause period.


## Recomendation
We recommend adding a mechanism to create something like a `grace period` where liquidation are blocked for 1-2 hours  while repayment, deposit and all operations that improve the health of positions are enabled. This will lead to more stability in the protocol and to avoid times when the debt becomes overdue but the protocol was paused and the positions get liquidated
