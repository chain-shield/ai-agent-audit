# Vulnerability details

## Impact

The `Claim.executeClaim` function is used to let the `lender` claim the `credit` after the repayment of the `debtPosition` related to the respective `creditPosition` is fully settled.

The `lender` can decide to let the `repayment credit amount` to remain within the `Size` contract and accrue `Aave USDC pool interest`. 

But the problem is `Size.claim` function does not have access control and any one can call this function. Hence as a result any other user can call the `Size.claim` and claim the `borrowAToken` amount on behalf of the lender thus depriving the `lender` to earn extra interest on the `Aave deposit`.

The `claimer` will have to spend the gas fee for the `claim` transaction execution but the lender will lose the `interest accrual` during the period his funds were removed from the `Aave pool`. (Given that lender decides to re-deposit his funds into Aave). Hence this could be loss of potential interest to the `lender`.

```solidity
    function executeClaim(State storage state, ClaimParams calldata params) external {
        CreditPosition storage creditPosition = state.getCreditPosition(params.creditPositionId);
        DebtPosition storage debtPosition = state.getDebtPositionByCreditPositionId(params.creditPositionId);

        uint256 claimAmount = Math.mulDivDown(
            creditPosition.credit, state.data.borrowAToken.liquidityIndex(), debtPosition.liquidityIndexAtRepayment
        );
        state.reduceCredit(params.creditPositionId, creditPosition.credit);
        state.data.borrowAToken.transferFrom(address(this), creditPosition.lender, claimAmount);

        emit Events.Claim(params.creditPositionId, creditPosition.debtPositionId);
    }
```
