# Vulnerability details

### Impact

The `compensate` function, intended for partial loan repayment, allows borrowers to bypass the fragmentation fee when fragmenting a lender's credit position. While the fragmentation fee is enforced when the borrower fragments their own position, the check is omitted when they fragment the lender’s position. This creates a loophole that can be exploited, especially on networks like Base where the gas cost is lower compared to Ethereum.

###  Proof of Concept

- **Fee Bypass**: Borrowers can fragment a lender’s position without paying the required fragmentation fee.
- **Cost Efficiency Exploitation**: Users can exploit this loophole more cheaply on networks like Base, circumventing the fragmentation fee and extending the lender's credit position by creating multiple fragments.

**Exploitation Scenario**:

1. **Setup**: A borrower has an existing debt position and a lender has a credit position associated with it.
2. **Borrower Action**: The borrower fragments the lender's position through the `compensate` function.
3. **Outcome**: The protocol does not enforce the fragmentation fee since the borrower is fragmenting the lender's position, leading to potential fee evasion.
