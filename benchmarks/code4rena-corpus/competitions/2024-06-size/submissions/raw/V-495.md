# Vulnerability details

vulnerability detail

users can repay their debt by calling repay users can only repay in full
however if the user accidently specified an amount larger than their debt balance, the excess will be stuck in the contract as the contract does not refund excess repaid amount

impact

innocent users can lose their funds



Recommendation: Compute the difference between the specified amount and the amount that was actually
repaid, and refund it to the user.
