# Vulnerability details

## Impact

In the `SellCreditMarket.executeSellCreditMarket` transaction the `seller of the credit position (msg.sender in this instance)` pays for the `fee` amount when selling the credit position to a new lender. The `seller` pays for both the `swapFee and the fragmentationFee` in this instance.

In the `BuyCreditMarket.executeBuyCreditMarket` still the `seller of the credit position` who is the lender of the existing credit position pays for the `swapFee` amount as shown below:

Here the cash amount sent to the `borrower` by the `buyer` is deducted by the `fees` amount.

```solidity
        state.data.borrowAToken.transferFrom(msg.sender, borrower, cashAmountIn - fees); //@audit-issue - charging fees from the borrower and not the lender
        state.data.borrowAToken.transferFrom(msg.sender, state.feeConfig.feeRecipient, fees);
``` 

The buyer of the credit position (who is the msg.sender) in this instance the market maker does not pay for the `swapFee` and he only pays for the `fragmentationFee` as shown below in the `AccountingLibrary.getCreditAmountOut` function:

```solidity
            uint256 netCashAmountIn = cashAmountIn - state.feeConfig.fragmentationFee; //@audit-info - account for hte fragmentation fee
            //@audit-info - account for the fragmentation fee in the form of creditAmountOut
            creditAmountOut = Math.mulDivDown(netCashAmountIn, PERCENT + ratePerTenor, PERCENT); 
```

But both the `swapFee and the fragmentationFee` should be paid by the market maker which is the `buyer of the credit position` in the `executeBuyCreditMarket` transaction. Because if `seller` pays for the `swapFee`, he does not have any control over the amount of fees going to be charged from him which depends on the `cashAmoutIn` amount paid by the `buyer`.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Hence it is recommended to change the logic to make the `buyer` (The market maker) pay for both the `swapFee and fragmentationFee` in the `BuyCreditMarket.executeBuyCreditMarket` function implementation.
