# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Borrower who has taken a loan out of a specific lender can DoS that lender if he is trying to borrow using the creditPosition with credit owed by that borrower.

## Proof of Concept
Users who have given out loans and have a `creditPosition` can use it to take a loan using `sellCreditMarket()`. They can specify the `creditPositionId` to either `RESERVED_ID` or a credit position they own. The former is used for taking a simple borrow while the second one is used when the lender wants to take out a loan using a credit position he already has (called an early exit in the context of the protocol). The borrower who owes money to the lender (the borrower of the debt position linked to the credit position of the lender) can DoS the lender and not allow him to do that.

The borrower can frontrun the lender who tries to borrow by compensating his debt position. By doing that, he can split his debt position into 2 parts and decrease the credit owed for the credit position the lender tries to use for his borrow. Then, he will fail this check as `creditPosition.credit` decreased:
```solidity
if (params.amount > creditPosition.credit) {
                revert Errors.NOT_ENOUGH_CREDIT(params.amount, creditPosition.credit);
            }
```
It makes sense for the lenders to specify `params.amount` to equal `creditPosition.credit` as otherwise they will get their position fragmented and they will owe extra fees for that (even if they specify a lower amount, the borrower can compensate with such an amount that this would still lead to a revert). Thus, by the borrower frontrunning and doing what I explained above, the credit for the position will be smaller than the specified amount and will lead to a revert. The borrower can repeat this all the way until the debt position reaches the minimum allowed amount for a position but at that point, the lender will only be able to take a borrow for that minimum allowed amount which is 5$ according to the deployment scripts (5e6).

Paste the following POC into `Compensate.t.sol` (this will revert as expected, you can comment out the lines where Alice frontruns and see that it won't revert):
```solidity
    function testBorrowersCanDosLendersWhoTryToBorrow() public {
        _setPrice(1e18);

        _deposit(bob, usdc, 100e6);
        _deposit(alice, weth, 150e18);
        _deposit(candy, usdc, 100e6);

        _buyCreditLimit(bob, type(uint256).max, YieldCurveHelper.pointCurve(1 hours, 0));
        uint256 debtPositionId = _sellCreditMarket(alice, bob, RESERVED_ID, 100e6, 1 hours, true);
        uint256 creditPositionId = size.getCreditPositionIdsByDebtPositionId(debtPositionId)[0];


        _buyCreditLimit(candy, type(uint256).max, YieldCurveHelper.pointCurve(1 hours, 0));

        // Alice frontrunning, currently this will revert, you can comment out next 2 lines to make it not revert
        vm.prank(alice);
        size.compensate(CompensateParams({creditPositionWithDebtToRepayId: creditPositionId, creditPositionToCompensateId: RESERVED_ID, amount: 5e6}));
        _sellCreditMarket(bob, candy, creditPositionId, 100e6, 1 hours, true);
    }
```
