# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In `sellCreditMarket` Their is a check that prevents users from selling from credit that is present in the credit position. But the check uses param.amount for the check when selling existing credit positions, but the check did not take into consideration that checkAmount in param, when it is true this check is correct and behaves correctly, but when it is false, this check can pass wrongly affeting th core function of Size

## Proof of Concept

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-size/blob/8850e25fb088898e9cf86f9be1c401ad155bea86/src/libraries/actions/SellCreditMarket.sol#L87-L89

```solidity
if (params.amount > creditPosition.credit) {
                revert Errors.NOT_ENOUGH_CREDIT(params.amount, creditPosition.credit);
            }
```
When `exactAmountIn` is false param.amount will be in cash not credit, which means this check will be comparing cash to credit and the equivalent amount of a cash is a higher credit because it is future value.

An example 

paramAmount is 100 but creditPosition is 105, but at 6% apr, the equivalent credit of 100 is 106, which mean 100 > 105 will pass but in reality the check should be 106 > 105, which will do its job and revert.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
A check for true/false of exactAmount in should be checked to implement correctly if the credit is larger than the credit of the position.
