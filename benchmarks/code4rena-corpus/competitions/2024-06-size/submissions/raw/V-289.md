# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Due to a division, in which de divisor is user controllable, it is possible to swap credit without paying the related fees.

## Proof of Concept
When buying the full amount of credit from a user, by specifying the amount of credit requested, the correspondent amount of borrowATokens is computed as follows:
```
        if (params.exactAmountIn) {
            cashAmountIn = params.amount;
            (creditAmountOut, fees) = state.getCreditAmountOut({
                cashAmountIn: cashAmountIn,
                maxCashAmountIn: params.creditPositionId == RESERVED_ID
                    ? cashAmountIn
                    : Math.mulDivUp(creditPosition.credit, PERCENT, PERCENT + ratePerTenor),
                maxCredit: params.creditPositionId == RESERVED_ID
                    ? Math.mulDivDown(cashAmountIn, PERCENT + ratePerTenor, PERCENT)
                    : creditPosition.credit,
                ratePerTenor: ratePerTenor,
                tenor: tenor
            });
        }
```
In particular the amount to pay will be computed as
```
: Math.mulDivUp(creditPosition.credit, PERCENT, PERCENT + ratePerTenor),
```
Plus some fees which will be dependent on the *amountIn* previously computed, if the amount being paid is 0, than also the fees will be 0.

Note that the *creditPosition.credit* is devided by *PERCENT + ratePerTenor*, which is user controllable, meaning that, by specifying an extremely high *ratePerTenor*, the price for the credit purchases, and therefor the fees, can be sent down to 0 (or 1 wei due to rounding errors).

This means that two users can agree for a trade off chain. Than the seller will create an offer with an extremely high *ratePerTenor*, the buyer will get the offer immediately (getting the credit for free, and paying 0 fees). Than, the buyer. deposits the agreed amount of USDCs to the seller's account.
This will enable users to swap credit on Size without paying the swap fees, resulting in losses for the organization.

Although part of the interaction happens outside of the protocol, the most crucial parts will take place inside of Size's infrastructure, including the final results and effects (the movement of credit from one users to the other, and the consequent management of debt/credit afterward). 
Meaning that the users are getting access to most of size's services and infrastructures for free.

    function test_no_fees() public {

        _deposit(alice, usdc, 100e6);
        _deposit(bob, weth, 100e18);
        _deposit(candy, usdc, 1); //Candy needs 1 wei to pay for rounding errors


        _mint(address(usdc), candy, 100e6); //Usdc to repay Alice

        uint256 creditPositionId = size.getCreditPositionIdsByDebtPositionId(debtPositionId)[0];


        Vars memory _before = _state();
        
        _buyCreditMarket(candy, creditPositionId , futureValue, false);

        assertEq(_before.candy.borrowATokenBalance,_after.candy.borrowATokenBalance +1 );//1wei rounding error
        assertEq(_before.feeRecipient.borrowATokenBalance + 1, _after.feeRecipient.borrowATokenBalance);//1 wei rounding error
        
        //Candy repays alice
        vm.prank(candy);
        size.deposit(DepositParams({token: address(usdc), amount: futureValue, to: alice})); 
        //Intead of futureValue candy would have to pay the amount agreed for buiyng the credit, this is justa place holder to show the steps
    }
