# Vulnerability details

## Description
The way borrowers sell credit on market is specifying the amount of credit to sell, the tenor and the interest rate. However, this function can be frontrunned by a any other borrower. If the sum of both borrower amount is greater than the credit bid, the second borrower will revert the transaction.

This is an issue because the amount of the first borrower may not fulfill the credit bid, forcing the second borrower transaction to revert. This could happen again in normal cirmustances, by a third borrower, and so on, leading to many honest borrowers transaction to revert as well.


## Impact
Race condition of `Size.sellCreditMarket(SellCreditMarketParams)` can force many honest transactions to revert in normal circumstances

## POC
For next example ignore fees and interest rates.

Alice is willing to lend 5,000 USDC for 1 year at a 20 APR, so she but a buy limit on the market with this conditions.

Bob, Eve, and Charlie are borrowers, Bob wants to get 3,000 USDC, Eve 3,000 USDC and Charlie 2,500 USDC.

1. The three borrowers calls `Size.sellCreditMarket(SellCreditMarketParams)` specifying the amount of USDC that they want to borrow from Alice. Eve transaction goes first, then Bob's and Charlie's. Bob and Charlie transactions will revert because after Eve partially fulfill the credit offer, the remaining credit bid is just 2,000 USDC.
2. Now, also Lenny wants to borrow 1000 USDC from Alice, so he also call `Size.sellCreditMarket(SellCreditMarketParams)`, Bob and Charlie do the same with 2,000 USDC. Lenny's transaction goes first, then Charlie and Bob's transaction revert again because credit bid remaining is not enough to fulfill their amount.
3. Finally Bob and Charlie insist with borrowing 1,000 USDC, only Bob transaction goes through, Charlie's transaction revert again because the credit bid is already fulfilled.

The reason why this happen is because `Size.sellCreditMarket(SellCreditMarketParams)` assumes the function caller is smart enough to specify and amount lower or equal to the remaining credit bid, but it does not consider the implicit race condition of this function.

## Recommended mitigation
If the amount of the credit bid is greater than the remaining credit offer, update the amount to borrow to the remaining credit offer. In this way, the amount to borrow acts like a slippage.
