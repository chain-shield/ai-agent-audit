# Vulnerability details

## Impact
H-01 buyCreditMarket() results in a lender losing funds due to buying a portion of a small credit

If we have Alice, Bob and James
in this scenario

1. Alice deposit usd
2. Bob deposit collateral
3. James deposit usd
4. Alice buycreditlimit
5. Bob sellcreditmarket
6. Alice sellcreditlimit (rate per tenor)
7. James buycreditmarket cashamountin = 50usd , 1 percent rate per tenor

We have unexpected loss of funds as James now is putting 50 usd cash and gets around 45 usd credit which is a loosing trade, 
this is due to the combination of a large fragmentation fee + swap fee and the ratePerTenor, 
but as it seems there are no checks, neither reverts for that so the protocol executes the trade
If lots of these trades happen it may have unexpected outcomes for the protocol.
