# Vulnerability details

Depending on `params.exactAmountIn` the amount specified in call parameters can be either cash expected to be received or credit amount expected to be used. These amounts differ in magnitude being future and present value. In the same time controls always assume it is credit, future value, this way denying correct operations with low enough present value cash amounts and bigger than threshold future value credit amounts.

## Impact

All these operations, while being correct in the protocol terms, will be permanently denied with the corresponding adverse effect on their callers. I.e. some positions cannot be closed or opened only because cash value is too low. The callers will have to run the other versions of the operations, which, given the other issues outlined (e.g. incorrect swap fee calculation), can have material impact on the operation results.

## Proof of Concept

If `params.exactAmountIn == true` in BuyCreditMarket or `params.exactAmountIn == false` in SellCreditMarket then `params.amount` is cash and in the case of high tenors or/and high rates can be substantially lower than the corresponding credit (that can be well above `minimumCreditBorrowAToken`), so these valid operations be denied:
