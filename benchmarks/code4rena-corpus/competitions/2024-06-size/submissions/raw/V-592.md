# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Borrowers pay more credit than necessary due to incorrect calculations

## Proof of Concept
Borrowers can call `sellCreditMarket()` to borrow cash. They can do a simple borrow by specifying `RESERVED_ID`. They can also either specify either the amount of cash they want to receive or the credit (amount to return with interest) that they want to pay. This is the block they end up in whenever they specify the cash they want to receive:
```solidity
cashAmountOut = params.amount;

            (creditAmountIn, fees) = state.getCreditAmountIn({
                cashAmountOut: cashAmountOut,
                maxCashAmountOut: params.creditPositionId == RESERVED_ID
                    ? cashAmountOut
                    : Math.mulDivDown(creditPosition.credit, PERCENT - state.getSwapFeePercent(tenor), PERCENT + ratePerTenor),
                maxCredit: params.creditPositionId == RESERVED_ID
                    ? Math.mulDivUp(cashAmountOut, PERCENT + ratePerTenor, PERCENT - state.getSwapFeePercent(tenor))
                    : creditPosition.credit,
                ratePerTenor: ratePerTenor,
                tenor: tenor
            });
```
If `cashAmountOut` and `maxCashAmountOut` are equal, the position doesn't get fragmented. As seen from the code above, if the user has specified `RESERVED_ID`, then they will be equal. Then, in `getCreditAmountIn()`, if those values are equal, then the `maxCredit` input is the returned `creditAmountIn`. The returned `fees` value is equal to this:
```solidity
fees = Math.mulDivUp(cashAmountOut, swapFeePercent, PERCENT);
```
Let's say `cashAmountOut` is 1000e6, `swapFeePercent` is equal to a value based on the swap fee APR and the tenor. In our example, the tenor will be a year so `swapFeePercent` will be equal to the swap fee APR which is 0.005e18 according to the deployment scripts. That makes the `fees` equal 5000000.

Then, these are the funds sent out to the borrower and the protocol:
```solidity
state.data.borrowAToken.transferFrom(params.lender, msg.sender, cashAmountOut);
state.data.borrowAToken.transferFrom(params.lender, state.feeConfig.feeRecipient, fees);
```
In total, 1005000000 are sent out. 1000e6 of those are `cashAmountOut` and the rest are fees for the protocol. The fees are owed by the borrower (the cash receiver always pays the swap fees), thus they are included in his credit position as extra money to repay to the lender as he is the one initially paying that amount to the protocol as seen above. This means that the credit position should equal $1000e6 + 5000000 + interest$. If the `ratePerTenor` is 1e17 (10%), then he should owe 1105000000. The issue is that if we calculate `maxCredit` which as said earlier, is the amount that the borrower will have to pay as credit, it will be larger than that value. Here is the calculation for it:
```solidity
Math.mulDivUp(cashAmountOut, PERCENT + ratePerTenor, PERCENT - state.getSwapFeePercent(tenor))
```
$1000e6 * (1e18 + 1e17) / (1e18 - 0.005e18) = 1105527638.19$ (or 1105527639 as we use `mulDivUp()`)
The borrower pays 527639 more than necessary. That is equal to a bit less than 0.53\$. This issue will occur every single time the borrower specifies `RESERVED_ID` as the credit position ID making this issue highly problematic and impactful despite the borrower not paying a lot more money than required, thus deserving of high severity.

Paste the following POC into `SellCreditMarket.t.sol` to see the calculated credit amount. I can't prove that amount is wrong just through code as it's just Maths, that's why the POC is super simple, you have to see that it's wrong based on the information given in my explanation above.
```solidity
function testBorrowerPaysMoreThanRequired() public {
        _setPrice(1e18);
        _deposit(alice, weth, 1800e18);
        _deposit(bob, usdc, 1200e6);
        _buyCreditLimit(bob, type(uint256).max, YieldCurveHelper.pointCurve(365 days, 1e17));
        uint256 debtPositionId = _sellCreditMarket(alice, bob, RESERVED_ID, 1000e6, 365 days, false);
        uint256 creditPositionId = size.getCreditPositionIdsByDebtPositionId(debtPositionId)[0];

        assertEq(size.getCreditPosition(creditPositionId).credit, 1105527639);
    }
```

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Do not include the swap fees as part of the percentage calculation. Include it as a flat fee instead.
