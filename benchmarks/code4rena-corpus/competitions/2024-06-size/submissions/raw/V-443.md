# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In `LiquidateWithReplacement`, the `issuanceValue` for the new borrower is calculated at

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-size/blob/8850e25fb088898e9cf86f9be1c401ad155bea86/src/libraries/actions/LiquidateWithReplacement.sol#L146

and then the `issuanceValue` is sent directly to the new borrower without charging swapping fee

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-size/blob/8850e25fb088898e9cf86f9be1c401ad155bea86/src/libraries/actions/LiquidateWithReplacement.sol#L161

As a result, this would break the `FEES_02` invariant

> FEES_02: Cash swap operations increase the fee recipient balance

which makes the protocol lose out on the swapping fee in `LiquidateWithReplacement`, and it is unfair for the normal borrowers that trade by other means.

## Tools Used
Manual Review.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Charge new borrowers in `LiquidateWithReplacement` the swapping fee 

`LiquidateWithReplacement.sol`

```diff
    issuanceValue = Math.mulDivDown(debtPositionCopy.futureValue, PERCENT, PERCENT + ratePerTenor);
+   uint256 fees =  AccountingLibrary.getSwapFee(state, issuanceValue, tenor);
    ...
-   state.data.borrowAToken.transferFrom(address(this), params.borrower, issuanceValue);
-   state.data.borrowAToken.transferFrom(address(this), state.feeConfig.feeRecipient, liquidatorProfitBorrowToken);
+   state.data.borrowAToken.transferFrom(address(this), params.borrower, issuanceValue - fees);
+   state.data.borrowAToken.transferFrom(address(this), state.feeConfig.feeRecipient, liquidatorProfitBorrowToken + fees);

```
