# Vulnerability details

## Impact
A lender could their their interest earned on aave 

## Proof of Concept
aTokens are rebasing tokens ie the balance of users increase to reflect their interest earned 
```
assume bob has a repaid credit position of 1000szeusdc and bob wants to leave his usdc in the pool to earn interest on aave 
1 . assume liquidity index = 1.01, bobs 1000 usdc in aave = 1000 * 1.01 = 1010aUSDC ie 1010 of the aUSDC in the size contract belongs to bob 
2. a malicious actor claims for bob and he is minted 1010szeUSDC 
3. assume liquidity index rises to 1.05,the total claimable aUSDC amount of the contract increases, bobs 1000 usdc in aave is now worth 1000 * 1.05 = 1050aUSDC that belongs to bob from the size pool 
bobs szeUSDC balance is now 1010 however, the total amount earned from bobs deposit is 1050aUSDC;
4. bob tries to withdraw his 1050aUSDC 

the above call will fail due to the following line

``
https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-size/blob/8850e25fb088898e9cf86f9be1c401ad155bea86/src/libraries/actions/Withdraw.sol#L55C9-L55C93
``
which ensures users cannot withdraw more than their szeToken amount which for bob is 1010
``
https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-size/blob/8850e25fb088898e9cf86f9be1c401ad155bea86/src/libraries/DepositTokenLibrary.sol#L87
``
which burns the users szeToken amount and would revert if the user is trying to withdraw more szeToken that they have 

in total leaving bob with a 40 usdc loss 
```
