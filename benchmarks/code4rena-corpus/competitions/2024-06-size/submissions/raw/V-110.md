# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In current code, users can not withdraw USDC when they are below the opening limit borrow CR. This might not be an optimal design for users, because withdrawing USDC does not change CR.

Consider this scenario, Alice deposited ETH to be able to borrow USDC. She borrowed USDC to minimum CR (150%), due to price fluctuation, her CR became lower than opening limit borrow, now she can not withdraw her USDC. 

Another scenario, Bob saw his debt could become liquidatable, so he wanted to withdraw USDC to swap into ETH to add more collateral, but he can not withdraw USDC because the CR is below 150%.

## Tools Used
Manual Review.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Only validate the CR when users withdraw WETH.
