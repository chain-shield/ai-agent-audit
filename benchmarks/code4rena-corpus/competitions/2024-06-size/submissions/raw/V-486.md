# Vulnerability details

## vulnerability details 
The repayDebt function is use for processing repayments and updating the debt position, and the current implementation only deducts the repayment amount from the future value of the debt without considering any accrued interest or fees.
and This is  can result in a discrepancy between the recorded debt and the actual amount owed by the borrower here : 
```solidity
        state.data.debtToken.burn(debtPosition.borrower, repayAmount);
        debtPosition.futureValue -= repayAmount; <----- issue 
```
the bug is from the reduction of the debt's future value by the repayment amount, without accounting for interest and fees that have accumulated over the life of the debt. and  The absence of calculations for these additional amounts leads to an incomplete and incorrect update of the debt position and a malicious borrower can exploit this vulnerability by making repayments that are insufficient to cover the accrued interest and fees, while still having their debt position updated as if a full repayment was made. This can result in the borrower's debt being marked as lower than it actually is, potentially allowing them to escape full repayment of their obligations.
## Impact
this bug is can lead to financial discrepancy in the lending protocol. 
Borrowers can potentially underpay their debts, resulting in losses for the protocol and lenders. Over time,

## Proof of Concept
as an attack path
- Borrower takes a loan and starts accruing interest.
- Borrower makes a repayment that only covers part of the accrued interest and fees.
- The repayDebt function updates the debt position's future value by simply subtracting the repayment amount, ignoring the accrued interest and fees.
- The borrower's debt position shows a reduced debt that does not accurately reflect the true remaining amount owed.
- let's say that Borrower takes a loan of 1000 tokens.
- The loan accrues an interest of 10 tokens over a period, making the total amount owed 1010 tokens.
- Borrower repays 1000 tokens.
- repayDebt function is called with repayAmount of 1000 tokens.
- The future value is reduced by 1000 tokens, ignoring the 10 tokens of accrued interest.
- The debt position shows 0 tokens remaining, but in reality, the borrower still owes 10 tokens
- as result Borrower escapes repayment of the 10 tokens of accrued interest. 
- The protocol incurs a loss of 10 tokens.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
it's need to ensure that the function is accounts for all accrued interest and fees before updating the debt position
