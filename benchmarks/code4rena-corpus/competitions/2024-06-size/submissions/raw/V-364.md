# Vulnerability details

## Impact

The whenNotPaused modifier in the Size::selfLiquidate function could result in financial losses for lenders. If the protocol is paused due to certain circumstances, lenders are unable to initiate self-liquidation of debt. This inability can lead to significant amounts of debt remaining unliquidated, potentially resulting in substantial financial losses for lenders. They rely on the ability to self-liquidate to mitigate losses, but this option is unavailable if the protocol is paused.

During times of high market volatility, unexpected events are more likely to occur. These can lead to protocols being paused for days or weeks. This situation can be particularly challenging for lender who are caught off guard, as they may not have anticipated such disruptions.

## Proof of Concept

Consider these scenario: (Fee isn't added for simplicity)

1. User deposited 10 WETH with $2000 per ETH as collateral which gave him 10 collateralToken.
2. User borrowed $13000 USDC. The collateral ratio is 153.85% which is above the minimum requirement of 150%, so the borrowing is allowed.
3. The price falls down to $1705 in 3 months or about 15% but this is unlikely to be liquidated because the collateral ratio is still 131%.
4. The whole protocol is paused because of unexpected circumstances in the market for 2 weeks.
5. During pause, lender try to self Liquidating the loan to avoid loss but unable to do so.
6. The price of ETH falls down to $1100 or about 35.48% (This is a reasonable percentage as we can see from 6 june 2022 until 20 june 2022 which falls down about 37.54% in total)
7. At the end, the lender will only get $11000 worth of eth from the loan.
8. Total loss from the lender is about $2000 because the protocol restrict selfLiquidate even though selfLiquidate could be called when the collateral ratio is 99%

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

```diff
-   function selfLiquidate(SelfLiquidateParams calldata params) external payable override(ISize) whenNotPaused {
+   function selfLiquidate(SelfLiquidateParams calldata params) external payable override(ISize) {
        state.validateSelfLiquidate(params);
        state.executeSelfLiquidate(params);
    }
```
