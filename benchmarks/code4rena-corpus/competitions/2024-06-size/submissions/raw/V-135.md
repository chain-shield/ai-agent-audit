# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The `executeLiquidateWithReplacement` function in [LiquidateWithReplacement.sol#L149-L151](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-size/blob/8850e25fb088898e9cf86f9be1c401ad155bea86/src/libraries/actions/LiquidateWithReplacement.sol#L149-L151)

The `debtPosition` is updated with the new borrower and future value, but the `dueDate` is not updated. This means the new borrower will have a debt position with the same due date as the original borrower, which may differ from the intended behavior.
```solidity
debtPosition.borrower = params.borrower;
debtPosition.futureValue = debtPositionCopy.futureValue;
debtPosition.liquidityIndexAtRepayment = 0;
```
The `dueDate` should also be updated to reflect the new borrower's loan term. Without updating the `dueDate`, the new borrower may have a shorter or longer loan term than intended, which could lead to unexpected behavior in the protocol.

**Proof of the bug**
   - Assume there is an existing `DebtPosition` with the following values:
     * `id`: 1
     * `borrower`: 0x1234...
     * `futureValue`: 1000
     * `dueDate`: 1700000000 (a future timestamp)
     * `liquidityIndexAtRepayment`: 0

   - A new borrower (0x5678...) wants to replace the existing borrower through the `liquidateWithReplacement` function.
   - The new borrower's `BorrowOffer` has a tenor of 30 days (2592000 seconds).
   - When the `executeLiquidateWithReplacement` function is called, the following updates are made to the `DebtPosition`:
     * `borrower`: 0x5678...
     * `futureValue`: 1000
     * `liquidityIndexAtRepayment`: 0
   - However, the `dueDate` remains unchanged at 1700000000.
   - This means that the new borrower (0x5678...) has a debt position with the same due date as the original borrower (0x1234...), even though their desired loan term is different.

**Impact of the bug**
   - The new borrower (0x5678...) will have a debt position with a due date that does not match their desired loan term of 30 days.
   - If the original borrower's remaining loan term was shorter than 30 days, the new borrower will have to repay the loan earlier than expected.
   - If the original borrower's remaining loan term was longer than 30 days, the new borrower will have more time to repay the loan than they intended.
   - This mismatch in loan terms can lead to unexpected behavior and potential financial risks for the new borrower and the protocol.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
The `dueDate` should be updated based on the new borrower's `borrowOffer` and the current timestamp. Something like this.
```solidity
debtPosition.borrower = params.borrower;
debtPosition.futureValue = debtPositionCopy.futureValue;
debtPosition.liquidityIndexAtRepayment = 0;
debtPosition.dueDate = block.timestamp + borrowOffer.tenor;
```
