# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Borrowers can make losses to lenders and lenders can make losses to borrowers by front-running. 



## Proof of Concept
1. Let’s assume, Alice(borrower) creates a borrow offer with 3 months(5% apr), 4 months(6% apr), 6 months(7% apr). 

2. bob(lender) calls the function buyCreditMarket with alice as borrower and 6  months(7% apr) as tenor.

3. Alice sees Bob's transaction in mempool, Alice frontrun Bob's transaction and creates a new borrow offer.  The new borrow offer is 3 months(5% apr), 4 months(6% apr), 6 months(3% apr).

4. Bob’s( lender) intention was to get 7% apr for 6 month  tenor from the borrower but Bob gets 3% apr from the borrower which is unintentional from the lender.

Similarly,
1. Let’s assume, Alice(lender) creates a loan offer with 3 months(5% apr), 4 months(6% apr), 6 months(7% apr). 

2. bob(borrower) calls the function  sellCreditMarket with alice as lender and 3  months(5% apr) as tenor.

3. Alice sees Bob's transaction in mempool, Alice frontrun Bob's transaction and creates a new loan offer.  The new loan offer is 3 months(7% apr), 4 months(6% apr), 6 months(3% apr).

4. Bob's(borrower) intention was to give 5% apr for 3 months tenor but Bob gives 7% apr to the lender(alice) which is unintentional from the borrower.

manual review 
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Prevent the frontrunning to work the protocol properly.
