# Vulnerability details

## Impact

* Lenders losing funds due to unliquidatable positions. Not only interest but including the principal.
* Borrowers with intention to repay gets unfairly liquidated loosing his collateral.

## Proof of Concept
When liquidation is done the caller repays using the ```borrowATokens```:
```
state.data.collateralToken.transferFrom(debtPosition.borrower, msg.sender, liquidatorProfitCollateralToken);
```
And to get the borrowATokens the liquidator must first deposit USDC through ```deposit``` which will deposit it to the variablePool and mint ```borrowATokens``` to the user.
```javascript
    function depositUnderlyingBorrowTokenToVariablePool(State storage state, address from, address to, uint256 amount)
        external
    {
        state.data.underlyingBorrowToken.safeTransferFrom(from, address(this), amount); 

        IAToken aToken =
            IAToken(state.data.variablePool.getReserveData(address(state.data.underlyingBorrowToken)).aTokenAddress); 

        uint256 scaledBalanceBefore = aToken.scaledBalanceOf(address(this)); 

        state.data.underlyingBorrowToken.forceApprove(address(state.data.variablePool), amount);
        state.data.variablePool.supply(address(state.data.underlyingBorrowToken), amount, address(this), 0);

        uint256 scaledAmount = aToken.scaledBalanceOf(address(this)) - scaledBalanceBefore;

        state.data.borrowAToken.mintScaled(to, scaledAmount);
    }
```
We also know that when this variable pool is paused withdraws are not allowed.

Suppose this pool is paused or inactive, the positions that are overdue or underwater cannot be liquidated leading to lenders not recieving their repayments on time or not at all. 

Example;
* There are 5 lenders each of them with 1000 borrowATokens
* four of them have lended to borrowers who withdrew the tokens to USDC 
* Now, there is only 1000 borrowATokens. 
* Liquidity pool becomes inactive
* loans are overdue or underwater, and becomes liquidatable 
* now since there is no other mechanism to mint borrowATokens, 1000 is not enough to liquidate all of the 4 borrowers, with the interest included it becomes more.

Lenders will never be paid back even with overdue loans, lenders only hope is that the collateral ratio decreases so that they can recieve atleast some collateralTokens even if they have to lose funds, or for aave to unpause the pool.

In this case, the protocol bots might have some reserves it is not guarrantee that they will always have enough ```borrowATokens``` at all times. This can be confirmed from [known issues]():

```The insurance fund (out of scope for this project) may not be able to make all lenders whole, may be unfair, and may be manipulated```.


#### There are actually two opposite outcomes of this issue;
The other one is a borrower willing to repay will also need borrowATokens. 
So, he may become unfairly liquidated;

Example:
* 5 lenders with 1000 borrowATokens each
* only 1000 is borrowed by Bob, and he withdraws it to USDC.
* 4000 borrowAToken remains 
* variable pool becomes inactive
* during inactivity of the pool, bob's loan is almost overdue or undercollateralized
* bob wants to repay, but since liquidity pool is paused bob cannot get enough tokens to repay the debt
* Now, bob gets unfairly liquidated, even when he intended to repay the loan. 

So, for the borrowers who has withdrawn their borrowATokens to USDC(since that is the purpose of borrowing) when the variable pool suddenly becomes inactive, they will not be allowed to repay it and risk being liquidated. The risk increases if the dueDate is near.

manual 

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
create a separate ```liquidationWhenVariablePoolInactive``` and ```repayWhenVariablePoolInactive``` functions ;
* the functions should check that the pool is indeed inactive or paused
* the functions should repay directly in USDC
* implement a boolean ```repaidDuringInactivePool``` variable in the ```debtPosition```, and set it to true when it is repaid during pool inactive.
* implement an if statement in the normal ```claim``` function that will instead send USDC directly to the lender address if this ```repaidDuringInactivePool``` is true.
* other accounting mechanisms should stay the same.
