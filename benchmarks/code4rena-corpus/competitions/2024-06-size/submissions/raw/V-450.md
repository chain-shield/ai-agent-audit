# Vulnerability details

Lender cannot do exactAmountIn in case of buyCreditMarketOrder and seller cannot do cashAmountOut  in case of sellCreditMarketOrder.

## Impact
Lender cannot do exactAmountIn in case of buyCreditMarketOrder and seller cannot do cashAmountOut  in case of sellCreditMarketOrder even if the amount served as input is valid but less than minimumCreditBorrowAToken.

minimumCreditBorrowAToken check should be done only when the amount is for credit.

## Proof of Concept

Lets take the issue for the case of BuyCreditMarketOrder. 

In the `validateBuyCreditMarket()`  we are validating whether `(params.amount < state.riskConfig.minimumCreditBorrowAToken)`.
Suppose if the user/lender is passing cash amount he is wishing to lend to the borrower , this validation will return false and revert. Because cash amount can be less than the minimumCreditBorrowAToken.Only the corresponding credit we need to check and that is checked while creating a new credit in the execute function.

Only if the exactAmountIn = false ,this check is valid.
