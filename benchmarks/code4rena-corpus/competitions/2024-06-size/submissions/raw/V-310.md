# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The lenders who sells part of their CreditPositions are always charged an inflated `swapfee` if they use `sellCreditMarket()` with params.exactAmountIn == True
## Proof of Concept
> N.B we are focusing on the case where params.creditPositonId != RESERVED_ID
In the `executeSellCreditMarket()` function, if the `params.exactAmountIn` is set `True` the `params.amount` will be handled as the amount  of credit a lender wants to sell from his `creditPosition`

This is where this handeled in the code
