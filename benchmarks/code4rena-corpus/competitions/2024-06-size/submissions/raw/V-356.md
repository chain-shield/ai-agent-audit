# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The Size protocol's borrowing mechanism, implemented in the `SellCreditMarket` library, allows users to accrue debt without actually receiving the borrowed funds. This discrepancy occurs due to an incorrect implementation of the token transfer logic in the `executeSellCreditMarket` function. 

This vulnerability can lead to several severe issues:

Users accrue debt without receiving borrowed funds, potentially leading to unfair liquidations.

The protocol's internal accounting becomes inconsistent with actual fund distribution.

Malicious actors could exploit this to manipulate their positions or the protocol's overall state.

It may lead to insolvency of the protocol if exploited at scale.

Loss of user trust and potential regulatory concerns due to the mismatch between recorded debt and actual fund distribution.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Checking the return value of the `transferFrom` call and ensuring it succeeds: (example below)
```javascript
bool success = state.data.borrowAToken.transferFrom(params.lender, msg.sender, cashAmountOut);
require(success, "Token transfer failed");
```

Verifying that the lender has sufficient balance and has approved the protocol: (example below)
```javascript
require(state.data.borrowAToken.balanceOf(params.lender) >= cashAmountOut, "Insufficient lender balance");
require(state.data.borrowAToken.allowance(params.lender, address(this)) >= cashAmountOut, "Insufficient allowance");
```

Ensuring that the `borrowAToken` correctly represents the underlying asset and that its balance updates are reflected in the protocol's accounting.

Implement proper error handling and event emission for failed transfers to aid in debugging and monitoring.

Consider implementing a mechanism to atomically link debt recording with successful fund transfers to prevent any possibility of debt accrual without fund receipt.
