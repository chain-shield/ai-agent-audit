# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Project will be prone to using stale data in the future

## Proof of Concept
As blockchain ecosystems evolve and the demand for certain assets increases, Chainlink often adjusts the heartbeats and deviation thresholds of its price feeds. This ensures the data's relevance and accuracy. For instance, a pair like AAVE/USD might have different heartbeats on Ethereum Mainnet and Arbitrum:

Ethereum Mainnet: 1-hour heartbeat
Arbitrum: 24-hour heartbeat
As more projects deploy on other networks, Chainlink is likely to increase/reduce the heartbeat to accommodate the increased demand for timely price data. However, in the PriceFeed contract, the stale price intervals (baseStalePriceInterval and quoteStalePriceInterval) are immutable, meaning they cannot be adjusted post-deployment. This immutability introduces a risk of using stale data as the intervals may no longer align with the updated heartbeat of the Chainlink feeds.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Do not use `baseStalePriceInterval` and `quoteStalePriceInterval` as immutable. Create a function to change the heartbeat.
