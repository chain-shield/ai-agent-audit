# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Whales might not be able to get liquidated due to no way to partially liquidate

## Proof of Concept
The `liquidate()` function allows users to liquidate other users that are either underwater or their payment is due.

The issue is that for someone to liquidate another user, he has to pay `debtPosition.futureValue` which is essentially the amount owed by the liquidatable user. Since there is no mechanism for partial liquidations, the liquidator must have that amount at his disposal in order to liquidate the borrower. However, the position might be huge and thus, liquidators might not have enough funds making the position non-liquidatable. Even worse, `futureValue` is equal to the amount received by the borrower + interest based on the APR. This means that even the whale might not have funds to cover the position, let alone other liquidators.

## Tools Used
Manual Review
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Implement a way for users to liquidate others partially
