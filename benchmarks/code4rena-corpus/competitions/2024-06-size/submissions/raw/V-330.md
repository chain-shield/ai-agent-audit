# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The size protocol is expected to be deployed on mainnet where the gas fees could range from 5 usd to 40 USDC, it might be important especially for lenders to decide the minamount of their Liquidity provided to be lent out in a CreditPosition, as the cost to claim those Positions could eat out at their yield. Please also note, as it is the case now, lenders are required to call the `claim()` function in order for their lent out assets to be accredited to their account in order to be lent out. 
> N.B please also note that the protocol has put in a place a `fragmentationFee` to disentivize credit traders from fragmenting creditPositions (later streamlined to be claimed by the bot), However this fragmentation fee wouldn't help if the creditPosition created first is itself really small   
## Proof Of Concept
- User: Alice
- Initial Liquidity Provided: 1000 USDC
- Minimum Debt Amount(Protocol): 50 USDC
- Gas Fee per Transaction on Eth mainnet: 15 USDC
- Annual Percentage Yield  set by alice (APY): 7%

#### Attack Execution
1. **Griefer Action**: A griefer opens multiple limit orders, each with the minimum debt amount of 50 USDC.
2. **Fragmentation**: Alice's 1000 USDC is fragmented into 20 separate positions of 50 USDC each.
3. Alice needs to call the `claim()` function to retrieve her lent out assets.
4. Each `claim()` call costs 15 USDC in gas fees. (could be batched together, but we are focusing on worst case now)

**Calculation of Loss**  
- Total Gas Fees: 20 positions * 15 USDC = 300 USDC
- Yield from Lending: 7% APY on 1000 USDC = 70 USDC per year.
- If Alice claims her positions within a year, the gas fees (300 USDC) far exceed the yield (70 USDC).

**Result**  
- **Total Yield**: 70 USDC
- **Total Gas Fees**: 300 USDC
- **Net Loss**: 300 USDC (gas fees) - 70 USDC (yield) = 230 USDC

## Recomendation
The perfect solution, that we would recommend to such a problem is to add a user define risk parameter `minCreditAmount` and to use `max(UserConfig.minCreditAmount,RiskConfig.minimumCreditBorrowAToken)` to validate the minimum size of newly opened position for the user. This way the users could chose which minimum Credit Amount they are okay with, considering multiple factors such as the APY and the desired maturity duration of the positions.
