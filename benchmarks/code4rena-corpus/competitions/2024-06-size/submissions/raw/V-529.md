# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Borrowers who deposit ETH as collateral will lose funds in the depegged event of WETH .

## Proof of Concept
Protocol implement that user can deposit ETH or WETH as collateral and  When user withdraw the collateral , they can only withdraw WETH , read [here](https://docs.size.credit/technical-docs/contracts/2.1-deposit-tokens-and-global-trackers).

In the depegged event of weth , 1 eth will not longer equal to 1 weth . So borrower who deposited eth collateral is gonna lose funds when they withdraw collateral from protocol in the depgged event.

## Tools Used
manual view
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Use only weth as collateral
