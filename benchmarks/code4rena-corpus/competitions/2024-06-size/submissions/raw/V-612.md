# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Users will not be incentivized to liquidate smaller positions 

## Proof of Concept
```
eg
bob has borrowed 100 usdc from alice 
bob has 125 dollars in eth as collateral 
using the the liquidator reward using the liquidator fee percent in ``deploy.sol`` ie 0.05%
the liquidator reward will be 5 dollars in eth  
the gas costs alone associated with the call might be more than 5 dollars in eth leaving the liquidator at a loss 
```

## Tools Used
manual analysis

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
the protocol team should consider adding liquidation tranches for different position 
eg havinng a higher liquidator fee percent for smaller ones and decreasing fee percent  as the amount in the position increases
