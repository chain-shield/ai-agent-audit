# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The new borrower will get less cash when his SellCredit LimitOrders are filled by a liquidation with replacement instead of a BuyCredit MarketOrder

## Proof of Concept
When doing a liquidation with replacement, the amount of cash the new borrower could get is wrongly rounded down.

The new borrower is a CreditSeller who posted a SellCredit LimitOrder on the BorrowBook, and, this LimitOrder could have been filled with a BuyCredit MarketOrder, which, if it had been filled with a MarketOrder, the CreditSeller gets benefitted by rounding up the amount of cash the CreditSeller can get. 
- **The problem is that when the LimitOrder is filled by a liquidation with replacement, the amount of cash the CreditSeller can get is rounded down**, meaning, the CreditSeller will receive less cash for the same amount of credit if the SellCredit LimitOrder would have been filled with a CreditBuy MarketOrder.

**When the SellCredit LimitOrder is filled by a CreditBuy MarketOrder,** ***the amount of cash the CreditSeller would get for a specific amount of credit is computed on the [`AccountingLibrary.getCashAmountIn() function`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-size/blob/main/src/libraries/AccountingLibrary.sol#L311-L337),*** which, it **rounds up the amount of cash.**
- Execution flow => [`executeBuyCreditMarket()`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-size/blob/main/src/libraries/actions/BuyCreditMarket.sol#L121-L177) => [`AccountingLibrary.getCashAmountIn()`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-size/blob/main/src/libraries/AccountingLibrary.sol#L311-L337)

But, on the other hand, **when the SellCredit LimitOrder is filled by a Liquidation with Replacement, [the amount of cash the CreditSeller would get for the existing credit of the liquidated position is rounded down](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-size/blob/main/src/libraries/actions/LiquidateWithReplacement.sol#L146)**, therefore, the amount of cash that could get for the same amount of credit will be lower if the LimitOrder would have been filled with a BuyCredit MarketOrder.
- Execution flow => [executeLiquidateWithReplacement()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-size/blob/main/src/libraries/actions/LiquidateWithReplacement.sol#L120-L163)

## Tools Used
Manual Audit

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Round the computed amount of cash (`issuanceValue`) in the same way that is rounded when computing the amount of cash the CreditSeller (borrower) should receive when a SellCredit LimitOrder is filled with a BuyCredit MarketOrder. (rounding up).

```
function executeLiquidateWithReplacement(State storage state, LiquidateWithReplacementParams calldata params)
    external
    returns (uint256 issuanceValue, uint256 liquidatorProfitCollateralToken, uint256 liquidatorProfitBorrowToken)
{
    ...
    
-   issuanceValue = Math.mulDivDown(debtPositionCopy.futureValue, PERCENT, PERCENT + ratePerTenor);
+   issuanceValue = Math.mulDivUp(debtPositionCopy.futureValue, PERCENT, PERCENT + ratePerTenor);

    ...
}
```
