# Vulnerability details

## Impact
**Problem:**
* **Intended Behavior:** Self-liquidation should only be allowed when the debt value (regarding collateral token) exceeds the total collateral assigned to the specific credit position being self-liquidated.
* **Code Behavior:** The code checks if the borrower is underwater (`state.collateralRatio(debtPosition.borrower)`), meaning it considers the borrower's overall collateralization, not just the collateral tied to the specific credit position.

**Why this is a bug:**
* **Profitable Self-Liquidation:** If a borrower has multiple credit positions, a lender could self-liquidate a credit position even if that specific position is overcollateralized. This could happen if the borrower's other positions are significantly undercollateralized, dragging their overall collateral ratio below the threshold.
* **Incorrect Incentive:** This creates an incorrect incentive for lenders. They might be encouraged to self-liquidate even when it's not in their best interest (if the specific credit position is safe), simply because the borrower is underwater overall.

The `validateSelfLiquidate` function checks if the borrower is underwater (`state.collateralRatio(debtPosition.borrower)`). This means it considers the borrower's overall collateralization across all their positions, not just the collateral assigned explicitly to the credit position being self-liquidated (`params.creditPositionId`).

**Let's say:**
* **Borrower (Bob):** Has two credit positions:
    - **Credit Position 1:** 100 ETH collateral, 5000 USDC debt (healthy, overcollateralized)
    - **Credit Position 2:** 10 ETH collateral, 8000 USDC debt (risky, undercollateralized)
* **Lender (Alice):** Holds Credit Position 1.
* **Liquidation Threshold:** 150% (meaning a borrower needs at least 1.5 ETH collateral for every 1 USDC borrowed).

1. **Bob's Overall Collateralization:** Bob has 110 ETH collateral (100 + 10) and 13000 USDC debt (5000 + 8000). His collateral ratio is below the liquidation threshold, making him underwater.
2. **Alice's Self-Liquidation Opportunity:** Even though Credit Position 1 (held by Alice) is overcollateralized, Alice can still call it `selfLiquidate` because Bob is underwater overall.
3. **Profitable Self-Liquidation:** Alice receives 100 ETH collateral for a 5000 USDC debt, even though the position was safe. This is a profitable self-liquidation at the protocol's or other lenders' expense.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Modify the `validateSelfLiquidate` function to check the collateralization of the credit position being self-liquidated specifically.

May be:
```solidity
if (state.collateralRatio(debtPosition.borrower) < PERCENT) {
    revert Errors.LIQUIDATION_NOT_AT_LOSS(params.creditPositionId, state.collateralRatio(debtPosition.borrower));
}
```
The original code prevents self-liquidation if the collateral ratio exceeds 100%. This means that even if the borrower has underwater assets, they cannot liquidate them.
