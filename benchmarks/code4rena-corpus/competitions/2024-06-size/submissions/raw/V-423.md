# Vulnerability details

## Impact
According to already established, logic anytime cash movement that can be claimed is done, a check for that validate the variable pool balance is greater than the amount that is being received, as shown in `buyCreditMArket`, `sellCreeditMarket` and `liqudateWithReplacement` but in the liquidate function, this implementation does not exist even though cash is being transferred in the liquidation process,

## Proof of Concept
The liqudate function does not validate the amount being transferred even though cash transfer is done, as shown in the `executeLiqudateFunction` 

https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-size/blob/8850e25fb088898e9cf86f9be1c401ad155bea86/src/libraries/actions/Liquidate.sol#L117C1-L125C74

```solidity
state.data.borrowAToken.transferFrom(msg.sender, address(this), debtPosition.futureValue);
        state.data.collateralToken.transferFrom(debtPosition.borrower, msg.sender, liquidatorProfitCollateralToken);
        state.data.collateralToken.transferFrom(
            debtPosition.borrower, state.feeConfig.feeRecipient, protocolProfitCollateralToken
        );

        debtPosition.liquidityIndexAtRepayment = state.data.borrowAToken.liquidityIndex();
        //@audit#-# Can this be called Twice 
        state.repayDebt(params.debtPositionId, debtPosition.futureValue);
```
As evident in the snippet above, borrowATokens is transferred to size, and credit owners/lenders are expected to claim, as evident from the liquidity index update and the fact that Loan.Status is set to repaid

But the amount being transferred is not checked against the balance of the borrowATokens of the variable pool.
