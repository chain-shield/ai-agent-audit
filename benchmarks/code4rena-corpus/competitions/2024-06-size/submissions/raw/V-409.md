# Vulnerability details

## Impact

Liquidating small positions do not present any incentive for liquidators as the gas costs to do so would surpass the bonus received from it. 

Since the protocol awards 5% of the value of the collateral which is assigned to that specific value, and allows loans as small as 5 USD, small positions which are in a liquidatable state (either overdue or undercollateralized) would present no incentive for liquidators as the gas costs would greatly surpass the rewards (especially on Ethereum). 

## Proof of Concept

Since borrowers are allowed to sell credit and open debt positions as small as 5 USD:

```
minimumCreditBorrowAToken: 5e6
```

A user's borrow positions can be divided into multiple smaller ones, which if they become overdue / undercollateralized would present no incentive for liquidators to liquidate them.

- The only way would be if they reach a collateralization ratio of under 1e18 for the lender to "withdraw" their collateral and close the position which again leads to the accrual of bad debt.
- This presents a challenge for overdue positions as well, both in terms of the small liquidation incentive and the inability to perform this for overdue loans.

Let's imagine the following scenario:
- Bob has a borrow position of 100 USD, if the position is liquidated, the amount of collateral which the liquidator will receive would be worth 105, which is not a large enough incentive to cover gas costs, especially during times of network congestion when gas prices are high.

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Either increase the minimum threshold for a loan OR create an incentivized bot system (financed through some fees) that will liquidate small positions.
