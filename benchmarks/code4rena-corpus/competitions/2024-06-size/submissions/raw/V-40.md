# Vulnerability details

## Impact
1. Precision Issues in Collateral Ratio Check:

Impact:
Incorrect Liquidation Decisions: The liquidation process might be incorrectly triggered or prevented due to precision errors in the collateral ratio calculation.
Financial Loss: Lenders or borrowers might face unexpected financial losses or gains due to incorrect liquidation status.
Reputation Risk: Inaccurate liquidations can harm the platform's reputation and trustworthiness.

2. Accurate Calculation of Assigned Collateral:

Impact:
Incorrect Collateral Transfer: Miscalculation of the pro-rata assigned collateral can result in transferring incorrect amounts of collateral.
Financial Discrepancies: Both lenders and borrowers could experience financial discrepancies, leading to potential disputes.
Legal and Compliance Issues: Incorrect collateral handling might lead to legal challenges and non-compliance with financial regulations.

3. Debt and Credit Reduction Accuracy:

Impact:
Underflow/Overflow Errors: Incorrect arithmetic operations could cause underflow or overflow, leading to incorrect debt and credit balances.
Financial Instability: The financial stability of the platform could be compromised, affecting all users.
Security Vulnerabilities: Arithmetic errors might be exploited by malicious actors, leading to potential security breaches and financial loss.
