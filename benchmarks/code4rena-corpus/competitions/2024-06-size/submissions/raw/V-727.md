# Vulnerability details

`forSale` flag behavior depends on whether it's a full credit size being transferred, while it's a property that doesn't imply any business logic and which borrower fully controls as they can arbitrarily run the operations listed below with partial or full credit position utilization.

As a result it's possible to manipulate `forSale` flag of the credit position created in `Compensate`, `SellCreditMarket` and `BuyCreditMarket`. While in `BuyCreditMarket` new position is created for the caller, in `Compensate` and `SellCreditMarket` it's created for a third party lender. Calling borrower can choose to set the flag to `true` of `false` based on the flag of the credit position they control and supply.

It's possible to sell credit position with `forSale == false` via `SellCreditMarket` and manipulate existing credit position inverting `forSale` via `Compensate`. The latter is independent of the `createDebtAndCreditPositions()` based flow discussed in another issue (there it has to be new position and `RESERVED_ID`, here it's an existing credit position of the borrower, which they can prepare).

## Impact

Forcing a sale can have material impact for a lender, provided that they have a non-empty `borrowOffer` reasonably far from the market, so it's profitable to execute it. Whenever their `allCreditPositionsForSaleDisabled` flag is set to `false` and their free collateral isn't available the `forSale` flag of the individual credit positions remains the only control.

Such forced sale of a profitable credit position constitutes a direct loss for the lender proportional to credit position size and distance of their borrow offer from the current market, i.e. to the interest rate volatility. That can be substantial and there are no additional prerequisites apart from this setup, which is feasible, so placing the severity to be high.

## Proof of Concept

`forSale` is reset if `exitCreditPosition.credit != credit` and kept otherwise, while `exitCreditPosition.credit == credit` condition by itself doesn't correspond to any particular business logic (can be any with no additional meaning), and is controlled by the same entity when caller is the position owner:

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Consider introducing a flag parameter to `createCreditPosition()` that determines what value to set for the new position's `forSale`.

For new positions of a lender (ones not existing before the operation) consider to passing `true` to match straightforward borrowing/lending behavior. For existing positions (whenever a lender already has one and the new positions are deriving from it) consider passing the old lender position's flag.
