# Vulnerability details

## Impact
When protocol fees are calculated it should also round up in favor of the protocol, likewise when calculating users amounts it should round down in favor of the protocol, the protocol profit should be the highest priority. But in liquidate function when calculating profit for the protocol it rounds down instead of up leading to loss of funds for the protocol

## Proof of Concept
```solidity
 protocolProfitCollateralToken = Math.mulDivDown(collateralRemainder, collateralProtocolPercent, PERCENT);
```
Rounding down here leads to less amount of profit being realized by the protocol overtime, causing loss of funds

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
MulDivUp should always be used for fees and profit meant for the protocol.
