# Vulnerability details

## Impact
In BuyMarket the total credit is undervalued due to rounding in favour of the borrowers and not the protocol and the lender. Due also to the fact that borrow token is usdc which has only 6 decimals and the fact that the wrong rounding is done on all operations, this will lead to a significant loss of yield on the long term for the protocol.
