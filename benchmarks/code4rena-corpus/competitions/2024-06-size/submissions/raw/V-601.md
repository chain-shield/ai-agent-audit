# Vulnerability details

## Description
NOTE : This issue has nothing to do with [known issue](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-size/blob/8850e25fb088898e9cf86f9be1c401ad155bea86/README.md?plain=1#L28) regarding price change of collateral. The known issue refers to the change of the price of the collateral and that this (the change of the price) can cause lots of issues upon the pausing of the contract. This report doesn't refer to price change and it can happen regardless if the price of the collateral changes or not during the time the contract is paused.

When users take a loan calling ```sellCreditMarket()```, they take ```szaUSDC``` tokens (which have value only inside the protocol) and they are supposed to ```withdraw()``` them so to actually use the ```USDC``` token they borrowed in the broader blockchain ecosystem. Then, after some time when they want to repay their loan and give the ```szaUSDC``` back, they have to deposit the ```USDC``` tokens in Size and then call ```repay()```. This action must be taken before the ```dueDate``` of their ```DebtPosition``` because otherwise the loan will be overdue and liquidatable. The problem arises in the ```repay()``` and ```deposit()``` functions, where both of them are under the ```whenNotPaused``` modifier. This means that if the contract is paused, users are not able to return the borrowed ```szaUSDC``` tokens but the time is ticking and in this frame, their ```DebtPosition``` can pass the ```dueDate``` and be liquidatable. Finally, when the contract is unpaused, liquidator bots will instantly liquidate the ```DebtPosition``` and extract some collateral of the user. This shouldn't happen as it is not the user's fault that the contract was paused and his ```DebtPosition``` became liquidatable.

## Impact
The impact of this issue is unwanted and unintended liquidations of users' DebtPositions who can not do anything to prevent them and it is out of their control to repay the debt because the contract is paused.

## Proof of Concept
To understand this scenario better, let's consider the following example:
1. Alice opens a debt position of 1000 szaUSDC and dueDate after 5 hours.
2. Alice withdraws 1000 szaUSDC to take 1000 USDC and use them.
3. After 4 hours, the contract is paused.
4. Alice, now, needs to deposit back the USDC and repay their DebtPosition but she can't.
5. After 1 hour, the contract is unpaused and liquidator bots, instantly, liquidate Alice's DebtPosition.

With this scenario, it is clear that the user is not able to repay their loan not because they don't want to but because the contract is paused.
This could happen even if the user had the szaUSDC tokens in the protocol, since they can't repay their loan, they will be liquidated.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Remove ```whenNotPaused``` from ```repay()``` and ```deposit()``` functions so users can repay their loans even if the contract is paused.
