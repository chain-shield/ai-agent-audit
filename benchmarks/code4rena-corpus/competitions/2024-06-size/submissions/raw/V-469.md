# Vulnerability details

## Impact

Impact on protocol earnings

## Proof of Concept

By definition, from the [docs](https://docs.size.credit/technical-docs/contracts/2.3-fees) the Protocol fee(swapFeeApr) is defined as **0.5% per year on all credit for cash operations and the recipient of the cash pays the fee**. However, during a `liquidateWithReplacement` operation which is also a credit for cash operation as a borrow limit order(BorrowOffer) is filled, the cash repcipient(borrower) does does not pay any fees. As seen from the [code](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-size/blob/8850e25fb088898e9cf86f9be1c401ad155bea86/src/libraries/actions/LiquidateWithReplacement.sol#L146-L147) the profit extracted is only dependent on the loans interest/rate.
