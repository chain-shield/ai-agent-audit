# Vulnerability details

## Impact
To be matched with a lender, a borrower must deposit ETH/WETH as collateral and create a limit order specifying their yield curve. After a lender picks a borrower's offer, the offer is not cleared, allowing the borrower to receive additional loans they may not want. This can decrease their collateral ratio, potentially making them eligible for liquidation if the price of ETH drops quickly.

## Proof of Concept
Consider the following scenario:
* Bob wants to borrow 10,000 USDC, and he also wants to over-collateralize his position to ensure he won't be liquidated even if the price of ETH drops significantly. 
* He deposits 30 ETH as collateral at price $1500 per ETH. He specifies his yield curve, calls `sellCreditLimit` and waits to be matched with lender. 
* Alice notice Bob's order and lends him the desired 10,000 USDC, resulting in a very high collateral ratio for Bob - 428%. Bob goes away **without** clearing his `borrowOffer` (this can happen by mistake, because he doesn't know or because he forgets, it doesn't matter). 
* After a while, Alice observes that Bob's offer remains uncleared, allowing her to lend him another 18,000 USDC, which decreases his collateral ratio from 428% to 153%. This is not necessarily a malicious action. Alice might simply be trying to maximize her lending returns without understanding the impact on Bob's collateral ratio. 
* Bob being unaware of this second loan, for various reasons , is unable to take any corrective actions. His position has now become much riskier due to the lower collateral ratio.
* Let's immagine now a significant decrease at ETH price, making Bob's two positions eligible for liquidation.

Paste the following test in `BuyCreditMarket.t.sol`
```solidity
function test_BuyCreditMarket_buyCreditMarket_overcollateralizedPosition() public {
        _setPrice(1500e18);

        _deposit(alice, usdc, 30000e6);
        _deposit(bob, weth, 30e18);

        _sellCreditLimit(bob, YieldCurveHelper.pointCurve(365 days, 0.05e18));

        uint256 firstDebtPosition = _buyCreditMarket(alice, bob, RESERVED_ID, 10000e6, 365 days, true);

        // 428% collateral ratio
        assertEq(size.collateralRatio(bob), 4285714285714285714);

        uint256 secondDebtPosition = _buyCreditMarket(alice, bob, RESERVED_ID, 18000e6, 365 days, true);

        // 153% collateral ratio
        assertEq(size.collateralRatio(bob), 1530612244897959183);

        // price drops making Bob's positions eligible for liquidation
        _setPrice(1200e18);
        
        assertTrue(size.isDebtPositionLiquidatable(firstDebtPosition));
        assertTrue(size.isDebtPositionLiquidatable(secondDebtPosition));
    }
```
