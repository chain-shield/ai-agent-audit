# Vulnerability details

## Impact
When compensating a creditposition with another creditposition(borrower  creditposition), there is no check whether the creditposition forSale - false/true. As a result lender creditposition(after replacing with new creditposition ) other lenders without their intention before making forSale - false for the creditposition.



## Proof of Concept
1. Let’s assume, alice has 3 creditpositionid . they are creditpositionid 1, creditpositionid 2, creditpositionid 3.

2. Alice’s creditpositionid 2’s debtpositionid is 2 whose borrower is bob.

3. Alice’s creditpositionid 2’s borrower is Bob who has also 3 creditpositionid and those are  creditpositionid 4, creditpositionid 5, creditpositionid 6.

4. Bob wants to compensate for debt with his creditposition. So Bob compensated debt of creditposition 2 by giving creditposition 4 to alice. 

5. Here Alice's  creditpositionid 2’s forSale - false but Bob's creditposition 4’s  forSale - true. Now alice gets creditposition 4 which forSale is true which is unintentional for alice.

## Tools Used
manual review
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
When compensating , replace forSale condition from old creditpositionid to new creditpositioid for lenders.
