# Vulnerability details

## Impact

Impact - High, Could result in a DOS on creating perfectly viable loan positions.
LikeliHood - Low, as protocol fees are almost negligible in most cases.

## Proof of Concept

Variable pool liquidity is checked when creating credit/debt positions via the following functions: [buyCreditMarket](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-size/blob/8850e25fb088898e9cf86f9be1c401ad155bea86/src/Size.sol#L184-L184), [sellCreditMarket](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-size/blob/8850e25fb088898e9cf86f9be1c401ad155bea86/src/Size.sol#L194-L194) and [liquidateWithReplacement](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-size/blob/8850e25fb088898e9cf86f9be1c401ad155bea86/src/Size.sol#L243-L243). The issue is that the amount(cashAmountIn) returned from `executeBuyCreditMarket` and validated in `buyCreditMarket` includes the protocol fees as seen here 
![image](https://gist.github.com/assets/133224432/104c6970-9eb5-46b1-b11f-f4186c37c1a0)
![image2](https://gist.github.com/assets/133224432/5d00dd14-ae7a-4a78-b8cb-3ecb05fe6d72)
This implies that in cases where there's just enough liquidity on aave to grant a loan the added protocol fees might Dos the transaction.

Admittedly, protocol fees are almost negligible for short term/average amount loans , it can be an issue for long term/high amount loans e.g a 6 month 500k loan will have a protocol fee at $1,250 (0.5% APR).
