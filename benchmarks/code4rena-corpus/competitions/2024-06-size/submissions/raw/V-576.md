# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Users don't have a chance to make their position whole in case of a pause which can cause their position to be liquidated once the protocol is unpaused.
## Proof of Concept
The address with the `PAUSER_ROLE` can pause the protocol. When the protocol is paused the following actions cannot be made:
- multicall
- deposit
- withdraw
- buyCreditLimit
- sellCreditLimit
- buyCreditMarket
- sellCreditMarket
- repay
- claim
- liquidate
- selfLiquidate
- liquidateWithReplacement
- compensate

If during a pause the price of collateral falls sharply the users who have opened positions cannot deposit any more collateral or repay the loan. This can make the position go underwater and eligible for liquidation.

Once the protocol resumes the liquidators can frontrun the users and immediately liquidate the loans without users even having a chance to make their position whole.
## Tools Used
Manual Review
## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Consider adding a grace period after unpausing where users can make their position healthy again.
