# Vulnerability details

## Impact
If the **Variable Pool (Aave v3)** fails to supply or withdraw for any reason, such as **supply caps**, it can have a **significant impact** on the **liquidation process** within the protocol. The **Variable Pool** is essential for providing the necessary **liquidity** to facilitate **liquidations**. Its failure can lead to a **liquidity crunch**, **increased insolvency risk** for borrowers, **financial losses** for the protocol, and overall **operational inefficiencies**.

## Proof of Concept
### Explanation

**Liquidation**: Liquidation is the process of selling off a borrower's collateral to cover their outstanding debt when they fail to maintain the required collateral ratio. This ensures that the protocol remains solvent and minimizes the risk of bad debt.

**Variable Pool (Aave v3)**: The Variable Pool in Aave v3 is a liquidity pool that allows users to supply and withdraw assets. It provides the necessary liquidity for various operations, including liquidations.

### Scenario

**Variable Pool Failure**: The Variable Pool (Aave v3) experiences a failure, preventing it from supplying or withdrawing liquidity due to reasons such as supply caps.

**Debt Position**: A borrower has a debt position that needs to be liquidated to cover their outstanding debt.

**Liquidity Crunch**: Due to the failure of the Variable Pool, there is insufficient liquidity available to cover the debt position during liquidation.

**Increased Insolvency Risk**: The borrower, who relies on the liquidation process to manage their debt, faces rapid insolvency due to the inability to liquidate their position effectively.

**Protocol Financial Losses**: The protocol incurs significant financial losses due to unresolved debt positions and the inability to collect expected fees from liquidations.

**Operational Inefficiencies**: The failure of the Variable Pool disrupts the normal operations of the protocol, leading to inefficiencies and potential delays in the liquidation process.
