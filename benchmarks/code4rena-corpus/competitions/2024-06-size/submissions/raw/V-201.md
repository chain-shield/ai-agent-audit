# Vulnerability details

## Impact
Borrower’s large position can be liquidated resulting in the bigger loss which could have been prevented. 
## Proof of Concept
A single borrower may have multiple debt positions of varying sizes in the system. If the borrower becomes susceptible to liquidation due to a price drop, the liquidation should always start from the smallest position. This approach allows the borrower an opportunity to improve their Collateralization Ratio (CR), if it is only slightly below the required level.

For instance, if a borrower has three positions:

1. $1000
2. $2000
3. $10000

And their CR is just one point below the requirement, for example 129%, liquidating the $1000 position instead of the $10000 one could prevent a significant loss.

From the borrower's perspective, it's not always feasible to continuously monitor their position. This approach enhances the platform's efficiency and minimizes the borrower's loss, especially for positions that are not critically unhealthy.

## Tools Used
Manual review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps

Only allow liquidator to liquidate position from the smaller one instead of allowing random liquidation.
