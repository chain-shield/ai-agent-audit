# Vulnerability details

## Description
NOTE : This issue has nothing to do with [known issue](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-06-size/blob/8850e25fb088898e9cf86f9be1c401ad155bea86/README.md?plain=1#L28) regarding price change of collateral. The known issue refers to the change of the price of the collateral and that this (the change of the price) can cause lots of issues upon the pausing of the contract. This report doesn't refer to price change and it can happen regardless if the price of the collateral changes or not during the time the contract is paused.

When a user wants to make a borrow limit order, he calls ```sellCreditLimit``` and creates a struct specifying the aprs, tenors and marketMultipliers  he want to apply. In the case, something happens and the user wants to delete this offer, he can call the same functions again passing all zero values to the struct. However, when the protocol is paused he can not delete it and this may have monetary consequences for him. Suppose that the protocol pauses and in the meantime the variablePoolBorrowRate changes this means that it will be the same marketMultipliers specified in the BorrowOffer for the previous rates and the new ones. This can possible create an opportunity for someone to lend his money at a lower rate than the current market instantly when the protocol unpauses. Now, the user who has his BorrowOffer with outdated market multipliers is forcefully lent money and payed swap fees for them. When he tries to repay, he will have to pay the extra cash that he didn't get(cause of fees) for this unwanted loan.

## Impact
User will be forced to take a loan that he didn't wanted and couldn't be able to delete due to the protocol being paused. All in all, he will lose moeny since he will have to pay fees for the unwanted funds he took.

## Proof of concept
To understand this vulnerability better, consider this scenario :
1. Alice creates a BorrowOffer with apr of 2% and marketMultiplier of 1 knowing that the variablePoolBorrowRate is 5%.
2. The protocol pauses and the variablePoolBorrowRate changes to 2%.
3. Alice can not delete her BorrowOffer or chaneg the marketMultiplier of it.
4. Protocol unpauses and instantly Alice is lent unwanted money at 2% + 1 * 2% = 4%.

Now, Alice got debt equals with X amount but she got cash equals with X / (1.04) * 0.98. This 2% she lost is the fees and she will have to pay it herself (suppose that she can find someone to give this credit in the same conditions).

## Tools Used
Manual Review

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Consider allowing users to delete their BorrowOffers even if the protocol is paused removing the ```whenNotPaused``` modifier from ```sellCreditLimit``` function.
