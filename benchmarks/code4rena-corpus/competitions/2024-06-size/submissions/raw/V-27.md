# Vulnerability details

## Impact
The reentrancy vulnerability in the multicall function can lead to severe financial loss and unintended state changes. An attacker could exploit this vulnerability to repeatedly reenter the contract and manipulate its state, potentially draining contract funds or causing other malicious effects. This could compromise the integrity and security of the entire protocol.
