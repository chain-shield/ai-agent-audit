# Vulnerability details

Lender can sell his credit to another lender via `sellCreditMarket` passing a credit position id and the amount that he want to sell, this is with the purpose to get his money early. or To exit early another lender have to call the `buyCreditMarket` passing the credit position id of the lender that want exit early.

The problem is that a lender trying to sell all his credit position may be front run for another lender buying the minimum credit opening position making revert the transaction due the next check:


Since the lender that is trying to sell early is passing `param.amount = creditPosition.credit ` another lender can front run this tx buying the minimum credit possible that is allow to open a position. making revert the transaction


## Impact
Any lender always can DoS a Lender trying to exit credit early.
