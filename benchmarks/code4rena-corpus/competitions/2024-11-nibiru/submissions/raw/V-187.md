# Vulnerability details

## Issue Description: 

The queryExchangeRate function does not impose limits on gas usage or ensure early termination on resource-intensive calls.

## Proof-of-Concept: 

A malicious input designed to force heavy computation or numerous external queries can deplete the gas limit:

    // Simulate malicious input that would trigger intensive oracle queries
    args := []any{"massive/pair"}

## Impact: 

Potential denial of service (DoS) by depleting gas resources.

## Risk Level: 

High
