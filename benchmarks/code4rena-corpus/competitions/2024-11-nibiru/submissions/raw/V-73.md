The delta calculation in SetAccBalance involves subtraction between two potentially unbounded values (amountEvmDenom and balance), which could lead to integer overflow/underflow.

Rounding issues in the delta calculation may lead to incorrect balance adjustments.

## Proof of Concept:

statedb.go#L77

## Impact:

If a large or malformed input is provided, it could result in unintended behavior, such as minting or burning an incorrect amount of tokens.

Medium
