# Vulnerability details

FunToken is not compatible with tokens that change balance outside of transfers

## Finding Description and Impact

Certain tokens, such as [Ampleforth (AMPL)](https://etherscan.io/token/0xd46ba6d942050d489dbd938a2c909a5d5039a161), utilize mechanisms that adjust token balances outside of traditional `transfer` calls. These mechanisms, often referred to as "rebasing," can increase or decrease token balances in holders' accounts dynamically.

### **Impact**
1. **Accounting Errors**: FunToken assumes token balances remain constant unless explicitly altered through transfers. Rebasing tokens violate this assumption, causing discrepancies between FunToken's internal accounting and the actual token balances.
2. **Potential Fund Loss**: Users might experience scenarios where:
   - Some users redeem more than their fair share of tokens, leading to **overcompensation**.
   - Other users lose funds due to **insufficient remaining balances** in the FunToken contract.
3. **Ecosystem Incompatibility**: FunToken cannot reliably interact with rebasing tokens, reducing its utility for such tokens and affecting user trust.


## Proof of Concept

The following scenario demonstrates the issue with rebasing tokens like AMPL:

1. Alice and Bob each convert **50 AMPL** into FunToken, resulting in the FunToken contract holding a total of **100 AMPL**.
2. A rebasing event occurs, reducing the total supply of AMPL. The FunToken contract's balance now reflects **80 AMPL** instead of 100.
3. Alice decides to redeem her FunToken for **50 AMPL**. After this redemption:
   - The FunToken contract balance becomes **30 AMPL** (80 - 50).
4. Bob attempts to redeem his **50 FunToken**, but only **30 AMPL** remains in the FunToken contract. Bob cannot fully redeem his FunToken, leading to a loss of **20 AMPL**.

This discrepancy arises because FunToken does not account for balance changes resulting from rebasing events.


## Recommended Mitigation Steps

To ensure compatibility with rebasing tokens, you can calculate the redemption amount as a proportion of the EVM module current token balance.
