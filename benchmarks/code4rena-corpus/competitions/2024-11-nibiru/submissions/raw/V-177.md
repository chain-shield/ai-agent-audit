# Vulnerability details

## Issue Description

The aggregated gas limit for all messages (txGasLimit) may not account for edge cases like varying gas costs across different validators or unexpected values in msgEthTx.GetGas().

## Proof of Concept

    txGasLimit += msgEthTx.GetGas()


## Impact

- Could lead to undercharging or overcharging of gas fees.

- Introduces risks of DoS attacks if improperly validated.

## Risk

High

## Mitigation

- Cap txGasLimit to a reasonable maximum value.

- Validate gas costs per transaction against network-wide averages or rules.
