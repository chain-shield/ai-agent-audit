The method does not validate blockCount or rewardPercentiles, potentially allowing invalid or malicious input.


## Proof-of-Concept: 

Supplying large blockCount or malformed rewardPercentiles can exhaust system resources or cause undefined behavior.

    api.FeeHistory(1e9, -1, []float64{-1.0, 200.0})

## Impact: High

Can cause resource exhaustion or undefined behavior.

## Mitigation: 

Add bounds checks for inputs:


    if blockCount > maxAllowedBlockCount || blockCount < 1 {
