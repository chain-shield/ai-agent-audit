If `parseArgsBankBalance` returns an error, it continues to execute and tries to get the balance using invalid addresses or denomination. 


All other methods ([`sendToBank`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-11-nibiru/blob/8ed91a036f664b421182e183f19f6cef1a4e28ea/x/evm/precompile/funtoken.go#L119-L122), [`balance`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-11-nibiru/blob/8ed91a036f664b421182e183f19f6cef1a4e28ea/x/evm/precompile/funtoken.go#L279-L283), [`whoAmI`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2024-11-nibiru/blob/8ed91a036f664b421182e183f19f6cef1a4e28ea/x/evm/precompile/funtoken.go#L456-L459)) properly check for errors from their respective parsing functions before proceeding with their logic.

## Impact
By not checking the error, the function may attempt to use invalid addresses or denominations which could lead to panics. 


## Recommendation
Consider checking the error immediately after `parseArgsBankBalance` and return if there's an error, similar to how other methods handle errors.
