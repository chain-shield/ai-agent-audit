# Vulnerability details

## Proof-of-Concept Code:

utils.go#L149

    gasUsedRatio := gasusedfloat / float64(gasLimitUint64)

## Impact:

- Division-by-zero errors if gasLimitUint64 is invalid.

- Incorrect gas usage ratio calculations.
## Risk: 

High

## Mitigation:
