    uid=21_353 StaticText "False positives:"

    uid=21_355 StaticText "validatePrice()"
    uid=21_356 StaticText " returns true even though the TWAP is based on outdated data and doesn't represent actual recent market activity."
    uid=21_357 StaticText "Given that "
